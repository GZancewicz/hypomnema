15:1 Anger slays even wise men; yet a submissive answer turns away wrath: but a grievous word stirs up anger.
15:2 The tongue of the wise knows what is good: but the mouth of the foolish tells out evil things.
15:3 The eyes of the Lord behold both the evil and the good in every place.
15:4 The wholesome tongue is a tree of life, and he that keeps it shall be filled with understanding.
15:5 A fool scorns his father's instruction: but he that keeps his commandments is more prudent. In abounding righteousness is great strength: but the ungodly shall utterly perish from the earth.
15:6 In the houses of the righteous is much strength: but the fruits of the ungodly shall perish.
15:7 The lips of the wise are bound by discretion: but the hearts of the foolish are not safe.
15:8 The sacrifices of the ungodly are an abomination to the Lord; but the prayers of them that walk honestly are acceptable with him.
15:9 The ways of an ungodly man are an abomination to the Lord; but he loves those that follow after righteousness.
15:10 The instruction of the simple is known by them that pass by; but they that hate reproofs die disgracefully.
15:11 Hell and destruction are manifest to the Lord; how shall not also be the hearts of men?
15:12 An uninstructed person will not love those that reprove him; neither will he associate with the wise.
15:13 When the heart rejoices the countenance is cheerful; but when it is in sorrow, the countenance is sad.
15:14 An upright heart seeks discretion; but the mouth of the uninstructed will experience evils.
15:15 The eyes of the wicked are always looking for evil things; but the good are always quiet.
15:16 Better is a small portion with the fear of the Lord, than great treasures without the fear of the Lord.
15:17 Better is an entertainment of herbs with friendliness and kindness, than a feast of calves, with enmity.
15:18 A passionate man stirs up strife; but he that is slow to anger appeases even a rising one.
15:18a A man slow to anger will extinguish quarrels; but an ungodly man rather stirs them up.
15:19 The ways of sluggards are strewn with thorns; but those of the diligent are made smooth.
15:20 A wise son gladdens his father; but a foolish son sneers at his mother.
15:21 The ways of a foolish man are void of sense; but a wise man proceeds on his way aright.
15:22 They that honour not councils put off deliberation; but counsel abides in the hearts of counsellors.
15:23 A bad man will by no means attend to counsel; neither will he say anything seasonable, or good for the common weal.
15:24 The thoughts of the wise are ways of life, that he may turn aside and escape from hell.
15:25 The Lord pulls down the houses of scorners; but he establishes the border of the widow.
15:26 An unrighteous thought is abomination to the Lord; but the sayings of the pure are held in honour.
15:27 A receiver of bribes destroys himself; but he that hates the receiving of bribes is safe.
15:27a [By alms and by faithful dealings sins are purged away;] but by the fear of the Lord every one departs from evil.
15:28 The hearts of the righteous meditate faithfulness; but the mouth of the ungodly answers evil things.
15:28a The ways of righteous men are acceptable with the Lord; and through them even enemies become friends.
15:29 God is far from the ungodly; but he hearkens to the prayers of the righteous.
15:29a Better are small receipts with righteousness, than abundant fruits with unrighteousness.
15:29b Let the heart of a man think justly, that his steps may be rightly ordered of God.
15:30 The eye that sees rightly rejoices the heart; and a good report fattens the bones.
15:32 He that rejects instruction hates himself; but he that minds reproofs loves his soul.
15:33 The fear of the Lord is instruction and wisdom; and the highest honour will correspond therewith.
