41:1 Hast thou not seen him? and hast thou not wondered at the things said of him?
41:2 Dost thou not fear because preparation has been made by me? for who is there that resists me?
41:3 Or who will resist me, and abide, since the whole world under heaven is mine?
41:4 I will not be silent because of him: though because of his power one shall pity his antagonist.
41:5 Who will open the face of his garment? and who can enter within the fold of his breast-plate?
41:6 Who will open the doors of his face? terror is round about his teeth.
41:7 His inwards are as brazen plates, and the texture of his skin as a smyrite stone.
41:8 One part cleaves fast to another, and the air cannot come between them.
41:9 They will remain united each to the other: they are closely joined, and cannot be separated.
41:10 At his sneezing a light shines, and his eyes are as the appearance of the morning star.
41:11 Out of his mouth proceed as it were burning lamps, and as it were hearths of fire are cast abroad.
41:12 Out of his nostrils proceeds smoke of a furnace burning with fire of coals.
41:13 His breath is as live coals, and a flame goes out of his mouth.
41:14 And power is lodged in his neck, before him destruction runs.
41:15 The flesh also of his body is joined together: if one pours violence upon him, he shall not be moved.
41:16 His heart is firm as a stone, and it stands like an unyielding anvil.
41:17 And when he turns, he is a terror to the four-footed wild beasts which leap upon the earth.
41:18 If spears should come against him, men will effect nothing, either with the spear or the breast-plate.
41:19 For he considers iron as chaff, and brass as rotten wood.
41:20 The bow of brass shall not wound him, he deems a slinger as grass.
41:21 Mauls are counted as stubble; and he laughs to scorn the waving of the firebrand.
41:22 His lair is formed of sharp points; and all the gold of the sea under him is as an immense quantity of clay.
41:23 He makes the deep boil like a brazen caldron; and he regards the sea as a pot of ointment,
41:24 and the lowest part of the deep as a captive: he reckons the deep as his range.
41:25 There is nothing upon the earth like to him, formed to be sported with by my angels.
41:26 He beholds every high thing: and he is king of all that are in the waters.
