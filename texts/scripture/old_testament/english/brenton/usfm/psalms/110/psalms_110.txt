110:1 Alleluia. I will give thee thanks, O Lord, with my whole heart, in the council of the upright, and in the congregation.
110:2 The works of the Lord are great, sought out according to all his will.
110:3 His work is worthy of thanksgiving and honour: and his righteousness endures for ever and ever.
110:4 He has caused his wonderful works to be remembered: the Lord is merciful and compassionate.
110:5 He has given food to them that fear him: he will remember his covenant for ever.
110:6 He has declared to his people the power of his works, to give them the inheritance of the heathen.
110:7 The works of his hands are truth and judgment: all his commandments are sure:
110:8 established for ever and ever, done in truth and uprightness.
110:9 He sent redemption to his people: he commanded his covenant for ever: holy and fearful is his name.
110:10 The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom, and all that act accordingly have a good understanding; his praise endures for ever and ever.
