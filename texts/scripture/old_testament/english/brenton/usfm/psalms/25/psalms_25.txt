25:1 A Psalm of David. Judge me, O Lord; for I have walked in my innocence: and hoping in the Lord I shall not be moved.
25:2 Prove me, O Lord, and try me; purify as with fire my reins and my heart.
25:3 For thy mercy is before mine eyes: and I am well pleased with thy truth.
25:4 I have not sat with the council of vanity, and will in nowise enter in with transgressors.
25:5 I have hated the assembly of wicked doers; and will not sit with ungodly men.
25:6 I will wash my hands in innocency, and compass thine altar, O Lord:
25:7 to hear the voice of praise, and to declare all thy wonderful works.
25:8 O Lord, I have loved the beauty of thy house, and the place of the tabernacle of thy glory.
25:9 Destroy not my soul together with the ungodly, nor my life with bloody men:
25:10 in whose hands are iniquities, and their right hand is filled with bribes.
25:11 But I have walked in my innocence: redeem me, and have mercy upon me.
25:12 My foot stands in an even place: in the congregations will I bless thee, O Lord.
