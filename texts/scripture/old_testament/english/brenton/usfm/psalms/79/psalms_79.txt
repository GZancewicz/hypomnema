79:1 For the end, for alternate strains, a testimony for Asaph, a Psalm concerning the Assyrian.
79:2 Attend, O Shepherd of Israel, who guidest Joseph like a flock; thou who sittest upon the cherubs, manifest thyself;
79:3 before Ephraim and Benjamin and Manasse, stir up thy power, and come to deliver us.
79:4 Turn us, O God, and cause thy face to shine; and we shall be delivered.
79:5 O Lord God of hosts, how long art thou angry with the prayer of thy servant?
79:6 Thou wilt feed us with bread of tears; and wilt cause us to drink tears by measure.
79:7 Thou hast made us a strife to our neighbours; and our enemies have mocked at us.
79:8 Turn us, O Lord God of hosts, and cause thy face to shine; and we shall be saved. Pause.
79:9 Thou hast transplanted a vine out of Egypt: thou hast cast out the heathen, and planted it.
79:10 Thou madest a way before it, and didst cause its roots to strike, and the land was filled with it.
79:11 Its shadow covered the mountains, and its shoots equalled the goodly cedars.
79:12 It sent forth its branches to the sea, and its shoots to the river.
79:13 Wherefore hast thou broken down its hedge, while all that pass by the way pluck it?
79:14 The boar out of the wood has laid it waste, and the wild beast has devoured it.
79:15 O God of hosts, turn, we pray thee: look on us from heaven, and behold and visit this vine;
79:16 and restore that which thy right hand has planted: and look on the son of man whom thou didst strengthen for thyself.
79:17 It is burnt with fire and dug up: they shall perish at the rebuke of thy presence.
79:18 Let thy hand be upon the man of thy right hand, and upon the son of man whom thou didst strengthen for thyself.
79:19 So will we not depart from thee: thou shalt quicken us, and we will call upon thy name.
79:20 Turn us, O Lord God of hosts, and make thy face to shine; and we shall be saved.
