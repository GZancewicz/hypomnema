92:1 For the day before the Sabbath, when the land was first inhabited, the praise of a Song by David. The Lord reigns; he has clothed himself with honour: the Lord has clothed and girded himself with strength; for he has established the world, which shall not be moved.
92:2 Thy throne is prepared of old: thou art from everlasting.
92:3 The rivers have lifted up, O Lord, the rivers have lifted up their voices,
92:4 at the voices of many waters: the billows of the sea are wonderful: the Lord is wonderful in high places.
92:5 Thy testimonies are made very sure: holiness becomes thine house, O Lord, for ever.
