122:1 A Song of Degrees. Unto thee who dwellest in heaven have I lifted up mine eyes.
122:2 Behold, as the eyes of servants are directed to the hands of their masters, and as the eyes of a maid-servant to the hands of her mistress; so our eyes are directed to the Lord our God, until he have mercy upon us.
122:3 Have pity upon us, O Lord, have pity upon us: for we are exceedingly filled with contempt.
122:4 Yea, our soul has been exceedingly filled with it: let the reproach be to them that are at ease, and contempt to the proud.
