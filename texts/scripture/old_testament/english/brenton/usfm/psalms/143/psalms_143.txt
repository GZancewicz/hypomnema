143:1 A Psalm of David concerning Goliad. Blessed be the Lord my God, who instructs my hands for battle, and my fingers for war.
143:2 My mercy, and my refuge; my helper, and my deliverer; my protector, in whom I have trusted; who subdues my people under me.
143:3 Lord, what is man, that thou art made known to him? or the son of man, that thou takest account of him?
143:4 Man is like to vanity: his days pass as a shadow.
143:5 O Lord, bow thy heavens, and come down: touch the mountains, and they shall smoke.
143:6 Send lightning, and thou shalt scatter them: send forth thine arrows, and thou shalt discomfit them.
143:7 Send forth thine hand from on high; rescue me, and deliver me out of great waters, out of the hand of strange children;
143:8 whose mouth has spoken vanity, and their right hand is a right hand of iniquity.
143:9 O God, I will sing a new song to thee: I will play to thee on a psaltery of ten strings.
143:10 Even to him who gives salvation to kings: who redeems his servant David from the hurtful sword.
143:11 Deliver me, and rescue me from the hand of strange children, whose mouth has spoken vanity, and their right hand is a right hand of iniquity;
143:12 whose children are as plants, strengthened in their youth: their daughters are beautiful, sumptuously adorned after the similitude of a temple.
143:13 Their garners are full, and bursting with one kind of store after another; their sheep are prolific, multiplying in their streets.
143:14 Their oxen are fat: there is no falling down of a hedge, nor going out, nor cry in their folds.
143:15 Men bless the people to whom this lot belongs, but blessed is the people whose God is the Lord.
