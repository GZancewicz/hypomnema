18:1 For the end, a Psalm of David.
18:2 The heavens declare the glory of God; and the firmament proclaims the work of his hands.
18:3 Day to day utters speech, and night to night proclaims knowledge.
18:4 There are no speeches or words, in which their voices are not heard.
18:5 Their voice is gone out into all the earth, and their words to the ends of the world.
18:6 In the sun he has set his tabernacle; and he comes forth as a bridegroom out of his chamber: he will exult as a giant to run his course.
18:7 His going forth is from the extremity of heaven, and his circuit to the other end of heaven: and no one shall be hidden from his heat.
18:8 The law of the Lord is perfect, converting souls: the testimony of the Lord is faithful, instructing babes.
18:9 The ordinances of the Lord are right, rejoicing the heart: the commandment of the Lord is bright, enlightening the eyes.
18:10 The fear of the Lord is pure, enduring for ever and ever: the judgments of the Lord are true, and justified altogether.
18:11 To be desired more than gold, and much precious stone: sweeter also than honey and the honey-comb.
18:12 For thy servant keeps to them: in the keeping of them there is great reward.
18:13 Who will understand his transgressions? purge thou me from my secret sins.
18:14 And spare thy servant the attack of strangers: if they do not gain the dominion over me, then shall I be blameless, and I shall be clear from great sin.
18:15 So shall the sayings of my mouth, and the meditation of my heart, be pleasing continually before thee, O Lord my helper, and my redeemer.
