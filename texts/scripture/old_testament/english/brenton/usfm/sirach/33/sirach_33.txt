33:1 Have mercy upon us, O Lord God of all, and behold us:
33:2 and send thy fear upon all the nations that seek not after thee.
33:3 Lift up thy hand against the strange nations, and let them see thy power.
33:4 As thou wast sanctified in us before them: so be thou magnified among them before us.
33:5 And let them know thee, as we have known thee, that there is no God but only thou, O God.
33:6 Shew new signs, and make other strange wonders: glorify thy hand and thy right arm, that they may set forth thy wondrous works.
33:7 Raise up indignation, and pour out wrath: take away the adversary, and destroy the enemy.
33:8 Make the time short, remember the covenant, and let them declare thy wonderful works.
33:9 Let him that escapeth be consumed by the rage of the fire; and let them perish that oppress the people.
33:10 Smite in sunder the heads of the rulers of the heathen, that say, There is none other but we.
33:11 Gather all the tribes of Jacob together.
33:12 A cheerful and good heart will have a care of his meat and diet.
