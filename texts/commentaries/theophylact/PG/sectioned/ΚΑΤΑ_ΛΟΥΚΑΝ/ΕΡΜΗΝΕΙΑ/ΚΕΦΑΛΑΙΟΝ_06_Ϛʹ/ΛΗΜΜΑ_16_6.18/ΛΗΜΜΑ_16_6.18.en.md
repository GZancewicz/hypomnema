# Luke 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 16 · Luke 6:18

> **they came to hear him... and to be healed from their diseases. (Luke 6:18)**

"They came to hear him"—this is the healing of souls; "and to be healed from their diseases"—this, the *healing* of bodies; for power went forth from him and he healed all. For the prophets and the other saints did not have power going forth from them, for they themselves were not fountains of powers. But the Lord had power going forth from him, for he himself was the fountain of the power. Whereas the prophets at least and the saints received power rather from above.
