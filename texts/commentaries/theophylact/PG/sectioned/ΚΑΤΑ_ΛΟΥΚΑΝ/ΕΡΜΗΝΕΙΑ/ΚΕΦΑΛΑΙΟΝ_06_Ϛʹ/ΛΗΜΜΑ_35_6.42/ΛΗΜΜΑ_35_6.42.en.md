# Luke 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 35 · Luke 6:42

> **Or how can you say to your brother, "Brother, allow that I cast out the speck that is in your eye," while you yourself do not see the beam that is in your eye? Hypocrite, cast out first the beam from your eye, and then you shall see clearly to cast out the speck that is in your brother's eye. For there is no good tree producing corrupt fruit, nor a corrupt tree producing good fruit. (Luke 6:42-43)**
