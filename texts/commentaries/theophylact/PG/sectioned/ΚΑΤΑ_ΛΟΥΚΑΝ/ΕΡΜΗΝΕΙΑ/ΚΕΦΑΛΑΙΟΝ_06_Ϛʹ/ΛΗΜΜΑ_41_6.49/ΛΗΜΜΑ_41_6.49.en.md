# Luke 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 41 · Luke 6:49

> **But he who heard and did not do is like a man having built a house upon the earth without a foundation; against which the river broke, and immediately it fell, and the ruin of that house became great. (Luke 6:49)**

And this too is necessary for us, who confess him as Lord with our lips but deny him by our works. For if I am Lord, he says, you ought by all means to do the part of servants. And the work of servants is to do what the master commands. Then he tells us what he gains who hears him, and not only hears but also does. For he is like a builder-man who built upon the rock. Now the rock is Christ, as the Apostle testifies (1 Cor. 10:4). And he digs and goes deep who does not receive the words of Scripture superficially, but searches out their depths together with the Spirit. Such a one, therefore, also founds upon the rock.

Then, when a flood comes — a persecution perhaps, or a temptation — the river will break against that house, namely the tempter, whether demon or man, and it is not able to shake it. And the tempting man might be compared to a river, and quite fittingly; for just as the rain falling from above makes the river flood, so also the Satan who fell from above makes the tempter-man swell. But the house of those who do not do the Lord's words falls, and the ruin of that house becomes great. For great are the falls of those who hear but do not do. For he who has neither heard nor done stumbles more lightly, but he who has heard yet not done sins more grievously.
