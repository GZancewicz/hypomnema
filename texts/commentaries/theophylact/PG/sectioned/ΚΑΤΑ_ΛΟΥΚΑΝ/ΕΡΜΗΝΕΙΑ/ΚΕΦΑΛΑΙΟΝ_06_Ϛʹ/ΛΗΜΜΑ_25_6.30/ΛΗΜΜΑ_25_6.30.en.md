# Luke 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 25 · Luke 6:30-35

> **And to everyone who asks you, give; and from the one who takes away what is yours, do not ask *it* back. And as you wish that men should do to you, you also do to them likewise. And if you love those who love you, what thanks is there to you? For even sinners love those who love them. And if you do good to those who do good to you, what thanks is there to you? For even sinners do the same. And if you lend to those from whom you hope to receive back, what thanks is there to you? For even sinners lend to sinners, that they may receive back the equal amount. But love your enemies, and do good, and lend, hoping for nothing in return, and your reward will be great; and you will be sons of the Most High, for he himself is kind toward the ungrateful and evil. (Luke 6:30-35)**[^1]

[^1]: This blockquote gives the full continuous reading of Luke 6:30-35, which in the source is split across six consecutive comment-less scripture fragments (Lemmata 25-30); each of the following lemmata carries only its own portion. The Greek as transmitted contains several OCR corruptions silently corrected here: in v. 30 the manuscript's «πὲ σὰ» reads for «τὰ σὰ» (ἀπὸ τοῦ αἴροντος τὰ σὰ μὴ ἀπαίτει); in v. 34 «ἀπολάβωαι» reads for «ἀπολάβωσι»; in v. 35 «Κσται» reads for «ἔσται», «ἑστιν ἑπὶ» for «ἐστιν ἐπὶ», and «ὰχαρίστους» for «ἀχαρίστους».
