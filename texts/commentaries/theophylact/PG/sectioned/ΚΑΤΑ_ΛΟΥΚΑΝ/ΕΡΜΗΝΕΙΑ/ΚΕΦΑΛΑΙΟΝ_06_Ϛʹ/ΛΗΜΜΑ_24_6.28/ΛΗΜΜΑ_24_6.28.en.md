# Luke 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 24 · Luke 6:28

> **Bless those who curse you, and pray for those who abuse you. To the one who strikes you on the cheek, offer[^1] the other also; and from the one who takes away your cloak, do not withhold your tunic either. (Luke 6:28)**

[^1]: The OCR reads πάρενε; reconstructed as πάρεχε ("offer, provide"). The blockquote runs on into the material of Luke 6:29 (the cheek and the cloak), which the fragment carries under the lemma's 6:28 heading.
