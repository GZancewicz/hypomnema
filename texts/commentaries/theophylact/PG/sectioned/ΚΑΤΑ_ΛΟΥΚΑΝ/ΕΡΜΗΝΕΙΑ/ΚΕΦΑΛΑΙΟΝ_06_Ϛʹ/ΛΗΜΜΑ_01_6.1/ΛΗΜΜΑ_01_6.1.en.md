# Luke 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 1 · Luke 6:1-4

> **Now it came to pass on the second-first Sabbath[^1] that he was passing through the grainfields. And his disciples were plucking the ears of grain, and were eating *them*, rubbing *them* with their hands. But some of the Pharisees said to them: Why do you do that which is not lawful to do on the Sabbaths? And answering them Jesus said: Have you not read even this, which David did, when he himself hungered, and those who were with him — how he entered into the house of God, and having taken the loaves of the presentation [i.e. the shewbread] he ate, and gave to those with him, which it is not lawful to eat except the priests alone? (Luke 6:1-4)**

[^1]: Greek δευτεροπρώτῳ, literally "second-first," a notoriously obscure term; Theophylact himself explains it at 6:5 (that a feast falling on the Preparation was called a Sabbath, so that the Sabbath properly so called was reckoned "second"). Rendered here literally.
