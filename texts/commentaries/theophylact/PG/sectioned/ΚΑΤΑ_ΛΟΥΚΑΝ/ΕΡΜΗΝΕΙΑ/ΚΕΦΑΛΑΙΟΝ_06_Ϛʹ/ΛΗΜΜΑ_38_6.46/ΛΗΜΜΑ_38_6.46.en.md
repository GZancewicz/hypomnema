# Luke 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 38 · Luke 6:46-48

> **But why do you call me, Lord, Lord, and do not the things which I say? Everyone who comes to me, and hears my words, and does them, I will show you to whom he is like. He is like a man building a house, who dug and went deep, and laid a foundation upon the rock. And when a flood came, the river broke against that house, and was not able to shake it; for it was founded upon the rock. (Luke 6:46-48)**
