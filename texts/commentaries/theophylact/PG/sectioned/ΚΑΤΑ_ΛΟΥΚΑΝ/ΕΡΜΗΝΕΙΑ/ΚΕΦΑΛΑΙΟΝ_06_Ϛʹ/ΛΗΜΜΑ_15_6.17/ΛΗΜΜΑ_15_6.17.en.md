# Luke 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 15 · Luke 6:17

> **having come down with them, he stood upon a level place, and a crowd of his disciples, and a great multitude of the people from all Judea and Jerusalem, and the seacoast of Tyre and Sidon, who came to hear him and to be healed from their diseases; and those troubled by unclean spirits, and they were being cured. And all the crowd sought to touch him, because power went forth from him. And he healed all. (Luke 6:17-19)**

The Lord does all things teaching us, that we too may act so. Behold therefore, being about to pray, he goes up into a mountain. For one ought to pray at leisure,[^1] and not in the midst of many, but even passing the whole night *at it*, not straightway standing up for prayer and then departing from it. And after the prayer he chooses the disciples, so that he might teach us also, whenever we are about to appoint someone to a spiritual ministry, to make this choice with prayer, being guided by God, and asking from him that the approved one be revealed to us. And having chosen the twelve, he comes down from the mountain, so that he might heal those who had come from the cities, and might doubly benefit *them*, both in soul and in body. For hear, that—

[^1]: The OCR here is corrupt, reading «Καὶ γὰρ Ἀαῖ σγολάζοντας προσεύχεσθαι»; reconstructed as «Καὶ γὰρ δεῖ σχολάζοντας προσεύχεσθαι», "for one ought, giving oneself leisure, to pray" (i.e. to pray unhurriedly and at length). Several words in the lemma itself are likewise damaged in the source (e.g. «Κστη» for ἔστη, «οἱ Φλθον» for οἳ ἦλθον, «ἰΖτο» for ἰᾶτο), reconstructed from context.
