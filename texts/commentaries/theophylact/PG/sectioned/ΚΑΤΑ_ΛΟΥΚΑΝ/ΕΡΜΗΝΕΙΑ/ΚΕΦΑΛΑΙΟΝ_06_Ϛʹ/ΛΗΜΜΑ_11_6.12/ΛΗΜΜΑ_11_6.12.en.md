# Luke 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 11 · Luke 6:12-14

> **And it came to pass in these days, he went out *into* the mountain to pray. And he was passing the whole night in the prayer of God.[^1] And when it became day, he called to himself his disciples. And having chosen from them twelve, whom he also named apostles: Simon, whom he also named Peter, and Andrew his brother, James, and John, Philip, and Bartholomew, (Luke 6:12-14)**

[^1]: τῇ προσευχῇ τοῦ Θεοῦ, literally "the prayer of God," i.e. prayer directed to God.
