# Luke 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 34 · Luke 6:41-44

> **But why do you look at the speck that is in the eye of your brother, but the beam that is in your own eye you do not perceive? Or how can you say to your brother, "Brother, allow that I cast out the speck that is in your eye," while you yourself do not see the beam that is in your eye? Hypocrite, cast out first the beam from your eye, and then you shall see clearly to cast out the speck that is in your brother's eye. For there is no good tree producing corrupt fruit, nor a corrupt tree producing good fruit. For each tree is known from its own fruit. For they do not gather figs from thorns, nor do they harvest a grape-cluster from a bramble. (Luke 6:41-44)**[^1]

[^1]: At v. 42 Theophylact's text reads αὐτὸς τὴν ἐν τῷ ὀφθαλμῷ σου δοκὸν οὐ βλέπων ("not seeing the beam that is in your eye"); the sense requires the speaker's own eye (cf. σεαυτοῦ). Rendered literally.
