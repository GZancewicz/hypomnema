# Luke 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 32 · Luke 6:37

> **Judge not, and you shall not be judged. Condemn not, and you shall not be condemned; release, and you shall be released. (Luke 6:37)**[^1]

[^1]: ἀπολύετε … ἀπολυθήσεσθε rendered "release … you shall be released"; the verb (ἀπολύω) means "set free / forgive / dismiss," and here carries the sense of remitting an offense.
