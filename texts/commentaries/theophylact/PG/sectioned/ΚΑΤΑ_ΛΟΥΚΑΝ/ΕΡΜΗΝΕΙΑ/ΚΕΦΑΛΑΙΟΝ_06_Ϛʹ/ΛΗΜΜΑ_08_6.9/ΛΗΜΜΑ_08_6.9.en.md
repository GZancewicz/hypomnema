# Luke 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 8 · Luke 6:9

> **Jesus therefore said to them: I will ask you something: is it permitted on the Sabbath to do good, or to do evil? to save a life, or to destroy *it*? And (Luke 6:9)**[^1]

[^1]: ψυχὴν σῶσαι here rendered "to save a life"; ψυχή can equally be "soul." The clause ends open with καὶ ("And"), continuing into the next verse.
