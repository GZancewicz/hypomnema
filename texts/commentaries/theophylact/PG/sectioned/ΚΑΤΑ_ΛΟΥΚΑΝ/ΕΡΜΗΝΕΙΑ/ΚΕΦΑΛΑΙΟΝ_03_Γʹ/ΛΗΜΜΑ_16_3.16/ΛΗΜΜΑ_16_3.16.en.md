# Luke 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 16 · Luke 3:16

> **John answered them all, saying: I baptize you with water; but there comes one mightier than I, the strap of whose sandals I am not worthy[^1] to loosen; he will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and with fire; (Luke 3:16)**

[^1]: Greek ἱκανός, "sufficient, competent," here "worthy." The words οὐκ εἰμὶ ἱκανός are corrupt in the OCR (οὐκ αἰαὶ / Ἀκανὸς) and reconstructed.
