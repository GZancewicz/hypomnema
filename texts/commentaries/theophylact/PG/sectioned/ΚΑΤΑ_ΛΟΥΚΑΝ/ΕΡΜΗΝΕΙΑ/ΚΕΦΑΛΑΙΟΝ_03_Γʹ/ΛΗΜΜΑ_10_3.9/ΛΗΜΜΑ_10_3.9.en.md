# Luke 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 10 · Luke 3:9

> **the axe upon the root of your trees (Luke 3:9)**

The *root*, then, is understood as life, as we said. But the *root* could also be understood as the kinship of Abraham; from which kinship of Abraham are cut off those who are not worthy to be his branches, according to the Apostle (cf. Rom. 11:17). And the punishment is double; for the sinful and fruitless man is not only cut off from kinship with the righteous, but is also cast into fire.
