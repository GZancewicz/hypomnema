# Luke 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 25 · Luke 3:22

> **Jesse, of Obed, of Boaz, of Salmon, of Nahshon, of Amminadab, of Aram, of Hezron, of Perez,[^1] of Judah, of Jacob, of Isaac, of Abraham, of Terah, of Nahor, of … (Luke 3:22)**

[^1]: OCR here reads τοῦ **ὡαρὲς**, a corruption of τοῦ **Φαρές** (Perez), the name required at this point in the ascending genealogy (between Hezron and Judah).
