# Luke 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 1 · Luke 3:2

> **Now in the fifteenth year of the governance of Tiberius[^1] Caesar, Pontius Pilate being governor of Judea, and Herod being tetrarch of Galilee, but Philip his brother being tetrarch of the region of Ituraea and Trachonitis, and Lysanias being tetrarch of Abilene, in the— (Luke 3:2)**

[^1]: The OCR reads Τιᾶρίου; the intended name is Τιβερίου, "Tiberius." The fragment breaks off mid-phrase at ἐπὶ ("in the [time of]"), which is completed in the next fragment: ἐπὶ ἀρχιερέων Ἄννα καὶ Καϊάφα ("in the [time of] the high priests Annas and Caiaphas").
