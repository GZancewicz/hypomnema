# Luke 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 14 · Luke 3:14

> **And soldiers also were questioning him, saying: And we, what shall we do? And he said to them: Intimidate[^1] no one, nor accuse falsely, and be content with your wages.[^2] (Luke 3:14)**

[^1]: Greek διασείσητε — literally "shake down," i.e. extort money by intimidation or violence; the following συκοφαντήσητε is the corresponding extortion by false accusation.
[^2]: The word is corrupt in the OCR (τοῖς ὀὸρλ / νίοις ὑμῶν); reconstructed as τοῖς ὀψωνίοις ὑμῶν, "your wages/rations."
