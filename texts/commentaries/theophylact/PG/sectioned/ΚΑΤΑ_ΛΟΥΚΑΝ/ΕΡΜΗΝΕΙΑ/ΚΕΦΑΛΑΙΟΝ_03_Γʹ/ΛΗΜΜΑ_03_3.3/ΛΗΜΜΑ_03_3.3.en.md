# Luke 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 3 · Luke 3:3

> **—he came into all the region around the Jordan, proclaiming a baptism of repentance for the remission of sins. (Luke 3:3)**

He mentions both the time and the rulers rightly, so that he might show that the succession of the Jewish rulers had failed in the time of Christ — Pilate, a man of another race, being governor, but the sons of Herod the Ascalonite being tetrarchs; and fittingly might it be believed that Christ had arrived,[^1] according to the prophecy of Jacob (Gen. 49:10).

[^1]: ἐπιστῆναι — "to have come to stand near," "to be present/at hand," used of Christ's appearance on the scene. The allusion is to Jacob's prophecy that "a ruler shall not fail from Judah… until the things laid up for him come" (Gen. 49:10 LXX): the ceasing of native Jewish rule signals that Christ has come.
