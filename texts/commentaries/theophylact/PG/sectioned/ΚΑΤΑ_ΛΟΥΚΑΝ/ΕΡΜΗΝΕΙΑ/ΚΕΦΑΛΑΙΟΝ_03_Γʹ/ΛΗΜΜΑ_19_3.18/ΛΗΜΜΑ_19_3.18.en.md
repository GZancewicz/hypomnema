# Luke 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 19 · Luke 3:18

> **And, exhorting many other things also, he preached good tidings to the people. (Luke 3:18)**

For good teaching is truly an exhortation; and it might reasonably be called good tidings[^1] as well.

[^1]: Theophylact plays on the verbs of the lemma. Its παρακαλῶν ("exhorting") is echoed by παράκλησις ("exhortation, encouragement"), and its εὐηγγελίζετο ("he preached good tidings") by εὐαγγέλιον ("gospel, good tidings"): good teaching is itself an "exhortation," and may fitly be called "gospel."
