# Luke 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 22 · Luke 3:22

> **...the Holy Spirit descended in bodily form, like a dove, upon him, and a voice came from heaven, saying: Thou art my beloved Son; in thee I am well pleased. (Luke 3:22)**

Reasonably did the evangelist here insert the things concerning Herod, all but saying this: The crowd was reasoning such things about John, but Herod, being reproved by him, added this also to all—namely, the shutting of him up in prison; as though, therefore, indignant, he recounts these things, and grieving deeply, seeing that, while the crowd held such an opinion of John, Herod displayed such deeds.

And the heaven is opened, that it might be shown to us that baptism opens to all the heaven which Adam had closed. And the Spirit comes down upon Jesus, that from this also we might learn that upon us too, when we are baptized, the Spirit comes; for the Lord surely had no need of the Spirit. But he does all things for our sake, and he himself becomes the firstfruits of all the things which we were about to receive afterward, that he might become the firstborn among many brethren. And *as* a dove, that we might learn that we must be gentle and pure; and that, just as in the case of Noah the dove made known the ceasing of God's wrath, so here also the Holy Spirit reconciled us to God, having wrought a deluge of sin.[^1] And he hears "Son" as well, the voice being borne from the Father, showing that to us too, when we are baptized, he has granted the adoption of sons.

> **[And Jesus himself was about thirty years of age when he] began,[^2] being, as was supposed, the son of Joseph, of Heli, of Matthat, of Levi, of Melchi, of Jannai, of Joseph, of Mattathias, of Amos, of Naum, of Esli[?], of Naggai, of Maath, of Mattathias, of Semein, of Josech, of Judah, of Joanan, of Rhesa, of Zerubbabel, of Salathiel, of Neri, of Melchi, of Addi, of Cosam, of Elmadam, of Er, of Jose[?], of Eliezer, of Jorim, of Matthat, of Levi, of... (Luke 3:23-29)**

[^1]: Greek κατακλυσμὸν τῆς ἁμαρτίας ποιησάμενον, literally "having made a deluge of sin." The sense is that the Spirit drowned or washed sin away as by a flood, extending the image of Noah: as the flood engulfed the world, so the Spirit engulfs and does away with sin.
[^2]: The OCR is corrupt at the transition into the genealogy: the manuscript reads «Ἐν σοὶ δὲ ... χόμενος ὢν, ὡς ἐνομίζετο, υἱὸς Ἰωσὴφ». The stray «Ἐν σοὶ δὲ» appears to be a dittography of the preceding «ἐν σοὶ [ηὐδόκησα]», and «-χόμενος» is the tail of ἀρχόμενος. The underlying text is Luke 3:23, «Καὶ αὐτὸς ἦν ὁ Ἰησοῦς ὡσεὶ ἐτῶν τριάκοντα ἀρχόμενος, ὢν ὡς ἐνομίζετο υἱὸς Ἰωσήφ...», reconstructed in brackets above. Several of the following names are also garbled in the OCR (e.g. «Ἐαλλ» for Ἐσλί = Esli, «Ἰησώ» for Ἰησοῦ = Jose); doubtful readings are flagged with [?].
