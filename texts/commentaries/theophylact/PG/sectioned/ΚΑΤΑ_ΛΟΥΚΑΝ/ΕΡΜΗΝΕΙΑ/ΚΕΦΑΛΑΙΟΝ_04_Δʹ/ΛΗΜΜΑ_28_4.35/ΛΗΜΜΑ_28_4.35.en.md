# Luke 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 28 · Luke 4:35

> **Jesus rebuked him, saying: Be silenced[^1] and come out of him; and the demon, having thrown him into the midst, came out from him, having harmed him in nothing. And (Luke 4:35)**

[^1]: Greek Φιμώθητι, aorist passive imperative of φιμόω, literally "be muzzled." Rendered "Be silenced" throughout; the exposition (Lemma 30) turns on this command.
