# Luke 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 27 · Luke 4:34

> **saying: Ah![^1] what to us and to thee, Jesus of Nazareth? Hast thou come to destroy us? I know thee, who thou art: the holy one of God. And (Luke 4:34)**

[^1]: Greek Ἔα, an interjection of surprise or displeasure; it may also be read as an imperative ("Let *us* be!," from ἐάω). Rendered here as the exclamation "Ah!"
