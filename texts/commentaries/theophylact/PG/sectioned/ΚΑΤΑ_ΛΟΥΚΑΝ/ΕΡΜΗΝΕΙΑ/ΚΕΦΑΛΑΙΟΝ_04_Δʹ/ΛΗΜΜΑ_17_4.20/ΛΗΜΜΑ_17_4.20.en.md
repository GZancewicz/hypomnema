# Luke 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 17 · Luke 4:20

> **"Now is the well-acceptable time; now is the day of salvation." (2 Cor. 6:2)**[^1]

And having read these things, he said, "Today this scripture is fulfilled in your ears," plainly presenting himself to those who heard that he is the one concerning whom these things have been written.

[^1]: The scripture marked in the manuscript at this point is not a Lukan verse but Paul's words (2 Cor. 6:2), which complete the sentence carried over from the preceding fragment ("...concerning which Paul says"). The Greek here quotes «Νῦν καιρὸς εὐπρόσδεκτος· νῦν ἡμέρα σωτηρίας.»
