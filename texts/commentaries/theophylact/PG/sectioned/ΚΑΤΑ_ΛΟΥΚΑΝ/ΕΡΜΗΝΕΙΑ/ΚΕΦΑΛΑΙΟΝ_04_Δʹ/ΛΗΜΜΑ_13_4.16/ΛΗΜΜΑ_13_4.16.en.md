# Luke 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 13 · Luke 4:16-19

> **He came into Nazareth, where he had been brought up, and he entered, according to his custom, on the day of the Sabbaths, into the synagogue, and he stood up to read, and there was handed to him a book of Isaiah the prophet; and having unrolled the book, he found the place where it was written: The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, for the sake of which he anointed me; to preach good news to the poor he has sent me; to heal those crushed in heart; to proclaim release to captives, and to the blind recovery of sight; to send forth the shattered in release; to proclaim the acceptable year of the Lord. (Luke 4:16-19)**[^1]

[^1]: The clause «ἀποστεῖλαι τεθραυσμένους ἐν ἀφέσει» is difficult: τεθραυσμένους means "the shattered / crushed / oppressed" (perfect passive participle of θραύω), and the phrase is rendered here literally "to send forth the shattered in release" (i.e. to set the oppressed at liberty). The Greek of the lemma shows some OCR corruption tidied silently: line 3 «ἐν τῇ ἡμέρα σῶν Σαββάτων» read as «ἐν τῇ ἡμέρᾳ τῶν Σαββάτων»; «ἐἀναπτύξας» as «ἀναπτύξας»; «καρΑίαν» as «καρδίαν».
