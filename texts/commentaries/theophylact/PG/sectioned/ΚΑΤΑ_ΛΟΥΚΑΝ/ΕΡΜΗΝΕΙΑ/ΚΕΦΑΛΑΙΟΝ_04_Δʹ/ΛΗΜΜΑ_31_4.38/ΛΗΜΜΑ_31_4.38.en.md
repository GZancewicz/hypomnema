# Luke 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 31 · Luke 4:38-42

> **And having risen from the synagogue, he entered into the house of Simon. But Simon's mother-in-law was gripped with a great fever; and they asked him concerning her; and standing over her, he rebuked the fever, and it left her; and immediately, having risen, she ministered to them. And when the sun was setting, all who had any that were sick with various diseases brought them to him; and he, laying his hands on each one of them, healed them. And demons also were going out from many, crying out and saying, "You are the Christ, the Son of God." And rebuking *them*, he did not permit them to speak, because they knew him to be the Christ. And when day came, having gone out he proceeded into a desert place; and the crowds were seeking him, and came up to him, and were detaining him that he might not depart from them. But he said to them, "To the other cities also I must proclaim the good news of the kingdom of God, for to this I was sent." (Luke 4:38-42)**
