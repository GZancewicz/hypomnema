# Luke 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 21 · Luke 4:27

> **many lepers were in the time of Elisha the prophet[^1] in Israel, and none of them was cleansed except Naaman[^2] the Syrian. And (Luke 4:27)**

[^1]: The OCR reads τοῦ προσήτὸυ; read τοῦ προφήτου, "the prophet."
[^2]: The OCR reads Γεεμὰν; the name intended is Ναιμάν/Νεεμάν, Naaman the Syrian (cf. 2 Kings 5).
