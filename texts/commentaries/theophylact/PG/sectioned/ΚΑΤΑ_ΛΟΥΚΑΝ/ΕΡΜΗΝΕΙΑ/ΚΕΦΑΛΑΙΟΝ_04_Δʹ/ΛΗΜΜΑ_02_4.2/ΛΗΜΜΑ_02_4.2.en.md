# Luke 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 2 · Luke 4:2

> **forty days being tempted by the devil; and he ate nothing in those days; and when they were completed, afterward he hungered. And the devil said to him: If you are the Son of God, tell this stone to become bread. And Jesus answered him, saying: It is written, that not[^1] by bread alone shall man live, but by every word of God. (Luke 4:2)**

[^1]: The OCR here reads «ὅτι σέ» ἐπ᾿ ἄρτῳ μόνῳ ζήσεται ἄνθρωπος», where «σέ»» is a corruption of the negative «οὐκ» required by the sense and the received text (Luke 4:4): «οὐκ ἐπ᾿ ἄρτῳ μόνῳ ζήσεται ὁ ἄνθρωπος». Translated accordingly as "not by bread alone."
