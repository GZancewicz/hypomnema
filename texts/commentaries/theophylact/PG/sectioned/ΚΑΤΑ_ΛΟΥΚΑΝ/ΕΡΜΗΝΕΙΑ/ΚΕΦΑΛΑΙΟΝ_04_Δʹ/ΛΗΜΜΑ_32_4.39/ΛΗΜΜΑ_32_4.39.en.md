# Luke 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 32 · Luke 4:39

> **And standing over her, he rebuked the fever, and it left her; and immediately, having risen, she ministered to them. And when the sun was setting, all who had any that were sick with various diseases brought them to him; and he, laying his hands on each one of them, healed them. (Luke 4:39)**[^1]

[^1]: The OCR of this fragment is badly corrupted at the transition into v. 40, reading «Αύνοντος Σὲ τοῦ ἡλίοι, φὲὸτες Χαοι εἶκον ἀσθενοῦντας νόσοις ποικίλαις». Reconstructed as «Δύνοντος δὲ τοῦ ἡλίου, πάντες ὅσοι εἶχον ἀσθενοῦντας νόσοις ποικίλαις» ("And when the sun was setting, all who had any that were sick with various diseases…"), the standard reading of Luke 4:40.
