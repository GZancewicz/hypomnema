# Luke 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 22 · Luke 1:24–28

> **And in the sixth month the angel Gabriel was sent by God to a city of Galilee, whose name was Nazareth, to a virgin betrothed to a man whose name was Joseph, of the house of David. And the name of the virgin was Mary. And the angel, having come in to her, said: Hail, favored one,[^1] the Lord is with you, blessed are you among women. But she, having seen *him*, was troubled at his word, and was pondering what manner of greeting this might be. And (Luke 1:24–28)**

[^1]: κεχαριτωμένη — a perfect passive participle of χαριτόω, "to have been graced/favored." No single English word captures both "endowed with grace" and "favored"; the traditional Latin *gratia plena* ("full of grace") renders the former, "favored one" the latter.
