# Luke 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 8 · Luke 1:11

> **And there appeared to him an angel of the Lord, standing at the right of the altar of the incense. (Luke 1:11)**

The angel does not appear to all, but to those pure in heart, such as Zacharias was. And he said "the altar of the incense," since there was also another altar, that of the whole burnt offerings.
