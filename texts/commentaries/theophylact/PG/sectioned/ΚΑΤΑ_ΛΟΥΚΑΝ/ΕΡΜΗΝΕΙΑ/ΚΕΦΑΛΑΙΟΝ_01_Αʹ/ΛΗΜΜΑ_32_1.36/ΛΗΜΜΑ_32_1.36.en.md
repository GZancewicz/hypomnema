# Luke 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 32 · Luke 1:36

> **Behold the handmaid of the Lord; let it be to me according to thy word. (Luke 1:36)**

But learn that Gabriel is interpreted "man of God";[^1] Mariam, "lady"; Nazareth, "sanctification." Fittingly therefore, when God was about to become man, Gabriel is sent, who is interpreted "man of God"; but the greeting also takes place in a holy place, that is, in Nazareth; for where God is, nothing is unhallowed.

[^1]: «ἄνθρωπος Θεός» — literally "man God," which may be taken either as "man of God" or, in the direction Theophylact intends, "a man who is God," foreshadowing the Incarnation. The scripture lemma «Ἰδοὺ ἡ δούλη Κυρίου, γένοιτό μοι κατὰ τὸ ῥῆμά σου» is Luke 1:38 in the received numbering, though the section is labelled 1:36. The OCR of the lemma is corrupt («δαύλη Κυοίου χένοιτό»); read «δούλη Κυρίου, γένοιτό».
