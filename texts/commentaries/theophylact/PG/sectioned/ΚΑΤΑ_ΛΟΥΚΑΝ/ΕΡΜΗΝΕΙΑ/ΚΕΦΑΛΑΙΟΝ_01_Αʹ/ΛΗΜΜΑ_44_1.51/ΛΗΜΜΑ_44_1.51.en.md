# Luke 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 44 · Luke 1:51-55

> **He has wrought might with his arm; he has scattered the proud in the thought of their heart; he has brought down potentates from thrones, and exalted the lowly; the hungry he has filled with good things, and the rich he has sent away empty; he has taken hold of Israel his servant,[^1] to remember mercy, even as he spoke[^2] to our fathers, to Abraham and to his seed, unto the age. (Luke 1:51-55)**

[^1]: παιδός — παῖς here can mean either "servant" or "child"; rendered "servant."
[^2]: OCR corrupt: the manuscript reads «ἐλάληος», restored to ἐλάλησεν (he spoke).
