> **But for Elizabeth the time was fulfilled that she should give birth, and she bore a son. And her neighbors and her kinsfolk heard that the Lord had magnified His mercy with her, and they rejoiced with her. And it came to pass on the eighth day, they came to circumcise the child, and they were calling it by the name of its father, Zachariah. And his mother answering said, "Not so; but he shall be called John." And they said to her that, "There is no one in thy kindred who is called by this name." (Luke 1:57-61)**
