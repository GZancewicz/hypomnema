# Luke 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 7 · Luke 1:10

> **the multitude of the people was praying outside at the hour of the incense. (Luke 1:10)**

Before God only the pure exercise the priesthood; but from those who are not pure God turns away his face. But when did the lot to burn incense fall to him?[^1] Assuredly at the time of the atonement, when the high priest alone entered into the Holy of Holies; that we might learn that, just as this high priest, having entered into the Holy of Holies, received fruit, so also the Lord Jesus, the only and in truth great high priest, having entered into the Holy of Holies — that is, into heaven with flesh — received the fruit of his incarnate sojourn, namely our adoption and salvation.

[^1]: Greek Πότε δὲ ἔλαχε τοῦ θυμιάσαι; taking up the ἔλαχε τοῦ θυμιᾶσαι ("it fell to him by lot to burn incense") of Luke 1:9.
