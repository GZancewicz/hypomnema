# Luke 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 56 · Luke 1:63

> **Having asked for a writing tablet, he wrote, saying: John is his name. And they all marveled. And immediately his mouth was opened, and his tongue, and he was speaking, blessing God. (Luke 1:63-64)**

The Virgin remained about three months with Elizabeth, perhaps astonished at the marvel, and needing some consolation, which she was going to have from being together with Elizabeth; but when the birth drew near, she withdrew. And when the time of bearing was fulfilled for Elizabeth — it is to be observed that in the case of sinners it is not said, "The time of bearing was fulfilled," but only where the one being born is righteous, there it is said; for, as it seems, the births of sinners are incomplete and unfulfilled, and it were better for them if they had not been born.

But why was the name given after the circumcision? Because one must first receive the seal from God, and then the human name; and besides, circumcision signifies this — the casting off of the fleshly things. No one, therefore,[^1] unless he first cast away and cut off the fleshly things, is worthy to be named a soldier of God, and to have his name enrolled in the book of the heavens. And Elizabeth, as a prophetess, spoke concerning the name, that John is his name; and perhaps John himself also set the name upon himself, for he it was who imparted to his mother the prophetic gift. And Zacharias, not being able to indicate the name by a sign, asks for a writing tablet; and since he agreed with his wife concerning the name of the child, all are astonished; for it was not *a name* in their kindred, so that someone might be able to say that both had premeditated this beforehand. And John is interpreted "grace of God"; wherefore also his father, immediately being graced, prophesies concerning Christ first, and concerning the child afterward.

[^1]: The OCR here reads «λὼἄεὶς οὖν»; I take it as «Οὐδεὶς οὖν» ("No one, therefore"), which the following construction (εἰ μὴ … ἄξιός ἐστιν, "unless … is worthy") requires.
