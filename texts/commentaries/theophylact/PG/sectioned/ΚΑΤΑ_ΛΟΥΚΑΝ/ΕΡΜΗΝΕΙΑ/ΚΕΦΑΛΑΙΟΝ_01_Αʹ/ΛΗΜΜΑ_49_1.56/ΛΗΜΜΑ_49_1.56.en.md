# Luke 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 49 · Luke 1:56

> **But Mary remained with her about three months. And she returned to her house. (Luke 1:56)**

The arm of the Father is the Son. Therefore God and Father wrought might and power against nature[^1] in His Son. For when the Son was made flesh, nature was overcome,[^2] a virgin having given birth, God having been made man, man having been deified; and the Lord scattered the proud demons, casting them out from human souls; and some He sent into the abyss, but others into the swine. But it can also be understood concerning the Jews, whom He scattered into every land, and now they are dispersed. And He pulled down[^3] rulers from thrones — I mean the demons, who tyrannized over men and had human souls as thrones, resting in them; but the Pharisees also are rulers, in that they plundered the goods of the poor, and had thrones as teachers, from which they were pulled down; and He exalted lowly men, whom sin had humbled, or the nations; for He exalted these, having graciously granted them adoption as sons. As therefore also He filled the same ones hungering — I mean those from the nations, for they had neither Scriptures, nor law, nor commandments — He filled them with the good things of the Scriptures, but the Jews, who were rich in both law and commandments, He sent away outside Jerusalem, both the upper and the lower, empty of every good thing; for the Jews now have nothing, even if they seem to have. And He took hold of Israel His servant — either he speaks concerning the perceptible Israelites. For many myriads even from these believed, and the promise to Abraham was fulfilled, God having said to him that, "In thy seed shall all the tribes of the earth be blessed" (Gen. 22:18). Or he also speaks concerning the intelligible Israel; for everyone who sees God is called Israel, for the name, thus interpreted, means "Seeing God." Therefore He took hold of these who see God, having led them up into the heavenly inheritance.

But Mary remained with Elizabeth about three months, then she returned. For since Elizabeth was about to give birth, the Virgin withdraws because of the multitude of those about to run together for the birth; for it is unseemly for a virgin to be present among such things. And that the virgin returned when Elizabeth was about to give birth is clear from this: in the sixth month of the conception of the Forerunner the angel came to Mary; and Mary remained with Elizabeth about three months. Behold, nearly nine.

[^1]: κατὰ τῆς φύσεως, literally "against nature": the might God wrought in the Incarnation overturns the natural order (a virgin bearing, God becoming man, man being deified). "Against" here carries the sense of "over against / contrary to" the ordinary course of nature.
[^2]: The OCR reads ἐνηκήθη; read ἐνικήθη, "was overcome / was conquered."
[^3]: The OCR reads ναθελε; read καθεῖλε, "he pulled down / deposed" (echoing καθεῖλε δυνάστας ἀπὸ θρόνων of the Magnificat, Luke 1:52).
