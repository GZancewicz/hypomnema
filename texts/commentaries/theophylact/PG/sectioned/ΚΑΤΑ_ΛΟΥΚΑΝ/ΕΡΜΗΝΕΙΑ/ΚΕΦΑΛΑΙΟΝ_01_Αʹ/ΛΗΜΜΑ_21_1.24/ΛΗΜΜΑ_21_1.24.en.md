# Luke 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 21 · Luke 1:24

> **And after these days Elizabeth his wife conceived, and hid herself five months, saying that, Thus has the Lord done for me in the days in which he looked upon me to take away my reproach among men. (Luke 1:24)**

Being chaste, Elizabeth felt shame, and for this reason she hid herself, on account of having conceived in old age; and for five months she hid herself, until Mary too should conceive. But from the time when she too conceived, and the babe leaped in her womb, she no longer hid herself, but would even speak with boldness,[^1] as the mother of such a child — one honored with prophetic dignity even before he was born.

[^1]: The Greek reads ἐπαῤῥησιάσατο ἄν, an aorist with the modal particle ἄν, giving a potential force: "she would (by then) speak openly/with boldness."
