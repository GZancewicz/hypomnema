# Luke 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 10 · Luke 1:14

> **he shall be joy to you and gladness, and many shall rejoice at his birth. (Luke 1:14)**

Zacharias is troubled. For an unaccustomed sight troubles even the saints. But the angel calms the trouble; for everywhere this was a mark for distinguishing the visions that are from God and the demonic ones: for if the reasoning begins from trouble and settles down at the last, the fear being quickly dissolved, the vision is truly divine; but if the fear and the trouble rather intensify, the vision is demonic.
