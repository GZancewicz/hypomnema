# Luke 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 60 · Luke 1:69

> **And he raised up a horn of salvation. (Luke 1:69)**

That is, a saving power and kingdom; for the horn signifies either power — for horned animals have their power in their horns — or kingship, for by the horn the kings were anointed. Christ, therefore, is both the power and the kingdom of the Father; therefore Christ was raised up for us as a horn of salvation. For he seemed to be sleeping, overlooking the many sins, and being long-suffering toward the madness for idols; but having been made flesh in the last times, he was roused up and crushed all the demons that hate us, no longer enduring to sleep and to be long-suffering. And he was raised up in the house of David, that is, in Bethlehem, for there he was born; and Bethlehem is assuredly a city of David, even as the prophets spoke.
