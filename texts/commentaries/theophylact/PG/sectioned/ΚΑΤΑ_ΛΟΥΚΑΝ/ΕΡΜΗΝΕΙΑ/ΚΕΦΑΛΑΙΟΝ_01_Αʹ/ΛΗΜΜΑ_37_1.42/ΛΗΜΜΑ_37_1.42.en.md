# Luke 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 37 · Luke 1:42

> **Blessed are you among women. (Luke 1:42)**

Then, since many women have borne certain profane *offspring*, as Rebecca bore Esau, she says that: But the fruit of your womb also is blessed. And it will also be understood in another way: Blessed are you among women; then, as though someone had asked, Why? she states the cause: For blessed is the fruit of your womb — that is, For God is the fruit of your womb, for God alone is blessed; as David also says: Blessed is he that comes (Ps. 118:26). For Scripture is accustomed to take the conjunction "and" in place of "for"; as in, Give us help, and vain is the salvation of man (Ps. 60:11), in place of, For vain is the salvation of man; and again, You were angry, and we sinned (Isa. 64:5), in place of, For we sinned. And she called the Lord the fruit of the womb of the Theotokos [i.e. the God-bearer], because the conception was without a man; for the other children are fruits of their fathers, but Christ is the fruit of the womb of the Theotokos alone, for she alone bore him in the womb.
