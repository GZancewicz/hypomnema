# Luke 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 31 · Luke 1:36

> **Behold the handmaid of the Lord; let it be to me according to thy word. (Luke 1:36)**

I am a painter's tablet: what the painter wishes, let him paint; what he wills, let the Lord do. So that also what she said before, "How shall this be to me?", was not of unbelief, but of a desire to learn the manner. For if she disbelieved, she would not have said—[^1]

[^1]: The fragment breaks off here mid-sentence; the Greek ends «εἰ γὰρ ἠπίστει, οὐκ ἂν εἶπαν,» (read «εἶπεν», "she would not have said"). The scripture lemma «Ἰδοὺ ἡ δούλη Κυρίου, γένοιτό μοι κατὰ τὸ ῥῆμά σου» is Luke 1:38 in the received numbering, though the section is labelled 1:36.
