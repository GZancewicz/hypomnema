# Luke 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 33 · Luke 1:39-41

> **And Mary, having risen up in these days, went into the hill country with haste, into a city of Judah; and entered into the house of Zacharias, and greeted Elizabeth. And it came to pass, as Elizabeth heard the greeting of Mary, the babe leaped in her womb; and Elizabeth was filled with the Holy Spirit, and (Luke 1:39-41)**[^1]

[^1]: The scripture of this and the two following fragments (Lemmata 33-35) runs on continuously without exposition; it is given here as one reading. The opening «Ἀναστᾶσα δὲ Μαριὰμ» is Luke 1:39, though the section is labelled 1:36. OCR of the opening is corrupt («Ἀνκστᾶσ ἄὲ Μαρίὰκ»); read «Ἀναστᾶσα δὲ Μαριάμ».
