# Luke 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 4 · Luke 1:7

> **And they had no child, inasmuch as Elisabeth was barren; and both were advanced in their days. (Luke 1:7)**

The wives of the righteous, and righteous women themselves, are often childless — that you may learn that the law does not require bodily fruitfulness in children, but spiritual. And both were advanced, both according to the body and according to the spirit; for indeed they also advanced according to the soul, that is, they made progress, setting ascents in their heart (cf. Ps. 83:6 LXX), and having day, not night, for their life, as walking becomingly in the light.
