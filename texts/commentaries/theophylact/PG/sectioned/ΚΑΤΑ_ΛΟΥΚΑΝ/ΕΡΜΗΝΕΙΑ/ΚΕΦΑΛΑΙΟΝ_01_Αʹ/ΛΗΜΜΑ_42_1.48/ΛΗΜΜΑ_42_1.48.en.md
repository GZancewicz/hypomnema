# Luke 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 42 · Luke 1:48

> **he looked upon the lowliness of his handmaiden; for behold, from now on all generations will call me blessed, because the Mighty One has done great things for me; and holy is his name. And his mercy is unto generation and generation to those who fear him. (Luke 1:48-50)**

The Virgin, being more fully assured, glorifies God, ascribing the wonder to him, not to herself. For it was he, she says, who looked upon me the lowly one, not I who looked up to him; it was he who had mercy on me, not I who sought him out; and from now on all generations will call me blessed — not Elizabeth alone, but also the generations of those who have believed. And why will they call me blessed? Is it, then, because of my own virtue? No; but because…[^1]

[^1]: The exposition breaks off here in the Greek («Οὐχί· ἀλλ' ὅτι»); the sentence is completed in the following lemma.
