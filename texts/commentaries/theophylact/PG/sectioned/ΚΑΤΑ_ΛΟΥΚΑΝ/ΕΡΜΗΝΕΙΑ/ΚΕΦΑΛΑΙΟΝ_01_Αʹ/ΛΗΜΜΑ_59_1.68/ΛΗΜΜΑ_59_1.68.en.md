# Luke 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 59 · Luke 1:68

> **Blessed be the Lord, the God of Israel, because he has visited and has wrought redemption for his people; and has raised up a horn of salvation for us in the house of David his servant; even as he spoke through the mouth of his holy prophets who have been from of old — salvation from our enemies, and from the hand of all who hate us; to work mercy with our fathers, and to remember his holy covenant, the oath which he swore to Abraham our father, to grant to us that, delivered without fear from the hand of our enemies, we should serve him in holiness and righteousness before him all the days of our life. (Luke 1:68-75)**

Fear came upon all, because of the marvel of Zacharias' utterance. For just as the people were astonished at the silence, so also at his speaking; so that through these two wonders all might reason concerning the one born, that he is above the many. And all these things came to pass by [providential] dispensation, so that he who was going to bear witness concerning Christ might be trustworthy, and that all might be fully assured, from the very birth of John, that he is above the many.

And Zacharias blesses God, as having visited the Israelites; for in truth he came to the lost sheep of the house of Israel, but the greater part of them did not wish to receive the grace; wherefore he also visited the true Israelites, who are those who believed.
