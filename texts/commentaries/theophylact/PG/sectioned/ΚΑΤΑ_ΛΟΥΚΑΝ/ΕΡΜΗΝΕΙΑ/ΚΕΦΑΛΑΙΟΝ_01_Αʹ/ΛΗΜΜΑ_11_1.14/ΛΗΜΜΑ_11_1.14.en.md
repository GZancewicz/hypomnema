# Luke 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 11 · Luke 1:14

> **Your prayer was heard, and your wife Elisabeth shall bear you *a son*? (Luke 1:13)**[^1]

For surely Zacharias was not praying for a child, but for the sins of the people? Some, then, say that, since he was praying on behalf of the sins of the people, and was about to beget a son who cries, "Behold the Lamb of God, who takes away the sin of the world," the angel fittingly says to him that "Your prayer was heard on behalf of the remission of the sins of the people. For you shall beget a son through whom there shall be remission of sins." But some understand it thus, that: "Your prayer was heard, O Zacharias, and God has pardoned the sins for the people"; then, as if he had said, "Whence is this evident?" the angel says, "This I give you as a sign, that Elisabeth shall bear you a son; so that from Elisabeth's bearing, it shall also be made credible to you that the sins are remitted for the people."

[^1]: Theophylact here re-quotes the angel's words for comment; the phrasing (Εἰσηκούσθη ἡ δέησίς σου... ἡ γυνή σου Ἐλισάβετ γεννήσει σοι) belongs to Luke 1:13, abbreviated. The object υἱόν ("a son") is not present in his abbreviated quotation but is supplied from the fuller verse.
