# Luke 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 39 · Luke 1:45

> **Blessed is she who believed, for there will be a fulfillment of the things spoken to her from the Lord. (Luke 1:45)**

Just as later, when Christ came to be baptized, John hindered him out of reverence, saying, I am not worthy (cf. Matt. 3:14), so also now he speaks through his mother: Whence is this to me, that the Mother of my Lord should come to me? — calling her who was carrying him in the womb "mother" before she had borne the Lord. For in the case of the others, before they have given birth, one ought not to call them mothers, because of the fear of miscarriage, that is, of loss. But in the case of the Virgin no such thing was suspected. Therefore even before giving birth you are both mother and blessed, O [Virgin, for there will be a fulfillment of the things]*[^1] spoken to you from the Lord.

[^1]: The end of the sentence is damaged in the OCR: the manuscript reads «μακαρία ὑπάρχεις σύ, ὦ … λημένοις σοι παρὰ Κυρίου», with a gap between «ὦ» and «λημένοις». The reconstruction supplies «[ὅτι ἔσται τελείωσις τοῖς λελα]λημένοις σοι παρὰ Κυρίου», echoing the lemma of Luke 1:45; the address «ὦ [Παρθένε]» ("O Virgin") is conjectural.
