# Luke 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 4 · Luke 2:4

> **And Joseph also went up from Galilee, out of the city of Nazareth,[^1] into Judea, into the city of David, which is called Bethlehem, because he was of the house and lineage of David,[^2] (Luke 2:4)**

[^1]: The OCR splits and garbles the place-name as «Ναζαρὲ τ»; read «Ναζαρέτ», Nazareth.
[^2]: The name of David appears twice, both corrupted in the OCR («Δαυΐθ» and «Δσυῖδ»); both read «Δαυίδ».
