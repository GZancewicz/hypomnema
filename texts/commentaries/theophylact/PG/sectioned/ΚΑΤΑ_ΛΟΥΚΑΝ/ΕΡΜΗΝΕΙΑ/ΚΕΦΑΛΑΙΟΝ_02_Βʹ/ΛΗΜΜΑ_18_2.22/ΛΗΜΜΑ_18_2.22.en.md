# Luke 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 18 · Luke 2:22

> **And the days of their purification were fulfilled according to the law of Moses. (Luke 2:22)**

Well did he say *according to the law of Moses*; for in truth the Virgin had no necessity to wait for the days of purification, which were forty for a male child born. For it is said in the law: *A woman who, if she conceive seed and bear a male...*; but the Virgin did not conceive seed, but bore of the Holy Spirit; so that she had no necessity, but, wishing to fulfill the law, she went up into the temple.

And why for a male seven days, but for a female twice as many? *For a woman*, it says, *if she conceive seed and bear a male, shall be unclean seven days; but if a female, twice seven.* Because she who bears the male brought a second Adam into the world; but she who bears the female bore a second Eve — the rotten vessel, the weak jar, the shattered earthen crock, the staff of deceit, the hand-leader of disobedience.

And whereas the law says, *Everything that opens the womb is holy to the Lord*, this came out, properly speaking, to Christ alone; for he himself opened the womb of the Virgin, since in the case of the other wombs it is the man that opens the womb. And the law commanded to offer a pair of turtledoves, showing that childbearing is from a chaste union; for they say that the turtledove is a chaste creature, so that even when it has lost its mate it does not marry a second time; and if they did not have turtledoves, they would offer two young pigeons, so that the life of that child might be marked by fruitfulness — for the pigeon is a prolific creature.
