# Luke 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 8 · Luke 2:7

"Be not slaves of men" (1 Cor. 7:23); but even if slaves in body, *they are* free in spirit, not being led away together with the impiety of their masters.

And he named the Lord the firstborn Son of the Virgin, although no second one was born, and *this* with reason; for he is called firstborn who is born first, even if no second was born after him. And he is laid down in a manger, perhaps teaching us frugality from the very beginning; and perhaps also showing symbolically that he sojourned in this world, which is a place belonging to us who have been made like to the irrational beasts. For just as the manger is the proper place of the irrational animals, so also is this world of us. The manger, therefore, is the world; we, the irrational beasts; and that he might redeem us from irrationality, for this reason he came here.
