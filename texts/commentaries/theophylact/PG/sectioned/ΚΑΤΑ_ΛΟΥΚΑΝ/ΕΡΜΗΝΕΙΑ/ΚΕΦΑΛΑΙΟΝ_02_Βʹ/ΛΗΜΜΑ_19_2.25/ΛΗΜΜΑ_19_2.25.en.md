# Luke 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 19 · Luke 2:25-30

> **And behold, there was a man in Jerusalem, whose name was Simeon;[^1] and this man was righteous and devout, awaiting the consolation of Israel; and the Holy Spirit was upon him, and it had been revealed to him by the Holy Spirit that he should not see death before he should see the Christ of the Lord. And he came in the Spirit into the temple; and when the parents brought in the child Jesus, that they might do concerning him according to what was customary of the law, he too received it into his arms, and blessed God, and said: Now Thou lettest Thy servant depart, Master, according to Thy word, in peace; for mine eyes have seen Thy salvation, which... (Luke 2:25-30)**

[^1]: The OCR here is corrupt: the manuscript reads «ὡ ἤνοιὰα ἀυμεών» and «Πνεῦμτ», reconstructed as «ᾧ ὄνομα Συμεών» ("whose name was Simeon") and «Πνεῦμα» ("Spirit").
