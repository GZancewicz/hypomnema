# Luke 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 10 · Luke 2:9

> **an angel of the Lord stood over them, and the glory of the Lord shone round about them; and they feared a great fear. And the angel said to them: Fear not; for behold, I bring you good tidings [?…] there was born to you this day a Saviour, who is Christ *the* Lord, in the city of David. And— (Luke 2:9)**[^1]

[^1]: OCR gap after εὐαγγελίζομαι ὑμῖν; see the note on the preceding fragment (Lemma 9). The stray «χθη» is the tail of ἐτέχθη ("there was born").
