# Luke 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 37 · Luke 2:44-2:49

> **But supposing him to be in the company, they went a day's journey; and they were seeking him among the kinsfolk and among the acquaintances; and not having found *him*, they returned to Jerusalem seeking him, and it came to pass, after three days they found him in the temple, sitting in the midst of the teachers, both hearing them and questioning them. And all who heard him were amazed at his understanding and his answers; and when they saw him, they were astonished; and his mother said to him: Child, why hast thou done thus to us? Behold, thy father and I, sorrowing, were seeking thee. And he said to them: Why is it that ye were seeking me? Knew ye not that I must be in the things of my Father? (Luke 2:44-2:49)**
