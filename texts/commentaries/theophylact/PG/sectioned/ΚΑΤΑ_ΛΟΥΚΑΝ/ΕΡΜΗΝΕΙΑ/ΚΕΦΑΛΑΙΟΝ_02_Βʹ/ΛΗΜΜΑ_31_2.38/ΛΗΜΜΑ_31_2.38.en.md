# Luke 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 31 · Luke 2:38–39

> **having come up, she returned thanks to the Lord, and spoke concerning him to all those awaiting redemption in Jerusalem; and when they had completed all things according to the law of the Lord, they returned into Galilee, into their own city Nazareth.[^1] (Luke 2:38–39)**

[^1]: OCR: the Greek here reads «εἰς τὸν πάλιν αὐτῶν Ναξαρέτ»; read «εἰς τὴν πόλιν αὐτῶν Ναζαρέτ» ("into their own city Nazareth"), as the citation in the following comment confirms.
