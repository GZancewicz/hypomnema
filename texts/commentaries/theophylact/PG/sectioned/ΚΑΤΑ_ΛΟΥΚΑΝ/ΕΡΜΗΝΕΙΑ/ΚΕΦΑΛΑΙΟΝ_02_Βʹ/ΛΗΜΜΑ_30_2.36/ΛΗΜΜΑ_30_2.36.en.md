# Luke 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 30 · Luke 2:36

> **And there was Anna a prophetess, daughter of Phanuel,[^1] of the tribe of Asher; she was advanced in many days, having lived seven years with a husband from her virginity. And she *was* a widow of about eighty-four years, who did not depart from the temple, serving with fastings and supplications night and day. And she at that very hour... (Luke 2:36)**

[^1]: The OCR of the name is corrupt: the manuscript reads ννα for Ἄννα ("Anna") and θυγάτορ ζανουλλ for θυγάτηρ Φανουήλ ("daughter of Phanuel"); both are reconstructed from Luke 2:36.
