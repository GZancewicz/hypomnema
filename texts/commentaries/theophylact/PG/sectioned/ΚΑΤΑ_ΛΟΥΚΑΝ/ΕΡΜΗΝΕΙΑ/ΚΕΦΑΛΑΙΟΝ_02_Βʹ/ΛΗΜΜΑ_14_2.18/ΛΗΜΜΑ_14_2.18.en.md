# Luke 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 14 · Luke 2:18

> **all who heard *it* marveled concerning the things spoken to them by the shepherds. (Luke 2:18)**

These shepherds are a symbol of the spiritual shepherds, the high priests [i.e. the prelates]. The high priests must therefore guard their flock and keep watch in the field[^1] — that is, sing certain spiritual things, and teach the people — and thus they will be deemed worthy of divine sights and hearings. And Bethlehem is interpreted "house of bread"; and what else is the house of bread but the Church, in which the bread is laid up? It is therefore the task of the rational shepherds to seek out the heavenly bread; and after they have seen the bread, they ought also to proclaim it to the others; just as the shepherds too, having seen the babe, spoke it abroad to the others as well.

[^1]: «ἀγραυλεῖν», literally "to lodge in the open field," picking up the shepherds "abiding in the field" (ἀγραυλοῦντες) of Luke 2:8; here used figuratively of the pastoral vigil.
