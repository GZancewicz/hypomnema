# Luke 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 43 · Luke 2:51

> **And he went down with them, and came to Nazareth. And he was subject to them; and his mother kept all these words in her heart. And… (Luke 2:51)**[^1]

[^1]: The fragment breaks off mid-clause at «Καὶ», whose continuation («…Ἰησοῦς προέκοπτε») is carried into the next lemma. OCR «καρθίᾳ» read as «καρδίᾳ» (“heart”). «ῥήματα» here rendered “words” (it can also mean “things/sayings”).
