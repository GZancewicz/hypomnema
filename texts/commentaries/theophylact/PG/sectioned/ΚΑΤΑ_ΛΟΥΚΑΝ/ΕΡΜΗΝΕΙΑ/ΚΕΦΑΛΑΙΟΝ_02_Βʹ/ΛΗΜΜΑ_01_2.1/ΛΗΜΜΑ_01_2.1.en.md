# Luke 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 1 · Luke 2:1-6

> **And it came to pass in those days, there went out a decree from Caesar Augustus that all the inhabited world should be enrolled. This enrollment was first made while Cyrenius was governor of Syria; and all went to be enrolled, each into his own city. And Joseph also went up from Galilee, out of the city of Nazareth,[^1] into Judea, into the city of David, which is called Bethlehem, because he was of the house and lineage of David,[^2] to be enrolled together with[^3] Mariam, the woman betrothed to him, who was with child; and it came to pass, while they were there, the days were fulfilled that she should be delivered; and (Luke 2:1-6)**

[^1]: The OCR splits and garbles the place-name as «Ναζαρὲ τ»; read «Ναζαρέτ», Nazareth.
[^2]: The name of David appears twice here, both times corrupted in the OCR («Δαυΐθ» and «Δσυῖδ»); both read «Δαυίδ».
[^3]: The OCR reads «αὺν»; read «σὺν», "together with."
