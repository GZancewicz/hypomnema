# Luke 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 34 · Luke 2:41–43

> **And his parents went every year into Jerusalem at the feast of the Passover; and when he became twelve years *old*,[^1] when they had gone up into Jerusalem according to the custom of the feast, and when they had completed the days, as they were returning, the boy Jesus remained behind in Jerusalem, and Joseph[^2] and his mother did not know it; (Luke 2:41–43)**

[^1]: OCR: the Greek reads «Ἀσῶν δώδεκα»; read «ἐτῶν δώδεκα» ("twelve years").

[^2]: OCR: the Greek reads «Ἡησοῦς ... Ἰωσὴρ»; read «Ἰησοῦς» ("Jesus") and «Ἰωσὴφ» ("Joseph").
