# Luke 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 9 · Luke 2:8-12

> **And there were shepherds in the same country, lodging in the fields and keeping the watches of the night over their flock. And behold, an angel of the Lord stood over them, and the glory of the Lord shone round about them; and they feared a great fear. And the angel said to them: Fear not; for behold, I bring you good tidings [?of great joy … for there was born] to you this day a Saviour, who is Christ *the* Lord, in the city of David. And this is the sign to you: You shall find a babe wrapped in swaddling-clothes, lying in the manger. And— (Luke 2:8-12)**[^1]

The intervening clause «χαρὰν μεγάλην, ἥτις ἔσται παντὶ τῷ λαῷ· ὅτι ἐτέ-» has dropped out; I have reconstructed the sense in brackets. A running-header fragment («ΚΑΣΟΑΡΙΤ Ιi.») intruding mid-quote has been omitted.
