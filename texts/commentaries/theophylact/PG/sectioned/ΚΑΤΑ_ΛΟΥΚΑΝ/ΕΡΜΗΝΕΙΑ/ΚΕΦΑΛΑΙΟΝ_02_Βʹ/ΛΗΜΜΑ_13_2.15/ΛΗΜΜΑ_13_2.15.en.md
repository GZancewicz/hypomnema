# Luke 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 13 · Luke 2:15

> **And it came to pass, as the angels departed from them into heaven, that the men, the shepherds, said to one another: Let us go over now as far as Bethlehem, and see this thing that has come to pass, which the Lord has made known to us. And they came, having hastened, and found both Mary and Joseph, and the babe lying in the manger; and having seen *it*, they made known concerning the saying that was spoken to them about this child; and (Luke 2:15-17)**[^1]

[^1]: Several words in this fragment are OCR-corrupt and reconstructed: «Ππωμεν» → ἴδωμεν ("let us see"); «στεύσαντες» → σπεύσαντες ("having hastened"); «παρὶ» → περὶ ("concerning"). The clause runs on into the next lemma.
