# Luke 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 16 · Luke 2:20

> **the shepherds returned, glorifying and praising God for all the things which they had heard and seen, just as it was spoken to them. (Luke 2:20)**

What sayings did the Virgin keep? Some say that they were as many things as the angel had told her, and as many as the shepherds said; for she was keeping watch over these things and comparing them in her heart — that is, she was collating them, and out of them all was finding one concordant judgment: that her Son is God. But to me it seems that here "sayings" means the deeds;[^1] so that what is said is of this sort: *Mary kept all the sayings*, that is, these deeds, which I now speak and make to be sayings; for a deed, when it is spoken, becomes a saying. And the shepherds returned giving thanks to God for all things; for they were not envious, like the Jews.

[^1]: The point turns on the double sense of «ῥῆμα»: both "word/saying" and, in Hebraic usage, "thing, matter, deed" (here «πράγματα»). A deed spoken becomes a "saying"; hence "she kept all the sayings" can mean "she kept all these matters/events."
