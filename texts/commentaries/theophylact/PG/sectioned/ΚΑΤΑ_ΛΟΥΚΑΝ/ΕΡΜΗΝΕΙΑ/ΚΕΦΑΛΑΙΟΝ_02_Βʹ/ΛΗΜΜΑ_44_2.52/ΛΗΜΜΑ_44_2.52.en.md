# Luke 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 44 · Luke 2:52

> **…Jesus advanced in wisdom and stature, and in grace with God and men. (Luke 2:52)**[^1]

He was subject to his parents, giving an example also to us, that we too might be subject to our fathers; and the Virgin kept all these things together. For the things done and the things said by the child were divine, and not those of a twelve-year-old but of a mature man.[^2] But see how here the evangelist, explaining what it is to advance in wisdom, adds "and in stature." For he says that the advance of stature is an advance of wisdom. And he was in favor both with God and with men, that is, he did things well-pleasing to God and praiseworthy to men. But first the "with God," then the "with men"; for first one must please God, then men.

[^1]: The lemma continues the quotation begun in the previous fragment («Καὶ Ἰησοῦς προέκοπτε…»). «ἡλικίᾳ» rendered “stature”; the word can also mean “age,” and Theophylact exploits the overlap when he says «τὴν προκοπὴν τῆς ἡλικίας, τῆς σοφίας εἶναι… προκοπήν» — the growth in bodily stature/age is itself a growth in wisdom.
[^2]: «ἀλλὰ ταλείου» read as «ἀλλὰ τελείου» (“but of a perfect/mature one”), contrasted with «δωδεκαετοῦς» (“a twelve-year-old”). Also read: «αἶναι» → «εἶναι»; «ἕποαπτα»/«ἔπραττα» → «ἔπραττε» (“he did/was doing”); «χὰρ» → «γὰρ».
