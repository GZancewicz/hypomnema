# Luke 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 7 · Luke 5:10

> **Likewise also James, and John the sons of Zebedee, who were partners with Simon. And Jesus said to Simon: Fear not; from now on you shall be catching men alive.[^1] (Luke 5:10)**

[^1]: Greek ἔσῃ ζωγρῶν, from ζωγρέω, "to take alive, to catch alive" (as game or fish taken living rather than killed). Rendered "catching alive" throughout to preserve the cognate play with the fishing imagery (cf. the ζωγρέω/θηρεύω language in the sequel).
