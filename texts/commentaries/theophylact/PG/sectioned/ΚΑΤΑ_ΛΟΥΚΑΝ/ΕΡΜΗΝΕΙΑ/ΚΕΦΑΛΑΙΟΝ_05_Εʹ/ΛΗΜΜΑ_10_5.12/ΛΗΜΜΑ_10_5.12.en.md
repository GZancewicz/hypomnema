# Luke 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 10 · Luke 5:12-14

> **And it came to pass, while he was in one of the cities, and behold, a man full of leprosy; and seeing Jesus, having fallen upon his face, he besought him, saying: Lord, if you will, you can cleanse me. And, having stretched out his hand, he touched him, saying, I will, be cleansed; and immediately the leprosy departed from him. And he himself charged him to tell no one; but, "having gone away, show yourself to the priest, and offer for your cleansing as Moses commanded, for a testimony to them." (Luke 5:12-14)**
