# Luke 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 26 · Luke 5:28

> **…leaving all, rising up he followed him. And Levi made him a great banquet in his house. And there was a great crowd of tax-collectors, and of others, who were reclining with them. And their[^1] scribes and the Pharisees murmured to his disciples, saying: Why do you eat and drink with tax-collectors and sinners? And Jesus answering said to them: They that are in health have no need of a physician, but they that are sick. Not… (Luke 5:28)**

[^1]: OCR — the manuscript reads «σολτῶν», restored to αὐτῶν ("their," i.e. the scribes of the tax-collectors); cf. Luke 5:30 «οἱ γραμματεῖς αὐτῶν καὶ οἱ Φαρισαῖοι».
