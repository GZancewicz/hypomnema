# Luke 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 19 · Luke 5:20-5:24

> **…*their* faith, he said to him: Man, your sins are forgiven you. And *they* began to reason, the Scribes and the Pharisees, saying: Who is this who speaks blasphemies? Who is able to forgive sins, except God alone? But Jesus, recognizing their reasonings, answering said to them: Why do you reason in your hearts? What is easier, to say, Your sins are forgiven you, or to say, Rise and walk? But that you may know that the Son of man has authority on the earth to forgive sins, he said to the paralytic: To you I say, rise; and taking up your little bed,[^1] go to your house. And… (Luke 5:20-24)**

[^1]: The Greek διminutive here reads κλινίδιον, "little bed / pallet"; the OCR of the following fragment spells it κλινίᾶιόν, evidently for κλινίδιόν σου.
