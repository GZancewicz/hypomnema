# Luke 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 9 · Luke 5:11

> **Behold, I will send forth many fishers, says the Lord; and after these things I will send forth many hunters, and they shall hunt them. (Jer. 16:16)**[^1]

*He calls* the fishers the holy apostles; and the hunters, the leaders and teachers of the Churches in the successive ages.

[^1]: The quotation belongs to the exposition carried over from the previous fragment (the "prophet" who "said," Luke 5:11); the words are Jeremiah 16:16, cited in a form close to the LXX (Ἰδοὺ ἐγὼ ἀποστελῶ ἀλιεῖς πολλούς … καὶ … πολλοὺς θηρευτάς).
