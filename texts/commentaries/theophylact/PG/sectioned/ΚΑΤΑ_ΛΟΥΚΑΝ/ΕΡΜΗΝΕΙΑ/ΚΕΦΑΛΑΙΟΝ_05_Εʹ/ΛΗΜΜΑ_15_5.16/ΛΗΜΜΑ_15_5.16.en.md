# Luke 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 15 · Luke 5:16

> **Go, show yourself to the priest, and offer the gift. (Luke 5:16)**

But what was the gift? Two small birds. And what is the *for a testimony to them*? That is, for a reproof of them and an accusation, that when they accuse me as one transgressing the law, from this they may be refuted that I do not transgress — from my having commanded you to offer the gift which Moses commanded. But how those two birds were offered to God, it is not untimely to tell. The one bird was slain, and they received its blood in a new earthen vessel; then both the wings of the other bird were dipped in the blood, and thus the bird was released alive. These things, therefore, imaged the things concerning Christ; for the two birds are the two natures of Christ, both the divine and the human, of which the one was slain, that is, the human; but the other was released alive. For the divine nature remained impassible, being anointed with the blood of the nature that suffered, and having taken up the suffering into itself; but the blood of the Lord a new earthen vessel received — the new people, I say, that from the nations, receptive of the New Covenant. But observe that after one is cleansed from the leprosy, then he is worthy to offer this gift, that is, to sacrifice Christ and to perform the sacred rite; for being a leper and unclean in soul, he would not be deemed worthy to offer such gifts, that is, to bring forward the body and blood of the Lord, which was united to the divine nature. But attend to this also, how unutterable a superiority the Lord has over Moses. For Moses, when his sister was stricken with leprosy, though he entreated much, was not able to heal her; but the Lord by a word alone cleansed the leper. But consider also the humility of the Lord, how, while the crowd desired to touch him, he himself rather gladly spent his time in the deserts, and prayed; thus indeed he gave us patterns in all things, both of praying in private, and of shunning glory.
