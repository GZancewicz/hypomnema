# Luke 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 29 · Luke 5:33

> **But they said to him: Why do the disciples of John fast often and make supplications, likewise also those of the Pharisees, but yours eat and drink? And he said to them: Can you make the sons of the bridechamber, in which the bridegroom is with them, fast?[^1] (Luke 5:33)**

[^1]: The construction «Μὴ δύνασθε … ποιῆσαι νηστεύειν;» with «μή» expects a negative answer: "You cannot make them fast, can you?" «ἐν ᾧ» ("in which") refers to the wedding-hall/bridechamber during which the bridegroom is present.
