# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 192 · John 1:42

> **Christ; and he brought him to Jesus. And Jesus, looking upon him, said: You are Simon the son of Jonah; you shall be called[^1] Cephas, which is interpreted, Peter. (John 1:42)**

The name of Andrew he makes known to us, but that of the other by no means. For some say that he was John, the very one who writes these things; but others, that that man was not one of the notable ones—and at the same time there was no profit in learning the name. Of Andrew, however, he does make mention, both as a notable man and as one who brought his own brother. And observe for me his brotherly love, how he did not hide the good thing from his brother, but shares the treasure with him, and lets out a cry of exceeding joy,

[^1]: The manuscript reads οὐ κληθήσῃ ("you shall *not* be called"), an OCR corruption of σὺ κληθήσῃ ("*you* shall be called"), which is the sense required by the passage.
