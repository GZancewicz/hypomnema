# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 96 · John 1:15

> **John testifies concerning him, and has cried out, saying: This was he of whom I said, He who comes after me has come to be before me, because he was before me. (John 1:15)**[^1]

The evangelist continually takes up John's testimony—not making the Master trustworthy on the strength of the servant, but, since the crowds held a great opinion concerning John, for this reason he takes up, for the testimony concerning Christ, the John who was reckoned great among them and on this account believed above all others. And the phrase,

[^1]: Greek «ὅτι πρῶτός μου ἦν» — literally "because he was first of me," i.e. prior to me. The comparative genitive expresses precedence: John declares that the one coming after him in time has nevertheless "come to be before" (ἔμπροσθέν μου γέγονεν) him in rank, because he was already prior in existence.
