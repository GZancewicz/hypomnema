# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 157 · John 1:29

> **sin. (John 1:29)**

Perhaps both because of[^1]

[^1]: The fragment breaks off here. The Greek reads «ἀμαρτίαν·» Τάχα μὲν καὶ διὰ — "«sin.» Perhaps both because of…" — and the sentence is truncated; it is taken up again in the next lemma, where «ἁμαρτίαν» εἰπεῖν, καθολικῶς εἶπεν ("in saying «sin» he spoke universally"). A stray, isolated ποῦ ("where") follows in the source and appears to be OCR noise or a misplaced fragment; it carries no legible sense here.
