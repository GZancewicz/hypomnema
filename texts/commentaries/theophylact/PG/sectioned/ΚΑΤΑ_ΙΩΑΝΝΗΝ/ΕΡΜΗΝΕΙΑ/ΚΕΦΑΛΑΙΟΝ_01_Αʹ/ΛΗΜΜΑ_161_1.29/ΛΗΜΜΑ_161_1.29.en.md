# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 161 · John 1:29

> **Because he was prior to me. (John 1:29)**

Hear, O Arius: he did not say "He was created prior to me," but[^1]

[^1]: The sentence is truncated here and continues in the next lemma, which supplies the contrast: «that he *was*» — that is, not "created" but simply "was," an existence without beginning.
