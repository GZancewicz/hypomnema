# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 49 · John 1:3

> **Life was. (John 1:3-4)**[^1]

And I think that neither is this reading mistaken, but that it holds to the same correct sense. For this saint too understood orthodoxly, that apart from the Word not even one thing came into being, which has come into being in him. For all things, as many as have come to be and were created in him — in the Word, I mean — apart from him have not come to be. Then, from another starting-point:

[^1]: The lemma quote is corrupt in the OCR, which reads «Ζωὴ ΚΧν·»; reconstructed as «Ζωὴ ἦν·» ("Life was"). The fragment belongs to Theophylact's discussion of the punctuation at the John 1:3-4 boundary: the reading here attaches «ὃ γέγονεν ἐν αὐτῷ» ("which has come into being in him") to the preceding clause, so that «Ζωὴ ἦν» begins a fresh sentence — hence "from another starting-point."
