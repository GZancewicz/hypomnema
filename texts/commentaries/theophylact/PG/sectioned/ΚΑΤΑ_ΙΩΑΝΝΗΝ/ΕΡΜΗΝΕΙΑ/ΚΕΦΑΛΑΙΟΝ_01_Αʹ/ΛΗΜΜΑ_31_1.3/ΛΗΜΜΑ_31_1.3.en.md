# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 31 · John 1:3

> **I manifested thy name to the[^1] (John 17:6)**

[^1]: The scripture quotation breaks off here mid-sentence; it is completed by the following fragment (Lemma 32) with «ἀνθρώποις», "men" — so the full lemma is «Ἐφανέρωσά σου τὸ ὄνομα τοῖς ἀνθρώποις» (John 17:6).
