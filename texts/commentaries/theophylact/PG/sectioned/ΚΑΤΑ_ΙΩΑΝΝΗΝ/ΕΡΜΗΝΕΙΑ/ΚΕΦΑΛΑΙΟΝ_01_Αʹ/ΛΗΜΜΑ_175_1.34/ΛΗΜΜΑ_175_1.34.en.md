# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 175 · John 1:34

> **And I have seen, and have borne witness, that this one is the Son of God. (John 1:34)**

And where did John bear witness that this one is the Son of God? For nowhere has this been written; for he calls him a lamb, but Son of God nowhere. Whence it is likely to conjecture that many other things also have been left out; for not all things were written down.
