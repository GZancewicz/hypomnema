# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 127 · John 1:23

> **Art thou the prophet? (John 1:21)**

with the,[^1]

[^1]: The section breaks off mid-sentence; the point (continued in what follows) is that the questioners framed their question with the definite article — μετὰ τοῦ «ὁ», "with the *the*" — i.e. asking whether he was *the* expected prophet.
