# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 174 · John 1:34

> **I did not know him, (John 1:34)**

this gives us to understand that he did not know that he himself is the one who will baptize in the Holy Spirit; but that he is above the many, he did know. Wherefore also, according to Matthew, he was preventing him (Matt. 3:14), knowing him to be altogether greater; but when the Spirit came down, then, having recognized him more clearly, he also proclaimed him to the others. And the Spirit was seen by all those present, and not only by John. Why then, one says, did they not believe? Because their senseless heart was darkened (cf. Rom. 1:21), since even when they saw him working miracles, they did not believe. But some say that not all saw it, but the more devout. For even if the Spirit came down bodily, yet it is likely that it was not seen by all, but by the worthy, since the prophets too saw many things in a perceptible form; such as Daniel and Ezekiel; but nevertheless no one else beheld these things.
