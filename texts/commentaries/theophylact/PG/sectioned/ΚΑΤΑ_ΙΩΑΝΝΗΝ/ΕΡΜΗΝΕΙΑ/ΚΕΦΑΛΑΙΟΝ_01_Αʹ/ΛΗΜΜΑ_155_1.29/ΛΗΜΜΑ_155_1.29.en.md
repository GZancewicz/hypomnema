# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 155 · John 1:29

> **the Lamb. (John 1:29)**

For there are many lambs, just as there are also many christs [i.e. anointed ones]; but the true lamb, the one prefigured in Moses and proclaimed in Isaiah, this is he. And Christ is called the Lamb *of God*, either of God as having accepted the death of Christ for our salvation, or as of God having given him for us unto death. Just as we are accustomed to say, "This victim is so-and-so's," instead of "So-and-so brought this offering forward," so therefore also the Lord is called the Lamb of God, as of God and Father who, through his love toward us, gave him unto slaughter on our behalf.
