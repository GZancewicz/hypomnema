# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 182 · John 1:39

> **What do you seek? (John 1:39)**

Not as being ignorant — for how *could he be*, who knows the hearts of men? — but that through the question he might call them forth to say what they want. For it is likely that they both blushed and feared him, as one witnessed by John to be beyond man. But do you marvel to me at the good sense of these men. For not only did they follow, but they also call him "rabbi," which is "teacher," and this though having as yet heard nothing from him.
