# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 117 · John 1:20

> **He confessed, and denied not; and he confessed that, "I am not the Christ." (John 1:20)**

He said above that John bears witness concerning him; then, having said in between what John testified concerning Christ — that «He was before me» (John 1:15),[^1] and that…

[^1]: The OCR here is corrupt, reading ὅτι εῦτα - Καπροσθέν μου γέγονε; this stands for ὅτι «Ἔμπροσθέν μου γέγονε» ("that «He was before me»," cf. John 1:15, 30). The clause breaks off at καὶ ὅτι ("and that"), continuing into the next fragment.
