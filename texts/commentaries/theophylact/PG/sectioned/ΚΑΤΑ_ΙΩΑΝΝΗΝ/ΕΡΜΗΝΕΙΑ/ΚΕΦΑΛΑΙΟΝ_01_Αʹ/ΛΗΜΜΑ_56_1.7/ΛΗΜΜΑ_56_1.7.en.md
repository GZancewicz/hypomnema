# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 56 · John 1:7

> **"That all might believe"? (John 1:7)**

How? As far as it lay with him, for this reason he bore witness, that he might draw all; but if some disbelieved, that one is not to be blamed. Since indeed the sun too rises for this purpose, that it may give light to all; but if someone, having shut himself up in a gloomy dwelling, does not enjoy its ray, what is this to the sun? So too *John bore witness*[^1] that they might believe; but if this did not come to pass, that one is not the cause.

[^1]: The Greek at this point is corrupt: line 7 ends with a stray «Θω» and line 8 reads «Θω πιστεύσωσι», with the main clause lost. The sense supplied ("so too John bore witness, that they might believe") follows the sun-analogy and the parallel «ἵνα πάντας ἑλκύσῃ» above.
