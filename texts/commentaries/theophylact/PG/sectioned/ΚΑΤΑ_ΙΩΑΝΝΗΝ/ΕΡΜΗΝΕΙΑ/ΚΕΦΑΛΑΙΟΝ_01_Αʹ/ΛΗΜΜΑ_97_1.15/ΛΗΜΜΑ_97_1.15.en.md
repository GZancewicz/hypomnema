# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 97 · John 1:15

> **he cried out, (John 1:15)**

This shows the great boldness of John, for not in a corner, but with much boldness did he cry out about Christ.[^1] But what was he saying?

[^1]: The OCR here reads λὰΚγὰρ, which is corrupt; the sense requires οὐ γὰρ ("for not"), giving "for not in a corner, but with much boldness." The Greek noun is παῤῥησία, "boldness of speech, outspokenness."
