# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 87 · John 1:14

> **He dwelt among us, (John 1:14)**

we are taught this: to believe two natures in the one Christ. For even if he is one according to hypostasis, that is, the person, yet [he is] two according to the natures; for [he is] God and man. And a divine nature and a human [nature] could not be one, even if they should be contemplated in one — I mean the Christ.[^1]

[^1]: The OCR reads τὸν Χριο ὄν φημι; restored as τὸν Χριστὸν ὄν φημι, "the Christ, [him] I mean."
