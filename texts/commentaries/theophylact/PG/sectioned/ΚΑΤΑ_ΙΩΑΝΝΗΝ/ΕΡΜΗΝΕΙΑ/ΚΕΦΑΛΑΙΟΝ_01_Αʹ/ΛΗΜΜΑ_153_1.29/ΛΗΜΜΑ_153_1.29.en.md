# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 153 · John 1:29

> **Behold the Lamb of God, who takes away the sin of the world.[^1] (John 1:29)**

For he who is so pure as even to take away and make vanish the sins of others—it is evident that he would not have received the baptism of confession equally with the others. But examine for me also the exact wording:

[^1]: Here Theophylact's text places the genitive before the noun: «ὁ αἴρων τοῦ κόσμου τὴν ἁμαρτίαν» ("who takes away, of the world, the sin"), a reordering of the phrase quoted in the previous lemma («ὁ αἴρων τὴν ἁμαρτίαν τοῦ κόσμου»). The sense is unchanged; the English preserves the natural order.
