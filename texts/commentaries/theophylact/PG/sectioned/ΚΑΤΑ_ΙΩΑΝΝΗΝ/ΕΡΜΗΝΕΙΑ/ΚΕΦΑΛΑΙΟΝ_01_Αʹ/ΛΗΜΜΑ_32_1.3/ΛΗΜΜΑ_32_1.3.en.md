# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 32 · John 1:3

> **…men. (John 17:6)**

These things, then, to whom do they befit—the Father, or the Son? All to the Son. For he himself announced the name of the Father, teaching *it*. Accordingly, after saying these things, the blessed David[^1] adds:

[^1]: OCR "Δαυγὸ" read as Δαυίδ, "David." The citation that follows in the next fragment (Lemma 33) is Ps 101:26 (LXX), which is applied to the Son (cf. Heb 1:10). In the same clause OCR "ανήγγειλε τὰν ὄώομα" is read as ἀνήγγειλε τὸ ὄνομα, "announced the name."
