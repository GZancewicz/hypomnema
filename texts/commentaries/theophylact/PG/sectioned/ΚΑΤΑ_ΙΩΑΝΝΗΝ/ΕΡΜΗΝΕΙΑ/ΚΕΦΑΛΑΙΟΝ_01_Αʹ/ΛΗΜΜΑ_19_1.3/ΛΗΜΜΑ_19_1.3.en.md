# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 19 · John 1:3

> **And the Word was with God. (John 1:1)**

For most clearly the evangelist here says that the Word is one, and God — that is, the Father — is another. For since the Word was with God, it is evident that two persons are introduced, even if there is one nature to these two. But that there is one nature, hear: **"And the Word was God."** Do you see that the Word too is God? So that there is one nature to the Father and to the Son, since there is also one Godhead. Let them therefore be ashamed, both Arius and Sabellius: Arius, saying that the Son of God is a creature and a thing made, let him be put to shame by that which follows —[^1]

[^1]: The sentence breaks off mid-clause (Greek ἐντρεπέσθω διὰ τοῦ, "let him be abashed by the [saying]"), leading directly into the scripture quoted at the head of the next lemma. The μέν of «Ἄρειος μὲν» is answered by «Σαβέλλιος δὲ» in the following section; left untranslated here since the antithesis is split across the lemma boundary.
