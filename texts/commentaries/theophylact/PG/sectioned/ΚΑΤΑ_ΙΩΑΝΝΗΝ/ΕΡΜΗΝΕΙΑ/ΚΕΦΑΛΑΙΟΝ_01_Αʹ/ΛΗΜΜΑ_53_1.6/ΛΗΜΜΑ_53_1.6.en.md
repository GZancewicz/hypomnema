# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 53 · John 1:6

> **There came to be *one* sent from God. (John 1:6)**

That is to say, he was sent from God. For the false prophets are not from God. But when you hear that he was sent from God, do not suppose that he speaks anything of his own or anything human, but all things divine. For this reason he is also called an angel [i.e. messenger], and the virtue of an angel is to speak nothing of his own. But when you hear "angel," do not think that he was an angel by nature, or that he came down from the heavens; but on account of his work and his ministry he is called an angel. For since he served the preaching and announced the Lord beforehand, on this account he was called an angel.
