# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 26 · John 1:3

> **«through» (John 1:3)**

addition[^1] [troubles you], and you seek to find in the Scripture some saying that states that the Word himself made all things, listen to David:

[^1]: Greek πρόσθεσις, "addition"; Theophylact means the added preposition «διά» ("through"). The clause continues the sentence begun in the previous fragment ("if the «through» addition troubles you..."), the object of ταράττῃ ("troubles").
