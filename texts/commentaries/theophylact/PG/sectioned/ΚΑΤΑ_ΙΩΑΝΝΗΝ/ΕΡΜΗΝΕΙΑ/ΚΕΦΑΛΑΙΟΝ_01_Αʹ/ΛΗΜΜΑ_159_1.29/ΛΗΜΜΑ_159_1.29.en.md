# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 159 · John 1:29

> **This is he concerning whom I said: After me comes a man, who has come to be before me, because he was prior to me. (John 1:29)**

Earlier, to those who had come from the Pharisees, John was saying that:[^1]

[^1]: The sentence is truncated here; the "that" (ὅτι) introduces the earlier saying quoted in the following lemma («In the midst of you stands one whom you know not…»). — On the two expressions: ἔμπροσθέν μου γέγονεν ("has come to be before me") and πρῶτός μου ἦν ("was prior to me") are distinguished in what follows: Theophylact glosses ἔμπροσθέν as rank ("more distinguished and more honored") and πρῶτος as pre-eternal existence.
