# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 135 · John 1:23

> **Make straight the way of the Lord. (John 1:23)**

Fittingly is John called a voice, being the Forerunner of Christ, just as the voice too runs ahead of the word.[^1] That is—to speak more clearly—a voice is an inarticulate breath which issues from the chest; but when it is articulated by the tongue, then it becomes speech. [^1]: The analogy turns on «φωνή» (voice) and «λόγος» (word/articulate speech): John is the «φωνή» that precedes the «Λόγος», the Word, that is, Christ. The one term «λόγος» carries both the ordinary sense "speech" and the divine title "Word."
