# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 114 · John 1:18

> **Grace and truth came through Jesus Christ, (John 1:17)**[^1]

wishing to establish this, he says that: I have said nothing incredible. For Moses neither saw God, just as no one else *did*, nor did he have a most distinct and clear exposition to tell us concerning him; but being a slave, he only ministered toward the service of the legal letter. But Christ, being Son and Only-begotten, and being in the paternal bosom, not only sees him, but also expounds concerning him to all men. So that, since he is Son and sees the Father — inasmuch indeed as being in his bosom — he fittingly gave us grace and truth.
