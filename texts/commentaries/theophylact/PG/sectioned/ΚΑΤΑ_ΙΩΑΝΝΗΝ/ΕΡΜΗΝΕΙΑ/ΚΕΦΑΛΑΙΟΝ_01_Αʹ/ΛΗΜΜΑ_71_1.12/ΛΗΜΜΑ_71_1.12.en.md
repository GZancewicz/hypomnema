# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 71 · John 1:12

> **"Who were begotten not of bloods," (John 1:12)**

that is, of the menstrual *blood*;[^1] for from these come the nourishment and growth for the embryo. And they say that the seed too is first turned into blood, then is transformed into flesh and the rest of the *bodily* structure.

But since it was likely that some would say that then the birth of Isaac too was such as those who believe in Christ are begotten—for Isaac too was not begotten of bloods, since the menstrual *flow* had ceased for Sarah—since, then, it was possible for some to suppose this, he adds the *phrase*:[^2]

[^1]: τῶν καταμηνίων, "of the menses/menstrual blood"; the genitive is supplied in explanation of the plural αἱμάτων ("bloods"). OCR reads corruptly: δηλαδή (for "δηλαῦής"), ἐμβρύῳ (for "ἐμόρύῳ"), σπέρμα (for "σπέριμα").
[^2]: The sentence runs on into the scripture quote of the next section. OCR reads corruptly here: ἦν (for "Σν"/"Χν"), εἰπεῖν (for "σἱπεῖν"), Σάῤῥᾳ (for "Σάῤψᾳ").
