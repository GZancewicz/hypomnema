# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 1 · John 1:3

> **In the beginning was the Word. (John 1:1)**

Which very thing I also said in the prologue, this too I will say again: that whereas the other evangelists narrated at length the lower birth of the Lord, his upbringing, and his growth, this one runs past those matters, as having been sufficiently told by his fellow-disciples, but discourses concerning the divinity of the One who for our sake became man. And yet, if you observe accurately, neither did those [evangelists] thrust aside the speaking about the divinity of the Only-begotten, but they made mention of it, even if not at length; nor did this one, having looked toward the loftier discourse, wholly neglect the fleshly economy; for there was one Spirit moving the souls of them all. He discourses, therefore, to us concerning the Son; for the Father was already known from the Old [Testament]. And yet neither did he pass over the Father in silence, but he both makes mention concerning him and speaks concerning the Son.
