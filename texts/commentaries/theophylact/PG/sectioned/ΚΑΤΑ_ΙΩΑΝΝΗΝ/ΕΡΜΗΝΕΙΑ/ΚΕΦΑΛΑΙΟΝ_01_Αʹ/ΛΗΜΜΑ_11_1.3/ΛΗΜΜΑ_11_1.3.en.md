# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 11 · John 1:3

> **created, (Gen. 1:1)**

with the *«was»*?[^1] For if it had been written here also, «In the beginning God created the Son», I would have kept silent; but now—

[^1]: The quoted words are being set in antithesis: the *«created»* (ἐποίησε) of Genesis against the *«was»* (ἦν) of John. The clause breaks off at «νῦν δὲ,», completed by the following fragment’s «Ἐν ἀρχῇ ἦν».
