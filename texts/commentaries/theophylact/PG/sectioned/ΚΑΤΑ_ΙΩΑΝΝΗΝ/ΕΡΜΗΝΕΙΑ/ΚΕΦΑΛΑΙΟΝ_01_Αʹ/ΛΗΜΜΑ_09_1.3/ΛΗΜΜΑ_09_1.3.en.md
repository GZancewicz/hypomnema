# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 9 · John 1:3

> **in the beginning (John 1:1)**

shall I understand,[^1] as though the Only-begotten were eternal. These things, then, [says] the heretic. But we, against this senseless objection, will say nothing else but this: O sophist of malice, how did you hold back what follows? Yet we will say this, even if you do not wish it. For there Moses says: «In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth» (Gen. 1:1); but here—

[^1]: The OCR reads ἀἵδίου ὄυντος for ἀϊδίου ὄντος (“being eternal”). The heretic completes his objection: “neither *here* shall I understand *«in the beginning»* as though the Only-begotten were eternal.” The sentence again breaks off at «ἐνταῦθα δὲ,», to be completed by the following fragment’s quotation of John.
