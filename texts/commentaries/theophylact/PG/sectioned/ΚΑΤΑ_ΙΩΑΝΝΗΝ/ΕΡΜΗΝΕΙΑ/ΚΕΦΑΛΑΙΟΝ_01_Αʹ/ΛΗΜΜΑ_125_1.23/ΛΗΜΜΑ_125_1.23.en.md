# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 125 · John 1:23

> **"of one crying in the wilderness: Make straight the way of the Lord, as Isaiah the prophet said." (John 1:23)**

They were expecting Elijah to come, being persuaded by an ancient tradition; therefore they ask him whether he is Elijah; and so he denies[^1] this too.

[^1]: The OCR reads "νεῖται" (with "προσεὸόκων" for προσεδόκων above); reconstructed as ἀρνεῖται, "he denies."
