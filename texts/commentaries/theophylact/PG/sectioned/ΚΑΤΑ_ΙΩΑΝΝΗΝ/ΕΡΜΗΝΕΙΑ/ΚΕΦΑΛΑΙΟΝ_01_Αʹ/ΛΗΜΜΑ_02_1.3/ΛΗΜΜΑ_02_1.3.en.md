# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 2 · John 1:3

> **In the beginning was the Word, (John 1:1)**

that is, it was from the beginning. For that which is from the beginning[^1] — by no means at all shall a time be found when it was not. And whence, he says, is it clear that the [phrase]—

[^1]: The Greek here is OCR-corrupt: «Τὸ γὰρ ἀε´ ἀρχῆς ὃν». Read either ἀπ᾿ ἀρχῆς ὄν ("that which is from the beginning," parallel to the preceding clause) or ἀεὶ ὄν ("that which always is"); the sense either way is that of unoriginate being.
