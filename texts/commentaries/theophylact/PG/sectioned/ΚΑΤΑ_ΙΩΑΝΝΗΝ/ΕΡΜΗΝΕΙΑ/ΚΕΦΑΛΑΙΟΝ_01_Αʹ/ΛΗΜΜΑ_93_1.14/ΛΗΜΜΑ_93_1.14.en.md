# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 93 · John 1:14

> **Full of grace and truth. (John 1:14)**

Full of grace, inasmuch as his word too was full of grace,[^1] so to speak, even as David also says:

[^1]: Greek «καθὸ καὶ ὁ Λόγος αὐτοῦ κεχαριτωμένος ἦν». Two puns operate here. Ὁ Λόγος αὐτοῦ can mean either "his word/speech" or "his Word" (the divine Logos); the following psalm-citation about grace poured on the lips favors "his speech," but the christological resonance is surely intended. Κεχαριτωμένος ("graced, filled with grace") echoes the χάρις ("grace") of the lemma and the same participle used of the Virgin at Luke 1:28.
