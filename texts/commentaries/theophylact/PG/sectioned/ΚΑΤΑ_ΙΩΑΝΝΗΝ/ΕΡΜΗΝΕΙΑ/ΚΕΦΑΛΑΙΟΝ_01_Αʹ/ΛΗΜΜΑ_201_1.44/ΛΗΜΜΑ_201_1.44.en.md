# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 201 · John 1:44

> **…Philip, from Bethsaida, from the city of Andrew and Peter. Philip finds Nathanael, and says to him: Him of whom Moses wrote in the law, and the prophets, we have found — Jesus the son of Joseph, the one from Nazareth. (John 1:44)**

Andrew, having heard the Forerunner, and Peter *having heard* Andrew, followed Jesus; but Philip appears to have heard nothing, and yet to have followed the Lord immediately, when He said to him the *word*:[^1]

[^1]: OCR in the lemma: line 2 Εὐοίσνει read as εὑρίσκει, "finds"; line 4 λΙωσῆς read as Μωσῆς, "Moses." The clause runs on into the following lemma, which supplies the quoted word «Follow me.»
