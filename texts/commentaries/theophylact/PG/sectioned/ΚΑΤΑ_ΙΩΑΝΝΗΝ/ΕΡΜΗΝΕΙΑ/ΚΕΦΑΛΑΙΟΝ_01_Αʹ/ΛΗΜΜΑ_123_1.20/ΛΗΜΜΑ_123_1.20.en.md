# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 123 · John 1:20

> **"I am not the Christ,"[^1] (John 1:20)**

looking to their aim, and from every side drawing them toward believing that the Christ is another[^2] — the one considered by them to be lowly, and of a lowly father (for *he was the son* of a carpenter) and of a lowly homeland (for *it was* Nazareth), out of which they believed nothing good could come. Concerning the Forerunner himself, however, they held a great opinion; for he was of a father who was high priest, and of a life nearly angelic and all but *a life* of ascetic struggle. Whence it is right to marvel how, from the very things by which those men supposed they would damage the glory of Christ, by these same things they are tripped up. For they question John as one worthy of trust, in order that they might have his testimony as a pretext for their unbelief toward Christ, if indeed he should not confess *Jesus* to be Christ;[^3] but the matter turned out for them to the contrary. For this man, whom they held to be trustworthy, they find testifying concerning Christ, and not drawing the honor to himself.

[^1]: The OCR reads Οὐκοείμὶ; the text is Οὐκ εἰμὶ ὁ Χριστός.
[^2]: ἄλλος ἐστὶν ὁ Χριστός: "the Christ is another [person]" — i.e., someone other than John himself, namely Jesus, whom the exposition goes on to describe as the one they despised.
[^3]: εἴπερ μὴ ὁμολογήσῃ αὐτὸν Χριστόν. The pronoun αὐτόν is ambiguous; the sense taken here is that they hoped John (the trusted witness) would fail to acknowledge Jesus as Christ, thereby furnishing them a ground for unbelief.
