# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 73 · John 1:12

> **Abba, the Father. (Rom. 8:15)**[^1]

But for those [of old], just as the baptism was typical and shadowy, so also was the adoption, prefiguring ours. Even if, therefore, they were called sons, yet only in shadow, and they did not have the very truth of the adoption, which we now *have* through baptism.

[^1]: The quoted phrase «Ἀββᾶ ὁ Πατήρ» is not from John but is the cry the previous section (on John 1:12) leads into — «κράζομεν» ("we cry"). The wording and context (adoption, the Spirit, «κράζομεν») match Rom. 8:15; cf. Gal. 4:6. In the Greek the relative pronoun «ἃς» (fem. acc. pl.) does not agree with its antecedent «τὴν ἀλήθειαν» (fem. acc. sg.); rendered here as "which."
