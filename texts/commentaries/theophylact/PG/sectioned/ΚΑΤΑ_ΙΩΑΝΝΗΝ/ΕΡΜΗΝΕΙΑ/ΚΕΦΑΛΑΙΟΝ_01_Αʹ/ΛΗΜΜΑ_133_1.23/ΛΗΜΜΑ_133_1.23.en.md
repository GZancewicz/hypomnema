# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 133 · John 1:23

> **Make straight the way of the Lord. (John 1:23)**

A servant, he says, am I, and I prepare the road beforehand for the Lord: your[^1] hearts. Make these straight, therefore, you crooked and deceitful ones, and level them, so that a way may be made for the Lord Christ through you. Then he brings in Isaiah as a witness. For having said great things about Christ—that he is Lord, and that he himself takes up the work of a servant and a herald—he takes refuge in the prophet. [^1]: The OCR reads «μῶν»; the sense requires «ὑμῶν», "of you / your."
