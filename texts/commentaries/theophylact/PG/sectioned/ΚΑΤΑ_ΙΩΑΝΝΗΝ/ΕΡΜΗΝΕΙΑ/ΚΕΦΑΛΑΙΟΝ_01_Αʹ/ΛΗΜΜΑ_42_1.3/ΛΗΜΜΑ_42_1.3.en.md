# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 42 · John 1:3

> **"In him was life, and the life was the light of men." (John 1:4)**[^1]

[^1]: The Greek breaks off mid-word: «τὸ φῶς τῶν ἀν-» followed by an unreadable run of characters («Β ν εΚε. ἔ»), evidently a column/catchword artifact of the OCR. The lemma is completed to «τῶν ἀνθρώπων» ("of men"), John 1:4. The quotation belongs to John 1:4, though this fragment is filed under John 1:3.
