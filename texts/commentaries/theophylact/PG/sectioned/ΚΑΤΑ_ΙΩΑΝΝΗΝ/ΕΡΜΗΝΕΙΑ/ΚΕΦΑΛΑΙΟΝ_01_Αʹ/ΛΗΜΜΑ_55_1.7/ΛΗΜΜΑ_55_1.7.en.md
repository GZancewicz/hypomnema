# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 55 · John 1:7

> **This one came for a witness, that he might bear witness concerning the light, that all might believe through him. (John 1:7)**

This one, he says, is the one sent from God, that he might bear witness concerning the light. Then, lest anyone should think that the Only-begotten therefore needed his witness as being deficient, for this reason he adds that he came to bear witness concerning the Son of God — not as though *the Son* needed that man's witness, but that all might believe through him. Did then all indeed believe through him? No. How then does the evangelist say,[^1]

[^1]: The sentence breaks off here and is completed in the following lemma («Ἵνα πάντες πιστεύσωσι;» — "That all might believe?"), where Theophylact resolves the objection.
