# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 138 · John 1:25

> **they asked him, and said to him: Why then do you baptize, if you are not the Christ, neither Elijah, nor the prophet? (John 1:25)**

Since through flattery they were not able to catch him, they frighten him with harsher and more reproachful words, so that he might say what they wanted and confess himself to be Christ—
