# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 128 · John 1:23

> **the, (John 1:21)**

having framed the question with the article,[^1] that is, *that* expected prophet, of whom Moses spoke, when: "A prophet shall the Lord God raise up for you." (cf. Deut. 18:15)[^2] John, therefore, did not deny being a prophet, but [denied being] the—

[^1]: Greek «ὁ», the definite article, quoted as a word: ἄρθρου ποιησάμενοι τὴν ἐρώτησιν, "making the question with the article." The article marks *the* awaited prophet.
[^2]: The Moses quotation is loose/conflated; the OCR reads «ΠΙροφήτην» for Προφήτην.
