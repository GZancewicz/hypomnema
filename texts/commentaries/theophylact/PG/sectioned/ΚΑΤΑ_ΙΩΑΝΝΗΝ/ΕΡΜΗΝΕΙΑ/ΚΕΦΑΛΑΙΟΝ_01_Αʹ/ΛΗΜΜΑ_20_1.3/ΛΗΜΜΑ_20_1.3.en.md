# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 20 · John 1:3

> **In the beginning he was, and the Word was God. (John 1:1)**[^1]

But Sabellius, who does not speak of a trinity of persons but a monad, [let him be put to shame] by that which follows —[^2]

[^1]: A compressed citation conflating John 1:1a («Ἐν ἀρχῇ ἦν») and 1:1c («καὶ Θεὸς ἦν ὁ Λόγος»); rendered as it stands on the page. This is the saying by which Arius (from the previous lemma) is "put to shame."
[^2]: The clause trails off (Greek διὰ τοῦ,), continuing the antithesis begun in the previous lemma («Ἄρειος μὲν … Σαβέλλιος δὲ …») and leading into the next scripture quotation. The verb of shaming is supplied from the parallel «ἐντρεπέσθω» / «ἐντρεπέσθω» above.
