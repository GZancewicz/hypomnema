# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 129 · John 1:23

> **the prophet, (John 1:21)**

*namely,* that expected one. For since they had heard Moses saying that a prophet would arise, they hoped that this prophet would come about at certain seasons. Then again they press him: "Tell us, then, who art thou?" (John 1:22) Then he answers them:[^1]

[^1]: OCR corruptions in this section: «σῆν» for οὖν ("then"), and «ταῆτον» for τοῦτον ("this").
