# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 109 · John 1:17

> **For the law was given through Moses, but grace and truth came through Jesus Christ. (John 1:17)**

He explains[^1] to us how we received a greatest grace in exchange for a small grace; and he says that the law was given through Moses, God having used a man as mediator — Moses, I mean; but the New Covenant *came* through Jesus Christ, which is also named grace, because God graced us not only with the forgiveness of sins, but also with the adoption as sons. And it is called truth, inasmuch as the things which those of old either saw or spoke in a figurative way, these this *covenant* proclaimed splendidly.[^2] Accordingly the New Covenant, which is named both grace and truth, did not use a mere man as mediator, but the Son of God.
