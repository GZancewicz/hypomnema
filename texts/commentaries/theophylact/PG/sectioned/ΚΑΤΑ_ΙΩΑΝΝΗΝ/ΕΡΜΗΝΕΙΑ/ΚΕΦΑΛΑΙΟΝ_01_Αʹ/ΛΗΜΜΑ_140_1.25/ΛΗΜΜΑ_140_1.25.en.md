# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 140 · John 1:25

> **If you are not the Christ, nor the prophet; (John 1:25)**

—supposing the Christ to be one *thing* and the prophet another, being ill-informed. For the prophet is himself[^1] the Christ and our God. But all these things they were saying, as I have said, so that they might compel him to confess himself to be Christ; or rather, if the truer thing must be said, it is because they envied his glory that they question him. Wherefore they do not even inquire whether he himself is the Christ, but:

[^1]: The OCR here reads a broken `αὐτά-`; reconstructed as αὐτός: "the prophet is himself the Christ."
