# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 37 · John 1:3

> **"Paul, an apostle through the will of God." (cf. 2 Cor. 1:1)**[^1]

But these things *are* sufficient; and we must return again to where we departed.[^2]

[^1]: Theophylact quotes an abbreviated form of the Pauline epistolary opening, «Παῦλος ἀπόστολος διὰ θελήματος Θεοῦ», omitting «Χριστοῦ Ἰησοῦ»; cf. 2 Cor. 1:1; Eph. 1:1; Col. 1:1; also 1 Cor. 1:1.
[^2]: «ἐπανιτέον δὲ πάλιν ὅθεν ἐξέβημεν» — literally "and we must go back again to where we went out," i.e. to where we digressed from the subject.
