# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 145 · John 1:27

> **But in the midst of you stands one whom you do not know. (John 1:26)**

For the Lord, being mingled with the crowd, was unknown as to who he might be and whence. But perhaps someone might say that in another manner too the Lord stood in the midst of the Pharisees, yet they did not know him. For since they themselves supposed that they were diligently studying the Scriptures, and in these the Lord was proclaimed, he was in the midst of them—that is, in their hearts—but they did not know him, inasmuch as they did not understand the Scriptures, even if they had these in their hearts. And perhaps also, insofar as the Lord was a mediator of God and men, he stood in the midst of the Pharisees, wishing to reconcile them to God, but they did not know him.
