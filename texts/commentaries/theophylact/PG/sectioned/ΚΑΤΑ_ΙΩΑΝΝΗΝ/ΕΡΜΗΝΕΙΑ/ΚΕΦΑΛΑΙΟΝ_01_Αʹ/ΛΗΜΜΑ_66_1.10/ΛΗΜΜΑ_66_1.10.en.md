# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 66 · John 1:10

> **And his own received him not. (John 1:10)**

Either the Jews, or also the rest of men, who were created by him. And so he, as it were, bewails the folly of men, and marvels at the love-for-mankind of the Master. Being his own, he says, not all received him. For the Lord draws no one by compulsion, but by *their own* judgment and deliberate choice—[^1]

[^1]: The sentence breaks off here in mid-clause («ἀλλὰ γνώμη καὶ προαιρέσει»). «προαίρεσις» is the deliberate, self-determining choice of the will; the point is that assent to the Lord is free, not compelled.
