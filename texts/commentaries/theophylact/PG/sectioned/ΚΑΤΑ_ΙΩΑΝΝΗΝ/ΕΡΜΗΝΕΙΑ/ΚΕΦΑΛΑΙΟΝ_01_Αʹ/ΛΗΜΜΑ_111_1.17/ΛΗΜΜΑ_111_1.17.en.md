# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 111 · John 1:17

> **"was given," but the *word* "came," (John 1:17)**

so that he might show that grace and truth came to be by our Lord Jesus Christ as master, not as slave, and advanced to completion. For the law was given from God through the medium of Moses; but grace *came to be*, not *was given*, through Jesus Christ. Authoritative *is* the *word*,
