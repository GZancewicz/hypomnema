# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 204 · John 1:44

> **We have found Jesus. (John 1:44)**

— which shows that he had indeed been seeking *Him*.[^1] But perhaps he had learned about the Christ from Andrew and Peter. For indeed, since he was from the same fatherland, it is likely[^2] that these men, having conversed *with him*, recounted about the Lord; which the evangelist also seems to hint at through saying:

[^1]: ὃ δείκνυσιν ὡς ἐζήτει ἄν — "which shows that he was seeking / would have been seeking"; the imperfect with ἄν carries an iterative or potential force. OCR: the lemma Ἐὐρήκαμεν read as Εὑρήκαμεν, "we have found"; Ἀείκνυσιν read as δείκνυσιν, "shows."

[^2]: OCR gap: εἰκὸς ανυ μιλήσαντας reconstructed as εἰκὸς [ἦν] ὁμιλήσαντας, "it was likely … having conversed." The trailing clause introduces the evangelist's remark quoted in the following lemma (that Philip was from Bethsaida, the city of Andrew and Peter).
