# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 160 · John 1:29

> **In the midst of you stands one whom you know not, who has come to be before me; (John 1:26)**[^1]

but now he even points him out with his finger, and makes him manifest to those who did not know him. "This is that one concerning whom I was bearing witness," he says to the Pharisees, "that he has come to be before me" — that is, more distinguished than me and more honored. Why?

[^1]: The lemma cited here is John the Baptist's earlier saying at John 1:26, which Theophylact recalls; the enclosing verse under comment remains John 1:29.
