# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 181 · John 1:39

> **He says to them: Come, and see. They came, therefore, and saw where he abides, and they stayed with him that day. And it was about the tenth hour.[^1] (John 1:39)**

Matthew, having narrated concerning the baptism of the Lord, leads him up immediately into the mountain to be tempted; but the present evangelist, having left aside the things said by that one, narrates the things after the descent from the mountain. And so the following of John's disciples, and their coming to Christ, takes place after the coming down from the mountain and the being tempted; the word showing, I think, that one ought not to be established as a teacher unless first he himself, having gone up upon the summit of virtue — for this is the mountain — and having conquered every temptation, may raise the trophy against the tempter. Accordingly the disciples follow first, and then they ask him where he abides. For it was necessary to converse not publicly, with many present, but privately, as about necessary matters.
