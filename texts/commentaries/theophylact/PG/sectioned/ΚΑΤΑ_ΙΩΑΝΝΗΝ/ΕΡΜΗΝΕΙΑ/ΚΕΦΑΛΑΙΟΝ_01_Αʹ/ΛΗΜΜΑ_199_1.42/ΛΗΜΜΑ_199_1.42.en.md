# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 199 · John 1:42

«…obedience,» while Jonah *means* «a dove.»[^1] A child, therefore, of meekness — which is signified through the dove — is obedience; and he who has obedience becomes Peter also, being advanced through obedience into the steadfastness of the good.

[^1]: This fragment continues an exposition (from the preceding lemma) of the name *Σίμων Βαριωνᾶ*, "Simon son of Jonah." The commentator is glossing the two names: Simon is taken as "obedience/hearing" (ὑπακοή), and Jonah (Ἰωνᾶς) as "dove" (περιστερά). The word-glosses «ὑπακοή» and «περιστερά» are quoted in the Greek.
