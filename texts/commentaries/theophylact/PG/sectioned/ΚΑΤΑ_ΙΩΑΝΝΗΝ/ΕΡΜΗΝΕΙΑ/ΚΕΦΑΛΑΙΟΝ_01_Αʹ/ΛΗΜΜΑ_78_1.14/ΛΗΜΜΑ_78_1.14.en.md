# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 78 · John 1:14

> **Every soul which is not circumcised, let it be put to death. (Gen. 17:14)**[^1]

For behold, instead of saying "every man," he said the part—the soul, I mean. And again from the flesh he names the whole, as when it says:

[^1]: A loose paraphrase of Gen. 17:14 (LXX), where the uncircumcised «ψυχή» ("soul," i.e. person) is to be "utterly destroyed" («ἐξολεθρευθήσεται»); Theophylact adapts it to «θανατούσθω» ("let it be put to death"). The stray final «ν» after «θανατούσθω» in the manuscript is a marker/OCR artifact and is not translated.
