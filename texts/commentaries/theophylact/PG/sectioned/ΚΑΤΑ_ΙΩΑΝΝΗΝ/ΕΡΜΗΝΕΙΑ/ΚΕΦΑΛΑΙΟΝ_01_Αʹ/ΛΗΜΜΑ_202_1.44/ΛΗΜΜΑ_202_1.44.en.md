# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 202 · John 1:44

> **Follow me. (John 1:44)**

First, the voice of the Lord *sufficed*, in his soul, to work some goad of love.[^1] For not simply was the voice of the Savior uttered, but for the worthy it at once kindled the inward parts toward love of Him; just as also those around Cleopas say:

[^1]: The Greek here lacks a finite verb: πρῶτον μὲν ἡ τοῦ Κυρίου φωνὴ ἐν τῇ ψυχῇ αὐτοῦ ἐργάσασθαί τι κέντρον ἀγάπης — literally "first the voice of the Lord … in his soul to work some goad of love," with only the infinitive ἐργάσασθαι. A governing verb (e.g. ἤρκεσε, "sufficed," or ἴσχυσε, "was able") appears to have dropped out; *sufficed* is supplied. κέντρον = "goad/sting/spur." OCR: the opening word Ἀκολούθτι read as Ἀκολούθει, "Follow." The πρῶτον μέν here is answered by Ἔπειτα δέ ("and then") in the following lemma.
