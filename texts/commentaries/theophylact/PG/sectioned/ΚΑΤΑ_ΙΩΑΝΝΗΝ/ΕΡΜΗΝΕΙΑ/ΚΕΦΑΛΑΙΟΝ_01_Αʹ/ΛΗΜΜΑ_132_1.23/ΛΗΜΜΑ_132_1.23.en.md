# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 132 · John 1:23

> **concerning whom it is written, (John 1:23)**[^1]

the construction of the phrase appears somewhat absurd. But why "crying"?[^2]

[^1]: The quoted words «περὶ οὗ γέγραπται» are not a scripture citation but the connective clause Theophylact says must be supplied for the sense of John 1:23 to cohere.
[^2]: OCR corruptions here: «φαένεται» for φαίνεται ("appears"), and «οὖ» for οὗ ("whom").
