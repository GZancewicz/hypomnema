# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 52 · John 1:6

> **There came to be a man sent from God; his name was John. (John 1:6)**

After discoursing to us concerning the pre-eternal existence of God the Word, since he is about also to speak concerning the incarnation of the Word, he inserts the account of the Forerunner. For what else *is fitting* than that John, being the Forerunner, should have also the account concerning his own birth running ahead of the Lord's in-fleshly birth?
