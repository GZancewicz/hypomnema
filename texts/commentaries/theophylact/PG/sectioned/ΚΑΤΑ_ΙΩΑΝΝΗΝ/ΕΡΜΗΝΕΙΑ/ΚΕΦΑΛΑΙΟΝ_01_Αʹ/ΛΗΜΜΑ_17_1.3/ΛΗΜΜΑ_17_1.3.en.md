# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 17 · John 1:3

> **Are not his brothers and his sisters with us? (Matt. 13:56)**

in place of, they dwell among us. So also here the word—[^1]

[^1]: Theophylact adduces the Gospel phrase «πρὸς ἡμᾶς εἰσιν» ("are with us") as a parallel use of «πρός» meaning "with, in the company of" (μεθ᾽ ἡμῶν, "among us"). The wording combines the "brothers... and sisters" of Matt. 13:55–56 (cf. Mark 6:3).
