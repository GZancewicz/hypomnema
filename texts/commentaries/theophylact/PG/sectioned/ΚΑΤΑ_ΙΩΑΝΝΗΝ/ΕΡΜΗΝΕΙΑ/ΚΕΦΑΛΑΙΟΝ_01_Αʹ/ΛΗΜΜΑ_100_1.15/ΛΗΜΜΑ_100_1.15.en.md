# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 100 · John 1:15

> **the one coming after me, (John 1:15)**

namely according to the times of birth; for the forerunner was six months prior to Christ according to the birth in flesh.[^1]

[^1]: The OCR reads νέννῃσιν, a corruption of γέννησιν ("birth"); likewise πρόδρομος = "forerunner."
