# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 176 · John 1:35

> **On the next day again John was standing, and two of his disciples, and[^1] (John 1:35)**

[^1]: The lemma breaks off mid-sentence. The OCR reads «καὶ ἐν τῶν μαθητῶν αὐτοῦ δύο», where ἐν is a corruption of ἐκ, giving «καὶ ἐκ τῶν μαθητῶν αὐτοῦ δύο» ("and two of his disciples").
