# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 30 · John 1:3

> **«Earth you are,» (John 1:3)**[^1]

which also he assumed,[^2] and he loosed us who were fettered by the entanglements of our own sins, and [who are] sons of those condemned to death, of Adam and of Eve, and he proclaimed in Zion the name of the Lord. For, standing in the temple, he was teaching about his Father; as he himself also says:

[^1]: The quotation is Genesis 3:19 (Γῆ εἶ, "Earth you are"). The (John 1:3) locator marks the lemma under discussion.
[^2]: Greek OCR ἓν, read as the relative ἣν (feminine accusative), referring to the flesh/earth ("Earth you are") just mentioned: "which [flesh] he also assumed [προσείληφεν]."
