# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 177 · John 1:36

> **looking upon Jesus as he walked, he says: Behold the lamb of God. And the two disciples heard him speaking, and they followed Jesus. (John 1:36)**

Because of the sluggishness of those who heard, John is compelled to say the same things, so that by the very persistence of the testimony he may at least accomplish something. Therefore neither was he proved false, but he brought two disciples to Christ. For being in truth a bridal escort,[^1] he did everything so as to bring human nature to the bridegroom; wherefore Christ is silent as a bridegroom, but the go-between utters everything. And the Lord comes to the multitude as a bridegroom too. For in marriages it is not the bride who goes off to the bridegroom, but he to her, even were he a king's son. Wherefore the Lord also, being about to wed our nature, came down to it upon the earth, and the marriage having taken place, he took it, being taken up into the paternal house.

[^1]: Greek νυμφαγωγός, the friend who escorts the bride to the bridegroom; below the same office is termed πρόξενος, the go-between or broker of the match.
