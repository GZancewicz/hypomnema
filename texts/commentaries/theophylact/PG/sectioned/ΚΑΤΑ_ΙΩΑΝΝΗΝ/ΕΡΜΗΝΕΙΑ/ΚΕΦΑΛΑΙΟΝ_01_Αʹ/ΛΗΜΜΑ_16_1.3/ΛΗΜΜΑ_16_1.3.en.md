# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 16 · John 1:3

> **with [πρός] (John 1:3)**

you ought to understand in place of "with" [μετά], as it also stands elsewhere:[^1]

[^1]: Theophylact glosses the preposition «πρός» (lit. "toward, in relation to") in ἦν πρὸς τὸν Θεόν by the preposition «μετά» ("with, together with"). English "with" collapses the two Greek prepositions he is carefully distinguishing; the point is that here «πρός» has the sense of accompaniment/company, not motion toward.
