# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 106 · John 1:15

> **has come to be before me, (John 1:15)**

that is, he became more glorious and more honored than me from the wonders that came to pass concerning him—from the birth, from the upbringing, from the wisdom. And reasonably so. For indeed he was prior to me,[^1] according to the birth from the Father before the ages, even if he came after me according to the presence in flesh.

[^1]: πρῶτός μου ἦν, a genitive of comparison indicating temporal priority ("was prior to me / existed before me"), kept distinct from ἔμπροσθέν μου γέγονεν ("has come to be before me") of rank and precedence.
