# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 203 · John 1:44

> **Was not our heart burning within us, as He was speaking to us on the way? (Luke 24:32)**

And then also, because Philip had a heart much occupied with care,[^1] and continually meditated on the things of Moses, and always awaited the Christ, immediately, when he saw, he was persuaded; wherefore also he says:

[^1]: μεμεριμνημένην καρδίαν ἔχειν — literally "to have a heart that had been made anxious/careful," i.e. a heart given over to earnest, preoccupied thought (from μεριμνάω, "to be anxious, to take thought"). Here in a positive sense: a heart intent on and pondering the Scriptures. OCR: line 5 κπείσθη read as ἐπείσθη, "he was persuaded."
