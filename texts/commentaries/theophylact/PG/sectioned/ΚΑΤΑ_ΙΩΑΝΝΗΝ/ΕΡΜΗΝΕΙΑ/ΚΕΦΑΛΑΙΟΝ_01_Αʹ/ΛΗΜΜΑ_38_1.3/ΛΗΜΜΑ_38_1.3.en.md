# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 38 · John 1:3

> **"All things came to be through him." (John 1:3)**

Moses, having treated of the visible creation, made nothing clear to us concerning the intelligible things;[^1] but the evangelist, having comprehended all things in one word, says:

[^1]: «τῶν νοητῶν» — the objects of thought, the intelligible (as opposed to «ὁρατῆς κτίσεως», the visible creation). The Greek shows two certain OCR slips corrected here: «κτίαεως» for «κτίσεως» and «ἐνὶ» for «ἑνὶ» ("in one").
