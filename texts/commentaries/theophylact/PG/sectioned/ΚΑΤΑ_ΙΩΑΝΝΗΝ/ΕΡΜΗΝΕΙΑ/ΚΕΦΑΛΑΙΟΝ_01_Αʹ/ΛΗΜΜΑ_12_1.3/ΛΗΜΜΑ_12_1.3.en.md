# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 12 · John 1:3

> **In the beginning was; (John 1:1)**

from which I show[^1] that the Word is eternally, and not afterward, as you prate. But for what reason did he not say, «In the beginning was the Son», but—[^2]

[^1]: The OCR reads «ἐξ αὗ ψοῶ», for which I read «ἐξ οὗ δηλῶ» (“from which I show/make clear”); the verb form ψοῶ is corrupt and the reconstruction is conjectural. Also «ἀδίως» for ἀϊδίως (“eternally”).
[^2]: The clause breaks off at «ἀλλ´,», to be completed by the following fragment; Theophylact is about to explain why the Evangelist wrote «the Word» rather than «the Son».
