# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 148 · John 1:27

> **For he did no sin, neither was guile found in his mouth. (John 1:27)**[^1]

And the sandal is the coming of the Lord to us; and its strap is the manner of the incarnation, and of how the Word of God was bound together with the body. This manner, therefore, it is impossible to loose;[^2] for who is able to loose[^2] how God was bound together with the body?

[^1]: The words quoted are not from John but from Scripture cited by Theophylact—Isaiah 53:9 (LXX), echoed at 1 Peter 2:22: «ἀμαρτίαν οὐκ ἐποίησεν, οὐδὲ εὑρέθη δόλος ἐν τῷ στόματι αὐτοῦ».
[^2]: A play on «λύειν»: throughout it means to "loose/untie" the sandal-strap, but here «τίς δύναται λῦσαι πῶς…» also carries the sense "who can solve/explain how God was bound to the body"—the untying is at once an explaining.
