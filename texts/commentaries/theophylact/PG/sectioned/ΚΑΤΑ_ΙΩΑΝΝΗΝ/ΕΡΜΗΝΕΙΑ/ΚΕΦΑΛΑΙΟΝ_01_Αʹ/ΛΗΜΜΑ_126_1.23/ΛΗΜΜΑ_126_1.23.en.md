# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 126 · John 1:23

> **"Are you the prophet?" (John 1:23)**

He denies this too, and yet he was a prophet. How then does he deny it? How? Because they did not ask him, "Are you a prophet?"[^1] but rather —

[^1]: The distinction turns on the article: they asked Ὁ προφήτης εἶ σύ; ("Are you *the* prophet?" — the awaited eschatological Prophet), not the anarthrous Προφήτης εἶ σύ; ("Are you *a* prophet?"). John, a prophet, could rightly deny being *the* Prophet.
