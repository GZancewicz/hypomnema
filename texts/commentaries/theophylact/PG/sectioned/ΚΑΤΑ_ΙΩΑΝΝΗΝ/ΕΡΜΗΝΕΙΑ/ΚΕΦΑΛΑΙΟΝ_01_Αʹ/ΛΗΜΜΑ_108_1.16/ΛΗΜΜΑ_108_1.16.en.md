# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 108 · John 1:16

> **Not because you were numerous, but because of your fathers did I choose you. (Deut. 7:7)**[^1]

The ancients, then, were received by grace; and we too, confessedly, are saved by grace.

[^1]: A loose paraphrase conflating Deut. 7:7–8 (οὐχ ὅτι πολυπληθεῖτε … ἀλλὰ παρὰ τὸ ἀγαπᾶν κύριον ὑμᾶς … καὶ διατηρῶν τὸν ὅρκον, ὃν ὤμοσεν τοῖς πατράσιν ὑμῶν). Cited by Theophylact within his exposition of John 1:16 (the lemma reference).
