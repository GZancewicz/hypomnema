# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 215 · John 1:51

> **he says to him: "Amen, amen I say to you, from now on you shall see heaven opened, and the angels of God ascending and descending upon the Son of Man." (John 1:51)**

Prophecy is most powerful for drawing some toward faith, and it has a greater power than the miracles. For the miracles even the demons are able to counterfeit in a phantasmal way; but the foreknowledge of things to come and their prediction no one has exactly—neither an angel, nor much more the demons. Wherefore the Lord also drew Nathanael to himself, having told him both the place, and that Philip had called him, and that he is truly an Israelite. Having heard these things, therefore, Nathanael comes to a perception of the Lord's majesty, in so far as was then possible, and confesses him Son of God. Yet even if he confesses him Son of God, still not as Peter did. For Peter confessed him Son of God as true God; wherefore he is also blessed, and is entrusted with the Church.
