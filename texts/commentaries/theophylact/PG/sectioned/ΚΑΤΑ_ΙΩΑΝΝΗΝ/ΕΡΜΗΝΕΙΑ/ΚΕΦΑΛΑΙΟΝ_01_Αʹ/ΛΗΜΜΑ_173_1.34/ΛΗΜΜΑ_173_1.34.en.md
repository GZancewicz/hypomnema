# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 173 · John 1:34

> **I did not know him, (John 1:34)**

understand it thus, that for a long time before, and before the baptism, he did not know him; but at the time of the baptism, then he came to know him. Or it is also possible to say otherwise, that he did indeed know him, that he is Christ; but that he himself is the one who will baptize in the Holy Spirit, this he learned then, when he saw the Spirit descending.[^1] Saying, therefore, that[^2]

[^1]: The OCR reads «ἴδε κασαβαῖνον», recoverable as «ἴδε καταβαῖνον» ("saw descending"); and «Ἡ» in line 4 for «Ἢ» ("Or").
[^2]: The fragment breaks off here in the source: «Λέγων οὖν, ὅτι» ("Saying, therefore, that—").
