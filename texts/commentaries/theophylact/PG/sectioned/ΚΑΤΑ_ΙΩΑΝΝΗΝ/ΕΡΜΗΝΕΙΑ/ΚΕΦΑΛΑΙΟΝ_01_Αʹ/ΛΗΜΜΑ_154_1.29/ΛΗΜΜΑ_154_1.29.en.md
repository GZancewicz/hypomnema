# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 154 · John 1:29

> **Behold the Lamb of God, (John 1:29)**

the saying is as though addressed to those desiring to see the lamb proclaimed by Isaiah (Isa. 53:7). Behold the lamb, he says, the one being sought—that lamb, behold, is present.
