# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 147 · John 1:27

> **has come to be before me, (John 1:27)**

means: more honored, more glorious—and to such a degree that I am not even numbered among the least of his servants; for the loosing of the sandal belongs to the lowliest service. And I know, having read in one of the saints, such an interpretation as this: The sandal is everywhere taken for the flesh of sinners, which is subject to corruption; and the strap, that is, the thong, for the bond of sins. John, then, in the case of the others—those going out to him and confessing—had power to loose the strap of sins; for bound tight by the cords of their own sins they went out to him; and by calling them to repentance he prepared the way for them wholly[^1] to cast off this strap and the sinful sandal. But in the case of Christ, finding no strap—that is, no bond of sin—he was reasonably not even able to loose it. For how *could he loose* what he did not find?

[^1]: OCR here reads «ελείως», restored as «[τε]λείως» ("completely, wholly").
