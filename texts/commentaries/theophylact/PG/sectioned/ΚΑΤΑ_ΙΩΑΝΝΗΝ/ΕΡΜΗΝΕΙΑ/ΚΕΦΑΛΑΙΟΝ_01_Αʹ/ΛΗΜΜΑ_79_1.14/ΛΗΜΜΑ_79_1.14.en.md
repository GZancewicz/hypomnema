# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 79 · John 1:14

> **And all flesh shall see the salvation of God. (Luke 3:6)**[^1]

For, being about to speak of every man, *the prophet* made use of the name of "flesh." Thus therefore the evangelist also, instead of saying, "The Word became man," said,

[^1]: The lemma reproduces Luke 3:6 verbatim (καὶ ὄψεται πᾶσα σὰρξ τὸ σωτήριον τοῦ Θεοῦ), which itself cites Isaiah 40:5 in the LXX. Theophylact adduces it to establish that Scripture uses "flesh" (σάρξ) to mean "man," preparing his reading of John 1:14.
