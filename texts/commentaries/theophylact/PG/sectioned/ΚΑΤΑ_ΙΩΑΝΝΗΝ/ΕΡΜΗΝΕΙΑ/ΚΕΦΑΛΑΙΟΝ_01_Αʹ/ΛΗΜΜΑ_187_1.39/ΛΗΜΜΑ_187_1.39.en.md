# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 187 · John 1:39

> **He has nowhere to lay his head, (cf. Matt. 8:20)**

he does not mean this, that he has no lodging at all, but that he has none of his own. If, therefore, he was staying in a house, he was not staying in *his own*, but in another's. And not in vain does the evangelist note the time—that[^1]

[^1]: The Greek breaks off here mid-sentence with ὅτι (the manuscript's ἄτι is an OCR corruption of ὅτι, "that/because"); the thought is completed in the following lemma: "...*that* «it was about the tenth hour», but in order that he might teach…".
