# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 41 · John 1:3

> **"All things came to be through him." (John 1:3)**

But what *are* "all things"? The things that came to be. As if he were saying: If anything is of the generated nature,[^1] that has come to be by the Word; but the Spirit is not of the generated nature, wherefore neither did it come to be by him. Therefore without the power of the Word nothing came to be that has come to be — that is to say, *nothing* that was of the generated nature.

[^1]: «τῆς γεννητῆς φύσεως» — "generated nature," i.e. that which has an origin, that which comes to be (as opposed to the uncreated Godhead). The argument turns on this: only what belongs to the originated order came to be through the Word; the Holy Spirit, not belonging to it, was not made.
