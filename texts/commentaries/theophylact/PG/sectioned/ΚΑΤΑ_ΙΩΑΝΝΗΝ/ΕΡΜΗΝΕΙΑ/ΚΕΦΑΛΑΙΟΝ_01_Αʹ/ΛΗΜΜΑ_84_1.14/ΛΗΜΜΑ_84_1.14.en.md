# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 84 · John 1:14

> **And he dwelt among us. (John 1:14)**[^1]

Since above he said, that

[^1]: Greek καὶ ἐσκήνωσεν ἐν ἡμῖν, from σκηνόω, "to pitch a tent, tabernacle" — hence "he tabernacled among us." The fragment breaks off here (Ἐπειδὴ ἄνω εἶπεν, ὅτι, "Since above he said, that"); the exposition continues in the following lemma.
