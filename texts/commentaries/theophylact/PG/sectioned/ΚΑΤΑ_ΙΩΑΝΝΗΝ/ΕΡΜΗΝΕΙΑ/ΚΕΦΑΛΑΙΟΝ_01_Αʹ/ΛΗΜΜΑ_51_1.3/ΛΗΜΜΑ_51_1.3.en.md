# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 51 · John 1:3

> **And the darkness did not comprehend it. (John 1:5)**

For neither did death prevail over him, nor error. For this light is unconquerable — the God-Word, I mean. But some have considered the flesh and *this* life to be the darkness. And so the Word, having come to be in flesh and in this life, shone; and the darkness — the opposing power, I mean — did indeed make trial of[^1] and pursue the light, but found it invincible and unconquered. And the flesh is called darkness, not as being such by nature — far be it! — but as *being so* on account of sin. For the flesh, when moved naturally, has nothing evil at all; but when moved contrary to nature, and having served toward sin, it both becomes and is called darkness.

[^1]: OCR «ἐποίρασε»; read «ἐπείρασε» ("tempted, made trial of, assailed").
