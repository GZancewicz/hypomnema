# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 210 · John 1:50

> **Can anything good be from Nazareth? But Philip—[^1] (John 1:50)**

[^1]: This fragment repeats Nathanael's question of John 1:46 (Ἐκ Ναζαρὲτ δύναταί τι ἀγαθὸν εἶναι;) and breaks off at Ὁ δὲ Φίλιππος ("But Philip"); it continues the exposition of the preceding lemma (209) and appears truncated in the source.
