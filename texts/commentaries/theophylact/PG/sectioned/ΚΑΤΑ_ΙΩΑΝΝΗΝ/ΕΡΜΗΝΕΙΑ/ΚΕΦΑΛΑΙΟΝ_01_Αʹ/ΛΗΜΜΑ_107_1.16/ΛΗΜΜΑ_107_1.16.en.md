# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 107 · John 1:16

> **And of his fullness we all received, and grace instead of grace. (John 1:16)**

And these too are words of the Forerunner, speaking concerning Christ, that "We all, the prophets, received of his fullness." For he does not have grace in such measure as the Spirit-bearing men, but, being a fountain of every good and of all wisdom and prophecy, he overflows it upon all the worthy, and in overflowing remains full, and is never emptied. And we received grace—namely that of the New Covenant—instead of the grace of the old legislation. For since that Covenant grew old and decayed, in place of it we received the New.
