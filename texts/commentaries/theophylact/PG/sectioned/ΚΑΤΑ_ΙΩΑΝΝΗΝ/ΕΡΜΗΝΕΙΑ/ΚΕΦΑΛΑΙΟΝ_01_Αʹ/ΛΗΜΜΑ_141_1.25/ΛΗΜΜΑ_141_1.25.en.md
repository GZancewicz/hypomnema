# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 141 · John 1:25

> **Who are you? (John 1:25)**

—as if saying: Who are you who undertake so great a work, that of baptizing and cleansing those who make confession? For indeed the Jews seem to me to be wishing that not even John should seem to the many to be Christ, but out of envy and malice to question him:
