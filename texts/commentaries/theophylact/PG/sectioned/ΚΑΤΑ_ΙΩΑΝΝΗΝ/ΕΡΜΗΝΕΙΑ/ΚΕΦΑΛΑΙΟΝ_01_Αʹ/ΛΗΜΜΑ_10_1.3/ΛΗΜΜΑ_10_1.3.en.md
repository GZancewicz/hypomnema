# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 10 · John 1:3

> **In the beginning was the Word. (John 1:1)**

What, then, is the fellowship of the—[^1]

[^1]: Theophylact begins his reply, contrasting the Genesis and John openings. The sentence breaks off at «τῷ», its complement being the quoted word «ἐποίησε» (“created”) supplied by the following fragment: “What fellowship has the *«created»* with the *«was»*?”
