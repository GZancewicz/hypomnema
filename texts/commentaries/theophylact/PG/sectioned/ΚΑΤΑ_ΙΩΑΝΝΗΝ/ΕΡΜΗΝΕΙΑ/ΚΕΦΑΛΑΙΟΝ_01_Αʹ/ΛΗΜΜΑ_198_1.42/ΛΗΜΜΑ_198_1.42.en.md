# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 198 · John 1:42

> **you shall be called. (John 1:42)**

For he did not wish from the beginning to display the marks of his authority, since they did not yet have firm faith concerning him. And for what reason does he surname this one Peter, but the sons of Zebedee "sons of thunder"? So that he might show that he himself is the one who also gave the Old *Testament* — he who changes the names now, just as then — having called Abram "Abraham," and Sara "Sarah." And know that Simon is interpreted[^1]

[^1]: The fragment breaks off here mid-sentence; the etymology of "Simon" (ἐρμηνεύεται, "is interpreted as") is left incomplete in the source and continues in the following lemma.
