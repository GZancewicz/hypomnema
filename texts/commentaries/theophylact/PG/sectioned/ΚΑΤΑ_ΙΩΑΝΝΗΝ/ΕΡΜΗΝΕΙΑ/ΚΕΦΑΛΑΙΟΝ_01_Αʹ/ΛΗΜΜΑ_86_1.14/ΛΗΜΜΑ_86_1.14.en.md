# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 86 · John 1:14

> **He dwelt among us, (John 1:14)**

so that he might show the two natures, both ours and that of the Word. For just as the tent is of one nature, and he who dwells in it is of another,[^1] so also the Word, being said to dwell among us — that is, in our nature — would be of another nature beside ours. Let the Armenians be ashamed, who champion one nature. We learn, therefore, through the phrase "The Word became flesh," that the Word himself became man, and being Son of God, he became also son of a woman — who is also properly called Theotokos [i.e. God-bearer], as having given birth to God in flesh.
