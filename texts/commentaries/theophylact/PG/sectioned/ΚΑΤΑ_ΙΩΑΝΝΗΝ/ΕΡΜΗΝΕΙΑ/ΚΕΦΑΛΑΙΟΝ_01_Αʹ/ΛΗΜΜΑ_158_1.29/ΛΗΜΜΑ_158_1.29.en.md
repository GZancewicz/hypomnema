# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 158 · John 1:29

> **sin, (John 1:29)**

In saying *«*sin*,»* he spoke universally,[^1] just as we are accustomed to say that "man has fallen away," instead of "all humanity *has fallen away* from God"; so then here too, having said "the sin," he indicated all sins. But perhaps, since man, having disobeyed God, was flung headlong into the passions, the sin of the world was the disobedience, which the Lord took away out of the midst, having become obedient unto death, and having healed the contrary by its contrary.

[^1]: Greek καθολικῶς εἶπεν — "he spoke catholically / in a universal sense," i.e. he used the singular "sin" to denote sins collectively.
