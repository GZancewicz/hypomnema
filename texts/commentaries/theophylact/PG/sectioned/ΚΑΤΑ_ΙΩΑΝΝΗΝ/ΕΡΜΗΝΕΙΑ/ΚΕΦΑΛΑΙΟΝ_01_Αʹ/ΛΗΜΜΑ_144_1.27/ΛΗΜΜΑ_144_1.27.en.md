# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 144 · John 1:27

> **he it is who comes after me, who has come to be before me, of whom I am not worthy to loose the strap of his sandal. (John 1:27)**

Behold both the gentleness of the saint and the truth: the gentleness, in that he utters nothing harsh toward them, although they were abusive; and the truth, in that he is an outspoken witness of the glory of Christ, and does not conceal the glory of the Lord while procuring good repute for himself, but confesses that: I baptize a lowly baptism; for it is in water only, which does not possess remission of sins, but which prepares beforehand for the receiving of the spiritual baptism, the baptism that bestows the remission of sins.
