# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 101 · John 1:15

> **This one has come to be before me. (John 1:15)**

instead of, he became more preferred than me and more glorious. Why? Because he was also prior to me according to the divinity. But the Arians interpreted this saying senselessly. For wanting to show that the Son of God was not begotten from the Father, but came to be as one of the creatures, they say that: Behold, John testifies about him the[^1]

[^1]: The lemma breaks off mid-clause; the citation continues in the next fragment. OCR corrections in this section: Οἰ → Οἱ, ἰέλοντες → θέλοντες ("wanting"), ἐγενγήθη → ἐγεννήθη ("was begotten"). The verb γέγονεν is rendered "has come to be" throughout to keep the contrast the Arians exploit between "become/come to be" and "be begotten."
