# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 82 · John 1:14

> **The Word became flesh. (John 1:14)**

For is he [i.e. Nestorius] not manifestly refuted here? For the Word himself became man. He did not say, "The Word, having found a man, was conjoined to him," but, "He himself became man." And by this Eutyches too, and Valentinus, and Manes are overthrown. For these said that the Word of God appeared *only* in phantasy.[^1] Let them hear, therefore, that

[^1]: Greek κατὰ φαντασίαν, "according to phantasy" — that is, that the incarnation was mere seeming appearance rather than a real assumption of flesh (the docetic error).
