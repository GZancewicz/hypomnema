# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 188 · John 1:39

> **It was about the tenth hour, (John 1:39)**

—but rather in order that he might teach both those who teach and those eager to learn,[^1] that one must not defer on account of the time, but that the teacher too should not put it off and say, "It is late; tomorrow you will learn," and that the disciple should recognize every time as a time for learning, and not defer the hearing until the morrow. And this too we learn: that they kept their belly so unburdened and were so sober, that the time which others spend on rest of the body—being weighed down, no doubt, with foods, and unable to set their hand to any earnest work—this *time* they themselves expended on hearing. Truly they were disciples of John the abstemious.[^2] And observe for me, that Jesus turns to those following him, and shows them his own face. For unless through good practice you have followed Jesus, you would not attain to the contemplation of the face of the Lord, nor would you go away to his abode—that is, you will not attain to the illumination of divine knowledge; for the house of Christ is the light.

[^1]: Greek τοὺς μαθητιῶντας, a desiderative form of μανθάνω, literally "those desiring to learn / minded to become disciples."
[^2]: Greek Ἰωάννου τοῦ ἀτρόφου, literally "John the ill-nourished / unfed," referring to the Baptist's ascetic, fasting manner of life.
