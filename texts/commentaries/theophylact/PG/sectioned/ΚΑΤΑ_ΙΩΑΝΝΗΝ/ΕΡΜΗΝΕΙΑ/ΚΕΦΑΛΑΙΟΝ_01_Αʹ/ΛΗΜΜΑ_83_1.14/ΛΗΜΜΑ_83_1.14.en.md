# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 83 · John 1:14

> **The Word became flesh. (John 1:14)**

He did not say that flesh was made to appear in phantasy, or seemed *so* by a phantasm.[^1] For it is absurd and senseless to believe that the Son of God, who is and is called Truth, falsified his becoming-man; for such is phantasy—an altogether false showing.

[^1]: The Greek here is damaged by OCR: «Οὐκ εἶπεν, ὅτι Σὰρξ ἐφαντάσθη, ἢ ἔδοξεν, [φαν]τασίᾳ», where the reading τασίᾳ preserves only the tail of φαντασίᾳ ("by a phantasm/appearance"). The reconstruction is secure from the parallel vocabulary (ἐφαντάσθη, φαντασία) in the surrounding lines.
