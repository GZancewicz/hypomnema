# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 95 · John 1:14

> **All marveled at the words of grace proceeding out of his mouth. (Luke 4:22)**

And to all he freely bestowed[^1] the healings, to those in need of them. And he was full of truth, inasmuch as the prophets also, and Moses himself—whatever they said or did—all were types; but Christ, what he said and did was all full of truth, being himself grace-itself and truth-itself,[^2] and supplying these to others. But where did they behold the glory? Perhaps some will suppose that it was on the mountain of Tabor. And perhaps this too is true; only, not on the mountain alone, but in all that he did and said they saw his glory.

[^1]: Greek «ἐχαρίζετο», from the same root χάρις ("grace") that governs the whole passage: "he graciously gave, bestowed as a favor."
[^2]: Greek «αὐτόχαρις ὢν καὶ αὐτοαλήθεια» — compound self-designations, "grace-itself" and "truth-itself," i.e. he is grace and truth in their very essence, not by participation.
