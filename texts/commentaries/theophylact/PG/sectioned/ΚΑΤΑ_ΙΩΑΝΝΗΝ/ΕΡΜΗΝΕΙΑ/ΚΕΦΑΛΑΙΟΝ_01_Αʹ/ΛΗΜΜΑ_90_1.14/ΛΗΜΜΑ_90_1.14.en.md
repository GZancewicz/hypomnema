# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 90 · John 1:14

> **We beheld his glory, (John 1:14)**

*he* being, clearly, in flesh. For if the Israelites could not look upon the face of Moses, made radiant from his converse with God, much more would the apostles not have been able to contain the naked divinity of the Only-begotten, had it not appeared through the flesh. But we beheld glory not such as Moses had, or such glory as that with which the Cherubim and Seraphim appeared to the prophet, but such glory as was fitting to be for an only-begotten Son, belonging to him naturally from the Father.
