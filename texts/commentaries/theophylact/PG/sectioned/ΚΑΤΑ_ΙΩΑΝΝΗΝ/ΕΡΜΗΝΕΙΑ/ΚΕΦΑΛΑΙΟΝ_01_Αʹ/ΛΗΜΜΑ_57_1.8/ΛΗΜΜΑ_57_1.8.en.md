# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 57 · John 1:8

> **That one was not the light, but *came* that he might bear witness concerning the light. (John 1:8)**

Since it for the most part happens that the one bearing witness is greater than the one witnessed to, lest you should think that John also, in bearing witness concerning Christ, was greater than he — removing this wicked suspicion, he says: That one was not the light. But someone might say: Then shall we call neither John nor any other of the saints light? We may indeed call each of the saints a light; but we would not call them the light with the article. For instance, if someone should ask you: Is John a light? — nod assent; but if he should ask thus: Is John the light? — say, No. For he is not the light properly, but a light by participation, having his radiance from the true light.
