# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 70 · John 1:12

> **"Who were begotten not of bloods, nor of the will of the flesh, nor of the will of man, but of God." (John 1:12)**[^1]

He makes, as it were, a comparison of the divine and the fleshly birth—not without reason reminding us of the fleshly birth-pangs, but in order that we, having recognized through the comparison the ignobility and lowliness of the fleshly birth, might run toward the divine grace. He says, then:[^2]

[^2]: Φησὶ γοῦν (OCR "νοῦν"); the sentence runs on into the scripture quote of the next section.
