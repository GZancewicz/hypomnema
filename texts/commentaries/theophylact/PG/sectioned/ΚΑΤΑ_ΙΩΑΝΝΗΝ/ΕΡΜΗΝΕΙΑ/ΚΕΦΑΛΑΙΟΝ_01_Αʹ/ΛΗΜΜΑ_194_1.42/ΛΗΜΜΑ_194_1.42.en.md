# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 194 · John 1:42

> **the Messiah, (John 1:42)**

with the article — that very one who is properly the Christ. For the one expected by them was one, even if many were called christs [i.e. anointed ones] and sons of God. And he led[^1] him to Jesus, not as being easy in his disposition and carried along with every word, but rather as sharp and fervent, and as one who had aptly received the words which his brother had spoken to him about Christ. For it is likely that Andrew discoursed more with him, and accurately declared the things concerning Christ, inasmuch as he had remained with Christ and been taught certain more mystical things.
