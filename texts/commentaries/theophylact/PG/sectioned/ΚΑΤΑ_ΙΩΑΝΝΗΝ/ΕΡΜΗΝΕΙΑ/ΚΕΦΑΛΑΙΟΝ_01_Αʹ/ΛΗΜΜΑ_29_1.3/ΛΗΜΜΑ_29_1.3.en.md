# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 29 · John 1:3

> **«The Lord looked down upon the earth, to hear the groaning, and to loose those condemned to death, and to proclaim in Zion the name of the Lord,» (John 1:3)**[^1]

whom else does it indicate than the Son of God? For this one looked down upon the earth—whether this [earth] on which we move, or our earthy nature,[^2] or the flesh—according to the [saying],

[^1]: The quotation is Psalm 101:20–22 (LXX; 102:19–21). "to loose those condemned to death" renders λῦσαι τοὺς τεθανατωμένους. The (John 1:3) locator marks the lemma under discussion. OCR damage: line 1–2 read ἐπέβλεψεν ἐ-λ / 2ὴν γῆν, reconstructed as ἐπέβλεψεν ἐπὶ τὴν γῆν.
[^2]: Greek OCR corrupt: τὴν ἡμετέραν φύσιν τὴν γεω,εῖσαν, reconstructed as τὴν γεώδη ("the earthy"), i.e. "our earthy nature." The following lemma «Earth you are» (Gen. 3:19) confirms the sense.
