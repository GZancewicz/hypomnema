# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 58 · John 1:9

> **It was the true light, which enlightens every man coming into the world. (John 1:9)**

He is about to speak concerning the incarnate dispensation of the Only-begotten, that he came into his own, that he became flesh. Accordingly, lest anyone should think that before becoming flesh he was not, for this reason he leads the mind up to the existence which is beyond every beginning, and says: The true light *was* even before becoming flesh. And by this he overturns the heresy of Photinus and Paul of Samosata, who dogmatized that the Only-begotten came into being then, when he was born from Mary, but before this was not. Hear also you, O Arian, who do not call the Son of God true God, what the evangelist says:[^1]

[^1]: The sentence breaks off and continues in the following lemma («τὸ φῶς, τὸ ἀληθινόν·» — "the true light"), where the argument turns to the Manichaean.
