# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 43 · John 1:3

> **[?And the life was the light] of men.[^1] (John 1:3)**

The Pneumatomachi, then, read the present passage thus: "And without him was made not even one thing"; then, having placed a stop here, they read, as from another beginning: "What has come to be, in him was life"; and they interpret the passage according to their own mind, saying that here the evangelist is treating of the Spirit — saying, that is, that the Holy Spirit was life. These things say those around Macedonius, hastening to show the Holy Spirit to be a creature, and to number it together with the things that have come to be.
