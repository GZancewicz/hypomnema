# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 8 · John 1:3

> **in the beginning (Gen. 1:1)**

is understood, so neither here[^1] [is] the—

[^1]: Continuing the heretic’s argument from the previous fragment: as the Genesis *«in the beginning»* does not imply that heaven is eternal, so neither *here* (in John). The clause breaks off at «τὸ,», its object supplied by the following fragment’s «ἐν ἀρχῇ».
