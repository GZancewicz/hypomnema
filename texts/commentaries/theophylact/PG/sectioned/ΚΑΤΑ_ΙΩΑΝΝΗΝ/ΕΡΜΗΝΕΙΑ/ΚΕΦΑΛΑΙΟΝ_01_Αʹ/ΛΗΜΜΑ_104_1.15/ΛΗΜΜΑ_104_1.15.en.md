# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 104 · John 1:15

> **has come to be before me, (John 1:15)**

that is, *they say,* "was created before me, because he was prior to me?"[^1] For it is altogether senseless to say that God created this one earlier because he was prior to me. For, on the contrary, he ought rather to have said, "This one is prior to me, because he has come to be before me," that is, was created. These are the arguments of the Arians. But we understand orthodoxly, that—

[^1]: Theophylact turns on the distinction between two phrases of John's testimony: ἔμπροσθέν μου γέγονεν ("has come to be before me," rendered here of rank/precedence) and πρῶτός μου ἦν ("was prior to me," a genitive of comparison indicating temporal priority). The Arian reading collapses the two into a temporal-created sense; the orthodox reading (developed in the following lemmata) keeps them distinct.
