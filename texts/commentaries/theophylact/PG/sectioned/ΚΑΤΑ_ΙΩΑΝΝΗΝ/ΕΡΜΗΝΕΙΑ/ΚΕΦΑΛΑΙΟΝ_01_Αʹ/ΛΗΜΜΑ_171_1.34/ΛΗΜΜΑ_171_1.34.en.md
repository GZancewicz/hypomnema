# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 171 · John 1:34

> **I did not know him. (John 1:34)**

John, making his testimony concerning Christ free from suspicion, as I said, traces the testimony up to God and Father. For I, he says, did not know him, but the Father revealed this one to me at the baptism. But someone might ask: If John did not know him, how does Matthew say that[^1]

[^1]: The fragment breaks off here in the source; the objection is completed and answered in the lemmata that follow (the reference is to Matt. 3:14, where John recognizes the Lord and tries to prevent him). The name in line 6 is misspelled «Κωάννης» in the OCR for «Ἰωάννης».
