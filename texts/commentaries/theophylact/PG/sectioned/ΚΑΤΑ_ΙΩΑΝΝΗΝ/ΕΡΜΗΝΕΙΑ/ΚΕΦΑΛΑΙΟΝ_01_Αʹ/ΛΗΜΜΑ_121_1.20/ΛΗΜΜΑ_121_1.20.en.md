# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 121 · John 1:20

The Jews therefore sent to John those whom they regarded as their superiors; for *they sent* priests and Levites, and these too men of Jerusalem, so that these men, being of course the more intelligent, might persuade John through flattery to confess himself to be the Christ. But behold the perversity:[^1] they do not ask him straightaway, "Are you the Christ?" but rather —

[^1]: σκαιότητα, from σκαιότης, literally "left-handedness," hence "awkwardness, boorishness, crookedness of character"; here their malicious perversity in the manner of their questioning.
