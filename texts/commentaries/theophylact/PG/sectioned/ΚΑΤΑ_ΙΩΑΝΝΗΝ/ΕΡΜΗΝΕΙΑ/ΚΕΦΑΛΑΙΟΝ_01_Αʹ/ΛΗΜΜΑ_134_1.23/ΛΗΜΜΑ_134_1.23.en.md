# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 134 · John 1:23

> **I am the voice of one crying, (John 1:23)**

—might so interpret it: that I am the voice of Christ who cries out, that is, of him who clearly proclaims the truth. For all those of the Law are feeble of voice,[^1] inasmuch as the truth of the Gospel did not yet have its season; and this, then, was that "feebleness of voice" of Moses—the indistinctness and obscurity of the Law. But Christ, being as it were truth-itself, and having announced to us all the things of the Father, is one who cries out. John, therefore, says: I am the voice of the Word who cries out, dwelling in the wilderness. Then, from another beginning:

[^1]: «ἰσχνόφωνοι», "feeble/thin of voice," taking up Moses's self-description as «ἰσχνόφωνος» (Exod. 4:10 LXX, rendered "slow of speech"), and set in deliberate contrast with «βοῶν», "crying out (loudly)."
