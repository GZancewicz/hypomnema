# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 34 · John 1:3

—a maker,[^1] but not an instrument? But if, again, the [preposition διά][^2]

[^1]: This fragment continues the sentence begun in Lemma 33. OCR "«πωπτὴν" is read as the tail of ποιητήν, "maker" (accusative), matching the antithesis ἀλλ' οὐκ ὄργανον, "but not an instrument."

[^2]: OCR "Εἰ δὲ ὴλιν ἡ" read as Εἰ δὲ πάλιν ἡ, "But if, again, the…"; the sentence runs on into the next fragment (Lemma 35), where the subject is ἡ «διὰ» πρόθεσις, "the preposition διά." Supplied words are bracketed.
