# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 195 · John 1:42

> **he led him to Jesus, (John 1:42)**

which is rather the mark of a firm mind and not of one that is carried along. For he did not simply accept[^1] Andrew's words, but wished to see Christ also; so that, if he found something noteworthy, he might follow, but if not, he might turn back. So that his being led to Jesus is a token not of naivety but of carefulness. What, then, does the Lord do? By way of prophesying about him, he begins to reveal himself to him. For since prophecies persuade men no less than signs — if not even more — he prophesies about Peter.

[^1]: The OCR reads «παραλίξατο», a corruption of «παρεδέξατο» ("he accepted, received").
