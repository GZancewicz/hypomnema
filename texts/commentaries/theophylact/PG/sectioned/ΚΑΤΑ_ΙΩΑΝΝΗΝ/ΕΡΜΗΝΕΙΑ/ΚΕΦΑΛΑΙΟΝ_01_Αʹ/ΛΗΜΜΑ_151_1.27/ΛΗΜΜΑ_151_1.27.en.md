# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 151 · John 1:27

> **«in Bethabara,»** it reads;[^1] for Bethany is not beyond the Jordan, but is somewhere near Jerusalem. (John 1:27)

[^1]: Theophylact is noting a textual reading: his copy has «ἐν Βηθαβαρᾷ» ("in Bethabara") rather than "in Bethany," and he defends this on geographical grounds. The verb ἔχει ("it has/reads") refers to what the text of the Gospel contains.
