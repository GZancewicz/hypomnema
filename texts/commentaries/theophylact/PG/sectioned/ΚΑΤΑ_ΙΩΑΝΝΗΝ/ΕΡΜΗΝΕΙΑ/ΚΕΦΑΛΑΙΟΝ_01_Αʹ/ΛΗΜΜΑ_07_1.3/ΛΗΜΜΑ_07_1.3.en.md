# John 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 7 · John 1:3

> **In the beginning God created. (Gen. 1:1)**

For just as there[^1] the *«in the beginning»* is understood not as though the heaven were eternal — the—

[^1]: I.e. in Moses (Genesis). The OCR reads ἀδίου for ἀϊδίου (“eternal”): «οὐχ ὡς ἀ<ϊ>δίου ὄντος τοῦ οὐρανοῦ». The sentence runs on into the next fragment, where the phrase «ἐν ἀρχῇ» supplies the subject of «νοεῖται» (“is understood”).
