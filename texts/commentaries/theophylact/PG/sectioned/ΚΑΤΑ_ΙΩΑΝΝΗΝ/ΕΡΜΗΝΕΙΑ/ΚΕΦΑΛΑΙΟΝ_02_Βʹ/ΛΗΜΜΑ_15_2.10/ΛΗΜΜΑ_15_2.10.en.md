# John 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 15 · John 2:10

> **says to him: Every man first sets out the good wine, and when they have become drunk, then the inferior; you have kept the good wine until now. (John 2:10)**

The master of the feast does not ask the servants, but the bridegroom. For what reason? Assuredly according to a divine dispensation. For if he had asked the servants, they would certainly have disclosed the miracle; but no one would have believed them. For it was the beginning of the signs, and as yet no one held a great opinion concerning Christ; so that, even with the servants recounting the miracle, no one would have believed. After these things, however, when he had displayed other signs as well, this too was going to be believed, and little by little the servants, recounting this to all, would have seemed trustworthy. For this reason the Lord dispensed that not the servants be asked, but the bridegroom.

And he did not simply make wine, but the finest as well. For such are the miracles of Christ—far better than the things accomplished through nature. Of the water's having become wine, then, the witnesses are the servants, those who had drawn the water; but of its having become finest, the master of the feast—all which things, as time went on, being proclaimed, were going to be confirmed more firmly to those who heard.

And you can understand as wine the evangelical word, but as water all the things before the Gospel; for they were more watery, and did not attain to the perfection of the word. For instance, what do I mean? The Lord gave various laws to man: one in paradise, a second in Noah's time, a third in Abraham's time—that of circumcision—a fourth through Moses, a fifth through the prophets. All these therefore, when compared with the exactness and power of the Gospel, are watery, if one understands them simply and according to the letter. But if, going deep by the spirit, one perceives the hidden things, he will find the water become wine; for the things spoken simply and understood according to the letter by the many, being water, the one who examines spiritually will assuredly find to be the finest wine, drunk later and kept by the bridegroom Christ—since indeed in the last times the Gospel was shown forth.
