# John 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 25 · John 2:16

> **Do not make the house of my Father a house of merchandise. (John 2:16)**[^1]

For it was as yet the beginning of the signs, and there was not yet in him such great boldness from the display of the signs. But there, in Matthew, I mean:

[^1]: The OCR reads τὸν **οἶνον** ("the wine") τοῦ Πατρός μου; this is plainly a corruption of τὸν **οἶκον** ("the house"), as both John 2:16 (μὴ ποιεῖτε τὸν οἶκον τοῦ πατρός μου οἶκον ἐμπορίου) and the following exposition ("house") require. Translated as "house."
