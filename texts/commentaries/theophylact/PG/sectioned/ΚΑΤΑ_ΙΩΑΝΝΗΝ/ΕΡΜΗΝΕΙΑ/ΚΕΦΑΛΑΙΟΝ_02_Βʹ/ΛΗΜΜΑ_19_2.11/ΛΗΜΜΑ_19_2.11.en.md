# John 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 19 · John 2:11

> **his disciples believed in him. (John 2:11)**

*These words* show[^1] that he did no other miracle before this one — for from that *first miracle* he manifested his glory. Nevertheless it makes no difference to us, whether this one came first, or another. But how, then, did he manifest his glory? For neither did many attend to what had happened, nor did it take place before a crowd.
