# John 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 24 · John 2:16

> **The Passover was near,[^1] (John 2:16)**

he shows that not many days before the Passover he was baptized. And having gone up to Jerusalem, he does a deed full of great authority. For he casts out those selling the sheep and the oxen. But even if Matthew also says that he did this, yet know that he did not do it once only, but the one in Matthew he does near the Passion, whereas the one now in John he does when he is beginning the signs; wherefore here he spoke more mildly:[^2]

[^1]: The OCR reads Ἔν; read Ἦν, "was" (ἦν ἐγγὺς τὸ Πάσχα).
[^2]: ἀφειμένως: "in a relaxed/gentler manner." The point is that in John's account the Lord says only "a house of merchandise," milder than the "den of thieves" of the account near the Passion (Matt. 21:13).
