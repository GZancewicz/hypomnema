# John 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 20 · John 2:11

> **His disciples believed in him,[^1] (John 2:11)**

understand thus, that is: they received a fuller and more firm faith in him; since even before they altogether believed, but not so firmly.

[^1]: The OCR reads ἀπίστευσαν ("disbelieved"), which contradicts the sense and the lemma just expounded; read ἐπίστευσαν, "they believed," as Theophylact's whole comment requires.
