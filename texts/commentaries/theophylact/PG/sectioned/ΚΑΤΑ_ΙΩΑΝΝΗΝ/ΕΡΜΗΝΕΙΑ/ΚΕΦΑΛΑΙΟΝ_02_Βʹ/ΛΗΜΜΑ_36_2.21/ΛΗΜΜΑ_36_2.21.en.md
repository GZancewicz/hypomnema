# John 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 36 · John 2:21

> **"I will raise it up, I myself," (John 2:19)**

using his own power, and not needing another from outside. But the Jews, thinking that he was speaking about the inanimate[^1] temple, laugh him to scorn. "For it having been built over forty-six years, how," they say, "do you yourself say that you will raise it up in three days?" But one must know that, when the temple was first built by Solomon, everything was completed without deficiency in twenty years; but later, after the captivity, the Hebrews, having received permission to build it, began to rebuild under the reign of Cyrus; then, being hindered by those who bore ill-will toward them, they prolonged the building until the reign of Artaxerxes; and under him, enjoying full immunity, they were able with much zeal to complete the work — at which time indeed the Jews are said, while building, to have held in the one of their two hands a spear, and in the other a builder's tool; so greatly were they in fear, dreading the neighboring Idumeans, not indeed the Persians; for from those, as I said — I mean the Persians — they received[^2] full immunity. This building, then, the one after the captivity, they say came to be over forty-six years, inasmuch as the Jews were hindered, and on this account prolonged it from the reign of Cyrus up to that also of Artaxerxes. And it is no wonder if the Jews did not understand, seeing that not even the disciples did. For two very great things hindered them: one, the fact concerning the resurrection, which was hard to grasp — or rather altogether unknown to them; and the other, that it is God who indwells the body.
