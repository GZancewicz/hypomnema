# John 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 7 · John 2:5-7

> **His Mother says to the servants: Whatever he may say to you, do. Now there were there six stone waterpots set out according to the purification of the Jews, holding two or three measures apiece. Jesus says to them: Fill the waterpots with water. And they filled them up to the brim. (John 2:5-7)**[^1]

[^1]: μετρητάς — the *metretes*, a liquid measure of roughly nine gallons; rendered "measures" here.
