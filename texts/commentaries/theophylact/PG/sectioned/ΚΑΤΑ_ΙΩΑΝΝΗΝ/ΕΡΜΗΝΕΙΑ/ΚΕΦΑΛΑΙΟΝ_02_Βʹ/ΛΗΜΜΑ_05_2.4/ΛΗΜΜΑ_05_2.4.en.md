# John 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 5 · John 2:4

> **My hour has not yet come, (John 2:4)**

he says—not as being subject to the necessities of seasons, or as one observing an hour (for how *could he be*, the Maker of times and of ages?), but because he works all things with the fitting season. Since therefore he was still unmanifest, and unknown to the many, and did not even have all the disciples, and not even those at the marriage knew him—for otherwise they themselves would have come forward and entreated concerning the wine—since therefore all these things were so, for this reason he says:
