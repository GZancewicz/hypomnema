# John 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 17 · John 2:11

> **This beginning of signs did he. (John 2:11)**

But someone will say that, even if this is a beginning of signs, yet *it is* not of all, but of those in Cana of Galilee. For, he says, of the various miracles *he* performed in Cana, this is the beginning. These things one might say; but we, although having many *arguments* toward showing that it is the beginning of all the signs as well—for indeed the *phrase*,[^1]
