# John 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 28 · John 2:16

> **The zeal of your house has consumed me. (John 2:17)**[^1]

For, receiving little by little their advance toward the better, they already call to mind the Scriptures also, and, taking the testimonies from there, they establish for themselves a more secure knowledge concerning Christ.

[^1]: The disciples' remembrance is John 2:17; the words quoted are Psalm 69:9 (LXX 68:10), ὁ ζῆλος τοῦ οἴκου σου κατέφαγέ με.
