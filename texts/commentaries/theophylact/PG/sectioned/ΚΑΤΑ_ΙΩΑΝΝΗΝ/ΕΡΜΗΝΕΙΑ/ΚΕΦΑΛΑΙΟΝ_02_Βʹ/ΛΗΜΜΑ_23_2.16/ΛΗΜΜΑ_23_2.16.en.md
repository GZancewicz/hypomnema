# John 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 23 · John 2:16

> **to those selling doves he said: Take these things away from here, and do not make the house of my Father a house of merchandise. And his disciples remembered that it is written: The zeal of your house has consumed me. (John 2:16)**

The Lord goes down to Capernaum for no other need than to settle his mother there, so that he might not drag her about everywhere. For since they had come to Cana for the sake of the wedding, the wedding being over, he brings his mother back to her house in Capernaum. And this is clear — that for this reason he went down to Capernaum — from the fact that he neither remained there many days, nor did miracles in it. For those inhabiting this city were unbelieving toward him; wherefore also elsewhere the Lord pronounces woe upon it (cf. Matt. 11:23; Luke 10:15).[^1] And by the evangelist's saying that
