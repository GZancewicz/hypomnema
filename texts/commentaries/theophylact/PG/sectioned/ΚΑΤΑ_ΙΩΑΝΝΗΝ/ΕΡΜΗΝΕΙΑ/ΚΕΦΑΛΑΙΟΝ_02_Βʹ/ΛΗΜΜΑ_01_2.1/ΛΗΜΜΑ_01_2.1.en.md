# John 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 1 · John 2:1-3

> **And on the third day a marriage took place in Cana of Galilee, and the mother of Jesus was there. And Jesus also was invited, and his disciples, [to the marriage. And when the wine ran short, the][^1] mother of Jesus says to him: They have no wine. (John 2:1-3)**

[^1]: OCR gap: the source jumps from «καὶ οἱ μαθηταὶ αὐτοῦ» straight to «μήτηρ τοῦ Ἰησοῦ», dropping the clause «εἰς τὸν γάμον. Καὶ ὑστερήσαντος οἴνου, λέγει ἡ» ("to the marriage. And when the wine ran short, the [mother] says"). The bracketed English restores this standard Johannine text (the source also reads «ἄχουσι» for ἔχουσι).
