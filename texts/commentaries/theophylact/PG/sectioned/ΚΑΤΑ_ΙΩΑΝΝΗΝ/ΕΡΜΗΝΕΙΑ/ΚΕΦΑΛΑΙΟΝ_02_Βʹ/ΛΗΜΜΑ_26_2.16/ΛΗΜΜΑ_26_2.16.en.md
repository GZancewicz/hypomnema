# John 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 26 · John 2:16

> **Do not make *it*, he says, a den of robbers. (John 2:16)**[^1]

For openly he named them robbers, as gaining from unjust means. For when someone deems what is valued at little worthy of a great price, and gathers his profit perhaps from the poor and from widows — for these who buy up necessary things and sell them do, for the most part,[^2] what else than the work of a robber, transacting profits out of the misfortunes of others? But for what reason does he cast out such men altogether from the temple?[^3] Not without reason; but since he was about to heal on the Sabbath and to relax the law, in order that he might not seem to be opposed to God, he corrects from here beforehand such a supposition. For the one who displayed such great zeal on behalf of the house would not have set at naught God, the master of the house. For he did not simply cast *them* out, but also striking with a plaited scourge, and overturning the tables, and pouring out the small coin — that is, the currency of the money-changers — and undertaking a hazardous deed. Therefore the one who casts himself into danger on behalf of the house of God would not relax the law of God as one opposed to God, but assuredly as being Son, and having authority equal[^4] to God and Father, who also gave the law concerning the Sabbath.
