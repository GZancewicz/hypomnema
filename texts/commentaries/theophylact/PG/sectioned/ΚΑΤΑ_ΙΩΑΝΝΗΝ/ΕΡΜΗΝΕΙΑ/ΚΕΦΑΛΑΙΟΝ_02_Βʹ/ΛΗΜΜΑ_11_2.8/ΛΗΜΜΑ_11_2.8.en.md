# John 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 11 · John 2:8

> **Whatever he may say to you, do. (John 2:8)**

*She says this* so that the request might become the greater, when they had come forward and made their petition; that it might be shown that the refusal was not from weakness, but from not wishing to seem to rush upon the working of wonders for the sake of display and empty conceit.

And the stone waterpots served for[^1] the purification of the Jews. For the Jews washed themselves off nearly every day, and thus ate; for instance, if they had touched a leper, or a corpse, or if they had had intercourse with a woman, being thereafter unclean they would wash themselves off. Since, therefore, Palestine was waterless, and it was not possible in many places to find springs, they were always filling the waterpots with water, so as not to run to the rivers whenever they should become unclean.
