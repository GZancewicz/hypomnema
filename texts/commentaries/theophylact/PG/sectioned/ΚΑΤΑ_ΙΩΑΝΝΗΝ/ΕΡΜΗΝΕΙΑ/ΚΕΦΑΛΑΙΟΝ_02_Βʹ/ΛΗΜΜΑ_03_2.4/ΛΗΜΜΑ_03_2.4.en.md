# John 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 3 · John 2:4

> **Jesus says to her: What to me and to you, woman?[^1] My hour has not yet come. (John 2:4)**

They call the Lord to the marriage, not as having seen miracles, nor as *inviting* some great one, but simply and as an acquaintance; whence, signifying this, the evangelist says:

[^1]: «Τί ἐμοὶ καὶ σοί, γύναι;» — a Hebraism (cf. 2 Kingdoms/2 Sam. 16:10), literally "what to me and to you"; the sense is "what have I to do with you?" The literal wording is kept in the body.
