# John 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 13 · John 2:8

> **confession. (John 2:8)**

And the one who confesses is cleansed through the five senses, through which he formerly sinned. The eye saw wickedly, but the eye again weeps in the confession, and thus it comes to be for cleansing. The ear heard fornicating songs, but again it inclines toward the words of the mouth of God; so also with the other senses. Again, the reasoning faculty[^1] errs in its doctrines, but again it purges away its former evil, having passed over into holding right doctrine. And *they are* stone water-jars either because our constitution is from earth, or because the vessels that are to hold such wine must be firm and hard to break down. Paul's reasoning faculty was a stone water-jar—both when he persecuted, for he was a zealot for the ancestral traditions above all his contemporaries (Gal. 1:14), and when he preached, for he had such power in his words that he was even reckoned to be Hermes by the Lycaonians, as being the leader of the discourse (Acts 14:12).

[^1]: τὸ λογιστικόν, the rational or reasoning faculty (the calculating part of the soul); rendered consistently as "the reasoning faculty." Several words in this fragment are OCR-corrupt but recoverable from context: OCR φσματα → ᾄσματα ("songs"); στεῤῥ → στεῤῥά ("firm"); ἔτεσθαι → ἔσεσθαι; σοῖς Λυκεῦσιν → τοῖς Λυκαῦσιν ("by the Lycaonians").
