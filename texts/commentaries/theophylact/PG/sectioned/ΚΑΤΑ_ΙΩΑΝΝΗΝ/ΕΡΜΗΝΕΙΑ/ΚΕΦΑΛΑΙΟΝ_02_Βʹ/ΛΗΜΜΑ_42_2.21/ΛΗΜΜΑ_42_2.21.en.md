# John 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 42 · John 2:21

> **You will not abandon my soul unto Hades, (Ps. 15:10)**

what does it seem to you? But you, for your part, perish along with your own—[^1]

[^1]: The sentence is cut off and continues in the next lemma. Greek σὺ μὲν φθείρου (imperative), "you, be undone / go to ruin"; the μέν is answered by the δέ of the following fragment. The stray numeral «Γʹ» (3) at the end of the Greek is a section/column marker, not part of the text.
