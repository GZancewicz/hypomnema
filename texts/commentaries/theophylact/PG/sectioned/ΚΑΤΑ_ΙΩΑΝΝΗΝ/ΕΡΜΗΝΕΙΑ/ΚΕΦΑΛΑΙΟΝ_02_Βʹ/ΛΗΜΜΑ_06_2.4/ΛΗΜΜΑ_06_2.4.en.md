# John 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 6 · John 2:4

> **My hour has not yet come; (John 2:4)**

that is, the suitable season is not present. But still the madness of Arius[^1] presses on, trying to show the Lord as subject to hours and seasons. Learn then, O accursed one, that if he were subject to hours, how then did he work the miracle? For it would have been necessary, according to your argument—since he is a slave to hours and seasons, but his hour had not yet come—that he be unable to work a wonder; but indeed he did work the wonder; therefore he is not a slave to hours. And observe for me how he does not resist the Mother to the end, nor in all things; but having rebuked her a little, he again fulfills her request, honoring her, and giving us patterns of the reverence *due* toward those who begot us.

[^1]: «ἡ τοῦ Ἀρείου λύττα» (source «Ἀρείσυ»), the raging frenzy of Arius; here of the Arian argument that the Son is subject to time.
