# John 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 2 · John 2:2

> **And Jesus also was invited, and his disciples, [to the marriage. And when the wine ran short, the][^1] mother of Jesus says to him: They have no wine. (John 2:2)**

[^1]: OCR gap: the source runs «καὶ οἱ μαθηταὶ αὐτοῦ» directly into «μήτηρ τοῦ Ἰησοῦ», omitting «εἰς τὸν γάμον. Καὶ ὑστερήσαντος οἴνου, λέγει ἡ». The bracketed English restores this standard text (the source also reads «ἄχουσι» for ἔχουσι).
