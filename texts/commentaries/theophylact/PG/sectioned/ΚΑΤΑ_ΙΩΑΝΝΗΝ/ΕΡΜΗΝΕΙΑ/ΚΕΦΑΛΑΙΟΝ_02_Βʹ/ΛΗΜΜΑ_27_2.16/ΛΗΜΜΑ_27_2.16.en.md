# John 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 27 · John 2:16

> **The house of my Father; (John 2:16)**

showing that he exercises authority, as Son, over all that belongs to the Father. Now money-changers are those who sell the small coins, that is, the *nummi*. For *kollybos* is what the small coin is called among the Greeks, which the Romans name a *nummus*.[^1] Many even of the high priests commit such things as the hucksters did then, who sold the oxen in the Church — I mean, not securing those who excel in the word of teaching, but handing them over to those who wish to do evil; but also the sheep, that is, the simpler and common people, and the doves, I mean the spiritual gifts, selling them, and enrolling those who give more into the greater ranks — whom the Lord casts out of the temple, judging them unworthy of the high priesthood. But if anyone also sells the coin and currency, that is, doctrine and word, and, being perchance a teacher, does not proclaim the word unless he has profit, of this one too the Lord overturns the table — I mean the throne of teaching — and the word which that one holds back through wickedness, not giving it to all, the Lord Jesus pours out through love of mankind and gives to the peoples; removing that one from office, but seating another, the worthy one, upon the throne of teaching. And the disciples remembered that it is written:

[^1]: A lexical note on κόλλυβος / *nummus*; the Greek is retained here because the terms are discussed as words.
