# John 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 22 · John 3:12

> **"How?" (John 3:12)**

And he names this birth "earthly," as being accomplished upon earth for the benefit of the men upon earth (for even if it is heavenly according to grace and honor, yet, being on earth, we are baptized). If therefore, having spoken concerning this earthly birth, I found you disbelieving, concerning the heavenly birth — the ineffable one, by which the only-begotten Son was begotten from the Father — how, on hearing *of it*, will you believe? But some understood "earthly" *to be* the example of the wind, so that what is said is of this sort: If I gave you the illustration from earthly things, and not even so were you persuaded, how will you be able to learn the higher things?
