# John 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 23 · John 3:13

> **"And no one has ascended into heaven except the *one* having descended from heaven, the Son of man, who is in heaven." (John 3:13)**[^1]

Neither does this appear to have any connection with the things before it. But if one accurately observes the mind of the Lord, it will indeed appear very much to be in connection with the things before it. For since Nicodemus was naming the Lord a teacher and a prophet, "Do not suppose me," he says, "to have been sent from God to teach as a prophet who is from earth; but suppose me to have come down from above, as Son, not to be from earth. No one therefore of the prophets has ascended into heaven, except I alone am about to ascend, as also I descended." And having heard that the Son of man descended from heaven, do not suppose that the flesh descended from heaven — for this is the teaching of Apollinaris, who laid it down as dogma that Christ had the body from heaven, coming through the Virgin as through a pipe[^2] — but since the hypostasis is one, or rather Christ was one person composed out of two natures, the names of the man are said of the Word, and again the *names* of the Word are attributed to the man. Thus therefore here also the Son of man[^3] is said to have descended from heaven, as being one person and one hypostasis.
