# John 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 12 · John 3:8

> **"The wind blows where it wills, and you hear its sound, but you do not know whence it comes and whither it goes. So is everyone who is born of the Spirit." (John 3:8)**[^1]

The Lord, leading Nicodemus away from the fleshly birth, says:

[^1]: The verse turns on a single Greek word, πνεῦμα, which means both "wind" and "Spirit," together with the cognate verb πνεῖ ("blows/breathes"). "The πνεῦμα blows where it wills" reads naturally of the wind, yet the closing "everyone born of the πνεῦμα" points to the Spirit; the rendering keeps "wind" in the image and "Spirit" in the application, but the two are one word in the Greek. φωνήν ("sound") is likewise the ordinary word for "voice."
