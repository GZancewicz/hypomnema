# John 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 3 · John 3:3

> **No one can do these things, unless God be with him. (John 3:2)**

You see that he approaches Jesus as a human prophet, and as one loved by God? What then does the Lord do? He does not rebuke him reproachfully, nor does he say: Why then do you come by night to the teacher sent from God? Why do you not speak openly? He says none of these things, but graciously converses with him about divine and lofty matters. And note that, though Christ was doing many wonders, the present evangelist relates none of these—either as having been told by the other evangelists, or as exceeding a detailed narration.[^1]

[^1]: The Greek line following in the source is a corrupt block of OCR (garbled marginal scripture citations) and cannot be reliably reconstructed; it is omitted here.
