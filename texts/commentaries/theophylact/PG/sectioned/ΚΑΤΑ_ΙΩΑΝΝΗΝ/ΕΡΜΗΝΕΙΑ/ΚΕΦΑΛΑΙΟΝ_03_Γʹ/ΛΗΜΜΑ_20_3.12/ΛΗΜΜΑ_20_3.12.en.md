# John 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 20 · John 3:12

> **"How can these things come to be?" (John 3:12)**

Wherefore also the Lord, showing him that out of simplicity[^1] he utters these things, says: You, being a teacher of Israel, if — having recalled the marvels beyond expectation in the Old [Covenant] from the fashioning of man, and what follows: how he was fashioned, how the woman *came* from a rib, how the wonders in Egypt, how the things at the Red *Sea*, how barren women bore children, and the like — if you would reckon up these things, as a teacher of Israel, you would not disbelieve even concerning the things I now speak of. [^1]: Greek ἀφελείας (ἀφέλεια), "simplicity, guilelessness, artlessness"; the Lord shows that Nicodemus's question springs from naïveté, not malice. The sentence breaks off mid-thought and is completed in the next lemma («Ἑωράκαμεν»).
