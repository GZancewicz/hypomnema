# John 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 21 · John 3:12

> **"We have seen," (John 3:12)**[^1]

*this* he means; but *it denotes* rather a most accurate knowledge; but you do not receive our testimony — that is, mine. And these things the Lord says, not aiming at Nicodemus, but directing them at the race of the Jews, at those who disbelieved to the end. If therefore «the earthly things I told you, and you do not believe» — that is, if concerning the regeneration that comes to be in baptism I told you, and you did not accept it, but you asked the—

[^1]: The catchword «Ἑωράκαμεν» ("We have seen," John 3:11) completes the sentence begun in the previous lemma: "it is not a bodily seeing that the «We have seen» indicates, but rather a most accurate knowledge." The exposition here runs on without break both from and into the neighboring fragments.
