# John 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 15 · John 3:8

> **For the wind, he says, blows where it wishes. (John 3:8)**[^1]

From the wind, he says, learn the greater things. For the wind — for this he here calls "spirit" — blows where it wishes; and you hear its sound, but nevertheless you do not know its course. For it is unrestrainable, and unhindered, and by a natural authority has whatever impulse *it has*. But the *phrase*,[^2]

[^1]: The Greek is τὸ πνεῦμα ὅπου θέλει πνεῖ — the same word πνεῦμα rendered "Spirit" in the preceding lemma. Theophylact expressly takes it here as ἄνεμος (wind), as the exposition states; hence "wind" in the lemma.
[^2]: The sentence breaks off here (τὸ δέ,) and continues into the following lemma, where the phrase «Ὅπου θέλει πνεῖ» is taken up.
