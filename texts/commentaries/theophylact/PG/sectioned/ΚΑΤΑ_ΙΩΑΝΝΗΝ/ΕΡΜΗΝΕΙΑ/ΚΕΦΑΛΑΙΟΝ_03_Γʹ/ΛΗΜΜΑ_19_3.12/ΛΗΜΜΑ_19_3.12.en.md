# John 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 19 · John 3:12

> **...*the* earthly things I told you, and you do not believe; how, if I tell you the heavenly things, will you believe? (John 3:12)**[^1]

Still Nicodemus stands upon the Jewish lowliness;[^2] therefore he asks again:

[^1]: The fragment opens mid-verse, at ἐπίγεια; the conditional "εἰ τὰ" ("if the") is supplied from the preceding context («εἰ τὰ ἐπίγεια εἶπον ὑμῖν...»).
[^2]: Greek εὐτελείας (from εὐτέλεια), "cheapness, meanness, lowliness" — here the low, unspiritual level of understanding at which Nicodemus still stands.
