# John 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 8 · John 3:3

> **"Unless someone be born from above," (John 3:3)**

—he thought that "from above"[^1] was said in this way, in the sense of "from the beginning, a second time"; so that what was said was of this sort: *Unless someone be born from the beginning and a second time.* On account of this, therefore, he says, *How can he, being old, enter a second time into the womb of his mother?* For there were two things making him perplexed, both the spiritual birth and the kingdom. For not even the name of the kingdom of the heavens had ever been heard among *the* Jews. For the time being, then, he is perplexed about the birth; wherefore also Christ reveals to him more clearly the manner of the spiritual birth,

[^1]: Greek ἄνωθεν, which means both "from above" (of source) and "again, anew" (of a second occurrence). The whole passage turns on Nicodemus taking it in the latter, temporal sense (ἐξ ἀρχῆς, ἐκ δευτέρου, "from the beginning, a second time") rather than the former. Rendered "from above" throughout to hold the sense the Lord intends.
