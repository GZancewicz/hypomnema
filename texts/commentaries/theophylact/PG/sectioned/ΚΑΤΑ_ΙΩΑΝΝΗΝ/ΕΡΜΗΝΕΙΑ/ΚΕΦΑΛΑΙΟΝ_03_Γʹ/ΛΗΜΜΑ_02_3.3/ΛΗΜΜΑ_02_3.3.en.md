# John 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 2 · John 3:3

> **For Jesus himself, it says, did not entrust himself to them, (John 2:24)**

nor did he, as to men truly faithful, commit to them all the doctrines, since he entered into their hearts and knew what was in them concerning him. For it did not escape him what sort of reasoning there was in each man who seemed to believe. And Nicodemus too was nearly of this kind; for this man also believed in Jesus, and is seen speaking to the Jews on behalf of the Lord, that one must condemn him only after inquiry and examination; and after the cross too he shows much care and zeal toward the burial.
