# John 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 1 · John 3:3

> **But when he was in Jerusalem at the Passover, at the feast, many believed in his name, seeing the signs which he was doing. But Jesus himself did not entrust himself to them, because he knew all men, and because he had no need that anyone should testify concerning man; for he himself knew what was in man. Now there was a man from the Pharisees, Nicodemus his name, a ruler of the Jews; this man came to Jesus by night, and said to him: Rabbi, we know that you have come from God as a teacher; for no one can do these signs which you do, unless God be with him. (John 2:23–3:2)**

Some seemed to believe in the name of Jesus, when he was at the feast, but their faith was not firm. For attending to Christ for a season—not as God, but as a man borne along by God[^1]—again they were falling away even from this very worthless faith. And that they were such is clear from what follows,

[^1]: θεοφορουμένῳ, literally "carried/borne by God," hence "God-inspired"; Nicodemus and his like esteemed Christ as an inspired man rather than as God.
