# John 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 13 · John 3:8

> **That which is born of the flesh, is flesh; but that which is born of the Spirit, is spirit. (John 3:8)**

That is, *spiritual* is the man who has been born through baptism; for you ought to understand "spirit" in place of "spiritual." For the one baptized does not indeed become a divine Spirit, but, having received the adoption in the Spirit and grace and honor, he is deemed worthy to be spiritual.
