# Mark 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 9 · Mark 3:10

> **For he healed many, so that they fell upon him in order that they might touch him, as many as had scourges, and the (Mark 3:10)**[^1]

[^1]: The lemma reads σοι εἶχον μάστιγας; the initial letter of ὅσοι ("as many as") has dropped out in the OCR. On μάστιγας ("scourges"), see Theophylact's own gloss at Mark 3:11, where he explains that by "scourges" the evangelist means the diseases.
