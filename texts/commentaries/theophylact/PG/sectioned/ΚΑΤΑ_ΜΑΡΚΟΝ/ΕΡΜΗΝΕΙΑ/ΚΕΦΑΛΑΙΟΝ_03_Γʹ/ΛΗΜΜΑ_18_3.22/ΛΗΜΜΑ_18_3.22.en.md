# Mark 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 18 · Mark 3:22

> **scribes who had come down from Jerusalem said that he has Beelzebul, and that by the ruler of the demons he casts out the demons. (Mark 3:22)**

"Having heard," he says, "those about him" — that is, his own household, perhaps those from the same fatherland, or even his brothers — "went out to seize him. For they said that he was beside himself"; that is, that he has a demon. For hearing that he casts out demons and heals diseases, out of envy they supposed that he has a demon and was beside himself; whence they wished to seize him, so that they might bind him as one possessed. And those about him — that is, his own household — spoke thus;[^1] and likewise also the scribes from Jerusalem said that he had a demon. For since they had no way to contradict the things that were happening, in another way they slander them, saying that they come from demons.

[^1]: The Greek here is corrupt: «Καὶ οἱ μὲν παρ᾽ αὐτοῦ, τουτέστιν, οἱ οἰκεῖοι αὐτοῦ, αβτως·» — the word after «οἰκεῖοι αὐτοῦ» is unreadable in the OCR; it is most likely οὕτως ("thus"), with the verb ("said," ἔλεγον) supplied from the parallel clause. Rendered "spoke thus." A stretch of Latin-column OCR garbage following the exposition has been omitted.
