# Mark 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 29 · Mark 3:35

> **should do the will of God, this one is my brother and my sister and mother. (Mark 3:35)**

The brothers of the Lord, being envious, came to seize him as one out of his mind and possessed by a demon. But the mother, perhaps mastered by the passion of vainglory, came drawing him away from the teaching, and showing the crowds that the one who was admired by them she thus leads about at will, so as even to draw him away from teaching. The Lord therefore answers that: It will profit my mother nothing to have become my mother, unless she also has the other virtue; and likewise neither does the kinship *profit* my brothers; for those are truly the kinsmen of Christ who do the will of God. Not, then, as disowning his mother does he say these things, but showing that not on account of the bearing alone will she be worthy of honor, but also on account of all the other virtue; so that, if indeed she should not have that *virtue*, others will be preferred as regards the kinship.[^1]

[^1]: The final clause is corrupt in the OCR: ὡς ἤν γε ἐκείνην οὐν ἔχῃ, ἄλλοι προτιμηθήσονται εἰς τὴν συνγένειαν, reconstructed as ὡς ἤν γε ἐκείνην οὐκ ἔχῃ (= ἐὰν ... οὐκ ἔχῃ), "so that if indeed she should not have that [virtue]." The fragment breaks off at εἰς τὴν συγγένειαν.
