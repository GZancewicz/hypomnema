# Mark 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 24 · Mark 3:29

> **blasphemes against the Holy Spirit has no forgiveness unto the age, but is liable to eternal judgment; because they said, He has an unclean spirit. (Mark 3:29)**

Something like this he says here: Whatever other sins men commit, they might perhaps have some excuse to plead and obtain pardon, since God condescends to the weakness of men. For example: they called the Lord a glutton and a wine-drinker, a friend of tax-collectors and sinners; for this they will be deemed worthy of pardon. But whenever, seeing him work extraordinary miracles, they blaspheme the Spirit—that is, the wonders wrought through the Holy Spirit—then how will they obtain forgiveness, unless they repent? For when they were scandalized at the flesh of Christ, for this—even if they did not repent—they would have been pardoned, as men who had been made to stumble. But whenever, seeing him do the works of God, they still went on blaspheming, how, having remained unrepentant, will they be forgiven?
