# Mark 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 8 · Mark 3:8

> **Jerusalem, and from Idumea, and beyond the Jordan; and those around Tyre and Sidon, a great multitude, having heard [ … ] to his *disciples*, that a small boat should attend upon him because of the crowd, so that they might not press upon him; (Mark 3:8)**[^1]

[^1]: The OCR of the lemma skips from ἀκούσαντες ("having heard") to ταῖς αὐτοῦ, dropping the intervening words of Mark 3:8–9 (something like ὅσα ἐποίει, ἦλθον πρὸς αὐτόν. καὶ εἶπε τοῖς μαθη-). The surviving ταῖς αὐτοῦ is the tail of τοῖς μαθηταῖς αὐτοῦ, hence "to his *disciples*." The gap is marked [ … ] rather than reconstructed.
