# Mark 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 10 · Mark 9:14

> **And coming to the disciples, he saw a great crowd around them and scribes questioning with them. And immediately all the crowd, seeing him, was greatly amazed, and running to him they greeted him. And he asked the scribes: What are you questioning with them? And one out of the crowd answering said: Teacher, I brought my son to you, having a mute spirit. And wherever it seizes him, it dashes him down, and he foams and gnashes his teeth and becomes stiff. And I told your disciples to cast it out, and they were not able. (Mark 9:14-18)**

Coming to the disciples—the nine, that is, who had not gone up with him—he saw them being questioned by the Pharisees. For the Pharisees, seizing upon the absence of Jesus, tried to subvert the disciples. The crowd, however, suddenly seeing him, greeted him; for they longed to see him, and as if seeing him after a long journey abroad, they greeted him. But some say that his countenance also, having become more beautiful from the light of the transfiguration, drew the crowds to greet him.

And one out of the crowd answering said: This man was weak concerning faith, as the Lord also testifies, saying, O unbelieving generation, and again, To the one who believes all things are possible; and that man himself *said*, Help my unbelief. But he accuses the disciples, being himself altogether unbelieving. For he ought not to have come and accused *them* before all, but privately.
