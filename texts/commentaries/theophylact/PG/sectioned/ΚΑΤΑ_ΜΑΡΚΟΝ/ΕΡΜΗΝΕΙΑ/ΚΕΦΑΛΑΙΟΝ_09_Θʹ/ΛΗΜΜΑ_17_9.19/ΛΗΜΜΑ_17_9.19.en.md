# Mark 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 17 · Mark 9:19

> **Whoever is not against us is for us. (Mark 9:40)**

These, therefore, seem contrary; but they are not. For the saying, «He who is not with me is against me,» was spoken concerning demons, who are eager to draw men away from God and who scatter the things of God; but that now spoken is concerning men, who rather bring some to God through performing wonders.
