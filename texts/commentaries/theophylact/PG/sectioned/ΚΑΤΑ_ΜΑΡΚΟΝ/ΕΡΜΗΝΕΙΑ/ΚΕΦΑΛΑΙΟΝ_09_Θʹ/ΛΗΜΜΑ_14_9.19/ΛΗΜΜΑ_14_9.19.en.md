# Mark 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 14 · Mark 9:19

> **And he came into Capernaum; and being in the house, he questioned them: What were you discussing among yourselves on the way? But they were silent. For they had disputed with one another on the way, who was greater. And having sat down, he called the twelve, and says to them: If anyone wishes to be first, he shall be last of all, and servant of all. And having taken a child, he set it in the midst of them, and having embraced it, he said to them: Whoever receives one of such children in my name, receives me, and whoever receives me, receives not me, but him who sent me. (Mark 9:19)**

The disciples, still thinking in a more human way, contended with one another about which of them was greater and more preferred in Christ's regard. But the Lord does not forbid the desire for preferment. For he wishes us to desire the higher rank. He does not, however, want us to snatch at the first places, but rather through humility to gain possession of the exalted. For he set a child in the midst, and he wants us also to become according to that *pattern*. For the child neither craves glory, nor envies, nor bears grudges. And not only, he says, if you yourselves become such will you receive a great reward, but even if you honor others of such kind for my sake, you would receive in return the kingdom of heaven. For you receive me; and, receiving me, you receive him who sent me. You see, therefore, how much humility can do, and the unfeignedness of the disposition, and its guilelessness. For it settles the Son and the Father within us. And it is plain that the Holy Spirit also *dwells there*.
