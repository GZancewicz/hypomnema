# Mark 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 15 · Mark 9:19

> **But John answered him, saying: Teacher, we saw someone casting out demons in your name, who does not follow us; and we forbade him, because he does not follow us. But Jesus said: Do not forbid him. For there is no one who will do a mighty work in my name, and be able soon to speak evil of me. For whoever is not against us is for us. (Mark 9:19)**

Not led by any jealousy or envy does the son of thunder[^1] forbid that man who was casting out the demons, but wishing that all who call upon the name of Christ should also follow him, and that all the disciples should be one body. For at the beginning of the preaching, certain men, held fast by a love of vainglory, wished themselves too, forsooth, to work signs. And seeing that the name of Jesus availed for much, they would pronounce it, and indeed they did signs, being otherwise unworthy of the divine grace. For the Lord willed that the preaching be broadened, even through the unworthy. What then does the Savior do? He does not permit John to forbid the one doing the signs, but, Let them be, he says, for no one will be able soon to speak evil of me who does a mighty work in my name. For how could he speak evil of me who has my name as an occasion of glory, and who through calling upon it works wonders? But the Lord seems to say things contrary to himself.
