# Mark 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 12 · Mark 9:19

> **And when he had entered into a house, his disciples questioned him privately: Why is it that we were not able to cast it out? And he said to them: This kind can come out by nothing, except by prayer and fasting. (Mark 9:28-29)**

The disciples feared lest they had lost the grace which the Lord had given them, and that for this reason they were not able to cast out the demon. And see also their reverence, how they come to him privately. And "this kind"—which kind? Either that of the moon-struck,[^1] or simply the whole kind of demons does not come out by any other means, except by prayer and fasting. For both the sufferer must fast, and the one who is about to heal. For there is need of both; but the argument especially requires the fasting of the sufferer. One must not fast only, but also pray; nor only pray, but also fast. For thus true prayer is accomplished, if it has fasting yoked together with it—when the one praying is not weighed down by the vapors from foods, but is light and unburdened.

[^1]: τῶν σεληνιαζομένων, literally "those affected by the moon," the term used for epileptics who were thought to be influenced by the moon's phases.
