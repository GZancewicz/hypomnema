# Mark 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 8 · Mark 9:11

> **And they questioned him, saying, The scribes say that Elias must come first? And he answering said to them: Elias indeed, coming first, restores all things; and how is it written concerning the Son of man, that he should suffer many things and be set at nought? But I say to you, that Elias also has come, and they did to him whatever they wished, as it is written concerning him. (Mark 9:11-13)**

Concerning Elias a certain report prevailed among the Jews, that Elias would come before Christ. The Pharisees, however, did not interpret the things about Elias as they ought, but willfully and maliciously[^1] concealed the truth. For there are two comings of Christ, this one that has taken place, and the one to come. Of the former, then, John became the forerunner; but of the second, Elias is to become *the forerunner*. Christ therefore calls John "Elias," as being one both censorious and zealous and a dweller in the desert. Accordingly the Lord takes his stand against the opinion of the Pharisees, who suppose that Elias is the forerunner of the former coming of Christ. But how does he take his stand against it?

[^1]: The Greek ἐθελοκάκως denotes a deliberate, willed maliciousness — "with willful bad faith," hiding the truth on purpose rather than through ignorance.
