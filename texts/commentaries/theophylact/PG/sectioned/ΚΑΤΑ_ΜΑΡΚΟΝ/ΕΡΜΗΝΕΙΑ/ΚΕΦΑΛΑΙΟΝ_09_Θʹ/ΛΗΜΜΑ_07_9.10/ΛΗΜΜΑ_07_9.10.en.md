# Mark 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 7 · Mark 9:10

> **...they kept the saying to themselves, questioning what the rising from the dead is. (Mark 9:10)**

For what reason does the Lord command the disciples to tell no one about the transfiguration? So that people not be scandalized, hearing such glorious things about Christ, and then later seeing him crucified. After he had risen from the dead, however, it was opportune to speak such glorious things about Christ that had come to pass before the cross. The apostles therefore kept the saying to themselves, questioning what the rising from the dead is. For they did not yet know, it says, that he must rise from the dead.
