# Mark 8 (Ηʹ) — Lemma 31 · Mark 8:32

> **openly[^1] he was speaking the word, and Peter, having taken him aside, began to rebuke him. But he— (Mark 8:32)**

[^1]: παρρησίᾳ (dative of manner): "openly, plainly," but also "with boldness, frankly." The sense here is that he was speaking of his coming Passion without concealment.
