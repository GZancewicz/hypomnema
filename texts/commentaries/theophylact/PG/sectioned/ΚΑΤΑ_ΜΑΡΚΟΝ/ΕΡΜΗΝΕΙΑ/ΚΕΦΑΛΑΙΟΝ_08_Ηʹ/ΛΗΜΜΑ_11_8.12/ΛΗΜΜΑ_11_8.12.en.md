# Mark 8 (Ηʹ) — Lemma 11 · Mark 8:12

> **having groaned in his spirit, he says: Why does this generation seek after a sign? Amen I say to you, if a sign shall be given to this generation.[^1] (Mark 8:12)**

After the wonder concerning the loaves, immediately he withdraws to another place, fearing lest the crowd, on account of such a wonder, should make an uprising and make him king. But the Pharisees seek a sign from heaven, such as to make the sun stand still, or the moon, to bring down thunderbolts, to alter the air. For they supposed that he would not be able to make a sign from heaven, as being one who by Beelzebul was able to make only the signs on the earth. But he does not yield to them. For the time of the signs from heaven is another — I mean that of the second coming, when the powers of the heavens shall be shaken, and the moon shall not give its light. But the *time* of the first coming has nothing such, but all things full of gentleness. So that there shall not be given to this generation such a sign, that is, one from heaven.

[^1]: «εἰ δοθήσεται τῇ γενεᾷ ταύτῃ σημεῖον» — a Hebraism: the conditional "if" introduces an emphatic oath of denial, i.e. "no sign shall by any means be given to this generation." The literal "if a sign shall be given" is retained in the body.
