# Mark 8 (Ηʹ) — Lemma 37 · Mark 8:38

> **For whoever shall be ashamed of me, and of my words, in this adulterous and sinful generation, of him also shall the Son of man[^1] be ashamed, when he comes in the glory of his Father with the holy angels. And he said to them: Amen I say to you, that there are some of those standing here who shall not taste of death, until they see the kingdom of God having come in power. (Mark 8:38–9:1)**

He is not content with the faith that is according to the mind, but demands also the confession through the mouth. For since man is twofold, let the sanctification also become twofold: the soul being sanctified through faith, and the body being sanctified through confession. Whoever therefore shall be ashamed to confess the crucified one as his God, of him he too shall be ashamed, judging him a slave unworthy of himself, when he shall come — no longer lowly, nor such as he appeared here, mean and worthless, so that some are ashamed on his account, but in glory and with the bodyguard[^2] of the angels.

But since he had spoken concerning his glory, wishing to show them that he does not boast in vain, he says that *There are some of those standing here* — that is, Peter and James and John — who shall not die until I show them, in the transfiguration, with what kind of glory I am about to come at the second coming. For the transfiguration was nothing other than a foretoken of the second coming. Thus…[^3]

[^1]: The OCR here reads ὁ χλὸς τοῦ ἀνθρώπου, corrupt for ὁ Υἱὸς τοῦ ἀνθρώπου, "the Son of man."
[^2]: Greek δορυφορίας — a retinue of spearmen, a royal bodyguard or armed escort; here of the attending angels.
[^3]: The fragment breaks off at Οὕτω ("Thus…"), the sentence left incomplete in the source.
