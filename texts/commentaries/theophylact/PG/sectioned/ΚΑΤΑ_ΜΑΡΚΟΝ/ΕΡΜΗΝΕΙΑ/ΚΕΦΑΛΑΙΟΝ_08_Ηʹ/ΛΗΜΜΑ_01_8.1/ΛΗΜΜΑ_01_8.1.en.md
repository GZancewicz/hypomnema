# Mark 8 (Ηʹ) — Lemma 1 · Mark 8:1-6

> **In those days, a very great crowd being present, and not having what they might eat, Jesus, having called to himself his disciples, says to them: I have compassion on the crowd, because already three days they remain with me, and they do not have what they might eat. And if I send them away fasting to their house, they will grow faint on the way; for some of them have come from afar. And his disciples answered him: From where will anyone be able here to satisfy these with loaves in a wilderness? And he asked them: How many loaves do you have? And they said, Seven. And he commanded the crowd to recline upon the ground, and having taken the seven loaves, having given thanks, he broke, and gave to his disciples, that they might set them before *the people*;[^1] and they set them before the crowd, and they had a few small fish,[^2] and having blessed, he said to set these also before them. (Mark 8:1-6)**

[^1]: The OCR here reads «ἵνα παραΞαι», which is corrupt; the sense requires «ἵνα παραθῶσι» ("that they might set before"), agreeing with the following «καὶ παρέθηκαν τῷ ὄχλῳ».
[^2]: The OCR reads «εἶχον ἐχθύδια δλέγα»; read «εἶχον ἰχθύδια ὀλίγα» ("they had a few small fish").
