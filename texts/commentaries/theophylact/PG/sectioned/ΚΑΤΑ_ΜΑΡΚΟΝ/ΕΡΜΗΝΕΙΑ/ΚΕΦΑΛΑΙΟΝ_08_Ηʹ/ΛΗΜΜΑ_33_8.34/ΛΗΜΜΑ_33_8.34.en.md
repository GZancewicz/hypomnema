# Mark 8 (Ηʹ) — Lemma 33 · Mark 8:34-36

> **And having called the crowd to himself together with his disciples, he said to them: Whoever wishes to follow after me, let him deny himself, and take up his own cross, and follow me. For whoever wishes to save his life[^1] shall lose it; but whoever shall lose his life for my sake and the gospel's, this one shall save it. For what shall it profit a man if he gain the whole world, and forfeit his soul? Or what (Mark 8:34-36)**

[^1]: ψυχή is one word here rendered both "life" and "soul." In the sayings of 8:35 the sense is one's physical life laid down for Christ ("save/lose his life"); from 8:36 onward (ζημιωθῇ τὴν ψυχὴν) the sense shifts toward the soul whose eternal welfare is at stake.
