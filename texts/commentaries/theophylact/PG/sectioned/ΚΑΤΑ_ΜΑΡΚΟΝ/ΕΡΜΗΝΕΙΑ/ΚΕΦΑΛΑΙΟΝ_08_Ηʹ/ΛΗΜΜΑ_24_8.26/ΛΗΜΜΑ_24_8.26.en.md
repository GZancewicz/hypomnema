# Mark 8 (Ηʹ) — Lemma 24 · Mark 8:26

Bethsaida appears to have been sick with much unbelief, wherefore also Christ pronounces woe upon her, as Matthew says (Matt. 11:21) —[^1]

[^1]: The fragment breaks off here after «ὡς ὁ Ματθαῖός φησιν·» ("as Matthew says·"), the promised words of Matthew not being given in this section; read φησιν for the OCR's «φηαιν». The verb ταλανίζει ("counts wretched, pronounces woe upon") alludes to the "Woe to thee, Bethsaida" of Matt. 11:21. A stray opening quotation mark «"» precedes the sentence in the source and has been omitted as an OCR artifact.
