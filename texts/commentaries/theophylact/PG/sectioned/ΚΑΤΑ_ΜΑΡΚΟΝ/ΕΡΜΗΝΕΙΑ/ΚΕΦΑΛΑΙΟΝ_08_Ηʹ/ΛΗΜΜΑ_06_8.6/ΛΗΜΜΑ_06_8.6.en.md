# Mark 8 (Ηʹ) — Lemma 6 · Mark 8:6

> **he commanded the crowd to recline upon the ground, and having taken the seven loaves, having given thanks, he broke, and gave to his disciples, that they might set them before *the people*;[^1] and they set them before the crowd, and they had a few small fish,[^2] and having blessed, he said to set these also before them. (Mark 8:6)**

[^1]: The OCR here reads «ἵνα παραΞαι», which is corrupt; the sense requires «ἵνα παραθῶσι» ("that they might set before"), agreeing with the following «καὶ παρέθηκαν τῷ ὄχλῳ».
[^2]: The OCR reads «εἶχον ἐχθύδια δλέγα»; read «εἶχον ἰχθύδια ὀλίγα» ("they had a few small fish").
