# Mark 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 1 · Mark 4:1

> **And again he began to teach beside the sea, and a great crowd gathered to him, so that he, having embarked into the boat, sat on the sea; and all the crowd was toward the sea upon the land,[^1] and (Mark 4:1)**

[^1]: The OCR reads «ἐκὶ τῆς γῆς»; corrected to «ἐπὶ τῆς γῆς» ("upon the land"). Also corrected in this verse: «θάλαα σαν» → «θάλασσαν», «ὁὄχλος» → «ὁ ὄχλος».
