# Mark 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 2 · Mark 4:2

> **he taught them many things in parables. (Mark 4:2)**

Even though he seemed to have sent his mother away,[^1] nevertheless he again obeys her. For it is on her account that he goes out beside the sea. And he sits in the boat, so that, having all before his face, he might speak within the hearing of all, and have no one at his back.

[^1]: An allusion back to the earlier episode (Mark 3:31–35), where Jesus appeared to dismiss his mother and brethren. «ἔδοξεν ἀποπέμψασθαι τὴν μητέρα» — literally "it seemed *good* / he seemed to send the mother away."
