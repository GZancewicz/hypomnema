# Mark 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 10 · Mark 4:11

> **"For seek," he says, "and it shall be given to you." (Mark 4:11)**

But the rest God blinded, so that their understanding of what is necessary might not be to their greater condemnation, since they were not doing it. In another way: Do you wish to know that God made all to see what is necessary? Listen. "That seeing" — this is God's; "they may not see" — this is of their own wickedness. For God fashioned them seeing, that is, perceiving the good, but they themselves do not see, willingly shutting their eyes, lest they turn back and be set right; for it is as if they begrudge their own salvation and betterment. But it is also possible to understand it thus, that: To the rest I speak in parables, so that seeing they may not see, and hearing they may not understand, lest perchance even from this they might turn back and be set right.
