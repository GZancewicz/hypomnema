# Mark 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 17 · Mark 4:20

> **These are the ones sown upon the good earth, whoever hear the word and accept it, and bear fruit — one thirtyfold and one sixtyfold and one a hundredfold. (Mark 4:20)**

There are here three ranks of the perishing of the word. For those beside the road are the inattentive; those beside the rock, the fainthearted; those among the thorns, the pleasure-lovers. Of those, however, who received and preserved the seed, again there are three ranks: those bearing fruit a hundredfold, who have the perfect and utmost[^1] life; those sixtyfold, who are in a middle state; and those thirtyfold, as many as bring forth little — yet these too contribute according to their ability. For example, some are virgins and solitaries; others of the mixed life[^2] and in a coenobium; others laypersons and in marriage. Yet the Lord receives all as bearing fruit; and thanks be to His love for mankind.

[^1]: The OCR reads ἀκρόσατον; read ἀκρότατον, "highest, utmost."
[^2]: Greek μιγάδες, literally "mixed ones" — here those living the communal (cenobitic) monastic life, as opposed to the solitary hermits (ἐρημικοί) just named; hence the following "and in a coenobium" (ἐν κοινοβίῳ).
