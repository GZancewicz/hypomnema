# Mark 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 25 · Mark 4:29-32

> **And he said: To what shall we liken the kingdom of God? or in what parable shall we compare it? As a grain of mustard, which, when it is sown upon the earth, is smaller than all the seeds that are upon the earth; and when it is sown, it comes up and becomes greater than all the herbs, and puts forth great branches, so that the birds of heaven can nest under its shade. (Mark 4:29-32)**[^1]

[^1]: OCR reconstruction of the lemma: the manuscript reads «Χαὶ Πεγε … τὴν βασλείαν τοῆ Δτοῦ … κόκκον σινάπειως»; read Καὶ ἔλεγε … τὴν βασιλείαν τοῦ Θεοῦ … κόκκον σινάπεως.
