# Mark 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 28 · Mark 4:35-38

> **And he says to them on that day, when evening had come: Let us cross over to the other side. And leaving the crowd, they take him along as he was in the boat. And there were also other little boats with him. And there arises a great storm of wind, and the waves were beating into the boat, so that it was already filling. And he himself was at the stern, sleeping on the cushion; and they wake him, and say to him: Teacher, do you not care that we are perishing? And having risen, he rebuked the wind, and said to the sea: Be silent, be muzzled.[^1] And the wind ceased, and there came a great calm, and (Mark 4:35-38)**

[^1]: πεφίμωσο — perfect passive imperative of φιμόω, "to muzzle"; literally "be muzzled / stay muzzled," i.e. remain shut fast. The verb of Σιώπα, πεφίμωσο is here rendered freshly rather than by the familiar "Peace, be still."
