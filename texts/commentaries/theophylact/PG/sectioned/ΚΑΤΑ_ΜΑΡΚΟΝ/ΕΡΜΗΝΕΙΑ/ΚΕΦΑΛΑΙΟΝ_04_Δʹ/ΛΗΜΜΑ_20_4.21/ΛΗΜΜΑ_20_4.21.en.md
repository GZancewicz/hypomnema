# Mark 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 20 · Mark 4:21

> **And he said to them: Take heed what you hear. With what measure you measure, it shall be measured to you, and it shall be added to you who hear. For whoever has, to him shall it be given; and whoever has not, even what he has shall be taken from him. (Mark 4:21)**

He rouses the disciples to sobriety. For take heed, he says, what you hear; let nothing of the things said by me escape you. For with what measure you measure, it shall be measured to you — that is, however great a measure of attention you bring, so great also shall the benefit be that is given to you. For the hearer, if he attends very closely and to the utmost, God too gives back to him much benefit; but if he is slack, the gain of benefit is proportionate. For whoever has eagerness and zeal, to him benefit also shall be given; but whoever has not eagerness and zeal, even what he seems to have shall be taken from him. For through sloth even the small spark which he had before is quenched, just as by diligence it is kindled.
