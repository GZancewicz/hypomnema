# Mark 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 13 · Mark 4:16-19

> **These likewise are the ones sown upon the rocky places, who, when they hear the word, immediately with joy receive it, and do not have root in themselves, but are temporary; then, when tribulation or persecution[^1] arises because of the word, immediately they are made to stumble. And these are the ones sown among the thorns, those who hear the word, and the cares of this age, and the deceit of riches, and the desires concerning the rest,[^2] entering in, choke the word, and it becomes unfruitful. (Mark 4:16-19)**

[^1]: The OCR reads δωγμοῦ; read διωγμοῦ, "persecution."
[^2]: The OCR reads περὶ τὰ λοιπὲ; read περὶ τὰ λοιπὰ, "concerning the rest / the remaining things."
