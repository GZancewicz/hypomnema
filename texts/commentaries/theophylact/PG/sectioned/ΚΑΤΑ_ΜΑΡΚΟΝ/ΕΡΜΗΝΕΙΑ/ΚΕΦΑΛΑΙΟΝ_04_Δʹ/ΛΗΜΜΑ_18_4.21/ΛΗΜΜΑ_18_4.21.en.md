# Mark 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 18 · Mark 4:21

> **And He was saying to them: Does the lamp come in order that it be placed under the measuring-vessel,[^1] or under the bed? Is it not that it be set upon the lampstand? For there is nothing hidden that will not be made manifest, nor did anything become secret, but that it should come into the open. If anyone has ears… (Mark 4:21)**

[^1]: Greek μόδιον, the *modius*, a dry-measure vessel.
