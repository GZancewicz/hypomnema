# Mark 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 27 · Mark 4:33

> **With many such parables he spoke the word to them, according as they were able to hear. But without a parable he did not speak to them; but privately to his own disciples he explained all things. (Mark 4:33)**

Small indeed is the word of faith; for "believe in Christ, and you shall be saved" (cf. Acts 16:31). You see how great *it becomes*?[^1] But once the preaching was sown upon the earth, it was spread wide and grew, so that the birds of heaven — that is, all who are lofty and exalted in mind and in knowledge[^2] — nest upon it. For how many wise men, having abandoned the Greek [i.e. pagan] wisdom, found rest upon the preaching? Therefore the preaching became greater than all, and put forth great branches. For one of the apostles *was parted off* to Rome, another to India, another to Achaia, and another to another part of the earth — they were split apart like branches. And he spoke to the crowds in many parables, setting the parables alongside the capacity of his hearers. For since the crowd were laymen and unlearned, for this reason he makes mention of a grain of mustard, and of grass, and of seed, so that from names familiar and customary to them he might teach them something profitable, or at least might stir them to come forward and ask, and through the asking to learn the things they did not know. For indeed to the disciples he explained all things privately — namely, to those who came forward and asked. But he explained all things, as many as they asked about as not knowing them. For *he did* not *explain* all things without qualification, even the clear ones. For from those things which they learned by asking, the rest also became clear to them; so that all things were explained to them.

[^1]: The Greek here is garbled and lacunose: the manuscript reads «Ὁρᾷς, πηλί—» running directly into Ἀλλὰ. Reconstructed as Ὁρᾷς πηλίκον [γίνεται]; "You see how great it becomes?"
[^2]: "in knowledge": OCR reads καὶ γῇ γνώσει; read καὶ τῇ γνώσει.
