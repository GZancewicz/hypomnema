> **And Jesus, having come out, saw a great crowd, and was moved with compassion upon them, because they were as sheep not having a shepherd. And he began to teach them many things. And, the hour now being far advanced, his disciples having come to him say to him, The place is desert, and the hour is already far advanced. Dismiss them, that having gone away into the surrounding fields and villages they may buy for themselves loaves; for they do not have what they may eat. But he answering said to them, Give them, you, to eat. And they say to him, Having gone away, shall we buy two hundred denarii of loaves, and give them to eat? But he says to them, How many loaves have you? Go and see. And having found out, they say, Five, and two fishes. And he ordered them to make all recline, company by company, upon the green grass.
