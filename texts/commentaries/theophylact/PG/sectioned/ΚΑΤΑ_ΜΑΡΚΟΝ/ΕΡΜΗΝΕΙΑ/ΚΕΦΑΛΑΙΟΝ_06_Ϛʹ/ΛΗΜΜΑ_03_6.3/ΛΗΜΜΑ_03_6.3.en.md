# Mark 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 3 · Mark 6:3

> **Is not this the carpenter, the son of Mary, and brother of James, and Joses, and Judas, and Simon? And are not his sisters here with us? And they were offended at him. (Mark 6:3)**

He sets foot in the homeland,[^1] not being ignorant that they will despise him, but in order that they might not have it later to say that, "If he had come, we would have believed"; and at the same time also reproving their envious disposition. For though it were fitting to boast rather in the Lord, as adorning their homeland both through the teaching and through the miracles, they instead disparage him for the lowliness of his descent. So great an evil is envy. For it ever casts a shadow over good things, and does not allow those who envy to see them. And so now too many slander some as low-born, though they are in other respects worthy of honor, thinking evilly and very ignobly.

[^1]: The Greek here is corrupt in the source; it reads «Ἐὰιβαίνει τῆς πατρίδος, κ ἀγνοῶν ὅτι καταφρονήσουσιν αὐτοῦ», reconstructed as «Ἐπιβαίνει τῆς πατρίδος, οὐκ ἀγνοῶν ὅτι καταφρονήσουσιν αὐτοῦ» — "He sets foot in the homeland, not being ignorant that they will despise him." Similarly the close of the lemma reads «Καὶ· κανδαλίζοντο ἐν αὐτῷ», i.e. «καὶ ἐσκανδαλίζοντο ἐν αὐτῷ».
