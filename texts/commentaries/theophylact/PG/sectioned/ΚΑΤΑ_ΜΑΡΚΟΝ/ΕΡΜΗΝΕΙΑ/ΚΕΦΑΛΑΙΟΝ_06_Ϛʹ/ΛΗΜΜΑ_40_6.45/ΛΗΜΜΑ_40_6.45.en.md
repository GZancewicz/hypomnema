# Mark 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 40 · Mark 6:45-47

> **And immediately he compelled his disciples to embark into the boat, and to go before to the other side toward Bethsaida, until he himself should dismiss the crowd. And having taken leave of them, he departed into the mountain to pray. And when evening had come, the boat was in the midst of the sea, and he alone upon the land, and he saw them straining at the rowing; for the wind was contrary to them. And about the fourth watch of the night he comes to them, walking upon the sea, and he wished to pass by them. But they (Mark 6:45-47)**[^1]

[^1]: The Greek of this scripture-only stretch is OCR-corrupt at several points; the reconstructed readings underlying the translation are ἀποταξάμενος ("having taken leave," for MS ἀποταῖάμενος), εἶδεν ("he saw," for MS τῖΑεν), and βασανιζομένους ("straining/distressed," for MS θασανιζομνους).
