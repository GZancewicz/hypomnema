# Mark 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 30 · Mark 6:31

> **And he said to them: "Come, you yourselves, apart, into a desert place, and rest a little"; for those coming and those going were many, and they had no leisure even to eat. And they departed into a desert place by the boat apart. And many saw them going away and recognized him. And on foot from all the cities they ran together there, and went before them, and came together to him. (Mark 6:31)**

After the apostles had preached, they gather together to Jesus; so that we too may learn, whenever we are put forward for some ministry, not to be unruly and to exalt ourselves against the one who put us forward, but to know him as head, and to turn back to him, and to report to him whatever we did or taught; for one must not only teach, but also do. And Christ gives the disciples rest, so that those who preside may in turn learn to count worthy of rest those who labor both in word and in teaching, and not always to strain them with toils. And though he withdraws into a desert place because of his freedom from love of glory, nevertheless not even here does he escape the notice of those seeking him. But they were so watchful, lest he escape them, that they even went before them — that is, the crowds got ahead of the apostles, and went off to the place where Jesus was about to rest. So therefore you too, anticipate Jesus, not waiting for him to summon you, but running ahead, and rather yourself getting there before him.
