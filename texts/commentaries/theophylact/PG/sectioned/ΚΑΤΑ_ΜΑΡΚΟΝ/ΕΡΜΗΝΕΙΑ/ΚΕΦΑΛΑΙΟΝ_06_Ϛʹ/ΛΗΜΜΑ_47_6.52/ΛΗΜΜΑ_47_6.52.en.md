# Mark 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 47 · Mark 6:52-6:54

> **And having crossed over, they came to the land of Gennesaret;[^1] and they came to anchor,[^2] and when they had gone out of the boat, immediately having recognized him, having run about all that surrounding region, they began to carry about upon the pallets those who were sick, wherever they heard that he was; and wherever *he entered*[^3] (Mark 6:52-6:54)**

[^1]: OCR corrupt here: the manuscript reads «Γκυνησαρέο», reconstructed as «Γεννησαρέτ» (Gennesaret).
[^2]: The word before «προσωρμίσθησαν» is OCR-garbled («Περὶ»); read as the connective «καὶ» ("and"). The verb «προσωρμίσθησαν» = "they moored / came to anchor."
[^3]: The fragment breaks off at «καὶ ὅπου ἂν» ("and wherever..."); the sense is continued in the following verse.
