# Mark 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 9 · Mark 6:8

> **he commanded them that they should take nothing for the road, except a staff only, no bag, no bread,[^1] no copper for the belt, but[^2] (Mark 6:8)**

[^1]: The OCR reads ἄρτον without the negative; the common text has μὴ ἄρτον ("no bread"), supplied here for sense.
[^2]: The OCR ὃλλ´ is read as ἀλλ' ("but").
