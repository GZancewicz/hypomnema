# Mark 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 49 · Mark 6:56

> **He entered into villages or cities or fields, *and* in the marketplaces they laid the sick, and they besought him that they might touch even the hem of his garment; and as many as touched him were saved.[^1] (Mark 6:56)**

After a time, as it seems,[^2] the Lord sojourned in that place. Wherefore the evangelist also says: *Having recognized him,* they brought the sick. For no longer did they call *him* into the houses, but they themselves rather brought those who were ill, beseeching that they might touch even the hem. For the miracle concerning[^3] the woman with the flow of blood had seized the ears of all, and instilled in them greater faith.

[^1]: Greek ἐσῴζοντο (from σῴζω), here of bodily healing — "were made whole"; the same verb carries the sense "were saved," which the commentary trades upon. Rendered "were saved" to preserve the ambiguity.
[^2]: The OCR reads Κσικαν; restored as ἔοικεν ("it seems"), the expected idiom (ὡς ἔοικεν, "as it seems").
[^3]: The OCR reads καρὶ (τὸ γὰρ καρὶ τὴν αἱμόῤῥουν θαῦμα); read as περὶ, "the miracle concerning the hemorrhaging woman" — the standard reference to Mark 5:25–34.
