# Mark 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 23 · Mark 6:21-6:22

> **And when a convenient day had come, when Herod on his birthday made a supper for his nobles, and for the chiliarchs[^1] and the chief men of Galilee; and when the daughter of Herodias herself had come in, and had danced and pleased Herod and those reclining with him, the king said to the girl: Ask me whatever you wish, and I will give it to you. And he swore to her that, Whatever you ask me I will give you, up to half of my kingdom. But she, having gone out, said to her mother, What shall I ask? And she said: The head of John the Baptist. And (Mark 6:21-6:22)**

[^1]: Greek «χιλιάρχοις», commanders of a thousand.
