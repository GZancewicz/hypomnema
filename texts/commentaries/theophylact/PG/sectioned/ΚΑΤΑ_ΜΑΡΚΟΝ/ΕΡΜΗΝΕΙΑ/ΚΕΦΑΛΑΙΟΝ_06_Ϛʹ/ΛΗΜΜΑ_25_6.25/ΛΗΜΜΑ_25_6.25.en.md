# Mark 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 25 · Mark 6:25-27

> **And having entered immediately with haste to the king, she asked, saying: "I desire that you give me forthwith upon a platter the head of John the Baptist." And the king, having become deeply grieved, on account of the oaths and those reclining at table with him, was not willing to refuse her. And immediately the king, having sent a speculator, commanded his head to be brought. And he, having departed, beheaded him in the prison, and brought his head upon a platter and gave it to the girl, and the girl gave it to her mother. (Mark 6:25-27)**
