# Mark 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 15 · Mark 6:13

> **they cast out many demons, and anointed with oil many that were sick, and healed them. (Mark 6:13)**

That the apostles anointed with oil, Mark alone relates; or *it is related by Mark* and also by James the brother of God [i.e. the Lord's brother], who in the catholic Epistle says: "Is anyone among you sick? Let him call for the elders of the Church, and let them pray over him, having anointed him with oil" (James 5:14). Oil, therefore, is both beneficial for toils, and a cause of light, and a procurer of cheerfulness; and it signifies the mercy of God and the grace of the Spirit, through which we are both released from toils and receive light and joy and spiritual cheerfulness.[^1]

[^1]: There is a play of sound between ἔλαιον ("oil") and ἔλεος ("mercy"): the oil is said to *signify* (σημαίνει) God's mercy, the near-homophony reinforcing the symbolism.
