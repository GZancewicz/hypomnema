# Mark 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 19 · Mark 6:17-6:19

> **For Herod himself, having sent, seized John, and bound him in prison on account of Herodias[^1] the wife of Philip his brother, because he had married her. For John was saying to Herod that, It is not lawful for you to have the wife of your brother. But Herodias held it against him, and wished to kill him, and was not able. For Herod feared John, (Mark 6:17-6:19)**

[^1]: OCR: the source reads «Ἐρωἄιάδα», a garbling of «Ἡρωδιάδα» (Herodias); so read throughout.
