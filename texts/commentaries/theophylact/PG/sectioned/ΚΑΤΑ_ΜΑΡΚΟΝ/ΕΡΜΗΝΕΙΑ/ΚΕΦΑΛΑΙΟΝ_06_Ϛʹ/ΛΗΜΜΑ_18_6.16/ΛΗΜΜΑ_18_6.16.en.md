# Mark 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 18 · Mark 6:16

> **But Herod, having heard, said, "John, whom I beheaded — this is he; he himself was raised from the dead." (Mark 6:16)**

This Herod was son of the one who killed the infants. Though he was a tetrarch, Mark calls him king, using the name indifferently. This one, therefore, hearing the miracles of the Lord, and knowing that he had slain John, a righteous man, without cause, supposed that he had risen from the dead, and that from the resurrection he had received in addition the working of miracles. For formerly John did no sign; but from the resurrection Herod supposed that he had received in addition the working of the signs. But others said that he is Elijah; for he reproved the many, as when he said, "O unbelieving generation!" (Mark 9:19). But Herod was afraid — so pitiable, therefore, *was he* that he feared the dead man.
