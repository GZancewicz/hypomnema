# Mark 7 (Ζʹ) — Lemma 8 · Mark 7:15

> **There is nothing outside the man entering into him which is able to defile[^1] him, but the things that go out from him, those are the things that defile the man. If anyone has ears (Mark 7:15)**

[^1]: Greek κοινῶσαι (verb κοινόω), literally "to make common," i.e. to render ritually unclean; rendered "defile."
