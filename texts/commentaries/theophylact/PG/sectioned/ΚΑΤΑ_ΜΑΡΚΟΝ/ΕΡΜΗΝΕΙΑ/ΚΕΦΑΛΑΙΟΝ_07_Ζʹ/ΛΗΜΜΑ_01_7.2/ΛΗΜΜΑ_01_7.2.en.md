# Mark 7 (Ζʹ) — Lemma 1 · Mark 7:2-3

> **And there gather together to him the Pharisees, and certain of the scribes, having come from Jerusalem, and seeing certain of his disciples eating loaves with common, that is, unwashed hands, they found fault. For the Pharisees and all the Jews, unless they wash their hands with the fist,[^1] do not eat, holding fast the tradition of the elders; and from (Mark 7:2-3)**
[^1]: Greek πυγμῇ, "with the fist," a notoriously obscure phrase; Theophylact glosses it below (Lemma 3) as washing "up to the elbow." Rendered here literally.
