# Mark 7 (Ζʹ) — Lemma 7 · Mark 7:13-21

> **And having called to him all the crowd, he said to them: Hear me, all of you, and understand: There is nothing outside the man entering into him which is able to defile[^1] him, but the things that go out from him, those are the things that defile the man. If anyone has ears to hear, let him hear. And when[^2] he entered into a house away from the crowd, his disciples were questioning him concerning the parable. And he says to them: Are you also thus without understanding? Do you not perceive that everything from outside entering into the man is not able to defile him, because it does not enter into his heart, but into the belly, and goes out into the privy,[^3] purging all the foods? And he said that: That which goes out from the man, that defiles the man. For from within, out of the heart of men, the evil thoughts go forth: adulteries, fornications, murders, thefts, covetousnesses, wickednesses, deceit, licentiousness, an evil eye, blasphemy, pride, foolishness; (Mark 7:13-21)**

[^1]: Greek κοινῶσαι (verb κοινόω), literally "to make common," i.e. to render ritually unclean; rendered "defile" throughout for consistency.
[^2]: The OCR reads Καὶ ὅτι; the sense (and the common text) requires ὅτε, "and when," which is followed here.
[^3]: Greek τὸν ἀφεδρῶνα, the latrine/privy (the "draught").
