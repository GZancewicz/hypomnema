# Mark 7 (Ζʹ) — Lemma 14 · Mark 7:24-28

> **And rising up from there, he went away into the confines of Tyre and Sidon, and, entering into a house, he wished no one to know *it*. And he could not be hidden. For a woman, whose little daughter had an unclean spirit, having heard about him, came and fell down at his feet (now the woman was a Greek, a Syrophoenician by race), and she kept asking him that he would cast the demon out of her daughter. But Jesus said to her: Let the children first be filled; for it is not good to take the children's bread and cast *it* to the little dogs. But she answered, and says to him: Yes, Lord; for even the little dogs under the table eat from the crumbs of the children. (Mark 7:24-28)**[^1]

[^1]: OCR reconstruction: the manuscript's «πρὸς τοὺς πόδας αὐτοῦν δὲ ἡ γυνὴ» has run together «αὐτοῦ (ἦν δὲ ἡ γυνὴ», restored above ("at his feet (now the woman was…"). Likewise «Ναὶ, Κύρις» → «Ναί, Κύριε» and «ἀπὸ τῶν ὡινίων πῶω παιδίων» → «ἀπὸ τῶν ψιχίων τῶν παιδίων».
