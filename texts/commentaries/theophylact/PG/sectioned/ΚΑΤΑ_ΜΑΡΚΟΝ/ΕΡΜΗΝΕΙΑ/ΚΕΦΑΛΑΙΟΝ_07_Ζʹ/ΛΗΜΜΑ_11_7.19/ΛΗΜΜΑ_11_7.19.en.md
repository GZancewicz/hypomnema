# Mark 7 (Ζʹ) — Lemma 11 · Mark 7:19

> **enter into his heart, but into the belly, and goes out into the privy,[^1] purging all the foods? And he said that: That which goes out from the man, that defiles[^2] the man. (Mark 7:19)**

[^1]: Greek τὸν ἀφεδρῶνα, the latrine/privy (the "draught").
[^2]: Greek κοινοῖ (verb κοινόω), literally "makes common," i.e. renders ritually unclean; rendered "defiles."
