# Mark 7 (Ζʹ) — Lemma 17 · Mark 7:31-32

> **And again, having gone out from the borders of Tyre and Sidon, he came to the sea of Galilee, through the midst of the borders of Decapolis, and they bring to him a deaf man who spoke with difficulty, and they beseech him that he would lay his hand upon him, and (Mark 7:31-32)**[^1]

[^1]: «μογιλάλον», rendered "who spoke with difficulty" (a speech impediment); the term denotes one who speaks only with hardship.
