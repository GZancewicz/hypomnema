# Mark 7 (Ζʹ) — Lemma 10 · Mark 7:17

> **he entered into a house away from the crowd, his disciples were questioning him concerning the parable. And he says to them: Are you also thus without understanding? Do you not perceive that everything from outside entering into the man is not able to defile[^1] him, because it does not (Mark 7:17)**

[^1]: Greek κοινῶσαι (verb κοινόω), literally "to make common," i.e. to render ritually unclean; rendered "defile."
