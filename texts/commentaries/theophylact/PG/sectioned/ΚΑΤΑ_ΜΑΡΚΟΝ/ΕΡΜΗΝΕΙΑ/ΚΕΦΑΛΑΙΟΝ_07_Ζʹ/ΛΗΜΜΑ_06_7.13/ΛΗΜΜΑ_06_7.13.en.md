# Mark 7 (Ζʹ) — Lemma 6 · Mark 7:13

> **making void the word of God by your tradition which you have handed down, and you do many such like things." (Mark 7:13)**

The Lord, reproving the Jews more weightily, brings forward also the prophet accusing them. They, therefore, were charging the disciples with transgressing the tradition of the elders, but the Lord brings against them in turn a more vehement accusation, namely, the transgressing of the law of Moses. For the law says, it declares: Honor your father and your mother; but you teach the children to say to their parents, that "It is Corban, that is, a Gift and a thing dedicated to God, whatever you seek from me." For the Pharisees, wishing to devour the goods of the simpler folk, taught the children — if they had anything as it were a private property[^1] and their parents sought this — to say, that "I have already consecrated this to God, and seek it no longer, since it has been dedicated to the Lord." Thus, therefore, deceiving the children, and persuading them ostensibly to dedicate to God what they had, they made the fathers to be despised, while they themselves devoured the things dedicated. This, therefore, the Lord charges against them, that for the sake of gain they transgress the law of God.

[^1]: Greek πεκούλιον, a Latin loanword (*peculium*): private property or personal savings, especially that of a son or slave held apart from the head of the household.
