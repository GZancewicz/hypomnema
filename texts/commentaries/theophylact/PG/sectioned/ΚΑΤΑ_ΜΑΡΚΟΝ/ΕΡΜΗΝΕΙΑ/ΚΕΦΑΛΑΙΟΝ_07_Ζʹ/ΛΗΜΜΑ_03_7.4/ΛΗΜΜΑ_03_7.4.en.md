# Mark 7 (Ζʹ) — Lemma 3 · Mark 7:4

> **the marketplace, unless they wash themselves, they do not eat; and there are many other things which they received to hold fast: washings of cups and pots[^1] and copper vessels and couches. Then the Pharisees and the scribes question him, "Why do your disciples not walk according to the tradition of the elders, but eat bread with unwashed hands?" (Mark 7:4-5)**

The disciples of the Lord, having been taught to cling to virtue alone, and to busy themselves with nothing else besides, ate with unwashed hands, without fuss and simply. The Pharisees therefore, wishing to find a pretext for mockery, seize upon this; and they do not fault them as transgressing the law, but the tradition of the elders. For it is not written in the law to wash with the fist,[^2] that is, up to the elbow (for "fist" is said of the part from the elbow up to the very tips of the fingers), but they had this handed down to them from the elders.

[^1]: Greek ξεστῶν, from ξέστης (a Roman *sextarius*), a measuring vessel or pot; rendered "pots."
[^2]: Greek πυγμῇ. The literal sense "with the fist" is obscure; Theophylact takes it to mean washing the forearm up to the elbow.
