# Mark 7 (Ζʹ) — Lemma 4 · Mark 7:6-12

> **But he answering said to them that, "Well did Isaiah prophesy concerning you hypocrites, as it is written: This people honors me with the lips, but their heart is far away from me; and in vain do they worship me, teaching for teachings the commandments of men. For, having left the commandment of God, you hold fast the tradition of men, washings of pots and cups, and many other such like things you do." And he said to them, "Well do you set aside the commandment of God, that you may keep your own tradition. For Moses said: Honor your father and your mother; and, He that speaks evil of father or mother, let him die the death. But you say: If a man say to his father or mother, Corban (which is, A gift), whatever from me you might have been profited — and you no longer allow him to do anything for his father or his mother, (Mark 7:6-12)**
