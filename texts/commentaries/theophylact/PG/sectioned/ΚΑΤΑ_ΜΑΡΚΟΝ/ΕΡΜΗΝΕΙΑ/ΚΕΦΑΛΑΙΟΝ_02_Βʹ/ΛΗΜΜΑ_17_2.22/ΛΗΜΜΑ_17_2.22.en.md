# Mark 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 17 · Mark 2:22

> **no one puts new wine into old wineskins; otherwise the new wine bursts the wineskins, and the wine is poured out, and the wineskins are destroyed; but new wine is to be put into new wineskins. (Mark 2:22)**[^1]

Just as, he says, if an unshrunk patch — that is, a new one — were sewn onto an old garment, it tears it apart by its own stiffness, and new wine by its own heat bursts the old wineskins, so also my disciples have not yet become strong, so that if we should burden them, we will harm them. For they resemble old garments because of the weakness of their resolve. Therefore one ought not to lay upon them the heavy injunction of fasting; or you may understand it also thus: The disciples of Christ, being new, cannot be enslaved to old customs and laws.

[^1]: The lemma is corrupt at the close (ἀλλμ αἷυα. νίον); it is reconstructed as ἀλλὰ οἶνον νέον εἰς ἀσκοὺς καινοὺς βλητέον, "but new wine is to be put into new wineskins."
