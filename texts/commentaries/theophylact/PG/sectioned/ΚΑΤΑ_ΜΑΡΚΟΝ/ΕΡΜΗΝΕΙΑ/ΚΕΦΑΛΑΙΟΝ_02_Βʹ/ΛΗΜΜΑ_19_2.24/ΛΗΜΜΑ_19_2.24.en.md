# Mark 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 19 · Mark 2:24-26

> **The Pharisees were saying to him: "Behold, why do they do on the Sabbath what is not permitted?" And he was saying to them: The Sabbath came to be for the sake of man, not man for the sake of the Sabbath; so that..." (Mark 2:24-26)**
