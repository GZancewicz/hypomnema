# Mark 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 14 · Mark 2:19

> **Jesus said to them: Can the sons of the bridechamber, in which the bridegroom is with them, fast? As long a time as they have the bridegroom with themselves, they cannot fast. (Mark 2:19)**[^1]

[^1]: The lemma reads νησταύειν, corrected to νηστεύειν ("to fast").
