# Mark 10 (Ιʹ) — Lemma 3 · Mark 10:10

> **For of such is the kingdom of God. (Mark 10:14)**

Observe, he did not say, For of *these* is the kingdom, but of *such*—that is, of those who have from discipline the guilelessness which little children have from nature. For the little child neither envies, nor bears a grudge, nor, when whipped by its mother, does it stand apart from her. But even if she wears rags, it prefers her to the queen.
