# Mark 10 (Ιʹ) — Lemma 12 · Mark 10:10

> **—they shall mock him, and shall scourge him, and shall spit upon him, and shall kill him, and on the third day he shall rise again. (Mark 10:10)**

For what reason does he foretell the things about to befall him? In order to smooth beforehand the mind of the disciples, so that, having heard beforehand, they might bear it more easily, and not be struck down by the suddenness of it, and so that they might know that he does so willingly and does not flee — it being plain that he voluntarily delivers himself up to the sufferings. And having taken the disciples privately, he speaks to them alone. For the passion, being a mystery, had to be revealed to the more intimate ones. For this reason, then, he also goes ahead of all on the way, wishing to separate his disciples from the rest of the crowd. At the same time, also, by going before all and getting ahead on the way, he shows that he runs toward the passion, and does not flee the death on behalf of our salvation; but even if he recounts all the gloomy things, yet of all those there is one consolation: the *word*, And on the third day he shall rise again.
