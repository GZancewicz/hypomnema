# Mark 10 (Ιʹ) — Lemma 19 · Mark 10:10

> **And they come to Jericho, and as he was going out from Jericho, along with his disciples and a considerable crowd, the son of Timaeus, Bartimaeus the blind man, was sitting beside the road begging. And having heard that it is Jesus the Nazarene, he began to cry out and to say: Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me. And (Mark 10:10)**[^1]

[^1]: The OCR carries several minor corruptions here, all readily emended: «Ἱσοῦς» for Ἰησοῦς and «Δαβὶὲ» for Δαβίδ. The lemma order is «Ὁ υἱὸς Δαβὶδ Ἰησοῦ, ἐλέησόν με» (literally "The Son of David, Jesus, have mercy on me"); rendered here as a vocative address.
