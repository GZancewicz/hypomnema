# Mark 10 (Ιʹ) — Lemma 16 · Mark 10:10

> **—to sit at my right hand and at my left, is not mine to give, but to those for whom it has been prepared. (Mark 10:10)**

"The martyrdom," he says, "you shall undergo, and you shall die the death that is for the sake of the truth. But the sitting is not mine to give." And here two things are inquired into. One is this: Has the sitting been prepared for someone? and the other, Is the Master of all unable to give this seat? We say, therefore, that no one will sit at the right or at the left. But even if in many places of Scripture you hear of seats, do not think of a seat, but of surpassing honor. And the phrase, *It is not mine to give*, has this meaning: It is not for me, the just judge, to give you this honor by favor; for otherwise I would not be just; but for those who have contended, for them this honor has been prepared. It is just as if a just king were presiding over some contest, and then some friends of his approached him and said, "Give us the crowns," he would say, "It is not mine to give, but if anyone contends and conquers, for him the crown has been prepared." So that you also, sons of Zebedee, will indeed bear witness for me; but if anyone should be found who, along with the martyrdom, has also all the other virtue beyond you, that one will take precedence over you.
