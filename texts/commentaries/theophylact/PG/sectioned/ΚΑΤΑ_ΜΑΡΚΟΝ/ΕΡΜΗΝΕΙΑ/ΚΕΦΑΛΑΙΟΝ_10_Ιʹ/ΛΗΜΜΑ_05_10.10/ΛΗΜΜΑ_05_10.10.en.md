# Mark 10 (Ιʹ) — Lemma 5 · Mark 10:10

> **Come, you blessed of my Father. (Matt. 25:34)**

But by "kingdom of God" he here means the preaching and the proclamation of good things. Whoever therefore receives the preaching of God as a little child—that is, in no way doubting or disbelieving—that one enters into the kingdom of God, and into the good things which he has already received by faith.
