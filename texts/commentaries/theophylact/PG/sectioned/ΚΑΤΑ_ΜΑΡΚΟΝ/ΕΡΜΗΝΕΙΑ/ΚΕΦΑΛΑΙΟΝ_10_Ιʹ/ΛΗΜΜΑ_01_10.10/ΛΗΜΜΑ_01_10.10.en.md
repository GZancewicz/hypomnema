# Mark 10 (Ιʹ) — Lemma 1 · Mark 10:10

> **And in the house again his disciples questioned him about the same thing, and he says to them: Whoever should divorce his wife and marry another commits adultery against her. And if a woman should divorce her husband and be married to another, she commits adultery. (Mark 10:10-12)**

The disciples were still being scandalized; therefore for this reason they also come to him again and ask, as though their reasoning had not yet been sufficiently healed.[^1] But he answers them, saying: Whoever should divorce his wife and marry another is an adulterer with respect to her, the second woman. Likewise therefore also the woman is an adulteress, she who has left her own husband and been joined to another.

[^1]: The OCR here reads ἀκθεραπευθέντος, which is corrupt; the intended word is almost certainly ἀποθεραπευθέντος (from ἀποθεραπεύω, "to heal thoroughly, cure completely"), rendered here "healed."
