# Mark 10 (Ιʹ) — Lemma 18 · Mark 10:10

> **—to become first shall be servant of all. For indeed the Son of Man came not to be served, but to serve, and to give his life a ransom for many. (Mark 10:10)**

The disciples, still being disposed in a human manner, were assailed by envy as well. Wherefore they also are indignant against the two. But when? When they saw them not accepted by the Lord, but, as it were, pushed away, then they are indignant. For as long as they were honored by the Lord, they bore it without being indignant, although they saw the two being preferred; but since here those very men asked for the honor, the rest no longer bear it. But now they were thus in an imperfect state, whereas later you will see them even yielding the first places to one another. But Christ heals them: first by soothing them, by drawing them near to himself—for this is what *having called them to himself* signifies; then by showing that to snatch at honor, and to love the first places, is heathen. For the rulers of the nations, they conduct themselves in an authoritarian and tyrannical manner. But my disciples, not so; rather, let the one who wishes to be great serve all, since this too belongs to a great soul: to endure all and to be a slave to all. And the example is close at hand: for indeed the Son of Man came not to be served, but to serve, and to give his life a ransom for many—which is greater than to serve; for when someone not only serves, but also dies for the one being served, what is greater or more wonderful than this? But nevertheless this service and this condescension became an exaltation and glory both for himself and for all. For before he became man, he was known only among the angels. But since he became man and was crucified, he has not only that glory, but has also taken on another besides, and reigns over the whole inhabited world.
