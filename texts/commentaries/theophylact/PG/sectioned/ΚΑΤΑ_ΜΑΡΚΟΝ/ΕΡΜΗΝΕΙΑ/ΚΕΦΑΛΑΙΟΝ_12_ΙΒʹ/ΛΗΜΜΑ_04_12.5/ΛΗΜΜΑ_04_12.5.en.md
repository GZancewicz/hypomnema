# Mark 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 4 · Mark 12:5

> **again he sent another, and him they killed, and many others, some beating, some killing. Still therefore having one beloved son[^1] (Mark 12:5)**

[^1]: The Greek here runs on into the next lemma: «Ἔτι οὖν ἕνα υἱὸν ἔχων ἀγαπητὸν αὐτοῦ» ("Still therefore having one son, his beloved"). The OCR reads να υἱὸν for ἕνα υἱὸν.
