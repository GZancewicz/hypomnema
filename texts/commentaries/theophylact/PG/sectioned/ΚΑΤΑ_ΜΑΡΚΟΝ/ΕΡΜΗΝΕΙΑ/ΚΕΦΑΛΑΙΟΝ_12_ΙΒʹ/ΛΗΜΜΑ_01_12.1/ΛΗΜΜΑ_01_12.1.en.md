# Mark 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 1 · Mark 12:1-8

> **And he began to speak to them in parables: A man planted a vineyard, and put a hedge around it, and dug a wine-vat, and built a tower. And he let it out to husbandmen, and went abroad, and sent to the husbandmen at the season a servant, that from the husbandmen he might receive of the fruit of the vineyard. But they, having taken him, beat *him* and sent *him* away empty. And again he sent to them another servant, and him they stoned and struck on the head,[^1] and sent *him* away dishonored. And again he sent another, and him they killed, and many others, some beating, some killing. Still therefore having one beloved son, he sent him also to them last, saying, They will respect my son. But those husbandmen said among themselves, This is the heir. Come, let us kill him, and the inheritance shall be ours. And having taken him, they killed *him* and cast *him* out of the vineyard. (Mark 12:1-8)**

[^1]: Greek ἐκεφαλαίωσαν, from κεφαλή ("head"): "wounded/struck on the head." The verb elsewhere means "to sum up," so the sense here is a play on the noun rather than the ordinary lexical meaning.
