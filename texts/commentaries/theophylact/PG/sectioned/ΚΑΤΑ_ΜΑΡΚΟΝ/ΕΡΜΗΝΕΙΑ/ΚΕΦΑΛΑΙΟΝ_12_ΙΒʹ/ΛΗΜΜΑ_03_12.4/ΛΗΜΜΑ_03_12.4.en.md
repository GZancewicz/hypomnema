# Mark 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 3 · Mark 12:4

> **again he sent to them another servant, and him they stoned and struck on the head,[^1] and sent *him* away dishonored. And (Mark 12:4)**

[^1]: Greek ἐκεφαλαίωσαν, from κεφαλή ("head"): "wounded/struck on the head." The verb elsewhere means "to sum up," so the sense here is a play on the noun rather than the ordinary lexical meaning.
