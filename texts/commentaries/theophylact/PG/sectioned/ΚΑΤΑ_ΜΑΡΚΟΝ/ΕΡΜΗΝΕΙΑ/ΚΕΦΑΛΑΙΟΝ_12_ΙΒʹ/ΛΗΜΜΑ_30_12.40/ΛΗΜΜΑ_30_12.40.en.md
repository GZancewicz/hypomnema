# Mark 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 30 · Mark 12:40

> **who devour the houses of the widows, and for a pretext pray at length; these will receive greater condemnation. (Mark 12:40)**

Since he was about to come to the Passion, [...][^1] the opinion of the scribes, who supposed the Christ to be son of David but not Lord. Therefore he demonstrates him to be God, from the very words of David, and not simply, but instilling fear, through saying,

[^1]: The Greek is defective by an OCR lacuna between ἔρχεσθαι and ‑ματέων: «Ἐπειδὴ πρὲς (πρὸς) τὸ πάθος ἔμελλεν ἔρχεσθαι … ‑ματέων δόξαν». The surviving ‑ματέων is the tail of Γραμματέων ("of the scribes"), and the lost words evidently governed δόξαν ("the opinion"), e.g. that the Lord overturns/corrects the scribes' opinion. Only the legible remainder is translated.
