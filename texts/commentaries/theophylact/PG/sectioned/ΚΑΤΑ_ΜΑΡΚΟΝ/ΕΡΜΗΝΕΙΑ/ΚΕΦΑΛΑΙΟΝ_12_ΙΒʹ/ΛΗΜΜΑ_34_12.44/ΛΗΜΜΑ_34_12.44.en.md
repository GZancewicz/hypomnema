# Mark 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 34 · Mark 12:44

> **For all cast in out of that which abounded to them, but she, out of her want, cast in all that she had, her whole living. (Mark 12:44)**

A wonderful custom prevailed among the Jews, that those who had and were willing would cast into the treasury in the temple (for this was called the *gazophylakion*), in order that both the priests and the poor and the widows might be nourished from these things. Many, therefore, doing such a thing, a widow also, having come forward, displayed her own resolve as more abundant than her ability. Glory to you, O Christ, who also receive the small things above the great, oftentimes. May it come to pass that my soul too become a widow, having cast off Satan, to whom it was joined, and cast into the sacred treasury two lepta—the flesh and the mind—having made them fine,[^1] the flesh through self-control, and the mind through humility, in order that I too may hear that I have made my whole life sacred, having left behind no thought or movement to the world.

[^1]: A wordplay: the two coins are λεπτά, and the verb is λεπτύνασαν, "having made thin/fine, refined." The soul offers as its two λεπτά the flesh and the mind, each "made λεπτόν" through ascetic refinement.
