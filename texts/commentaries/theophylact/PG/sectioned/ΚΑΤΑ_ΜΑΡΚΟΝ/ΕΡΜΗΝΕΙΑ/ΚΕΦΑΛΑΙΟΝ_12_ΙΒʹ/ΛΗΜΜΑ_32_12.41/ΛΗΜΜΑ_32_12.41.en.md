# Mark 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 32 · Mark 12:41-43

> **Jesus, having sat down opposite the treasury, was watching how the crowd casts copper into the treasury. And many rich cast in much, and one poor widow, having come, was casting in two lepta, which is a kodrantes.[^1] And having called to him his disciples, he says to them: Amen I say to you that this poor widow has cast in more than all those who cast into the treasury. (Mark 12:41-43)**

[^1]: Greek λεπτὰ δύο, ὅ ἐστι κοδράντης. The λεπτόν was the smallest bronze coin; the κοδράντης is the Roman *quadrans*. Both terms are retained transliterated here, as the coins have no exact English equivalent.
