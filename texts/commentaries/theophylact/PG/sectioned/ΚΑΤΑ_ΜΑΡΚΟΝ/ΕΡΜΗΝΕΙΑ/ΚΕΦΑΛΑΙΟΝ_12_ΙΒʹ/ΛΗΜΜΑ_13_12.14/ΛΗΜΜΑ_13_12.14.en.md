# Mark 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 13 · Mark 12:14

> **coming, they say to him: Teacher, we know that you are true, and you care about no one; for you do not look upon the face of men, but in truth you teach the way of God. Is it lawful to give tribute[^1] to Caesar, or not? Should we give, or should we not give? But he, (Mark 12:14)**

[^1]: Greek κῆνσον, the Latin loanword *census*, the head- or poll-tax paid to the Roman state.
