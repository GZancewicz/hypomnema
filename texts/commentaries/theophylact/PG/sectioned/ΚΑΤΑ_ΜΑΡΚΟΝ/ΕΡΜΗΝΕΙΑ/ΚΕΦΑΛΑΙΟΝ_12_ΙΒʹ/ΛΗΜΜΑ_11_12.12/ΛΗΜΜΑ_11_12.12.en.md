# Mark 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 11 · Mark 12:12

> **…they sought to seize him, and they feared the crowd. For they knew that he had spoken the parable against them. And leaving him, they went away. (Mark 12:12)**

Through all these things he shows the Jews cast off, but the nations received. For the stone is the Lord himself, and the builders are the teachers of the people. Therefore, whom the teachers rejected, this one became the head of the corner, having become the head of the Church. For the Church is a corner, as joining together and making one all Jews and Greeks, just as the corner also joins two walls to one another, mediating between both. And this corner, that is the Church, came to be from the Lord, and it is marvelous in the eyes of us the faithful. For to the unbelieving even the miracles are held in suspicion. The Church, then, is marvelous, as having been established out of miracles, the Lord working together with the apostles and confirming the word through the signs that followed (Mark 16:20).
