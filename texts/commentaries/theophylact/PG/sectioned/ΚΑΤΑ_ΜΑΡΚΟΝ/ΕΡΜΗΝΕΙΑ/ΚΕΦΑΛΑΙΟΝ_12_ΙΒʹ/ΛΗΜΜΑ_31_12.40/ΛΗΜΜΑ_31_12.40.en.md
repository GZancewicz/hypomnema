# Mark 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 31 · Mark 12:40

> **Until I set your enemies as a footstool of your feet. (Mark 12:36)**

But these were assuredly his enemies, whom God and Father set as a footstool of the feet of Christ. And observe also how he asks. For he did not say, *What* think you concerning me, but, concerning the Christ? so as not to strike them. But neither can you say to me that David said this apart from the Spirit, but in the Spirit he called him Lord, that is, being moved by the grace of the Spirit. How therefore is Christ the son of David, and not also his lord and God?

The crowd therefore heard him gladly. And he said to them—I mean to those of the crowd—Beware of the scribes (Mark 12:38-40), who wear the more stately robes, and wish to be preferred above all, and who accept the greetings and the acclamations in the marketplaces, and all the other things they practice for glory, who also devour the houses of the widows. For they would insinuate themselves upon the unprotected women, as though they would be their protectors, and on the pretext of praying at length, and with an outward show of piety and by hypocrisy deceiving the more simple, they devoured the houses of the rich. These, then, shall be condemned more than the other Jews who sin. For the mighty shall be mightily examined.[^1] And at the same time, through these things he also teaches the apostles not to conduct themselves after the manner of the scribes, but to imitate himself. For ordaining them as teachers, he reasonably also lays down instruction for them concerning their manner of life. And

[^1]: An allusion to Wisdom of Solomon 6:6, δυνατοὶ δὲ δυνατῶς ἐτασθήσονται, "the mighty shall be mightily tested/scrutinized." Theophylact reproduces the wording nearly verbatim: δυνατῶς ἐτασθήσονται.
