# Mark 15 (ΙΕʹ) — Lemma 7 · Mark 15:8-13

> **The crowd, having cried aloud, began to ask, just as he always did for them. But Pilate answered them, saying: Do you wish that I release[^1] to you the King of the Jews? For[^2] he knew that for envy the chief priests had delivered him up. But the chief priests stirred up the crowd, that he should rather release Barabbas[^3] to them. But Pilate, answering, again said to them: What then do you wish that I do with *him* whom you call King of the Jews? But they again cried out: Crucify[^4] him. (Mark 15:8-13)**

[^1]: The OCR reads «ἁπολύσοος»; the intended form is «ἀπολύσω», "(that) I release," a deliberative subjunctive after «θέλετε».
[^2]: OCR «δα» at the line-break stands for «διὰ» in «διὰ φθόνον», "for/on account of envy."
[^3]: The OCR reads «Βαραεᾶν»; read «Βαραββᾶν», "Barabbas."
[^4]: The text reads «Σταύρωσαν αὐτόν». Taken as a command from the crowd this is the aorist imperative «Σταύρωσον αὐτόν», "Crucify him" (the received reading); the printed «-αν» is an itacistic spelling.
