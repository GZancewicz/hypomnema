# Mark 15 (ΙΕʹ) — Lemma 1 · Mark 15:1-7

> **And immediately in the morning, the chief priests, having made a council with the elders and scribes, and the whole Sanhedrin, having bound Jesus, carried *him* away and delivered *him* to Pilate. And Pilate questioned him: Are you the king of the Jews? But he answering said to him: You say *it*.[^1] And the chief priests were accusing him of many things. But Pilate again questioned him, saying: Do you answer nothing? See how many things they testify against you. But Jesus answered nothing more, so that Pilate marveled. Now at *the* feast he used to release to them one prisoner, whomever they requested. And there was the *one* called Barabbas, bound with the fellow-insurgents,[^2] who in the insurrection had committed murder. And… (Mark 15:1-7)**

[^1]: Greek Σὺ λέγεις, literally "You say," here an idiom of affirmation — "You say *it*," i.e. "It is as you say."
[^2]: Greek συστασιαστῶν, "fellow-insurgents," those who had taken part in the same στάσις (uprising). The OCR of this batch is corrupt at several points (e.g. Γραιαματέων for Γραμματέων "scribes"; Αέσμιον for δέσμιον "prisoner"; Ἥν for Ἦν "there was"; Ἀν τῇ στάσει for ἐν τῇ στάσει); the legible reading has been reconstructed and translated.
