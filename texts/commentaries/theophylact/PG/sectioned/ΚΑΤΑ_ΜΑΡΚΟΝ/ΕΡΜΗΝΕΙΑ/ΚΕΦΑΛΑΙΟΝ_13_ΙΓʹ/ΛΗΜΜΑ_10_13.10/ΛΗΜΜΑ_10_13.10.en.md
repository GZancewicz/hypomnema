# Mark 13 (ΙΓʹ) — Lemma 10 · Mark 13:10

> **Into all the earth their sound went forth, and unto the ends of the inhabited world their words. (Rom. 10:18)**[^1]

And this too came about for the greater condemnation of the Jews—the being preached everywhere before the capture. For they ought, having seen how in a short time the preaching ran through into all the world, to have recognized the power of God, and to have repented, and to have been released from their calamities; but they did not understand. Therefore their judgment also is the greater.

[^1]: Theophylact cites the words as Paul's (ἄκουε τοῦ Παύλου), i.e. Rom. 10:18, which itself quotes Ps. 19:4 (LXX Ps. 18:5).
