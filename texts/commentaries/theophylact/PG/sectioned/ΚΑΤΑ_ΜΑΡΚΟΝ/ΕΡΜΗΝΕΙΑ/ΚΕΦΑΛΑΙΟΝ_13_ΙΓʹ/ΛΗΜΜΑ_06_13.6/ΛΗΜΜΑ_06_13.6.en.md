# Mark 13 (ΙΓʹ) — Lemma 6 · Mark 13:6

> **for many shall come in my name, saying that, I am the Christ, and they shall lead many astray. But when you hear of wars and rumors of wars, be not troubled; these things must come to pass, but the end is not yet. (Mark 13:6-7)**[^1]

[^1]: The printed Greek of the latter half is badly corrupted — «ὅταν δὲ ἀκοῖσητε πωλέιλοιλκαὶ ἀκοὰς πολέμων»; read «ὅταν δὲ ἀκούσητε πολέμους καὶ ἀκοὰς πολέμων» ("but when you hear of wars and rumors of wars"). His lemma adds Χριστός to «Ἐγώ εἰμι» ("I am *the Christ*"), retained here; the reading also carries over into Mark 13:7.
