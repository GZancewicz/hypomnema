# Mark 13 (ΙΓʹ) — Lemma 17 · Mark 13:17

> **But woe to those who are with child and to those who are nursing in those days. (Mark 13:17)**

The abomination of desolation is the statue of the one who captured the city. For every idol is called an abomination. And "of desolation," because, when Jerusalem was captured and suffering desolation, they set it up within the innermost sanctuary of the temple. But Pilate too, having brought the images of Caesar into the temple by night, became the cause of great tumult to the multitude. From that time began both the war and the desolation of Jerusalem. Then, accordingly, let those who are in Judea flee to the mountains. And well did he say, "those in Judea." For the apostles were not in Judea, but, as we have said, before the war they were driven out from Jerusalem; or rather, they themselves went out, moved by the divine Spirit. Those, then, who are found in Judea, let them flee, and he who is upon the housetop, let him not turn back for any of the things in the house. For it is much to be desired if one should be brought safely through even with only his bare body. But also to those who have children and to those who are pregnant, woe. Why? Because those who have children, held fast by their affection toward them, will not be able to flee, and those who are with child, because of the burden of childbearing, neither will these have easy flight. And I think that here he also signifies the eating of children. For, besieged in the famine, they laid hands even upon their own children.
