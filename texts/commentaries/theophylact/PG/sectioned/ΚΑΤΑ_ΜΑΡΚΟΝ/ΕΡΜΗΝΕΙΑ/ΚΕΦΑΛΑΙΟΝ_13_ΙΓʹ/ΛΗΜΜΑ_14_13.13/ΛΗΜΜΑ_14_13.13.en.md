# Mark 13 (ΙΓʹ) — Lemma 14 · Mark 13:14-16

> **But when you see the abomination of desolation, spoken of by Daniel the prophet, standing where it ought not — let the reader understand — then let those in Judea flee to the mountains; and he who is upon the housetop, let him not go down into the house, nor enter to take anything out of his house, and he who is in the field, let him not turn back to the things behind, to take up his garment. (Mark 13:14-16)**[^1]

[^1]: The folder ref for this fragment reads "13.13," but its content is unmistakably Mark 13:14 onward (Ὅταν δὲ ἴδητε τὸ βδέλυγμα...); the range 13:14-16 reflects the merged comment-less reading.
