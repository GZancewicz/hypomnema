# Mark 13 (ΙΓʹ) — Lemma 13 · Mark 13:13

> **you will be hated by all on account of my name. But he who endures to the end, this one will be saved. (Mark 13:13)**

For before the war was completely kindled, what did the apostles not suffer at their hands, being brought before tribunals and led away before kings — Herod and Agrippa and Nero? "Therefore do not be anxious beforehand what you shall answer in defense, but in that very hour the Holy Spirit will give you what you shall speak" (cf. Mark 13:11). And he proclaims beforehand to them also the worst thing of all:[^1] that you shall be warred upon even by your nearest kin. But he says this so that, having heard it beforehand, they may be prepared and thereafter bear the terror lightly. Then [comes] the consolation, [namely] this: "On account of me you shall be hated." For to be hated on account of him is sufficient to lighten all misfortunes. But that the one who has endured to the end shall be saved — this too is the greatest encouragement.

[^1]: The OCR here is corrupt, "τὸ πάντων χείριστ. κ,"; reconstructed as τὸ πάντων χείριστον, "the worst thing of all."
