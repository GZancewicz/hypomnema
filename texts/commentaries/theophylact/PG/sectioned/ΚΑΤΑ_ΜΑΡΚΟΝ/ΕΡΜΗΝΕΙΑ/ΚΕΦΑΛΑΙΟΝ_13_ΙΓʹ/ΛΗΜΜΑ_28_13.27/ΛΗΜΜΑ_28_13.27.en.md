# Mark 13 (ΙΓʹ) — Lemma 28 · Mark 13:27-30

> **And then he will send forth his angels, and will gather together his elect from the four winds,[^1] from the end of the earth to the end of heaven. But from the fig tree learn the parable: when its branch has already become tender, and puts forth the leaves, you know that summer is near. So you also, when you see these things happening, know that it is near, at the doors. Truly I say to you that this generation will not pass away until all these things come to pass. (Mark 13:27-30)**

[^1]: Greek «τεσσάρων» ("four"); the word is corrupted by OCR in the manuscript to «σεααὰι.λνιν».
