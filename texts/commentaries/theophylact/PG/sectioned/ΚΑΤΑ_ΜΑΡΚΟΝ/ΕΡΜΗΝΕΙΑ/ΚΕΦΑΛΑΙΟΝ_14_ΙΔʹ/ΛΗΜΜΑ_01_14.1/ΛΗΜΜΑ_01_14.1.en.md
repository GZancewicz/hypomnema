# Mark 14 (ΙΔʹ) — Lemma 1 · Mark 14:1-4

> **The Passover and the unleavened bread, after two days. And the chief priests and the Scribes were seeking how, having seized him by guile, they might kill him. But they said, Not on the feast, lest there be an uproar of the people. And while he was in Bethany, in the house of Simon the leper, as he reclined *at table*, there came a woman having an alabaster jar of ointment of pure nard, very costly. And having broken the alabaster jar, she poured it out upon his head. But there were some who were indignant among themselves and saying, To what end has this waste of the ointment come about? (Mark 14:1-4)**
