# Mark 14 (ΙΔʹ) — Lemma 19 · Mark 14:21

> **…*the Son* of man goes, as it has been written concerning him; but woe to that man through whom the Son of man is betrayed; it were good for him, if that man had not been born. (Mark 14:21)**

How were they reclining, when the law commands to eat the Passover standing?[^1] It is likely, therefore, that first he completed the legal *Passover*, then reclined, being about to hand over his own Passover. [^1]: On eating the Passover standing, cf. Exodus 12:11 (LXX ἐσθίεσθε ... ἑστηκότες). Theophylact raises the difficulty that the Lord and the disciples reclined at table.
