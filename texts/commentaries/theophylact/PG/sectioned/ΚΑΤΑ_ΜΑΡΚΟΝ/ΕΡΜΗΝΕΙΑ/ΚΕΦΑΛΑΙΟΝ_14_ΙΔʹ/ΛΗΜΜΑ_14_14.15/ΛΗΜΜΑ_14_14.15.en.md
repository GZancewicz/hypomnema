# Mark 14 (ΙΔʹ) — Lemma 14 · Mark 14:15

> **…he will show you a large upper room, furnished and ready; there prepare for us." (Mark 14:15)**

By *the first day of the Unleavened Bread* he means the fifth [i.e. Thursday], which was before the [days of] Unleavened Bread. For the unleavened breads were eaten on the Preparation day.[^1] The disciples therefore approach, asking where they might eat the Passover. And from this it is clear that Christ had no lodging of his own, but neither did the disciples have houses; for if *they had*, they would have taken him in there. And he sends two of his disciples, Peter and John, as Luke says (Luke 22:8), to an unknown man, showing them that he was able not to suffer. For he who persuaded the mind of this unknown *man* to receive him, what would he not have accomplished among the others? And he gives them also a sign for finding the house: to follow a man carrying a jar of water.

But these things can also be raised to a higher sense. A jar of water is carried by the one who has been baptized; and he departs into a house, the state befitting rational beings. For he who carries baptism comes to rest, living according to reason, and, as it were resting in a house, reposing in this state. And the master of the house is the mind, who shows a large upper room, the height of his own thoughts. But this upper room is also *furnished*, that is: even if it is lofty, yet it has nothing rough or haughty, but has been spread out and made level by humility. There, then—in such a mind, I mean—the Passover is prepared for Christ by two disciples, Peter and John, that is, by action and by contemplation. For Peter, being fervent, is a man of action, whereas John is contemplative, as a theologian.

[^1]: Theophylact's chronology: he identifies "the first day of the Unleavened Bread" with the fifth day of the week (Thursday), reckoning the actual eating of the ἄζυμα (unleavened loaves) to the Preparation (παρασκευή, Friday). The Greek: «Πρώτην ἡμέραν τῶν Ἀζύμων, τὴν πέμπτην λέγει, ἥτις πρὸ τῶν Ἀζύμων ἦν. Τὰ γὰρ ἄζυμα τῇ παρασκευῇ ἠσθίοντο.»
