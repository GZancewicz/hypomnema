# Mark 14 (ΙΔʹ) — Lemma 25 · Mark 14:27-29

> **Jesus says to them, that: You will all be made to stumble[^1] because of me on this night, for it is written: I will strike the shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered. But after I have been raised, I will go before you into Galilee. But Peter said[^2] to him: Even if all will be made to stumble, yet not I. And— (Mark 14:27-29)**

[^1]: σκανδαλισθήσεσθε, "you will be made to stumble / caused to fall away"; rendered consistently as "made to stumble" here and in the exposition at Lemma 28. The quotation "I will strike the shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered" is from Zechariah (Zech. 13:7).
[^2]: The OCR reads a corrupt «Κοῃ» where the text requires a verb of speaking; reconstructed as ἔφη, "said" (so the common text of Mark 14:29).
