# Mark 14 (ΙΔʹ) — Lemma 20 · Mark 14:21

> **One of you will betray me.[^1] (Mark 14:21)**

For although they were outside this suffering, nevertheless they were in anguish, trusting God who knows the hearts more than themselves. And observe also the expression *He goes*. For it was as though the death of Christ were a going abroad, not a death. But the *It were good, if he had not been born* was said with respect to the punishment which the traitor will undergo. For it is better not to be born at all than to be born unto punishment. As far, therefore, as the outcome goes, it appears better if Judas had never existed at all. For God, on the one hand, made him for good works; but since he himself fell into so great a wickedness, it was better, as it seems, if he had not been born at all.

[^1]: The lemma is corrupt in the OCR («Ες ἐξμῶν παραδώσει με»); read «Εἷς ἐξ ὑμῶν παραδώσει με», "One of you will betray me."
