# Mark 14 (ΙΔʹ) — Lemma 10 · Mark 14:11

> **...having heard, rejoiced, and promised to give him money; and he sought how he might conveniently betray him.» (Mark 14:11)**

When the woman displayed her own generous devotion,[^1] then the disciple rages—the one of the twelve. For "one of the twelve" is not set down without purpose, but that it might show that he too was one of the select, being himself also chosen. And what is "that he might betray him"? That is, that he might inform them when he is alone; for they were afraid to fall upon him while teaching, because of the crowd. Therefore that one promised to betray him to them when he was set apart alone.

[^1]: Greek «φιλοτιμίαν», the woman's ambitious generosity, her lavish zeal to honor Christ.
