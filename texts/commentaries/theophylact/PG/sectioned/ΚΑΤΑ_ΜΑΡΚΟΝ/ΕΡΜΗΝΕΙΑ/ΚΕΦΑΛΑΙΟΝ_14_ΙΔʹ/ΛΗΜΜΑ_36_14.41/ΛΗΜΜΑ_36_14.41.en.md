# Mark 14 (ΙΔʹ) — Lemma 36 · Mark 14:41

> **he comes the third time, and says to them: Sleep on now and take your rest; it is enough.[^1] The hour has come; behold, the Son of Man is delivered into the hands of sinners. (Mark 14:41)**

[^1]: Greek ἀπέχει, an impersonal idiom notoriously hard to render: literally "it holds off / it is distant," commonly taken as "it is enough" or "the matter is settled." The chosen rendering keeps the traditional "it is enough."
