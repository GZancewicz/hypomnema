# Mark 11 (ΙΑʹ) — Lemma 19 · Mark 11:23

> **...to this *mountain*, "Be lifted up, and be cast into the sea," and does not doubt in his heart, but believes that what he says comes to pass — it shall be to him whatever he says. (Mark 11:23)**
