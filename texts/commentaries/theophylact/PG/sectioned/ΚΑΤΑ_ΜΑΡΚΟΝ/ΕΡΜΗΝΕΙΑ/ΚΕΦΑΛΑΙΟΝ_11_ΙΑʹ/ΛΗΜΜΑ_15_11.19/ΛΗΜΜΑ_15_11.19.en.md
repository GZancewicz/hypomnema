# Mark 11 (ΙΑʹ) — Lemma 15 · Mark 11:19-22

> **And when it became evening, he was going out outside the city. And in the morning, passing by, they saw the fig tree withered from the roots. And Peter, having remembered, says to him: Rabbi, behold, the fig tree which you cursed has withered. And Jesus, having answered, says to them: Have faith of God;[^1] for amen I say to you, that whoever says to the mountain (Mark 11:19-22)**

[^1]: πίστιν Θεοῦ — literally "faith of God" (objective genitive), i.e. "faith in God"; the literal rendering is retained.
