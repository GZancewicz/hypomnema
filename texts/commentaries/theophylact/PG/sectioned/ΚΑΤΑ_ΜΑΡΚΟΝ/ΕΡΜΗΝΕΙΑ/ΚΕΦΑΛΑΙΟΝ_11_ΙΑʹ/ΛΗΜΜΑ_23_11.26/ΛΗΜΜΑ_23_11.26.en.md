# Mark 11 (ΙΑʹ) — Lemma 23 · Mark 11:26

> **...you do not forgive, neither will your Father who is in the heavens forgive your trespasses." (Mark 11:26)**

He who believes in God with inward disposition[^1] has, throughout, lifted up his heart, and — that I may use David's phrase — has poured out his soul before God (cf. Ps. 41:5). And he who has lifted up his heart toward God is united to that one, and his heart, being warmed through, is fully assured that it will obtain its request. Whoever, then, has experienced[^2] this, that one will recognize it. And I think that all experience this, at least those who attend even moderately. For this reason, therefore, the Lord says that you shall receive all things whatsoever you ask with faith. For he who believes gives his whole self to God, conversing with him with tears and, as it were, grasping the feet of the Master in prayer. But do you wish to receive in yet another way what you ask? Forgive if your brother has sinned against you in anything. Do you see the easiness of God's gift?

[^1]: ἐνδιαθέτως, from ἐνδιάθετος: the inward, dispositional state of the soul, as opposed to what is merely outwardly expressed. Rendered here "with inward disposition," i.e. believing not with the lips only but from the depths of the heart.

[^2]: The OCR reads ἔπαυε ("was ceasing"), which yields no sense here; read ἔπαθε, "has experienced," matching the following πάσχειν ("experience") — hence the rendering "has experienced."
