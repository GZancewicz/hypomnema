# Mark 11 (ΙΑʹ) — Lemma 1 · Mark 11:2-5

> **He says to them: Go into the village opposite you; and immediately, as you are entering into it, you will find a colt tied, on which no man has sat. Having loosed it, bring it, and if anyone should say to you, "Why are you doing this?" say that the Lord has need of it, and immediately he will send it back here. And they went away, and found a colt tied at the door outside, in the open street, and they loose it. And some of those standing there were saying to them, "What are you doing, loosing the colt?" (Mark 11:2-5)**
