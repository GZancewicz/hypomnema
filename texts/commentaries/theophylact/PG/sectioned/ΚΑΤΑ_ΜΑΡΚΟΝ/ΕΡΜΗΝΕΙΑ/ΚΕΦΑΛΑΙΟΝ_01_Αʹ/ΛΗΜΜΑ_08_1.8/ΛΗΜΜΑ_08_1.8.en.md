# Mark 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 8 · Mark 1:8

> **I indeed baptized you with water, but he shall baptize you with the Holy Spirit. (Mark 1:8)**[^1]

Not even his last slave, he says, am I worthy to be, so as to loose the strap—that is, the fastening made from the leather thong of his sandals. But it is understood also thus: All who came and were baptized by John were loosed, through repentance, from the bond of their sins, in believing in Christ. Of all the others, then, John loosed the straps and the bonds of their sins; but the strap of Jesus he had not the strength to loose, because he did not even find a strap—that is, sin—in him.

[^1]: OCR βαπτίσοι corrected to βαπτίσει, and ἀμᾶς to ὑμᾶς (αὐτὸς δὲ βαπτίσει ὑμᾶς ἐν Πνεύματι ἁγίῳ).
