# Mark 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 38 · Mark 1:43-44

> **And having sternly charged[^1] him, immediately he sent him out, and he says to him: See that you say nothing to anyone, but go, show yourself to the priest, and offer for your cleansing the things which Moses commanded, for a testimony to them. (Mark 1:43-44)**

[^1]: ἐμβριμησάμενος: a strong verb — "having snorted with indignation," hence "having sternly charged / admonished."
