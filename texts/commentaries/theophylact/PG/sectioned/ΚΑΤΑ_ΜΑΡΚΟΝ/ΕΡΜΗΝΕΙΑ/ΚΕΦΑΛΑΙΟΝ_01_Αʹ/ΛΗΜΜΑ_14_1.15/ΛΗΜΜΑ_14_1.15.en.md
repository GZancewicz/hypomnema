# Mark 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 14 · Mark 1:15

> **saying that: The time is fulfilled, and the kingdom of God has drawn near; repent, and believe in the Gospel. (Mark 1:15)**

He withdraws into Galilee, having heard that John was delivered up to prison, in order to show also to us that one must not come to close quarters with temptations, but steal away *from them*. But whenever we do fall into them, *we must* bear *them*. And the Lord appears to preach the same things as John, such as the "Repent," and the "The kingdom of God has drawn near." But it is not *the same*; for John said the "Repent" so as to withdraw from sins, whereas Christ says the "Repent" so as to flee from the letter of the law. Wherefore he also added the "Believe in the Gospel"; for he who is about to believe through the Gospel has thereupon abolished the law. For the Lord says that the time of the law also has been fulfilled; for up until now, he says, the law was in force, but from now on the kingdom of God—the polity[^1] according to the Gospel—is present, which is reasonably likened to the kingdom of the heavens. For whenever you see the one who lives according to the Gospel conducting himself well-nigh as one without flesh, would you not say that this man already has the kingdom of the heavens (which is not food or drink) (Rom. 14:17), even if it seem to be far off?
