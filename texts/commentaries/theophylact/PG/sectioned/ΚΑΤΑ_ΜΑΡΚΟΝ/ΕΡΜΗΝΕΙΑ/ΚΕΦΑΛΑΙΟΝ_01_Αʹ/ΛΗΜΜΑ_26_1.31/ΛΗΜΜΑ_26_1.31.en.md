# Mark 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 26 · Mark 1:31

> **...coming to her, he raised her up, having taken hold of her hand; and immediately the fever left her, and she ministered to them. (Mark 1:31)**

He withdrew, as was his custom on the Sabbath toward evening, for a meal, into the house of the disciples; but she who ought to serve was held down by a fever, but *he* heals her, and *she* ministers to them — the passage showing that, if God should heal you when you are held down by a sickness, you are to use your health for the ministry of the saints and for the service of God. And he too runs a fever who is angry, who from his anger is rash with his hand; but if the Word take hold of his hand, and he does not rashly stretch it out, then he who is for the while feverish from anger ministers to the Word; for the wrathful man, if the Word take hold of his hand, rises up, and then the anger ministers to the Word.
