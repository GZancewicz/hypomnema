# Mark 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 1 · Mark 1:1

> **Beginning of the Gospel of Jesus Christ, Son of God, as[^1] (Mark 1:1)**

[^1]: The lemma breaks off mid-sentence at ὡς ("as"), continuing into the next fragment. The OCR reads *δὴεοῦ* for Θεοῦ; the text is Υἱοῦ τοῦ Θεοῦ, "Son of God" (anarthrous, hence "Son of God").
