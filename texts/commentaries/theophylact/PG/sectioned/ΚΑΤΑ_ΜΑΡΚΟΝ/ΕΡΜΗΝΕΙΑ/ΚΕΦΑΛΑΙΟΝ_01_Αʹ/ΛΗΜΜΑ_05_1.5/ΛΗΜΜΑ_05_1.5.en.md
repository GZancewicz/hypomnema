# Mark 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 5 · Mark 1:5

> **there went out to him all the country of Judea, and the people of Jerusalem, and they were all baptized in the river Jordan by him, confessing their sins.» (Mark 1:5)**

The baptism of John did not have remission of sins, but only introduced repentance to men; how then does Mark here say that it was *for the remission of sins*? We say, therefore, that he preached a baptism of repentance; but this preaching of repentance, whither did it lead? To the remission of sins—that is, to the baptism of Christ, which had the remission of sins. Just as when we say, So-and-so, the king's guardsman,[^1] came proclaiming a preparation of foods for the benefit of those preparing them, we do not mean this, that the guardsman is going to benefit those preparing the royal foods, but that he proclaimed the preparation of the foods, so that those having prepared it, having received the king, might be benefited by him; so, therefore, here too the Forerunner preached a baptism of repentance, that those having repented, having received Christ, might receive remission of sins.

[^1]: δορυφόρος, literally "spear-bearer," a member of the royal bodyguard.
