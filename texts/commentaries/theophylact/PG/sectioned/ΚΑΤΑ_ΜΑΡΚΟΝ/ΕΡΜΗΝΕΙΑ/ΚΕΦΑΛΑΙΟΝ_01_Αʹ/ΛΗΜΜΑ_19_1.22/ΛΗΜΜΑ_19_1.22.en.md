# Mark 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 19 · Mark 1:22

> **they were astonished at his teaching. For he was teaching them as one having authority, and not as the scribes. (Mark 1:22)**

Whence do they enter into Capernaum?[^1] From Nazareth. Therefore on the day of the Sabbath, when they were gathering together and reading, then Christ also entered to teach. For indeed the law too commanded resting on the Sabbath for this very purpose, that men might have leisure for the reading, being gathered together in the same place. The Lord, therefore, was teaching reprovingly, not with flattery like the Pharisees; for while urging them toward the good, he was threatening punishment to the disobedient.

[^1]: OCR Καπεοναοὔμ here read as Καπερναούμ (Capernaum). The verb εἰσπορεύονται is present ("do they enter"), the subject (Jesus with his disciples) supplied from context.
