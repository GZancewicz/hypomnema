# Mark 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 7 · Mark 1:7

> **And he preached, saying: There comes one mightier than me after me, the strap of whose sandals I am not worthy to stoop down and loose. (Mark 1:7)**[^1]

[^1]: The OCR of this fragment is corrupt but recoverable to the standard text of the verse: read μα. as μου, ἀπίσω as ὀπίσω, and ἱαάντα as ἱμάντα ( Ἔρχεται ὁ ἰσχυρότερός μου ὀπίσω μου, οὗ οὐκ εἰμὶ ἱκανὸς κύψας λῦσαι τὸν ἱμάντα τῶν ὑποδημάτων αὐτοῦ ). The exposition belonging with this saying appears in the next fragment (Lemma 8), which completes the quotation.
