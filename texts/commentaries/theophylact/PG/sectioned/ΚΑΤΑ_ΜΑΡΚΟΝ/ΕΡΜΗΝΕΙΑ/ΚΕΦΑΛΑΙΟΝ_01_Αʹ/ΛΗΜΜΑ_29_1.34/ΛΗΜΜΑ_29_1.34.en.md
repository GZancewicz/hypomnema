# Mark 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 29 · Mark 1:34

> **...he healed many that were sick with various diseases, and cast out many demons, and did not allow the demons to speak, because they knew him.[^1] (Mark 1:34)**

Not without purpose is the phrase added, "When the sun had set," but since they thought it was not permitted for anyone to heal on the day of the Sabbath, for this reason they awaited the setting of the sun, and then they were bringing those who were to be healed. And "he healed many" is used instead of "all"; for the "all" are "many." Or he did not heal all — for there were some unbelievers, who were not healed because of their unbelief — but he cured many of those who were brought, namely those who had faith. And he did not allow the demons to speak, as I said, teaching us not to believe them, even if they should speak true things. For if they should find any who believe them at all, what would they not do, the accursed ones, mingling falsehoods with the truths? So also Paul restrained the spirit of Python, when it said, "These men are servants of the Most High God" (Acts 16:17); for the holy one did not wish to be commended and borne witness to by an unclean mouth.

[^1]: The OCR reads Κδεισα, a corruption of ᾔδεισαν ("they knew," pluperfect of οἶδα with present sense): the demons were silenced *because they knew him*.
