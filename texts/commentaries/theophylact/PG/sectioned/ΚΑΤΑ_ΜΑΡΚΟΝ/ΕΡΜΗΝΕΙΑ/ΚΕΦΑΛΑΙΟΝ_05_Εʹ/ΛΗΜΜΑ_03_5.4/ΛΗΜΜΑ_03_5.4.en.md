# Mark 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 3 · Mark 5:4

> **—to bind him, because he had often been bound with fetters and chains, and the chains had been torn apart by him, and the fetters shattered; and no one had strength to tame him; and through—[^1] (Mark 5:4)**

[^1]: OCR: the manuscript reads τὰς πάδας (read τὰς πέδας, "the fetters"); the sentence breaks off at καὶ διὰ and is completed in the next fragment with παντός ("continually").
