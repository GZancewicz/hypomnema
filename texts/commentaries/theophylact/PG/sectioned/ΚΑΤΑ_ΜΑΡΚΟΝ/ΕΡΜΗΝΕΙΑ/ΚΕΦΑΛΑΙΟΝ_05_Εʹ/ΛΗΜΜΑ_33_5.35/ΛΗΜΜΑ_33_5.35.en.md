# Mark 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 33 · Mark 5:35-38

> **While he was still speaking, there come from the ruler of the synagogue, saying, "Your daughter has died; why do you still trouble the Teacher?" But Jesus, immediately having heard the word being spoken, says to the ruler of the synagogue: "Do not fear, only believe." And he did not permit anyone to follow along with him, except Peter and James and John the brother of James. And he comes into the house of the ruler of the synagogue, and beholds a tumult, those weeping and wailing greatly, [?][^1] "…do you weep? The child has not died, but is sleeping." (Mark 5:35-38)**

[^1]: A stretch of the lemma is lost or garbled in the OCR here: the text runs "…καὶ ἀλαλάζοντας πολλὰ, τλαίετε;" A clause of the sort καὶ εἰσελθὼν λέγει αὐτοῖς· Τί θορυβεῖσθε καὶ κλαίετε; ("and entering he says to them: Why are you troubled and weeping?") stood in the gap; only κλαίετε ("do you weep") survives legibly.
