# Mark 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 20 · Mark 5:21-25

> **And when Jesus had crossed over again in the boat to the other side, a great crowd was gathered together unto him, and he was beside the sea. And behold, there comes one[^1] of the rulers of the synagogue, Jairus by name. And having seen him, he falls at his feet and besought him much, saying, "My little daughter is at the point of death;[^2] that thou come and lay thy hands upon her, so that she may be saved and shall live." And he went away with him; and a great crowd followed him, and they pressed upon him. And a certain woman who was in a flow of blood twelve years, and… (Mark 5:21-25)**

[^1]: The manuscript reads εἰς ("into"); read εἷς ("one"), as the sense requires.
[^2]: ἐσχάτως ἔχει, "is at the last extremity" (i.e., at the point of death); the manuscript reads ἐσχάσως.
