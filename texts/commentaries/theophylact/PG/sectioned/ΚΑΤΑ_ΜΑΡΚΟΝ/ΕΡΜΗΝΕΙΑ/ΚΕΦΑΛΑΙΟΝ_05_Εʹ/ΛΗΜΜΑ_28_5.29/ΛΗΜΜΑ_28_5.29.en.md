# Mark 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 28 · Mark 5:29

> **immediately the fountain of her blood was dried up, and she knew in her body that she was healed from the scourge. (Mark 5:29)**

After the miracle upon the demoniac, the Lord works another, raising up the daughter of the ruler of the synagogue. But he also states the name of the ruler of the synagogue, on account of the Jews who knew what had happened. The man, however, is only half a believer; for insofar as he falls at his feet, *he is* a believer, but insofar as he entreats him to come, he does not display as much faith as is needful. For he ought to have said that, "Speak by a word."[^1] But as the Lord was departing, as it were in passing, the woman with the issue of blood is also healed; and the woman is most faithful, since she supposed that she would be benefited even from his garments; wherefore also she obtains the healing.

But you will understand these things also with reference to human nature, which was afflicted with an issue of blood. For it brought on sin, which is the murder of the soul, and pours out the blood of our souls. And under many physicians — I mean the wise men of this world, and indeed the law and the prophets as well — it could not be healed. But as soon as it touched the garments of Christ, that is, his flesh, it was also healed. For he who believes that Christ was made flesh, this is the one who has touched his garments.

[^1]: Greek Εἰπὲ λόγῳ, literally "Say by a word" — i.e. heal by a mere word of command rather than requiring bodily presence.
