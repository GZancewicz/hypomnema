# Mark 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 19 · Mark 5:20

> **…he departed, and began to proclaim in the Decapolis how much Jesus had done for him, and all were marveling. (Mark 5:20)**

The men of that city went out to Jesus, being astonished at the miracle; but having heard *of it*, they feared the more. Wherefore they also beseech Jesus to depart from their borders. For they had dreaded lest they should suffer something even greater. For, having lost the swine and being grieved at the loss, they beg off even the presence of the Lord. But the one who had been demon-possessed besought him, that he might be with him; for he was afraid lest the demons, finding him alone, should come upon him again. But the Lord dismisses him to his own house, showing him that even if he himself be not present, yet his power and his oversight[^1] will guard him; and at the same time, that by being seen he might benefit others also. Behold, then, he began to proclaim, and all marveled. And observe also the unassuming humility[^2] of the Savior. He did not say, "Announce how much I have done for you," but, "How much the Lord has done for you."[^3] So you also, whenever you do something good, ascribe the deed not to yourself, but to God.

[^1]: ἐπισκοπὴ — literally "oversight" or "visitation"; the sense is God's watchful, protecting care.
[^2]: τὸ ἄτυφον — "freedom from pride/vanity, unpretentiousness."
[^3]: The manuscript is corrupt here, reading ἈπάννειλονἘαα σοι πεποίηκα; reconstructed as Ἀπάγγειλον ὅσα σοι πεποίηκα ("Announce how much I have done for you"), echoing the Lord's charge at Mark 5:19 (ἀπάγγειλον… ὅσα σοι ὁ Κύριος πεποίηκεν).
