# Mark 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 32 · Mark 5:34

> **said to her: Daughter, your faith has saved you. Go in peace, and be whole from your scourge. (Mark 5:34)**

A power goes forth from Christ, not passing over locally, but both being distributed to the others and remaining undiminished in Christ; just as lessons too both are present with those who teach and are handed over to those who learn. But see how the crowds were pressing upon him, yet no one touched him. But she who was not pressing upon him, she touched him. For from this we learn[^1] a mystery, that no one of those who have crowds and disturbances touches Jesus, but only presses upon him. But if anyone should not crowd Jesus, nor surround with disturbances the reason within himself,[^2] that one touches him. But for what reason does the Lord make the woman manifest? First, that he might glorify the faith of the woman; then, that he might also embolden the ruler of the synagogue, that his own little daughter too will thus be saved; and at the same time, that he might also release the woman from her great fear; for the woman was afraid, as having stolen the healing; wherefore also the evangelist says: "But fearing and trembling she came." The Lord therefore does not say, "I have saved you," but, "Your faith." Go, then, into peace, that is, into rest; so that what is said is of this sort: "Depart, be at rest, since up to the present at least you were in sorrows and disturbances."

[^1]: The OCR reads μανθάνοιεν; the sense requires the indicative μανθάνομεν ("we learn"), which is adopted here.
[^2]: Greek τὸν ἐν ἑαυτῷ λόγον — "the *logos* within himself," i.e. one's own reason or rational faculty; the term also carries the sense of "word."
