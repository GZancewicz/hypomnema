# Mark 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 8 · Mark 5:10

> **And he besought him much, that he would not send them out of the country. (Mark 5:10)**

The demons consider it a torment to go out from the man. Therefore they say, "Do not torment *me*," that is, "Do not lead us out of our dwelling, namely the man"; and in another way too, they thought that, on account of the excess of the things dared by them, he would not bear with it, but would hand them over to punishment from that very moment. For this reason also they beseech him not to torment them. But the Lord asks him, not that he himself might learn, but that the others might learn the multitude of the demons dwelling in him. For since he appeared to be one, Christ shows with how many enemies that pitiable man was wrestling.
