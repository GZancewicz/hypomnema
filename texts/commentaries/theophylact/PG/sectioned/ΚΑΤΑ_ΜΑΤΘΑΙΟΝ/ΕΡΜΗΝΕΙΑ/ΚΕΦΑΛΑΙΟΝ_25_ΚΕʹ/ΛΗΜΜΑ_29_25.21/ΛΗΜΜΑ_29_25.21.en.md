# Matthew 25 (ΚΕʹ) — Lemma 29 · Matt. 25:34

> **Then shall the king say to those on his right: Come, you blessed of my Father, inherit the kingdom prepared for you from *the* foundation of *the* world.**[^1] (Matt. 25:34)

This lemma carries only the scripture quotation (Matt. 25:34); it has no exposition of its own. The words of the king to the righteous continue into Lemma 30.

[^1]: Greek «Τότε ἐρεῖ ὁ βασιλεὺς τοῖς ἐκ δεξιῶν αὐτοῦ· Δεῦτε, οἱ εὐλογημένοι τοῦ Πατρός μου, κληρονομήσατε τὴν ἡτοιμασμένην ὑμῖν βασιλείαν ἀπὸ καταβολῆς κόσμου.» The opening word is OCR-garbled as «εῦτε» for **Δεῦτε** ("Come," plural summons); the reconstruction is certain. Both *καταβολῆς* ("foundation") and *κόσμου* ("world") are anarthrous, so the supplied "*the*" is italicized in each.
