# Matthew 25 (ΚΕʹ) — Lemma 6 · Matt. 25:6–7

> **But at midnight a cry arose: Behold, the bridegroom comes, go out to meet him! Then all those virgins arose, and trimmed their lamps; but the[^1] (Matt. 25:6–7)**

*This lemma carries the quotation of the parable and no exposition of its own; the sentence breaks off at "but the," completed at Lemma 7 ("foolish said to the wise…").*

[^1]: Greek *Μέσης δὲ νυκτὸς κραυγὴ γέγονεν· Ἰδοὺ ὁ νυμφίος ἔρχεται, ἐξέρχεσθε εἰς ἀπάντησιν αὐτοῦ. Τότε ἠγέρθησαν πᾶσαι αἱ παρθένοι ἐκεῖναι, καὶ ἐκόσμησαν τὰς λαμπάδας αὐτῶν, αἱ δὲ* — "But at midnight a cry arose: 'Behold, the bridegroom comes, go out to meet him!' Then all those virgins arose and trimmed (*ἐκόσμησαν*, adorned/set in order) their lamps; but the…" The OCR *γέγονεν* stands for the perfect "has arisen / arose"; *μγέρθησαν* is securely *ἠγέρθησαν* ("they were roused, arose"); *ἐαιιπάδας* is securely *λαμπάδας* ("lamps"). The lemma breaks off at *αἱ δὲ* ("but the"), resumed at Lemma 7 with *μωραί* ("*the* foolish said to the wise…").
