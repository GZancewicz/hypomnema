# Matthew 25 (ΚΕʹ) — Lemma 2 · Matt. 25:2

> **But five of them were wise, and the five foolish;[^1] (Matt. 25:2)**

*This lemma continues the quotation of the parable and carries no exposition of its own; see Lemma 5 for Theophylact's comment.*

[^1]: Greek *Πέντε δὲ ἦσαν ἐξ αὐτῶν φρόνιμοι, καὶ αἱ πέντε μωραί* — "But five of them were wise (*φρόνιμοι*, prudent/sensible), and the five foolish (*μωραί*, dull/senseless)." The definite article on the second clause (*αἱ πέντε*, "the five") is present in the Greek and rendered.
