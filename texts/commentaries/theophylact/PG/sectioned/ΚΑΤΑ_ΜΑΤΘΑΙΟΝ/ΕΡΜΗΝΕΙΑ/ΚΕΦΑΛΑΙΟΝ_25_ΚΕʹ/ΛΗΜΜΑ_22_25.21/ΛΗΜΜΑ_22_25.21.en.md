# Matthew 25 (ΚΕʹ) — Lemma 22 · Matt. 25:27

> **to cast my silver to the bankers,[^1] and having come I would have received back my own with interest;**[^2] (Matt. 25:27)

This lemma carries only the words of the parable; Theophylact's exposition is given below, under lemma 24, where he explains that the "bankers" are the disciples and the "interest" is the demonstration of works.

[^1]: Greek *τραπεζίταις* — "money-changers, bankers." Theophylact allegorizes the term in lemma 24 (the disciples, who "approve or reject the word"). *ἀργύριόν* = "silver / money."
[^2]: The lemma opens mid-sentence, continuing *ἔδει οὖν σε* from the previous lemma ("it was necessary therefore for you *to cast* …"), and breaks off at the raised point. Greek *ἐκομισάμην ἂν* — *ἄν* + aorist, the potential/counterfactual "I would have received back."
