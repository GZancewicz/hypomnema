# Matthew 25 (ΚΕʹ) — Lemma 35 · Matt. 25:41

> **"Then he will say also to those on the left: Depart from me, you cursed, into the eternal fire, which has been prepared for the devil and his angels. (Matt. 25:41)**[^1]

*This section carries only the scripture lemma opening the sentence against those on the left; Theophylact appends no exposition here.*

[^1]: Greek «Τότε ἐρεῖ καὶ τοῖς ἐξ εὐωνύμων· Πορεύεσθε ἀπ᾽ ἐμοῦ, οἱ κατηραμένοι, εἰς τὸ πῦρ τὸ αἰώνιον, τὸ ἡτοιμασμένον τῷ διαβόλῳ καὶ τοῖς ἀγγέλοις αὐτοῦ.» *τοῖς ἐξ εὐωνύμων* = literally "to those from *the* left-hand *side*." *οἱ κατηραμένοι* = "the cursed ones / you cursed" (perfect passive participle). *τὸ ἡτοιμασμένον* = "which has been prepared." Note the lemma says the fire is prepared "for the devil and his angels," not for men.
