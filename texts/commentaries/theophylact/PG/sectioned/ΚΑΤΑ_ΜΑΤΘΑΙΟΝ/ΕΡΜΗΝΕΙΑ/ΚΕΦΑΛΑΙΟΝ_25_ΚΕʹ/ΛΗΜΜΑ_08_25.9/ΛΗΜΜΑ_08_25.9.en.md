# Matthew 25 (ΚΕʹ) — Lemma 8 · Matt. 25:9

> **But the wise answered, saying: Lest it not suffice for you and for us.[^1] But go rather to those who sell, and buy for yourselves. (Matt. 25:9)**

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text; the exposition of vv. 8–13 is given together at Lemma 12 (Matt. 25:13).*

[^1]: Greek *μήποτε οὐκ ἀρκέσῃ ὑμῖν καὶ ἡμῖν* — an elliptical clause of apprehension, literally "lest it may not suffice for you and for us"; English idiom would fill it out as "*there might* not *be* enough for both you and us." The order here is "for you and for us"; in the exposition (Lemma 12) Theophylact cites it the other way, "for us and for you" (*ἡμῖν καὶ ὑμῖν*).
