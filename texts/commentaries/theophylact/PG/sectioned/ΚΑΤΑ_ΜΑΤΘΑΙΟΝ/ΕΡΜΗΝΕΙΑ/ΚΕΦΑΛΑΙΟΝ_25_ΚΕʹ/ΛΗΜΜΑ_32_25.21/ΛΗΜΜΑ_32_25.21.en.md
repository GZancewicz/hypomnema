# Matthew 25 (ΚΕʹ) — Lemma 32 · Matt. 25:37

> **"...*Then* the righteous will answer him, saying: Lord, when did we see you hungering, and fed *you*? or thirsting, and gave *you* drink? And when *did we see* you..." (Matt. 25:37)**[^1]

*This section carries only the continuation of the righteous ones' reply; Theophylact appends no exposition here — his comment falls at the close of the passage (Lemma 34).*[^2]

[^1]: Greek OCR «ἀποκριθήσονται αὐτῷ οἱ δίκαιοι, λέγοντες· Κύριε, πότε σε εἴδομεν πεινῶντα, καὶ ἐθρέψαμεν; ἢ διψῶντα, καὶ ἐποτίσαμεν; Πότε δέ σε». *πεινῶντα* / *διψῶντα* are present participles, kept as "hungering" / "thirsting." *ἔθρεψα* is "I nourished, fed"; the object "*you*" is supplied. The closing «Πότε δέ σε» ("And when *did we see* you") is left incomplete, resumed in the next lemma.
[^2]: The verb «ἀποκριθήσονται» ("will answer") governs the «Τότε» ("Then") left dangling at the end of Lemma 31; I restore "*Then*" in italics at the head for sense.
