# Matthew 25 (ΚΕʹ) — Lemma 31 · Matt. 25:36

> **"*I was* naked, and you clothed me; I fell sick, and you visited me; I was in prison, and you came to me. Then..." (Matt. 25:36)**[^1]

*This section carries only the continuation of the Lord's words to those on the right; Theophylact appends no exposition here — his comment falls at the close of the passage (Lemma 34).*[^2]

[^1]: Greek OCR «γυμνὸς, καὶ περιεβάλετέ με· ἠσθένησα,, καὶ ἐπεσκέψασθέ με· ἐν φυλακῇ ἤμην, καὶ ἤλθετι πρός με. Τότε». Two minor OCR slips: the doubled comma after «ἠσθένησα», and «ἤλθετι» for **«ἤλθετε»** ("you came"); both securely corrected. *γυμνός* is anarthrous nominative with the subject "I" carried from the prior clauses, hence the supplied "*I was*." *περιβάλλω* is literally "to throw/put *clothing* around," here "clothed"; *ἐπισκέπτομαι* is "to look in on, visit *the sick/imprisoned*."
[^2]: The clause breaks off at «Τότε» ("Then"), which is picked up in the next lemma («Τότε ἀποκριθήσονται…», "Then the righteous will answer…").
