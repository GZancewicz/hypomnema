# Matthew 25 (ΚΕʹ) — Lemma 33 · Matt. 25:38

> **"...*did we see* you a stranger, and took *you* in? or naked, and clothed *you*? And when *did we see* you..." (Matt. 25:38)**[^1]

*This section carries only the continuation of the righteous ones' reply; Theophylact appends no exposition here — his comment falls at the close of the passage (Lemma 34).*[^2]

[^1]: Greek OCR «εἴδομεν ξένον, καὶ συνηγάγομεν; ἢ γυμνὸν, καὶ περιεβάλομεν; Πότε δέ σε» (source «περισβάλομεν» is an OCR slip for **«περιεβάλομεν»**, "we clothed"). *συνάγω* here is "to gather in, take in *as a guest*" (cf. Latin *colligere/hospitio recipere*). The objects "*you*" are supplied throughout.
[^2]: The clause opens mid-sentence — «εἴδομεν» ("did we see") completes the «Πότε δέ σε» ("And when… you") left hanging at the end of Lemma 32 — and closes again on the same unfinished formula, resumed in Lemma 34.
