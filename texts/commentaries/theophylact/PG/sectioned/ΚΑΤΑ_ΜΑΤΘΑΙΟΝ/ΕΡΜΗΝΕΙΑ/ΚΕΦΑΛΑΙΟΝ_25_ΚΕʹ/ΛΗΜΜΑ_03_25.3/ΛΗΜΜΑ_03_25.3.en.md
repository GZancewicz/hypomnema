# Matthew 25 (ΚΕʹ) — Lemma 3 · Matt. 25:3

> **who, *being* foolish, having taken their lamps, took not with themselves oil; but the[^1] (Matt. 25:3)**

*This lemma continues the quotation of the parable and carries no exposition of its own; see Lemma 5 for Theophylact's comment.*

[^1]: Greek *αἵτινες μωραί, λαβοῦσαι τὰς λαμπάδας αὐτῶν, οὐκ ἔλαβον μεθ᾽ ἑαυτῶν ἔλαιον· αἱ δὲ* — "who, *being* foolish, having taken their lamps, took not with themselves oil; but the…" The predicate *μωραί* ("foolish") is here in apposition, hence the supplied "*being*." The participle *λαβοῦσαι* ("having taken") is kept as a participle. The lemma breaks off at *αἱ δὲ* ("but the"), completed at the head of Lemma 4 ("*the* wise took oil…").
