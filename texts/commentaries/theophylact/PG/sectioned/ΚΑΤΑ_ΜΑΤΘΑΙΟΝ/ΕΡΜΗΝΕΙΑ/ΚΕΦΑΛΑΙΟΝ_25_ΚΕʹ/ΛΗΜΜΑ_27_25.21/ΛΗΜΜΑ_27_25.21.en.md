# Matthew 25 (ΚΕʹ) — Lemma 27 · Matt. 25:32

> **...there shall be gathered before him all the nations, and he shall separate them from one another, just as the shepherd separates the sheep from the goats, and—**[^1] (Matt. 25:32)

This lemma continues the scripture quotation (Matt. 25:32); it has no exposition of its own. It carries on from Lemma 26 and breaks off again at "and—," resuming in Lemma 28, where Theophylact's exposition of the passage begins.

[^1]: Greek «συναχθήσονται ἔμπροσθεν αὐτοῦ πάντα τὰ ἔθνη, καὶ ἀφοριεῖ αὐτοὺς ἀπ᾽ ἀλλήλων, ὥσπερ ὁ ποιμὴν ἀφορίζει τὰ πρόβατα ἀπὸ τῶν ἐρίφων, καὶ—». *ἔθνη* rendered "nations" (its plain sense; "Gentiles" would import a narrower reading Theophylact does not press here). *ἐρίφων* = "goats"; the diminutive *ἐρίφια* ("kids") appears in the fuller phrase at Lemma 28 — see the note there.
