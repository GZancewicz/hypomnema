# Matthew 25 (ΚΕʹ) — Lemma 7 · Matt. 25:8

> **_The_ foolish said to the wise: Give us of your oil, for our lamps are going out.[^1] (Matt. 25:8)**

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text; Theophylact quotes the parable continuously through Lemma 11, and expounds the whole of vv. 8–13 together at Lemma 12 (Matt. 25:13).*

[^1]: Greek *σβέννυνται* is present passive — "are being extinguished," i.e. "are going out" as an ongoing action, not "have gone out." The opening *μωραὶ* is anarthrous here (the common text has *αἱ … μωραὶ*), so the article "*The*" is supplied and italicized.
