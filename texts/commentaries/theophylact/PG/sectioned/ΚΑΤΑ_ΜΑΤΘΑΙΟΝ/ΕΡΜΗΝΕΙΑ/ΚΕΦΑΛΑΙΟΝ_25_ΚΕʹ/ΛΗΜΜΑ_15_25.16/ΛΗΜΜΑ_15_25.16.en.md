# Matthew 25 (ΚΕʹ) — Lemma 15 · Matt. 25:16

> **But he who had received the five talents, having gone, worked with them, and made another five talents;**[^1]

*This lemma carries only scripture, without comment; the continuous exposition of the parable is given at Lemma 18.*

[^1]: Greek «Πορευθεὶς δὲ ὁ τὰ πέντε τάλαντα λαβὼν, εἰργάσατο ἐν αὐτοῖς, καὶ ἐποίησεν ἄλλα πέντε τάλαντα». The participles are kept as participles ("having gone"); «εἰργάσατο ἐν αὐτοῖς» ("worked with/in them") is Theophylact's wording, where the common text reads «ἠργάσατο ἐν αὐτοῖς» / «διεπραγματεύσατο» ("traded with them").
