# Matthew 25 (ΚΕʹ) — Lemma 16 · Matt. 25:17

> **likewise also he who *had received* the two gained, he also, another two; but he who *received* the one**[^1]

*This lemma carries only scripture and breaks off mid-sentence; it is completed in Lemma 17. The continuous exposition of the parable is given at Lemma 18.*

[^1]: Greek «ὡσαύτως καὶ ὁ τὰ δύο, ἐκέρδησε καὶ αὐτὸς ἄλλα δύο· ὁ δὲ τὸ ἓν». The participle "having received" is only implied by the article + accusative («ὁ τὰ δύο», "the *one who had* the two"); supplied in italics. The clause breaks off at «ὁ δὲ τὸ ἓν» ("but he who *received* the one"), completed by «λαβὼν…» at the head of Lemma 17.
