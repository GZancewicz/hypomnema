# Matthew 25 (ΚΕʹ) — Lemma 37 · Matt. 25:43-44

> **I was a stranger, and you did not gather me *in*; naked, and you did not clothe me; sick and in prison, and you did not visit me. Then they also will answer, saying: Lord, when did we see you hungering, or thirsting, or a stranger, or naked, or sick, or in prison, and did not minister to you? Then—[^1] (Matt. 25:43-44)**

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text, and no exposition of its own.*[^2] The King's charge against those on the left continues from the preceding lemma («For I was hungry, and you gave me not to eat; I was thirsty, and you gave me not to drink», Matt. 25:42), and it breaks off here mid-clause at «Then—», which the following lemma completes: «he will answer them, saying: Amen I say to you…»

[^1]: The servants' answering speech is OCR-garbled in the source as «Κύρις, πότε σε εδοιιεν σεινῶντα»; it is securely recoverable as **«Κύριε, πότε σε εἴδομεν πεινῶντα»** ("Lord, when did we see you hungering"). Note that Theophylact's text renders the deeds as predicate adjectives — *ξένος* ("a stranger"), *γυμνός* ("naked"), *ἀσθενής* ("sick") — rather than the finite verbs of the fuller Gospel wording (*ἠσθένησα καὶ ἐν φυλακῇ ἤμην*, "I was sick and was in prison"); translated as the adjectives he actually writes. *συνηγάγετέ με* is rendered "gather me *in*" (i.e. receive/take in as a guest); the supplied "*in*" is italicized.
[^2]: The lemma ends at «Τότε» ("Then"), breaking off mid-sentence; the sentence is completed at the head of Lemma 38, which supplies «ἀποκριθήσεται αὐτοῖς, λέγων» ("he will answer them, saying"). Theophylact's comment on this whole section begins in Lemma 38.
