# Matthew 25 (ΚΕʹ) — Lemma 36 · Matt. 25:42

> **"For I was hungry, and you did not give me to eat; I thirsted, and you did not give me drink;..." (Matt. 25:42)**[^1]

*This section carries only the continuation of the sentence against those on the left; Theophylact appends no exposition here.*

[^1]: Greek OCR «Ἐπείνασα γὰρ, καὶ οὐκ ἐδώκατέ μοι φαγεῖν· ἐδίησα, καὶ οὐκ ἐποτίσατέ με·» («ἐδίησα» is an OCR slip for **«ἐδίψησα»**, "I thirsted"). *γάρ* = "For"; kept roman as a present particle. This negated litany mirrors verbatim the positive litany of Lemma 30 (Matt 25:35), now with «οὐκ» ("not") at each member.
