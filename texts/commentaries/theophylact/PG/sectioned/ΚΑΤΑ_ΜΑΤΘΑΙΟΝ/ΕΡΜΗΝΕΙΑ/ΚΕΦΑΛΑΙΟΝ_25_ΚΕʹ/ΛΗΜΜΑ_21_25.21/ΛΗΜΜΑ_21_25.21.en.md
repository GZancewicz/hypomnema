# Matthew 25 (ΚΕʹ) — Lemma 21 · Matt. 25:26–27

> **[his] Lord, said to him: Wicked and slothful servant, you knew that I reap where I did not sow, and gather from where I did not scatter; it was necessary therefore for you**[^1] (Matt. 25:26–27)

This lemma carries only the words of the parable; Theophylact's exposition is given below, under lemma 24.

[^1]: The lemma opens mid-sentence, continuing from the previous lemma's *Ἀποκριθεὶς δὲ ὁ* ("And answering, the [Lord] …"), and breaks off at *ἔδει οὖν σε* ("it was necessary therefore for you [to …]"), completed at the head of the next lemma. The OCR here reads *Κᾶει οὗν σε*, securely = *ἔδει οὖν σε* ("it was necessary therefore for you"). Greek *ὀκνηρέ* = "slothful, sluggish, hesitant."
