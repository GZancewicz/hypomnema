# Matthew 25 (ΚΕʹ) — Lemma 17 · Matt. 25:18

> **having received *it*, having gone away, dug in the earth, and hid the silver of his lord. But after**[^1]

*This lemma completes the sentence begun in Lemma 16 ("...but he who received the one, having received *it*, having gone away, dug..."). It carries only scripture; it breaks off at "But after", completed by "a long time" at the head of Lemma 18, where the continuous exposition of the parable begins.*

[^1]: Greek «λαβὼν, ἀπελθὼν ὤρυξεν ἐν τῇ γῇ, καὶ ἀπέκρυψε τὸ ἀργύριον τοῦ κυρίου αὐτοῦ. Μετὰ δὲ…». The direct object of «λαβών» ("having received *it*", i.e. the one talent) is supplied. Theophylact reads «ὤρυξεν ἐν τῇ γῇ» ("dug in the earth") and «ἀπέκρυψε» ("hid away"), where the common text has «ὤρυξεν [ἐν τῇ] γῇ καὶ ἔκρυψε». The clause breaks off at «Μετὰ δὲ» ("But after"), completed by «χρόνον πολύν» ("a long time") at the head of Lemma 18.
