# Matthew 25 (ΚΕʹ) — Lemma 30 · Matt. 25:35

> **For I was hungry, and ye gave me to eat; I thirsted, and ye gave me drink; I was *a* stranger, and ye took me in;**[^1] (Matt. 25:35)

This lemma carries only the scripture quotation (Matt. 25:35); it has no exposition of its own. The king's words to the righteous, begun in Lemma 29, continue here and run on into Lemma 31.

[^1]: Greek «Ἐπείνασα γὰρ, καὶ ἐδώκατέ μοι φαγεῖν· ἐδίψησα, καὶ ἐποτίσατέ με· ξένος ἤμην, καὶ συνηγάγετέ με·». The particle *γὰρ* ("for") is rendered roman, as a word present in the Greek. *ξένος* is anarthrous, so the supplied "*a*" is italicized. *συνηγάγετέ με* (from *συνάγω*, "gather together, bring in") is rendered "took me in" in the sense of receiving a stranger with hospitality; "ye gathered me in" is equally literal. The folder-name verse (25.21) is a placeholder; the Greek is Matt. 25:35.
