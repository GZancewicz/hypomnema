# Matthew 25 (ΚΕʹ) — Lemma 26 · Matt. 25:31

> **...*the* Man in his glory, and all the holy angels with him, then he shall sit upon *the* throne of his glory, and—**[^1] (Matt. 25:31)

This lemma continues the scripture quotation begun at Lemma 25 (Matt. 25:31); it has no exposition of its own. The words carry on directly from "But when the Son of *the*—" and break off again at "and—," resuming in Lemma 27. Theophylact's exposition of the whole passage begins at Lemma 28.

[^1]: Greek «ἀνθρώπου ἐν τῇ δόξῃ αὐτοῦ, καὶ πάντες οἱ ἅγιοι ἄγγελοι μετ᾽ αὐτοῦ, τότε καθίσει ἐπὶ θρόνου δόξης αὐτοῦ, καὶ—». The opening word *ἀνθρώπου* ("of Man") completes the phrase "the Son of *the* Man" from the previous lemma. The article before "throne" is absent in the Greek (*ἐπὶ θρόνου*, anarthrous), so "*the*" is supplied and italicized.
