# Matthew 25 (ΚΕʹ) — Lemma 14 · Matt. 25:15

> **he gave five talents, but to another two, but to another[^1] one, to each according to his own ability; and he went abroad immediately.**

*This lemma completes the sentence begun in Lemma 13 (". . . and to one, on the one hand, he gave five talents . . ."). It carries only scripture, without comment; the continuous exposition of the parable is given at Lemma 18.*

[^1]: The OCR reads «ᾧ ἄα ἕν», securely = **«ᾧ δὲ ἕν»** ("but to another one"), matching the parallel «ᾧ δὲ δύο» just before; the reconstruction is certain and low-stakes, noted only for consistency with the other flagged OCR corruptions. The μὲν … δὲ … δὲ series ("on the one hand … but … but …") distributes the three servants; the connectives are present words and so remain roman.
