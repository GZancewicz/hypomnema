# Matthew 25 (ΚΕʹ) — Lemma 20 · Matt. 25:23–26

> **[said to him] his Lord:[^1] Well *done*, good and faithful servant; over few things you were faithful, over many things I will set you; enter into the joy of your Lord. And having come forward also the one who had received the one talent said: Lord, I knew you, that you are a hard man, reaping where you did not sow, and gathering from where you did not scatter; and having been afraid, having gone away I hid your talent in the earth; behold, you have what is yours. And answering, the**[^2] (Matt. 25:23–26)

This lemma carries only the words of the parable; Theophylact's exposition is given below, under lemma 24.

[^1]: The lemma opens mid-sentence, continuing from the previous one (*Ἔφη αὐτῷ ὁ* / "He said to him the …"): the supplied "[said to him]" completes the sense. Greek *Εὖ* again rendered "Well *done*," the "*done*" supplied (see lemma 19).
[^2]: The lemma breaks off at *Ἀποκριθεὶς δὲ ὁ* ("And answering, the …"); the subject "[Lord]" and his reply are supplied at the head of the next lemma. Minor OCR slips corrected: *Κύρεε* → *Κύριε*, *ἴε* → *ἴδε*.
