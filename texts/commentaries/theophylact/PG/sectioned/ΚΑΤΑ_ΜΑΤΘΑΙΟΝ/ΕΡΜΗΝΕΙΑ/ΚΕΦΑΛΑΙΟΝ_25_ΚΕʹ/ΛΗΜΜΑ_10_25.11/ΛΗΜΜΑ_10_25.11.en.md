# Matthew 25 (ΚΕʹ) — Lemma 10 · Matt. 25:11

> **But later come also the rest of the virgins, saying: Lord, Lord, open to us. But he[^1] (Matt. 25:11)**

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text; the exposition of vv. 8–13 is given together at Lemma 12 (Matt. 25:13).*

[^1]: The lemma breaks off at *Ὁ δὲ* ("But he—"); the sentence is completed in the next lemma with *ἀποκριθεὶς εἶπεν* ("answering said…"). *ὕστερον* = "later, afterward"; the verb *ἔρχονται* is present ("come").
