# Matthew 25 (ΚΕʹ) — Lemma 23 · Matt. 25:28–30

> **Take therefore[^1] from him the talent, and give *it* to the one having the ten talents. For to everyone having *it* shall be given, and he shall have abundance; but from the one not having, even what he has shall be taken away from him; and the**[^2] (Matt. 25:28–30)

This lemma carries only the words of the parable; Theophylact's exposition is given below, under lemma 24.

[^1]: The OCR reads *ἄρατε αῆν ἀπ᾽ αὐτοῦ*; *αῆν* is a garble for *οὖν* ("therefore"), giving *ἄρατε οὖν* ("Take therefore"). Further OCR slips corrected against the plain text: *παντὶ ὅοθήσεται* → *παντὶ δοθήσεται*, *καὶ Δ Κχει* → *καὶ ὃ ἔχει*.
[^2]: The lemma breaks off at *καὶ τὸν* ("and the …"), completed at the head of the next lemma ("*unprofitable servant* …"). Greek *περισσευθήσεται* ("he shall have abundance / shall be given in overflowing measure"), a divine passive.
