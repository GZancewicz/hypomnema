# Matthew 25 (ΚΕʹ) — Lemma 19 · Matt. 25:20–22

> **And having come forward, the one who had received the five talents brought other five talents, saying: Lord, five talents you delivered to me; behold, other five talents I gained upon them. His Lord said to him: Well *done*,[^1] good and faithful servant; over few things you were faithful, over many things I will set you; enter into the joy of your Lord. And having come forward also the one who had received the two talents said: Lord, two talents you delivered to me; behold, other two talents I gained upon them. He said to him the**[^2] (Matt. 25:20–22)

This lemma carries only the words of the parable; Theophylact's exposition of the whole talents-passage is given below, under lemma 24, where the quotation completes.

[^1]: Greek *Εὖ* — the bare adverb "well." The supplied "*done*" (italicized) fills out the idiomatic English commendation; the Greek has no verb here. Theophylact expounds *Εὖ, δοῦλε ἀγαθὲ καὶ πιστέ* in lemma 24.
[^2]: The lemma breaks off mid-sentence at *Ἔφη αὐτῷ ὁ* ("He said to him the …"); the subject "[Lord]" and the words of commendation are supplied at the head of the next lemma. Minor OCR slips corrected silently against the plain sense: *λέγοων* → *λέγων*, *αὐτολ* → *αὐτοῦ*, *ἴἂε* → *ἴδε*.
