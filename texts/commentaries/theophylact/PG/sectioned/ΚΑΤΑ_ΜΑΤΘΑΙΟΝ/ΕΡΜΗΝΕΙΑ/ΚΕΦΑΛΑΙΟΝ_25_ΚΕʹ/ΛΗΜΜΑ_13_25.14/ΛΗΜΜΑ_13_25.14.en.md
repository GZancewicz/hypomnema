# Matthew 25 (ΚΕʹ) — Lemma 13 · Matt. 25:14

> **For just as a man going abroad called his own servants, and delivered to them his goods; and to one, on the one hand,**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the opening of the parable of the talents, and breaks off mid-sentence; it is completed in Lemma 14. Theophylact quotes the parable through several lemmata (13–17) and gives his continuous exposition of it at Lemma 18.*

[^1]: The scripture is quoted alone, without comment. Theophylact's text opens with «Ὥσπερ γὰρ ἄνθρωπος ἀποδημῶν, ἐκάλεσε…», omitting the words «*the kingdom of heaven is*» which the common Gospel text supplies (Matt. 25:14, "For *the kingdom of heaven is* as a man travelling into a far country, who…"); rendered here as it stands on his page, without importing the fuller wording. The clause breaks off at «καὶ ᾧ μὲν» ("and to one, on the one hand,"), the μὲν anticipating the δὲ ("but to another") that opens Lemma 14; the "on the one hand" is roman, since μὲν is a present word.
