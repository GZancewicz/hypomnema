# Matthew 25 (ΚΕʹ) — Lemma 38 · Matt. 25:45-46

> **—he will answer them, saying: Amen I say to you, insofar as you did *it*…[^1] And these shall go away into eternal punishment, but the righteous into eternal life. (Matt. 25:45-46)**

Those on *his* left he sends out into the fire, which has been prepared for the devil.[^2] For since the demons are without compassion, and are disposed harshly and inimically toward us, fittingly are those deemed worthy of the same punishment who are of the same mind — who indeed, out of their own works, became accursed. But observe that God did not prepare the fire for men, nor did he make the punishment on our account, but on account of the devil; yet I make myself liable to the punishment.

Shudder, then, O man, considering from this that, behold, these are punished neither as fornicators, nor as robbers, nor as having committed any other evil, but as not having done good. For in truth, if you consider it rightly, he who has much and does not show mercy is a robber, even if he wrongs no one openly. For however much he has beyond what is needful, he has assuredly plundered from those who are in need, but do not receive from him. For if he had laid these things down for common *use*, those others too would be without want; but as it is, since he shut these things up and made them his own, for this reason those others are poor. So that the unmerciful man is a robber, wronging as many as he was able to benefit and did not benefit; whence—[^3]

[^1]: Theophylact abbreviates the citation. His «ἐφ᾽ ὅσον ἐποιήσατε» ("insofar as you did") is a shortened gesture toward the full verse «ἐφ᾽ ὅσον οὐκ ἐποιήσατε ἑνὶ τούτων τῶν ἐλαχίστων, οὐδὲ ἐμοὶ ἐποιήσατε» ("insofar as you did **not** do it to one of these least ones, neither did you do it to me," Matt. 25:45). Note the negative *οὐκ* has dropped out of the quotation as transmitted; the sense is negative ("did not do"). Rendered as the abbreviated words on the page, with a supplied italic "*it*" and the ellipsis marking the truncation.
[^2]: Referring back to «Depart from me, you cursed, into the eternal fire prepared for the devil and his angels» (Matt. 25:41). *τοὺς ἐξ εὐωνύμων* = "those on the left-hand side"; the supplied "*his*" is italicized.
[^3]: The comment breaks off at «ὅθεν» ("whence"); the sentence runs on into Lemma 39, whose opening words «these shall go away into eternal punishment» grammatically complete this clause even as they resume the Gospel lemma.
