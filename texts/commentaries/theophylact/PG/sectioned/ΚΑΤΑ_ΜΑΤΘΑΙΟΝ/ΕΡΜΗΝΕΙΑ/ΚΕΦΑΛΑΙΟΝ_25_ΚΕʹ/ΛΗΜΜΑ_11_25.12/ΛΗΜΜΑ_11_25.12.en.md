# Matthew 25 (ΚΕʹ) — Lemma 11 · Matt. 25:12

> **—answering said: Truly I say to you, I do not know you.[^1] (Matt. 25:12)**

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text; the exposition of vv. 8–13 is given together at Lemma 12 (Matt. 25:13).*

[^1]: Continues the sentence broken off in Lemma 10 (*Ὁ δὲ …*). *ἀποκριθεὶς* is an aorist participle, "having answered / answering." *Ἀμὴν* is rendered "Truly" per the plain sense of Theophylact's Greek; it could equally be left as "Amen."
