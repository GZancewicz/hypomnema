# Matthew 25 (ΚΕʹ) — Lemma 4 · Matt. 25:4

> **wise took oil in their vessels with their lamps.[^1] (Matt. 25:4)**

*This lemma continues the quotation of the parable and carries no exposition of its own; see Lemma 5 for Theophylact's comment.*

[^1]: Greek *φρόνιμοι ἔλαβον ἔλαιον ἐν τοῖς ἀγγείοις αὐτῶν μετὰ τῶν λαμπάδων αὐτῶν* — "*the* wise took oil in their vessels with their lamps." The clause completes the *αἱ δὲ* ("but the") left hanging at the end of Lemma 3, so read continuously: "but the wise took oil…" The OCR *ἀγγείοις ευτῶν* is securely *ἀγγείοις αὐτῶν* ("their vessels").
