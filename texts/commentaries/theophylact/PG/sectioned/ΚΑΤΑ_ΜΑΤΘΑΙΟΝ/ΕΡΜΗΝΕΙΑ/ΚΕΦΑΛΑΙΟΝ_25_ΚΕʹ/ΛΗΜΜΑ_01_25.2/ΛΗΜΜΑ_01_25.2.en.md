# Matthew 25 (ΚΕʹ) — Lemma 1 · Matt. 25:1

> **Then shall the kingdom of the heavens be likened to ten virgins, who having taken their lamps, went out to *the* meeting of the bridegroom.[^1] (Matt. 25:1)**

*This lemma carries only the opening words of the parable, with no exposition of its own; Theophylact quotes the Ten Virgins parable in segments (Lemmata 1–7) and gathers his comment upon them at Lemma 5.*[^2]

[^1]: Greek *ἐξῆλθον εἰς ἀπάντησιν τοῦ νυμφίου* — literally "went out unto *a* meeting of the bridegroom" (*εἰς ἀπάντησιν* + genitive, "to the meeting of / to meet"). Rendered "to *the* meeting of the bridegroom" to keep the noun visible; the supplied article is italicized. The OCR reads *Τάτε ὁκοιωθήσεται … δκα παρθένοις*, securely = **«Τότε ὁμοιωθήσεται … δέκα παρθένοις»** ("Then shall … be likened … to ten virgins"), the opening of Matt. 25:1.
[^2]: The folder-name verse (25.2) is off by one; the Greek here is Matt. 25:1.
