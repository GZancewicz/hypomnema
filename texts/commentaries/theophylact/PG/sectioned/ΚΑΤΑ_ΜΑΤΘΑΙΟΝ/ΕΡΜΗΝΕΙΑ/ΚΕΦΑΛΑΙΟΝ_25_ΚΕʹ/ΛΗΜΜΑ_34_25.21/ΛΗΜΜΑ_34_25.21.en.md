# Matthew 25 (ΚΕʹ) — Lemma 34 · Matt. 25:39-40

> **"...*did we see* you sick or in prison, and came to you? And the king, answering, will say to them: Amen I say to you, inasmuch as you did *it* to one of these my brethren, the least, to me you did *it*." (Matt. 25:39-40)**[^1]

He does not honor or punish before he has passed judgment. For he is loving-toward-man,[^2] and he teaches us this very thing also — not to punish before we have examined. For in this way those who are punished after a tribunal will be the more without defense.[^3] And he calls the saints "blessed," as *those* accepted by the Father; and he makes them heirs of the kingdom, so that he might show that God makes them partakers of his own glory, as his sons. For he did not say, "Take," but, "Inherit," as though *it were* some ancestral property.[^4] And he calls "least brethren" either his own disciples, or, simply, all the poor. For every poor man is a brother of Christ in this very respect, since Christ too passed *his life* in poverty. But see also the righteousness of God, how he proclaims the saints publicly. See also the graciousness of those *saints*,[^5] how out of forbearance they deny having done the feeding;[^6] but the Lord takes upon himself the things done toward the poor.

[^1]: Greek OCR «εἴδομεν ἀσθενῆ ἢ ἐν φυλακῇ, καὶ ἤλθομεν πρὸς σέ; Καὶ ἀποκριθεὶς ὁ βασιλεὺς ἐρεῖ αὐτοῖς· Ἀμὴν λέγω ὑμῖν, ἐφ᾽ ὅσον ἐποιήσατε ἑνὶ τούτων τῶν ἀδελφῶν μου τῶν ἐλαχίστων, ἐμοὶ ἐποιήσατε.» «εἴδομεν» ("did we see") completes the formula left open at the end of Lemma 33. *ἐφ᾽ ὅσον* is literally "to the extent that / inasmuch as." The object "*it*" of «ἐποιήσατε» ("you did") is supplied in both members. The Greek keeps the emphatic word order «ἐμοὶ ἐποιήσατε» ("to me you did *it*"), which I preserve.
[^2]: Greek *φιλάνθρωπος* — "benevolent, loving toward humankind," a standard divine epithet; rendered by the calque "loving-toward-man" to keep the sense of God's love *for human beings* visible.
[^3]: Greek *ἀναπολόγητοι… ἔσονται* ("will be without defense/excuse"), a legal-forensic term: after formal trial the condemned can offer no *ἀπολογία*. The source «Κσονται» is an OCR corruption of **«ἔσονται»** ("will be"). *μετὰ δικαστήριον* = "after a court/tribunal."
[^4]: Greek *οἱονεί τινα πατρῴαν περιουσίαν*. *πατρῴα* = "belonging to *the* father, ancestral/paternal"; *περιουσία* = "abundance, substance, property." The point turns on «Κληρονομήσατε» ("Inherit") over «Λάβετε» ("Take"): what is inherited is a father's estate, which suits the sonship just asserted.
[^5]: Greek *εὐγνωμοσύνη* — "fair-mindedness, candor, right disposition," here the saints' modest honesty in not claiming credit; rendered "graciousness."
[^6]: Greek *πῶς δι᾽ ἐπιείκειαν ἀρνοῦνται τὸ θρέψαι*. *ἐπιείκεια* = "reasonableness, forbearance, modesty"; *ἀρνοῦνται τὸ θρέψαι* is literally "they deny the to-have-fed" — i.e. out of humility they disown having fed *him*, though the Lord credits it to himself.
