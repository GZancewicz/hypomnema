# Matthew 25 (ΚΕʹ) — Lemma 25 · Matt. 25:31

> **But when the Son of *the*—**[^1] (Matt. 25:31)

This lemma carries only the opening words of Christ's discourse on the Last Judgment (Matt. 25:31), which the source has split across several folders. It breaks off at the genitive article *τοῦ* ("of the—"), the noun following in the next lemma (*ἀνθρώπου*, "man"). The scripture quotation continues through Lemmata 26–28, and Theophylact's exposition begins only at Lemma 28.

[^1]: Greek «Ὅταν δὲ ἔλθῃ ὁ Υἱὸς τοῦ—» ("But when the Son of the— shall come"). The opening particle *δὲ* is rendered "but"; the sentence is cut off mid-phrase at the article *τοῦ*, which governs *ἀνθρώπου* ("man") supplied at the head of the next lemma, yielding "the Son of *the* Man." The verb ("shall come") is deferred in the Greek word order and appears in the sense once the object is complete.
