# Matthew 7 (Ζʹ) — Lemma 22 · Matt. 7:24

> **…who hears these words of mine, and does them, I will liken him to a prudent man,[^1] who built his house upon the rock, and—[^2] (Matt. 7:24)**

[^1]: «ἀνδρὶ φρονίμῳ» — *φρόνιμος* "prudent / sensible / thoughtful," here contrasted below (Lemma 24) with *μωρός* "foolish." Rendered "prudent" for the practical-wisdom sense.
[^2]: Read continuously with Lemma 21: «Πᾶς οὖν ὅστις ἀκούει μου τοὺς λόγους τούτους, καὶ ποιεῖ αὐτούς…» — "Everyone, therefore, who hears these words of mine and does them…" The quotation breaks off at «ἐπὶ τὴν πέτραν, καὶ» ("upon the rock, and—") and continues «κατέβη ἡ βροχή…» at the head of Lemma 23.
