# Matthew 7 (Ζʹ) — Lemma 16 · Matt. 7:18

> **A good tree cannot make evil fruits, neither can a corrupt tree make good fruits.** (Matt. 7:18)

As long as it is corrupt, it cannot; but if it be changed, it can. For see, that he did not say, "It shall not be able," but that, for as long as it is corrupt, it does not beget good things.[^1]

[^1]: Theophylact's point turns on tense: Christ says the tree "cannot" (present, οὐ δύναται) bear good fruit, not that it "will never be able" (future, οὐ δυνήσεται). The impossibility is bound to the tree's *present* corrupt state, not to its nature forever — leaving room for change (ἐὰν μεταβληθῇ, "if it be changed"). Greek of the last clause: *ἐν ὅσῳ ἐστὶ σαπρόν, καλὰ οὐ γεννᾷ* ("while it is corrupt, it does not beget good things").
