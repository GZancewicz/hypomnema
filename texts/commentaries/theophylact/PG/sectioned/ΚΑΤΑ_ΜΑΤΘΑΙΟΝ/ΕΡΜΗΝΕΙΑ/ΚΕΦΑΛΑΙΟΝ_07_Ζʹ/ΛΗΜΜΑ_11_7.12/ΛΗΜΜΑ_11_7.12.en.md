# Matthew 7 (Ζʹ) — Lemma 11 · Matt. 7:12

> **All *things* therefore, whatever you wish that men should do to you, you also do to them. For this is the law and the prophets. (Matt. 7:12)**

He shows the way of virtue *to be* a concise one.[^1] For we men know from ourselves[^2] what is needful. For if you wish to be benefited, do good; if you wish to be loved by *your* enemies, love, you too, *your* enemies. For indeed the law of God and the prophets say those *things* which the natural law also enjoins upon us.

[^1]: The OCR is disturbed here: «Σύντομον δείκνυσι τὴν [εν] ἀρετὴν ὁδόν». Read either as *τὴν ἐνάρετον ὁδόν* ("the virtuous way") or, more likely, *τὴν τῆς ἀρετῆς ὁδόν* ("the way of/to virtue"), with *σύντομον* ("short, concise") as predicate: "He shows the way of virtue *to be* a short one." The point is that the Golden Rule reduces the whole path of virtue to a single, brief measure.
[^2]: Greek *οἴκοθεν* — literally "from home," i.e. from ourselves, innately, from our own resources without needing external instruction.
