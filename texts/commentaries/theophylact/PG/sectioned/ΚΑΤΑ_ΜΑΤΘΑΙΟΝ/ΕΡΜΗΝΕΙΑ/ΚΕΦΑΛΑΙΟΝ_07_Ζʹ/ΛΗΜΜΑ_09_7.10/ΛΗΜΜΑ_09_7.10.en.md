# Matthew 7 (Ζʹ) — Lemma 9 · Matt. 7:10

> **…he ask *for* a fish, will he give him a serpent? (Matt. 7:10)**[^1]

He instructs here that one must both ask vehemently, and ask *for* the things that are beneficial. For you, he says, see your children asking *for* the fitting things — bread and fish — and you give to them then, whenever they seek such things; so therefore you also, seek *for* the spiritual things, not the fleshly.

[^1]: This lemma opens by completing the sentence begun at the end of lemma 08. Greek: «ἰχθῦν αἰτήσῃ, μὴ ὄφιν ἐπιδώσει αὐτῷ;» ("*if he* ask *for* a fish, will he not give him a serpent?" — the interrogative *μὴ* expects the answer "no").
