# Matthew 7 (Ζʹ) — Lemma 2 · Matt. 7:2

> **For with what *judgment* ye judge, ye shall be judged; and with what measure ye mete, it shall be measured to you. But why … (Matt. 7:2)**[^1]

[^1]: Greek «Ἐν ᾧ γὰρ κρίνετε, κριθήσεσθε· καὶ ἐν ᾧ μέτρῳ μετρεῖτε, μετρηθήσεται ὑμῖν» (Matt. 7:2), running on into «Τί δὲ …» ("But why …"), the opening of Matt. 7:3, completed in Lemma 3. The first relative clause *ἐν ᾧ κρίνετε* has no expressed noun ("with which ye judge"); the parallel second clause supplies *μέτρῳ* ("measure"), so I supply "*judgment*" in the first — italicized as not present in the Greek. The folder-name verse (7.3) is off by one; the Greek here is 7:2.
