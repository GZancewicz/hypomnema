# Matthew 7 (Ζʹ) — Lemma 2 · Matt. 7:2

> **For with what *judgment* ye judge, ye shall be judged; and with what measure ye mete, it shall be measured to you.**[^1]

*This lemma-file carries only the scripture quotation; Theophylact adds no exposition here. The Gospel text runs on directly — the file ends with the opening words «Τί δὲ …» ("But why …") of Matt. 7:3, whose quotation is continued in the next lemma and whose comment does not appear until the exposition at 7:5 (Lemma 4).*[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Ἐν ᾧ γὰρ κρίνετε, κριθήσεσθε· καὶ ἐν ᾧ μέτρῳ μετρεῖτε, μετρηθήσεται ὑμῖν» (Matt. 7:2). The first relative clause *ἐν ᾧ κρίνετε* has no expressed noun ("with which ye judge"); the parallel second clause supplies *μέτρῳ* ("measure"), so I supply "*judgment*" in the first — italicized as not present in the Greek. The folder-name verse (7.3) is off by one; the Greek here is 7:2.
[^2]: The file breaks off at «Τί δὲ» ("But why"), the beginning of Matt. 7:3 (*δὲ* = "but/and"), completed in Lemma 3.
