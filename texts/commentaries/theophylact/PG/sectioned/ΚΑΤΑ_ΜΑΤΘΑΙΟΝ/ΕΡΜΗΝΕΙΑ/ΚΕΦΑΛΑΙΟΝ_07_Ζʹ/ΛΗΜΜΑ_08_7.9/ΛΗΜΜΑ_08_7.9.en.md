# Matthew 7 (Ζʹ) — Lemma 8 · Matt. 7:9

> **Or what man is there of you, whom, if his son ask *for* bread, will he give him a stone? And if… (Matt. 7:9)**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation, which breaks off mid-sentence; the exposition begins in the following lemma (09), after the completion of the sentence.*[^2]

[^1]: OCR "υΙὸε αὐτοῦ" is securely *υἱὸς αὐτοῦ* ("his son"). The Greek: «Ἢ τίς ἐστιν ἄνθρωπος ἐξ ὑμῶν, ὃν ἐὰν αἰτήσῃ ὁ υἱὸς αὐτοῦ ἄρτον, μὴ λίθον ἐπιδώσει αὐτῷ; καὶ ἐὰν…».
[^2]: The quotation breaks off at «καὶ ἐὰν» ("and if"); it is completed at the head of the next lemma: «ἰχθῦν αἰτήσῃ, μὴ ὄφιν ἐπιδώσει αὐτῷ;» ("*he* ask *for* a fish, will he give him a serpent?", Matt. 7:10), where Theophylact's comment then follows.
