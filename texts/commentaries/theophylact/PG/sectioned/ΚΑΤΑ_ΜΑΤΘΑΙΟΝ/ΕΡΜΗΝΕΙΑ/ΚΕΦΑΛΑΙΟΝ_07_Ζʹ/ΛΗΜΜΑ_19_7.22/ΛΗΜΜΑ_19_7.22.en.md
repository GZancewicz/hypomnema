# Matthew 7 (Ζʹ) — Lemma 19 · Matt. 7:22

> **Many will say to me in that day: Lord, Lord, did we not prophesy in your name, and in your name cast out demons, and in your name perform many mighty works?[^1] And then—[^2] (Matt. 7:22)**

[^1]: Greek «δυνάμεις πολλὰς ἐποιήσαμεν». *δύναμις* is "power"; in the plural, of things done, "mighty works / acts of power / miracles." Rendered "mighty works" to keep the sense of manifest power without importing a fixed English-Bible wording. The three parallel clauses each carry «τῷ σῷ ὀνόματι» ("in your name"), present in the Greek.
[^2]: The interrogative «οὐ… προεφητεύσαμεν;» expects the answer "yes." The lemma ends mid-sentence at «Καὶ τότε» ("And then—"); the apodosis follows at the head of Lemma 20 («ὁμολογήσω αὐτοῖς…», "I will confess to them…").
