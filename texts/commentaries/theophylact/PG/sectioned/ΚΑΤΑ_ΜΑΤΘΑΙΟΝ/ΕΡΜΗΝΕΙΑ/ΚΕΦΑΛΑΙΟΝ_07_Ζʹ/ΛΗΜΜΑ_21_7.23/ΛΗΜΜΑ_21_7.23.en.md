# Matthew 7 (Ζʹ) — Lemma 21 · Matt. 7:23–24

This lemma is a sectioning fragment: it carries only the two words that complete the previous sentence, followed by the opening words of the next Gospel quotation.[^1]

Completing the sentence begun in Lemma 20:

…*is here* so called.[^2] — that is, "for love is here called knowledge."

Then the next lemma opens:

> **Everyone, therefore,— (Matt. 7:24)**

The Gospel quotation and its exposition continue in Lemma 22.[^3]

[^1]: Greek in full: «λέγεται ἐνταῦθα. Πᾶς οὖν». The first two words (*λέγεται ἐνταῦθα*, "is here said/called") close the sentence broken off at the end of Lemma 20; *Πᾶς οὖν* ("Everyone therefore") begins the citation of Matt. 7:24, which runs on into Lemma 22.
[^2]: «λέγεται ἐνταῦθα» — "is here spoken of / is here called," completing «γνῶσις γὰρ ἡ ἀγάπη λέγεται ἐνταῦθα»: "for love is here called knowledge." See Lemma 20, note 3.
[^3]: «Πᾶς οὖν» — the folder metadata files this fragment under 7:23, but these words already begin the citation of Matt. 7:24; the verse continues «…ὅστις ἀκούει μου τοὺς λόγους τούτους…» at the head of Lemma 22.
