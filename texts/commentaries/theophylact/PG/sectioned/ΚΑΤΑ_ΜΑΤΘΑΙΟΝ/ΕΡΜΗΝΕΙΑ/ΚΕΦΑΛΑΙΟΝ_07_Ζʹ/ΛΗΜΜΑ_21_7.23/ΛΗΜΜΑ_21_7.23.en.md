# Matthew 7 (Ζʹ) — Lemma 21 · Matt. 7:23–24

…is here so called[^1] — that is, "for love is here called knowledge."

> **Everyone, therefore,—[^2] (Matt. 7:24)**

[^1]: «λέγεται ἐνταῦθα» — "is here said/called," completing «γνῶσις γὰρ ἡ ἀγάπη λέγεται ἐνταῦθα»: "for love is here called knowledge." See Lemma 20, note 3.
[^2]: «Πᾶς οὖν» — the folder metadata files this fragment under 7:23, but these words already begin the citation of Matt. 7:24, which continues «…ὅστις ἀκούει μου τοὺς λόγους τούτους…» at the head of Lemma 22.
