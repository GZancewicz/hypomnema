# Matthew 7 (Ζʹ) — Lemma 23 · Matt. 7:25

> **…and the rain came down, and the rivers came, and the winds blew, and fell upon[^1] that house, and it did not fall, for it had been founded upon the rock. (Matt. 7:25)**

Without God, virtue cannot be rightly accomplished; for this reason he says, "I will liken him to a prudent man." And the rock is Christ; and the house is the soul. Whoever, therefore, shall build his soul upon the working of the commandments of Christ — him neither the rain (I mean the devil,[^2] the one fallen from heaven), nor the rivers (the injurious men,[^3] who are swollen by such rain), nor the winds (the spirits of wickedness),[^4] and, in short, all the temptations, are able to cast down.

And everyone who—[^5]

[^1]: «προσέπεσον» (from *προσπίπτω*), "fell upon / dashed against / beat upon" that house. Note the deliberate difference from the parallel verse about the house on sand (Lemma 24), which uses «προσέκοψαν» "struck / beat against"; there the house falls, here it does not.
[^2]: «τὸν διάβολον» — the OCR reads «διάβαος»; securely *διάβολον* ("the devil"), identified as "the rain… the one fallen from heaven" (cf. Luke 10:18). The following words, OCR «ῤ ἐξ οὐρανοῦ πεσών», are securely «ὁ ἐξ οὐρανοῦ πεσών» ("the one fallen from heaven").
[^3]: «οἱ ἐπηρεασταὶ ἄνθρωποι» — *ἐπηρεαστής*, "one who insults / injures / acts spitefully" (cf. *ἐπηρεάζω*, Matt. 5:44; Luke 6:28). Rendered "injurious men." Theophylact keeps the water-image: such men are «πληθυνόμενοι» ("multiplied / swollen") by "such rain," as rivers are swelled by rainfall — the persecuting devil breeds the persecuting men.
[^4]: «τὰ πνεύματα τῆς πονηρίας» — "the spirits of wickedness," cf. Eph. 6:12 («τὰ πνευματικὰ τῆς πονηρίας»).
[^5]: The exposition ends and a new Gospel quotation begins with «Καὶ πᾶς ὁ» ("And everyone who—"), the opening of Matt. 7:26, which continues «ἀκούων μου τοὺς λόγους τούτους…» at the head of Lemma 24.
