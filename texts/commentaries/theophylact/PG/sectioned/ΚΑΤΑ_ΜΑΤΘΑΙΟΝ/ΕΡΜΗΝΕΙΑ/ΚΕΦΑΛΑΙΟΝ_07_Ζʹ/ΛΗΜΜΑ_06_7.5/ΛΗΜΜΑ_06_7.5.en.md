# Matthew 7 (Ζʹ) — Lemma 6 · Matt. 7:7

> **Ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and ye shall find; knock, and it shall be opened to you; for every one who …**[^1]

*This lemma-file carries only the scripture quotation; Theophylact adds no exposition here. The sentence breaks off at «πᾶς γὰρ ὁ …» ("for every one who …"), the beginning of Matt. 7:8, which is completed in the next lemma — «… αἰτῶν λαμβάνει, καὶ ὁ ζητῶν εὑρίσκει, καὶ τῷ κρούοντι ἀνοιγήσεται» ("… asks receives, and he who seeks finds, and to him who knocks it shall be opened") — where Theophylact's comment then follows.*

[^1]: Greek «Αἰτεῖτε, καὶ δοθήσεται ὑμῖν· ζητεῖτε, καὶ εὑρήσετε· κρούετε, καὶ ἀνοιγήσεται ὑμῖν· πᾶς γὰρ ὁ …» (Matt. 7:7, running into 7:8). OCR "Λἰτεῖτε" = **Αἰτεῖτε** ("Ask"), secure. *κρούετε* = "knock"; the three present imperatives are iterative ("keep asking / seeking / knocking"), as the exposition in Lemma 7 makes explicit (*αἰτεῖτε … ἀντὶ τοῦ ἀεί*, "ask, meaning continually"). The folder-name verse (7.5) is off; this is 7:7.
