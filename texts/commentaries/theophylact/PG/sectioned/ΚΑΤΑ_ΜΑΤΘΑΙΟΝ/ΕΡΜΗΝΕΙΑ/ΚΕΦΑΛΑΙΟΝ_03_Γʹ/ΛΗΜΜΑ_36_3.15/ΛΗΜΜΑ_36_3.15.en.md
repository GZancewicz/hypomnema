# Matthew 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 36 · Matt. 3:16

> **Straightway he went up from the water.**[^1]

The Manichaeans[^2] say that his body he laid aside in the Jordan, but according to appearance[^3] he displayed another body. But they are muzzled[^4] from this *very text.* For Jesus, he says, went up; not another went up, but the *same one* who had gone down into the water.

[^1]: Greek «Εὐθὺς ἀνέβη ἀπὸ τοῦ ὕδατος.» — Matt. 3:16 (the folder-name "3.15" is refined to 3:16 from the Greek). *Εὐθύς* ("straightway, immediately"); *ἀνέβη*, aorist of *ἀναβαίνω* ("went up, came up"). The word *ἀνέβη* is the hinge of the anti-Manichaean argument below.
[^2]: OCR *Αανχαῖοι*; securely = *Μανιχαῖοι* ("Manichaeans"), the dualist sect whose docetic Christology denied that Christ had a real, enduring human body.
[^3]: Greek *κατὰ φαντασίαν* — "according to appearance / in phantasm / by way of an apparition," the technical term for docetic unreality (cf. *φαντασία*, "appearance to the imagination"). The Manichaean claim: the real body was left behind in the Jordan and only a phantom body was shown thereafter.
[^4]: Greek *Ἐπιστομίζονται* ("they are muzzled, silenced, stopped in the mouth"), present passive of *ἐπιστομίζω* — literally to put something over the mouth; hence "are refuted, silenced." Rendered "muzzled" to keep the vivid image. The supplied "*from this very text*" (*ἐντεῦθεν*, "from here, hence") points to the Gospel's own wording as the refutation.
