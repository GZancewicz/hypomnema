# Matthew 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 39 · Matt. 3:17

> **This is my beloved Son, (Matt. 3:17)**[^1]

— came about on account of John, but that, having seen the Spirit, they might believe that this voice is on account of Jesus.[^2]

And "like a dove," on account of *its* guilelessness and gentleness; and because the dove is a most pure *creature*, and where there is a foul smell, it does not remain; so also *is* the Holy Spirit.[^3] But also in the *time* of Noah, a dove announced the release from the flood, bearing a twig of olive; so also here the Holy Spirit signifies *the* release of sins. There, a twig of olive; but here, *the* mercy of God.[^4]

[^1]: Folder metadata assigns 3:15; the lemma «Οὗτός ἐστιν ὁ Υἱός μου ὁ ἀγαπητός» is Matt. 3:17, and the header is corrected.
[^2]: This lemma opens by completing the sentence begun in Lemma 38 (*ὡς ἂν μὴ νομίσωσιν ὅτι ἡ φωνὴ ἡ λέγουσα, «Οὗτός ἐστιν ὁ Υἱός μου ὁ ἀγαπητός,» διὰ τὸν Ἰωάννην ἐγένετο, ἀλλ᾽ ἰδόντες τὸ Πνεῦμα πιστεύσωσιν…*): "so that they might not suppose that the voice which says, 'This is my beloved Son,' came about on account of John, but that, having seen the Spirit, they might believe…". The em-dash marks the resumption.
[^3]: The clause is OCR-corrupt: *καὶ ὅτι καθαρεῶτατάν λοτιά ἡ περιστερά* is recoverable as *καὶ ὅτι καθαρώτατόν [ἐστι(ν)] ἡ περιστερά* ("and because the dove is a most pure *creature*"); *λοτιά* is garbled, best-guess the verb *ἐστι*. Also *δυσοδία* stands for *δυσωδία* ("foul smell / stench").
[^4]: A play on sound underlies the close: *κάρφος ἐλαίας* ("twig of olive," *ἐλαία* = olive tree) is capped by *ἔλεος Θεοῦ* ("mercy of God") — olive/oil (*ἔλαιον*) being the traditional token of *ἔλεος* (mercy). Greek *λύσιν τοῦ κατακλυσμοῦ* is here rendered "release from the flood" (i.e. its ending); the same word *λύσιν* then returns as *λύσιν τῶν ἁμαρτιῶν* ("release of sins"), the parallel Theophylact is drawing.
