# Matthew 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 15 · Matt. 3:8 (citing Ps. 33:15 LXX)

> **For turn away, he says, from evil, and do good. (cf. Ps. 33:15 LXX / 34:14)**[^1]

[^1]: Greek «Ἔκκλινον γὰρ, φησὶν, ἀπὸ κακοῦ, καὶ ποίησον ἀγαθόν.» This is not a Matthean lemma but a scriptural proof-text Theophylact adduces within the comment on Matt. 3:8, quoting Ps. 33:15 LXX (= Ps. 34:14 MT / 37:27). It continues the previous lemma's point that repentance requires not merely fleeing vice (*ἔκκλινον ἀπὸ κακοῦ*, "turn away from evil") but doing good (*ποίησον ἀγαθόν*). The *γὰρ* ("for") and *φησὶν* ("he says") are present words, hence roman; only the folder-metadata verse 3.7 has been refined to the 3:8 context.
