# Matthew 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 24 · Matt. 3:11

> **He will baptize you in *the* Holy Spirit.**[^1]

That is, He will flood you abundantly with the graces of the Spirit.[^2] Since my baptism, he says, does not give spiritual grace — for neither does it give the forgiveness of sins — but he will both forgive you and will give the Spirit lavishly.[^3]

[^1]: Greek «Αὐτὸς ὑμᾶς βαπτίσει ἐν Πνεύματι ἁγίῳ» (Matt. 3:11); the source's «Λύτὸς» is an OCR slip for *Αὐτὸς* ("He *himself*"). The Greek is anarthrous (*ἐν Πνεύματι ἁγίῳ*, "in Holy Spirit"); the supplied "*the*" is italicized.
[^2]: Greek *Κατακλύσει ὑμᾶς ἀφθόνως ταῖς τοῦ Πνεύματος χάρισιν* — *κατακλύζω* = "to inundate, flood over," continuing the water-imagery of baptism: the Spirit's *χάρισιν* ("graces, gifts") poured out as a flood.
[^3]: Greek *αὐτὸς δέ, καὶ ἀφήσει ὑμῖν, καὶ Πνεῦμα δαψιλῶς δώσει*; the source's *δώτει* is an OCR slip for *δώσει* ("will give"). The *μὲν … δέ* pair (*τὸ μὲν ἐμὸν … αὐτὸς δέ*) is rendered as a plain contrastive: *μέν* is left untranslated and *δέ* carries the contrast ("but"). There is a wordplay in *ἀφήσει*: *ἀφίημι* means both "to forgive" and "to release / grant"; here "will forgive you" (of sins), answering the *ἄφεσιν ἁμαρτιῶν* ("forgiveness of sins") just denied to John's baptism.
