# Matthew 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 18 · Matt. 3:10

> **And already the axe also lies at the root of the trees. (Matt. 3:10)**[^1]

By "axe" he means the judgment of Christ; and by "trees," each one of us. Therefore the one who has not believed is, from this point, already cut off root and all, and is cast into Gehenna.[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Ἤδη δὲ καὶ ἡ ἀξίνη πρὸς τὴν ῥίζαν τῶν δένδρων κεῖται.» *ἀξίνη* = "axe"; *κεῖται* = "lies / is laid / is set." The particle *δὲ* ("and/but") and *καὶ* ("also") are rendered roman.
[^2]: Greek *Ὁ οὖν μὴ πιστεύσας, ἐντεῦθεν ἤδη αὐτόῤῥιζος ἀποτέμνεται, καὶ εἰς γέενναν ἐμβάλλεται.* The adjective *αὐτόῤῥιζος* means "root and all, together with the very root" — rendered adverbially ("cut off root and all") since English lacks a single adjective. *ἐντεῦθεν ἤδη* = "from here already / from this very point already." Minor OCR: *γέκνναν* → *γέενναν* ("Gehenna").
