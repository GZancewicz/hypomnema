# Matthew 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 3 · Matt. 3:1-2

> **Preaching in the wilderness of Judea, and saying: Repent.**

Boastful[^1] were the Jews; for this reason he presses[^2] them into repentance.

[^1]: Greek «ἀλαζόνες» — "boastful, arrogant, vainglorious," the ἀλαζών being the braggart or impostor. Rendered "boastful"; "arrogant" would do equally.
[^2]: Greek «συνωθεῖ» (from συν-ωθέω, "push/thrust together, force, compel"). The verb carries a note of compulsion — herding or driving them together toward repentance; rendered "presses… into," with "drives together" or "compels" as alternatives.
