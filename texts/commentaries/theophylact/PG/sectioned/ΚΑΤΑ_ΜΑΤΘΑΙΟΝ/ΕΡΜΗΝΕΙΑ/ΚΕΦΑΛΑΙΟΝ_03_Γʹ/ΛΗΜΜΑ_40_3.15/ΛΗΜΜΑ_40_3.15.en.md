# Matthew 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 40 · Matt. 3:17

> **This is my beloved Son, in whom I was well pleased; (Matt. 3:17)**[^1]

that is, "In whom I am at rest, in whom I am pleased."[^2]

[^1]: Folder metadata assigns 3:15; the lemma is Matt. 3:17, and the header is corrected. The source «Οὗτός ἑστιν ἃ Υλέμου ὁ ἀγαπητός» is OCR-corrupt for «Οὗτός ἐστιν ὁ Υἱός μου ὁ ἀγαπητός» ("This is my beloved Son"); the reconstruction is secure, being the same phrase quoted in Lemmata 38–39. Greek *εὐδόκησα* is aorist ("I was well pleased / took delight"); rendered as a past to follow the tense on the page.
[^2]: Greek *τουτέστιν, Ἐν ᾧ ἀναπαύομαι, ἐν ᾧ ἀρέσκομαι* — Theophylact glosses *εὐδόκησα* with two present verbs: *ἀναπαύομαι* ("I rest / am at rest") and *ἀρέσκομαι* ("I am pleased / find satisfaction").
