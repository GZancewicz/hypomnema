# Matthew 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 32 · Matt. 3:14

> **And dost thou come?**[^1]

— thus reverent[^2] was he.

[^1]: Greek «Καὶ σὺ ἔρχῃ;» — a re-quotation of the same words of Matt. 3:14 (folder-name "3.13" corrected to 3:14), here abbreviated to the bare «Καὶ σὺ ἔρχῃ;» to isolate John's modest phrasing. This lemma completes the thought left hanging at *ἀλλά* in Lemma 31: instead of the blunt "art thou baptized by me?" John said only "and dost thou come?" — and *thus* he showed his reverence.
[^2]: Greek *οὕτως ηὐλαβεῖτο*, imperfect of *εὐλαβέομαι* ("to be reverent, cautious, scrupulous, to show pious awe"). The single verb carries the sense "so reverently / cautiously did he refrain *from speaking more plainly*"; the supplied "*was he*" completes the English clause. Rendered "reverent" to keep the note of pious awe before Christ that governs the whole exchange.
