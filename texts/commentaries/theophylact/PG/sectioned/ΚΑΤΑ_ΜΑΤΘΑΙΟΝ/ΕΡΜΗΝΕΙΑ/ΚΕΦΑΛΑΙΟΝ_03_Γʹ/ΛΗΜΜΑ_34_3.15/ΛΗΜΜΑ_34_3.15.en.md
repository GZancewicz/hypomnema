# Matthew 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 34 · Matt. 3:15

> **For thus it is fitting for us to fulfil all righteousness.**[^1]

He calls the law "righteousness."[^2] Accursed,[^3] he says, was human nature, because it could not fulfil the law. I accordingly have fulfilled also the other things of the law; but this is lacking to me, the being baptized. Having accordingly fulfilled this, I shall set nature free[^4] from the curse; for this is fitting for me.

[^1]: Greek «Οὕτω γὰρ πρέπον ἐστὶν ἡμῖν πληρῶσαι πᾶσαν δικαιοσύνην.» *πρέπον ἐστίν* = "it is fitting/becoming"; *πληρῶσαι* ("to fulfil, complete, fill up") governs the whole of the following comment, which plays on *fulfil the law* / *fulfil righteousness*.
[^2]: Greek *Τὸν νόμον λέγει δικαιοσύνην* — Theophylact's own identification: "he calls the law 'righteousness.'" Kept in the body as his exegetical move, not a variant reading.
[^3]: Greek *Ἐπικατάρατος* ("under a curse, accursed"); the word echoes Deut. 27:26 as cited at Gal. 3:10 («Ἐπικατάρατος πᾶς ὃς οὐκ ἐμμένει...»), the text on nature's inability to keep the law and Christ's freeing it from the curse (cf. Gal. 3:13). Translated plainly as "accursed."
[^4]: Greek *ἀπαλλάξω τὴν φύσιν τῆς κατάρας* — future of *ἀπαλλάσσω* ("release, deliver, set free, rid"); "I shall rid/deliver nature of the curse."
