# Matthew 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 10 · Matt. 3:7

> **But having seen many of the Pharisees. (Matt. 3:7)**[^1]

"Pharisee" is interpreted "one cut off."[^2] For they seemed, in their manner of life and in their knowledge, to have been cut off *from the rest*, and to surpass the others.

[^1]: OCR *ταν παρισαίων* = *τῶν Φαρισαίων* ("of the Pharisees"). The particle δὲ is rendered "but." The participle *Ἰδὼν* ("having seen") is kept as a participle.
[^2]: Theophylact's etymology turns on a word-play: *Φαρισαῖος* is glossed *ἀποκεκομμένος* ("one cut off / severed / set apart"), and the verb recurs just below in *ἀποκεκόφθαι* ("to have been cut off," perfect infinitive). The play is preserved by rendering both from the same root ("cut off"). The name in fact derives from Aramaic *pĕrîšayyā* ("separated ones"), which underlies the gloss. OCR *ἀρμηνεύεται* = *ἑρμηνεύεται* ("is interpreted"); *Κλλων* = *ἄλλων* ("others").
