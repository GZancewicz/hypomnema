# Matthew 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 25 · Matt. 3:12

> **Whose winnowing-fan is in his hand.**[^1]

Do not think that if you are baptized by that one, and then you sin, he will pardon you. But he also has a winnowing-fan, that is, judgment and examination.[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Οὗ τὸ πτύον ἐν τῇ χειρὶ αὐτοῦ» — literally "of whom the winnowing-fan *is* in his hand." The verb "is" is not present in the Greek and is supplied (the ordinary copula English requires, left roman); the relative *οὗ* ("whose / of whom") refers to the Coming One of the preceding verses. *πτύον* is the winnowing-fan (a fork/shovel used to toss threshed grain into the air so the wind carries off the chaff), here read as an image of judgment.
[^2]: Greek *κρίσιν καὶ ἐξέτασιν* — "judgment and examination/scrutiny." *ἐξέτασις* is a close inquiry or testing; rendered "examination" to keep the forensic sense paired with "judgment."
