# Matthew 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 14 · Matt. 3:8

> **Make therefore fruits worthy of repentance. (Matt. 3:8)**[^1]

You see what he says: that one must not only flee vice, but also make fruits of virtue.[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Ποιήσατε οὖν καρποὺς ἀξίους τῆς μετανοίας.» The folder metadata assigns this to 3:7, but the lemma is Matt. 3:8. The idiom *ποιήσατε καρπούς* is "produce/bear fruits," but Theophylact's comment turns on the plain verb *ποιεῖν* ("make/do"), which he repeats below (*καρποὺς ἀρετῆς ποιεῖν*); "make fruits" is kept to preserve that verbal echo.
[^2]: Greek *οὐ δεῖ μόνον φεύγειν τὴν κακίαν, ἀλλὰ καὶ καρποὺς ἀρετῆς ποιεῖν* — literally "it is necessary not only to flee vice, but also to make fruits of virtue." The antithesis is *κακία* ("vice / wickedness") vs. *ἀρετή* ("virtue").
