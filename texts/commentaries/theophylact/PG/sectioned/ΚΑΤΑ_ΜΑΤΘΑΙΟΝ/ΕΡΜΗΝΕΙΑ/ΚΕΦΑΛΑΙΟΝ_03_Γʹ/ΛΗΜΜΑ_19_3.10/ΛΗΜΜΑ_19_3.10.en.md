# Matthew 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 19 · Matt. 3:10

> **Every tree, therefore.**[^1]

Even if *it* be descended from Abraham.[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Πᾶν οὖν δένδρον» — the continuation of Matt. 3:10 ("therefore every tree which does not bear good fruit…"). The particle *οὖν* ("therefore") is a present word and so is set in roman, not italics.
[^2]: Greek *Κἂν ἀπὸ τοῦ Ἀβραὰμ κατάγηται* (the source's OCR «Ἀβοαὰμ» is a slip for *Ἀβραὰμ*) — *κατάγομαι* here = "to trace one's descent, be descended from." The subject ("it," i.e. the "tree" = each individual person, per the preceding lemma) is carried by the verb and supplied in italics. The point: no tree escapes the axe on the ground of Abrahamic descent (cf. Matt. 3:9).
