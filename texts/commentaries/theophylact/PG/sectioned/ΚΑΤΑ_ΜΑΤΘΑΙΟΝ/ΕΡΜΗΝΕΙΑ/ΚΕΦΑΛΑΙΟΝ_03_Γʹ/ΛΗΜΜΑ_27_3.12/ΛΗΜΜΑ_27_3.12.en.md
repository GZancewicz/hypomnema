# Matthew 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 27 · Matt. 3:12

> **And he will gather the wheat into his storehouse; but the chaff he will burn up with unquenchable fire.**[^1]

Unquenchable is that fire; so that Origen talks nonsense,[^2] saying that there will be an end of the punishment.[^3]

[^1]: Greek «Καὶ συνάξει τὸν σῖτον εἰς τὴν ἀποθήκην αὐτοῦ· τὸ δὲ ἄχυρον κατακαύσει πυρὶ ἀσβέστῳ.» The OCR «αυνάξει» is securely *συνάξει* ("he will gather"). *ἄσβεστος* = "unquenchable, not to be extinguished," the word on which the whole comment turns.
[^2]: Greek *φλυαρεῖ ὁ Ὠριγένης* — *φλυαρέω* is "to talk nonsense, prattle, babble." Rendered "talks nonsense" to keep the dismissive force.
[^3]: The reference is to Origen's teaching of the *ἀποκατάστασις* (universal restoration), the doctrine that punishment is not everlasting but will come to an end. Theophylact rejects it on the strength of *ἀσβέστῳ* ("unquenchable"). Greek *ἔσται τέλος τῆς κολάσεως* — "there will be an end of the punishment."
