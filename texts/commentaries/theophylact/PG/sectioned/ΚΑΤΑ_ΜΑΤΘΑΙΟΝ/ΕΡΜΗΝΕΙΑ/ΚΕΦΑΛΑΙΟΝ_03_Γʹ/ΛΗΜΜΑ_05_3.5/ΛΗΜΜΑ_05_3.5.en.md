# Matthew 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 5 · Matt. 3:4

> **And John himself had his garment of camel's hair. (Matt. 3:4)**

He was calling them to repentance also from his attire.[^1] For he had a robe of mourning. And they say the camel to be between clean and not clean. For in so far as it chews the cud, it is clean; but in so far as it is not cloven-hoofed as to its nails, it is not clean. Since, therefore, John also was bringing to God both the people seeming clean — the Jewish — and the unclean — the gentile — and was a mediator of the old covenant and the new, for this reason he wore the hairs of the camel.

[^1]: Greek «ἀπὸ τοῦ σχήματος». «σχῆμα» means "outward form, figure, fashion, dress"; here his outward guise or attire. Rendered "attire" for sense; "outward appearance" or "guise" would render the word more broadly.
