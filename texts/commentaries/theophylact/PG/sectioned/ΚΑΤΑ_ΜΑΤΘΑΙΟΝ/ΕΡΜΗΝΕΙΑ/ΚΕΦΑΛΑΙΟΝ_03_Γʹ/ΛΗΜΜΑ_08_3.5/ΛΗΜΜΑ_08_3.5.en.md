# Matthew 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 8 · Matt. 3:5

> **Then *there* was going out to him Jerusalem, and all Judaea, and all the region round about the Jordan. And[^1] (Matt. 3:5)**

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation and no exposition; Theophylact's comment resumes in the next lemma, where the sentence is completed with "were baptized in the Jordan by him…"*[^2]

[^1]: Greek *ἐξεπορεύετο* is imperfect ("was going out"), with the collective subjects taking a singular verb; rendered "there was going out" to keep the durative aspect. OCR *τᾶσα* = *πᾶσα* ("all"). The lemma ends abruptly on *Καὶ* ("And"), which runs on into the next lemma.
[^2]: The next lemma (09) opens with *ἐβαπτίζοντο ἐν τῷ Ἰορδάνῃ ὑπ᾽ αὐτοῦ, ἐξομολογούμενοι τὰς ἁμαρτίας αὐτῶν* ("were baptized in the Jordan by him, confessing their sins," Matt. 3:6), completing the sentence Theophylact has here broken off, and it is there that his exposition begins.
