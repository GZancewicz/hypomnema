# Matthew 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 8 · Matt. 3:5

> **Then there was going out to him Jerusalem, and all Judaea, and all the region round about the Jordan. And[^1] (Matt. 3:5)**

[^1]: Greek *ἐξεπορεύετο* is imperfect ("was going out"), with the collective subjects taking a singular verb; rendered "there was going out" to keep the durative aspect. OCR *τᾶσα* = *πᾶσα* ("all"). The lemma ends abruptly on *Καὶ* ("And"), which runs on into the next lemma.
