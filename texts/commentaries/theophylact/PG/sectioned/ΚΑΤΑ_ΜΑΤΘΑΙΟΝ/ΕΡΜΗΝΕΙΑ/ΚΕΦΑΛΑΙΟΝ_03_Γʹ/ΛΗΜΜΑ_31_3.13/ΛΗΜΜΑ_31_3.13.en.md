# Matthew 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 31 · Matt. 3:14

> **And dost thou come to me?**[^1]

He did not dare[^2] to say, "And art thou baptized by me?" but[^3]

[^1]: Greek «Καὶ σὺ ἔρχῃ πρός με·» — the closing words of John the Baptist's protest at Matt. 3:14 (following «Ἐγὼ χρείαν ἔχω ὑπὸ σοῦ βαπτισθῆναι», expounded in the previous lemma). The folder-name verse "3.13" is corrected to 3:14 from the Greek. Present-tense verb *ἔρχῃ* ("thou comest"); the supplied "*dost*" marks the interrogative English form the Greek carries by intonation alone.
[^2]: OCR *ἐπόλμησεν*; securely = *ἐτόλμησεν* ("he dared"), aorist of *τολμάω*. Low-stakes reconstruction, noted for consistency with the other flagged corruptions.
[^3]: The comment breaks off mid-sentence at *ἀλλά* ("but,"): John, out of reverence, did not put it as bluntly as "art thou baptized by me?" but *rather in the more modest form that the next lemma re-quotes* («Καὶ σὺ ἔρχῃ;»). The thought is completed in Lemma 32.
