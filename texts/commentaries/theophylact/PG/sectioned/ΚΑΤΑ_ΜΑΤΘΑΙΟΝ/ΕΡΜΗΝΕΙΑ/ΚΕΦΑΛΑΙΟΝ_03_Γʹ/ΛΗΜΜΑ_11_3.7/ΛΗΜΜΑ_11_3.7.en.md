# Matthew 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 11 · Matt. 3:7

> **And of the Sadducees. (Matt. 3:7)**

These believed neither *in* resurrection, nor *in* angel, nor *in* spirit.[^1] And they are interpreted "righteous ones." For *sedek* is righteousness;[^2] for they named themselves righteous. Or else they are named from the heresiarch Sadok.[^3]

[^1]: Cf. Acts 23:8. Greek *οὔτε … οὔτε … οὔτε* ("neither … nor … nor"); the objects (*ἀνάστασιν, ἄγγελον, πνεῦμα*) are accusatives governed by *ἐπίστευον*, so the "*in*" is supplied.
[^2]: Theophylact derives *Σαδδουκαῖος* from *Σεδὲκ* (Hebrew *ṣedeq*, "righteousness"): they called themselves "righteous ones" (*δίκαιοι*). The Greek is *Σεδὲκ γὰρ, ἡ δικαιοσύνη* — "for *sedek* is (the word for) righteousness"; *Σεδέκ* kept in transliteration as a word being discussed.
[^3]: OCR *Ἡ ἀπὸ αἱρεσιάρχου Σαδὼκ* = *Ἢ ἀπὸ αἱρεσιάρχου Σαδώκ* ("Or *else* they are named from the heresiarch Sadok"). This is the competing derivation, from a proposed founder Sadok/Zadok (*αἱρεσιάρχης*, "head of a sect / heresiarch"), rather than from the Hebrew word for righteousness. Further trivial OCR corrections: *ὰνομάζονται* = *ὀνομάζονται* ("they are named"); *ὡνόμαζον* = *ὠνόμαζον* ("they named," above); and *Ἐρμηνεύον-/ται* (line-broken in the source) = *ἑρμηνεύονται* ("they are interpreted").
