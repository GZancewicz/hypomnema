# Matthew 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 28 · Matt. 3:13

> **Then Jesus comes from Galilee to the Jordan, to**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the opening scripture words and breaks off mid-sentence at «πρὸς τὸν» ("to the—"); it has no exposition of its own. The quotation is completed at the head of the next lemma («John, to be baptized by him…»), where Theophylact's comment begins.*[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Τότε παραγίνεται ὁ Ἰησοῦς ἀπὸ τῆς Γαλιλαίας ἐπὶ τὸν Ἰορδάνην πρὸς τὸν». The OCR «ἐσὶ» is a certain misprint for *ἐπὶ* ("to / upon"). *παραγίνεται* is a historic present ("comes / arrives"), kept as a present tense. The sentence is left unfinished in the source.
[^2]: The lemma resumes in Lemma 29 with «Ἰωάννην, τοῦ βαπτισθῆναι ὑπ´ αὐτοῦ· ὁ δὲ Ἰωάννης διεκώλυεν αὐτὸν, λέγων» ("…John, to be baptized by him; but John was preventing him, saying"), after which the exposition proper follows.
