# Matthew 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 28 · Matt. 3:13

> **Then Jesus comes from Galilee to the Jordan, to**[^1]

[^1]: Greek «Τότε παραγίνεται ὁ Ἰησοῦς ἀπὸ τῆς Γαλιλαίας ἐπὶ τὸν Ἰορδάνην πρὸς τὸν». The OCR «ἐσὶ» is a certain misprint for *ἐπὶ* ("to / upon"). *παραγίνεται* is a historic present ("comes / arrives"), kept as a present tense. The sentence is left unfinished in the source and is completed at the head of Lemma 29.
