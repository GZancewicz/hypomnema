# Matthew 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 26 · Matt. 3:12

> **And he will thoroughly cleanse his threshing-floor.**[^1]

That is, the Church, which has many baptized, as on the threshing-floor: *some* are chaff,[^2] being light and moved by the spirits of wickedness; but others are wheat, as many as also benefit others and nourish *them* through teaching and practice.

[^1]: Greek «Καὶ διακαθαριεῖ τὴν ἅλωνα αὐτοῦ» — the OCR reads «τὴν ἅλω α αὐτοῦ», securely = *τὴν ἅλωνα αὐτοῦ*, "his threshing-floor." The compound *διακαθαρίζω* is intensive ("cleanse thoroughly / clean out"); the winnowing purges the floor of chaff.
[^2]: The Greek of the middle clause is OCR-corrupt: «πᾶν ἄω κοῦφοί εἰσι καὶ τοῖς πνεύμασι τῆς πονηρίκς κινούμενοι». It is recoverable from sense and from the μὲν … δὲ frame («πολλοὺς μὲν … οἱ δὲ σῖτος») as roughly *οἱ μὲν ἄχυρόν εἰσι, κοῦφοι ὄντες καὶ τοῖς πνεύμασι τῆς πονηρίας κινούμενοι* — "some are chaff, being light and moved by the spirits of wickedness." «πονηρίκς» is a certain misprint for *πονηρίας* ("of wickedness"). The phrase *τὰ πνεύματα τῆς πονηρίας* is the standard idiom for the evil spirits (cf. Eph. 6:12), hence "spirits of wickedness"; *πνεῦμα* also means "wind," so the chaff-imagery admits a secondary sense "winds of wickedness." The chaff/wheat antithesis is the point; the exact wording of the corrupt words is not recoverable and is here reconstructed for sense.
