# Matthew 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 12 · Matt. 3:7

> **coming to his baptism, he said to them. (Matt. 3:7)**[^1]

They were not coming with a right intention, as the rest did.[^2] On account of this he also rebukes them.[^3]

[^1]: This lemma completes Matt. 3:7 begun in lemmata 10–11 ("But having seen many of the Pharisees and of the Sadducees…"). OCR *αἷσεν αὐτοῖς* = *εἶπεν αὐτοῖς* ("he said to them").
[^2]: Greek *μετὰ γνώμης ὀρθῆς* — *γνώμη* spans "mind, purpose, intention, disposition, judgment"; rendered "intention." OCR *ς οἱ λοιποί* = *ὡς οἱ λοιποί* ("as the rest," i.e. as the other people who came, in contrast to these Pharisees and Sadducees).
[^3]: Greek *καθάπτεται αὐτῶν* — *καθάπτομαι* + genitive here means to assail or rebuke with words (not to seize physically); rendered "rebukes," with "reproaches / upbraids" as alternatives and "lays hold of" the literal, etymological gloss. *Διὰ τοῦτο* = "on account of this"; *καὶ* here = "also."
