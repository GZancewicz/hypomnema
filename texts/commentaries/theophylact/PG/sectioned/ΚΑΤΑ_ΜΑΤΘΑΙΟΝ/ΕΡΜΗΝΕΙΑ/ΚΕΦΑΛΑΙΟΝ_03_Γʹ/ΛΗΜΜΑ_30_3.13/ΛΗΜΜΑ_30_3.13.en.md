# Matthew 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 30 · Matt. 3:14

> **I have need to be baptized by you.**[^1]

For the Forerunner had need to be cleansed by the Lord. For, as being descended from Adam, he too was dragging along with *him* the defilement *that came* from the disobedience;[^2] but Christ, having been made flesh, cleansed all.

[^1]: Greek «Ἐγὼ χρείαν ἔχω ὑπὸ σοῦ βαπτισθῆναι.» (Matt. 3:14 — the folder-name verse 3.13 is corrected here to 3:14, the words John speaks). The OCR «ὡπὼ σοῦ» is securely *ὑπὸ σοῦ* ("by you"). The emphatic *Ἐγὼ* ("I") is expressed by the Greek and so is roman, not italic.
[^2]: Greek *ἐπεσύρετο τὸ μόλυσμα τὸ ἐκ τῆς παρακοῆς* — *ἐπισύρω* in the middle, "to drag along after/with oneself"; *μόλυσμα* is the "stain, pollution, defilement," and *παρακοή* is "disobedience" (literally a mis-hearing, a failure to heed), i.e. the ancestral fault of Adam. The OCR «Ἀὰμ» is a certain misprint for *Ἀδάμ* ("Adam").
