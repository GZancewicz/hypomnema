# Matthew 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 16 · Matt. 3:9

> **And do not presume to say within yourselves: We have Abraham *as* father. (Matt. 3:9)**[^1]

This was for them unto destruction: that they took confidence in *their* noble birth.[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Καὶ μὴ δόξητε λέγειν ἐν ἑαυτοῖς· Πατέρα ἔχομεν τὸν Ἀβραάμ.» The verb *δόξητε* (from *δοκέω*) is "think / suppose / presume"; "do not presume to say" captures the sense of unwarranted self-assurance the comment develops. The supplied "*as*" renders the predicate accusative *πατέρα … τὸν Ἀβραάμ* ("Abraham *as* father"), carried in Greek by case alone.
[^2]: Greek *Τοῦτο ἦν αὐτοῖς εἰς ἀπώλειαν, ὅτι ἐν εὐγενείᾳ ἐθάρρουν.* — "This was for them unto destruction, because they trusted in *their* noble birth." *εὐγένεια* = "good/noble descent, high birth" (here, descent from Abraham); *ἐθάρρουν* (imperfect of *θαρρέω*) = "were taking confidence, relied confidently."
