# Matthew 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 21 · Matt. 3:10

> **Is hewn down, and cast into fire.**[^1]

The *fire* of Gehenna.[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Ἐκκόπτεται, καὶ εἰς πῦρ βάλλεται» — the closing words of Matt. 3:10.
[^2]: Greek *Τὸ τῆς γεέννης* — literally "the [*fire*] of Gehenna": the neuter article *τὸ* refers back to the neuter noun *πῦρ* ("fire") of the lemma, which Theophylact leaves to be understood. He glosses the "fire" as the fire of Gehenna (hell), not an earthly one. "*fire*" is supplied in italics.
