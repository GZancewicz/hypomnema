# Matthew 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 38 · Matt. 3:16–17

> **And he saw the Spirit of God descending like a dove, and coming upon him, and behold, a voice from the heavens, saying. (Matt. 3:16–17)**[^1]

The Spirit descends, in order that it may testify that the one being baptized *is* greater than the one baptizing. For the Jews held John *to be* great, but of Christ they did not take so much thought. And all saw the Spirit coming upon Jesus, so that they might not suppose that the voice which says,[^2]

[^1]: Folder metadata assigns 3:15, but the lemma «Καὶ εἶδε τὸ Πνεῦμα τοῦ Θεοῦ καταβαῖνον ὡσεὶ περιστερὰν…» spans the end of 3:16 into 3:17; the header is corrected. The source reads *εδε*, an OCR corruption securely = *εἶδε* ("he saw"), and *Ἀκ τῶν οὐρανῶν* for *ἐκ τῶν οὐρανῶν* ("from the heavens").
[^2]: The sentence breaks off mid-clause at *ἡ φωνὴ ἡ λέγουσα* ("the voice which says,"); it is completed in the next lemma, which supplies «Οὗτός ἐστιν ὁ Υἱός μου ὁ ἀγαπητός». The source *Εἶδου δὲ… πάντες* is OCR for *εἶδον δέ… πάντες* ("but all saw"), and *ὡς ἂνμὴ νομίσωσιν* for *ὡς ἂν μὴ νομίσωσιν* ("so that they might not suppose").
