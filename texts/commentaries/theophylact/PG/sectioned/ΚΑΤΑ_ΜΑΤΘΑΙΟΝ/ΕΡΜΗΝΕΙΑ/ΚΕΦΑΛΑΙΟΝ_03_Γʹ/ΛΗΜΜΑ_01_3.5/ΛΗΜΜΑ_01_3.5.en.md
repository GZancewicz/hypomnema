# Matthew 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 1 · Matt. 3:1

> **And in those days. (Matt. 3:1)**

Not when the Lord was a child, and was dwelling in Nazareth, but he says it simply thus, as if to say, in that time, the *time* before the present generation.[^1]

[^1]: The OCR reads «τῷ πρὸ τῆς υῆν νενεκς», recoverable as «τῷ πρὸ τῆς νῦν γενεᾶς» ("the *time* before the present generation" — υῆν = *νῦν*, "now/present"; νενεκς = *γενεᾶς*, "generation"; the article τῷ stands for καιρῷ, "time," from the preceding «ἐν τῷ καιρῷ ἐκείνῳ"). Theophylact's point is that "in those days" fixes a general past period, not the years of the Lord's childhood in Nazareth.
