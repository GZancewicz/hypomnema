# Matthew 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 1 · Matt. 3:1

> **And in those days.**

Not when the Lord was a child, and was dwelling in Nazareth, but he says *it* simply thus, as *if to say*, in that time, the *one* before the [?].[^1]

[^1]: The clause breaks off in OCR corruption: «τῷ πρὸ τῆς υῆν νενεκς.» The opening «τῷ πρὸ τῆς» ("the *one* before the…," the article standing for καιρῷ, "time," from the preceding «ἐν τῷ καιρῷ ἐκείνῳ") is secure, but the governing noun is unrecoverable from the garbled «υῆν νενεκς». Theophylact's point is that "in those days" fixes a general period — the *time* before John's/Christ's public appearance, not the years of the Lord's childhood in Nazareth — so the lost word is plausibly something like «φανερώσεως» ("manifestation") or «βαπτίσματος» ("baptism"), but this is left bracketed rather than invented.
