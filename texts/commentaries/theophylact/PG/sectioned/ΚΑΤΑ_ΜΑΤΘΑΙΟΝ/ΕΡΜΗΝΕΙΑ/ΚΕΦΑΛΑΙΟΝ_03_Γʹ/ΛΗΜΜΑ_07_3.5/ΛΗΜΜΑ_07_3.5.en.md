# Matthew 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 7 · Matt. 3:4

> **But his food was locusts and wild honey. (Matt. 3:4)**[^1]

Some say the locusts are herbs, which they also call *melagra*;[^2] but others, *that they are* tree-fruits, that is, wild orchard-fruits;[^3] and wild honey *is* that which is worked by wild bees, found in trees and rocks.[^4]

[^1]: The folder metadata assigns this lemma to Matt. 3:5, but the quoted words «Ἡ δὲ τροφὴ αὐτοῦ ἦν ἀκρίδες, καὶ μέλι ἄγριον» ("his food was locusts and wild honey") belong to Matt. 3:4; the header verse is corrected accordingly. The opening particle δὲ is rendered "but" (roman, since it is a present word).
[^2]: OCR *Τι ἐς λέγουσι* securely = *τινὲς λέγουσι* ("some say"); *ἂς* = *ἃς*. The word the OCR gives as **μέλαγρα** is uncertain — it names some plant/herb equated here with the "locusts," but the exact form and referent are not securely recoverable; retained in transliteration and flagged. Theophylact reports a botanical reading of *ἀκρίδες*: that "locusts" here means edible plant-shoots rather than the insects.
[^3]: Greek *τὰ ἀκρόδρυα ἤτοι ὀπώρας ἀγρίας* — *ἀκρόδρυα* are hard-shelled tree-fruits (nuts and the like); *ὀπώρα* is late-summer/autumn fruit. Rendered "tree-fruits, that is, wild orchard-fruits."
[^4]: Greek *γεωργούμενον* literally "farmed/cultivated/husbanded," here paradoxically of *wild* bees (*ὑπὸ ἀγρίων μελισσῶν*); rendered "worked by" to keep the productive sense without the domestic overtone. OCR *εὐρισκόμενον* = *εὑρισκόμενον* ("found").
