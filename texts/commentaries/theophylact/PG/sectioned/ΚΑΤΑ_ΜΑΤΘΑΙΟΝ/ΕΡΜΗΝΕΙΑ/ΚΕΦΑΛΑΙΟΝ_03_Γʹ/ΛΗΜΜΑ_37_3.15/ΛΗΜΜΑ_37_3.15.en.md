# Matthew 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 37 · Matt. 3:16

> **And behold, the heavens were opened to him. (Matt. 3:16)**[^1]

Adam closed them; but through Christ they are opened; so that you may learn that you too, whenever you are baptized, open them.[^2]

[^1]: The folder metadata assigns this lemma to 3:15, but the Greek «Καὶ ἰδοὺ ἀνεῴχθησαν αὐτῷ οἱ οὐρανοί» is the opening of Matt. 3:16; the header is corrected accordingly. Greek *ἀνεῴχθησαν* is aorist passive ("were opened").
[^2]: Greek *καὶ σὺ ὅταν βαπτίζῃ, ἀνοίγεις αὐτούς* — the verb *ἀνοίγεις* is present active, 2nd person ("you open them"), so the baptized person is the one who opens the heavens; the emphatic *καὶ σύ* ("you too") is kept. *ὅταν βαπτίζῃ* is rendered "whenever you are baptized," taking the verb as passive/middle in force.
