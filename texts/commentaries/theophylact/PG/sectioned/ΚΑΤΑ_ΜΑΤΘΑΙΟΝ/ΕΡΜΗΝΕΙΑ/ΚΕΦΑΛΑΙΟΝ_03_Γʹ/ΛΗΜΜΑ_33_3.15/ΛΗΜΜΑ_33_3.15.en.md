# Matthew 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 33 · Matt. 3:15

> **But Jesus answering said to him: Permit *it* just now.**[^1]

Now, he says, give way;[^2] but there will be a time when we shall have the glory that is worthy,[^3] even if now we do not appear so.

[^1]: Greek «Ἀποκριθεὶς δὲ ὁ Ἰησοῦς, εἶπε πρὸς αὐτόν· Ἄφες ἄρτι.» *Ἄφες* is the aorist imperative of *ἀφίημι* ("let go, permit, allow, suffer"); *ἄρτι* = "just now, for the present, at this moment." Rendered "Permit *it* just now" to catch the temporal force of *ἄρτι* (permit for the present time — the point Theophylact draws out); the object "*it*" is supplied.
[^2]: Greek *παραχώρησον*, aorist imperative of *παραχωρέω* ("give way, yield, make room, concede"). Theophylact glosses the terse «Ἄφες» with this fuller word: "give way *to me for now.*"
[^3]: Greek *τὴν ἀξίαν δόξαν ἕξομεν* — "we shall have the worthy glory," i.e. the honour due *to us*, which is not yet manifest. *ἀξίαν* ("worthy, deserved, befitting") qualifies the glory as that which properly belongs to Christ though presently hidden.
