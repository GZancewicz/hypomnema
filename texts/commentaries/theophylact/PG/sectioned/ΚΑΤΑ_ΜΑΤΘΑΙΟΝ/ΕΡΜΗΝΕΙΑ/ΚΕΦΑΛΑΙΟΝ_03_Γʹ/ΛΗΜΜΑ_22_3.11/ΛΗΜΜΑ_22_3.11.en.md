# Matthew 3 (Γʹ) — Lemma 22 · Matt. 3:11

> **I baptize you in water unto repentance; but the one coming after me is stronger than I.**[^1]

He had said to them, "Bear fruit." He shows, therefore, what the fruit is: that it is to believe in the one coming after him. And after him came Christ; inasmuch as he was also later than him according to his birth, by six months only,[^2] but also according to the manifestation. For the Forerunner was manifested first, then Christ, witnessed to by him.

[^1]: Greek «Ἐγὼ μὲν βαπτίζω ὑμᾶς ἐν ὕδατι εἰς μετάνοιαν· ὁ δὲ ὀπίσω μου ἐρχόμενος, ἰσχυρότερός μου ἐστίν» (Matt. 3:11). The *μὲν … δὲ* pair is rendered as a plain contrastive: *μέν* is left untranslated and *δέ* carries the contrast ("but"). *ὁ ὀπίσω μου ἐρχόμενος* is kept as a participle-phrase, "the one coming after me."
[^2]: Greek *ἓξ μόνοις μησίν* — "by six months only." The reference is to Luke 1:26, 36: the annunciation to Mary in the sixth month of Elizabeth's pregnancy, so that Christ was born some six months after John. *ὕστερος αὐτοῦ … κατὰ τὴν γέννησιν* = "later than him with respect to birth," i.e. younger; "his" is supplied.
