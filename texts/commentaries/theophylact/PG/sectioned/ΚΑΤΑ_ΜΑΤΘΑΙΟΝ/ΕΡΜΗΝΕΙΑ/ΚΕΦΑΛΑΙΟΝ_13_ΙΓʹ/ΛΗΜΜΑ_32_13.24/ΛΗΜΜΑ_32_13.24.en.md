# Matthew 13 (ΙΓʹ) — Lemma 32 · Matt. 13:49

> **So shall it be in the consummation of the age. The angels shall go forth, and shall separate the wicked from *the* midst of the righteous, and (Matt. 13:49)**[^1]

*This lemma carries only scripture words; it continues from the preceding lemma and breaks off at "and," to be completed in the next. Theophylact's exposition of the whole dragnet parable appears at Lemma 33.*

[^1]: Greek *οὕτως ἔσται ἐν τῇ συντελείᾳ τοῦ αἰῶνος. Ἐξελεύσονται οἱ ἄγγελοι, καὶ ἀφοριοῦσι τοὺς πονηροὺς ἐκ μέσου τῶν δικαίων, καί*. *συντελείᾳ τοῦ αἰῶνος* rendered "consummation of the age" (the technical eschatological term Theophylact uses; not conformed to any English-Bible "end of the world"). *ἐκ μέσου* literally "out of *the* midst"; the supplied "*the*" is italicized. The lemma breaks off at *καί* ("and").
