# Matthew 13 (ΙΓʹ) — Lemma 24 · Matt. 13:41

> **… the Son of man will send forth his angels, and they will gather out of his kingdom all the stumbling-blocks,[^1] and those doing lawlessness, and … (Matt. 13:41)**

*This lemma carries the Gospel text only, continuing the sentence begun in Lemma 23 and breaking off at "and …"; Theophylact adds no exposition here — see Lemma 25.*

[^1]: Greek *τὰ σκάνδαλα* — literally "the traps / snares," in scriptural usage "the stumbling-blocks, causes of stumbling, offenses." Rendered "stumbling-blocks."
