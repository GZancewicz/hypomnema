# Matthew 13 (ΙΓʹ) — Lemma 38 · Matt. 13:54

> **…so that they were astonished, and said: Whence *is* this wisdom to this *man*, and the powers?[^1] *Is* not[^2]— (Matt. 13:54–55)**

*This section carries only the scripture lemma; Theophylact's exposition of these verses is given below, at Lemma 40.*[^3]

[^1]: Greek *αἱ δυνάμεις* — literally "the powers," but in this context the "mighty works / miracles" of Jesus (the crowd asks whence his wisdom and his miracles come). Rendered "powers" for consistency with the core gloss of *δύναμις*; the sense here is the deeds of power. The same word recurs in the lemma at 13:58 (Lemma 42, «he did not do there many *δυνάμεις*»).
[^2]: The lemma breaks off mid-question at *Οὐχ* ("*Is* not"), completed in the next lemma: «…this the son of the carpenter?» (Matt. 13:55). Guillemets in the source close the quotation at this break.
[^3]: The source .txt for this section contains no exposition — only Theophylact's running quotation of the Gospel, which he divides across several sections before commenting. His comment on the Nazarenes' words (13:54–57) is written out at Lemma 40.
