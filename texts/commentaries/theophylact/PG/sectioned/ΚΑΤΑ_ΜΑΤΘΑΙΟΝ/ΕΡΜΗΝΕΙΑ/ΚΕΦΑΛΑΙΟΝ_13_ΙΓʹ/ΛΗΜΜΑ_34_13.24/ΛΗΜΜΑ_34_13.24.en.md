# Matthew 13 (ΙΓʹ) — Lemma 34 · Matt. 13:51

> **Jesus says to them: Have you understood all these things? They say to him: Yes, Lord. And he (Matt. 13:51)**[^1]

*This lemma carries only scripture words; it breaks off at "And he," to be completed in the next lemma («…said unto them: Therefore every scribe…»), where Theophylact's exposition begins.*

[^1]: Greek *Λέγει αὐτοῖς ὁ Ἰησοῦς· Συνήκατε ταῦτα πάντα; Λέγουσιν αὐτῷ· Ναί, Κύριε. Ὁ δέ*. The OCR reads «στάντα» for *πάντα* ("all") — a secure correction, "all these things." The present tense *λέγει / λέγουσιν* ("says / say") is kept, against the past-tense English-Bible convention. The lemma breaks off at *Ὁ δέ* ("And he").
