# Matthew 13 (ΙΓʹ) — Lemma 19 · Matt. 13:34-36

> **All these things spoke Jesus in parables to the crowds, and without a parable he did not speak to them, so that there might be fulfilled that which *was* spoken through the prophet, saying: I will open my mouth in parables, I will utter[^1] things hidden from *the* foundation of *the* world. (Matt. 13:34-35)**

He brings forward the prophet foretelling how Jesus was about to teach, and that *it would be* in parables, so that you might not think that Christ discovered some new manner of teaching. But the *word* "so that" [ὅπως], do not take *it* causally,[^2] but from the outcome of the matter. For Christ did not teach in such a manner in order that the prophecy might be fulfilled, but because he taught in parables, from the result the *saying* of the prophet is found *to have been* fulfilled in him. And *as for* "without a parable he did not speak to them" — *this was so* then only; for indeed he did not always speak in parables. And the Lord uttered the things which were hidden from *the* foundation of *the* world. For he himself made manifest to us the mysteries in *the* heavens.

> **Then, having sent away the crowds, Jesus went into the house. (Matt. 13:36a)**

He dismissed the crowds at that time, when they were profited nothing by the teaching. For he, on the one hand, spoke in parables, so that they might question him; but they, on the other hand, not having taken thought of this, nor having sought to learn anything, are reasonably dismissed by him.

[^1]: Greek *ἐρεύξομαι* (Matt. 13:35, quoting Ps. 77:2 LXX / 78:2), literally "I will belch / pour forth"; used metaphorically of vehement utterance, hence "utter, give vent to." Theophylact takes it up again just below in the finite form *ἐρεύξατο* ("the Lord uttered"); rendered with the same gloss to keep the echo visible.
[^2]: Greek *αἰτιολογικῶς* — "causally / as stating the reason." His point is that ὅπως ("so that") here marks not purpose but result (*ἐκ τῆς ἐκβάσεως*, "from the outcome"): Christ did not teach in parables *in order to* fulfil the prophecy, but his teaching in parables *turned out* to fulfil it.
