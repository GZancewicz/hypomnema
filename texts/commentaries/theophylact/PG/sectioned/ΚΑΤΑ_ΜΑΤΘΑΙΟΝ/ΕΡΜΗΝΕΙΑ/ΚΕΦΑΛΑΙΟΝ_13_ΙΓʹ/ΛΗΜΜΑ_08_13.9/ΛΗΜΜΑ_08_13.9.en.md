# Matthew 13 (ΙΓʹ) — Lemma 8 · Matt. 13:9

> **…to hear, let him hear. (Matt. 13:9)**[^1]

He makes clear that these things must be understood spiritually by those who have acquired the spiritual ears. But many, on the one hand, have ears, but not for the hearing; therefore, on this account, he added the *words*, "He who has ears for the hearing, let that one hear." And


[^1]: This lemma opens mid-sentence: the previous lemma (7) broke off at «Ὁ ἔχων ὦτα» ("He who has ears"), and the words carried here — «ἀκούειν, ἀκουέτω» ("to hear, let him hear") — complete the saying of Matt. 13:9. The comment itself then repeats the full clause with the emphatic *εἰς τὸ ἀκούειν* ("for the hearing"), which Theophylact reads as marking the *spiritual* ears. The closing «Καὶ» ("And") runs on into lemma 9.
