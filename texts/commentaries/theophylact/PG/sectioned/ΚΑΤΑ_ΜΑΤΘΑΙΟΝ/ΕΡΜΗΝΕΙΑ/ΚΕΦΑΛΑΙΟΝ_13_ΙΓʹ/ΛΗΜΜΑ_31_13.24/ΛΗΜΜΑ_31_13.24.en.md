# Matthew 13 (ΙΓʹ) — Lemma 31 · Matt. 13:48

> **…was filled, having drawn *it* up onto the shore, and having sat down, they gathered the good into vessels, but the rotten they cast outside; (Matt. 13:48)**[^1]

*This lemma carries only scripture words: it continues the sentence begun in the previous lemma («the kingdom of heaven is like unto a net cast into the sea and gathering of every kind, which, when…») and runs on into what follows. Theophylact gives no exposition here; his comment on the whole dragnet parable appears at Lemma 33.*

[^1]: The lemma continues Matt 13:48 mid-sentence. Greek *ἐπληρώθη, ἀναβιβάσαντες ἐπὶ τὸν αἰγιαλόν, καὶ καθίσαντες, συνέλεξαν τὰ καλὰ εἰς ἀγγεῖα, τὰ δὲ σαπρὰ ἔξω ἔβαλον*. *ἀναβιβάσαντες* ("having brought/drawn up") has no expressed object; the supplied "*it*" (the net) is italicized. *τὰ καλά / τὰ σαπρά* are bare neuter substantives, "the good [ones] / the rotten [ones]," rendered "the good / the rotten" without importing "fish."
