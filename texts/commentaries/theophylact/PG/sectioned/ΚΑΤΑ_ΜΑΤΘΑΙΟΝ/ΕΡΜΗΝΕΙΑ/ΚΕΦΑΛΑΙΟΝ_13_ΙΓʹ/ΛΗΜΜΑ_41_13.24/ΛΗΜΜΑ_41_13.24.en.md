# Matthew 13 (ΙΓʹ) — Lemma 41 · Matt. 13:57

> **But Jesus said to them: A prophet is not without honor,[^1] except in his native place and in his *own* house. And… not— (Matt. 13:57)**

*This section carries only the scripture lemma; Theophylact's exposition of this verse is given below, at Lemma 42.*[^2]

[^1]: Greek *ἄτιμος* ("without honor, unhonored, held in no esteem"), the privative of *τιμή* ("honor"). The saying turns on this word, and Theophylact plays on it at Lemma 42 (the Jews *dishonor* him; we the strangers *honor* him).
[^2]: The source .txt for this section contains no exposition — only Theophylact's running quotation of the Gospel, breaking off at *Καὶ οὐκ* ("And… not—"), which is completed in the next section: «he did not do there many mighty works, because of their unbelief» (Matt. 13:58). His comment on this verse is written out at Lemma 42.
