# Matthew 13 (ΙΓʹ) — Lemma 13 · Matt. 13:18-19

> **You therefore, hear the parable of the sower. Of everyone hearing the word of the kingdom and not understanding *it*, there comes the evil one and snatches away that which has been sown in his heart; this is he who was sown by the wayside. (Matt. 13:18-19)**

He urges us on toward the understanding of the things spoken by those who teach, so that we too may not be like those *sown* by the wayside. And perhaps,[^1] since Christ is the way, those by the wayside are those outside of Christ, who are not in the way, but beside the way.

[^1]: Greek *μήποτε δέ*. Here *μήποτε* is not the "lest" of warning but the tentative "perhaps / it may be that," introducing a proposed interpretation. The comment turns on a play on *ὁδός* ("way, road"): those "beside the way" (*παρὰ τὴν ὁδόν*) are read as those "beside/outside" Christ, who is himself "the way" (cf. John 14:6). The closing *παρεκτὸς τῆς ὁδοῦ* ("beside the way / outside the way") restates *παρὰ τὴν ὁδόν* with a stronger preposition of exclusion.
