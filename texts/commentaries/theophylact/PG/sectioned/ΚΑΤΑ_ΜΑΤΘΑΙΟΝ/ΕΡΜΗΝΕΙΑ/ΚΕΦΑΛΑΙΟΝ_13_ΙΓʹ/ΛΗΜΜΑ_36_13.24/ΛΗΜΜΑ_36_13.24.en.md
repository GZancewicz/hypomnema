# Matthew 13 (ΙΓʹ) — Lemma 36 · Matt. 13:53

> **And it came to pass, when Jesus finished these parables, he departed thence. And (Matt. 13:53)**[^1]

*This lemma carries only scripture words; it breaks off at "And," to be completed in the next lemma («…having come into his own country, he taught them in their synagogue»), where Theophylact's exposition begins.*

[^1]: Greek *Καὶ ἐγένετο ὅτε ἐτέλεσεν ὁ Ἰησοῦς τὰς παραβολὰς ταύτας, μετῆρεν ἐκεῖθεν. Καί*. *ἐγένετο* rendered "it came to pass" (the Septuagintal narrative formula Theophylact's text carries). *μετῆρεν* ("he departed / removed himself"). The lemma breaks off at *Καί* ("And").
