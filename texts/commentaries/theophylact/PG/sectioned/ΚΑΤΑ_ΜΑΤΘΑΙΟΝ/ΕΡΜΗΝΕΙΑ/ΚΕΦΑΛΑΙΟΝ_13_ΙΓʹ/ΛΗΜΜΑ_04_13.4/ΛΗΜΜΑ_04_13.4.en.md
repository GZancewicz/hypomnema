# Matthew 13 (ΙΓʹ) — Lemma 4 · Matt. 13:4-5

> **And in his sowing, some fell beside the way; and the birds of the heaven came, and devoured them. But others fell upon the rocky places, where it had not much earth, and immediately sprang up on account of not having depth of earth;[^1]**

[^1]: The lemma is quotation only, breaking off in mid-sentence (Matt. 13:5) at *διὰ τὸ μὴ ἔχειν βάθος γῆς* ("on account of not having depth of earth;"). The sentence is completed at the head of the next lemma («ἡλίου δὲ ἀνατείλαντος, ἐκαυματίσθη…»), and the whole is then interpreted there.
