# Matthew 13 (ΙΓʹ) — Lemma 5 · Matt. 13:6

> **and the sun having risen, it was scorched, and on account of not having root, it was withered. (Matt. 13:6)**[^1]

Those beside the way are the careless[^2] and sluggish, who do not at all receive the word. For their reasoning is a way trodden down and hard, and not plowed at all; from these, therefore, the birds of the heaven snatch away the word — that is, the spirits of the air, that is to say, the demons. But those upon the rocky places are the ones who heard, on the one hand, but on account of their weakness did not stand against[^3] temptations or afflictions, but betrayed their own salvation; for by the sun having risen, understand the temptations, because the temptations show men forth, and make manifest, as the sun *does*, the things *that are* hidden.

[^1]: The lemma opens with the completion of the sentence begun at the end of the previous lemma (Matt. 13:5-6): *ἡλίου δὲ ἀνατείλαντος, ἐκαυματίσθη, καὶ διὰ τὸ μὴ ἔχειν ῥίζαν, ἐξηράνθη*.
[^2]: Greek *ῥᾴθυμοι* — "easy-tempered," hence "heedless, careless, slothful"; paired with *ὀκνηροὶ* ("hesitant, sluggish, shrinking from effort").
[^3]: Greek *μὴ ἀντιβάντες* — "not having stood against, not having withstood"; the aorist participle kept as a participle.
