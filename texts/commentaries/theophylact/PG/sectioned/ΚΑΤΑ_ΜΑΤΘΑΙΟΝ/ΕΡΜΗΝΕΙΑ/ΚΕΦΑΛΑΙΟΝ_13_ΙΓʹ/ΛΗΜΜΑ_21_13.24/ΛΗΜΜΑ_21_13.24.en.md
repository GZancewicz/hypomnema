# Matthew 13 (ΙΓʹ) — Lemma 21 · Matt. 13:37-38

> **But he answering said to them: The one sowing the good seed is the Son of man; and [the field is the world; and the good seed,][?][^1] these are the sons of the kingdom. But the tares are the sons of the evil one; and the … (Matt. 13:37-38)**

*This lemma carries the Gospel text only; Theophylact adds no exposition here but quotes the interpretation straight through to the close (see Lemma 25), where he remarks that "what needed to be said has been said above" — referring back to his comment on the parable of the tares in the earlier lemmata.*

[^1]: The OCR here is collapsed: the source reads «ὁ Υἱὸς τοῦ ἀνθρώπου· ὁ δὲ εἰειν οἷ υἱοὶ τῇς βασιλείας», where the clause "and the field is the world; and the good seed, these" (*ὁ δὲ ἀγρός ἐστιν ὁ κόσμος· τὸ δὲ καλὸν σπέρμα, οὗτοί*, Matt. 13:38a) has dropped out, leaving only "ὁ δὲ", and *εἰειν* = *εἰσιν* ("are"). The missing words are securely recoverable from the Gospel lemma and are supplied here in brackets. The quotation breaks off at *ὁ δὲ* ("and the …"), completed in the next lemma.
