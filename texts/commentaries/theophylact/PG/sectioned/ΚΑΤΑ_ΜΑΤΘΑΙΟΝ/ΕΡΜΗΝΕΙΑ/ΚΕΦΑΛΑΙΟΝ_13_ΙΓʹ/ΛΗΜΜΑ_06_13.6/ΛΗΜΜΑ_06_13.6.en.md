# Matthew 13 (ΙΓʹ) — Lemma 6 · Matt. 13:7[^1]

> **But others fell upon the thorns, and the thorns came up, and choked them. (Matt. 13:7)**

These are those who through the cares[^2] choke the word; for even if the rich man seem to do some good deed, yet nevertheless this deed does not increase, nor does it advance, being hindered by the anxieties.[^3]

[^1]: The folder-name assigns this lemma to 13.6, but its scripture quotation — the thorns springing up and choking the seed — is Matt. 13:7; the reference is corrected accordingly.
[^2]: Greek *φροντίδων* ("cares, preoccupations"); in the closing clause the near-synonym *μεριμνῶν* ("anxieties, worries," cf. Matt. 13:22) is used. Rendered "cares" / "anxieties" to keep the two distinct.
[^3]: At this point the OCR carries an intrusive fragment «ΔΑΡΓΓ ΣΙ.», a garbled running page-header / column marker from the Migne column, not part of Theophylact's text; it is omitted from the translation.
