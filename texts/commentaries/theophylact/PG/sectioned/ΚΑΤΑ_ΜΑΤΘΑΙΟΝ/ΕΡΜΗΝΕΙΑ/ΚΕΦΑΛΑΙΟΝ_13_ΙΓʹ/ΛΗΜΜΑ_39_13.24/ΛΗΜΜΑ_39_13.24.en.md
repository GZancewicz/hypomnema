# Matthew 13 (ΙΓʹ) — Lemma 39 · Matt. 13:55

> **…this *man* the son of the carpenter? Is not his mother called Mariam,[^1] and his brothers James, and Joses,[^2] and Simon, and Judas? And the— (Matt. 13:55)**

*This section carries only the scripture lemma; Theophylact's exposition of these verses is given at Lemma 40.*[^3]

[^1]: Greek *Μαριάμ*, the Semitic form of the name, retained here as "Mariam" since Theophylact writes it thus (contrast the Hellenized *Μαρία*, "Mary," which he uses for the daughter of Clopas at Lemma 40).
[^2]: Greek *Ἰωσῆς* ("Joses"). The received text of Matt. 13:55 more commonly reads *Ἰωσήφ* ("Joseph"); Theophylact's lemma has "Joses," a genuine textual variant, kept as it stands per the rule of following his text.
[^3]: The source .txt for this section contains no exposition — only Theophylact's running quotation of the Gospel. The lemma breaks off at *Καὶ αἱ* ("And the—"), completed in the next section: «…sisters, are they not all with us?» (Matt. 13:56). His comment on these verses is written out at Lemma 40.
