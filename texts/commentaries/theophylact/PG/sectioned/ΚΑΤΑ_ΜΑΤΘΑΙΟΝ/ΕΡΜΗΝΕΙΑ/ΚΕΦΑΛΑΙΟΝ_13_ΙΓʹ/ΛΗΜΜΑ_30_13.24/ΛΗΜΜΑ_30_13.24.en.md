# Matthew 13 (ΙΓʹ) — Lemma 30 · Matt. 13:47

> **Again, the kingdom of the heavens is like unto a net cast into the sea and gathering of every kind,[^1] which, when…**[^2]

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation, which breaks off mid-sentence at* «ἣν ὅτε» *("which, when"). The sentence is completed, and the exposition given, in the following lemma (Matt. 13:48).*

[^1]: Greek *ἐκ παντὸς γένους συναγαγούσῃ* ("gathering of every kind"); *γένους* ("kind/race") is OCR-corrupted in the source as *γάνους*, but is secure from the text of Matt. 13:47.
[^2]: The sentence continues into the next lemma, «…ἐπληρώθη ἀναβιβάσαντες ἐπὶ τὸν αἰγιαλόν…» ("…was filled, they drew *it* up onto the shore…"). No commentary of Theophylact's own accompanies this segment.
