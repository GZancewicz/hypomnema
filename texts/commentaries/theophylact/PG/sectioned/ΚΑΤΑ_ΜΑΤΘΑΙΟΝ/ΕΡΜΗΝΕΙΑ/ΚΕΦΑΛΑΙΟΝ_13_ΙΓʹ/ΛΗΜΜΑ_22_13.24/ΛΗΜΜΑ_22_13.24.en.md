# Matthew 13 (ΙΓʹ) — Lemma 22 · Matt. 13:39

> **… *the* enemy who sowed them is the devil; and the harvest is the consummation of the age;[^1] and the reapers are the angels. (Matt. 13:39)**

*This lemma carries the Gospel text only, continuing the sentence broken off in Lemma 21; Theophylact adds no exposition here — see Lemma 25.*

[^1]: Greek *ἡ συντέλεια τοῦ αἰῶνος* — "the consummation / completion / end of the age." Rendered "consummation of the age" throughout this passage for consistency.
