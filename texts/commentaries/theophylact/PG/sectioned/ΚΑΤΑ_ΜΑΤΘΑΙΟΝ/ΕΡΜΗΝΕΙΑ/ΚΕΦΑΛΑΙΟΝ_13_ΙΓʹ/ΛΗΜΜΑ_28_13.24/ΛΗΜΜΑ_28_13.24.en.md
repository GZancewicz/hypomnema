# Matthew 13 (ΙΓʹ) — Lemma 28 · Matt. 13:45

> **Again, the kingdom of the heavens is like unto a man, a merchant, seeking good pearls, who…**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation, which breaks off mid-sentence at* «ὅς» *("who"). The clause is completed, and the exposition given, in the following lemma (Matt. 13:46).*

[^1]: The quotation is Matt. 13:45; the sentence continues into the next lemma, «εὑρὼν ἕνα πολύτιμον μαργαρίτην…» ("having found one pearl of great price…"). No commentary of Theophylact's own accompanies this segment.
