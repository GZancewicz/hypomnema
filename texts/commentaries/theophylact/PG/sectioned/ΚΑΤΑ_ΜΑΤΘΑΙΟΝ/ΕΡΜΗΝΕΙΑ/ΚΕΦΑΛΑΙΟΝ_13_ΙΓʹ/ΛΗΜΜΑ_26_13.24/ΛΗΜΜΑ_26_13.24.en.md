# Matthew 13 (ΙΓʹ) — Lemma 26 · Matt. 13:43

> **Then the righteous shall shine forth as the sun in the kingdom of their Father; he who has ears to hear, let him hear. (Matt. 13:43)**

Since the sun seems to us the most brilliant of all the stars, for this reason he compares the brightness of the righteous to the sun — since indeed they will shine even beyond the sun. But perhaps, since Christ is *the* Sun of righteousness,[^1] then the righteous shall shine forth as Christ; for they too shall be gods.[^2]

[^1]: Greek *Ἥλιος δικαιοσύνης* ("Sun of righteousness"), an allusion to Mal. 4:2 (LXX 3:20); Theophylact turns on the fact that the same word *ἥλιος* ("sun") names both the physical sun of the parable and Christ.
[^2]: Greek *ἔσονται γὰρ καὶ αὐτοὶ θεοί* ("for they too shall be gods") — the doctrine of *θέωσις* (deification), that the righteous are made gods by grace through participation in Christ. Rendered literally; the plain word is "gods."
