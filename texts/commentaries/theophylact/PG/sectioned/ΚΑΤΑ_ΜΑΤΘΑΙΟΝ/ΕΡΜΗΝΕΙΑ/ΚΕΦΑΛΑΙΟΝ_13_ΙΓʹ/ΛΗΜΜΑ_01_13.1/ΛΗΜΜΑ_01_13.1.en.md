# Matthew 13 (ΙΓʹ) — Lemma 1 · Matt. 13:1-2

> **On that[^1] day Jesus, having gone out from the house, was sitting beside the sea. And great crowds were gathered together to him, so that he, having embarked into the boat, sat down, and all the crowd stood upon the shore. (Matt. 13:1-2)**

He sat in the boat, so that he might set all[^2] the hearers face to face,[^3] and *that* all might hear. And indeed from the sea he fishes those on the land.

[^1]: The lemma as sectioned begins mid-clause at *ἡμέρᾳ ἐκείνῃ* ("on that day"); the opening «Ἐν τῇ» ("On the") is understood from Matt. 13:1 and supplied here without italics as part of the restored quotation.
[^2]: OCR *φάντας*, securely = *πάντας* ("all"); the reconstruction is certain, noted for consistency with the other flagged corruptions.
[^3]: Greek *κατὰ πρόσωπον στήσῃ* — literally "might set *them* according to *the* face," i.e. positioned facing him. Rendered "face to face" for the plain sense.
