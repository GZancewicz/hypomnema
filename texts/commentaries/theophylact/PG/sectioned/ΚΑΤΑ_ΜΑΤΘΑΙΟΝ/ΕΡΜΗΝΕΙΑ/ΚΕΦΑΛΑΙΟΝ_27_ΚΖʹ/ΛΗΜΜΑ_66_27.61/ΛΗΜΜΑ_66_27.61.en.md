# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 66 · Matt. 27:62

> **But on the next day, which is after the Preparation, the chief priests and the Pharisees were gathered together to Pilate,[^1] (Matt. 27:62)**

This lemma carries only the Gospel text and breaks off before Theophylact's exposition; the sentence continues into the next lemma («λέγοντες· Κύριε, ἐμνήσθημεν…», "saying: Lord, we have remembered…," Matt. 27:63).[^2]

[^1]: The folder metadata labels this lemma 27.61, but the Greek it quotes — «Τῇ δὲ ἐπαύριον, ἥτις ἐστὶ μετὰ τὴν Παρασκευήν, συνήχθησαν οἱ ἀρχιερεῖς καὶ οἱ Φαρισαῖοι πρὸς Πιλᾶτον» — is **Matt. 27:62**; the header and reference are corrected accordingly. *Παρασκευή* ("Preparation") is the Friday, the day of preparation for the Sabbath; so "the next day" is the Sabbath itself.
[^2]: The lemma ends at «πρὸς Πιλᾶτον,» ("to Pilate,") with a trailing comma; the participial phrase «λέγοντες…» that governs the chief priests' request opens the following lemma.
