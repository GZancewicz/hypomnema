# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 12 · Matt. 27:12

> **his being accused[^1] by the chief priests and the elders, he answered nothing. Then[^2]**

This lemma carries only the running Gospel text; Theophylact appends no exposition here. His comment on this section (Matt. 27:11–14) follows once the quotation is completed at Lemma 14.

[^1]: Greek *ἐν τῷ κατηγορεῖσθαι αὐτόν*, an articular infinitive completing the "And in the…" left hanging at the end of the previous lemma: "And while he was being accused by the chief priests and the elders, he answered nothing."
[^2]: The lemma breaks off at *Τότε* ("Then"), which is continued in the next lemma (*λέγει αὐτῷ ὁ Πιλάτος…*, "Pilate says to him…").
