# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 58 · Matt. 27:54

> **«And the centurion and those with him keeping guard over Jesus, having seen the earthquake and the things that happened, were exceedingly afraid, saying: Truly this was *the* Son of God.» And there were there…**[^1]

[^1]: This lemma is scripture quotation only, carrying no exposition; Theophylact's comment on it comes at the next-but-one lemma, after the list of the women is completed. The quotation is Matt. 27:54 and runs on into 27:55 (*Ἦσαν δὲ ἐκεῖ*, "And there were there…"), breaking off mid-sentence; the words "many women, watching from afar…" continue in the next lemma. Greek *ἀληθῶς Θεοῦ Υἱὸς ἦν οὗτος* is anarthrous before *Υἱός*, hence the supplied "*the*." (Source OCR *λωοβήθησαν* = *ἐφοβήθησαν*, "were afraid"; *Ἔσαν* = *Ἦσαν*, "there were.")
