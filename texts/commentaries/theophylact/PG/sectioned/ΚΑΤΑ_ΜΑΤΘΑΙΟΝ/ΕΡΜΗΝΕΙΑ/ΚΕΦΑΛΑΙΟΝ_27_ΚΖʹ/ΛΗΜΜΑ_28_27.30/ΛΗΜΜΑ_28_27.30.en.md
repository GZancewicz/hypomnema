# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 28 · Matt. 27:30

> **having spat upon him, they took the reed and kept striking *him* upon his head.[^1]**

Here was being fulfilled that *saying* of David:[^2]

[^1]: Greek *ἐμπτύσαντες εἰς αὐτὸν* ("having spat into/upon him") and *ἔτυπτον εἰς τὴν κεφαλὴν αὐτοῦ*, with *ἔτυπτον* in the imperfect — "they were striking / kept striking upon his head," an iterative blow rather than a single one.
[^2]: Greek *Ἐνταῦθα ἐπληροῦτο τὸ τοῦ Δαυΐδ* — "here was being fulfilled the *thing* of David," with *ἐπληροῦτο* in the imperfect ("was being fulfilled") and the neuter article *τὸ* standing for "the saying / word." The citation itself follows at the head of the next lemma, «Ὄνειδος ἄφρονι ἔδωκάς με» (Ps. 38:9 LXX / Ps. 39:8 Heb.).
