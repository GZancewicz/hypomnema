# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 39 · Matt. 27:40

> **…saying: You who destroy the temple and in three days build *it*,[^1] save yourself; if you are Son of God, come down from the cross.** *(Matt. 27:40)*

This lemma carries only Theophylact's scripture quotation, completing the taunt of the passers-by begun in Lemmata 37–38. He defers his exposition of the whole crucifixion passage (Matt. 27:38–44) to Lemma 42.

[^1]: Greek *Ὁ καταλύων τὸν ναὸν καὶ ἐν τρισὶν ἡμέραις οἰκοδομῶν* — two articular present participles in direct address, "the one *who* destroys the temple and *who* in three days builds *it*." Rendered as second-person address ("You who destroy… build…") to match the vocative force. Both participles are objectless in the second member; the "*it*" (the temple) is supplied.
