# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 15 · Matt. 27:15–17

> **Now at *the* feast the governor was accustomed to release to the crowd one prisoner, whom they wished. And they had at that time a notable[^1] prisoner, called Barabbas. When therefore they were gathered together, Pilate said to them: Whom do you wish *that* I release to you, Barabbas, or Jesus who is called Christ? For he knew that because of**[^2]

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation; it has no exposition of its own.*

[^1]: Greek *ἐπίσημον* (OCR *Ἀπίσημον*) — "notable, marked, conspicuous," and so also "notorious." Rendered "notable" to keep both the neutral and the pejorative sense open.
[^2]: The quotation breaks off at *διὰ* ("because of"), completed in the next lemma (ΛΗΜΜΑ_16): *φθόνον παρέδωκαν αὐτόν* — "because of **envy** they had delivered him up." The OCR here corrupts several proper forms — *κὺτῶν* = *αὐτῶν*, *δμῖν* = *ὑμῖν*, *Ἀαραββᾶν* = *Βαραββᾶν*, *Χριστόνς* = *Χριστόν* — all securely recovered.
