# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 53 · Matt. 27:51

> **…veil of the temple was torn into two, from top[^1] to bottom, and the earth was shaken, and the rocks were split, and the…[^2] (Matt. 27:51)**

*This lemma carries only the Gospel words, continuing the sentence from Lemma 52; Theophylact gives no exposition here.*[^2]

[^1]: Greek *ἀπὸ ἄνωθεν ἕως κάτω* — literally "from from-above until below," a pleonasm (*ἀπό* + *ἄνωθεν*); rendered "from top to bottom."
[^2]: This is a continuation of the lemma-quotation begun at Lemma 52 («καὶ ἰδοὺ τὸ καταπέτασμα…»). It breaks off at «καὶ τὰ» ("and the…"), continued in Lemma 54 (…tombs were opened…). The exposition begins only at Lemma 55.
