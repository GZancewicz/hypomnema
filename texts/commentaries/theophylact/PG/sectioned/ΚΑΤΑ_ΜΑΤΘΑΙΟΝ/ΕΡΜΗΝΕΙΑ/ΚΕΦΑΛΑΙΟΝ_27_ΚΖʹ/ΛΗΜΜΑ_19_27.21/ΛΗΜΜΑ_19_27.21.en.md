# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 19 · Matt. 27:21

> **And answering, the governor said to them: "Whom of the two do you wish *that* I should release to you?" But they said: "Barabbas." (Matt. 27:21)**[^1]

[^1]: This lemma carries only the scripture quotation, with no exposition of its own; Theophylact quotes the passion narrative in short fragments and gathers his comment together at the release of Barabbas (Lemma 23). "Whom of the two do you wish" renders *Τίνα θέλετε ἀπὸ τῶν δύο* — literally "Whom of the two do you wish"; the following *ἀπολύσω* ("I should release") is a deliberative subjunctive standing without a conjunction, so the connecting "*that*" is supplied. "Answering" keeps the aorist participle *ἀποκριθεὶς* as a participle.
