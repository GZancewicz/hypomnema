# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 54 · Matt. 27:52

> **…tombs were opened, and many bodies of the fallen-asleep[^1] Saints were raised, and…[^2] (Matt. 27:52)**

*This lemma carries only the Gospel words, continuing the sentence from Lemma 53; Theophylact gives no exposition here.*[^2]

[^1]: Greek *τῶν κεκοιμημένων* — a perfect participle, "of those having fallen asleep," the customary euphemism for the dead (whence "cemetery," *κοιμητήριον*).
[^2]: This continues the lemma-quotation from Lemma 53 («…καὶ τὰ μνημεῖα ἀνεῴχθησαν…»). It breaks off at «καὶ» ("and…"), continued in Lemma 55 («ἐξελθόντες ἐκ τῶν μνημείων μετὰ τὴν ἔγερσιν αὐτοῦ…», "having come out of the tombs after his resurrection…"), where the exposition also begins.
