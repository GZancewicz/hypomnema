# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 8 · Matt. 27:8

> **that field was called, Field of blood, until this day. Then[^1]**

This lemma carries only the running Gospel text; Theophylact appends no exposition here. His comment on the whole section (Matt. 27:7–10) follows once the quotation is completed at Lemma 10.

[^1]: The lemma breaks off at *Τότε* ("Then"), OCR-garbled in the source as «Τότι»; the sentence is continued in the next lemma (*ἐπληρώθη τὸ ῥηθὲν…*, "was fulfilled that which was spoken…").
