# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 40 · Matt. 27:41

> **And likewise also the chief priests, mocking with the scribes and elders and Pharisees, said:…** *(Matt. 27:41)*

This lemma carries only Theophylact's scripture quotation, and it breaks off at the verb of speaking (ἔλεγον·, "said:…"), the mockers' words following in Lemmata 41–42. Theophylact defers his exposition of the whole crucifixion passage (Matt. 27:38–44) to Lemma 42.
