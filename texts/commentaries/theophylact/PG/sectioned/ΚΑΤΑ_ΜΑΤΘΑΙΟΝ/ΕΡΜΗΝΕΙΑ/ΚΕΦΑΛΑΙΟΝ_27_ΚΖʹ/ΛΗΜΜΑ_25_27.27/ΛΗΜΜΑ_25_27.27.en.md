# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 25 · Matt. 27:27

> **Then the soldiers of the governor, having taken Jesus into the praetorium, gathered together unto him the whole band,[^1] and**[^2]

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text; Theophylact does not comment here. His exposition of the mockery scene begins at Lemma 28 (on Matt. 27:30) and runs through the following lemmata.*

[^1]: Greek *σπεῖρα* — a Roman military unit, the "cohort" (nominally some hundreds of men); rendered "band" for the plain sense. The verb *συνήγαγον ἐπ' αὐτὸν* (aorist) is "they gathered together against/unto him": *ἐπί* + accusative here marks direction toward the object of their attention rather than hostility as such, so "unto him."
[^2]: The lemma breaks off at *καὶ* ("and"), the sentence continuing into the next lemma («ἐκδύσαντες αὐτὸν…»).
