# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 9 · Matt. 27:9

> **was fulfilled that which was spoken through Jeremiah[^1] the prophet, saying: And they took[^2] the thirty pieces of silver, the price of him who was priced,[^3] whom they priced from *the* sons of Israel, and**

This lemma carries only the running Gospel text; Theophylact appends no exposition here. His comment on the whole section (Matt. 27:7–10) follows once the quotation is completed at Lemma 10.

[^1]: Greek *διὰ Ἱερεμίου τοῦ προφήτου*. The citation that follows is drawn chiefly from Zechariah (Zech. 11:12–13), yet Matthew's text names Jeremiah; the lemma is rendered as it stands, per the rule of following the text on the page.
[^2]: Greek *ἔλαβον* is ambiguous in form between first-person singular ("I took") and third-person plural ("they took"). Rendered "they took" to agree with the third-person plural *ἔδωκαν* ("they gave") that completes the sentence in the next lemma; note, however, that the same sentence ends "as the Lord appointed **me**" (first person), so the prophetic voice shifts persons.
[^3]: Greek *τὴν τιμὴν τοῦ τετιμημένου* — "the price of the priced/valued one." The participle *τετιμημένου* (from *τιμάω*, "to price, value, honor") is picked up and expounded in Theophylact's comment at Lemma 10; "priced" is kept to preserve the pricing/valuation play, though *τιμάω* also carries "honor."
