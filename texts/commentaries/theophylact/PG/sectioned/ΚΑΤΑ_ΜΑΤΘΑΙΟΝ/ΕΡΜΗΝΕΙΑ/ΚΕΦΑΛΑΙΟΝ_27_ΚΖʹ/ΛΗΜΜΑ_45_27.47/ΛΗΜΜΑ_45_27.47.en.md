# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 45 · Matt. 27:47

> **And some of those standing there, having heard, were saying that "This *man* calls Elijah";[^1] and[^2] (Matt. 27:47)**

[Editorial note: this lemma folder carries only the Gospel text; Theophylact's exposition of these verses falls in the following lemmata.]

[^1]: The bystanders mishear Jesus' cry *Ἠλί* ("my God," Lemma 44) as an invocation of the prophet Elijah, Greek *Ἡλίας* (KJV "Elias"). Rendered "Elijah" per the convention of conventional English biblical names; the pun turns on the near-identity of *Ἠλί* and *Ἡλί-αν*. Greek *φωνεῖ οὗτος* = "this *one* calls / summons."
[^2]: The sentence breaks off at *καί* ("and"); it is completed in the next lemma («straightway one of them, having run…»).
