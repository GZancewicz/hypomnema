# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 4 · Matt. 27:4

> **saying: I have sinned, having betrayed innocent blood. But they said: What *is that* to us? Thou shalt see *to it thyself*; and**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation; the quotation continues in the next lemma, and Theophylact's exposition of this passage follows in Lemma 5.*

[^1]: Matt. 27:4. Greek «λέγων· Ἥμαρτον παραδοὺς αἷμα ἀθῷον· οἱ δὲ εἶπον· Τί πρὸς ἡμᾶς; σὺ ὄψει· καὶ». The source OCR reads *οὐ ὄψει* ("thou shalt not see"), but this is securely *σὺ ὄψει* ("thou — see to it / thou shalt see to it"), the idiomatic brush-off "that is thy concern, not ours"; the sense of the whole passage requires it, and the parallel «ὑμεῖς ὄψεσθε» stands at 27:24. *αἷμα ἀθῷον*, "innocent blood." The lemma breaks off at *καὶ* ("and"); the sentence is completed in the next lemma («ῥίψας τὰ ἀργύρια…»).
