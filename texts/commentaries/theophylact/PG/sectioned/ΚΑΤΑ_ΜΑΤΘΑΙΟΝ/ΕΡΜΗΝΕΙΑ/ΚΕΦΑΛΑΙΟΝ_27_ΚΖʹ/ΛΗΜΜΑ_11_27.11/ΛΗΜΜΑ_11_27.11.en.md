# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 11 · Matt. 27:11

> **But Jesus stood[^1] before the governor, and the governor questioned him, saying: Art thou the king of the Jews? And Jesus said to him: Thou sayest. And in the[^2]**

This lemma carries only the running Gospel text; Theophylact appends no exposition here. His comment on this section (Matt. 27:11–14) follows once the quotation is completed at Lemma 14.

[^1]: Greek *ἔστη* ("stood"), OCR-garbled in the source as «Ιστη».
[^2]: The lemma breaks off at *Καὶ ἐν τῷ* ("And in the…"), the beginning of an articular-infinitive construction ("And while he was being accused…") that is completed in the next lemma (*κατηγορεῖσθαι αὐτόν…*).
