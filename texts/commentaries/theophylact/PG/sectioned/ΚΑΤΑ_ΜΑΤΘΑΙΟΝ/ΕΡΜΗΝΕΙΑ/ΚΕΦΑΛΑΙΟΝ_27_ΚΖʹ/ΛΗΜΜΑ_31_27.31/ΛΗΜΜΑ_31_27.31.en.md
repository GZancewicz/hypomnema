# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 31 · Matt. 27:31

> **And when they had mocked him, they stripped him *of* the cloak,[^1] and put on him his own garments, and led him away to crucify *him*.**[^2]

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text; Theophylact adds no exposition here. The scripture quotation runs on without a break into the following lemma (27:32).*

[^1]: Greek *τὴν χλαμύδα* — the *chlamys*, a short military/officer's cloak; here the mock-royal cloak thrown over Jesus in vv. 28–31. Rendered "cloak." The verb *ἐξέδυσαν αὐτὸν τὴν χλαμύδα* takes a double accusative ("stripped him *the* cloak"); the supplied "*of*" is italicized.
[^2]: The articular infinitive *εἰς τὸ σταυρῶσαι* ("to crucify") has no expressed object; the supplied "*him*" is italicized.
