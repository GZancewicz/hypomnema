# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 59 · Matt. 27:55

> **…many women, watching from afar, who had followed Jesus from Galilee, ministering to him, among whom was…**[^1]

[^1]: This lemma too is scripture quotation only, with no exposition; it continues the sentence begun in the previous lemma (Matt. 27:55) and breaks off at *ἐν αἷς ἦν* ("among whom was…"), which the next lemma completes with the names of the women. Greek *διακονοῦσαι αὐτῷ* ("ministering to him"), the same verb (*διακονέω*) from which "deacon" derives. (Source OCR *μακρόεν* = *μακρόθεν*, "from afar"; *θεωοοῦσαι* = *θεωροῦσαι*, "watching"; *αἴτινες* = *αἵτινες*, "who.")
