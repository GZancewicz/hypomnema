# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 6 · Matt. 27:6

> **But the chief priests, having taken the pieces of silver, said: It is not lawful to cast them into the korbanas,[^1] since it is *the* price of blood.**[^2]

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation; Theophylact's exposition of the passage follows in the next lemma.*

[^1]: Greek *κορβανᾶν* (accusative of *κορβανᾶς*), a Semitic loanword (Heb. *qorbān*, "offering, that which is dedicated"; cf. Mark 7:11) here meaning the sacred temple treasury into which dedicated money was cast. Left transliterated rather than flattened to "treasury," since it is a technical term for the consecrated fund; "treasury" is its sense.
[^2]: Matt. 27:6. Greek «Οἱ δὲ ἀρχιερεῖς λαβόντες τὰ ἀργύρια, εἶπον· Οὐκ ἔξεστι βαλεῖν αὐτὰ εἰς τὸν κορβανᾶν, ἐπεὶ τιμὴ αἵματός ἐστι». OCR *ἀρνιερεῖς* is *ἀρχιερεῖς* ("chief priests"). *τιμὴ αἵματος*, "*the* price of blood" (anarthrous; the supplied "*the*" is italicized). The folder name labels this lemma 27.5, but the Greek is Matt. 27:6; the header is corrected accordingly.
