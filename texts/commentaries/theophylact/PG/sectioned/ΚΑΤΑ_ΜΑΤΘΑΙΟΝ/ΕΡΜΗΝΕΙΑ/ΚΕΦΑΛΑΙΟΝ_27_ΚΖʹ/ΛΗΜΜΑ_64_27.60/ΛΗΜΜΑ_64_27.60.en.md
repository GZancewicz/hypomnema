# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 64 · Matt. 27:60

> **laid it in his new tomb, which he had hewn in the rock. And having rolled a great stone to the door of the tomb, he went away; and there was there[^1] (Matt. 27:60)**

This lemma carries only the Gospel text; the exposition is given collectively at Lemma 65 below.[^2]

[^1]: The lemma completes the sentence begun at Lemma 62 and trails off at «ἦν δὲ ἐκεῖ» ("and there was there"), which is picked up by the next lemma's «Μαρία ἡ Μαγδαληνὴ…» (Matt. 27:61). "Rock" is OCR-garbled as «πέτρ.»; securely = **«πέτρᾳ»** (*πέτρα*, rock/bedrock).
[^2]: See the note at Lemma 61: Theophylact expounds the whole burial block (27:57–61) at Lemma 65.
