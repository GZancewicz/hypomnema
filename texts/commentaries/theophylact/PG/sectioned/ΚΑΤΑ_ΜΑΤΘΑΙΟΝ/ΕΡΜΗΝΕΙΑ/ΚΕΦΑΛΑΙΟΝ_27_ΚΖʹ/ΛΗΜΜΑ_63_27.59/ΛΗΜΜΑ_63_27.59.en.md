# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 63 · Matt. 27:59

> **Joseph, having taken the body, wrapped it in a clean linen cloth, and[^1] (Matt. 27:59)**

This lemma carries only the Gospel text; the exposition is given collectively at Lemma 65 below.[^2]

[^1]: The lemma is a continuation of the sentence begun in Lemma 62 and breaks off again at *καὶ* ("and"), running on into Lemma 64. The word for the linen cloth is OCR-garbled as «σινὸόνι»; securely = **«σινδόνι»** (*σινδών*, a fine linen sheet/shroud).
[^2]: See the note at Lemma 61: Theophylact expounds the whole burial block (27:57–61) at Lemma 65.
