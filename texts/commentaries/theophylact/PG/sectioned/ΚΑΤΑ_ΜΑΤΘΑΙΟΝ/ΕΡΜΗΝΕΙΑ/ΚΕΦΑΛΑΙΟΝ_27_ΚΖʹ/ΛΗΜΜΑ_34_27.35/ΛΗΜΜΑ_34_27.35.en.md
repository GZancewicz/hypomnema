# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 34 · Matt. 27:35

> **But having crucified him, they divided his garments, casting a lot; that there might be fulfilled that which was spoken by the prophet: "They divided my garments among themselves, and upon my clothing they cast a lot."**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text; Theophylact adds no exposition here. He remarks on the soldiers' dividing of the garments in the exposition attached to Lemma 36 (27:37). The Gospel quotation continues into the next lemma with "And."*

[^1]: The cited prophecy is Ps. 22:18 (LXX 21:19), *διεμερίσαντο τὰ ἱμάτιά μου ἑαυτοῖς, καὶ ἐπὶ τὸν ἱματισμόν μου ἔβαλον κλῆρον*. Rendered from Theophylact's Greek: *τὰ ἱμάτια* ("the garments") and *ὁ ἱματισμός* ("the clothing / raiment") are two distinct words, kept distinct here.
