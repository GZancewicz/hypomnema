# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 62 · Matt. 27:58

> **This *man*, having come to Pilate, asked for the body of Jesus. Then Pilate commanded the body to be given back; and[^1] (Matt. 27:58)**

This lemma carries only the Gospel text; the exposition is given collectively at Lemma 65 below.[^2]

[^1]: The lemma breaks off at *καὶ* ("and") and runs directly into Lemma 63 («λαβὼν τὸ σῶμα ὁ Ἰωσὴφ…», "Joseph, having taken the body…"): 27:58–60 form a single continuous sentence in the source. The name is OCR-garbled as «Λιλατος»; securely = **«Πιλᾶτος»** (Pilate).
[^2]: See the note at Lemma 61: Theophylact expounds the whole burial block (27:57–61) at Lemma 65.
