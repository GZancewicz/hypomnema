# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 67 · Matt. 27:63

> **saying: Lord, we remembered[^1] that that deceiver[^2] said, while yet living: After three days I rise.[^3]**

This lemma carries only the Gospel text: it continues the citation begun in the previous lemma («On the next day, which is after the Preparation, the chief priests and the Pharisees were gathered together unto Pilate,» Matt. 27:62). It supplies the participle "saying" (λέγοντες) that governs the chief priests' and Pharisees' words. Theophylact adds no exposition here; his comment on the whole pericope (Matt. 27:62–66) comes at Lemma 70.

[^1]: Greek *ἐμνήσθημεν*, aorist passive-deponent of *μιμνήσκομαι* ("we remembered / we have recalled"). Rendered as a simple past; the perfect sense ("we have remembered / we now recall") is also defensible.
[^2]: Greek *ἐκεῖνος ὁ πλάνος* ("that deceiver / that impostor"). The OCR prints the article as a stray «Ἀ» at the line-break («ἐκεῖνος / Ἀ πλάνος»); it is securely *ὁ*. The noun *πλάνος* ("deceiver, one who leads astray") is kept consistent with *πλάνη* ("deception / error") below at Lemma 68 and *πλάνον* ("a deceiver") at Lemma 72.
[^3]: Greek *μετὰ τρεῖς ἡμέρας ἐγείρομαι*. The verb *ἐγείρομαι* is present middle/passive: "I rise" (intransitive middle) or "I am raised" (passive). The vividness of the present-for-future ("after three days I rise") is retained; the passive alternative "I am raised" is theologically weightier but grammatically indistinct here.
