# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 13 · Matt. 27:13

> **Pilate says to him: Do you not hear how many things they testify against you? And not**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation, continuing from the previous lemma; it has no exposition of its own.*[^2]

[^1]: The verse opens with "Then" (*Τότε*), which stands at the close of the preceding lemma (ΛΗΜΜΑ_12), so the words carried here begin at *λέγει αὐτῷ ὁ Πιλάτος*. Greek *πόσα σου καταμαρτυροῦσι* — "how many things they testify against you"; *καταμαρτυρέω* is to bear witness *against* someone, hence "testify against."
[^2]: The quotation breaks off at *Καὶ οὐκ* ("And not"), which is completed in the next lemma (ΛΗΜΜΑ_14): *ἀπεκρίθη αὐτῷ πρὸς οὐδὲ ἓν ῥῆμα* — "he answered him not even one word."
