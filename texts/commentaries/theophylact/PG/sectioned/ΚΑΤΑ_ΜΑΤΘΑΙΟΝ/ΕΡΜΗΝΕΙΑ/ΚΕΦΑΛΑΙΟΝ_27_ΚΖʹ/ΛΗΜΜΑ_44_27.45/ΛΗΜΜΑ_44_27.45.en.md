# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 44 · Matt. 27:45-46

> **Now from *the* sixth hour darkness came over all the earth[^1] until *the* ninth hour; and about the ninth hour, Jesus cried out with a loud voice, saying: Eli, Eli, lama sabachthani[^2] — that is: My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me?[^3] (Matt. 27:45-46)**

[Editorial note: this lemma folder carries only the Gospel text quoted above; Theophylact's exposition of these verses is distributed across the following lemmata (47-49).]

[^1]: Greek *ἐπὶ πᾶσαν τὴν γῆν*: *γῆ* is "earth" or "land." Rendered "earth" because Theophylact reads the darkness as *κοσμικόν* ("cosmic, worldwide"), not localized (see Lemma 47).
[^2]: Matthew's form *Ἠλί, Ἠλί* (the second here OCR'd *Ἡλὶ*) transliterates the Hebrew ʾēlī ("my God"); it is this form that the bystanders mishear as a call to Elijah (*Ἡλίας*), in Lemma 45.
[^3]: Greek *ἵνα τί με ἐγκατέλιπες* — *ἵνα τί* = "to what end / for what purpose / why"; a citation of Ps. 21(22):1. The bracketed verse ref is a locator only.
