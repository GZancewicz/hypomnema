# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 50 · Matt. 27:46-48

> **"You forsook me,"[^1]**

he says — instead of, "My kindred, my people: for what reason did you abandon *them*, so as to do such an evil against itself?" But those of the crowd, being rustic and unlearned in the prophetic *writings*,[^2] not having understood the utterance, thought that he was calling Elijah; for not all the Jews knew the prophetic *matters*, just as perhaps not even now *do* all Christians *know* the things of the Gospels. And they were giving him vinegar[^3] to drink, so that he might die more quickly before Elijah came *to* help him. Whence the rest say:[^4]

[^1]: Head-quote «Ἐγκατέλιπές με» = **Matt. 27:46** (the folder-name reads 27.49). This continues the persona-reading begun in Lemma 49: Christ, speaking in the character of the Jewish people, addresses the Father. The gloss «ἀντὶ τοῦ» = "instead of" (lit. "in place of the"), introducing Theophylact's paraphrase.
[^2]: Greek *ἀμαθεῖς τῶν προφητικῶν* — "unlearned in the prophetic [things]"; the noun is elided, so *writings* is supplied. The parallel below, *τὰ προφητικά*, is rendered "the prophetic *matters*."
[^3]: Greek *ὄξος* — sour wine / vinegar (the cheap drink of soldiers and laborers).
[^4]: «Ὅθεν οἱ λοιποὶ λέγουσιν·» ("Whence the rest say:") breaks off, leading into the quotation that opens Lemma 51 (Matt. 27:49).
