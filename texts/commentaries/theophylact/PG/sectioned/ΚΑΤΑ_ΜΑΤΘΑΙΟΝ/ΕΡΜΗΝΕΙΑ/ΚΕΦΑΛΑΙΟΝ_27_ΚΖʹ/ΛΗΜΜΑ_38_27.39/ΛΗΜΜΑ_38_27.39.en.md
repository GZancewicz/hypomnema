# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 38 · Matt. 27:39

> **…passing by were blaspheming him, shaking their heads,[^1] and…** *(Matt. 27:39)*

This lemma carries only Theophylact's scripture quotation. It continues the sentence begun in Lemma 37 (οἱ δὲ παραπορευόμενοι, "but those passing by…") and again breaks off mid-clause (…καὶ, "and…"), running on into Lemma 39. Theophylact defers his exposition of the whole crucifixion passage (Matt. 27:38–44) to Lemma 42.

[^1]: Greek *κινοῦντες τὰς κεφαλὰς αὐτῶν* — literally "moving their heads." The verb *κινέω* is the plain "move, set in motion"; here it denotes the derisive wagging of the head (cf. Ps. 21[22]:8 LXX, *ἐκίνησαν κεφαλήν*). Rendered "shaking their heads" for the gesture; "moving their heads" would be more wooden.
