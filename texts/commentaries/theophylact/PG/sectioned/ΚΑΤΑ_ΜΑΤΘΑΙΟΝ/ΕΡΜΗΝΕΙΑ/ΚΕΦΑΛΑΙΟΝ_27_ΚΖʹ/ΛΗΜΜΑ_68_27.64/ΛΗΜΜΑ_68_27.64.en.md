# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 68 · Matt. 27:64

> **Command therefore that the tomb be made secure[^1] until the third day, lest his disciples, having come by night, steal him, and say to the people, He is risen from the dead; and the last deception[^2] shall be worse than the first.**

This lemma continues the Gospel citation with no exposition. It closes the speech of the chief priests and Pharisees (Matt. 27:64) and then opens the narration of the reply with the single word *Ἔφη* ("He said"), which runs on unbroken into Lemma 69 («…to them Pilate: You have a guard…»).

[^1]: Greek *ἀσφαλισθῆναι τὸν τάφον*, aorist passive infinitive of *ἀσφαλίζομαι* ("to make secure, make safe, secure by guard"). The cognate verb recurs throughout the pericope (*ἀσφαλίσασθε*, *ἠσφαλίσαντο*) and is kept consistently as "make secure."
[^2]: Greek *ἡ ἐσχάτη πλάνη χείρων τῆς πρώτης* ("the last deception worse than the first"). The OCR prints *ἐσνάτη* for *ἐσχάτη* ("last / final"), a secure correction. *πλάνη* ("deception, error, going-astray") is kept cognate with *πλάνος* ("deceiver") at Lemmata 67 and 72.
