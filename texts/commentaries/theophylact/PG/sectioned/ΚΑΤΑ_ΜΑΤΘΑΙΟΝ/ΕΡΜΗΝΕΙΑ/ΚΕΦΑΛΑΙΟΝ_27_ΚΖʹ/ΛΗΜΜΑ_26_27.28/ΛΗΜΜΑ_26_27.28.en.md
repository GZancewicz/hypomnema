# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 26 · Matt. 27:28

> **having stripped him, they put around him a scarlet cloak,[^1] and**[^2]

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text; Theophylact does not comment here. His exposition begins at Lemma 28 (on Matt. 27:30).*

[^1]: Greek *χλαμύδα κοκκίνην*. The *χλαμύς* is a short military/officer's mantle (rendered "cloak"); *κοκκίνη* is "scarlet, crimson" (from the *kermes* dye). Theophylact will read this scarlet cloak allegorically at Lemma 29 as our bloodstained human nature. — The lemma reads *ἐκδύσαντες* ("having stripped"), the majority-text reading; some witnesses have *ἐνδύσαντες* ("having clothed").
[^2]: The lemma breaks off at *καὶ* ("and"), continuing into the next lemma («πλέξαντες στέφανον…»).
