# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 3 · Matt. 27:3

> **Then Judas, the *one* betraying him, having seen that he was condemned, having repented, returned the thirty pieces of silver to the chief priests and the elders,**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation; the quotation continues in the next lemma, and Theophylact's exposition of this passage follows in Lemma 5.*

[^1]: Matt. 27:3. Greek «Τότε ἰδὼν Ἰούδας ὁ παραδιδοὺς αὐτὸν ὅτι κατεκρίθη, μεταμεληθεὶς ἀπέστρεψε τὰ τριάκοντα ἀργύρια τοῖς ἀρχιερεῦσι καὶ τοῖς πρεσβυτέροις». *ὁ παραδιδοὺς αὐτόν*, present participle, "the one betraying him / who was betraying him." *μεταμεληθείς*, "having repented / changed his mind" (regret, not necessarily saving repentance — a distinction Theophylact presses in Lemma 5). *ἀπέστρεψε*, "returned, gave back." The lemma breaks off at a comma; the participle «λέγων» ("saying") opens the next lemma.
