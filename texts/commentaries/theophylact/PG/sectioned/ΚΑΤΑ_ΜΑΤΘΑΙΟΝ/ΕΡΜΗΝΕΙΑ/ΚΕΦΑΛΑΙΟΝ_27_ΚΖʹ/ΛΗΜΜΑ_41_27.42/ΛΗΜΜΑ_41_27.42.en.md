# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 41 · Matt. 27:42

> **Others he saved; himself he is not able to save? If he is king of Israel,[^1] let him come down now from the cross, and we will believe in him.** *(Matt. 27:42)*

This lemma carries only Theophylact's scripture quotation, the mockery of the chief priests introduced in Lemma 40. He defers his exposition of the whole crucifixion passage (Matt. 27:38–44) to Lemma 42.

[^1]: The OCR reads «Εἰ βασιλεὺς Ἰσραήλἐστ.», with the running-together *Ἰσραήλἐστ.* securely resolved to *Ἰσραήλ ἐστιν* ("is king of Israel"); the reconstruction is certain and noted only for the record. The subject "he" is carried by *ἐστιν* (hence not italicized).
