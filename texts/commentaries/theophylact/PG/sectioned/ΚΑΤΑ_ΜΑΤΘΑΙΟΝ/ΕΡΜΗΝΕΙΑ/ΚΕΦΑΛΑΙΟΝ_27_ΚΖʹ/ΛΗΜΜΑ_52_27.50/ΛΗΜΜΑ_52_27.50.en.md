# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 52 · Matt. 27:50-51

> **But Jesus, having again cried out with a loud voice, released the spirit;[^1] and behold, the…[^2] (Matt. 27:50-51)**

*This lemma carries only the Gospel words; Theophylact gives no exposition here.*[^2]

[^1]: Greek *ἀφῆκε τὸ πνεῦμα* — "released / gave up the spirit (breath)"; *πνεῦμα* is "spirit / breath." The verb is *ἀφίημι*, the same as *Ἄφες* ("Let *be*") in Lemma 51. *Πάλιν κράξας* ("having again cried out") is an aorist participle, kept as a participle.
[^2]: The lemma breaks off at «καὶ ἰδοὺ τὸ» ("and behold, the…"), continued in Lemma 53 (…the veil of the temple…). The exposition of this loud cry is deferred: it begins in Lemma 55, «Κράζει μεγάλῃ φωνῇ ὁ Ἰησοῦς, ἵνα γνῶμεν ὅτι…» ("Jesus cries with a loud voice, that we may know that…").
