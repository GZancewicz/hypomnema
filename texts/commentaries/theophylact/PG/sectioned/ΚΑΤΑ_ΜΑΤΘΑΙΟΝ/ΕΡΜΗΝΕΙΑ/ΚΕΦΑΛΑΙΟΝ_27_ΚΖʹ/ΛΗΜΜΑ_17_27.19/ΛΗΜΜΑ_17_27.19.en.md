# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 17 · Matt. 27:19

> **But as he was sitting upon the judgment seat, his wife sent to him, saying: *Let there be* nothing between you and that righteous man.[^1] For I have suffered many things today in a dream because of him. But the**[^2]

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation; it has no exposition of its own.*

[^1]: Greek *Μηδὲν σοὶ καὶ τῷ δικαίῳ ἐκείνῳ* — literally "Nothing to you and to that righteous man," a Hebraizing idiom (*τί ἐμοὶ καὶ σοί*-type) meaning "have nothing to do with that righteous man." The supplied "*Let there be*" makes the elliptical construction into English; the words "you," "that righteous man" are all present in the Greek.
[^2]: The quotation breaks off at *Οἱ δὲ* ("But the"), completed in the next lemma (ΛΗΜΜΑ_18): *ἀρχιερεῖς καὶ οἱ πρεσβύτεροι* — "the chief priests and the elders."
