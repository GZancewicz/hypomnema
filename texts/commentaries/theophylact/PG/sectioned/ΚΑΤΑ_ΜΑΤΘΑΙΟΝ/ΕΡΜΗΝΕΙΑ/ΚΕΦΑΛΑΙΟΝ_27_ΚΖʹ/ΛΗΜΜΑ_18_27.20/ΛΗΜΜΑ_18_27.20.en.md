# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 18 · Matt. 27:20

> **chief priests and the elders persuaded the crowds that they should ask for Barabbas, but should destroy Jesus.**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation; it has no exposition of its own.*[^2]

[^1]: The verse opens with "But the" (*Οἱ δὲ*), which stands at the close of the preceding lemma (ΛΗΜΜΑ_17), so the words carried here begin at *ἀρχιερεῖς*. Greek *ἵνα … τὸν δὲ Ἰησοῦν ἀπολέσωσιν* — *ἀπόλλυμι* "to destroy / do away with," here of procuring Jesus' death; rendered "should destroy" rather than a softer "put to death" to keep the force of the verb.
[^2]: The exposition resumes in the following lemma (ΛΗΜΜΑ_19), which opens with the governor's next question (Matt. 27:21).
