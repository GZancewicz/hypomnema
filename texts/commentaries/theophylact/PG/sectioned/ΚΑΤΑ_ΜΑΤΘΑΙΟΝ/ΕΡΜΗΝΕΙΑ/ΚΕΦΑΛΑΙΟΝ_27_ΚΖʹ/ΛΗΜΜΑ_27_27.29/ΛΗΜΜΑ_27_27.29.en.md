# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 27 · Matt. 27:29

> **having plaited a crown out of thorns, they set *it* upon his head, and a reed *in* his right hand.[^1] And having fallen on *their* knees before him, they mocked him, saying: "Hail,[^2] the King of the Jews!" and**[^3]

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text; Theophylact does not comment here. His exposition begins at Lemma 28 (on Matt. 27:30).*

[^1]: Greek *κάλαμον ἐπὶ τὴν δεξιὰν αὐτοῦ* — "a reed onto his right hand," i.e. placed in his right *hand* (as a mock scepter); *δεξιά* is the substantive "right hand." Theophylact will play on this *δεξιά* at Lemma 30, quoting «δεξιὰ Κυρίου ὕψωσέ με» ("the right hand of the Lord exalted me"). The participle *γονυπετήσαντες* is "having fallen on the knees," to which he returns at Lemma 29 as an act of (mock) obeisance.
[^2]: Greek *Χαῖρε* — literally "Rejoice," the ordinary Greek salutation, hence "Hail." *ὁ βασιλεὺς τῶν Ἰουδαίων* stands in the nominative (with article) used as a vocative, "*O* King of the Jews."
[^3]: The lemma breaks off at *καὶ* ("and"), continuing into the next lemma («ἐμπτύσαντες εἰς αὐτὸν…»).
