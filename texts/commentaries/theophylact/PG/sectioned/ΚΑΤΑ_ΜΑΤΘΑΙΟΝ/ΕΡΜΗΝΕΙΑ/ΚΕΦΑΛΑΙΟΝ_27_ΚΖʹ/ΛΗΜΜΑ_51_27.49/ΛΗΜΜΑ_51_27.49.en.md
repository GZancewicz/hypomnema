# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 51 · Matt. 27:49

> **"Let *be*,[^1] let us see if Elijah comes *to* save him,"[^2] (Matt. 27:49)**

that is, "Do not make him die, so that we may know if Elijah will help him."

[^1]: Greek *Ἄφες* — imperative of *ἀφίημι*, "let alone, permit, leave *be*"; the object is unexpressed, so *be* is supplied. (The same verb recurs in Lemma 52 as *ἀφῆκε τὸ πνεῦμα*, "released the spirit.")
[^2]: Greek *σώσων* is a future participle expressing purpose, "*about* to save / who will save"; rendered "to save" for readability. *Τουτέστι* = "that is." *Ὡς ἂν γνῶμεν* = "so that we may know."
