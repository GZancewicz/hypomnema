# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 20 · Matt. 27:22

> **Pilate says to them: "What then shall I do *with* Jesus who is called Christ?" They all say to him: "Let him be crucified." But the—**[^1]

[^1]: A scripture-quotation fragment with no exposition of its own (see Lemma 23). "What then shall I do *with* Jesus" renders *Τί οὖν ποιήσω Ἰησοῦν*, where "Jesus" stands in the accusative governed by *ποιήσω* ("shall I do"); English needs the supplied "*with*." The particle *οὖν* ("then / therefore") is present in the Greek and so is roman. "Let him be crucified" renders the aorist passive imperative *Σταυρωθήτω*. The fragment breaks off at *Ὁ δὲ* ("But the—"); the subject "governor" follows at the head of the next lemma.
