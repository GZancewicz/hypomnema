# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 35 · Matt. 27:36

> **...and sitting down they kept watch over him there, and...**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text; Theophylact adds no exposition here. The quotation runs on into the following lemma (27:37).*

[^1]: Greek *καθήμενοι ἐτήρουν αὐτὸν ἐκεῖ, καὶ* — a fragment opening and closing mid-sentence; the connective *καὶ* ("and") links into the next lemma.
