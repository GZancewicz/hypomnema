# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 33 · Matt. 27:33–34

> **...having come to a place called Golgotha, which is called *the* Place of a Skull,[^1] they gave him to drink vinegar mingled with gall,[^2] and having tasted *it* he would not drink.**

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text; Theophylact adds no exposition here. He expounds Golgotha ("Place of a Skull") and the vinegar-with-gall further below, in the exposition attached to Lemma 36 (27:37).*

[^1]: Greek *Κρανίου τόπος* ("Place of a Skull"); the OCR *Χἐστι* is securely *ὅ ἐστι* ("which is"). The participle *λεγόμενος* ("called") agrees with *τόπος*.
[^2]: Greek *ὄξος μετὰ χολῆς μεμιγμένον* — *ὄξος* is sour wine / wine-vinegar; *χολή* is "gall." Theophylact discusses the divergence among the evangelists (Mark's "wine mingled with myrrh," John's "vinegar and gall with hyssop") in the exposition at Lemma 36.
