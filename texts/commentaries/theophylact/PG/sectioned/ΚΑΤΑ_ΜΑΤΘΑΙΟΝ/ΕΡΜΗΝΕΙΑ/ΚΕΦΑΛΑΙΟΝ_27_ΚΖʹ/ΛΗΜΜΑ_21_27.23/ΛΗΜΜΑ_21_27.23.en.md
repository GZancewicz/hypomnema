# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 21 · Matt. 27:23

> **—governor said: "For what evil did he do?" But they were crying out the more, saying: "Let him be crucified." But having seen, the—**[^1]

[^1]: A scripture-quotation fragment with no exposition of its own (see Lemma 23). It opens with the word "governor" (*ἡγεμὼν*) that completes the sentence broken off at the end of the previous lemma. The particle *γὰρ* ("for") in *Τί γὰρ κακὸν ἐποίησεν* is rendered roman. "Were crying out the more" renders the imperfect *περισσῶς ἔκραζον* (*περισσῶς*, "exceedingly / all the more"). The fragment again breaks off at *Ἰδὼν δὲ ὁ* ("But having seen, the—"); the subject "Pilate" opens the next lemma, and the participle *Ἰδὼν* is kept as "having seen."
