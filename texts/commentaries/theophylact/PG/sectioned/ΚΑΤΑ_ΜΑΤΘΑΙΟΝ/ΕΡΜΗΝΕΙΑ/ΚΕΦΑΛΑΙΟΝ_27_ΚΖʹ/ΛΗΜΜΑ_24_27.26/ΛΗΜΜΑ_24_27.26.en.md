# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 24 · Matt. 27:26 (Isa. 50:6)

> **"My back I gave to scourges." (Isa. 50:6)**[^1]

[^1]: This lemma carries only the prophetic citation and has no exposition of its own; it completes the sentence broken off at the end of Lemma 23 ("Therefore was fulfilled also the *word*:"). The words are from Isaiah 50:6 (LXX, *τὸν νῶτόν μου δέδωκα εἰς μάστιγας*), adduced by Theophylact as fulfilled in Pilate's scourging of Jesus; the folder-name verse (27.26) is the Gospel locus to which the prophecy is attached, not the source of the quoted words. "My back I gave to scourges" follows the Greek word order; *μάστιγας* ("scourges / whips") echoes the *ἐμάστιξεν* ("he flogged") of the preceding comment.
