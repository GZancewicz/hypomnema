# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 71 · Matt. 27:66 (exposition, continued)

This lemma carries no Gospel lemma of its own; it continues the exposition of Lemma 70. The sentence there ended "For Christ said that —"; here it is completed with the saying about Jonah.

«just as Jonah was three days in the belly of the sea-monster,[^1] so also I *shall be* in the belly of the earth;»[^2] or also from the *saying*:[^3]

[^1]: Greek *κῆτος* ("sea-monster, huge fish, whale"); the LXX word at Jonah 2:1. The OCR opens the quotation with *λαπερ* for *ὥσπερ* ("just as"), a secure correction.
[^2]: Greek *οὕτω καὶ ἐγὼ ἐν τῇ κοιλίᾳ τῆς γῆς* ("so also I in the belly of the earth"). Theophylact quotes Christ's word (cf. Matt. 12:40) loosely and abbreviated: the verb is left to be supplied (hence italic "*shall be*"), the "three days and three nights" of the second half is dropped, and *κοιλίᾳ τῆς γῆς* ("belly of the earth") stands where Matt. 12:40 has *καρδίᾳ τῆς γῆς* ("heart of the earth") — the "belly" (κοιλία) being carried over from the Jonah half of the comparison. Translated as he cites it, per the rule of following the text on the page.
[^3]: Greek *ἢ καὶ ἀπὸ τοῦ* ("or also from *that saying*"). The bare genitive article *τοῦ* stands for the coming quotation; "*the saying*" is supplied. The sentence runs on into Lemma 72 («Destroy this temple…»), a second source from which the Jews might have learned of the third day.
