# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 7 · Matt. 27:7

> **And having taken counsel, they bought with them[^1] the field of the potter, for a burial-place for strangers; wherefore[^2] (Matt. 27:7)**

This lemma carries only the running Gospel text; Theophylact appends no exposition here. His comment on the whole section (Matt. 27:7–10) follows once the quotation is completed at Lemma 10.

[^1]: Greek *ἐξ αὐτῶν*, literally "out of them" — that is, out of the thirty pieces of silver of the preceding verse.
[^2]: The lemma breaks off at *διὸ* ("wherefore"), OCR-garbled in the source as «δτὸ»; the sentence is continued in the next lemma (*ἐκλήθη ὁ ἀγρὸς ἐκεῖνος…*, "that field was called…").
