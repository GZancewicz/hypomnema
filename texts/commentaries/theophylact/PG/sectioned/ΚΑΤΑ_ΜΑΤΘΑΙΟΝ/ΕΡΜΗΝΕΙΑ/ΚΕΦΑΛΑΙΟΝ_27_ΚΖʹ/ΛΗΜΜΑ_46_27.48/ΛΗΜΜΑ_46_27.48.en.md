# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 46 · Matt. 27:48

> **straightway one of them, having run, and having taken a sponge, and having filled *it* with vinegar and put *it* around a reed, was giving him to drink. But the[^1] (Matt. 27:48)**

[Editorial note: this lemma folder carries only the Gospel text; Theophylact's exposition follows from Lemma 47.]

[^1]: The sentence breaks off at *Οἱ δέ* ("But the…"); it is completed in the next lemma, "…rest were saying: Let be…". Greek *περιθεὶς καλάμῳ* = "having put *it* around a reed" (the sponge fixed on the reed); *ἐπότιζεν* is imperfect, "was giving *him* to drink."
