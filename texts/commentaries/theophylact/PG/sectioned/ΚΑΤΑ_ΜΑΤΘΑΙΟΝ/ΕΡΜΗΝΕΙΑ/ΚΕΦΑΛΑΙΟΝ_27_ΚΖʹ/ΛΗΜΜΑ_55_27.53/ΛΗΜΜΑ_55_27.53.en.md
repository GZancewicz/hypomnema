# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 55 · Matt. 27:53

> **…*who,* having come out of the tombs after his resurrection, entered into the holy city, and were made manifest to many. (Matt. 27:53)**[^1]

Jesus cries out with a loud voice, in order that we might know that what he was saying, that—[^2]

[^1]: The lemma opens mid-quotation, continuing the Gospel citation begun in the preceding lemma (Matt. 27:52–53, the bodies of the saints raised at the crucifixion); the supplied "*who*" renders the relative force the Greek carries by the participle *ἐξελθόντες*. Greek *ἐνεφανίσθησαν* ("were made manifest / appeared / were shown"), passive of *ἐμφανίζω*; rendered "were made manifest" to keep the passive.
[^2]: The exposition breaks off mid-sentence at *ὅτι* ("that"); Theophylact's clause is completed in the next lemma, which supplies the quoted words «Ἐξουσίαν ἔχω θεῖναι τὴν ψυχήν μου» ("I have authority to lay down my soul"). Here the comment pivots from the raised saints back to the moment of the loud cry (Matt. 27:50).
