# Matthew 27 (ΚΖʹ) — Lemma 56 · Matt. 27:50 (cf. John 10:18)

> **«I have authority to lay down my soul»**[^1]

—was true. For it is according to *his* authority that he releases the soul. But what was the cry?[^2]

[^1]: The embedded scripture quotation is John 10:18 (*ἐξουσίαν ἔχω θεῖναι τὴν ψυχήν μου*, "I have authority to lay down my soul/life"); Theophylact weaves it in as a proof-text to explain the loud cry at the moment of Christ's death (Matt. 27:50), not as a Matthaean lemma. Greek *ψυχή* here spans "soul" and "life"; rendered "soul" throughout this passage for consistency, though "life" is equally defensible.
[^2]: The Greek runs on continuously into the next lemma: the words *ἀληθὲς ἦν* ("was true") complete the sentence begun in the previous lemma, and the question *Τίς δὲ ἦν ἡ φωνή;* ("But what was the cry?") is answered in the next lemma by the quotation of Luke 23:46. In the source the closing quotation mark is OCR-garbled as a stray *ο* (*τὴν ψυχήν μου, ο ἀληθὲς ἡν*); read as «…ψυχήν μου» ἀληθὲς ἦν.
