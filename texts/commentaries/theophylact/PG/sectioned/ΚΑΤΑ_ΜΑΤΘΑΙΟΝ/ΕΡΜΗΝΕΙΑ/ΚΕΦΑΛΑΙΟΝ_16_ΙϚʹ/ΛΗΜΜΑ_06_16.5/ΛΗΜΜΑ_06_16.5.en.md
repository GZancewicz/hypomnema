# Matthew 16 (ΙϚʹ) — Lemma 6 · Matt. 16:5

> **And his disciples, having come to the other side, forgot to take loaves. But he—[^1]**

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation, which breaks off mid-sentence; Theophylact's exposition begins in the following lemma, after the Lord's saying has been quoted.*

[^1]: The lemma breaks off at *Ὁ δὲ* ("But he—"); the sentence is completed at the head of the next lemma, which supplies «Ἰησοῦς εἶπεν αὐτοῖς…» ("Jesus said to them…"). "The other side" renders *τὸ πέραν* (the farther shore of the lake); "loaves" renders *ἄρτους* (plural), kept concrete rather than as "bread."
