# Matthew 16 (ΙϚʹ) — Lemma 1 · Matt. 16:1

> **And the Pharisees and Sadducees, having come, tempting *him*, asked him to show them a sign from heaven. (Matt. 16:1)**

Even though the Pharisees and Sadducees stood apart in their doctrines,[^1] yet against Christ they breathe together.[^2] But a sign from heaven they seek, such as to make the sun stand still, *or* the moon; for they supposed that the signs upon the earth come to pass from a demonic power and by Beelzebul. For the foolish *men* were ignorant that even Moses in Egypt did many signs from the earth, and *that* the fire which came down from heaven upon the cattle of Job[^3] was from the devil; so that not all the things from heaven *are* from God, nor *are* all the things from earth from demons.

[^1]: Greek OCR *διίστανοο*, securely = *διΐσταντο* / *διΐστανται* ("stood apart, were divided"). The reconstruction is certain; noted for consistency with the other flagged OCR corruptions.
[^2]: Greek *συμπνέουσι* ("breathe together"), i.e. conspire, are of one accord. The verb literally carries "breathe/blow together" — hence rendered "breathe together" to preserve the image, though the sense is "they conspire against Christ." The OCR *κατὰ Χοι-τοῦ* is securely *κατὰ Χριστοῦ* ("against Christ").
[^3]: Cf. Job 1:16, where "the fire of God fell from heaven" and consumed Job's sheep — which Theophylact here notes was in fact the devil's doing (Job 1:12), his point being that heavenly origin does not guarantee divine agency.
