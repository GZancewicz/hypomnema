# Matthew 16 (ΙϚʹ) — Lemma 9 · Matt. 16:9

> **…do you understand, nor do you remember the five loaves of the five thousand, and how many baskets you took up? nor the seven…**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text, with no exposition of its own; it continues the sentence begun in Lemma 8 and runs on into Lemma 10. Theophylact's comment on the whole stretch (16:8-11) stands at Lemma 11.*

[^1]: The lemma opens with *νοεῖτε* ("do you understand"), completing the *Οὔπω* ("Not yet…") of Lemma 8, and breaks off at *τοὺς ἑπτὰ* ("the seven…"), to be completed in Lemma 10 by "loaves of the four thousand." *κοφίνους* here = "baskets" (the wicker kind, as at the feeding of the five thousand); a different word, *σπυρίδας*, appears in the next verse for the feeding of the four thousand — the distinction is preserved in Lemma 10.
