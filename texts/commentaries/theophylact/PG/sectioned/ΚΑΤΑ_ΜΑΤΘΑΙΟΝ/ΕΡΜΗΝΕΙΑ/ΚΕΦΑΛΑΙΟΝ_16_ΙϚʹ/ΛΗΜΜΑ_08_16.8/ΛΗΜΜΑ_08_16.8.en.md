# Matthew 16 (ΙϚʹ) — Lemma 8 · Matt. 16:8

> **But Jesus, having known *it*, said to them: Why do you reason among yourselves, O you of little faith, that you took no loaves? Not yet…**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text, with no exposition of its own; the sentence runs on unbroken through Lemmata 9 and 10 and Theophylact gathers his comment on the whole stretch (16:8-11) into Lemma 11.*[^2]

[^1]: The sentence breaks off at *Οὔπω* ("Not yet…") and is completed in the next lemma with *νοεῖτε* ("do you understand"). *Γνοὺς* is an aorist participle, "having perceived / having known"; the supplied "*it*" (the object) is not in the Greek. *ὀλιγόπιστοι* ("O you of little faith") is a vocative address, the same word Theophylact expounds in Lemma 11.
[^2]: Noted per the convention for continuation-lemmata that carry scripture but no comment.
