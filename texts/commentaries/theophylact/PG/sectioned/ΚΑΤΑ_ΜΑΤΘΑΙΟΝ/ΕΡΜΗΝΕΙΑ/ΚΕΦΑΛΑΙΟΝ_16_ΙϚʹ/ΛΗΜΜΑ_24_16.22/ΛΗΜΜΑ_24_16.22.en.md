# Matthew 16 (ΙϚʹ) — Lemma 24 · Matt. 16:24[^1]

> **But I live no longer, but Christ lives in me.[^2]**

And he takes up the cross who has mortified and crucified himself to the world.

[^1]: This lemma continues Theophylact's comment on Matt. 16:24 (the taking up of the cross). It opens not with a Gospel lemma but with the Pauline words that complete the sentence broken off at the end of Lemma 23 (Paul as the man who "denied himself"). The lemma folder is labelled 16.22; the true subject is 16:24, corrected here.
[^2]: Greek *Ζῶ δὲ οὐκέτι ἐγώ, ζῇ δὲ ἐν ἐμοὶ Χριστός* = Gal. 2:20. Literally "But I live no longer — but *it is* Christ *who* lives in me." The two *δέ* particles are adversative-continuative; the supplied "*it is … who*" makes explicit the emphatic Greek word order (*ζῇ δὲ ἐν ἐμοὶ Χριστός*), where "Christ" stands last for stress.
