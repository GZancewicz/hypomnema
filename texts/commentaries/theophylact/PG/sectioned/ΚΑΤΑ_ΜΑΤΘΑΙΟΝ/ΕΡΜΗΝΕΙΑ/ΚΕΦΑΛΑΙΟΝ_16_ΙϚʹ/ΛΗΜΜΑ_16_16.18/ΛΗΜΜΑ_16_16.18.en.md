# Matthew 16 (ΙϚʹ) — Lemma 16 · Matt. 16:18

> **… *are* Peter, and upon this rock I will build my Church, and *the* gates of Hades shall not prevail against it.[^1] (Matt. 16:18)**

The Lord recompenses Peter, giving him a great reward: the being-built of the Church upon him. For since Peter confessed him *to be the* Son of God, he says that "This confession which you have confessed shall be a foundation of those who believe, so that every person who is about to build the house of faith should lay down this confession *as* foundation." For even if we should build ten thousand virtues, but should not have the right confession *as* foundation, we build *things that are* rotten. And having said, "My Church," he shows himself *to be* Master of all; for all things together *are* servants of God.

And *the* "gates of Hades" *are* the persecutors at *various* times, who were dispatching the Christians into Hades. And the heretics, too, *are* gates leading into Hades; over many persecutors, therefore, and many heretics, the Church has prevailed. And each of us also is a Church, becoming a house of God. If therefore we are established upon the confession of Christ, the gates of Hades — that is, the sins — shall not prevail against us; for, having been lifted up out of these gates, David too said:[^2]

[^1]: The famous word-play, which Theophylact everywhere assumes: *σὺ εἶ Πέτρος, καὶ ἐπὶ ταύτῃ τῇ πέτρᾳ* — "you are *Petros* (Peter), and upon this *petra* (rock)." The masculine name *Πέτρος* and the feminine noun *πέτρα* ("rock") are cognate; his gloss below ("the confession… shall be a foundation") takes "this rock" to be Peter's confession. *Ἅδης* ("Hades") is the abode of the dead; rendered "Hades" rather than "hell" to keep it distinct from *γέεννα*.
[^2]: The sentence runs on into Lemma 17, which supplies the Davidic quotation, «Ὁ ὑψῶν με ἐκ τῶν πυλῶν τοῦ θανάτου» (Ps. 9:14 LXX).
