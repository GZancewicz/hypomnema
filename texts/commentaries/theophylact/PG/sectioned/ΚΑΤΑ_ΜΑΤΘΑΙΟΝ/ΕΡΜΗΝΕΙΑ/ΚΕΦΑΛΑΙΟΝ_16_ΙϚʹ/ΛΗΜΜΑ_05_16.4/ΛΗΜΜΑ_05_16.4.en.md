# Matthew 16 (ΙϚʹ) — Lemma 5 · Matt. 16:4

> **A sign shall not be given to it, except the sign of Jonah the prophet.**

For to them[^1] the signs were being given — that is, for their sake they came to pass — even though those *men* disbelieved. Wherefore also, having left them as incurable, he went away.

[^1]: This lemma carries out the observation "on the wording" promised at the end of the previous lemma. The Gospel says the sign shall not be given "to it" (*αὐτῇ*, feminine singular, agreeing with "generation"), yet Theophylact glosses with the plural "to them" (*αὐτοῖς*): Greek *Αὐτοῖς γὰρ ἐδίδοντο τὰ σημεῖα, τουτέστι, δι᾿ αὐτοὺς ἐγίνοντο* ("for to them the signs were being given, that is, for their sake they came to pass"). His point is that "given to them" means "wrought on their account / for their benefit," not that they received or believed — hence the following "even though those *men* disbelieved."
