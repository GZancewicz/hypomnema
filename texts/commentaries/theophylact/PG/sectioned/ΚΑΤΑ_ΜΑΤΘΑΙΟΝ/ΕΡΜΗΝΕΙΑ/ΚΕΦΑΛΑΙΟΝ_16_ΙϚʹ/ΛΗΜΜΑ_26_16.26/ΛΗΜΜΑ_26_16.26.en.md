# Matthew 16 (ΙϚʹ) — Lemma 26 · Matt. 16:26–27

> **For what is a man profited, if he should gain the whole world, but should forfeit his soul? Or what shall a man give *as* an exchange for his soul?[^1] For the Son of man is about to come in the glory of his Father with his angels, and then he shall render to each according to his deed. (Matt. 16:26–27)**

"Let it be," he says, "by way of supposition, that you have gained the whole world"; but what *is* the profit — while the soul is ill-disposed — that the body should fare well? *It is* just as if, while the mistress of the house wears rags *that are* torn through, the maidservants should go about in splendid attire.[^2] For neither in the age to come is anyone able to give an exchange on behalf of his soul. For here, on the one hand, one is able to give tears, groanings, almsgiving; but there, on the other hand, no.[^3] For a judge who takes no bribes shall receive *us* in turn;[^4] for he judges each according to *his* works. But *he is* also fearsome — for he comes in his glory and with the angels, and not *as one* despised.

[^1]: Greek *ἀντάλλαγμα* — "a thing given in exchange, a ransom-price." The word carries the commercial imagery (buying, exchange) that governs the whole passage: no price can buy back a soul once forfeited.
[^2]: Greek *ῥάκια διερρηγμένα φορούσης … αἱ θεραπαινίδες λαμπροφοροῖεν*. The soul is the *οἰκοδέσποινα* (mistress of the house), the body her *θεραπαινίδες* (maidservants); *λαμπροφορέω* = "to wear bright/splendid clothing." The absurdity: the maids finely dressed while their mistress is in tatters.
[^3]: Greek *ἐκεῖ δὲ οὔ* — elliptical, "but there, not," i.e. there one is *not* able to give any such thing.
[^4]: Greek *Ἀδωροδόκητος γὰρ κριτὴς διαδέξεται*. *Ἀδωροδόκητος* = "not to be bribed, incorruptible" (literally "one who accepts no gifts"); *διαδέξεται* = "shall receive in turn / take *us* over *in succession*" — the object ("us") is unexpressed and supplied. The point contrasts with the tears and alms one may still "give" here: the judge there accepts no such payment.
