# Matthew 16 (ΙϚʹ) — Lemma 10 · Matt. 16:10

> **…loaves of the four thousand, and how many hampers you took up? How *do* you not…**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text, with no exposition of its own; it completes the clause begun in Lemma 9 and runs on into Lemma 11, where Theophylact's comment on the whole stretch (16:8-11) is found.*

[^1]: The lemma completes "nor the seven…" of Lemma 9 with *ἄρτους τῶν τετρακισχιλίων* ("loaves of the four thousand"; OCR «τετρακισχιίων») and breaks off at *Πῶς οὐ* ("How *do* you not…"), completed in Lemma 11 by "understand." *σπυρίδας* ("hampers, large baskets") is rendered distinctly from *κοφίνους* ("baskets") of the previous verse; the Gospel keeps two different words for the two feedings, and the translation follows suit.
