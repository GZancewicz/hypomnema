# Matthew 16 (ΙϚʹ) — Lemma 2 · Matt. 16:2

> **But he answering said to them: When evening has come, you say: *It will be* fair weather, for the heaven is red. And in *the* morn—[^1]**

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation, which breaks off mid-word; Theophylact's exposition begins in the following lemma, after the rest of the Lord's saying has been quoted.*

[^1]: The lemma breaks off mid-word at *Καὶ πρω·*, securely *καὶ πρωΐ* ("and in *the* morning"); the sentence is completed at the head of the next lemma («Σήμερον χειμών…», "Today *there will be* a storm…"). "Fair weather" renders *εὐδία*, the clear-sky weather-term; the supplied "*It will be*" is italicized, since the Greek is a bare noun-predicate.
