# Matthew 16 (ΙϚʹ) — Lemma 15 · Matt. 16:18

> **And I also say to you that you are …[^1]**

*This lemma carries only the opening words of the verse and no exposition; the scripture quotation and Theophylact's comment continue in the next lemma.*[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Κἀγὼ δέ σοι λέγω ὅτι σὺ εἶ …» — "And I also say to you that you are …" These are the first words of Matt. 16:18 (not 16:17, as the folder name has it); the sentence breaks off before the predicate, «Πέτρος» ("Peter"), which opens Lemma 16.
[^2]: See Lemma 16, which resumes «Πέτρος, καὶ ἐπὶ ταύτῃ τῇ πέτρᾳ οἰκοδομήσω μου τὴν Ἐκκλησίαν …» ("Peter, and upon this rock I will build my Church …") and supplies the exposition.
