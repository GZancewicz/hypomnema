# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 54 · Matt. 5:41

> **And whoever shall compel you *to go* one mile, go with him two.** (Matt. 5:41)[^1]

Why do I speak, he says, of cloaks and tunics? Give even your very body to the one who unjustly drags *you*, and do more than that one wishes.

[^1]: Greek ἀγγαρεύσει, from ἀγγαρεία — the Persian institution of forced public service or requisitioned transport (whence the "compelling" of Simon of Cyrene, Matt. 27:32). Rendered "shall compel," with "*to go*" supplied for sense, since the Greek carries the notion of pressing into service for a journey. μίλιον ("mile") is itself a Latin loan-word (*mille passuum*).
