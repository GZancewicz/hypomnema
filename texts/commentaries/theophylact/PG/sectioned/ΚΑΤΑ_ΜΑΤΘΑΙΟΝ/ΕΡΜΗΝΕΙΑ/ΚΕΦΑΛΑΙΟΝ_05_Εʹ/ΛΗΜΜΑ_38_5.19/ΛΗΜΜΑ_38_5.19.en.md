# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 38 · Matt. 5:22

> **«Raca».**[^1] (Matt. 5:22)

«Raca», in Syriac, they say means "contemptible."[^2] Whoever, therefore, shall insult his brother as *being* contemptible shall be liable to the council[^3] of the holy apostles, when they shall sit judging the twelve tribes.

[^1]: A continuation-lemma: it carries no new «…» Gospel quote of its own but resumes the same word «Ῥακά» of Matt. 5:22 and completes the sentence begun at the end of the previous lemma (*Τινὸς δὲ τὸ*, "But according to a certain *interpreter*, the *word*—"). Reading the two together: "But according to a certain *interpreter*, the *word* «Raca», in Syriac, they say means 'contemptible.'"
[^2]: Greek *κατάπτυστον*, literally "spat-down-upon," hence "abominable, contemptible, worthy of being spat at." An intensified term of scorn; "contemptible" is chosen to match the register while noting the concrete image of spitting.
[^3]: Greek *συνέδριον* ("council, Sanhedrin"). In the previous lemma Theophylact glossed the Gospel's *συνέδριον* as "the tribunal of the Hebrews"; here he reads it eschatologically as the council of the apostles who will judge the twelve tribes (cf. Matt. 19:28). The same word is rendered "council" throughout for consistency.
