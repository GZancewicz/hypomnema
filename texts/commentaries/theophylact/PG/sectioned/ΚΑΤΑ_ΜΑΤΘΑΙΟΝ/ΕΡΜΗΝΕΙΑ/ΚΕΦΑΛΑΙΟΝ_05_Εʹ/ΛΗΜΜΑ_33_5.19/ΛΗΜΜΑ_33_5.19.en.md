# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 33 · Matt. 5:21

> **"It was said to the ancients:"**[^1]

But he shows that the law has been made old,[^2] in the *very* saying that—[^3]

[^1]: Greek «Ἐρρέθη τοῖς ἀρχαίοις», the phrase from Matt. 5:21 ("it was said to the ancients / to *them* of old time"), here isolated for comment.
[^2]: Greek *ἐπαλαιώθη ὁ νόμος* (aorist passive of *παλαιόω*) — "the law was made old / has grown old"; the language anticipates Heb. 8:13, which the next lemma develops. *Δείκνυσι δὲ* is rendered "But he shows."
[^3]: The sentence breaks off at *ὅτι* ("that"); it is completed in lemma 34.
