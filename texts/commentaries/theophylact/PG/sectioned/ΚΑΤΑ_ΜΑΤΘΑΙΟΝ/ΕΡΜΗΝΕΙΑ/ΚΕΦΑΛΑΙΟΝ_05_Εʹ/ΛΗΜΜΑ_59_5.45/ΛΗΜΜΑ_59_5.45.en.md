# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 59 · Matt. 5:46

> **For if you love those that love you, what reward have you? Do not the tax-collectors also do the same? (Matt. 5:46)**[^1]

Let us shudder,[^2] when we are not even equal to the tax-collectors, but even hate those that love us

[^1]: The folder metadata assigns this to 5:45, but the lemma is Matt. 5:46. Where the common text (TR, Byzantine) reads *οὐχὶ καὶ οἱ τελῶναι τὸ αὐτὸ ποιοῦσιν;*, some witnesses read *οἱ ἐθνικοί* ("the Gentiles"); Theophylact's text has *οἱ τελῶναι* ("the tax-collectors"), followed here.
[^2]: Greek OCR *Θρίξωμεν*, securely = *φρίξωμεν* (aorist subjunctive of *φρίσσω*, "to shudder, to bristle with horror"); "let us shudder." The clause *ὅταν μηδὲ τῶν τελωνῶν ὦμεν ἴσοι* is rendered "when we are not even equal to the tax-collectors" (*ἴσοι* here construed with the genitive *τῶν τελωνῶν*). The sentence breaks off without closing punctuation with "but even hate those that love us."
