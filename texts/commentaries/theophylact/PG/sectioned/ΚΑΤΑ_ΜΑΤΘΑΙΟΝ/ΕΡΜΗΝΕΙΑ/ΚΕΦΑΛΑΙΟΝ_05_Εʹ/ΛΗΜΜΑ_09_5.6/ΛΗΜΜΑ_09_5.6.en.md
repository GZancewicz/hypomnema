# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 9 · Matt. 5:6

> **Hungering and thirsting. (Matt. 5:6)**[^1]

— this it signifies. And since the covetous seem to be well-off and to be filled, he says that the righteous rather shall be filled even here, for they themselves possess their own things with security.

[^1]: This lemma completes the sentence broken off at the end of Lemma 8 ("for the *phrase*,"): «Πεινῶντες καὶ διψῶντες» τοῦτο σημαίνει — "for the *phrase* 'hungering and thirsting' — this it signifies." The opening "— this it signifies" (τοῦτο σημαίνει) is the predicate of that carried-over sentence. οἱ πλεονέκται ("the covetous / graspers") are the greedy, contrasted with οἱ δίκαιοι ("the righteous").
