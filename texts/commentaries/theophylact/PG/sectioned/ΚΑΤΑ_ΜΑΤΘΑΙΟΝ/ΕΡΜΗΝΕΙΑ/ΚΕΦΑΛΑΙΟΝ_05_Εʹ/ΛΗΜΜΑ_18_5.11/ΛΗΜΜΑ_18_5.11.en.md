# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 18 · Matt. 5:14

> **A city cannot be hid that is set upon a mountain. (Matt. 5:14)**

He trains them to be strenuously earnest[^1] and exact concerning their life, as ones who are about to be beheld by all. For do not think, he says, that you will be hidden in a corner, but you will be conspicuous *on every side*; so, see to it how blamelessly you shall live, in order that you may not scandalize others also.

Neither—[^2]

[^1]: Greek *ἐναγωνίους*, from *ἐν* + *ἀγών* ("contest, struggle")—literally "in the contest," an athletic metaphor: strenuously earnest, ever on the alert, keyed up as if under contest. Rendered "strenuously earnest" to keep the note of vigilant striving.
[^2]: The lemma ends with the bare word «Οὐδὲ» ("Neither"), which in fact begins the scripture quotation of the following lemma—«Οὐδὲ καίουσι λύχνον…» ("Neither do they light a lamp…," Matt. 5:15). Left here as the seam it is; it is taken up at the head of the next lemma.
