# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 19 · Matt. 5:15

> **…*Neither do they* light a lamp and set *it* under the bushel-measure,[^1] but upon the lampstand, and it shines for all those in the house. (Matt. 5:15)**[^2]

I, on the one hand, he says, kindled the light; but on the other hand, that the grace be not quenched[^3] — let this be *a matter* of your own diligence,[^4] so that the splendor of your life may shine also to the others. At any rate he says:[^5]

[^1]: Greek *τὸ μόδιον*, a loanword from Latin *modius*, a dry measuring-vessel (roughly a peck). Rendered "bushel-measure" for the plain sense of the container; not adopting a fixed English-Bible wording.
[^2]: The quotation opens mid-sentence: the governing negation *οὐδὲ* ("neither/nor") stands at the very end of the previous lemma (18), so this lemma carries the words «καίουσι λύχνον, καὶ τιθέασιν ὑπὸ τὸ μόδιον, ἀλλ᾽ ἐπὶ τὴν λυχνίαν, καὶ λάμπει πᾶσι τοῖς ἐν τῇ οἰκίᾳ.» The supplied "*Neither do they*" restores the sense from that carried-over particle.
[^3]: Greek *τὸ δὲ μὴ σβεσθῆναι τὴν χάριν* — an articular infinitive with *τὴν χάριν* ("the grace") as its subject: literally "but the grace's not-being-quenched," rendered "that the grace be not quenched."
[^4]: Greek *τῆς ὑμετέρας ἔστω σπουδῆς* ("let it be of your diligence"), a genitive of belonging: the responsibility is assigned to their zeal. The source reads OCR *σῆουδῆς*, securely = *σπουδῆς* ("diligence, zeal, earnest effort").
[^5]: Greek *Φησὶ γοῦν* ("He says, then / at any rate:"), a lead-in introducing the scripture quotation expounded in the next lemma (20).
