# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 15 · Matt. 5:12

> **For thus they persecuted the prophets who *were* before you. (Matt. 5:12)**[^1]

In order that the apostles might not suspect that they will be persecuted because of teaching certain things *that are* contrary,[^2] he consoles them, saying that: "The prophets *who were* before you also were driven out[^3] for virtue's sake"; so that you have the sufferings[^4] of those *men as* a consolation—

[^1]: OCR «Οὕτω γὰρ ἐδίωξαν τοὺς πρι φήτας τοὺς πρὸ ὑμῶν» = **«τοὺς προφήτας»**. The lemma runs on directly into the exposition without an intervening quotation mark in the source.
[^2]: Greek *διὰ τὸ ἐναντία τινὰ διδάσκειν*—"because of teaching certain contrary things," i.e. things contrary to God or the received law. Theophylact's point: they are hounded not for false doctrine but for virtue, as the prophets were.
[^3]: OCR «ἠλαύνοντο» (for *ἠλαύνοντο*, imperfect passive of *ἐλαύνω*)—"were driven out / were hounded / were harried." Also in this sentence «ὑπονοήσωαιν» = *ὑπονοήσωσιν*, «ἀπό τολοι» = *ἀπόστολοι*, «ἐνανσία» = *ἐναντία*—secure OCR corrections.
[^4]: The lemma breaks off mid-word: source «τὰ ἐκείνων παθη-», i.e. *τὰ ἐκείνων παθήματα* ("the sufferings of those *men*"). The sentence is completed at the head of the next lemma.
