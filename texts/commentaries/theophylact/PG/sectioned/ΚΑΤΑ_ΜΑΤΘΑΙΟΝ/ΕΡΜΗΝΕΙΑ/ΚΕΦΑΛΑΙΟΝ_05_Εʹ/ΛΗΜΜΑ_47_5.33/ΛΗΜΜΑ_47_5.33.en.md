# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 47 · Matt. 5:34–35

> **But I say to you, not to swear at all: neither by heaven, because it is the throne of God; nor by the earth, because it is the footstool of his feet; nor toward Jerusalem, because it is the city of the great King. (Matt. 5:34–35)**

[^1]: The lemma breaks off mid-sentence at «Ἐπειδὴ οἱ Ἰουδαῖοι τοῦ Θεοῦ λέγοντος ἤκουον» ("Since the Jews used to hear God saying"); the sentence is completed at the head of the next lemma, which supplies the words God is heard to say — «Ὁ οὐρανός μοι θρόνος …» (Isa. 66:1).
