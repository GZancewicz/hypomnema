# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 30 · Matt. 5:20

> **For I say to you that unless your righteousness abound[^1] more than *that* of the scribes and Pharisees, you shall by no means enter into the kingdom of the heavens.**[^2]

By "righteousness" he means the whole of virtue;[^3] as *in the saying*,[^4]

[^1]: Greek *περισσεύσῃ* (aorist subjunctive of *περισσεύω*), "abound, be over and above, exceed"; the comparative *πλέον … τῶν Γραμματέων* ("more than *that* of the scribes") makes it "abound beyond." The supplied "*that*" (standing for the scribes' and Pharisees' righteousness) is italicized.
[^2]: The folder metadata assigns this lemma to 5:19, but the quoted words are Matt. 5:20; the header verse is corrected. OCR «Ἀοκαιοσύνη» = *δικαιοσύνη* ("righteousness"), the word Theophylact glosses next.
[^3]: Greek *τὴν πᾶσαν ἀρετήν*, "all virtue / the whole of virtue"; Theophylact reads *δικαιοσύνη* here not in the narrow sense of the one virtue of justice but as comprehensive righteousness, the sum of the virtues.
[^4]: The lemma breaks off at *ὡς τὸ* ("as *in the saying*"), introducing a proof-text; the sentence is completed in the next lemma (31), which supplies the words «Δίκαιος ἦν Ἰὼβ, ὅσιος, ἄμεμπτος» ("Job was righteous, holy, blameless"; cf. Job 1:1). The supplied "*in the saying*" is italicized.
