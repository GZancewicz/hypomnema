# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 44 · Matt. 5:31

> **And it was said, that: Whoever shall put away his wife, let him give her a bill of divorcement.[^1] (Matt. 5:31)**

Moses commanded, if anyone should hate his wife, to be separated from her, lest something worse should happen; for, being hated, perhaps she would even have been murdered. And *he commanded* to give to her who was divorced a document of release, which was called *apostasion*;[^1] so that[^2] she might no longer come back to him, and from this a confusion arise, the man living together with another *woman*.

[^1]: Greek *ἀποστάσιον* — the technical term for the Jewish writ of divorce (Deut. 24:1, LXX «βιβλίον ἀποστασίου»). Rendered "bill of divorcement" in the lemma and left transliterated where Theophylact names the term itself («ὃ ἐκαλεῖτο ἀποστάσιον», "which was called *apostasion*").
[^2]: The OCR reads «ὡς λυ μηκέτι ἐπ᾽ αὐτὸν ἐπανέλθοι» — the «λυ» after «ὡς» is corrupt and untranslatable; the connective sense ("so that she might no longer return to him") is clear and is rendered on the strength of «μηκέτι … ἐπανέλθοι». The garbled syllable is not represented.
