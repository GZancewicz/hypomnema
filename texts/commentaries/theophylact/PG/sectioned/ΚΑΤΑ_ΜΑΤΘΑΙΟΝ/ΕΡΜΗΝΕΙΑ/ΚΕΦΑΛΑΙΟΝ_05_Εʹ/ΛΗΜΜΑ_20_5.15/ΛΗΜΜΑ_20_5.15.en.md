# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 20 · Matt. 5:16

> **So let your light shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father who is in the heavens. (Matt. 5:16)**[^1]

He did not say that "You display *your* virtue"[^2] — for this is not good — but let that *virtue* alone shine, so that even your enemies may marvel and glorify, not you,[^3] but your Father. So that, if we practice virtue, we ought to practice this unto the glory of God, not unto our own.

[^1]: The folder-name metadata assigns this lemma to 5:15, but the Greek quotes and expounds Matt 5:16. The source has two OCR slips in the lemma: *Οθτω* = *Οὕτω* ("So/Thus") and *ἀιύθρώπων* = *ἀνθρώπων* ("men").
[^2]: Greek *Ὑμεῖς ἐπιδεικνύετε τὴν ἀρετήν*; the verb *ἐπιδείκνυμι* carries the sense of "show off, put on display," hence the point that self-display "*is* not good."
[^3]: Greek *οὐχ ὑμᾶς* ("not you"); the source drops the initial letter, reading OCR *οὐχ μᾶς*, securely = *οὐχ ὑμᾶς*.
