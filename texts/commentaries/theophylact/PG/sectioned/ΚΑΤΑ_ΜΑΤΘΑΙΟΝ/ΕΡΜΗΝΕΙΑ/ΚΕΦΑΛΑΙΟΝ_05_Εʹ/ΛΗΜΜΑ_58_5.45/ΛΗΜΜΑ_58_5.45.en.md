# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 58 · Matt. 5:45

> **That you may become sons of your Father who is in the heavens; for he makes his sun to rise upon evil and good, and rains upon just and unjust. (Matt. 5:45)**

Do you see how great a good he who hates you and spitefully uses you gives you, if only you are willing to endure? But by "rain" and "sun" understand also knowledge and teaching;[^1] for God enlightens and teaches all.

[^1]: Greek *Βροχὴν δὲ καὶ ἥλιον νόει, καὶ τὴν γνῶσιν καὶ τὴν διδασκαλίαν* — Theophylact reads the natural "rain" and "sun" allegorically as *γνῶσις* ("knowledge") and *διδασκαλία* ("teaching"), which God dispenses to all alike. The OCR "οιδασκαλίαν" is *διδασκαλίαν*.
