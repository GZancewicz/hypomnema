# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 24 · Matt. 5:17

> **"Do not murder;" (Matt. 5:21; cf. Ex. 20:13)**[^1]

this one *set forth* the: "Neither be angry without cause."[^2] Just as also the painter[^3] does not destroy the outline-sketch, but rather fills it up.

[^1]: The sentence begun at the end of the previous lemma (23), "That one *set forth* the:", runs into this quotation. Greek «Μὴ φονεύσῃς» ("Do not murder") — the sixth commandment (Ex 20:13 / Deut 5:17), quoted by Christ at Matt 5:21. The verb *φονεύω* means specifically "murder," not general killing.
[^2]: Greek *οὗτος, τὸ Μηδὲ θυμωθῇς εἰκῆ* — "this one [Christ], the: 'Neither be angry without cause.'" A paraphrase of Matt 5:22 (*ὁ ὀργιζόμενος τῷ ἀδελφῷ αὐτοῦ εἰκῆ*, "whoever is angry with his brother without cause"). The adverb *εἰκῆ* = "without cause, rashly, in vain." *ἐκεῖνος*/*οὗτος* continue to denote the law and Christ respectively (see lemma 23).
[^3]: Greek *ὁ ζωγράφος* ("the painter"), resuming the painting metaphor of the previous lemma: Christ does not abolish (*οὐ καταλύει*) the shadow-sketch (*σκιαγραφία*) but rather fills it up (*ἀναπληροῖ*) — mirroring "I came not to destroy but to fulfill" (5:17).
