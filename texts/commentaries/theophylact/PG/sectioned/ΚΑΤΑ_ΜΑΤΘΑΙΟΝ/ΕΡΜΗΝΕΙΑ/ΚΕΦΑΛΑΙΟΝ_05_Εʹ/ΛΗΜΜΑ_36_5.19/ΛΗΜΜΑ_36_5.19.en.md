# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 36 · Matt. 5:22

> **But whoever shall say to his brother, "Raca," shall be liable to the council. (Matt. 5:22)**[^1]

By "council" he means the tribunal of the Hebrews. And the *word*—[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Ὃς δ᾽ ἂν εἴπῃ τῷ ἀδελφῷ αὐτοῦ, Ῥακά, ἔνοχος ἔσται τῷ συνεδρίῳ». OCR *συνεδοίῳ* = *συνεδρίῳ* ("council," the Sanhedrin). *Ῥακά* is an Aramaic term of contempt, left untranslated (as Matthew himself leaves it); its force is explained in the next lemma.
[^2]: The sentence breaks off at *Τὸ δὲ* ("And the *word*" / "But as for the *term*"); lemma 37 completes it: "«Raca» signifies, in place of 'you' …," comparing it to a dismissive way of addressing someone held in contempt.
