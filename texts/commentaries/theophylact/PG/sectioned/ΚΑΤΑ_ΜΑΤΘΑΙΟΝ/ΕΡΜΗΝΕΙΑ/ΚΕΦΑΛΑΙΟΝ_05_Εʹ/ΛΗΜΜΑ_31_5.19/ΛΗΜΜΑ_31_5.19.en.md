# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 31 · Matt. 5:20

> **Righteous was Job, holy, blameless.**[^1]

Shudder therefore, O man, having considered how much is required of us. Then he teaches us how we might abound;[^2] and he enumerates the virtues.

[^1]: This lemma continues, mid-sentence, the exposition of Matt. 5:20 begun in lemma 30, which ended: "He calls 'righteousness' the whole *of* virtue; as *in* the *saying*:" — whereupon this Job citation follows. Greek «Δίκαιος ἦν Ἰὼβ, ὅσιος, ἄμεμπτος.» It is a loose quotation of Job 1:1, where the LXX has *ἄμεμπτος, δίκαιος, ἀληθινός, θεοσεβής* ("blameless, righteous, true, God-fearing"); Theophylact abbreviates and substitutes *ὅσιος* ("holy / devout"). Translated as it stands, per the rule of following the text on the page. The header verse (Matt. 5:20) is what the exposition expounds; the folder-name verse (5.19) is corrected accordingly.
[^2]: Greek *περιττεύσωμεν*, deliberately echoing the *περισσεύσῃ* ("exceed / abound") of the lemma at Matt. 5:20 ("except your righteousness abound more than *that* of the scribes and Pharisees"). The same root ties the "abounding" of virtue back to the Gospel word.
