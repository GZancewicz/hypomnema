# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 3 · Matt. 5:2

> **And having opened his mouth.**

For what reason is there appended the *phrase*,[^1]

[^1]: The lemma breaks off mid-sentence at «Διὰ τί πρόσκειται τὸ,» ("For what reason is there appended the *phrase*,"); the question is completed in the next lemma, which supplies the phrase in question, «Ἀνοίξας τὸ στόμα αὐτοῦ;» ("'Having opened his mouth'?"). *πρόσκειται* = "is added / is appended / lies in addition to."
