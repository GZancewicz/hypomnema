# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 6 · Matt. 5:3

> **Blessed *are* the poor in spirit, for theirs is the kingdom of the heavens. (Matt. 5:3)**[^1]

He lays down humility beforehand as a foundation.[^2] For since Adam fell from pride, through humility Christ sets us upright again; for Adam hoped to become God. And those who are crushed in soul, these *are* the poor in spirit.[^3]

[^1]: *μακάριοι* = "blessed"; the copula "*are*" is supplied. *ἡ βασιλεία τῶν οὐρανῶν* is rendered literally "the kingdom of the heavens" (plural *οὐρανῶν*), Matthew's characteristic idiom.
[^2]: Greek *προκαταβάλλεται ... ὥσπερ θεμέλιον* (OCR «προκαταάλλεται»): the middle-voice verb means "he lays down / casts down beforehand," as one lays a foundation-stone first; *ταπεινοφροσύνη* = "humility / lowly-mindedness." *ἀνορθοῖ* (below) = "sets upright again, raises up straight."
[^3]: Theophylact glosses «πτωχοὶ τῷ πνεύματι» ("poor in the spirit," dative of respect) by «οἱ συντετριμμένοι τῇ ψυχῇ» ("those crushed/broken in soul") — i.e. he reads the phrase not of material poverty but of humility and contrition. *συντετριμμένος* ("crushed, contrite") echoes the LXX language of «καρδία συντετριμμένη» (Ps. 50:19 LXX). The dative *τῷ πνεύματι* could also be construed "poor in respect of the spirit / voluntarily poor in spirit"; the plain "in spirit" is kept in the body.
