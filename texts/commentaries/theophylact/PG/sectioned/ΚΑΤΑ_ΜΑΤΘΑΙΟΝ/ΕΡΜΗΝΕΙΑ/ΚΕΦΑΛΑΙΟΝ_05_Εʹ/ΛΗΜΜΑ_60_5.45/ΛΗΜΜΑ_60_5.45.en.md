# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 60 · Matt. 5:47

> **And if you greet your friends only, what more do you do? Do not the tax-collectors also do so? (Matt. 5:47)**[^1]

[^1]: The folder metadata assigns this to 5:45, but the lemma is Matt. 5:47. Two genuine textual points: (a) where the common text (TR, Byzantine) reads *τοὺς ἀδελφοὺς ὑμῶν* ("your brethren"), Theophylact's text reads *τοὺς φίλους ὑμῶν* ("your friends"), followed here; (b) for the concluding *οἱ τελῶναι* ("the tax-collectors") some witnesses read *οἱ ἐθνικοί* ("the Gentiles"). Theophylact's wording is retained rather than conformed to any standard text.
