# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 2 · Matt. 5:1

> **And when he had sat down, his disciples came to him.**[^1]

The crowds, on the one hand, come for the sake of the miracles, but the disciples, on the other hand, for the sake of the teachings; whence also, after completing the miracles and healing the bodies, he heals the souls as well; that we might learn that he himself is *the* creator both of souls and of bodies.

[^1]: The folder-name metadata assigns this lemma to Matt. 5:2, but «Καὶ καθίσαντος αὐτοῦ προσῆλθον αὐτῷ οἱ μαθηταὶ αὐτοῦ» is Matt. 5:1; corrected here. *καθίσαντος αὐτοῦ* is a genitive absolute, literally "he having sat down"; rendered "when he had sat down" for readable English while keeping the temporal force.
