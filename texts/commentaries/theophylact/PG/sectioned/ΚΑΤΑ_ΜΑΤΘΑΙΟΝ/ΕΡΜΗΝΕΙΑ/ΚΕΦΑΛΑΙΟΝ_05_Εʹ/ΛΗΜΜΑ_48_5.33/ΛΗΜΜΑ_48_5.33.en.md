# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 48 · Matt. 5:34–35 (continuation)

"The heaven is my throne, and the earth a footstool"[^1] — they used to swear by such things. The Lord, therefore, forbidding them,[^2] does not say that: "Since the heaven is beautiful and great, and the earth useful, on this account do not swear"; but that the one is *the* throne of God, and the other a footstool — in order that idolatry might not have room. For the elements would have been made into gods by those swearing by them; which also happened formerly.
