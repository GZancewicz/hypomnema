# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 12 · Matt. 5:10

> **Blessed *are* they that have been persecuted for the sake of righteousness, for theirs is the kingdom of the heavens. (Matt. 5:10)**

Not the martyrs only are persecuted, but also many for the sake of helping those who are wronged, and, simply, *for the sake* of every virtue; for righteousness *is* the whole of virtue.[^1] Since thieves also and murderers are persecuted, but *are* not blessed.[^2]

[^1]: Greek δικαιοσύνη γάρ, ἡ πᾶσα ἀρετή — "for the whole of virtue *is* righteousness (justice)." δικαιοσύνη is taken in its comprehensive sense, embracing all virtue, which is why persecution "for the sake of righteousness" covers persecution for any virtuous cause.
[^2]: The clause supplies the implicit qualification of the beatitude: persecution as such does not confer blessedness — it must be "for the sake of righteousness," which the persecution of thieves and murderers is not. Ἐπεί here is "since / for," introducing the confirming instance.
