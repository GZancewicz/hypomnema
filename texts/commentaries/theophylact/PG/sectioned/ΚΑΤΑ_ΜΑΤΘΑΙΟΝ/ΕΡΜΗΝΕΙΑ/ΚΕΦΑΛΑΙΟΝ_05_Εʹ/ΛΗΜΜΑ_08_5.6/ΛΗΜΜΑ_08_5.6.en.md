# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 8 · Matt. 5:6

> **Blessed are they that hunger and thirst *after* righteousness, for they shall be filled. (Matt. 5:6)**[^1]

Since he is about to speak concerning almsgiving, he shows first that it is necessary to pursue righteousness, and not to show mercy out of plunder; and that it is necessary to pursue righteousness with all desire; for the *phrase*,[^2]

[^1]: Theophylact quotes with the accusative object τὴν δικαιοσύνην ("hungering and thirsting *the* righteousness"), where the common text has the genitive τῆς δικαιοσύνης. The supplied "*after*" renders the object relation the Greek carries by case; "righteousness" (δικαιοσύνη) here also carries the sense "justice / right dealing," which the comment exploits ("not to show mercy out of plunder").
[^2]: The sentence breaks off at τὸ γὰρ ("for the…") and is completed in the next lemma, which supplies the quoted words «Πεινῶντες καὶ διψῶντες» ("Hungering and thirsting") — "for the *phrase* 'hungering and thirsting' signifies this."
