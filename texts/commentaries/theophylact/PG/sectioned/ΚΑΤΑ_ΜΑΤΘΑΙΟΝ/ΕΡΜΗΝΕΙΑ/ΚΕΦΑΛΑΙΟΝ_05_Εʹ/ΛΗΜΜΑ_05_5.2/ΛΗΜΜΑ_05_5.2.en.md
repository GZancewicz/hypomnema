# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 5 · Matt. 5:2

> **He was teaching them, saying.**[^1]

Not the disciples only, but also the crowd; and he begins from the beatitudes, just as David also began from a beatitude,[^2]

[^1]: The OCR reads «Ἐλί.δασκεν αὐτοὺς, λέγων», securely = «Ἐδίδασκεν αὐτοὺς, λέγων» ("He was teaching them, saying") — the close of Matt. 5:2, completing the sentence broken off across the preceding two lemmata. Imperfect *ἐδίδασκεν* rendered "was teaching."
[^2]: The reference is to Psalm 1:1 LXX, «Μακάριος ἀνήρ...» ("Blessed *is* the man..."), with which the Psalter opens. *μακαρισμός* ("beatitude / blessing-pronouncement") is cognate with *μακάριος* ("blessed"), the word beginning each of the beatitudes that follow. The sentence trails off with a comma in the source and its thought is taken up in the next lemma, which quotes the first beatitude.
