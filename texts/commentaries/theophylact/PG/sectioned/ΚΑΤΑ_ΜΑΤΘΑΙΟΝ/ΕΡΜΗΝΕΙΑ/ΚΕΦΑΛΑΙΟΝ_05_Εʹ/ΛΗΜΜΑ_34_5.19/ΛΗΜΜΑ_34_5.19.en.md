# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 34 · Matt. 5:21

> **"It was said to the ancients:"**[^1]

But since it has now, moreover, grown old, and is near to vanishing away,[^2] one must let it go and run to the new *things*,[^3]

[^1]: Greek «Ἐρρέθη τοῖς ἀρχαίοις», the same phrase from Matt. 5:21 that lemma 33 began to expound; the sectioning repeats the Gospel tag here.
[^2]: Greek *ἐπεὶ δὲ λοιπὸν ἐπαλαιώθη, καὶ ἐγγύς ἐστιν ἀφανισμοῦ* — echoing Heb. 8:13 (*τὸ δὲ παλαιούμενον καὶ γηράσκον ἐγγὺς ἀφανισμοῦ*, "that which is being made old and is aging *is* near to vanishing away"). *ἀφανισμός* = "disappearance, abolition, vanishing." *λοιπὸν* rendered "moreover / now."
[^3]: In the source the guillemets enclose this whole clause, but only the opening tag "It was said to the ancients" is the Gospel quotation; the remainder ("but since it has grown old … run to the new things") is Theophylact's own gloss, drawing on Heb. 8:13, and is rendered here as exposition. *αὐτὸν* ("it") refers to the law (masc. *ὁ νόμος*). The sentence trails off; the argument continues in the following lemma.
