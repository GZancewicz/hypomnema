# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 57 · Matt. 5:44

> **Bless those that curse you, do good to those that hate you, and pray for those that spitefully use you and persecute you. (Matt. 5:44)**[^1]

For *you* must hold them as benefactors. For everyone that persecutes and tries[^2] *us* lightens for us the punishment for our sins. And besides, *this is so* especially when God gives us so great a prize.[^3] For hear:[^4]

[^1]: This lemma continues the citation of Matt. 5:44 begun in the previous lemma. In the OCR the article *τοὺς* appears as "τυος" and *ἐπηρεάζοντας* as "ἐπηρεαζόντον"; both are securely recoverable. Greek *ἐπηρεάζοντας* means "those who insult, threaten, or maliciously mistreat"; rendered "that spitefully use you."
[^2]: Greek *πειράζων* — "tempts, tests, tries"; here of the persecutor who puts the believer to the proof, so "tries" (i.e., subjects to trial).
[^3]: Greek *τηλικοῦτον ἔπαθλον* — "so great a prize" (*ἔπαθλον*, the prize awarded in a contest). "*this is so*" is supplied to complete the elliptical *Ἄλλως τε, καὶ ὅταν…* ("And besides, *it is so* also when…"). The OCR "Δ Θεός" stands for *ὁ Θεός*, "God."
[^4]: Greek *Ἄκουε γάρ* ("For hear"); the sentence breaks off and introduces the scriptural words quoted in the next lemma (Matt. 5:45).
