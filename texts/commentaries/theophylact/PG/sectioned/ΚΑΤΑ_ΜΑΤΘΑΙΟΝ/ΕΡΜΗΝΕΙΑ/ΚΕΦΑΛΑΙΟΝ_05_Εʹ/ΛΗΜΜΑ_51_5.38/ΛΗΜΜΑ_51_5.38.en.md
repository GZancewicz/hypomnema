# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 51 · Matt. 5:38

> **Ye have heard that it was said, An eye for an eye, and a tooth for a tooth.** (Matt. 5:38)

The law, condescending, permitted retaliation in kind,[^1] so that by fear of suffering the same things they might not wrong one another.

[^1]: Greek ταυτοπάθεια, literally "the suffering of the same thing" — repayment of a like injury, the *lex talionis*. Rendered "retaliation in kind"; one might also render "like-for-like suffering." The verb συγκαταβαίνων ("condescending, coming down to *their* level") frames the concession as an accommodation to a hard people, not an ideal.
