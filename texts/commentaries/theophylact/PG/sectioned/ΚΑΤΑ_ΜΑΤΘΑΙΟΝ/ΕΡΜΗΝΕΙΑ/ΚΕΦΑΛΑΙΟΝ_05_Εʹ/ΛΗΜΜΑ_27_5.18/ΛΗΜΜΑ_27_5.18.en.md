# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 27 · Matt. 5:19

> **Whoever, therefore, shall loose[^1] one of the[^2]**

*This lemma carries only the opening words of the verse and breaks off; it has no exposition of its own. The sentence is completed, and the comment supplied, in the following lemma (28), which continues:* "…of these least commandments, and shall teach men so, shall be called least in the kingdom of the heavens."[^3]

[^1]: The folder metadata assigns this lemma to 5:18, but the quoted words «Ὃς ἐάν οὖν λύσῃ μίαν τῶν» begin Matt. 5:19; the header verse is corrected. Greek *λύσῃ*, aorist subjunctive of *λύω*, "loose / dissolve / annul / break," here of setting aside a commandment; rendered "loose" to keep the literal image (contrast "bind"). The particle *οὖν* ("therefore") is present and rendered in roman.
[^2]: The Greek breaks off at «μίαν τῶν» ("one of the"), the genitive plural article left dangling before its noun *ἐντολῶν* ("commandments"), which opens the next lemma.
[^3]: See Lemma 28 for the completed sentence and Theophylact's exposition.
