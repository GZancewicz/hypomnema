# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 25 · Matt. 5:18

> **For amen I say to you.**[^1]

The "amen" is confirmatory,[^2] equivalent to "Yes, I say to you."

[^1]: The folder metadata assigns this lemma to 5:17, but the Greek quoted, «Ἀμὴν γὰρ λέγω ὑμῖν», opens Matt. 5:18 (the verse Theophylact goes on to expound in the following lemmata); the header verse is corrected accordingly. The particle *γάρ* ("for") is present in the Greek and so is rendered in roman.
[^2]: Greek *βεβαιωτικόν*, "confirmatory / serving to confirm or make firm," from *βεβαιόω* ("to confirm, make secure"). The word *Ἀμήν* is here discussed as a word and so retained in Greek; Theophylact glosses it not as a mere solemn opener but as an affirmation, *Ναί* ("Yes").
