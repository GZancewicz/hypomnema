# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 1 · Matt. 5:1

> **But having seen the crowds, he went up into the mountain.**[^1]

Teaching us to do nothing for display,[^2] he goes up into the mountain; for since he was about to teach, he instructs us how to withdraw in *the* midst of tumults, whenever we teach.[^3]

[^1]: The folder-name metadata assigns this lemma to Matt. 5:2, but the Greek «Ἰδὼν δὲ τοὺς ὄχλους, ἀνέβη εἰς τὸ ὄρος» is Matt. 5:1; corrected here. *ἰδὼν* is an aorist participle, kept as a participle ("having seen"); *δὲ* rendered "but/and" (here "But"). *τοὺς ὄχλους* = "the crowds/multitudes," glossed consistently as "crowds."
[^2]: Greek *κατ᾿ ἐπίδειξιν* — "for display / for show / ostentatiously." The sense is doing a thing so as to be seen.
[^3]: The OCR reads «παιδεύει ἡμλς πῶν ἐν μέσῳ ἀναχωρεῖν θορύβων», securely = «παιδεύει ἡμᾶς πῶς ... ἐν μέσῳ θορύβων ἀναχωρεῖν» ("he instructs us how to withdraw in the midst of tumults"); likewise «ἔμαλλε» = *ἔμελλε* ("was about to"). The reconstructions are certain and low-stakes.
