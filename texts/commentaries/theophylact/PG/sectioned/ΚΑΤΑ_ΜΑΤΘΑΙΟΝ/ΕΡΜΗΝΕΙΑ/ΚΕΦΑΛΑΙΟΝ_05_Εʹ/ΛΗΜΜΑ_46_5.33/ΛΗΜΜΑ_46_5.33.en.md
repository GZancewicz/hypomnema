# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 46 · Matt. 5:33

> **Again you have heard, that it was said to the ancients: You shall not swear falsely,[^1] but you shall render to the Lord your oaths.[^2] (Matt. 5:33)**

That is, When you swear, speak the truth.

[^1]: Greek *οὐκ ἐπιορκήσεις* — "you shall not perjure yourself / forswear yourself," i.e., not swear a false oath (as distinct from a prohibition of oaths altogether, which comes only in the Lord's own words below). Rendered "swear falsely."
[^2]: Greek *ἀποδώσεις … τοὺς ὅρκους σου* — "you shall pay back / discharge your oaths," i.e., perform the vows you have sworn. Rendered "render … your oaths" to keep the verb *ἀποδίδωμι* ("pay what is owed") visible.
