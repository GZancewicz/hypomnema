# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 13 · Matt. 5:11

> **Blessed are ye when they shall revile you and persecute *you*. (Matt. 5:11)**[^1]

To them now[^2]—the apostles themselves—he directs *his discourse*, showing that it is most especially proper to teachers to be reviled.

[^1]: Greek «Μακάριοί ἐστε ὅταν ὀνειδίσωσιν ὑμᾶς καὶ διώξωσιν». The verb is present ("ye *are* blessed"), and here the Beatitudes shift from the third person ("Blessed *are* the poor…") into the second person ("Blessed are *ye*")—precisely the point Theophylact seizes on. The verb *διώξωσιν* ("they persecute") is left without an object; "*you*" is supplied.
[^2]: Greek *λοιπόν*, a transitional particle here—"now / henceforth / for the rest"; rendered "now" to mark the turn to direct address.
