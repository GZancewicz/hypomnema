# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 4 · Matt. 5:2

> **"Having opened his mouth"?**[^1]

For it seems that it is superfluous. But no; for even without opening his mouth he was teaching. How? Through *his* life and *his* miracles; but now, opening his mouth, he teaches.

[^1]: This lemma resumes the question begun in the previous lemma («Διὰ τί πρόσκειται τὸ...»): why does the Evangelist add the phrase «Ἀνοίξας τὸ στόμα αὐτοῦ;» ("Having opened his mouth")? The quoted words are here treated as the phrase under discussion, hence the interrogative punctuation. *φαίνεται γὰρ, ὅτι περιττόν ἐστιν* = "for it seems that it is superfluous / redundant."
