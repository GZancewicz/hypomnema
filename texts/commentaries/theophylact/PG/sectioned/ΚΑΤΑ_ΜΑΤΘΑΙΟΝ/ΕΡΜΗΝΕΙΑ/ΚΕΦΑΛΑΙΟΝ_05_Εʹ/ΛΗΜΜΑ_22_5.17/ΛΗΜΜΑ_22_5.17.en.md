# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 22 · Matt. 5:17

> **That the thing spoken through the prophet might be fulfilled. (cf. Matt. 1:22)**[^1]

But he fulfilled also all the commandments of the law.[^2]

[^1]: This is not a saying of Matt 5:17 but the evangelist's own recurring fulfillment-formula (Matt 1:22; 2:15; 2:23; 4:14, etc.), here quoted by Theophylact as the instance of what, per the previous lemma, "the evangelist frequently says." Greek «Ἵνα πληρωθῇ τὸ ῥηθὲν διὰ τοῦ προφήτου.» The lemma continues to expound Matt 5:17 (how Christ fulfilled the law and prophets).
[^2]: Greek *Ἀλλὰ καὶ τὰς τοῦ νόμου ἐντολὰς πάσας ἐπλήρωσεν* — the *καὶ* ("also") marks the second mode of fulfillment, beyond the fulfilling of prophecy just treated: not only the prophets' words but the law's commandments.
