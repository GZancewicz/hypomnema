# Matthew 5 (Εʹ) — Lemma 10 · Matt. 5:7

> **Blessed *are* the merciful, for they shall obtain mercy. (Matt. 5:7)**

It is possible to show mercy not through money only, but also through a word; and even if you should have nothing, through tears. And they shall be shown mercy even here, from men; for he who *was* merciful yesterday, if he should fall into need today, shall be shown mercy by all; and there, on the other hand, by God to a greater degree.
