# Matthew 28 (ΚΗʹ) — Lemma 11 · Matt. 28:12

> **having been gathered together with the elders, and having taken counsel, they gave sufficient[^1] pieces of silver to the soldiers,**[^2]

*This lemma carries only the scripture text; Theophylact adds no exposition here. The quotation continues, and its comment follows, in the lemmata below.*

[^1]: Greek *ἀργύρια ἱκανά*, "sufficient / a considerable amount of silver pieces" — *ἱκανά* = "enough (to serve the purpose), a good deal." Rendered "sufficient," as in the common text of Matt. 28:12.
[^2]: The clause is grammatically continuous with the previous lemma (the chief priests being the subject of "gathered… took counsel… gave") and runs on into the next lemma («λέγοντες·…», Matt. 28:13).
