# Matthew 28 (ΚΗʹ) — Lemma 20 · Matt. 28:18

*This folder carries no Gospel lemma at its head; it continues, mid-sentence, the exposition of "All authority has been given to me" (Matt. 28:18) begun in Lemma 19, opening with a Davidic proof-text.*[^1]

"For all things are your servants,"[^2] David says to God — but they did not have the voluntary subjection. "Now, therefore, I am about to have this also; for all things will be made subject to me, since through the cross I conquered him who has the power of death." For subjection is twofold: the one involuntary, in that we are all servants of God even unwillingly, just as also *are* the demons; but the other voluntary, in the manner in which[^3] Paul was a servant of Christ. Formerly, on the one hand, therefore, when all had only the involuntary subjection, the Saviour had the authority over all *only* by half, as it were; but after the cross, the knowledge of God having been spread out to all, and all having been made subject with the voluntary subjection *also*, fittingly Christ says that "Now I have received all the authority." "For formerly the authority belonged to me partially, inasmuch as *men* served me only involuntarily, in that I am *their* Maker; but now, *since* men serve me with recognition also, all and entire authority has been given to me."
