# Matthew 28 (ΚΗʹ) — Lemma 22 · Matt. 28:18

> **All authority has been given to me in heaven;**[^1] (Matt. 28:18)

for indeed human nature, which formerly was in bondage, now in Christ rules over all. To put it concisely,[^2] then, understand thus the *saying*:[^3]

[^1]: Greek «Ἐδόθη μοι πᾶσα ἐξουσία ἐν οὐρανῷ·» — Matt. 28:18, re-quoted (as in Lemmata 21, 23, 24) as the peg for a further gloss. Theophylact cites only «…ἐν οὐρανῷ» ("in heaven"), without the common text's continuation «καὶ ἐπὶ γῆς» ("and on earth"); his wording is followed. The folder-name verse (28.20) is a placeholder; the Greek expounds 28:18.

[^2]: Greek *συνελόντι … φάναι* (for *φᾶναι* in the OCR), the fixed idiom *συνελόντι εἰπεῖν/φάναι*, "to speak in brief, to sum up" (literally "for one having gathered *it* together to say"). *οὖν* ("then/therefore") is present in the Greek and rendered roman.

[^3]: The sentence breaks off at *οὕτω νόει τὸ* ("understand thus the *saying* / *word*"); the article *τὸ* points forward to the quotation re-cited in Lemma 23. The supplied noun after "the" is italicized.
