# Matthew 28 (ΚΗʹ) — Lemma 10 · Matt. 28:11

> **But as they were going, behold, some of the guard,[^1] having come into the city, reported to the chief priests all the things *that had* happened; and**[^2]

*This lemma carries only the scripture text; Theophylact adds no exposition here. The quotation continues, and its comment follows, in the lemmata below.*

[^1]: Greek *τῆς κουστωδίας*, a transliterated Latin loanword, *custodia* ("the watch, the guard"), as also at Matt. 27:65-66. Rendered "the guard" for sense; the word is a Latinism in the Greek itself.
[^2]: The quotation breaks off at *καί* ("and"), continued at the head of the next lemma (Matt. 28:12).
