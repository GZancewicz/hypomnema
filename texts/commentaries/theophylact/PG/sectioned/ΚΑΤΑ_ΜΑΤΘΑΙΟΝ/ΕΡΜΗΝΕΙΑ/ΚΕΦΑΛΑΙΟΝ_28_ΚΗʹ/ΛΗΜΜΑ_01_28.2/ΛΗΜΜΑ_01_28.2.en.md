# Matthew 28 (ΚΗʹ) — Lemma 1 · Matt. 28:2–3

> **… a great earthquake occurred; for an angel of *the* Lord, having descended from heaven, having come forward, rolled away the stone from the door, and was sitting upon it; and his appearance was like lightning,[^1] and his garment *was* white as snow. And from the … (Matt. 28:2–3)**

*This lemma folder carries only the running Gospel citation, with no exposition of its own. Theophylact quotes the resurrection narrative continuously across Lemmata 1–5, breaking it off mid-phrase at each folder boundary (here at "And from the …," which is completed by "fear of it" at the head of Lemma 2). His commentary proper begins at Lemma 5.*

[^1]: The OCR reads «ἦν δὲ ἡ ἰδέα αὐτοῦ ὡς ἀσέραπὴ»; the corrupt word is securely **«ἀστραπή»** ("lightning"), i.e. «ὡς ἀστραπή» ("like lightning"), the reading of Matt. 28:3. *ἰδέα* is rendered "appearance / form."
