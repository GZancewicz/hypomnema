# Matthew 28 (ΚΗʹ) — Lemma 3 · Matt. 28:6

> **…*He is* not here; for he is risen, as he said. Come, see the place where the Lord lay; and quickly…**[^1] (Matt. 28:6)

This lemma carries only the scripture quotation — the angel's words at the tomb (Matt. 28:6). It has no exposition of its own; the sentence begun here runs on into Lemma 4.

[^1]: Greek «…ἔστιν ὧδε· ἠγέρθη γὰρ, καθὼς εἶπε. Δεῦτε, ἴδετε τὸν τόπον ὅπου ἔκειτο ὁ Κύριος· καὶ ταχὺ». The negation *οὐκ* ("not") belongs to the clause **οὐκ ἔστιν ὧδε** ("he is not here"), whose opening was carried in the previous lemma; supplied here in brackets for sense. The OCR reads *εἴδετε* for the imperative **ἴδετε** ("see"); reconstruction certain. Theophylact's text reads «ὅπου ἔκειτο ὁ Κύριος» ("where **the Lord** lay") — his wording is followed, rather than the shorter "where he lay." The lemma breaks off at *καὶ ταχὺ* ("and quickly"), completed in Lemma 4.
