# Matthew 28 (ΚΗʹ) — Lemma 16 · Matt. 28:17

> **…having seen him, they worshipped him; but some doubted. And…[^1] (Matt. 28:17)**

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text, with no exposition of its own; Theophylact treats the doubting at 28:20 (Lemma 19), where he asks who the doubters were.*[^2]

[^1]: Greek OCR «ἰδόντες αὐτὸν, προσεκύνησαν αὐτῷ· οἱ δὲ ἐδί ατασαν. Καὶ» — the garbled «ἐδί ατασαν» is securely *ἐδίστασαν* ("they doubted / hesitated / wavered," aorist of *διστάζω*). *οἱ δὲ ἐδίστασαν* — "but some doubted": *οἱ δὲ* here is partitive, "but some / but others" (of the group), which Theophylact himself discusses at Lemma 19 (*ἀντὶ τοῦ, τινὲς δὲ ἐδίστασαν*, "in the sense of, 'but some doubted'"). *προσεκύνησαν* = "worshipped / did obeisance / prostrated." The lemma breaks off at «Καὶ» ("And"), completed in the next lemma.
[^2]: Editorial note: the file contains no exposition, only scripture.
