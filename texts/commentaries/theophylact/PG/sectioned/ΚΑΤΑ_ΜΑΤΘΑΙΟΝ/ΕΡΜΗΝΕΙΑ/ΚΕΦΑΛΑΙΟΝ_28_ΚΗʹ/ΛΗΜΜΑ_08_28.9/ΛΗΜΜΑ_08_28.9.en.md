# Matthew 28 (ΚΗʹ) — Lemma 8 · Matt. 28:9

> **But as they were going to announce *it* to his disciples, behold also, Jesus met them, saying: Rejoice.[^1] And they, having come to *him*, took hold of his feet, and worshipped him. Then**[^2]

*This lemma carries only the scripture text; Theophylact adds no exposition here. The comment follows in the next lemma, which completes the quotation and expounds it.*

[^1]: Greek *χαίρετε*, the ordinary Greek greeting ("Hail!"), but literally "Rejoice." Rendered "Rejoice" because Theophylact's comment in the next lemma turns on the word-play with *χαρά* ("joy") — the risen Lord procures *joy* for the women. English "Hail" would obscure this.
[^2]: The quotation breaks off at *Τότε* ("Then"), completed at the head of the following lemma («λέγει αὐταῖς ὁ Ἰησοῦς…», Matt. 28:10).
