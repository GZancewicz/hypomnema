# Matthew 28 (ΚΗʹ) — Lemma 15 · Matt. 28:16

> **But the eleven disciples went into Galilee, to the mountain where Jesus had appointed them; and…[^1] (Matt. 28:16)**

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text, with no exposition of its own; Theophylact expounds the whole final section (28:16–20) at 28:20 (Lemma 19).*[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Οἱ δὲ ἕνδεκα μαθηταὶ ἐπορεύθησαν εἰς τὴν Γαλιλαίαν, εἰς τὸ ὄρος οὗ ἐτάξατο αὐτοῖς ὁ Ἰησοῦς· καὶ». *ἐτάξατο* (aorist middle of *τάσσω*) — "appointed / arranged / ordered *for* them," the middle voice marking Jesus' own appointing of the place. The lemma breaks off at «καὶ» ("and"), completed in the next lemma, «ἰδόντες αὐτόν…» ("having seen him…").
[^2]: Editorial note: the file contains no exposition, only scripture.
