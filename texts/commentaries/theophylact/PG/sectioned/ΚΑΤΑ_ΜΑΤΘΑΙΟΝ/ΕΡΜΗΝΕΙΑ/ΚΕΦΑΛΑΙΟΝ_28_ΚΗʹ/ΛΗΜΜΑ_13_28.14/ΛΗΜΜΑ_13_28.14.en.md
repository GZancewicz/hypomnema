# Matthew 28 (ΚΗʹ) — Lemma 13 · Matt. 28:14

> **…*should* this be heard before the governor, we will persuade him, and will make you free from care.[^1] But they,[^2] (Matt. 28:14)**

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text — the continuation of the chief priests' instruction to the soldiers — with no exposition of its own; Theophylact comments on the whole episode at 28:15 (Lemma 14).*[^3]

[^1]: The lemma opens mid-sentence: the conditional "and if" (καὶ ἐὰν) stands at the end of the previous lemma (28:13), so the clause here begins with the bare subjunctive verb *ἀκουσθῇ* ("be heard"). The modal "*should*" is supplied to carry the subjunctive into English and is italicized; the conditional force itself is genuinely present in the *ἐὰν* of the prior lemma. Full clause: «καὶ ἐὰν ἀκουσθῇ τοῦτο ἐπὶ τοῦ ἡγεμόνος». *ἀμερίμνους ποιήσομεν* — "we will make *you* free from care / without anxiety."
[^2]: The lemma breaks off at «Οἱ δὲ,» ("But they,"), which begins the next verse (28:15); the sentence is completed in the following lemma, «λαβόντες τὰ ἀργύρια…» ("having taken the pieces of silver…").
[^3]: Editorial note: the file contains no exposition, only scripture.
