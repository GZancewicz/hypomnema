# Matthew 28 (ΚΗʹ) — Lemma 18 · Matt. 28:19

> **…having gone, make disciples of all the nations, baptizing them into the name of the Father, and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,[^1] (Matt. 28:19)**

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text, with no exposition of its own; Theophylact's comment on the commission continues at 28:20 (Lemma 19).*[^2]

[^1]: Greek OCR «πορευθέντες μαθητεύσατε πάντα τὰ ἔθνη, βαπτίζοντες αὐτοὺς εἰς τὸ ὄνομα τοῦ Πατρὸς, καὶ τοῦ Υἱοῆ καὶ τοῦ ἀνίου Πνεύματος» — «Υἱοῆ» is securely *Υἱοῦ* ("of the Son") and «ἀνίου» is *ἁγίου* ("of the Holy"). *πορευθέντες* = "having gone / going"; *μαθητεύσατε* (aorist imperative of *μαθητεύω*) = "make disciples of / disciple," here transitive with *πάντα τὰ ἔθνη* ("all the nations") as its object. *εἰς τὸ ὄνομα* = "into the name."
[^2]: Editorial note: the file contains no exposition, only scripture.
