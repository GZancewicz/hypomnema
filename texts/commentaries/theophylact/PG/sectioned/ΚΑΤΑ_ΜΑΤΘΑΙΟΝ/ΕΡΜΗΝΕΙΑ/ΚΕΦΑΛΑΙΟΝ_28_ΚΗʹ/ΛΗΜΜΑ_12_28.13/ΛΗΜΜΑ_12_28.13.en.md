# Matthew 28 (ΚΗʹ) — Lemma 12 · Matt. 28:13

> **saying: Say ye that, His disciples, having come by night, stole him while we were sleeping; and if**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture text; Theophylact adds no exposition here. The quotation continues, and its comment follows, in the lemmata below.*

[^1]: The quotation breaks off at *καὶ ἐάν* ("and if"), completed at the head of the next lemma: *ἀκουσθῇ τοῦτο ἐπὶ τοῦ ἡγεμόνος…* ("if this be heard before the governor…," Matt. 28:14). The imperative *εἴπατε* ("say ye") introduces the false report the soldiers are instructed to spread.
