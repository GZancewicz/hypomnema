# Matthew 28 (ΚΗʹ) — Lemma 23 · Matt. 28:18

> **All authority has been given to me;**[^1] (Matt. 28:18)

if, on the one hand, you should take *it* as spoken from *the person of* God the Word,[^2] that[^3]

[^1]: Greek «Ἐδόθη μοι πᾶσα ἐξουσία·» — Matt. 28:18, again re-cited (cf. Lemmata 21, 22, 24) to introduce a two-sided exegesis. The folder-name verse (28.20) is a placeholder; the Greek expounds 28:18.

[^2]: Greek *εἰ μὲν ὡς ἀπὸ τοῦ Θεοῦ Λόγου λεγόμενον ἐκλάβοις* — "if, on the one hand, you should take *it* as spoken from God the Word." *ὡς ἀπὸ …* is Theophylact's idiom for a saying uttered "in the person of / as from the standpoint of" a given subject — here God the Word (the divine nature); the supplied "*the person of*" is italicized. *μέν* ("on the one hand") anticipates the answering *δέ* ("but, on the other hand") in Lemma 24, which sets the reading "from the human nature" against this one. *ἐκλάβοις* is the aorist optative, "you should take / understand."

[^3]: The clause breaks off at *ὅτι* ("that"); the completed thought — both the "God the Word" reading and its counterpart "from the human nature" — is given in Lemma 24.
