# Matthew 28 (ΚΗʹ) — Lemma 4 · Matt. 28:7

> **…having gone, tell his disciples that he is risen from the dead; and behold, he goes before you into Galilee. There shall ye see him; behold, I have told you. And…**[^1] (Matt. 28:7)

This lemma carries only the scripture quotation — the continuation of the angel's charge to the women (Matt. 28:7). It has no exposition of its own; the sentence runs on into Lemma 5.

[^1]: Greek «πορευθεῖσαι εἴπατε τοῖς μαθηταῖς αὐτοῦ, ὅτι ἠγέρθη ἀπὸ τῶν νεκρῶν, καὶ ἰδοὺ προάγει ὑμᾶς εἰς τὴν Γαλιλαίαν. Ἐκεῖ αὐτὸν ὄψεσθε· ἰδοὺ εἶπον ὑμῖν. Καὶ». *πορευθεῖσαι* is the aorist participle (feminine plural, the women), kept as a participle: "having gone." The OCR reads *ὄψεσθι* for **ὄψεσθε** ("ye shall see"); reconstruction certain. The lemma breaks off at *Καὶ* ("And"), completed in Lemma 5.
