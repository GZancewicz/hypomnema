# Matthew 14 (ΙΔʹ) — Lemma 3 · Matt. 14:4

> **For John was saying to him, It is not lawful for you to have her; and[^1]**

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text (a continuation of the quotation begun at Lemma 2); Theophylact's exposition follows at the close of Lemma 4.*

[^1]: The verse breaks off mid-sentence at *καὶ* ("and"), the clause being completed in Lemma 4 with «θέλων αὐτὸν ἀποκτεῖναι…» ("and *though* wishing to kill him…"). Greek «Οὐκ ἔξεστί σοι ἔχειν αὐτήν» — "It is not lawful for you to have her," rendered fresh from Theophylact's text.
