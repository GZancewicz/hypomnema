# Matthew 14 (ΙΔʹ) — Lemma 16 · Matt. 14:20

> **…they all ate and were filled, and they took up what was left over of the fragments, twelve baskets full. But the…[^1] (Matt. 14:20)**

This lemma carries only the Gospel words; Theophylact adds no exposition to it here. His comment on the whole miracle follows under the next lemma (Lemma 17), into which the quotation runs.[^2]

[^1]: The lemma is Matt. 14:20 and breaks off at «Οἱ δὲ» ("But the…"), which is completed in the following lemma with «ἐσθίοντες ἦσαν ἄνδρες…» ("those eating were men…", Matt. 14:21).
[^2]: A bare scripture-lemma with no attached commentary in the source.
