# Matthew 14 (ΙΔʹ) — Lemma 2 · Matt. 14:3

> **For Herod, having seized John, bound him and put *him* in prison on account of Herodias the wife of Philip his brother. (Matt. 14:3)**

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text; Theophylact's exposition of it (why Matthew recounts John's death at all, and the unlawfulness of the marriage) is deferred to the close of Lemma 4, where the Gospel quotation of vv. 3–5 is completed.*[^1]

[^1]: The quotation runs continuously across Lemmata 2–4 (Matt. 14:3–5) and is broken off mid-sentence here; it opens with *γὰρ* ("for"), continuing the narrative Herod began in vv. 1–2. The comment attaches only after the closing words «…ὡς προφήτην αὐτὸν εἶχον» at Lemma 4.
