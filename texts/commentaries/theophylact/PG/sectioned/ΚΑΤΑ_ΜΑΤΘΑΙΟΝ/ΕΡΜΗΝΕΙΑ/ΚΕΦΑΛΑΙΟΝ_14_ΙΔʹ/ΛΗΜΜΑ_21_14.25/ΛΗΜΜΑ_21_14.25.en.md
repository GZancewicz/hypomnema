# Matthew 14 (ΙΔʹ) — Lemma 21 · Matt. 14:25

> **And in the fourth watch of the night Jesus went off[^1] to them, walking upon the sea; and—**[^2]

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation; Theophylact's exposition follows once the quotation is completed in the next lemmata.*

[^1]: Greek *ἀπῆλθε πρὸς αὐτοὺς* — "went away/off to them." The common text has *ἦλθεν* ("came"); Theophylact's verb *ἀπέρχομαι* carries the prefix ἀπο- ("away from"), here paired with *πρὸς αὐτοὺς* ("toward them"), i.e. "set off toward them." Rendered as it stands rather than conformed to the usual "came."
[^2]: The quotation breaks off at *καὶ* ("and—") and continues in the next lemma: "…the disciples, having seen him walking upon the sea, were troubled…" (Matt. 14:26).
