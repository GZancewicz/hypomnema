# Matthew 14 (ΙΔʹ) — Lemma 4 · Matt. 14:5

> **and *though* wishing to kill him, he feared the crowd, because they held him as a prophet. (Matt. 14:5)**[^1]

Matthew did not make mention, in the preceding *passages*,[^2] of the narrative concerning John, because his aim was to write only the things concerning Christ. For indeed not even now would he have made mention *of it*, if this too did not bear upon Christ. And John was reproving Herod as holding unlawfully his brother's wife; for the law commanded the brother to take the brother's wife then, when that one should die childless. But here Philip did not die childless, for there was to him a child — the very *girl* who danced.[^3] But some say that, while Philip was still living, Herod took away both the wife and the tetrarchy. But whether this or that, the thing done was unlawful. And not fearing God, he *yet* feared the crowd, whence also he kept deferring the murder; but the devil found out for him a fitting occasion —[^4]

[^1]: This completes the Gospel quotation running from Lemma 2 (Matt. 14:3–5). The words are the tail of a single sentence begun there; the supplied "*though*" renders the concessive force the participle *θέλων* carries against *ἐφοβεῖτο* ("he *feared*").
[^2]: Greek «ἐν τοῖς προλαβοῦσι» ("in the things going before / earlier *passages*"). The OCR here is corrupt — «ἐν τοῖς πραΟΑΡΝΤΧΙΝλαβοῦσι» — but is securely recoverable as *προλαβοῦσι*; noted for consistency with the other flagged corruptions.
[^3]: Greek «Ἦν γὰρ αὐτῷ παῖς ἡ ὀρχησαμένη» — "for there was to him a *παῖς*, she who danced." *παῖς* here means "child / daughter" (the levirate provision applied only if the brother died *ἄπαις*, childless); the same word denoted "servants" at Lemma 1, and the recurrence is incidental to the argument. Rendered "child" with "*girl*" supplied for the dancer.
[^4]: The sentence breaks off mid-clause at «καιρὸν ἐπιτήδειον» ("a fitting occasion"); it is completed in Lemma 5, where the birthday-feast supplies the devil's occasion. Greek *ἐξεῦρεν* ("found out, devised, contrived").
