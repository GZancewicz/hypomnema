# Matthew 14 (ΙΔʹ) — Lemma 8 · Matt. 14:9

> **"And the king was grieved, but on account of the oaths and those reclining at table *with him* he commanded *it* to be given, and… (Matt. 14:9)**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture text, with no exposition of its own; Theophylact's comment on the beheading follows at Lemma 10.*[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Καὶ ἐλυπήθη ὁ βασιλεὺς, διὰ δὲ τοὺς ὅρκους καὶ τοὺς συνανακειμένους ἐκέλευσε δοθῆναι, καὶ». The OCR reads «καὶρὶς συνανακειμένους», securely = «καὶ τοὺς συνανακειμένους» ("and those reclining at table together *with him*," i.e. his fellow guests). The clause breaks off mid-sentence at «καὶ» ("and"), completed in the next lemma. The object of "to be given" (the head) is unexpressed in the Greek and supplied as "*it*."
[^2]: The Gospel narrative is here divided across several short lemmata (14:9, 14:10, 14:11–12); Theophylact reserves his exposition for the fuller lemma at Matt. 14:11–12 (Lemma 10).
