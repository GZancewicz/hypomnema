# Matthew 14 (ΙΔʹ) — Lemma 32 · Matt. 14:34

> **And having crossed over, they came into the land of Gennesaret. And**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the opening words of the Gospel text and no exposition; the scripture quotation continues through lemmata 33 and 34, and Theophylact's comment on the whole passage (Matt. 14:34–36) appears in lemma 34.*[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Καὶ διαπεράσαντες, ἦλθον εἰς τὴν γῆν Γεννησαρέτ. Καὶ» (Matt. 14:34); the OCR *Γεννησαρἕτ* is securely *Γεννησαρέτ*. The participle *διαπεράσαντες* ("having crossed over") is kept as a participle; the sentence breaks off at "And" (*Καὶ*), which is resumed in lemma 33.
[^2]: The verse-quotation runs across three lemma files (32 «...came into the land of Gennesaret. And»; 33 «having recognized him, the men of that place sent...and»; 34 «besought him, that they might only touch the hem...were saved»), after which Theophylact gives his exposition in lemma 34.
