# Matthew 14 (ΙΔʹ) — Lemma 9 · Matt. 14:10

> **…having sent, he beheaded John in the prison, and… (Matt. 14:10)**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture text, with no exposition of its own; it continues the sentence from the previous lemma and runs on into the next, where the comment begins.*[^2]

[^1]: Greek «πέμψας, ἀπεκεφάλισε τὸν Ἰωάννην ἐν τῇ φυλακῇ, καὶ». The participle *πέμψας* ("having sent") is kept as a participle; the sentence opens and closes mid-flow (from Lemma 8's "…and" and on to Lemma 10's "his head was brought…").
[^2]: See the note at Lemma 8: the narrative is split into short scripture units, with Theophylact's exposition gathered at the lemma for Matt. 14:11–12 (Lemma 10).
