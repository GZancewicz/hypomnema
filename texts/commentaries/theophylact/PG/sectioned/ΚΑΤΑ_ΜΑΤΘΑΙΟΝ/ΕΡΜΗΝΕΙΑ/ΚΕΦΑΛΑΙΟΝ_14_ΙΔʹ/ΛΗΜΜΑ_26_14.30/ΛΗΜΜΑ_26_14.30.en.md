# Matthew 14 (ΙΔʹ) — Lemma 26 · Matt. 14:30

> **But seeing the wind strong he was afraid;[^1] and having begun to sink, he cried out, saying: Lord, save me. (Matt. 14:30)**

The Lord spread the sea out beneath Peter,[^2] showing his own power. See how Peter, having prevailed over the greater thing — I mean the sea — feared the wind: so weak is human nature. And immediately, when he was afraid, then he began to sink; for when the faith weakened, then Peter is submerged. But this both made him not to be high-minded, and consoled the other disciples. For perhaps they envied him; but it also showed[^3] how much Christ surpasses him.

[^1]: Greek «βλέπων δὲ τὸν ἄνεμον ἰσχυρὸν ἐφοβήθη» (the OCR corruptions *ἐοοββθοη* and, below, *ἓφοβθθη* are both securely *ἐφοβήθη*, "he was afraid"). The participle *βλέπων* is kept as a participle ("seeing"); *δέ* rendered "but."
[^2]: Greek *ὑπέστρωσε … τὴν θάλασσαν* — from *ὑποστρώννυμι*, "to spread/strew under" (as one spreads a carpet or bedding beneath). The image is of the Lord laying the sea down as a firm floor beneath Peter's feet. Rendered "spread the sea out beneath," to keep the concrete metaphor rather than flattening to "made the sea firm."
[^3]: Greek *ἔδειξεν* (OCR *ἴαιξεν*), "showed." The subject is unexpressed and ambiguous: it may be the event just described ("this … also showed") continuing *τοῦτο δέ* above, or Christ himself. Rendered impersonally "it also showed"; the sense either way is that the sinking displayed the measure by which Christ exceeds Peter.
