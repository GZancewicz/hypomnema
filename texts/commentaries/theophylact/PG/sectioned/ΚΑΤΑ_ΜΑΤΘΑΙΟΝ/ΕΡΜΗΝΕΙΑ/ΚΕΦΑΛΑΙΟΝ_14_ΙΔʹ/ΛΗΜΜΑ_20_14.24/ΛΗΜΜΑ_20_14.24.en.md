# Matthew 14 (ΙΔʹ) — Lemma 20 · Matt. 14:24

> **…boat was already *in the* midst of the sea, being tormented[^1] by the waves; for the wind was contrary.**[^2]

Showing us that one must pray without distraction,[^3] he goes up into the mountain; for he does all things for our sake — for he himself did not have need of prayer. And until evening he prays, teaching us not to withdraw quickly from prayer, but rather to do this especially at night; for then *there is* much stillness. And he permits the disciples to be tossed by the surge,[^4] so that they might learn to bear trials nobly, and might recognize his power. But *he set* the boat also *in the* midst of the sea, so that the fear might be the greater.

[^1]: Greek *βασανιζόμενον* — properly "being tortured/tormented"; of the boat, "battered, buffeted, hard-pressed" by the waves. The vivid word is kept.
[^2]: OCR note: the source reads «ἣν γὰρ ἐναντίος ἃ ἄνεμος», securely = **«ἦν γὰρ ἐναντίος ὁ ἄνεμος»** ("for the wind was contrary"). Also «ἤθη» → «ἤδη» ("already"), «θαλάσοης» → «θαλάσσης».
[^3]: Greek *ἀπερισπάστως* — "undistractedly, without being drawn this way and that."
[^4]: Greek *κλυδωνισθῆναι* — "to be tossed by the billow/surge."
