# Matthew 14 (ΙΔʹ) — Lemma 22 · Matt. 14:26

> **…the disciples, having seen him walking upon the sea, were troubled, saying that it is a phantom;[^1] and from fear they cried out.**[^2]

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation (a continuation; the source has no opening quotation mark here); Theophylact's exposition begins once the quotation is completed in the next lemma.*

[^1]: Greek *φάντασμα* — "phantom, apparition, ghost." The OCR reads «θάντασμά», securely = «φάντασμά».
[^2]: The quotation runs on into the next lemma, which completes it: "…But immediately Jesus spoke to them, saying: Take courage, I am; do not be afraid" (Matt. 14:27), where the exposition begins.
