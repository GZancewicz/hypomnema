# Matthew 14 (ΙΔʹ) — Lemma 5 · Matt. 14:6

> **But when a birthday-feast[^1] was being held for Herod, the daughter of Herodias danced in the midst, and pleased Herod; whence with[^2]**

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text; Theophylact's exposition of the whole episode (vv. 6–8) follows at the close of Lemma 7.*

[^1]: Greek *γενεσίων* ("birthday celebrations / birthday-feast"). The OCR reads «Γκνεσίων», securely = *γενεσίων*; the plural is the neuter festival-noun, rendered as a singular "birthday-feast." The folder-name verse (14.5) is corrected to Matt. 14:6, which these words expound.
[^2]: Breaks off mid-phrase at *μεθ´* ("with"), completed in Lemma 6 as «μεθ´ ὅρκου» ("with an oath"). Greek «ὠρχήσατο ἡ θυγάτηρ τῆς Ἡρωδιάδος ἐν τῷ μέσῳ» — "the daughter of Herodias danced in the midst."
