# Matthew 14 (ΙΔʹ) — Lemma 13 · Matt. 14:17-18

> **But they say to him: We have not *anything* here, except five loaves and two fishes. But he said: Bring them to me here. And…[^1] (Matt. 14:17-18)**

This lemma carries only the Gospel words; Theophylact adds no exposition to it here. The comment on the reclining of the crowds and the blessing of the loaves follows under the next lemma (Lemma 14), into which the quotation runs.[^2]

[^1]: The lemma breaks off at «Καὶ» ("And"), the sentence being completed in the following lemma with «κελεύσας τοὺς ὄχλους ἀνακλιθῆναι…» ("having commanded the crowds to recline…", Matt. 14:19). Greek «Οὐκ ἔχομεν ὧδε» is literally "We do not have here" with the object unexpressed; the supplied "*anything*" is italicized. «αὐτοὺς» ("them") is masculine, agreeing with «ἄρτους» ("loaves").
[^2]: This is a bare scripture-lemma with no attached commentary, not an omission in the source.
