# Matthew 14 (ΙΔʹ) — Lemma 28 · Matt. 14:32

> **when they had entered[^1] into the boat, the wind ceased; but those in the[^2]**

*This lemma carries only the Gospel words — the continuation of the sentence begun in the previous lemma — with no exposition of its own; Theophylact's comment follows in the next lemma, after «Truly you are God's Son».*

[^1]: Greek *ἐμβάντων αὐτῶν* ("when they had embarked / gone into"). The common Gospel text has *ἀναβάντων* ("when they had gone up"); Theophylact's text reads *ἐμβάντων*, a genuine variant of near-identical sense, followed here.
[^2]: The lemma breaks off at «οἱ δὲ ἐν τῷ» ("but those in the…"); the next word, «boat» (*πλοίῳ*), opens the following lemma. *δέ* rendered "but."
