# Matthew 14 (ΙΔʹ) — Lemma 19 · Matt. 14:23

> **And having dismissed the crowds, he went up *into* the mountain privately[^1] to pray; and evening having come, he was alone there; and the—**[^2]

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation; Theophylact's exposition of it begins in the following lemma.*

[^1]: Greek *κατ᾽ ἰδίαν* — an adverbial idiom (literally "according to *one's* own"), i.e. "privately, apart, by himself." Rendered "privately" for the plain sense.
[^2]: The quotation breaks off mid-sentence at *τὸ δὲ* ("and the—") and is completed in the next lemma, whose scripture opens with *πλοῖον* ("boat"): "and the boat was already in the midst of the sea…" (Matt. 14:24). The exposition likewise begins there.
