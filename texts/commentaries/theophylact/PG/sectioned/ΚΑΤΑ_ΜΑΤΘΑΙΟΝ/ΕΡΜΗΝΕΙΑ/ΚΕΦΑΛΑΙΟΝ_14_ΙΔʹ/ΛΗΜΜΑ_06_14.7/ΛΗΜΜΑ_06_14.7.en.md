# Matthew 14 (ΙΔʹ) — Lemma 6 · Matt. 14:7

> **an oath he promised to give her whatever she might ask. And she,[^1]**

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text (a continuation of the quotation begun at Lemma 5); Theophylact's exposition follows at the close of Lemma 7.*

[^1]: This continues «…ὅθεν μεθ´» from Lemma 5: "whence *with* an oath he promised to give her whatever she might ask." Greek «ὃ ἐὰν αἰτήσηται» — "whatever she might ask *for*." The sentence breaks off at «Ἡ δὲ,» ("And she,"), completed in Lemma 7 with «προβιβασθεῖσα ὑπὸ τῆς μητρὸς αὐτῆς…» ("having been prompted by her mother…").
