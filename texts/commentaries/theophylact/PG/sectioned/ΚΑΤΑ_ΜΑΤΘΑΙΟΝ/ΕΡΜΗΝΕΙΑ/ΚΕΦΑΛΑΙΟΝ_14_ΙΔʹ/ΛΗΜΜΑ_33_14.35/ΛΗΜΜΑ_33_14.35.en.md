# Matthew 14 (ΙΔʹ) — Lemma 33 · Matt. 14:35

> **having recognized him, the men of that place sent *word* into all that surrounding region, and brought to him all those who were ill, and**[^1]

*This lemma too carries only a further stretch of the Gospel text and no exposition; the quotation is completed in lemma 34, where Theophylact's comment on the whole passage (Matt. 14:34–36) is given.*[^2]

[^1]: Greek «ἐπιγνόντες αὐτὸν οἱ ἄνδρες τοῦ τόπου ἐκείνου, ἀπέστειλαν εἰς ὅλην τὴν περίχωρον ἐκείνην, καὶ προσήνεγκαν αὐτῷ πάντας τοὺς κακῶς ἔχοντας, καὶ» (Matt. 14:35). The participle *ἐπιγνόντες* ("having recognized / having recognized fully") continues the sentence begun in lemma 32. *ἀπέστειλαν* ("they sent") has no expressed object; "*word*" is supplied. *τοὺς κακῶς ἔχοντας* is literally "those having *it* badly," i.e. the sick; rendered "those who were ill." The fragment again breaks off at "and" (*καὶ*), resumed in lemma 34.
[^2]: See lemma 34 for the continuation of the quotation (Matt. 14:36) and the exposition.
