# Matthew 14 (ΙΔʹ) — Lemma 15 · Matt. 14:19

> **And having broken *them*, he gave the loaves to the disciples, but the disciples to the crowds; and…[^1] (Matt. 14:19)**

This lemma carries only the closing Gospel words of the verse; Theophylact adds no exposition to it here.[^2]

[^1]: The lemma is the end of Matt. 14:19 and breaks off at «καὶ» ("and"), the sentence running on into the next lemma («ἔφαγον πάντες…», "they all ate…", Matt. 14:20). The supplied object "*them*" (the loaves) is italicized; the Greek «κλάσας» ("having broken") stands without expressed object.
[^2]: A bare scripture-lemma with no attached commentary in the source.
