# Matthew 14 (ΙΔʹ) — Lemma 27 · Matt. 14:31[^1]

> **But immediately Jesus, having stretched out *his* hand, took hold of him,[^2] and says to him: *O you* of little faith, for what did you doubt? And[^3]**

*This lemma carries only the Gospel words; Theophylact's exposition upon them is gathered further on, at the words «Christ, showing that not the wind was the cause…».*

[^1]: The lemma folder is labeled 14:30, but the Gospel text quoted here — «Εὐθέως δὲ ὁ Ἰησοῦς ἐκτείνας τὴν χεῖρα ἐπελάβετο αὐτοῦ…» — is Matt. 14:31; the header is corrected accordingly.
[^2]: Greek *ἐπελάβετο αὐτοῦ*, "took hold of him" (from *ἐπιλαμβάνομαι*). The OCR reads *ἐπελάθετο* ("forgot," from *ἐπιλανθάνομαι*), which is a certain error for *ἐπελάβετο* — both the sense of the passage and the common Gospel text require "took hold of." (Likewise *Εὐθέως*, "immediately," for the OCR *Εὸθμως*.)
[^3]: The lemma breaks off at «Καί» ("And"); the Gospel sentence is completed in the next lemma («when they had entered into the boat, the wind ceased…»).
