# Matthew 14 (ΙΔʹ) — Lemma 25 · Matt. 14:29

> **And he said: Come.[^1] And Peter, having descended from the boat, was walking[^2] upon the waters, to come to Jesus.**

*This lemma carries only the Gospel words; Theophylact's exposition does not begin here but resumes in the following lemma, upon the words «seeing the wind strong».*[^3]

[^1]: Greek «Ὁ δὲ εἶπεν· Ἐλθέ» — "But he said: Come." The particle *δέ* is rendered "and" here (the loose narrative connective); the reply is Christ's one-word answer to Peter's request in the preceding lemma («Command me to come to you»). "Come" (Ἐλθέ) is a present word (imperative), hence roman.
[^2]: Greek *περιεπάτει*, imperfect ("was walking / kept walking"), rendered as such. The common Gospel text has the aorist *περιεπάτησεν* ("walked"); Theophylact's text (or the OCR) gives the imperfect, which is followed here.
[^3]: The lemma folder isolates only Theophylact's quotation of Matt. 14:29; his comment on this verse and on v. 30 is gathered under the next lemma, which opens with the continuation of the Gospel text.
