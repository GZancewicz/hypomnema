# Matthew 24 (ΚΔʹ) — Lemma 4 · Matt. 24:4

> **And Jesus, answering, said to them: See that no one lead you astray.[^1] (Matt. 24:4)**

*The lemma breaks off here and carries no exposition of its own; the quotation is continued in Lemma 5, where Theophylact's comment follows.*

[^1]: «Βλέπετε μή τις ὑμᾶς πλανήσῃ» — "See that no one lead you astray / deceive you"; «πλανάω» ("cause to wander, mislead, deceive") is the recurring verb of this section, glossed consistently "lead astray."
