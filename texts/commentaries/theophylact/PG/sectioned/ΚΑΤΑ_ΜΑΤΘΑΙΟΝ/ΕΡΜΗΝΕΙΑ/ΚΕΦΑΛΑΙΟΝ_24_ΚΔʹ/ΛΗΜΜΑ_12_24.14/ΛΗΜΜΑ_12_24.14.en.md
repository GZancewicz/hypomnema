# Matthew 24 (ΚΔʹ) — Lemma 12 · Matt. 24:14

> **And this Gospel of the kingdom shall be preached in the whole inhabited world,[^1] for a testimony to all the nations, and then the end shall come. (Matt. 24:14)**

You will not be hindered in the preaching; take courage. For the Gospel will be preached among all the nations, for a testimony — that is, for a reproof, for an accusation[^2] of those who did not believe — and then the end will come: not of the world, but of Jerusalem.[^3] For before Jerusalem was taken the Gospel was preached, as Paul also says: "The Gospel having been preached in all the creation which is under heaven."[^4] For that he speaks concerning the end of Jerusalem is clear from what follows; for he says:[^5]

[^1]: Greek *ἐν ὅλῃ τῇ οἰκουμένῃ* — *ἡ οἰκουμένη* is "the inhabited (world)," often the Roman world; rendered "the whole inhabited world" to keep the term visible, since Theophylact's whole point turns on the Gospel's reach *before* the fall of Jerusalem.
[^2]: Greek *εἰς ἔλεγχον, εἰς κατηγορίαν* — *ἔλεγχος* is "proof, reproof, conviction (of wrong)"; *κατηγορία* is "accusation, charge." The "testimony" tells against the unbelievers, convicting and indicting them.
[^3]: Theophylact makes *τὸ τέλος* ("the end") here the end of Jerusalem, not the end of the world — the local "end," in contrast to the "common consummation" of Lemma 8.
[^4]: Col. 1:23, Greek *τοῦ Εὐαγγελίου κηρυχθέντος ἐν πάσῃ τῇ κτίσει τῇ ὑπ᾽ οὐρανόν* ("in all the creation which is under heaven"). Cited as proof that the worldwide preaching was already accomplished before Jerusalem's fall.
[^5]: The exposition breaks off at "for he says," which is completed by the next scripture quotation (Matt. 24:15, "When therefore you see the abomination of desolation…"), heading Lemma 13.
