# Matthew 24 (ΚΔʹ) — Lemma 28 · Matt. 24:30

> **And then shall appear the sign of the Son of Man in the heaven, and then shall all the tribes of the earth mourn, and they shall see the Son of Man coming upon the clouds of the heaven, with power and much glory. (Matt. 24:30)**

The cross then will be seen in the heaven, shining brighter than the sun, for *the* reproof of the Jews; for as *one* who has the cross as *a* very great vindication,[^1] the Lord comes against the Jews; just as if someone struck by a stone were to display the stone. And he calls the cross "sign," as *being* a kind of trophy and royal ensign.[^2]

Then accordingly shall all the tribes of the earth, the Jewish *ones*, mourn, lamenting their own unbelief; and also all those who mind earthly things, even if they are Christians, shall mourn. For those attached to earthly things might be called "tribes of the earth." But even if the Lord comes with the cross, yet *he comes* also with power and much glory.

[^1]: Greek *δικαίωμα* — "just claim, ground of vindication, proof of right." The image is forensic: Christ comes bearing the cross as the decisive piece of evidence that convicts the Jews, like a victim producing the very stone that struck him. Rendered "vindication"; "just claim / evidence of right" would serve equally.
[^2]: Greek *τρόπαιον καὶ παράσημον βασιλικόν* — a "trophy" (the victory-monument set up over a defeated enemy) and a "royal ensign / standard / imperial insignia." Both terms are military-imperial: the cross functions as Christ's victory-monument and his sovereign's badge of office.
