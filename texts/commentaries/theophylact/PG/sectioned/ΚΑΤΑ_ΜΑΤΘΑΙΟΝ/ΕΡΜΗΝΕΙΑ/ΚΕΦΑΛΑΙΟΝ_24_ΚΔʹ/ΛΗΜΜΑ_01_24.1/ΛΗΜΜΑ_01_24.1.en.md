# Matthew 24 (ΚΔʹ) — Lemma 1 · Matt. 24:1

> **And having gone out, Jesus was going away from the temple, and his disciples approached him, to show him the buildings of the temple. But he—[^1] (Matt. 24:1)**

*The lemma breaks off mid-sentence and carries no exposition of its own; Theophylact's comment begins in Lemma 2, after the quotation is completed.*[^2]

[^1]: The Greek breaks off at «Ὁ δὲ» ("But he—"), where the article «Ὁ» awaits its noun. The sentence is completed at the head of Lemma 2 with «Ἰησοῦς εἶπεν αὐτοῖς» ("Jesus said to them"), so that the full subject is «Ὁ δὲ Ἰησοῦς» ("But Jesus").
[^2]: «ἐπιδεῖξαι» is an aorist infinitive of purpose ("to show / point out"); «τὰς οἰκοδομὰς» = "the buildings / structures" of the temple. The imperfect «ἐπορεύετο» ("was going away") is kept as a continuous past.
