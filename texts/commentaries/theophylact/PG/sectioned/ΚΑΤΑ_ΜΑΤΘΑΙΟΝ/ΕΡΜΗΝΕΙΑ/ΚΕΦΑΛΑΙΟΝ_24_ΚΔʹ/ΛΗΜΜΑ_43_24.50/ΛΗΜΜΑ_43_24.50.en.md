# Matthew 24 (ΚΔʹ) — Lemma 43 · Matt. 24:50

> **…*the* lord of that servant *will come* on a day on which he does not expect *it*, and at an hour which he does not know, and…**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the continuation of the scripture text; Theophylact supplies no exposition of his own here. The sentence begins in the previous lemma (24:49, «ἥξει ὁ…», "the lord will come") and runs on into the next (24:51, «διχοτομήσει αὐτὸν…»), where the exposition follows.*[^2]

[^1]: Greek «κύριος τοῦ δούλου ἐκείνου ἐν ἡμέρᾳ ᾗ οὐ προσδοκᾷ, καὶ ἐν ὥρᾳ ᾗ οὐ γινώσκει, καὶ» (OCR: «προσδοκξ» for *προσδοκᾷ*, securely recoverable). The finite verb *ἥξει* ("will come") stands at the end of the preceding lemma (24:49); the subject *ὁ κύριος* ("the lord") is thus split across the lemma boundary, and I have supplied "*will come*" in italics so the fragment reads. The datives *ᾗ … ᾗ* are relative ("on which… at which"); *προσδοκᾷ* is here rendered "does not expect *it*," with the object supplied.
[^2]: The trailing «καὶ» ("and") is left in place: it links directly to *διχοτομήσει αὐτὸν* ("he will cut him asunder") at the head of the next lemma.
