# Matthew 24 (ΚΔʹ) — Lemma 20 · Matt. 24:23

> **Then if anyone say to you, "Behold, here *is* the …" (Matt. 24:23)**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture citation, which breaks off in mid-clause; Theophylact gives no exposition here. His comment on vv. 23–25 is set down below at Lemma 23.*[^2]

[^1]: The Greek is «Τότε ἐάν τις ὑμῖν εἴπῃ, Ἰδοὺ ὧδε ὁ» — the citation stops at the article «ὁ» ("the"), whose noun «Χριστός» ("Christ") opens the next lemma. There is no copula in «Ἰδοὺ ὧδε ὁ Χριστός» ("Behold, here *is* the Christ"), so "*is*" is supplied in italics.
[^2]: This text follows the Textus-Receptus reading «Ἰδοὺ ὧδε ὁ Χριστός, ἢ ὧδε» ("Behold, here *is* the Christ, or here"), rather than the «ὧδε … ἐκεῖ» ("here … there") of other witnesses; see Lemma 21, which continues the quotation.
