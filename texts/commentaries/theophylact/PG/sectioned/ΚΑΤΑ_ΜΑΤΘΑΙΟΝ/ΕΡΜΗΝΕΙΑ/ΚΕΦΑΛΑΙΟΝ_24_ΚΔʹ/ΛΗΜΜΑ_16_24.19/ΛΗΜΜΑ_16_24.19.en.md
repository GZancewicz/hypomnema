# Matthew 24 (ΚΔʹ) — Lemma 16 · Matt. 24:19

> **But woe to those having *a child* in *the* womb and to those giving suck in those days. (Matt. 24:19)**

For the pregnant *women*, on the one hand, will not be able to flee, being weighed down by the burden of the womb. But those giving suck, on the other hand, on account of *their* fellow-feeling toward *their* children — being able neither to leave them nor indeed to carry *them* and be saved together with them — will not escape the wrath *that comes* then. Or Christ hints also at this, *namely,* the eating of children.[^1] For Josephus says that, there being a famine on account of the siege, a woman roasted her child and ate[^2]

[^1]: Greek *τεκνοφαγία* ("child-eating"), the cannibalizing of one's own offspring under the famine of the siege — reported by Josephus (*Jewish War* 6.201-213, the account of the woman Mary). Rendered "the eating of children"; the single Greek compound is noted here.
[^2]: The exposition breaks off here mid-sentence (*ὤπτησε γυνὴ τὸ τέκνον αὐτῆς καὶ ἔφαγε*, "a woman roasted her child and ate…"); the object and continuation are not carried in this section, and the next lemma opens a new quotation (Matt. 24:20).
