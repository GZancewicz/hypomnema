# Matthew 24 (ΚΔʹ) — Lemma 38 · Matt. 24:42

> **Watch therefore, for you do not know in what hour your Lord comes. (Matt. 24:42)**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture; Theophylact's exposition is given together with the following lemma, where the quotation continues at Matt. 24:43.*[^2]

[^1]: The Greek OCR reads «Γρηγορεῖτε οὖν, ὅτι οὐκ οἴδατε πυίᾳ ὥρᾳ ὁ Κύριος ὑμῶν ἄρχεται»; securely = «… ποίᾳ ὥρᾳ ὁ Κύριος ὑμῶν ἔρχεται» (*πυίᾳ* → *ποίᾳ*, "in what/which"; *ἄρχεται* → *ἔρχεται*, "comes"). The Byzantine text here reads *ὥρᾳ* ("hour"), against the Alexandrian *ἡμέρᾳ* ("day"); rendered "hour," following the text Theophylact quotes.
[^2]: The Gospel sentence continues without a break into the next lemma («Ἐκεῖνο δὲ γινώσκετε …», Matt. 24:43), where the exposition covering vv. 42–44 is supplied.
