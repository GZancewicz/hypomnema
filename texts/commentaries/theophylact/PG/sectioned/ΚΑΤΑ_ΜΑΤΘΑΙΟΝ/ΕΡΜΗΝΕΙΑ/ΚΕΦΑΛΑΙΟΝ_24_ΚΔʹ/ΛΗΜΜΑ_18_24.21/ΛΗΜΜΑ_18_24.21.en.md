# Matthew 24 (ΚΔʹ) — Lemma 18 · Matt. 24:21

> **For there shall be then a great tribulation, such as has not happened[^1] from *the* beginning of *the* world until now, nor ever shall happen; and if[^2]**

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation and breaks off mid-sentence; Theophylact's exposition follows in the next lemma.*[^2]

[^1]: The OCR reads *γέγονειν* for *γέγονεν* ("has happened / has been"); the correction is certain and low-stakes.
[^2]: The quotation is cut off at *καὶ εἰ μὴ* ("and if… not"). The sentence is completed at the head of the next lemma (Lemma 19), which supplies *ἐκολοβώθησαν αἱ ἡμέραι ἐκεῖναι, οὐκ ἂν ἐσώθη πᾶσα σάρξ…* ("…those days had been shortened, no flesh would have been saved…"), where the exposition begins. There is no exposition in this file.
