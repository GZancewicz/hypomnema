# Matthew 24 (ΚΔʹ) — Lemma 23 · Matt. 24:25 (comment on 24:23–25)

> **…I have foretold *it* to you. (Matt. 24:25)**[^1]

Since the disciples had brought forward two questions — concerning the capture of Jerusalem, and concerning the coming of the Lord — having spoken about the capture, from here he begins about his own coming, and about the consummation of the world. But the *word* "then" does not indicate this, that immediately after Jerusalem's being taken, "if anyone say to you," and the *words* following; rather, it indicates that time in which it is about to come to pass. So that what is said is of this sort: "Then" — that is, when the Antichrist is about to come — there will be many false Christs and false prophets, who by *the* illusion of demons make sport of[^2] the eyes of those who see, so as to deceive some; so that, unless they are watchful,[^3] even the righteous will be deceived. But to you, behold, I have foretold *it*; you have no excuse; for you are able not to be deceived.

[^1]: This lemma continues the citation from Lemma 22: «Ἰδοὺ» ("Behold") stands there, and «προείρηκα ὑμῖν» ("I have foretold to you") here, together forming Matt. 24:25, «Ἰδοὺ προείρηκα ὑμῖν» ("Behold, I have told you before"). The object ("*it*") is not expressed in the Greek and is supplied. The exposition below covers vv. 23–25 as a whole. OCR corrections taken as certain: «προσΚήνεγκαν» → «προσήνεγκαν» ("brought forward"); «καυτοῦ» → «ἑαυτοῦ» ("his own"); «μέλλαι» → «μέλλει» ("is about to"); «νήφιωσι» → «νήφωσι» ("are watchful").
[^2]: Greek «διαπαίζοντες» — "mocking, making sport of, playing tricks upon." The sense is that the demons produce phantom appearances (ἐκ φαντασίας δαιμόνων, "from an illusion of demons") that toy with the beholders' sight so as to deceive.
[^3]: Greek «ἐὰν μὴ νήφωσι» — "unless they are sober / vigilant." «νήφω» is the New Testament term for spiritual sobriety and watchfulness (cf. 1 Pet. 5:8); rendered "watchful" here. The OCR reads «ἢν» for «ἐὰν» ("unless").
