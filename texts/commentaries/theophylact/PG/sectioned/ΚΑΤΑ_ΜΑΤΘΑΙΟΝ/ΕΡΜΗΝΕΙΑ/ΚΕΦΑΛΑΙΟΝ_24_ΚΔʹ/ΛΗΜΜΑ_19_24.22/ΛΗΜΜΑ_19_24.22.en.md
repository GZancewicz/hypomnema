# Matthew 24 (ΚΔʹ) — Lemma 19 · Matt. 24:22

> **…were those days shortened, all flesh would not have been saved; but for the sake of the elect those days will be shortened. (Matt. 24:22)**[^1]

The affliction at that time was unbearable. For the soldiers of the Romans had been commanded to spare no one; but God, on account of those who were about to believe from among them, or *those* who had already believed, did not permit *them* to be utterly[^2] destroyed, but shortened both the afflictions and the war. For if the war had prevailed for a longer *time*, all those within would have been destroyed by the famine. But some understand these things concerning the days of the Antichrist; these things, however, are not about the Antichrist, but about the capture of Jerusalem. But the things concerning the Antichrist begin from here; listen, therefore:[^3]

[^1]: This lemma continues the scripture quotation begun at the end of Lemma 18, which breaks off at «καὶ εἰ μὴ» ("and unless / and if… not"). The full sentence, of which this file carries only the apodosis, is «καὶ εἰ μὴ ἐκολοβώθησαν αἱ ἡμέραι ἐκεῖναι, οὐκ ἂν ἐσώθη πᾶσα σάρξ· διὰ δὲ τοὺς ἐκλεκτοὺς κολοβωθήσονται αἱ ἡμέραι ἐκεῖναι» — "And unless those days were shortened, all flesh would not have been saved; but for the sake of the elect those days will be shortened." The opening ellipsis marks the missing conditional «καὶ εἰ μή». The OCR here reads «σώθη» for «ἐσώθη» (the augment dropped), a trivial correction.
[^2]: The OCR reads «Χοδην», which is corrupt; it is securely recoverable as **«ἄρδην»** ("utterly, root and branch, wholesale"), governing «ἀναιρεθῆναι» ("to be destroyed") — hence "to be utterly destroyed." The object ("them," i.e. the inhabitants / the believers among them) is not expressed in the Greek and is supplied in italics.
[^3]: Several minor OCR corruptions in this lemma are corrected silently as certain: «αὸν πόλεμον ἐκολώβωσεν» → «τὸν πόλεμον ἐκολόβωσεν» ("shortened the war"); «Α πόλεμος» → «ὁ πόλεμος» ("the war"); «Κνοοῦσι» → «νοοῦσι» ("they understand"); «Τὰ Εὲ» → «Τὰ δὲ» ("but the things"); «ἄκουε Ρὺν» → «ἄκουε οὖν» ("listen, therefore").
