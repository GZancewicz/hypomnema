# Matthew 24 (ΚΔʹ) — Lemma 22 · Matt. 24:24

> **For there shall arise false Christs and false prophets; and they shall give great signs and wonders, so as to lead astray, if possible, even the elect. Behold, … (Matt. 24:24)**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture citation, which breaks off at "Behold"; Theophylact gives no exposition here. His comment on vv. 23–25 is set down below at Lemma 23.*

[^1]: The Greek is «Ἐγερθήσονται γὰρ ψευδόχριστοι καὶ ψευδοπροφῆται· καὶ δώσουσι σημεῖα μεγάλα καὶ τέρατα, ὥστε πλανῆσαι, εἰ δυνατὸν, καὶ τοὺς ἐκλεκτούς. Ἰδοὺ» — the citation stops at «Ἰδοὺ» ("Behold"), whose sequel «προείρηκα ὑμῖν» ("I have foretold to you," Matt. 24:25) opens Lemma 23. The OCR reads «ψευδόχριστο» for «ψευδόχριστοι». Greek «πλανῆσαι» is rendered "to lead astray" (also "to deceive").
