# Matthew 24 (ΚΔʹ) — Lemma 3 · Matt. 24:3

> **And as he was sitting on the Mount of Olives, his disciples approached him privately, saying: Tell us, when shall these things be, and what *is* the sign of your coming, and of the consummation of the age? (Matt. 24:3)**

Privately they approach, as *men* about to ask concerning great matters. For they ask concerning two things: "When shall these things be" — that is, the destruction of the temple and the capture of Jerusalem — and another, "What *is* the sign of your coming?"[^1]

[^1]: «παρουσία» is rendered "coming" (its technical sense of the advent/presence of Christ); «συντέλεια τοῦ αἰῶνος» = "consummation of the age." «ἄλωσις» = "capture / taking" (of a city by siege). The disciples' two questions structure Theophylact's whole exposition of the chapter.
