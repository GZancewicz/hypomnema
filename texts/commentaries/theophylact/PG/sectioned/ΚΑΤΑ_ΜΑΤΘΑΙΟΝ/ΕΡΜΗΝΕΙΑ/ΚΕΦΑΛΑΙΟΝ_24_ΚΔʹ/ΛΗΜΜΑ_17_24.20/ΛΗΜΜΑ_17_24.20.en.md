# Matthew 24 (ΚΔʹ) — Lemma 17 · Matt. 24:20

> **But pray that your flight may not happen in winter, nor on *the* Sabbath. (Matt. 24:20)**

In the person of the apostles he says these *things* to *the* Jews;[^1] for the apostles had already gone out of Jerusalem beforehand. To the Jews, therefore, he says to pray that their flight may not happen in winter — since, on account of the difficulty of the season, they *would* not be able to run away — nor on *the* Sabbath, for then they keep rest according to the law, and would not dare *to venture upon* the flight.

But do you understand it also thus: that *one* must pray that our flight out of this life — that is, *our* death[^2] — may not happen on *a* Sabbath, *that is,* in the idleness of good deeds, nor in winter, *that is,* in the unfruitfulness of good *things,* but in fair weather of soul and in tranquility.

[^1]: Greek *ἐν προσώπῳ τῶν ἀποστόλων* — "in the person / under the character of the apostles," i.e. the words are addressed to the Jews *through* / *in the guise of* the apostles, since the apostles themselves had already left Jerusalem and so would not be the ones fleeing.
[^2]: Greek *ἡ τελευτή* — "the end," here of life, i.e. death. Rendered "death"; the sense is one's departure/decease.
