# Matthew 24 (ΚΔʹ) — Lemma 24 · Matt. 24:26

> **If therefore they say to you, "Behold, he is in the wilderness," go not out; "behold, *he is* in the inner chambers," do not believe. (Matt. 24:26)**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture citation; Theophylact gives no exposition here. His comment continues below at Lemma 25, with the saying about the lightning (v. 27).*

[^1]: The Greek is «Ἐὰν οὖν εἴπωσιν ὑμῖν, Ἰδοὺ ἐν τῇ ἐρήμῳ ἐστὶ, μὴ ἐξέλθητε· ἰδοὺ ἐν τοῖς ταμείοις, μὴ πιστεύσητε.» The first clause has the copula «ἐστὶ» ("he is"); the second, «ἐν τοῖς ταμείοις» ("in the inner chambers"), has none, so "*he is*" is supplied in italics. Greek «ταμεῖον» is a "storeroom, private/inner chamber, treasury"; rendered "inner chambers" (cf. the "secret chambers" of the received English).
