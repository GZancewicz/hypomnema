# Matthew 24 (ΚΔʹ) — Lemma 42 · Matt. 24:49

> **…should begin to beat *his* fellow-slaves, and should eat and drink with the drunken, *the lord* will come… (Matt. 24:49)**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture, continuing the sentence begun in the previous lemma and again breaking off; Theophylact's exposition of the whole passage about the evil slave (Matt. 24:48–51) is given at its concluding lemma.*[^2]

[^1]: This fragment continues the conditional from the previous lemma (Matt. 24:48) and breaks off at *ἥξει ὁ* ("the lord will come…"), which the next lemma completes. Greek: «ἄρξηται τύπτειν τοὺς συνδούλους, ἐσθίῃ δὲ καὶ πίνῃ μετὰ τῶν μεθυόντων, ἥξει ὁ [κύριος …]». *τοὺς συνδούλους* = "*his* fellow-slaves"; *μετὰ τῶν μεθυόντων* = "with the drunken / with those who are drunk."
[^2]: The comment is found below, with the lemma at Matt. 24:51, where Theophylact expounds the whole sentence together.
