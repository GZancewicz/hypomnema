# Matthew 24 (ΚΔʹ) — Lemma 27 · Matt. 24:29

> **But immediately after the tribulation of those days the sun shall be darkened, and the moon shall not give her light, and the stars shall fall from the heaven, and the powers of the heavens shall be shaken. (Matt. 24:29)**[^1]

After the coming of the Antichrist, he says, which will quickly be dissolved — for this he indicated through the *word* "immediately"[^2] — the sun shall be darkened, that is, in the sense of "shall be dimmed,"[^3] not being made to vanish, but being overcome by the light of the coming of Christ; just as also the stars and the moon *shall be*. For *there is* no longer need of such perceptible light, night not being *present*, and the Sun of Righteousness[^4] having appeared.

But also, "the powers of the heavens shall be shaken," that is, they shall be astonished and shall shudder, seeing the creation being altered, and all the men from Adam until then *who are* about to give account.[^5]

[^1]: The initial *δέ* is present in Theophylact's text and rendered "But" (roman). *Εὐθέως* ("immediately") is the word he singles out for comment below.
[^2]: Greek *διὰ τοῦ εὐθέως* — literally "through the 'immediately.'" Theophylact treats the Gospel's own word *εὐθέως* as a lexical item: the "immediately" signals that the Antichrist's reign is short-lived.
[^3]: Greek *σκοτισθήσεται, ἀντὶ τοῦ ἀμαυρωθήσεται* — he glosses the verse's "shall be darkened" (*σκοτισθήσεται*) with *ἀμαυρωθήσεται* ("shall be dimmed / obscured"). *Ἀντὶ τοῦ* here = "in the sense of, i.e." The point of the gloss is that the sun is not annihilated but merely outshone.
[^4]: An allusion to Mal. 4:2 (LXX 3:20), *ἥλιος τῆς δικαιοσύνης* — a title for Christ; hence "night" (the need for created light) is abolished at his appearing.
[^5]: The construction is compressed and the final verb OCR-garbled («δώσιν» for an expected *δώσειν / δοῦναι λόγον*, "to give account"). The participle *μέλλοντας* ("about to") and the men "from Adam until then" stand as further objects of *ὁρῶσαι* ("seeing"): the heavenly powers shudder at seeing both the creation transformed and all humanity now due to render account.
