# Matthew 24 (ΚΔʹ) — Lemma 10 · Matt. 24:12–13

> **the lawlessness is multiplied, the love of the many shall grow cold.[^1] But he that endures unto *the* end, this one shall be saved.**[^2]

*This lemma carries only a continuation of the Gospel quotation (Matt. 24:12–13); Theophylact's exposition of these words is given in Lemma 11.*

[^1]: Greek *ψυγήσεται ἡ ἀγάπη τῶν πολλῶν* — *ψύχομαι* is "to grow cold, be chilled"; *τῶν πολλῶν* is "of the many" (i.e. of the multitude), not "of many." The clause completes the "and because" left hanging at the end of Lemma 9: "because the lawlessness is multiplied…"
[^2]: Greek *ὁ δὲ ὑπομείνας εἰς τέλος, οὗτος σωθήσεται*. The *δέ* ("but") marks the turn from those who fall away to the one who endures; *ὑπομείνας* is an aorist participle ("having endured / he that endures"). The supplied "*the*" before "end" is italicized (the Greek *εἰς τέλος* is anarthrous).
