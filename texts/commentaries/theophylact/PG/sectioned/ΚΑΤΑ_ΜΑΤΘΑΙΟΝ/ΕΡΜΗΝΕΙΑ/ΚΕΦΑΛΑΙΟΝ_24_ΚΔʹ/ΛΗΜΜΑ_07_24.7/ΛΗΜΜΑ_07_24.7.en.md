# Matthew 24 (ΚΔʹ) — Lemma 7 · Matt. 24:7

> **For nation shall be raised up against nation, and kingdom against kingdom, and there shall be famines and pestilences,[^1] and earthquakes in *various* places;**[^2]

*This lemma carries only a continuation of the Gospel quotation (Matt. 24:7); Theophylact's exposition of these words follows in Lemma 8.*[^3]

[^1]: Greek *λιμοὶ καὶ λοιμοί* — an assonant pair ("famines and pestilences") whose sound-play is lost in English; the two nouns differ by a single letter. Theophylact makes the pairing significant in his comment (Lemma 8): wars can be ascribed to human malice, but famines and pestilences come from God alone.
[^2]: Greek *σεισμοὶ κατὰ τόπους* — literally "earthquakes place by place"; the phrase means "in one place after another / in various places." The supplied "*various*" is italicized, the distributive sense being carried by *κατά* + accusative alone.
[^3]: The quotation opens with *γάρ* ("for"), continuing the scripture block begun at Matt. 24:6 (Lemma 6); it breaks off before "But all these *things are* the beginning of birth-pangs," which heads Lemma 8.
