# Matthew 24 (ΚΔʹ) — Lemma 9 · Matt. 24:10–11

> **many shall be caused to stumble,[^1] and shall deliver one another up, and shall hate one another; and many false prophets shall be raised up, and shall lead many astray; and because**[^2]

*This lemma carries only a continuation of the Gospel quotation (Matt. 24:10–11); Theophylact's exposition of these words is given in Lemma 11.*

[^1]: Greek *σκανδαλισθήσονται* ("shall be made to stumble / be scandalized / be caused to fall away"), from *σκάνδαλον*, a snare or stumbling-block; the passive denotes being tripped up into apostasy. Theophylact glosses these as *σκανδαλισμοί* among the forerunners of the Antichrist (Lemma 11).
[^2]: The passage runs on from Lemma 8 (Matt. 24:9) and breaks off at "and because" (*καὶ διὰ τό*), which is completed in Lemma 10: "because the lawlessness is multiplied, the love of the many shall grow cold."
