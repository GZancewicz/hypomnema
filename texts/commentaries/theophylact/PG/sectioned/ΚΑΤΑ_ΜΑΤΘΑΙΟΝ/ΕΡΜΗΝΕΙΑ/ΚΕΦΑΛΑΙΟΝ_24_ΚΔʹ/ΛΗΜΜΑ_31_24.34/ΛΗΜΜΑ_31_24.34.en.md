# Matthew 24 (ΚΔʹ) — Lemma 31 · Matt. 24:34

> **Amen I say to you, this generation shall by no means pass away, until all these things come to pass; the**[^1] (Matt. 24:34)

This lemma carries only Theophylact's Gospel citation, and it breaks off mid-sentence at «ὁ» ("the"), the opening word of Matt. 24:35. His exposition does not begin here but in the following lemma (32), where the quotation is completed and both verses are expounded together.[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Ἀμὴν λέγω ὑμῖν, οὐ μὴ παρέλθῃ ἡ γενεὰ αὕτη, ἕως ἂν πάντα ταῦτα γένηται». The double negative *οὐ μὴ* with the aorist subjunctive is emphatic ("shall by no means / shall certainly not pass away"), rendered here to keep both particles visible; a plainer "shall not pass away" is equally defensible.
[^2]: The citation stops at the bare article «ὁ», which continues in Lemma 32 as «οὐρανὸς καὶ ἡ γῆ παρελεύσονται…» ("heaven and the earth shall pass away…"). The article is left untranslated-in-place here and rendered "the" as the head of that clause.
