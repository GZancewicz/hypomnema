# Matthew 24 (ΚΔʹ) — Lemma 41 · Matt. 24:48

> **But if that evil slave should say in his heart, "My lord delays to come," and… (Matt. 24:48)**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture, and breaks off mid-sentence; Theophylact's exposition of the whole passage about the evil slave (Matt. 24:48–51) is given at its concluding lemma.*[^2]

[^1]: The Gospel sentence breaks off at *καί* ("and"), continuing into the next lemma (*ἄρξηται τύπτειν τοὺς συνδούλους …*, Matt. 24:49). *Χρονίζει ὁ κύριός μου ἐλθεῖν* — "My lord delays to come," i.e. is slow / tarries in coming.
[^2]: The comment on this clause is found below, with the lemma at Matt. 24:51, where Theophylact expounds the whole conditional sentence together: the delaying, the beating of fellow-slaves, and the punishment.
