# Matthew 24 (ΚΔʹ) — Lemma 29 · Matt. 24:31

> **And he shall send his angels with a trumpet of a great voice, and they shall gather together his elect from the four winds, from *the* extremities of *the* heavens unto *the* extremities of them. (Matt. 24:31)**

He shall send the angels to gather together the saints, even those risen from *the* dead, so as to meet him in *the* clouds.[^1] He honors them therefore also by the summons through angels. And even if Paul also says that they shall be caught up through *the* clouds,[^2] this *is* not contrary; for the clouds shall catch up *those who have been* collected by the angels. And the trumpet *is* for greater astonishment.
