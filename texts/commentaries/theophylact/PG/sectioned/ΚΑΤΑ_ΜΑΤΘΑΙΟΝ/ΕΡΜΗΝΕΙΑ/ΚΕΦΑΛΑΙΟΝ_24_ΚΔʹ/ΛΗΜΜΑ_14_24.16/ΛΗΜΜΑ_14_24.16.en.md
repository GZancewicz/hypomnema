# Matthew 24 (ΚΔʹ) — Lemma 14 · Matt. 24:16-18a

> **Then let those in Judea flee to the mountains; let the *one* on the housetop not come down to take the *things* out of his house, and the *one* in the field, not[^1]**

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation and breaks off mid-sentence; Theophylact's exposition follows in the next lemma.*[^1]

[^1]: The quotation is cut off at *καὶ ὁ ἐν τῷ ἀγρῷ, μὴ* ("and the *one* in the field, not…"). The sentence is completed at the head of the next lemma (Lemma 15), which supplies *ἐπιστρεψάτω ὀπίσω ἆραι τὰ ἱμάτια αὐτοῦ* ("…turn back to take his garments"), where the comment begins. There is no exposition in this file.
