# Matthew 24 (ΚΔʹ) — Lemma 5 · Matt. 24:5

> **…For many shall come in my name, saying: I am the Christ; and they shall lead many astray. (Matt. 24:5)**

Many, he says, shall come proclaiming themselves *to be* Christs; for indeed Dositheus the Samaritan used to say that, "I am the prophet foretold by Moses";[^1] and Simon too, the Samaritan, named himself *a* great power of God.[^2]

[^1]: «ὁ ὑπὸ τοῦ Μωσέως προαγορευθεὶς προφήτης» — "the prophet foretold by Moses" (cf. Deut. 18:15). On Dositheus the Samaritan as a self-styled messianic figure, cf. the early heresiologists.
[^2]: «μεγάλην τοῦ Θεοῦ δύναμιν ἑαυτὸν ὠνόμαζεν» — an allusion to Simon Magus of Acts 8:10, «Οὗτός ἐστιν ἡ δύναμις τοῦ Θεοῦ ἡ καλουμένη μεγάλη» ("This man is the power of God which is called great"). Theophylact's phrase is anarthrous, hence the supplied "*a*."
