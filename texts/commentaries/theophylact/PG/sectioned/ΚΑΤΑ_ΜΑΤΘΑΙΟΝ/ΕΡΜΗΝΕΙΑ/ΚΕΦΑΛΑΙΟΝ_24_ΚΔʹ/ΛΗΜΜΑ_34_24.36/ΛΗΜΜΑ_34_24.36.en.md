# Matthew 24 (ΚΔʹ) — Lemma 34 · Matt. 24:37

> **But just as the days of Noah, so also will the coming of the Son of Man be.**[^1] (Matt. 24:37)

This lemma carries only Theophylact's Gospel citation, and it breaks off *there*; his exposition begins in the following lemma (35), where the quotation is continued («Ὥσπερ γὰρ ἦσαν ἐν ταῖς ἡμέραις ταῖς πρὸ τοῦ κατακλυσμοῦ…» — "For just as they were in the days before the flood…") and expounded.[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Ὥσπερ δὲ αἱ ἡμέραι τοῦ Νῶε, οὕτως ἔσται καὶ ἡ παρουσία τοῦ Υἱοῦ τοῦ ἀνθρώπου». The particle *δὲ* ("but/and") is rendered as a present word (roman).
[^2]: The folder name assigns this lemma to 24.36, but the Greek it cites is Matt. 24:37; the header and reference are corrected accordingly. The verse-reference in the folder name is best-effort metadata, not authoritative.
