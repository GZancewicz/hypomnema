# Matthew 24 (ΚΔʹ) — Lemma 35 · Matt. 24:38–39

> **For just as they were in the days before the flood eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage, until the day *on which* Noah entered into the ark, and they did not know until the flood came and took away all; so also will the coming of the Son of Man be.**[^1] (Matt. 24:38–39)

For the confirmation of the [?] words,[^2] he has made use of the things that came to pass in Noah's *time*. For just as then some regarded the building of the ark *as* a mockery, until the calamity came and made all vanish, so also now some mock the words concerning the consummation; but suddenly, he says, the destruction will come upon *them*.[^3] And he shows that, when the Antichrist has come, pleasure will overpower men, and they will incline toward marriages and luxuries more insolently, just as also the giants in Noah's *time did*.[^4]

[^1]: Greek «Ὥσπερ γὰρ ἦσαν ἐν ταῖς ἡμέραις ταῖς πρὸ τοῦ κατακλυσμοῦ τρώγοντες καὶ πίνοντες, γαμοῦντες καὶ ἐκγαμίζοντες, ἄχρις ἧς ἡμέρας εἰσῆλθε Νῶε εἰς τὴν κιβωτόν, καὶ οὐκ ἔγνωσαν ἕως ἦλθεν ὁ κατακλυσμὸς, καὶ ἦρεν ἅπαντας· οὕτως ἔσται καὶ ἡ παρουσία τοῦ Υἱοῦ τοῦ ἀνθρώπου». The OCR corrupts several words securely recoverable from the sense and the common text: «τρώοντες»→*τρώγοντες*, «ἐκγαμίζον‑σες»→*ἐκγαμίζοντες*, «εἰ λθε»→*εἰσῆλθε*, «ἦλθ»→*ἦλθεν*. "*on which*" renders the relative *ἧς*.
[^2]: The opening clause is badly OCR-garbled: «Ποὸς ἃεβᾳίσʹν τῶν δολειδῶν ανλ-ολλ λόγων». It is securely «Πρὸς βεβαίωσιν τῶν … λόγων» ("For the confirmation of the … words"), but the qualifier of "words" (the corrupt «δολειδῶν ανλ-ολλ») is unrecoverable — plausibly *προειρημένων* ("aforesaid") or *οἰκείων* ("his own"). The [?] marks the gap; only the legible frame is translated.
[^3]: OCR «ἀπελεαεται». The context (destruction/*ὄλεθρος* falling on the mockers) favors *ἐπελεύσεται* ("will come upon"); the transmitted form could also be read *ἀπελεύσεται* ("will depart/come away"), less apt here. Rendered "will come upon"; the "*them*" is supplied. Greek «ἀλλ᾽ αἰφνιδίως, φησὶν, [ἐπ]ελεύσεται ὁ ὄλεθρος».
[^4]: The verb printed «πρατήσει» is *κρατήσει* ("will overpower/dominate"). The "giants in Noah's time" (*οἱ ἐπὶ Νῶε γίγαντες*) alludes to Gen. 6:4; "*did*" is supplied to complete the comparison.
