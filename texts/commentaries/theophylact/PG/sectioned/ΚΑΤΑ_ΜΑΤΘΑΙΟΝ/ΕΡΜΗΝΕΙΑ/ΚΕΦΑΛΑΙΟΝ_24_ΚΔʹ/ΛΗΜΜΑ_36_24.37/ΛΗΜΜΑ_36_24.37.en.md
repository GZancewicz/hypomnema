# Matthew 24 (ΚΔʹ) — Lemma 36 · Matt. 24:40

> **Then two will be in the field; the one is taken, and the one is left. Two…**[^1] (Matt. 24:40)

This lemma carries only Theophylact's Gospel citation, and it breaks off at «Δύο» ("Two…"), which continues in the following lemma (37) as «ἀλήθουσαι ἐν τῷ μύλωνι…» ("*women* grinding at the mill…", Matt. 24:41). His exposition of both verses begins there.[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Τότε δύο ἔσονται ἐν τῷ ἀγρῷ· ὁ εἷς παραλαμβάνεται, καὶ ὁ εἷς ἀφίεται. Δύο…». Theophylact's text reads "the one… and the one" (*ὁ εἷς… καὶ ὁ εἷς*) in both members, rather than the "the one… and the other" (*ὁ εἷς… καὶ ὁ ἕτερος*) of the common text; the wording is preserved as it stands. The presents *παραλαμβάνεται* / *ἀφίεται* are rendered literally ("is taken" / "is left").
[^2]: The folder name assigns this lemma to 24.37, but the Greek it cites is Matt. 24:40; the header and reference are corrected accordingly. The citation runs on into Lemma 37 (the two women at the mill, Matt. 24:41), which is where the exposition — the righteous taken up to meet the Lord, the sinner left below — is given.
