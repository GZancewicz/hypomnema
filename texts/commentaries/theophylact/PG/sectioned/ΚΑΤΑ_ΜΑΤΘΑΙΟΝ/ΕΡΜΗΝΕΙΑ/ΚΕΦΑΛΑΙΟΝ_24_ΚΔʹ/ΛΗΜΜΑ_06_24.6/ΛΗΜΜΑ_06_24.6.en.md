# Matthew 24 (ΚΔʹ) — Lemma 6 · Matt. 24:6

> **But you shall be about to hear of wars and rumors of wars; see that you be not alarmed, for all things must come to pass, but not yet is the end;[^1] (Matt. 24:6)**

*The lemma breaks off mid-sentence and carries no exposition of its own; the quotation continues through Lemma 7 (Matt. 24:7), and Theophylact's comment begins in Lemma 8, after «all these things *are* the beginning of birth-pangs» (Matt. 24:8).*

[^1]: «Μελλήσετε δὲ ἀκούειν πολέμους καὶ ἀκοὰς πολέμων» — "you shall be about to hear of wars and rumors of wars"; the future of «μέλλω» + infinitive is kept periphrastic ("shall be about to hear"). «ὁρᾶτε μὴ θροεῖσθε» = "see that you be not alarmed / thrown into confusion." «δεῖ… γενέσθαι» = "must come to pass."
