# Matthew 24 (ΚΔʹ) — Lemma 21 · Matt. 24:23

> **…Christ, or, here; do not believe. (Matt. 24:23)**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the tail of the scripture citation begun in Lemma 20; Theophylact gives no exposition here. His comment on vv. 23–25 is set down below at Lemma 23.*

[^1]: The Greek is «Χριστὸς, ἢ ὧδε, μὴ πιστεύσητε» — the continuation of «Ἰδοὺ ὧδε ὁ Χριστός» from Lemma 20, completing Matt. 24:23: "Behold, here *is* the Christ, or, here; do not believe." The OCR reads «ἠ» for «ἤ» ("or").
