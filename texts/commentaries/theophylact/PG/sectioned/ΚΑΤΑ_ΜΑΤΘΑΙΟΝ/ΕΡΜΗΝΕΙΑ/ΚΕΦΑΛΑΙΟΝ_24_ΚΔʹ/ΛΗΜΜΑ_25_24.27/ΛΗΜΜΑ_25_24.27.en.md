# Matthew 24 (ΚΔʹ) — Lemma 25 · Matt. 24:27

> **For just as the lightning goes forth from *the* east and appears as far as *the* west, so shall be the coming of the Son of Man; for wherever the—[^1] (Matt. 24:27–28a)**

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation and breaks off mid-verse; Theophylact's exposition of it begins in the following lemma.*[^2]

[^1]: The quotation is cut off at Greek *ὅπου γὰρ ἐὰν ᾗ τὸ* ("for wherever the … may be"), the opening of Matt. 24:28. The noun it governs — *πτῶμα* ("corpse") — falls at the head of the next lemma, which completes the verse: «…πτῶμα, ἐκεῖ συναχθήσονται οἱ ἀετοί» ("…corpse, there shall the eagles be gathered together"). The opening *γάρ* ("for") is present in Theophylact's Gospel text and so is rendered in roman, not italics.
[^2]: This folder's `.txt` contains no exposition — only the lemma. The comment on Matt. 24:27–28 is given whole under Lemma 26, where the scripture sentence is completed.
