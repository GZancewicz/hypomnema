# Matthew 20 (Κʹ) — Lemma 18 · Matt. 20:23

> **Sit at my right hand?[^1]**

But the Lord said this[^2] in reference to their supposition; for, not understanding that to sit upon twelve thrones[^3] is to be glorified on account of virtue, they were seeking such a *literal* seat.

[^1]: Greek «Κάθου ἐκ δεξιῶν μου» = Ps. 109:1 LXX (Ps. 110:1), quoted also at Heb. 1:13. It completes the question begun at the end of Lemma 17 ("For to which of the angels did he say: 'Sit at my right hand?'"): the enthronement at God's right hand is said of the Son alone, not of any angel. The folder-name verse 20:23 is retained as locator, but the words quoted here are the Psalm, not Matthew.
[^2]: I.e. the whole answer of vv. 22–23 about drinking the cup and the seats; the Lord accommodated his reply to the crude, "perceptible" notion of kingship the disciples still held.
[^3]: An allusion to Matt. 19:28 ("you shall sit upon twelve thrones"). Greek *τὸ δοξασθῆναι … δι᾽ ἀρετήν* — the thrones signify "being glorified on account of virtue," not literal seats; the disciples erred by "not understanding" (*μὴ νοήσαντες*) this and craving a bodily throne. The connecting particle in the OCR (*Ἐσι*, best read as *ὅτι*, "that") is corrupt but the sense is secure.
