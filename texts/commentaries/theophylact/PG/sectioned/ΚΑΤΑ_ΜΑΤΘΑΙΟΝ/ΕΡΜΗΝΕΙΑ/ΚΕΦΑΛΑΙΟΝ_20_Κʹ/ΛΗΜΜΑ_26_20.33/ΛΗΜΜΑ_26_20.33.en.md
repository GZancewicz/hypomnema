# Matthew 20 (Κʹ) — Lemma 26 · Matt. 20:33

> **They say to him: "Lord, that our eyes may be opened." (Matt. 20:33)**

*This lemma carries only the words of the Gospel; Theophylact gives no exposition here. His comment on the whole healing of the blind men (Matt. 20:29–34) is given in Lemma 27, at the close of the narrative.*[^1]

[^1]: Greek «Λέγουσιν αὐτῷ· Κύριε, ἵνα ἀνοιχθῶσιν ἡμῶν οἱ ὀφθαλμοί.» The reply is elliptical: "*that* our eyes may be opened" answers the preceding question ("What do you wish?"), so the ἵνα-clause stands as the whole of what they ask — rendered as it stands, without supplying a governing verb such as "*we wish*."
