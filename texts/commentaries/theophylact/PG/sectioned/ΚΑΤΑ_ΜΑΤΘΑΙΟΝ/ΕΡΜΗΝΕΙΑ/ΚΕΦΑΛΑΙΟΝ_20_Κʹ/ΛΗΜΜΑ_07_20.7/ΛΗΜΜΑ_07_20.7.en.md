# Matthew 20 (Κʹ) — Lemma 7 · Matt. 20:1–7 (exposition)

*This lemma carries no fresh scripture quotation at its head; it is Theophylact's allegorical exposition of the parable of the laborers just quoted (vv. 1–7).*[^1]

Christ is the kingdom of the heavens, who is likened to a man, in that he took our form; and *he is* a householder, as *one* ruling over the house — that is, over the Church. This Christ, therefore, went forth from the bosom of the Father, and hires laborers into the vineyard, that is, the Scriptures or the commandments. Or *rather* he hires each one to work the vineyard, that is, his own soul.

And he hires, on the one hand, one in the morning — that is, according to the youthful age; but another, on the other hand, at the third hour — the adolescent age; and others at the sixth and ninth *hour*, when someone might be twenty-five years *old*, or thirty, and in short of *the* manly age; but about the eleventh *hour*, the old men — for many old men also believed.

Or also otherwise: the present age is called "day," for in it we work as in *the* day. The Lord, therefore, called at the first hour those around Enoch and Noah; at the third, those around Abraham; at the sixth, those around Moses; [?… at the ninth, the prophets …][^2] at the end of the ages, the gentiles, who were idle from every good work — for no one had hired them, because not even a single prophet had been sent to the gentiles.

[^1]: The OCR opens this exposition-block with a stray guillemet («), as though it were a scripture lemma; but the words «Ὁ Χριστός ἐστιν ἡ βασιλεία τῶν οὐρανῶν…» ("Christ is the kingdom of the heavens…") are plainly Theophylact's own comment, not a Gospel quotation. The guillemet is treated as a mis-segmentation artifact and dropped. The scripture text itself was given piecemeal in the preceding lemmata (vv. 1–7).
[^2]: The Greek sequence is defective here: after "at the sixth *hour*, those around Moses" (τῇ ἕκτῃ τοὺς περὶ τὸν Μωσέα·) the text jumps to «τέλει τῶν αἰώνων τοὺς ἐθνικούς» ("at the end of the ages, the gentiles"), with the intervening OCR fragment «Ιε.» (a stray column/line marker). The members for the ninth and eleventh hours have dropped out; conventionally these are the prophets (ninth hour) and the gentiles/final generation (eleventh hour). The bracketed supplement is a best-guess reconstruction, not present in the source; only the legible members are rendered as text.
