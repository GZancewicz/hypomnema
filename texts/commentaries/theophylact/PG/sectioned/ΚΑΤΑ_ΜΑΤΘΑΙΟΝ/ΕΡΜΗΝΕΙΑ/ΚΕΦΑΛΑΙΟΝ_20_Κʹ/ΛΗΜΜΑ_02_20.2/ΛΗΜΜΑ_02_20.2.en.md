# Matthew 20 (Κʹ) — Lemma 2 · Matt. 20:2

> **having agreed with the laborers for a denarius[^1] a day, he sent them into his vineyard. And**

*Continuation of the parable's text (Matt. 20:2); the exposition is given at Lemma 7.*

[^1]: Greek *ἐκ δηναρίου τὴν ἡμέραν* — literally "out of a denarius the day," i.e. at the rate of a denarius per day. The construction *ἐκ* + genitive marks the agreed wage.
