# Matthew 20 (Κʹ) — Lemma 11 · Matt. 20:13–14

> **answering said to one of them: Friend, I do you no wrong. Did you not agree with me for a denarius? Take what is yours, and go. But I wish to give to this last one as also to you; or is it not[^1] (Matt. 20:13–14)**

*This lemma carries the Gospel text only; Theophylact's exposition of this segment follows at the close of the parable (Lemma 13).*

[^1]: OCR slips, securely corrected: «Ἀνι αὐτῶν» → **ἑνὶ αὐτῶν** ("to one of them"); «πούτῳ» → **τούτῳ** ("to this"). «Ἑταῖρε» is the friendly address "Friend / Comrade." The clause breaks off at «ἢ οὐκ» ("or is it not"), completed in the next lemma with «ἔξεστί μοι…» ("lawful for me…").
