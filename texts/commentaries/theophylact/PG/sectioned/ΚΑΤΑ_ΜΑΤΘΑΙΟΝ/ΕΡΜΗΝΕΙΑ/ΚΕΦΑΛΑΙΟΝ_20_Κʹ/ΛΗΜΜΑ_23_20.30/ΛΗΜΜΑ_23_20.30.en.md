# Matthew 20 (Κʹ) — Lemma 23 · Matt. 20:30

> **blind *men* sitting beside the road, having heard that Jesus was passing by, cried out saying: Have mercy on us, Lord, son of David. But the—[^1] (Matt. 20:30)**

This lemma carries only the scripture text, and it breaks off in mid-phrase; Theophylact provides no exposition here. His comment on the whole episode of the two blind men is set down together in Lemma 27.

[^1]: The lemma continues the sentence begun in the previous lemma («And behold, two—») and ends «Ὁ δὲ», article + particle, completed by the next lemma with «ὄχλος ἐπετίμησεν αὐτοῖς» ("But the crowd rebuked them"). The OCR «κραξαν» = «ἔκραξαν» ("cried out").
