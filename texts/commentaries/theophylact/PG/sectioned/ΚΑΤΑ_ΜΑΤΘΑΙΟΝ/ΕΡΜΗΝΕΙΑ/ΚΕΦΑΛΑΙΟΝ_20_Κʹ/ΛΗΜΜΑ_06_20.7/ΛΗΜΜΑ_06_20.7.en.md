# Matthew 20 (Κʹ) — Lemma 6 · Matt. 20:7

> **They say to him: Because no one hired *us*.[^1] He says to them: Go you also into the vineyard, and whatever may be just, you shall receive.**

*Conclusion of the parable's text (Matt. 20:7); Theophylact's exposition of the whole parable follows in Lemma 7.*

[^1]: Greek *ὅτι οὐδεὶς ἐμισθώσατο* — "because no one hired." The object "*us*" is not expressed in the Greek and is supplied.
