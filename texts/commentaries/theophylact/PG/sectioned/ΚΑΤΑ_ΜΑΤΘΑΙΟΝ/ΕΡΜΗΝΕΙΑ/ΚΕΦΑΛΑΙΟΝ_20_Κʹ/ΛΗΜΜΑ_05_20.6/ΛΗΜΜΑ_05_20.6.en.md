# Matthew 20 (Κʹ) — Lemma 5 · Matt. 20:6

> **eleventh hour, having gone out, he found others standing idle;[^1] and he says to them: Why stand you here the whole day idle?**

*Continuation of the parable's text (Matt. 20:6); the exposition is given at Lemma 7.*

[^1]: Greek *ἑστῶτας ἀργούς* — "standing idle." The word "idle" (*ἀργός*, unemployed, at leisure, unproductive) recurs at the sentence's close and is picked up in Theophylact's comment at Lemma 7 (the gentiles who "were idle of every good work").
