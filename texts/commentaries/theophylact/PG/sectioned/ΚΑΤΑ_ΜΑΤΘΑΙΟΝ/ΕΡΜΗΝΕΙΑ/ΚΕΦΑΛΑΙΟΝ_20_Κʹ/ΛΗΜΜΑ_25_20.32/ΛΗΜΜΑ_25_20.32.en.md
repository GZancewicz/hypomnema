# Matthew 20 (Κʹ) — Lemma 25 · Matt. 20:32

> **Jesus[^1] called them, and said: "What do you wish *that* I should do for you?" (Matt. 20:32)**

*This lemma carries only the words of the Gospel; Theophylact gives no exposition here. His comment on the whole healing of the blind men (Matt. 20:29–34) is deferred to Lemma 27, where the narrative reaches its close.*

[^1]: The sentence begins in the preceding lemma, which breaks off at «Καὶ στὰς ὁ» ("And having stood, the —"); the subject «Ἰησοῦς» ("Jesus") with which this lemma opens completes it: «Καὶ στὰς ὁ Ἰησοῦς ἐφώνησεν αὐτούς» ("And Jesus, having stopped, called them"). The OCR reads «ἐφώνησν», securely = **«ἐφώνησεν»** ("called, summoned"). On «Τί θέλετε ποιήσω ὑμῖν;» the syntax is paratactic (a subjunctive without ἵνα): literally "What do you wish — shall I do for you?"; the supplied "*that*" makes the English clause; it could equally be rendered "What do you wish me to do for you?"
