# Matthew 20 (Κʹ) — Lemma 8 · Matt. 20:8–9

> **But when evening had come, the lord of the vineyard says to his steward: Call the laborers, and give them the wage, having begun from the last unto the first. And when those about the eleventh hour came, they received a denarius apiece.[^1] (Matt. 20:8–9)**

*This lemma carries the Gospel text only; Theophylact's exposition of this segment follows at the close of the parable (Lemma 13).*

[^1]: The OCR of this scripture block has several certain slips, corrected silently in the translation: «πῶν ἐσχάτων» → **τῶν ἐσχάτων** ("the last"); «των πρώτων» → **τῶν πρώτων** ("the first"); «οἱ παρὶ τὴν ἐνδεκάτην» → **οἱ περὶ τὴν ἑνδεκάτην** ("those about the eleventh"); «λαβον» → **ἔλαβον** ("they received"). None affects the sense, which agrees with the received text of Matt. 20:8–9. "A denarius apiece" renders the distributive ἀνὰ δηνάριον.
