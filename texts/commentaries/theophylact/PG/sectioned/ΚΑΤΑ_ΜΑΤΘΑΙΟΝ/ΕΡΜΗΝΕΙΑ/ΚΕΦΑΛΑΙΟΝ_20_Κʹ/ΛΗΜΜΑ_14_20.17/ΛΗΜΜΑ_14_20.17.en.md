# Matthew 20 (Κʹ) — Lemma 14 · Matt. 20:17

> **And Jesus, going up to Jerusalem, took the twelve disciples apart privately on the way, and said to them: Behold, (Matt. 20:17)**

*This lemma carries only the scripture words, breaking off at "Behold,"; the exposition of the passage begins with the following lemma, which completes the sentence with «we are going up to Jerusalem…».*[^1]

[^1]: The Gospel text is here divided across lemma boundaries: the quotation is cut at *Ἰδοὺ* ("Behold,") and resumed at the head of Lemma 15 with *ἀναβαίνομεν εἰς Ἰεροσόλυμα* ("we are going up to Jerusalem"). No commentary of Theophylact's own attaches to this segment.
