# Matthew 20 (Κʹ) — Lemma 22 · Matt. 20:29-30

> **And as they were going out from Jericho, a great crowd followed him. And behold, two—[^1] (Matt. 20:29-30)**

This lemma carries only the scripture text, and it breaks off in mid-sentence; Theophylact provides no exposition here. His comment on the whole episode of the two blind men is set down together in Lemma 27, after the Gospel narrative has been quoted in full.

[^1]: The lemma ends «Καὶ ἰδοὺ δύο» ("And behold, two—"), completed by the following lemma with «τυφλοὶ καθήμενοι παρὰ τὴν ὁδόν» ("blind *men* sitting beside the road"). *Ἰεριχώ* is Jericho.
