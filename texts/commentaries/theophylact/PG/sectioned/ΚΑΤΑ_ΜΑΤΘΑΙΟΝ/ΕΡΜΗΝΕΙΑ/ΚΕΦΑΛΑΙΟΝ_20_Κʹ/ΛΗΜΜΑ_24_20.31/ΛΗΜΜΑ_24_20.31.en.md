# Matthew 20 (Κʹ) — Lemma 24 · Matt. 20:31

> **crowd rebuked them that they should be silent. But they cried out the more, saying: Have mercy on us, Lord, son of David. And having stood *still*, the—[^1] (Matt. 20:31)**

This lemma carries only the scripture text, and it breaks off in mid-phrase; Theophylact provides no exposition here. His comment on the whole episode of the two blind men is set down together in Lemma 27.

[^1]: The lemma completes the sentence begun in the previous lemma («But the—») and itself ends «Καὶ στὰς ὁ», article + participle, completed by the next lemma with «Ἰησοῦς ἐφώνησεν αὐτούς» ("And Jesus, having stood still, called them"). *στὰς* = "having stood *still* / having halted." The OCR «σιπήσωσιν» = «σιωπήσωσιν» ("that they should be silent"). Greek *μεῖζον ἔκραζον*, "they cried out the greater / the more."
