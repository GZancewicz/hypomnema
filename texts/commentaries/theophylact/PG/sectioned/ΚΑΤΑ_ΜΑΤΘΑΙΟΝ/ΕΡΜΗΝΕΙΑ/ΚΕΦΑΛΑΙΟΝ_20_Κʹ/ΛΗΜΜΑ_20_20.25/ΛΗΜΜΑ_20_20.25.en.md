# Matthew 20 (Κʹ) — Lemma 20 · Matt. 20:25-26

> **Jesus, having summoned them, said: You know that the rulers of the nations lord it over them,[^1] and the great *ones* exercise authority over them. It shall not be so among you, but whoever wishes to become great among you shall be your servant, and whoever wishes among you—[^2] (Matt. 20:25-26)**

This lemma carries only the scripture text, and it breaks off in mid-sentence; Theophylact provides no exposition here. His comment on the whole passage is given together in Lemma 21, once the Gospel quotation has been completed.

[^1]: The OCR reads «κατακοριεύουσιν … κατεξουσιάζουαιν αὐτ ν», securely = «κατακυριεύουσιν … κατεξουσιάζουσιν αὐτῶν» ("lord it over them … exercise authority over them"); the two intensive compounds (*κατα-κυριεύω*, *κατ-εξουσιάζω*) are a near-synonymous pair — "hold *down* in lordship" / "wield authority *down* upon" — both conveying domineering, oppressive rule.
[^2]: The lemma ends «καὶ ὃς ἂν θέλῃ ἐν ὑμῖν» ("and whoever wishes among you—"), completed by the next lemma with «εἶναι πρῶτος, ἔσται ὑμῶν δοῦλος» ("to be first, shall be your slave"). The OCR twice writes «ἡμῖν/ἡμῖν» for «ὑμῖν» ("among you"); corrected to the second person throughout, as the sense and the parallel occurrences require.
