# Matthew 20 (Κʹ) — Lemma 9 · Matt. 20:10–11

> **But when the first came, they supposed that they would receive more; and they too received a denarius apiece. But having received *it*, they murmured against the householder,[^1] (Matt. 20:10–11)**

*This lemma carries the Gospel text only; Theophylact's exposition of this segment follows at the close of the parable (Lemma 13).*

[^1]: OCR slips, securely corrected: «λήονται» → **λήψονται** ("they would receive"); «ἐγόγηυζον» → **ἐγόγγυζον** ("they murmured"). The text agrees with the received Matt. 20:10–11.
