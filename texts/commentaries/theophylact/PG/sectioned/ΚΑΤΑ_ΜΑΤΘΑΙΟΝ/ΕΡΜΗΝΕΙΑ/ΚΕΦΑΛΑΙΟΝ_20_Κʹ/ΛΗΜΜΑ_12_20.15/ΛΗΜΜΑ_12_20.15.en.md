# Matthew 20 (Κʹ) — Lemma 12 · Matt. 20:15

> **lawful for me to do what I wish with my own *things*? *Is*[^1] your eye evil, because I am good?[^2] (Matt. 20:15)**

*This lemma carries the Gospel text only; Theophylact's exposition of this segment follows at the close of the parable (Lemma 13).*

[^1]: Theophylact's text reads «εἰ ὁ ὀφθαλμός σου πονηρός ἐστιν…» — literally "*if* your eye is evil…," where the received text more usually has «ἢ ὁ ὀφθαλμός σου…» ("*Is* your eye evil…?"). The εἰ here functions to introduce a direct question (a Koine idiom), so it is rendered as an interrogative; the supplied "*Is*" is italicized. Following his text on the page rather than conforming to the common wording.
[^2]: OCR «αου» → **σου** ("your"). "Evil eye" (ὀφθαλμὸς πονηρός) is the idiom for a grudging, envious disposition.
