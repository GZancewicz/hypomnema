# Matthew 20 (Κʹ) — Lemma 1 · Matt. 20:1

> **For the kingdom of the heavens is like *to* a man, a householder, who went out early in the morning[^1] to hire laborers into his vineyard. And**

*This lemma carries only the opening words of the parable of the laborers; Theophylact's exposition of the whole parable (Matt. 20:1–7) is deferred to Lemma 7, where he begins to comment.*[^2]

[^1]: Greek *ἅμα πρωΐ*, literally "just at daybreak / together with the early *hour*"; *ἅμα* intensifies (i.e. "right at dawn"). Rendered "early in the morning" for the plain sense.
[^2]: In this PG source, lemmata 1–6 present the Gospel text of the parable broken into fragments, each ending on a hanging "And" (*Καὶ*) or particle; no comment accompanies them. The connected exposition opens at Lemma 7 (marked in the source with an opening guillemet).
