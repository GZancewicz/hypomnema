# Matthew 20 (Κʹ) — Lemma 19 · Matt. 20:24

> **And the ten, having heard, were indignant concerning the two brothers. But the—[^1] (Matt. 20:24)**

This lemma carries only the scripture text, and it breaks off in mid-phrase; Theophylact provides no exposition here. His comment on the whole passage (the indignation of the ten and the Lord's reply) is set down together in Lemma 21, after the Gospel quotation has been given in full.

[^1]: The lemma ends «Ὁ δὲ», article + particle («ὁ δὲ»), with the sentence completed by the following lemma, which supplies the subject «Ἰησοῦς» ("But Jesus…", Matt. 20:25). The supplied em-dash marks the break; the bare article "the" awaits "Jesus." Greek *ἠγανάκτησαν* ("were indignant / were displeased"), the same reaction ascribed below in the exposition (Lemma 21) to the ten toward the two sons of Zebedee.
