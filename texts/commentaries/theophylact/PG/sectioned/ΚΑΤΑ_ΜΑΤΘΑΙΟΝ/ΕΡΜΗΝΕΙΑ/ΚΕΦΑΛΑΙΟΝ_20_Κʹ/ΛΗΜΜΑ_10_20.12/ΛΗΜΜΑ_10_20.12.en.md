# Matthew 20 (Κʹ) — Lemma 10 · Matt. 20:12

> **saying that: These last worked one hour, and you made them equal to us, who bore the burden of the day and the scorching heat? But he[^1] (Matt. 20:12)**

*This lemma carries the Gospel text only; Theophylact's exposition of this segment follows at the close of the parable (Lemma 13).*

[^1]: OCR «αὐτοὺὲ» → **αὐτούς** ("them"). The clause breaks off at «Ὁ δὲ» ("But he"), completed in the next lemma. The verb «ἐποίησαν» is here rendered "worked" ("spent/did *but* one hour"); «τὸν καύσωνα» is the scorching midday heat / burning wind.
