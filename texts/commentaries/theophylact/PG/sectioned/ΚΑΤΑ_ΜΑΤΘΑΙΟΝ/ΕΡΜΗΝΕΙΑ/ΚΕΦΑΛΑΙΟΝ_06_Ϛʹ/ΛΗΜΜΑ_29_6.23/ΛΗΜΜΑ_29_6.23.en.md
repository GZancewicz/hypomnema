# Matthew 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 29 · Matt. 6:23

> **eye be evil, your whole body[^1] will be dark; if therefore the light *that is* in you is darkness, the darkness — how great *is it*?**[^2]

This he says, *namely*:[^3] If you nail your mind to the care of possessions, you have quenched the lamp, you have darkened your soul. For just as the eye, *being* single — that is, healthy — illumines the body, but if it be evil — that is, diseased — darkens *it*, so also the mind is blinded by the care. And if the mind should be darkened, the soul becomes darkness, and much more the body.

[^1]: Continuing from "but if the" that ended the previous lemma: "but if your eye be evil" (*ἐὰν δὲ ὁ ὀφθαλμός σου πονηρὸς ᾖ*). The OCR of the following words is garbled — *ὅλον ἀὴ σῶμά σου* — but is securely *ὅλον τὸ σῶμά σου* ("your whole body"), perhaps originally *ὅλον δὲ τὸ σῶμά σου*.
[^2]: Greek *τὸ σκότος πόσον;* — literally "the darkness, how much/great?" A terse rhetorical exclamation; "*is it*" is supplied.
[^3]: The OCR reads *Τοῦτό φησιν, Κσι Ἐὰν*; *Κσι* is corrupt, most plausibly *ὅτι* ("that / namely"), introducing the paraphrase that follows. Translated as a sense-marker ("*namely*"), flagged because the exact connective is uncertain. Note also *προσηλώσῃς* ("nail," aorist subjunctive), echoing the "being nailed" of Lemma 26.
