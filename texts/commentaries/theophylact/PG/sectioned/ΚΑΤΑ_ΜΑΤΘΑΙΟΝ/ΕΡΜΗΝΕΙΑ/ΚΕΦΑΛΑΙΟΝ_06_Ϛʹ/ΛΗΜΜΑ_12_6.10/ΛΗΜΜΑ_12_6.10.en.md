# Matthew 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 12 · Matt. 6:10

> **Let your kingdom come. (Matt. 6:10)**

That is, the second coming; for he who has a conscience made bold[^1] wishes[^2] the resurrection and the judgment to come.

[^1]: Greek *ὁ γὰρ συνειδὸς ἔχων πεπαρρησιασμένον* — "he who has a conscience *emboldened / made confident*." The perfect participle *πεπαρρησιασμένον* derives from *παρρησία* ("boldness, frankness, freedom of speech before God/others"); here a conscience free of guilt, which can therefore face the judgment without fear and so long for it.
[^2]: Greek *εὔχεται* (OCR *οὔχεται*) — "prays for / wishes for," here fittingly echoing the theme of prayer (*εὐχή*, cf. Lemma 10); such a man makes the coming of resurrection and judgment his very prayer.
