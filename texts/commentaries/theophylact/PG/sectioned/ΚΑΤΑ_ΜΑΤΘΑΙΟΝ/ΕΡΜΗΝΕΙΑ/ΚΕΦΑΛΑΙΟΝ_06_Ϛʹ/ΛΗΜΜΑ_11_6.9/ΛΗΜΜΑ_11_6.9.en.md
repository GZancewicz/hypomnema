# Matthew 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 11 · Matt. 6:9

> **Hallowed be your name. (Matt. 6:9)**

That is: Make us holy, so that you too may be glorified through us. For as *God* is blasphemed on account of me, so also God is hallowed on account of me[^1] — that is, is glorified as holy.

[^1]: Greek *ὡς γὰρ βλασφημεῖται δι᾽ ἐμὲ, οὕτω καὶ ἁγιάζεται δι᾽ ἐμὲ ὁ Θεός* — the subject "God" (*ὁ Θεός*) stands at the end of the second clause and governs both verbs; supplied ("*God*") in the first for sense. The parallel is exact: my conduct causes God's name to be either blasphemed or hallowed among men (cf. Rom. 2:24; Isa. 52:5).
