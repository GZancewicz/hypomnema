# Matthew 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 3 · Matt. 6:2

> **Truly I say to you, that they have received their reward in full.[^1] (Matt. 6:2)**

For, being praised, they received back the whole from men.

[^1]: Greek *ἀπέχουσι τὸν μισθὸν αὐτῶν* — *ἀπέχω* is the commercial term for having a payment "in full," acknowledging a debt discharged in its entirety. Theophylact himself glosses it with *τὸ πᾶν ἀπέλαβον* ("they received back the whole"), which is preserved here as "received back the whole."
