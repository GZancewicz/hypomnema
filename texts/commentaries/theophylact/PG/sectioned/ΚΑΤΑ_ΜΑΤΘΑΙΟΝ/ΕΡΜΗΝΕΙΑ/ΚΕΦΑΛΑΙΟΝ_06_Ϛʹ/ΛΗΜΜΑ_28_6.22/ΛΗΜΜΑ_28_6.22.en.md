# Matthew 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 28 · Matt. 6:22

> **lamp of the body is the eye;[^1] if therefore your eye be single,[^2] your whole body *is* full of light for you; but if the**[^3]

*This lemma carries only the scripture words; it has no exposition of its own. The quotation breaks off at "but if the" (ἐὰν δὲ ὁ), and is completed at the head of the next lemma («ὀφθαλμός σου πονηρὸς ᾖ», "eye be evil").*

[^1]: Continuing from the article "The" (Ὁ) that ended the previous lemma: "The lamp of the body is the eye" (*Ὁ λύχνος τοῦ σώματός ἐστιν ὁ ὀφθαλμός*).
[^2]: Greek *ἁπλοῦς*, "single, simple, undivided," here of the eye. Theophylact glosses it in the next lemma as *ὑγιής* ("healthy, sound"); rendered "single" to keep his word-play, with the gloss handled there.
[^3]: The clause "your whole body *is* full of light" (*ὅλον τὸ σῶμά σοι φωτεινόν*) has no verb in Greek; "is" is supplied. The lemma ends on "but if the" (*ἐὰν δὲ ὁ*), the sentence being finished in the next lemma.
