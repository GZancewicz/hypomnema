# Matthew 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 15 · Matt. 6:12

> **And forgive us our debts, as we also forgive our debtors. (Matt. 6:12)**[^1]

Since even after baptism we sin, we entreat that he forgive us; and he will forgive thus, as we also *do*. For if we bear a grudge,[^2] he will not forgive us; for God has me *as* a pattern, and what I do to another, he does to me.

[^1]: Greek «Καὶ ἄφες ἡμῖν τὰ ὀφειλήματα ἡμῶν, ὡς καὶ ἡμεῖς ἀφίεμεν τοῖς ὀφειλέταις ἡμῶν» (Matt. 6:12). The folder-name verse metadata reads 6.11, but the Greek is unambiguously 6:12; the header and blockquote are corrected accordingly. Theophylact's text reads the present indicative «ἀφίεμεν» ("we forgive," we do forgive), not the aorist «ἀφήκαμεν» ("we have forgiven") of many manuscripts — the difference is doctrinally live (an ongoing versus a completed forgiving), so it is rendered as it stands: "as we also forgive."
[^2]: Greek «μνησικακῶμεν», from *μνησικακέω*, "to remember wrongs, harbor resentment, be vindictive." Rendered "bear a grudge"; the cognate noun-idea returns in Lemma 19 («ἀμνησικάκους», "not remembering wrongs / free of grudges").
