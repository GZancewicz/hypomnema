# Matthew 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 39 · Matt. 6:31

> **Do not therefore be anxious, saying: What shall we eat, or what shall we drink, or what shall we put on;[^1] (Matt. 6:31)**

[^1]: This lemma-file carries only the Gospel quotation, with no exposition of its own. The clause breaks off at «what shall we put on;» and Theophylact's comment resumes in the following lemma (40), which quotes the continuation «Πάντα γὰρ ταῦτα τὰ ἔθνη ἐπιζητεῖ» ("For all these things the nations seek after") and expounds vv. 31–32 together. «περιβαλώμεθα» is middle subjunctive ("we should put around ourselves, clothe ourselves"); rendered "we… put on."
