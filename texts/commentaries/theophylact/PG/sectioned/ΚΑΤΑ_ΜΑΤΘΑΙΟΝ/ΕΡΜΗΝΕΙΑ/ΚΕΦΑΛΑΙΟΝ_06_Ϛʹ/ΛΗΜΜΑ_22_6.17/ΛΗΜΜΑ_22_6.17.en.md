# Matthew 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 22 · Matt. 6:17

> **But you, *when* fasting, anoint your head, and wash your face, so that you may not— (Matt. 6:17)**[^1]

[^1]: The particle *δέ* ("but") is present and rendered roman. Greek *σὺ δὲ νηστεύων* is a nominative participle ("you, fasting / while you fast"); "*when*" is supplied for the sense. The lemma breaks off at «ὅπως μὴ» ("so that … not"), whose verb stands in the next lemma.
