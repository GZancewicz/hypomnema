# Matthew 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 21 · Matt. 6:16

> **But whenever you fast, do not become gloomy like the hypocrites; for they disfigure their faces, so that they may appear to men *to be* fasting. Amen I say to you, that they have their reward *in full*. (Matt. 6:16)**[^1]

Disfigurement of the face is the pallor,[^2] whenever someone appears not such as he is, but feigns[^3] a gloomy look.

[^1]: The particle *δέ* ("but") is present and rendered roman. The OCR «ὅπῳς» is a slip for **«ὅπως»** ("so that"). Note the play on the root *φαν-*: they "disfigure" (*ἀφανίζουσι*, literally "make unseen / do away with") their faces precisely in order that they "may appear / be seen" (*φανῶσι*) as fasting — the same antithesis recurs in the comment. Greek *ἀπέχουσι* ("they have in full, receive back completely"); "*in full*" renders the intensive force of the compound.
[^2]: Greek *ὠχρότης* ("paleness, sallowness, pallor") — the wan complexion produced (or affected) by fasting.
[^3]: Greek *προσποιῆται στυγνότητα* — "puts on / pretends a sullen (gloomy) aspect." *στυγνότης* ("gloominess, sullenness") answers to the *σκυθρωποί* ("gloomy, sullen-faced") of the lemma.
