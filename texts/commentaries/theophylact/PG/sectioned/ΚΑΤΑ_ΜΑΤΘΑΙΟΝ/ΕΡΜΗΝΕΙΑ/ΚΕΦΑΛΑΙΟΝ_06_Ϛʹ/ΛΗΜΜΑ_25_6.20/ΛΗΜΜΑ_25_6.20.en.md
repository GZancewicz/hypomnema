# Matthew 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 25 · Matt. 6:20

> **But treasure up for yourselves treasures in heaven, where neither moth nor corrosion[^1] destroys, and where thieves do not dig through, nor steal; for where**[^2]

*This lemma carries only the scripture words; it has no exposition of its own. The quotation breaks off at "for where" (ὅπου γάρ) and is completed at the head of the next lemma, where the comment begins.*

[^1]: Greek *βρῶσις*, literally "eating, consuming, corrosion" (the KJV's "rust" at Matt. 6:20). Theophylact's own exposition (next lemma) pairs it with the moth as destroying "foods and garments" (*βρώματα καὶ ἱμάτια*), so the sense is the general "eating away / wasting" of perishable goods, not specifically metallic rust; rendered "corrosion" to keep that breadth.
[^2]: The lemma breaks off mid-sentence at *ὅπου γάρ* ("for where"); the sentence is completed in the following lemma with «ἐστιν ὁ θησαυρὸς ὑμῶν, ἐκεῖ ἔσται καὶ ἡ καρδία ὑμῶν» ("is your treasure, there will your heart also be").
