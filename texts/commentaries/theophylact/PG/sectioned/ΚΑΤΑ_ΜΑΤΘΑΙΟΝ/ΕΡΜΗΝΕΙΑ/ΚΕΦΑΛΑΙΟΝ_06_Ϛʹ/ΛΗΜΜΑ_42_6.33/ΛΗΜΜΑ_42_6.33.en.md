# Matthew 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 42 · Matt. 6:33

> **But seek first the kingdom of God, and his righteousness, and all these things shall be added to you. (Matt. 6:33)**

The kingdom of God *is* the enjoyment of the good things; and this accrues through righteousness. Whoever therefore seeks the spiritual things, to him shall be added[^1] also the bodily things, according to *the* munificence of God.[^2]

[^1]: The verb is OCR-garbled as «προσἐθεται»; securely = **«προστεθήσεται»** ("shall be added"), the very word of the lemma («καὶ ταῦτα πάντα προστεθήσεται ὑμῖν»).
[^2]: Greek «κατὰ φιλοτιμίαν [τοῦ] Θεοῦ» — «φιλοτιμία» is "love of honor," and hence "munificence, liberality, generous bounty"; here of God's open-handedness in giving the bodily things over and above the spiritual. Rendered "munificence"; "liberality" or "bounty" are equally good. The closing words of the file are badly OCR-corrupted («Π ἱὓοιαίῖΝαιο Οοἱ. Θεοῦ»); the recoverable sense is «…τοῦ Θεοῦ» ("…of God"), and the remaining garble carries no additional legible content.
