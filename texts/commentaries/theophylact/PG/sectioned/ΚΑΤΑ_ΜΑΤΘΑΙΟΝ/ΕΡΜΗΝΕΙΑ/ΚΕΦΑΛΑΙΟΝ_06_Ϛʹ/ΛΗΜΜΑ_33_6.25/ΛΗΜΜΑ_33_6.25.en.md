# Matthew 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 33 · Matt. 6:25

> **Is not the soul more than the food, and the body than the raiment? (Matt. 6:25)**[^1]

That is: He who gave the greater — he who formed the soul and the body — will he not give also both food and raiment?

[^1]: This continues the same verse (Matt. 6:25) begun in the previous lemma. Greek *ψυχή* again "soul / life" (see the note on lemma 32); here the point is that the giver of the greater gift (soul, body) will surely supply the lesser (food, raiment). *τροφή* = "food / nourishment"; *ἔνδυμα* = "raiment / clothing."
