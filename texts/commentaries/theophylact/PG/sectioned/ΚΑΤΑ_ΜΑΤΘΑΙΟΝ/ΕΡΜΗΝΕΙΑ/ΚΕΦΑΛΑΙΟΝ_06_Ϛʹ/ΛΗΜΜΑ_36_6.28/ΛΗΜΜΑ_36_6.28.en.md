# Matthew 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 36 · Matt. 6:28

> **And concerning raiment, why are you anxious? Learn well the lilies of the field, how it grows; it does not toil, nor does it spin. But I say to you, that not even… (Matt. 6:28)**[^1][^2]

[^1]: The verb «αὐξάνει» ("it grows") is singular though its subject «τὰ κρίνα» ("the lilies") is a neuter plural — the regular Greek concord of neuter-plural subject with singular verb; hence "how it grows… it does not toil, nor spin," retained rather than smoothed to "they." *καταμάθετε* = "learn thoroughly, observe closely, consider well." *κρίνα* = "lilies."
[^2]: The quotation breaks off mid-verse at «Λέγω δὲ ὑμῖν, ὅτι οὐδὲ» ("But I say to you, that not even…"), which continues into Matt. 6:29 ("…not even Solomon in all his glory…").
