# Matthew 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 2 · Matt. 6:2

> **Therefore, when you do alms, do not sound a trumpet before you, as the hypocrites do in the synagogues and in the streets, so that they may be glorified by men. (Matt. 6:2)**

The hypocrites did not have[^1] trumpets, but here he ridicules their thinking, because they wished their almsgiving to be trumpeted. And hypocrites are those who, on the one hand, are one thing, but, on the other hand, appear another; and these, at any rate, on the one hand appear merciful, but, on the other hand, are otherwise.

[^1]: The source prints *εἶνον*, an OCR corruption of *εἶχον* ("they had / were having"); the reading is secure from the sense. Likewise below the OCR *οἰ ἄλλο μλν ὄντες, ἄλλο Αὲ φαινόμενοι* is securely *οἱ ἄλλο μὲν ὄντες, ἄλλο δὲ φαινόμενοι*.
