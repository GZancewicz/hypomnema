# Matthew 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 17 · Matt. 6:13b

> **But deliver us from the evil one. (Matt. 6:13)**[^1]

He did not say, "from evil men"; for it is not those *men* who wrong us, but the evil one.

[^1]: Greek «Ἀλλὰ ῥῦσαι ἡμᾶς ἀπὸ τοῦ πονηροῦ» (Matt. 6:13). «τοῦ πονηροῦ» is ambiguous in form — neuter ("from evil") or masculine ("from the evil one"). Theophylact resolves it decisively as masculine and personal: he contrasts it with «τῶν πονηρῶν ἀνθρώπων» ("evil men") and then names «ὁ πονηρός» ("the evil one," i.e. the devil) as the true wrongdoer. Rendered "the evil one" to follow his reading; "from evil" would be the neuter alternative he rejects.
