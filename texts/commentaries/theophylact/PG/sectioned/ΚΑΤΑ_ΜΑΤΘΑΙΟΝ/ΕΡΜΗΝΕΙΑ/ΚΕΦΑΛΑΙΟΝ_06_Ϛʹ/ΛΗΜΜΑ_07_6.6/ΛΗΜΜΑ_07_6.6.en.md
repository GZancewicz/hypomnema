# Matthew 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 7 · Matt. 6:6

> **But you, when you pray, enter into your chamber,[^1] and having shut your door, pray to your Father who is in secret, and your Father who sees in secret will render to you in the open. (Matt. 6:6)**

What then? Should I not pray in church? Yes, indeed I should — but with a right intention, and so as not to make a display. For the place does not do harm, but the manner and the aim.[^2] Many, at any rate, praying in secret, do this for the pleasing of men.[^3]

[^1]: Greek *ταμεῖον* — a private inner room, storeroom, or treasury-chamber; the innermost, closed-off room of a house. Rendered "chamber" for the plain sense; "closet"/"inner room"/"storeroom" are equally defensible.
[^2]: Greek *ἐπεὶ ὁ τόπος οὐ βλάπτει, ἀλλ᾽ ὁ τρόπος καὶ ὁ σκοπός* — "since the place does not harm, but the manner and the aim *do*." The verb "does harm" is not repeated in the second clause; supplied.
[^3]: Greek *πρὸς ἀνθρωπαρεσκείαν* (OCR *Ἀνθρωπαρεσκίαν*) — "toward man-pleasing," i.e. to win human approval; the same fault Christ condemns in the openly public prayer.
