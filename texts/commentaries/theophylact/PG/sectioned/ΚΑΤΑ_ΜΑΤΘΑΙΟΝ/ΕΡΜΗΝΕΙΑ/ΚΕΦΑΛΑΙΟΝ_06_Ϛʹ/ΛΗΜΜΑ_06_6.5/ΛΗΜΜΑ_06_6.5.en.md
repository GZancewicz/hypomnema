# Matthew 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 6 · Matt. 6:5

> **And when you pray, you shall not be[^1] as the hypocrites, for they love to pray standing in the synagogues and in the corners of the broad streets, so that they may appear to men. Truly I say to you, that they have received their reward in full. (Matt. 6:5)**

These too he names hypocrites, as seeming to attend to God but attending to men; from whom they have it in full — that is, they receive their reward.

[^1]: The source prints *οὐκ ἱσῃ*, an OCR corruption of *οὐκ ἔσῃ* ("you shall not be," singular), the Byzantine reading Theophylact follows; the modern critical text has the plural *οὐκ ἔσεσθε*.
