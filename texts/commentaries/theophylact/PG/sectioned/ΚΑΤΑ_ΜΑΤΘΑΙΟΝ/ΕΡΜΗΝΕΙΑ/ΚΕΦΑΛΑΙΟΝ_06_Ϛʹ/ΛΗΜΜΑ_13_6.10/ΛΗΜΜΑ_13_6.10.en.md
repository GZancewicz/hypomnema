# Matthew 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 13 · Matt. 6:10

> **Let thy will be done, as in heaven, also upon the earth. (Matt. 6:10)**[^1]

Just as, he says, the angels do thy will, so also grant to us to do it,[^2]

[^1]: Greek OCR «Γʹνηθήτω τὸ θέλημά σου ὡς ἐν οὐρανῷ καὶ ἐπὶ τῆς γῆς», securely = **«Γενηθήτω τὸ θέλημά σου, ὡς ἐν οὐρανῷ, καὶ ἐπὶ τῆς γῆς»** (Matt. 6:10). The stray «ν» after «γῆς» in the source is a mis-OCR of the closing guillemet «»». The clause is anarthrous at «οὐρανῷ» ("in heaven," no article); the connective «καὶ» is rendered "also" — the correlative "so" that English idiom wants for «ὥσπερ … οὕτω» in the comment is not present here and is left unsupplied to keep the Greek visible.
[^2]: The lemma breaks off mid-sentence: the exposition ends with «ποιεῖν αὐτό,» ("to do it,") followed by a comma in the source, with no main verb of purpose or result completing the thought. Translated as it stands; the sense ("grant us to do it *as the angels do*") is plain from «οὕτω καὶ» ("so also"). Greek «δὸς ἡμῖν» is "give/grant to us"; «ἡμῖν» ("to us") stands emphatically before the infinitive.
