# Matthew 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 18 · Matt. 6:13c

> **For thine is the kingdom and the power and the glory unto the ages. Amen. (Matt. 6:13)**[^1]

He emboldens us here. For if the Father is king, and powerful, and glorious, then altogether we too shall conquer the evil one, and shall be glorified afterward.[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Ὅτι σοῦ ἐστιν ἡ βασιλεία καὶ ἡ δύναμις καὶ ἡ δόξα εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας, Ἀμήν» (Matt. 6:13, the doxology). «εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας» rendered "unto the ages" (literal), not the familiar "for ever"; the doxology is absent from many manuscripts but stands in Theophylact's text and is expounded here.
[^2]: OCR corruptions in this lemma are securely recoverable and low-stakes: «χὰρ» = «γὰρ» ("for"); «θστερον» = «ὕστερον» ("afterward, later"). The exposition unfolds the doxology's three nouns as three predicates of the Father — «βασιλεύς … δυνατὸς … ἔνδοξος» ("king … powerful … glorious"), answering «βασιλεία … δύναμις … δόξα» ("kingdom … power … glory") — so that the worshipper is emboldened to victory.
