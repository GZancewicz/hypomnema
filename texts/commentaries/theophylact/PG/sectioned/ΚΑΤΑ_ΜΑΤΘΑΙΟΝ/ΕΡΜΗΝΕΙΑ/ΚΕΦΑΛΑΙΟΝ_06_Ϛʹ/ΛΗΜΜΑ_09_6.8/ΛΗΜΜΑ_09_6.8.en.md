# Matthew 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 9 · Matt. 6:8

> **Do not, therefore, be made like them, for your Father knows the things of which you have need before you ask him. (Matt. 6:8)**

For not in order that we may teach *him* do we ask, but that, having detached ourselves from the affairs of life,[^1] we may be benefited by conversing with him.

[^1]: Greek *ἵνα ἑαυτοὺς ἀπασχολήσαντες τῶν βιωτικῶν* — literally "that, having occupied ourselves *away* from the things of life." The verb *ἀπασχολέω* here carries a separative force with the genitive *τῶν βιωτικῶν* ("worldly / everyday affairs"): prayer withdraws us from mundane business so that we profit from communion with God, not so that we inform him of our needs.
