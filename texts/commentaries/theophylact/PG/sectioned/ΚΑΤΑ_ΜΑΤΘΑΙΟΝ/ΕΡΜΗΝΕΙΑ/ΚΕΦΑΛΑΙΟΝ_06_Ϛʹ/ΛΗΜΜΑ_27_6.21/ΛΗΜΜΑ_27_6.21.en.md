# Matthew 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 27 · Matt. 6:21

> **For where your treasure *is*, there also *is* your heart. The**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture words — a re-citation of the verse just expounded, leading into the next section. The quotation breaks off at "The" (Ὁ), the opening word of Matt. 6:22, and is continued at the head of the next lemma («λύχνος τοῦ σώματός ἐστιν ὁ ὀφθαλμός», "lamp of the body is the eye"). There is no exposition here.*

[^1]: The verb "is" is supplied both times (the Greek *ὅπου γὰρ ὁ θησαυρὸς ὑμῶν, ἐκεῖ καὶ ἡ καρδία ὑμῶν* has no copula). The lemma ends on the bare article *Ὁ* ("The"), which begins the next sentence, "The lamp of the body is the eye."
