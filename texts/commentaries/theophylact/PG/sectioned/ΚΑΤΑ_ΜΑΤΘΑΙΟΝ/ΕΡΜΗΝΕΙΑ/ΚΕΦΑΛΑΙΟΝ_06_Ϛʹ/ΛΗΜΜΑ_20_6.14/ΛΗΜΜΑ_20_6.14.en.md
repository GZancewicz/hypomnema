# Matthew 6 (Ϛʹ) — Lemma 20 · Matt. 6:15

> **But if you do not forgive men their trespasses, neither will your Father forgive your trespasses. (Matt. 6:15)**[^1]

Nothing does the gentle God so hate as savagery.[^2]

[^1]: The folder-name assigns this lemma to 6:14, but the Greek is the verse that follows, **Matt. 6:15**; the header and reference are corrected accordingly. The OCR «Ἑὰν» is a slip for **«Ἐὰν»** ("if"). The particle *δέ* ("but") is present and rendered roman.
[^2]: Greek *ὠμότητα* (from *ὠμότης*, literally "rawness," hence "cruelty, savagery, ferocity"). The adjective *πρᾶος* ("gentle, meek") is set against it as a deliberate antithesis: the meek God most hates the raw/brutal disposition. Rendered "savagery" to carry over from the *ἐκθηριώμεθα* ("become savage") of the preceding lemma.
