# Matthew 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 25 · Matt. 9:26

> **…and there went out this report into all that land. (Matt. 9:26)**[^1]

Where *there is* a crowd and distraction,[^2] Jesus does not work wonders. If you be deadened by *your* sins, then you will rise, when he grasps your practical hand,[^3] having cast out the crowd and the distraction.

[^1]: This lemma completes the sentence broken off at the end of the preceding lemma (9:25), which ended «…καὶ ἡγέρθη τὸ κοράσιον, καὶ» ("…and the maiden arose, and"); the opening word *ἐξῆλθεν* here supplies its verb. The carried words «ἐξῆλθεν ἡ φήμη αὕτη εἰς ὅλην τὴν γῆν ἐκείνην» are Matt. 9:26. Greek *φήμη* spans "report, rumor, common talk, fame"; rendered "report" for the plain sense rather than importing a fixed English-Bible wording. The folder metadata verse (9.25) reflects the raising of the girl that the exposition also looks back to; the words actually quoted belong to v. 26.
[^2]: Greek *περισπασμός* — "distraction, being pulled this way and that, turmoil"; paired here with *ὄχλος* ("crowd"). The same pair recurs in the next sentence.
[^3]: Greek *τῆς χειρὸς τῆς πρακτικῆς*, literally "the practical hand" — the *hand of action / practice*, i.e. the active-practical faculty (as opposed to the contemplative). Rendered "practical hand" to keep the technical term visible; the sense is the hand by which one does deeds.
