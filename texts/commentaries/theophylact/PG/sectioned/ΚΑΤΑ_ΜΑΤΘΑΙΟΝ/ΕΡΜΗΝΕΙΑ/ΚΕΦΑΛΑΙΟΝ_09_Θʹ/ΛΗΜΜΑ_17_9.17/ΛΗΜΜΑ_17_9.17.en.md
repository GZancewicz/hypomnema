# Matthew 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 17 · Matt. 9:17

> **…do they put new wine into old wineskins; but if not,[^1] the wineskins burst, and the wine is poured out, and the wineskins perish; but they put new wine into new wineskins, and both are preserved together. (Matt. 9:17)**

Not yet, he says, have the disciples become strong, but they need condescension;[^2] and one must not lay upon them a burden of injunctions. And these things he said, teaching also the disciples, that they too, when they make disciples of the inhabited world,[^3] might condescend. The unfulled rag, therefore, is fasting, and so is the new wine; but the old garment and the wineskins are the weakness of the disciples.

[^1]: This lemma continues the sentence begun in the previous one ("Nor…"). Greek *εἰ δὲ μήγε*, literally "but if not *so*," i.e. otherwise. The closing *ἀμφότερα συντηροῦνται* = "both are preserved together."
[^2]: Greek *συγκατάβασις*, "condescension, accommodation" — a gracious lowering of oneself to another's weakness; the disciples must be dealt with gently, not burdened. The cognate verb *συγκαταβαίνωσι* ("might condescend") recurs just below.
[^3]: Greek *ὅταν μαθητεύωσι τὴν οἰκουμένην*, "when they disciple the inhabited world," i.e. make disciples of all the *οἰκουμένη*, the inhabited earth.
