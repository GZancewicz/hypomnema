# Matthew 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 28 · Matt. 9:29

> **Then he touched their eyes, saying: According to your faith let it be done to you. And… (Matt. 9:29)**[^1]

[^1]: This lemma carries only the scripture quotation, which breaks off mid-sentence at «Καὶ» ("And…"); the sentence is completed in the following lemma with «ἀνεῴχθησαν αὐτῶν οἱ ὀφθαλμοί» ("their eyes were opened"), where Theophylact's exposition begins. There is no commentary attached to this lemma itself.
