# Matthew 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 18 · Matt. 9:18

> **While he was speaking these things to them, behold, a certain ruler was doing obeisance[^1] to him, saying: "My daughter has just now died; but having come, lay your hand upon her, and she will live"; and[^2] (Matt. 9:18)**

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text, which breaks off at "and"; Theophylact's comment begins under the following lemma.*

[^1]: Greek *προσεκύνει* (imperfect), "was doing obeisance / prostrating himself / worshipping"; *προσκυνέω* covers reverent kneeling before a superior as well as worship offered to God.
[^2]: The sentence breaks off at «καὶ» ("and…"); it is completed at the head of the next lemma ("…Jesus, having risen, followed him, and his disciples").
