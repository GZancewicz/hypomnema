# Matthew 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 29 · Matt. 9:30

> **…their eyes were opened. (Matt. 9:30)**[^1]

In the house he heals, and privately, on account of his freedom from love-of-glory.[^2] For everywhere he teaches to be humble-minded.

[^1]: This lemma completes the sentence broken off at the end of the preceding lemma (9:29), which ended «…Καὶ» ("…And"); the carried words «ἀνεῴχθησαν αὐτῶν οἱ ὀφθαλμοί» are the opening of Matt. 9:30.
[^2]: Greek *διὰ τὸ ἀφιλόδοξον*, "on account of the un-vainglorious *quality*" — his not being a lover of glory (*φιλοδοξία*). Rendered "freedom from love-of-glory" to keep the privative *ἀ-* visible; cf. the "humble-minded" (*ταπεινοφρονεῖν*) that follows.
