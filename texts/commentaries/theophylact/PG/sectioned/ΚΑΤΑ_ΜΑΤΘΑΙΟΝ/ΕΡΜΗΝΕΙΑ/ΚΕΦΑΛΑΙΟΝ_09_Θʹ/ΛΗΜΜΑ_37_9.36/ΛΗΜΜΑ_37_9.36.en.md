# Matthew 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 37 · Matt. 9:36

> **But having seen the crowds he was moved with compassion concerning them, because they were exhausted[^1] and cast down like sheep not having a shepherd. (Matt. 9:36)**

They had no shepherd, since their rulers not only did not set *them* right, but indeed even did *them* harm; and it belongs to a true shepherd to be moved with compassion over the flock.

[^1]: Greek OCR «ἐκλελυμένοι καὶ ἐῤῥιμμένοι». Theophylact's first participle is *ἐκλελυμένοι* ("unloosed, worn out, fainting, exhausted"), where the common Gospel text reads *ἐσκυλμένοι* ("harassed, distressed"); the second, *ἐρριμμένοι* ("thrown down, cast down / scattered"), agrees with the received text. Translated as the page stands per the rule of following his Greek rather than conforming to a familiar wording; "exhausted" for *ἐκλελυμένοι*, which could also be rendered "worn out" or "fainting."
