# Matthew 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 31 · Matt. 9:31

> **…having gone out, spread the news of him in all that land. (Matt. 9:31)**[^1]

Do you see the freedom-from-ostentation?[^2] But those *men* spread the news not as *ones* disobeying, but as *ones* giving thanks. But if elsewhere he appears saying, "Go away, and recount the glory of God,"[^3] *there is* nothing contrary: for he wishes, on the one hand, to say nothing about himself, but, on the other hand, to recount the glory of God.

[^1]: This lemma is a continuation: the Gospel sentence begins at the end of the previous lemma (30), which carries «Οἱ δὲ» ("But they"). The full verse is «Οἱ δὲ ἐξελθόντες διεφήμισαν αὐτὸν ἐν ὅλῃ τῇ γῇ ἐκείνῃ» ("But they, having gone out, spread the news of him in all that land"). *διεφήμισαν* is "spread abroad the report/fame of." Rendered here from Theophylact's Greek without conforming to any English Bible.
[^2]: Greek *τὸ ἄτυφον* — literally "the un-puffed-up *thing*," i.e. freedom from vanity or self-display; the abstract neuter noun names the quality Christ shows in forbidding the healed men to publicize him. Alternatively "the modesty / lack of pomp."
[^3]: A conflated allusion to the parallel healing narratives where Christ tells the healed man to declare God's work (cf. Luke 8:39, "Return to thine own house, and shew how great things God hath done unto thee"; also Mark 5:19). Greek here: *Ἄπελθε, καὶ διηγοῦ τὴν δόξαν τοῦ Θεοῦ* ("Go away, and recount the glory of God"). Theophylact quotes loosely; translated as it stands on the page.
