# Matthew 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 33 · Matt. 9:33

> **…the demon having been cast out, the dumb *man* spoke. (Matt. 9:33)**[^1]

The affliction was not natural, but of the demon; wherefore also others bring him forward.[^2] For he was not able through himself to make entreaty, the demon having bound his tongue. Whence neither does he demand faith,[^3] but immediately he heals *him*, having cast out the demon, the one hindering the speech.

[^1]: This lemma completes the sentence begun in the previous lemma (32): «Καὶ ἐκβληθέντος τοῦ δαιμονίου, ἐλάλησεν ὁ κωφός» ("And the demon having been cast out, the dumb *man* spoke"). The genitive absolute *ἐκβληθέντος τοῦ δαιμονίου* is kept as a participial phrase.
[^2]: Greek *ἕτεροι προσάγουσιν αὐτόν* — "others bring him forward / present him," i.e. because the mute man could not speak for himself, others had to bring him to Christ. The OCR «πρυσἐγουσιν» is securely *προσάγουσιν*.
[^3]: Greek *οὐδὲ ἀπαιτεῖ πίστιν* — "neither does he demand faith," the point being that, since the man could not speak to profess faith, Christ heals without first requiring it. The OCR «ἰται» is *ἰᾶται* ("he heals").
