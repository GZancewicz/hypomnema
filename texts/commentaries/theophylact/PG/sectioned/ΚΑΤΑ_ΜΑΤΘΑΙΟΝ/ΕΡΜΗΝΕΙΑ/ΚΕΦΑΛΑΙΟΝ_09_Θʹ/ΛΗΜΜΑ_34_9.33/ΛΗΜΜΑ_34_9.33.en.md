# Matthew 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 34 · Matt. 9:33

> **And the crowds marveled, saying that never was *it* manifested thus in Israel. (Matt. 9:33)**[^1]

The crowd, having marveled, sets Christ above both *the* prophets and *the* patriarchs. For with authority he was healing, and not as those *men did*, *who healed* by praying.[^2] But let us see also what the Pharisees say:[^3] But they…[^4]

[^1]: Greek «Καὶ ἐθαύμασαν οἱ ὄχλοι, λέγοντες ὅτι οὐδέποτε ἐφάνη οὕτως ἐν τῷ Ἰσραήλ» — "never did *it* appear/was *it* shown thus in Israel." *ἐφάνη* is impersonal ("it appeared, such a thing was seen"). The OCR «βρλοι» is *ὄχλοι* ("crowds"), «οὐβέποτε» is *οὐδέποτε* ("never").
[^2]: Greek *οὐχ ὡς ἐκεῖνοι, εὐχόμενοι* — "not as those *men*, praying," i.e. unlike the prophets and patriarchs, who worked cures by petitioning God in prayer; Christ healed by his own authority (*μετ᾽ ἐξουσίας*). The supplied words fill out the elliptical comparison.
[^3]: The OCR «Ἵω μεν» is ambiguous between *ἴδωμεν* ("let us see / consider") and *ἴωμεν* ("let us go / proceed"). With the following *τί φασιν* ("what they say"), the sense is "let us see / turn to consider what the Pharisees say"; rendered "let us see." Greek: *ἴδωμεν δὲ καὶ τοὺς Φαρισαίους τί φασιν.*
[^4]: The lemma ends with «Οἱ δὲ» ("But they"), the opening of the next Gospel quotation, which is completed in the following lemma (35): «Οἱ δὲ Φαρισαῖοι ἔλεγον…» ("But the Pharisees said…").
