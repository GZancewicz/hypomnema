# Matthew 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 10 · Matt. 9:9

…by *the* word alone to convert him.[^1]

[^1]: Greek «λόγῳ μόνῳ μεταστρέψαι αὐτόν». This completes the clause «Θεοῦ δὲ ἔργον, τῷ…» of Lemma 9: "but *it is* God's work, to convert him by *the* word alone." *μεταστρέψαι* is "to turn about / turn around / convert." The opening «...» in the source-file before *λόγῳ* is a stray quotation-mark artifact, not the head of a new lemma.
