# Matthew 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 1 · Matt. 9:1–2

> **And having embarked into the boat, he crossed over and came into his own city. And behold, they were bringing to him a paralytic laid upon *a* bed. (Matt. 9:1–2)**[^1]

By "his own city" he means Capernaum, for there he dwelt. For, on the one hand, Bethlehem bore *him*, and Nazareth reared *him*, but Capernaum had *him* continually dwelling *there*. But this paralytic is a different one from the *one* in John;[^2] for that one, on the one hand, *was* by the Sheep *Pool* in Jerusalem, but this one *is* in Capernaum. And the one, on the one hand, had no man, but this one is carried by four, as Mark says, so that he was even let down through the roof — which *thing* Matthew omitted.

[^1]: The OCR carries several minor, securely recoverable corruptions here, corrected in the reading above: «ηλθεν» → *ἦλθεν* ("came"), «κλίὕης» → *κλίνης* ("bed/couch"). The lemma is anarthrous at «ἐπὶ κλίνης» ("upon *a* bed"), so the supplied "*a*" is italicized; "the boat" (*τὸ πλοῖον*) carries the article and is roman.
[^2]: The reference is to the paralytic of John 5:2–9, healed «παρὰ τῇ προβατικῇ» — literally "by the sheep *[pool]*." Greek *προβατική* ("of the sheep") is the adjective standing alone for the pool/gate near which the sick lay (John's *ἡ προβατικὴ κολυμβήθρα*, "the Sheep Pool"); the noun is unexpressed, so "*Pool*" is supplied in italics.
