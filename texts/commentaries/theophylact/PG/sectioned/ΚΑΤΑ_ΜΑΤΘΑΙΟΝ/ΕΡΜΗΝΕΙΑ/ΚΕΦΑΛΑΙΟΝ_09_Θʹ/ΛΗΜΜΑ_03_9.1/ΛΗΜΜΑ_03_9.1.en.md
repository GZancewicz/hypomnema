# Matthew 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 3 · Matt. 9:2

> **He said to the paralytic: Take courage, child, your sins have been forgiven you.[^1] (Matt. 9:2)**

He calls *him* "child," either as *a* thing formed by God,[^2] or as one who had believed; and, showing that the paralysis *is* especially from sins, he looses those first.

[^1]: Greek «ἀφέωνταί σοι αἱ ἁμαρτίαι σου» — perfect passive «ἀφέωνται», "have been forgiven / stand forgiven"; the perfect marks a completed act with abiding effect, rendered "have been forgiven you."
[^2]: Greek «ὡς πλάσμα Θεοῦ» — *πλάσμα* is a "thing moulded/formed," God's handiwork (cf. the potter's clay); rendered "*a* thing formed by God" to keep the sense of creaturely fashioning rather than the smoother "creature."
