# Matthew 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 38 · Matt. 9:37

> **Then he says to his disciples, The harvest is great, but the workers are few;[^1] (Matt. 9:37)**

[^1]: Greek «Ὁ μὲν θερισμὸς πολύς, οἱ δὲ ἐργάται ὀλίγοι». The correlative *μὲν … δέ* is rendered as a plain contrastive ("… but …"), μέν left untranslated; the copulas "is/are" are supplied, the Greek having none. OCR «Τόιε» = *Τότε* ("then") and «σλίγοι» = *ὀλίγοι* ("few"), both securely recoverable. The sentence breaks off mid-verse and continues in Lemma 39 with «δεήθητε οὖν τοῦ Κυρίου τοῦ θερισμοῦ …».
