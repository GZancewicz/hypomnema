# Matthew 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 38 · Matt. 9:37

> **Then he says to his disciples, The harvest on the one hand *is* great, but the workers *are* few;[^1] (Matt. 9:37)**

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation, which breaks off mid-sentence; Theophylact's exposition of it, together with the completion of the verse and Matt. 9:38, follows in the next lemma.*[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Ὁ μὲν θερισμὸς πολύς, οἱ δὲ ἐργάται ὀλίγοι». The correlative *μὲν … δέ* is rendered "on the one hand … but" (both being present particles, hence roman, not italic); the two forms of "*is*/*are*" are supplied, the Greek having no copula. OCR «Τόιε» = *Τότε* ("then") and «σλίγοι» = *ὀλίγοι* ("few"), both securely recoverable.
[^2]: The sentence continues in Lemma 39 with «δεήθητε οὖν τοῦ Κυρίου τοῦ θερισμοῦ …» ("pray therefore to the Lord of the harvest …"), where the commentary proper begins.
