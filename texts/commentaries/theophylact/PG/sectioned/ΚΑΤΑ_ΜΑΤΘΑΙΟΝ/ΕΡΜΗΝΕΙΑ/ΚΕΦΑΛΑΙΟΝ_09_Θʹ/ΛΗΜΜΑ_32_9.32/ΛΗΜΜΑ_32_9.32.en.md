# Matthew 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 32 · Matt. 9:32

> **But as they were going out, behold, they brought to him a man dumb,[^1] possessed by a demon. And…[^2] (Matt. 9:32)**

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation; the exposition begins in the next lemma (33), which completes the sentence and comments on it.*

[^1]: Greek *κωφόν* — the adjective can mean "deaf" or "dumb (mute)." Here the sense is "mute," as the next lemma makes explicit: «ἐλάλησεν ὁ κωφός» ("the dumb *man* spoke") and «τὸν τὴν λαλιὰν κωλύοντα» ("the *demon* hindering speech"). Rendered "dumb."
[^2]: The lemma breaks off at «Καὶ» ("And"); the Gospel sentence is completed at the head of the next lemma: «Καὶ ἐκβληθέντος τοῦ δαιμονίου, ἐλάλησεν ὁ κωφός» ("And the demon having been cast out, the dumb *man* spoke"). The OCR has «Κνθρωπον» for «ἄνθρωπον» ("a man").
