# Matthew 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 35 · Matt. 9:34

> **…*the* Pharisees were saying: By the ruler of the demons he casts out the demons. (Matt. 9:34)**[^1]

The words *are* of utmost folly. For no demon casts out a demon. But let it be *granted* that he cast out the demons, as *one* serving the ruler of the demons, that is to say, *as one* practicing magic:[^2] but the diseases and the sins — how was he loosing *them*, and proclaiming *a* kingdom? For the demon, on the contrary rather, both brings on diseases and draws *one* away from God.

[^1]: This lemma completes the sentence begun at the end of the previous lemma (34): «Οἱ δὲ Φαρισαῖοι ἔλεγον· ἐν τῷ ἄρχοντι τῶν δαιμονίων ἐκβάλλει τὰ δαιμόνια» ("But the Pharisees were saying: By the ruler of the demons he casts out the demons"). *ἐν τῷ ἄρχοντι* is instrumental, "by/through the ruler." The OCR «φαρισεῖος» is *Φαρισαῖοι* ("Pharisees").
[^2]: Greek *ἤτοι μαγεύων* — "that is, practicing magic / working sorcery," glossing what serving the demons' ruler would amount to. Theophylact grants the hypothesis only to refute it: sorcery could not undo diseases and sins.
