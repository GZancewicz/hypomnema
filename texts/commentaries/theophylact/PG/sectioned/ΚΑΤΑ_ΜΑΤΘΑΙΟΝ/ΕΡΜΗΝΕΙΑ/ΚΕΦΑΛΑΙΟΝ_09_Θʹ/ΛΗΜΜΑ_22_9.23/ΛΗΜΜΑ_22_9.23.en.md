# Matthew 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 22 · Matt. 9:23

> **And Jesus, having come into the house of the ruler, and having seen the flute-players and the crowd making a tumult,…[^1] (Matt. 9:23)**

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text; Theophylact's exposition begins in the following lemma. The quoted sentence breaks off here and is completed there with "…he says to them: Withdraw…"*

[^1]: The lemma is cut off mid-sentence; the main clause follows in Lemma 23. Greek *τοὺς αὐλητάς* — "the flute-players" (the hired mourners), *τὸν ὄχλον θορυβούμενον* — "the crowd being in an uproar / making a tumult."
