# Matthew 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 11 · Matt. 9:10

> **And it came to pass, as he was reclining *at table* in the house, that behold, many tax-collectors and sinners, having come, were reclining together with[^1] Jesus and his disciples; and[^2] (Matt. 9:10)**

[^1]: The OCR is corrupt here: the source reads «ἐλθόντε· σκανἑκειντο», securely recoverable as *ἐλθόντες συνανέκειντο* ("having come, they were reclining together with"), the standard text of Matt. 9:10. *ἀνακειμένου* / *συνανέκειντο* are the verbs for reclining at a meal; "*at table*" is supplied for sense.
[^2]: The citation breaks off at *καὶ* ("and"); it is continued in Lemma 12 with «ἰδόντες οἱ Φαρισαῖοι…» ("the Pharisees, having seen…").
