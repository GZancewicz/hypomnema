# Matthew 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 20 · Matt. 9:20–21

> **And behold, a woman having a flow of blood twelve years, having come up from behind, touched the hem of his garment.[^1] For she said within herself: If only I touch his garment, I shall be made whole.[^2] But the…[^3] (Matt. 9:20–21)**

[^1]: OCR notes for the lemma, all securely recoverable: *προσελδοῦσα* → **προσελθοῦσα** ("having come/approached," δ for θ); *ἑκυτῇ* → **ἑαυτῇ** ("herself"); *ἰματίου* → **ἱματίου** ("garment," twice).
[^2]: Greek *σωθήσομαι*. The verb *σῴζω* here spans "be saved" and "be healed/made whole"; the same root recurs deliberately in the next lemma (*σέσωκέ σε*, *ἐσώθη*), where the physical healing is read as salvation through faith. Rendered "made whole" for the plain sense of bodily cure, with the wider sense in view.
[^3]: The lemma is cut off at «Ὁ δὲ» ("But the…"); the sentence resumes in Lemma 21.
