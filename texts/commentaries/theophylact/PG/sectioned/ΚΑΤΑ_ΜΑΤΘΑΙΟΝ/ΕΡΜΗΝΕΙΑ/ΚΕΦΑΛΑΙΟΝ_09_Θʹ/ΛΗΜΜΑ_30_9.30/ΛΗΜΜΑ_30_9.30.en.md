# Matthew 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 30 · Matt. 9:30

> **And Jesus sternly charged them, saying: See *that* no one know *it*. But they… (Matt. 9:30)**[^1]

[^1]: This lemma carries only the scripture quotation, which breaks off mid-sentence at «Οἱ δὲ» ("But they…"); the sentence is completed in the following lemma with «ἐξελθόντες διεφήμισαν αὐτὸν ἐν ὅλῃ τῇ γῇ ἐκείνῃ» ("going out, they spread abroad *the report of* him in all that land"), where Theophylact's exposition begins. There is no commentary attached to this lemma itself. Greek *ἐνεβριμήσατο* (from *ἐμβριμάομαι*), rendered "sternly charged," carries a note of vehemence — to snort with displeasure, to admonish urgently and strictly.
