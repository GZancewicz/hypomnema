# Matthew 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 30 · Matt. 9:30

> **And Jesus sternly charged them, saying: See that no one know it. But they… (Matt. 9:30)**[^1]

[^1]: Greek *ἐνεβριμήσατο* (from *ἐμβριμάομαι*), rendered "sternly charged," carries a note of vehemence — to snort with displeasure, to admonish urgently and strictly.
