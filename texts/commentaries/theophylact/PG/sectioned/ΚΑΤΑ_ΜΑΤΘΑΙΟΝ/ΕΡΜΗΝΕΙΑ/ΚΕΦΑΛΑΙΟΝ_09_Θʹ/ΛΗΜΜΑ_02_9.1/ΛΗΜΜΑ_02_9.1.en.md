# Matthew 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 2 · Matt. 9:2

> **And Jesus, having seen their faith. (Matt. 9:2)**

*The faith* of those who brought *him*[^1] — for indeed often on account of the faith of those who bring *the sick* he works a miracle — or also that of that *man* himself.

[^1]: The exposition is elliptical, answering the implied question "whose faith?" Greek «Ἡ τῶν προσενεγκάντων» is a bare feminine article agreeing with the understood *πίστις* ("faith") of the lemma: "*The faith* of those who brought *him*." Theophylact glosses the ambiguous "their" of the Gospel — either the bearers' faith, «ἢ καὶ αὐτοῦ ἐκείνου» ("or also that of that *man* himself," i.e. the paralytic's own).
