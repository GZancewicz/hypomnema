# Matthew 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 4 · Matt. 9:3–4

> **And behold, certain ones of the scribes said within themselves: This *man* blasphemes. And Jesus, having seen their thoughts, said: To what end do you think evil things in your hearts? For what[^1] (Matt. 9:3–4)**[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Ἵνα τί ὑμεῖς ἐνθυμεῖσθε πονηρὰ» — «ἵνα τί» is the idiom "to what end / why," rendered "To what end" to keep the purpose-force of the phrase visible.
[^2]: The OCR carries minor corruptions, corrected above: «Οἷτος» → *Οὗτος* ("this *man*"), «μὐτῶν» → *αὐτῶν* ("their").
