# Matthew 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 12 · Matt. 9:11

> **…*the Pharisees*, having seen *it*, said to his disciples: Why does your teacher eat with the tax-collectors and sinners?[^1] (Matt. 9:11)**

Matthew, being gladdened at the reception of Christ,[^2] called the tax-collectors; and Christ dines together with them, so as to benefit them, even if on account of this he was being reproached. For the Pharisees, wishing to draw his disciples away from him, kept slandering[^3] the dining-together with the tax-collectors.

[^1]: The head of this lemma continues the citation of Lemma 11 (Matt. 9:10), so it opens mid-sentence: *ἰδόντες οἱ Φαρισαῖοι* ("the Pharisees, having seen *it*"). The OCR «Κμετὰ» is simply *μετὰ* ("with"). *ὁ διδάσκαλος ὑμῶν* is "your teacher / master."
[^2]: Greek *τῇ τοῦ Χριστοῦ ὑποδοχῇ* — *ὑποδοχή* is "reception, welcome, entertaining *as a guest*." The sense is Matthew's joy at receiving Christ *into his house*; he then invited his fellow tax-collectors.
[^3]: Greek *διέβαλλον* (imperfect of *διαβάλλω*) — "were slandering / kept casting aspersions on / attacking," the imperfect giving repeated or ongoing action; *διαβάλλω* is the verb cognate with *διάβολος* ("slanderer, devil"). *ἐψέγετο* just above (also imperfect) is "was being blamed/reproached."
