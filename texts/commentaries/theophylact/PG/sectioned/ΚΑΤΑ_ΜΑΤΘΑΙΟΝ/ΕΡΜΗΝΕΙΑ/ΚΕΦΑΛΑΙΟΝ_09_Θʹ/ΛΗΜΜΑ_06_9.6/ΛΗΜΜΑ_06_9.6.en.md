# Matthew 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 6 · Matt. 9:6

> **But that you may know that the Son of Man has authority upon the earth to forgive sins — then he says to the paralytic: Having risen, take up your bed, and go into your house. And …[^1] (Matt. 9:6)**

[^1]: The OCR is corrupt at several points here, corrected above from the plain sense: «Ταδὲ» → *Ἵνα δὲ* ("But that"), «ἐπὶ τῆσ τῆς» → *ἐπὶ τῆς γῆς* ("upon the earth"), «ἅρόν» → *ἆρον* ("take up"), and the fused «ἀφιέναιἀμαρτίας» → *ἀφιέναι ἁμαρτίας* ("to forgive sins"). Greek «Ἐγερθεὶς» is the aorist passive participle, "having risen / having been raised."
