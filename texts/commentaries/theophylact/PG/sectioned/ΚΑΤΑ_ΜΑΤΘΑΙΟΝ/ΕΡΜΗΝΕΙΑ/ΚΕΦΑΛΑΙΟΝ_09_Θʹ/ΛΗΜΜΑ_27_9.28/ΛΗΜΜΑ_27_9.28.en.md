# Matthew 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 27 · Matt. 9:28

> **And when he had come into the house, the blind men came to him. And Jesus says to them: Do you believe that I am able to do this? They say to him: Yes, Lord. (Matt. 9:28)**

He draws the blind men along even as far as the house,[^1] so that he might show their persevering faith, and might condemn the Jews. And he asks them whether they believe, showing that faith sets all things aright.[^2]

[^1]: Greek *παρέλκει*, "he draws along / leads on / prolongs *the journey*"; the sense is that he lets them keep following him all the way to the house rather than healing them at once on the road.
[^2]: Greek *κατορθοῖ* (from *κατορθόω*), "accomplishes rightly, brings to a successful issue, sets straight"; here, that faith is what carries everything through to a good end.
