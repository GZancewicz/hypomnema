# Matthew 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 7 · Matt. 9:7

> **And having risen up, he went away into his house.[^1] (Matt. 9:7)**

*This lemma carries no exposition of its own; it is the continuation of the Gospel citation begun in Lemma 6 (the Lord's command to the paralytic, «Ἐγερθεὶς, ἆρόν σου τὴν κλίνην, καὶ ὕπαγε εἰς τὸν οἶκόν σου»), and it runs on into the citation continued and expounded in Lemma 8.*

[^1]: The Greek of this lemma-file reads only «ἐγερθεὶς, ἀπῆλθεν εἰς τὸν οἶκον αὐτοῦ»; the connective «Καὶ» ("And") stands at the very end of the Lemma 6 file, so it is carried over here. *ἀπῆλθεν* is "went away / departed"; *ἐγερθείς* is the aorist passive participle "having been raised up / having risen up," kept as a participle.
