# Matthew 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 39 · Matt. 9:38

> **pray therefore to the Lord of the harvest, that he may send out workers into his harvest. (Matt. 9:38)**[^1]

By "harvest" he names the multitude that is in need of tending;[^2] and by "workers," those who ought to teach — who were not, at that time, in Israel. And the Lord of the harvest *is* Christ himself, as *being* Lord of prophets and apostles; and this *is* plain from his appointing[^3] the twelve without asking God. Hear—[^4]

[^1]: This lemma completes the sentence begun in Lemma 38 (Matt. 9:37, «Ὁ μὲν θερισμὸς πολύς …»). Greek «δεήθητε οὖν τοῦ Κυρίου τοῦ θερισμοῦ, ὅπως ἐκβάλῃ ἐργάτας εἰς τὸν θερισμὸν αὐτοῦ». The verb *ἐκβάλῃ* is literally "may thrust/cast out," here rendered "may send out."
[^2]: Greek *τὸ πλῆθος τὸ χρῇζον τῆς θεραπείας*. *θεραπεία* spans "healing / attention / care / service"; given the preceding image of sheep without a shepherd (9:36) and Christ's healing "every disease" (9:35), it carries the sense of tending or care, with an overtone of "healing." Rendered "tending."
[^3]: Greek *προχειρίσασθαι* ("to appoint, designate, put forward *with the hand*").
[^4]: The comment breaks off at *Ἄκουε* ("Hear" / "Listen"), which introduces what follows in the next lemma.
