# Matthew 9 (Θʹ) — Lemma 24 · Matt. 9:25

> **But when the crowd had been cast out, having entered he took hold of her hand, and the girl arose; and…[^1] (Matt. 9:25)**

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text; Theophylact's exposition begins in the following lemma. The quoted sentence breaks off at "and…" and is completed there with "…this report went out into all that land," which occasions his remark that where there is a crowd and distraction Jesus does not work wonders.*

[^1]: The lemma is cut off mid-sentence at «καὶ» ("and…"); it resumes in Lemma 25 with «ἐξῆλθεν ἡ φήμη αὕτη…» ("this report went out…"). Greek *ἐξεβλήθη ὁ ὄχλος* — "the crowd was cast/thrown out"; *ἐκράτησε τῆς χειρὸς αὐτῆς* — "he took hold of / grasped her hand."
