# Matthew 19 (ΙΘʹ) — Lemma 21 · Matt. 19:17

> **But if thou wilt enter into life, keep the commandments.**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture words, with no exposition; the commandments enumerated in the young man's question and the Lord's reply are expounded in the following lemmata.*[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Εἰ δὲ θέλεις εἰσελθεῖν εἰς τὴν ζωήν, τήρησον τὰς ἐντολάς.» This is the second half of Matt. 19:17.
[^2]: A bare scripture citation with no attached comment.
