# Matthew 19 (ΙΘʹ) — Lemma 33 · Matt. 19:30

> **But many *who are* first shall be last, and *the* last first. (Matt. 19:30)**

Here he hints at the Jews and the gentiles. For the *former*, on the one hand, being first, became last; but the gentiles, being last, were established first.[^1] And so that you may clearly learn the thing *that is* said, he attaches also the parable that follows.[^2]

[^1]: The OCR reads *κατέστημεν* ("we were established," first person plural), which does not fit the third-person subject "the gentiles"; securely *κατέστησαν* ("they were established / came to stand"). Rendered accordingly.
[^2]: I.e. the parable of the laborers in the vineyard (Matt. 20:1–16), which closes with the same saying (Matt. 20:16). The source line following this lemma («ΟΑΡΗΤ ΧΣΧ.») is a garbled running header/column marker, not part of the text, and is left untranslated.
