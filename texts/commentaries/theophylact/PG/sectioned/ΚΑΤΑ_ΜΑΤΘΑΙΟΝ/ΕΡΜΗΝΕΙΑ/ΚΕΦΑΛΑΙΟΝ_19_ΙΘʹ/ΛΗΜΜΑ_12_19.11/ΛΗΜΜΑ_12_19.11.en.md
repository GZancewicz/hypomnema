# Matthew 19 (ΙΘʹ) — Lemma 12 · Matt. 19:11 (cont.)

> **«For ask, he says, and it shall be given to you. For everyone who asks receives.»**[^1]

[^1]: This lemma carries only the scriptural citation (Matt. 7:7–8 / Luke 11:9–10) that completes the sentence broken off at the end of Lemma 11 ("And it is given … to those who ask —"). Greek *«Αἰτεῖτε γάρ, φησί, καὶ δοθήσεται ὑμῖν. Πᾶς γὰρ ὁ αἰτῶν, λαμβάνει.»* The *γάρ* ("for") is Theophylact's connective linking the citation to his preceding clause; the interposed *φησί* ("he says") marks it as a spoken quotation. No exposition of its own follows.
