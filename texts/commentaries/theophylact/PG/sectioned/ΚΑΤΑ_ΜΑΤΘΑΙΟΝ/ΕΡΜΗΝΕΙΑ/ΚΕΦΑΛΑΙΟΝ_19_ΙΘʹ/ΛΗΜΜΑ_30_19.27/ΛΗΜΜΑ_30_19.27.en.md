# Matthew 19 (ΙΘʹ) — Lemma 30 · Matt. 19:27

> **Then Peter, answering, said to him: Behold, we have left all *things*, and have followed you; what then shall there be for us? (Matt. 19:27)**

Even if Peter seems not to have left any great things, being poor, yet nonetheless know that, though what he possessed was little, he left many things — indeed these very *possessions themselves*.[^1] For indeed we men have a greater attachment in the case of few *things*;[^2] but Peter also disregarded every worldly pleasure-seeking, and the fleshly affection toward *his* parents.[^3] For these passions campaign not only against *the* rich, but also against *the* poor. What, therefore, *does* the Lord *say*?[^4]

[^1]: The clause is badly OCR-corrupted: *ἀλλ´ οὔν χίνωσκς ἤτι νῷ ὄντι πολλὰ ἁφῆκε καὶ αὐτάς*. Securely: *ἀλλ' οὖν γίνωσκε ὅτι* = "yet nonetheless know that." The middle is uncertain; *νῷ ὄντι* is best taken as a corruption of *ὀλίγῳ ὄντι* ("though what he had was little / though he was a man of little"), which fits the argument that follows (that attachment is proportionally *greater* over few possessions). The final *καὶ αὐτάς* ("even these very *ones*," feminine plural) has no secure antecedent in the garbled text — possibly the "attachments" (*σχέσεις*) named in the next sentence, or Peter's few belongings. Translated to render the recoverable sense and flagged; the exact wording cannot be established.
[^2]: Greek *σχέσιν πλείονα ἔχομεν … ἐν τοῖς ὀλίγοις* — "we have a greater *attachment/relation* (*σχέσις*) in the few things." The poor man clings the more tightly to the little that he has, so that his renunciation is no smaller a thing than the rich man's.
[^3]: Greek *φιλοσαρκία* — "love of the flesh," here "fleshly affection," i.e. carnal, natural attachment toward one's parents; Peter renounced even this.
[^4]: The lemma breaks off at the question *Τί οὖν ὁ Κύριος;* ("What, therefore, *does* the Lord *say*?"); the answer follows in the next lemma, which quotes the Lord's reply (19:28), "Amen I say to you, that you who have followed me, in the regeneration…"
