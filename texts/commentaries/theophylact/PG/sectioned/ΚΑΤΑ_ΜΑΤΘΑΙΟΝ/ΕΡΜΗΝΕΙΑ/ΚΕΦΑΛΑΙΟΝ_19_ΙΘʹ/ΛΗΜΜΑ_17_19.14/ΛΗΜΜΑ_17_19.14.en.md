# Matthew 19 (ΙΘʹ) — Lemma 17 · Matt. 19:14

> **Jesus said: Let the little children *be*, and do not hinder them from coming to me; for of such is the kingdom of the heavens.**[^1]

Mothers were bringing *their* children, that they might be blessed through the touch of *his* hands. But they were approaching in a disorderly and tumultuous manner, and for this reason the disciples hinder *them* — at the same time also thinking that the dignity of the teacher is diminished by little children being brought *to him*. Christ, then, showing that he rather welcomes what is unassuming,[^2] says:

[^1]: Matt. 19:14, completing the sentence begun in Lemma 16. The OCR reads «τὰ παι-[δία]» (word broken at the line end) and is straightforwardly restored. Greek *ἄφετε τὰ παιδία*: *ἄφετε* is "let *be*, allow, permit, leave *alone*" (aorist imperative of *ἀφίημι*); with no infinitive attached to *τὰ παιδία*, the sense is "leave the little children *alone*" / "let them *be*," spelled out by the following "and do not hinder them from coming to me." The supplied "*be*" is italicized.
[^2]: Greek *τὸ ἄτυφον*: "the unassuming / unpretentious *quality*," freedom from *τῦφος* (vanity, self-importance, conceit). The neuter abstract is rendered "what is unassuming"; *ἀποδέχεται* is "accepts, welcomes, approves."
