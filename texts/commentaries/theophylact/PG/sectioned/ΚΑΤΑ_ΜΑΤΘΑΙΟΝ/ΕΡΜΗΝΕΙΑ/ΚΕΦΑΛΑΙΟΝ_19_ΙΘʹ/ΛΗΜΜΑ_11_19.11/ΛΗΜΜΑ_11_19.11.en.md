# Matthew 19 (ΙΘʹ) — Lemma 11 · Matt. 19:11

> **«*But he*[^1] said to them: Not all make room for this word, but *those* to whom it has been given.»**

Since the disciples said that it is better not to come to marriage, the Lord says that great, on the one hand, is the matter of virginity,[^2] but it is not achievable by all, but only by those with whom God works together.[^3] For the *word* "it has been given" stands here in place of "those with whom God works together." And it is given to those, *namely* to those who ask,[^4]

[^1]: The source opens this lemma with the garbled *ϛθδὲ* carried over from the end of Lemma 10; it is read as *Ὁ δὲ* ("But he"), which supplies the subject of *εἶπεν αὐτοῖς* ("said to them"). "Not all make room for this word" renders *οὐ πάντες χωροῦσι τὸν λόγον τοῦτον*: the verb *χωρέω* means "to make room for, contain, receive, hold" — not everyone has the capacity to receive this saying.
[^2]: Greek *μέγα μὲν τὸ τῆς παρθενίας χρῆμα* — "great, on the one hand, is the *matter/thing* of virginity" (*χρῆμα*, "affair, matter, thing of value"). The *μὲν* is answered by the following *ἀλλ᾿* ("but") rather than by a *δέ*.
[^3]: Greek *συνεργεῖ ὁ Θεός* — "God works together with / cooperates with, lends his aid to." Rendered consistently "works together with."
[^4]: The sentence breaks off into the citation opening Lemma 12 (Matt. 7:7 / Luke 11:9–10): *«Αἰτεῖτε … καὶ δοθήσεται ὑμῖν»* ("Ask … and it shall be given to you").
