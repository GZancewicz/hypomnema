# Matthew 19 (ΙΘʹ) — Lemma 9 · Matt. 19:9 (cont.)

> **«Who shall separate us from the love of Christ?»**[^1]

The things, therefore, which God has yoked together,[^2] neither man, nor any other creature, nor angels, nor principalities, nor powers, as Paul says, are able to separate.[^3]

[^1]: The lemma is the Pauline citation (Rom. 8:35) that completes the sentence broken off at the end of Lemma 8. Greek *«Τίς ἡμᾶς χωρίσει ἀπὸ τῆς ἀγάπης τοῦ Χριστοῦ;»*.
[^2]: Greek *Ἃ οὖν ὁ Θεὸς συνέζευξεν* (OCR *Ἀ οὖν*). Theophylact echoes Matt. 19:6, *ὃ οὖν ὁ Θεὸς συνέζευξεν, ἄνθρωπος μὴ χωριζέτω* ("what God has yoked together, let not man separate"), but with the neuter *plural* relative *ἅ* ("the things which") rather than the singular *ὅ*; hence the plural rendering. *συζεύγνυμι* is literally "to yoke together" (the root of "conjugal").
[^3]: A conflation of Matt. 19:6 with Rom. 8:38–39 (*οὔτε ἄγγελοι οὔτε ἀρχαὶ … οὔτε … δυνήσεται ἡμᾶς χωρίσαι*). Preserved as Theophylact blends the two.
