# Matthew 19 (ΙΘʹ) — Lemma 25 · Matt. 19:21

> **Jesus said to him: If you wish to be perfect, go, sell your possessions, and give to *the* poor, and you shall have treasure in heaven, and come, follow me. (Matt. 19:21)**

*This lemma folder carries only the scripture quotation, with no exposition of its own; Theophylact's comment on these words begins in the following lemma, which opens with the next verse (19:22) and then expounds this saying.*[^1]

[^1]: The Greek text of this lemma is Matt. 19:21 in full, with no commentary appended. The exposition that unpacks "if you wish to be perfect… sell your possessions… give to the poor" is set out under Lemma 26, where Theophylact writes: "As many things as you said you had accomplished, these you accomplished in a Jewish manner; but if you wish *to be* perfect, that is, to be my disciple…" The imperative "give" here is aorist *δὸς* ("give once"), which Theophylact deliberately contrasts, in the next lemma, with the present *δίδου* ("keep giving"). The noun "poor" (*πτωχοῖς*) is anarthrous; the supplied "*the*" is italicized.
