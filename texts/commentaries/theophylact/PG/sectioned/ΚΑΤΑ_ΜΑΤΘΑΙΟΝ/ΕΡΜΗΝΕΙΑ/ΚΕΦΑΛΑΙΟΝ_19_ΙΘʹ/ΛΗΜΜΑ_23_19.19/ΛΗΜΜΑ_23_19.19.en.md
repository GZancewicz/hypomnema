# Matthew 19 (ΙΘʹ) — Lemma 23 · Matt. 19:19

> **…father and mother, and thou shalt love thy neighbor as thyself.**[^1]

The Lord refers[^2] the questioner to the commandments of the law, in order that the Jews might not be able to say that he despises the law. What then?[^3]

[^1]: Greek «πατέρα σου καὶ τὴν μητέρα, καὶ ἀγαπήσεις τὸν πλησίον σου ὡς σεαυτόν.» — the completion of the lemma broken off in Lemma 22 (Matt. 19:19). OCR *πατέρα οου* = *πατέρα σου*.
[^2]: Greek *παραπέμπει*, "refers, sends over, directs (someone) on to." OCR *παραπέιπει* = *παραπέμπει*; the phrase «ἐπὶ τὰς νομικὰς ἐντολὰς παραπέμπει τὸν ἐρωτήσαντα» = "refers the questioner on to the legal commandments" (i.e. the commandments of the Mosaic law).
[^3]: Greek *Τί οὖν;* (OCR *Τἱ οὖν*) — a rhetorical lead-in that breaks off here; it is answered in the following lemma, which takes up the young man's reply "All these things have I kept."
