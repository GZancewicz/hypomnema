# Matthew 19 (ΙΘʹ) — Lemma 2 · Matt. 19:2

> **there followed him many crowds, and he healed them there. (Matt. 19:2)**[^1]

Again he sojourns in Judea, so that those of the unbelievers in Judea might not be able to allege as a pretext *that* "He did not visit us more frequently, but *rather* the Galileans." Again therefore, after the teaching and after the words were completed, the miracles follow. For it behooves us both to teach, but also to act. But the foolish Pharisees, having seen the miracles — *though* it was necessary to believe — they instead tempt *him*; for listen.[^2]

[^1]: This lemma opens with the continuation of the scripture sentence begun in Lemma 1 (Matt. 19:1); the OCR marks the close of the quotation with a doubled stop «ἐκεῖ..». The bracketed reference locates the completed clause at Matt. 19:2.
[^2]: The clause structure is *δέον πιστεῦσαι, οἱ δὲ πειράζουσιν* — literally "*it* being necessary to believe, but they tempt *him*"; the *δὲ* answering the concessive participle is rendered "instead." The final words *ἄκουε γάρ* ("for listen / for hear") point forward to the Pharisees' question, quoted in Lemma 3. OCR "ἄκους vάρ" is securely *ἄκουε γάρ*.
