# Matthew 19 (ΙΘʹ) — Lemma 15 · Matt. 19:12

> **He who is able to receive *it*, let him receive *it*.**[^1]

For he does not compel *anyone* to be a virgin, nor does he abolish marriage, but he sets virginity before *them* *as preferable*.[^2]

[^1]: The closing clause of Matt. 19:12, «Ὁ δυνάμενος χωρεῖν, χωρείτω». *χωρεῖν* means "to make room for, contain, hold, receive"; the supplied object "*it*" is italicized.
[^2]: Greek *προτίθησιν* ("he sets before, puts forward, proposes, prefers"). The verb can mean simply "set out/propose" or, more strongly, "set before *others* as the preferable thing"; Theophylact's contrast (he neither compels virginity nor abolishes marriage) shows he means that Christ commends virginity as the higher option without imposing it — hence "sets *it* before *them* as preferable." Greek: «οὐδὲ τὸν γάμον καταλύει, ἀλλὰ τὴν παρθενίαν προτίθησιν».
