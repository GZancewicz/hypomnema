# Matthew 19 (ΙΘʹ) — Lemma 18 · Matt. 19:14

> **Let the little children *be*; for of such is the kingdom of the heavens.**[^1]

He did not say "of these," but "of such" — that is, of those having simplicity, of the guileless, of those without malice.[^2] And even if someone teaches, and some come to him asking childlike questions, let him receive these, and let him not hinder *them*.

[^1]: Matt. 19:14 (the same clause quoted again, now for its own exposition). On *ἄφετε τὰ παιδία* ("let the little children *be*"), see Lemma 17.
[^2]: Theophylact glosses the demonstrative: «Οὐκ εἶπε, τούτων, ἀλλὰ, τῶν τοιούτων» — "not 'of these' (τούτων, the actual children), but 'of such' (τῶν τοιούτων, those like them)." The three genitives: *τῶν τὴν ἁπλότητα ἐχόντων* ("of those having simplicity"; OCR «ἀπλότητα» → «ἁπλότητα»), *τῶν ἀκάκων* ("of the guileless, innocent of evil"), *τῶν ἀπονήρων* ("of those free from wickedness / without malice").
