# Matthew 19 (ΙΘʹ) — Lemma 19 · Matt. 19:15–16

> **And having laid his hands upon them, he departed thence. And behold, one**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture words, with no exposition; the sentence breaks off at "one" (εἷς) and is completed in the following lemma, which continues "having come, said to him: Good teacher…"*[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Καὶ ἐπιθεὶς αὐτοῖς τὰς χεῖρας, ἐπορεύθη ἐκεῖθεν. Καὶ ἰδοὺ εἷς». The OCR reads *ἐπιθε ς*, securely = *ἐπιθείς* ("having laid on"). This is Matt. 19:15 running into 19:16 (the folder-name "19.14" is off by one or two verses; corrected here from the Greek). The clause "And behold, one…" opens the episode of the rich young man.
[^2]: The lemma is a bare scripture citation; Theophylact's comment on the young man's approach begins in the next lemma.
