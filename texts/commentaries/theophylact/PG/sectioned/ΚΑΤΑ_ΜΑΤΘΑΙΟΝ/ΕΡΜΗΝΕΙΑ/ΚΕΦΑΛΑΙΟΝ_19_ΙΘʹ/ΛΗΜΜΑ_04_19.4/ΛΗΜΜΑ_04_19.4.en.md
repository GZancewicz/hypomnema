# Matthew 19 (ΙΘʹ) — Lemma 4 · Matt. 19:4

> **answering said to them: Have you not read that he who made *them* from *the* beginning made them male and female? And**[^1]

[^1]: This lemma carries only the scripture quotation, which breaks off mid-sentence at «καὶ» ("And"); Theophylact supplies no exposition here. The sentence continues in Lemma 5 with «εἶπεν· Ἔνεκεν τούτου καταλείψει ἄνθρωπος…» ("said: For this cause a man shall leave…"). Theophylact's text reads *ὁ ποιήσας* ("he who made"), not the *ὁ κτίσας* ("he who created") of much of the manuscript tradition; rendered per his wording. *ἀπ᾽ ἀρχῆς* = "from *the* beginning" (anarthrous; the supplied "*the*" is italicized).
