# Matthew 19 (ΙΘʹ) — Lemma 13 · Matt. 19:12

> **For there are eunuchs who from *their* mother's womb were born so, and**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation, with no exposition of its own; it breaks off at "and," and the citation is continued and expounded in Lemma 14.*[^2]

[^1]: The folder-name verse is 19.11, but the Greek quoted here is the opening of Matt. 19:12: «Εἰσὶ γὰρ εὐνοῦχοι οἵτινες ἐκ κοιλίας μητρὸς ἐγεννήθησαν οὕτω…» ("For there are eunuchs who from *their* mother's womb were born so…"). The header verse is corrected to 19:12 accordingly. The OCR reads «οὖτω» for «οὕτω» ("so, thus"), a trivial and certain correction. Greek *εὐνοῦχος* is retained conventionally as "eunuch."
[^2]: The citation resumes in Lemma 14 with «εἰσὶν εὐνοῦχοι οἵτινες εὐνουχίσθησαν ὑπὸ τῶν ἀνθρώπων…» and runs through «Ὁ δυνάμενος χωρεῖν, χωρείτω», where Theophylact takes up the exposition.
