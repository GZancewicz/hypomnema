# Matthew 19 (ΙΘʹ) — Lemma 22 · Matt. 19:18–19

> **He says to him: Which? But Jesus said the *saying*:[^1] Thou shalt not kill, thou shalt not commit adultery, thou shalt not steal, thou shalt not bear false witness;[^2] honor the**[^3]

*This lemma carries only the scripture words; the sentence breaks off at "honor the…" (τίμα τόν) and is completed in the following lemma, "…father and mother," where Theophylact's exposition begins.*

[^1]: Greek «Ὁ δὲ Ἰησοῦς εἶπε τὸ, Οὐ φονεύσεις…». The neuter article *τὸ* here substantivizes the quotation that follows — "spoke the *saying/word*, namely: Thou shalt not…" — introducing the commandments as a cited formula. Rendered "said the *saying*," with "saying" supplied for the bare article.
[^2]: OCR *οὐ ὡψεωδομαρτυρήσεις*, securely = *οὐ ψευδομαρτυρήσεις* ("thou shalt not bear false witness").
[^3]: Greek «Λέγει αὐτῷ· Ποίας; Ὁ δὲ Ἰησοῦς εἶπε τὸ, Οὐ φονεύσεις, οὐ μοιχεύσεις, οὐ κλέψεις, οὐ ψευδομαρτυρήσεις· τίμα τὸν [πατέρα]…». This is Matt. 19:18 running into 19:19. "Which?" (Ποίας;) = "Which *commandments*?" (feminine, agreeing with the understood *ἐντολάς*).
