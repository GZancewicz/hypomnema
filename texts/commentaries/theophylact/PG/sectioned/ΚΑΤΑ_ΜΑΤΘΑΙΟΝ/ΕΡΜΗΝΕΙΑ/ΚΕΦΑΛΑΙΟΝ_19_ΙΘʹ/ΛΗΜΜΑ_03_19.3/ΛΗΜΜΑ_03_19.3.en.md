# Matthew 19 (ΙΘʹ) — Lemma 3 · Matt. 19:3

> **And the Pharisees came to him, tempting him, and saying: Is it lawful for a man to put away his wife for every cause? But he**[^1]

[^1]: This lemma carries only the scripture quotation, which breaks off mid-sentence at «Ὁ δὲ» ("But he"); Theophylact supplies no exposition here. The sentence resumes in Lemma 4 with «ἀποκριθεὶς εἶπεν αὐτοῖς…» ("answering said to them…"). Greek *ἀπολῦσαι* is "to release / dismiss / put away" (of a wife, "to divorce"); *κατὰ πᾶσαν αἰτίαν* = "for every cause / reason."
