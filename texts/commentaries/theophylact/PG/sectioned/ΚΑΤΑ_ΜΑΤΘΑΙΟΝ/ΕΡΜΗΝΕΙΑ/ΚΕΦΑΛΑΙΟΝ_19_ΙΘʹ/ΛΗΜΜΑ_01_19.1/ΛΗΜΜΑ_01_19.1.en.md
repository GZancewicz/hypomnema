# Matthew 19 (ΙΘʹ) — Lemma 1 · Matt. 19:1

> **And it came to pass, when Jesus finished these words, he departed from Galilee, and came into the borders of Judea beyond the Jordan; and**[^1]

[^1]: This lemma carries only the scripture quotation, which breaks off mid-sentence at «καὶ» ("and"); Theophylact supplies no exposition here. The sentence is completed in Lemma 2, which continues «ἠκολούθησαν αὐτῷ ὄχλοι πολλοί…» ("there followed him many crowds…") before the comment begins. Greek *μετῆρεν* is "he removed / betook himself away"; rendered "he departed."
