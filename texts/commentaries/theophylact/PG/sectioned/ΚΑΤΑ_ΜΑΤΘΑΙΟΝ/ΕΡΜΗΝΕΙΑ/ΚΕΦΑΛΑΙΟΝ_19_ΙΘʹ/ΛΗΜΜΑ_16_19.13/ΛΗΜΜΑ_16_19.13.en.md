# Matthew 19 (ΙΘʹ) — Lemma 16 · Matt. 19:13

> **Then were brought to him little children, that he might lay *his* hands on them and pray; but the disciples rebuked them; but the**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation, with no exposition of its own; it breaks off at "but the," and the sentence is completed in Lemma 17 with "Jesus said…"*[^2]

[^1]: Matt. 19:13. The OCR reads «προσηνέχθη» (singular) for «προσηνέχθησαν» ("were brought," plural, agreeing with *παιδία*), «Ἀπιθῇ» for «ἐπιθῇ» ("might lay upon"), and «ἐπατίμησαν» for «ἐπετίμησαν» ("rebuked") — all trivial, certain corrections.
[^2]: The subject «ὁ δὲ» ("but the…") is completed in Lemma 17: «Ἰησοῦς εἶπεν» ("Jesus said").
