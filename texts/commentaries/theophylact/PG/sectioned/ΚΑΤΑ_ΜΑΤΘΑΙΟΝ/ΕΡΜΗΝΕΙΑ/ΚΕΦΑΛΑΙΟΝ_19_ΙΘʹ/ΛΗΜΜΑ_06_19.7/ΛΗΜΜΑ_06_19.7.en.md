# Matthew 19 (ΙΘʹ) — Lemma 6 · Matt. 19:7

> **They say to him: Why then did Moses command to give a writing of divorce, and to put her away? (Matt. 19:7)**[^1]

[^1]: This lemma carries only the scripture quotation; Theophylact supplies no exposition here. Christ's answer is quoted and expounded from Lemma 7 onward («Λέγει αὐτοῖς, ὅτι Μωσῆς πρὸς τὴν σκληροκαρδίαν ὑμῶν…», "He says to them, that Moses on account of your hardness of heart…"). Greek *βιβλίον ἀποστασίου* = "a document of divorcement / a bill of separation" (LXX Deut. 24:1); rendered "a writing of divorce."
