# Matthew 19 (ΙΘʹ) — Lemma 7 · Matt. 19:8

> **He says to them, that Moses, in view of your hard-heartedness,[^1] permitted you to put away your wives; but from *the* beginning it has not come to be so. But I say to you that whoever—**[^2]

[^1]: Greek *πρὸς τὴν σκληροκαρδίαν ὑμῶν* — "in view of / with regard to your hard-heartedness." The preposition *πρός* + accusative here is concessive-causal ("because of / to accommodate"): Moses' permission was a concession made toward their hardness of heart. *σκληροκαρδία* is literally "hardness of heart."
[^2]: This lemma carries only the Gospel words (no exposition of its own); the exposition begins in Lemma 8. The quotation breaks off mid-sentence at *ὅς ἂν* ("whoever"), and the sentence is completed in Lemma 8: *«…ἀπολύσῃ τὴν γυναῖκα αὐτοῦ μὴ ἐπὶ πορνείᾳ, καὶ γαμήσῃ ἄλλην, μοιχᾶται»* ("…puts away his wife not for fornication, and marries another, commits adultery"). Rendered here as it stands.
