# Matthew 15 (ΙΕʹ) — Lemma 21 · Matt. 15:23

> **«And his disciples, having come *to him*, were asking him, saying: Send her away, because she cries out behind us. But he,[^1]**

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation; Theophylact's exposition begins in the next lemma, which completes the sentence and supplies the words «ἀποκριθεὶς εἶπεν· Οὐκ ἀπεστάλην...» ("answering said: I was not sent...").*[^2]

[^1]: Matt. 15:23b. The quotation breaks off at «Ὁ δὲ» ("But he,") mid-sentence.
[^2]: Per the layout convention, a lemma whose text is exhausted by the Gospel quotation is rendered as the blockquote alone; the continuation and its exposition follow in Lemma 22.
