# Matthew 15 (ΙΕʹ) — Lemma 7 · Matt. 15:8

> **This people draws near to me with their mouth, and honours me with *the* lips, but their heart is far distant from me.**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the continuation of the Isaiah citation begun in the preceding lemma («Hypocrites, well did Isaiah prophesy concerning you, saying…»); Theophylact gives his exposition of the whole quotation below, at lemma 8.*[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Ἐγγίζει μοι ὁ λαὸς οὗτος τῷ στόματι αὐτῶν, καὶ τοῖς χείλεσί με τιμᾷ, ἡ δὲ καρδία αὐτῶν πόῤῥω ἀπέχει ἀπ᾽ ἐμοῦ» (cf. Isa. 29:13, as quoted at Matt. 15:8). The number mismatch is Theophylact's text as it stands: the collective *ὁ λαὸς οὗτος* ("this people") is grammatically singular and governs singular verbs (*ἐγγίζει*, *τιμᾷ*, *ἀπέχει*), yet the pronoun *αὐτῶν* ("their") is plural — preserved rather than harmonized. Note the asymmetry of the possessives: "mouth" carries *αὐτῶν* ("their"), but "lips" (*τοῖς χείλεσι*) has only the article, hence "with *the* lips." *πόῤῥω ἀπέχει* renders "holds itself far off / is far distant."
[^2]: In the source, this folder's .txt contains only the scripture words with no comment; the exposition of the Isaiah quotation (lemmata 6–8) is placed at lemma 8, after the citation is complete at «commandments of men».
