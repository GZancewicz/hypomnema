# Matthew 15 (ΙΕʹ) — Lemma 16 · Matt. 15:17

> **… do you perceive, that everything entering into the mouth passes into the belly, and is cast out into the privy?[^1] But the things …**

*Continuation lemma:* this section carries only the scripture text, with no exposition of Theophylact's own. It opens mid-sentence — completing the "Not yet …" (*Οὔπω*) of the previous lemma with "…do you perceive" (*νοεῖτε*) — and breaks off again at "But the things …" (*Τὰ δὲ*), which is completed in the following lemma with "…going out from the mouth …" (*ἐκπορευόμενα ἐκ τοῦ στόματος*).

[^1]: Greek *ἀφεδρῶνα* — the "latrine, privy, draught" (the place, and by extension the bodily discharge, of what passes out below).
