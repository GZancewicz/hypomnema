# Matthew 15 (ΙΕʹ) — Lemma 31 · Matt. 15:34

> **And Jesus says to them: "How many loaves do you have?" And they said: "Seven, and a few little fishes."**[^1] And…[^2]

*This section carries only the running Gospel lemma; Theophylact gives no exposition here. His comment on the whole feeding of the four thousand follows at Lemma 35.*

[^1]: Greek *ἰχθύδια* is the diminutive of *ἰχθύς* — "little fishes"; the diminutive is preserved. The OCR reads «ὀλίγαἰχθόδια» (unspaced, with *ο* for *υ*); securely = **«ὀλίγα ἰχθύδια»**.
[^2]: The lemma breaks off at *Καὶ* ("And…"); the sentence continues into Lemma 32 («ἐκέλευσε τοῖς ὄχλοις ἀναπεσεῖν…»).
