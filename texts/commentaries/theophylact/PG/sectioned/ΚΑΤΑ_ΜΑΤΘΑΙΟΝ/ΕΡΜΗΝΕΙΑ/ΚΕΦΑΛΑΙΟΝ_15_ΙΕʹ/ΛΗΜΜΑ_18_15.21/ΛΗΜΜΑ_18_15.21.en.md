# Matthew 15 (ΙΕʹ) — Lemma 18 · Matt. 15:21

> **And Jesus, having gone out from there, withdrew into the parts[^1] of Tyre and Sidon; and behold, a woman …**

*Continuation lemma:* this section carries only the scripture text, with no exposition of Theophylact's own. It opens with the "And" (*Καὶ*) left hanging at the close of the previous lemma, and breaks off at "and behold, a woman …" (*καὶ ἰδοὺ γυνὴ*), which is completed in the following lemma with "…a Canaanite, having come out from those borders, cried out to him …" (*Χαναναία ἀπὸ τῶν ὁρίων ἐκείνων ἐξελθοῦσα, ἐκραύγαζεν αὐτῷ …*), Matt. 15:22.

[^1]: Greek *τὰ μέρη* — the "parts, districts, region" of Tyre and Sidon (Gentile cities); the exposition proper, on why Jesus goes to Gentile territory after forbidding the disciples to go there, begins in the next lemma.
