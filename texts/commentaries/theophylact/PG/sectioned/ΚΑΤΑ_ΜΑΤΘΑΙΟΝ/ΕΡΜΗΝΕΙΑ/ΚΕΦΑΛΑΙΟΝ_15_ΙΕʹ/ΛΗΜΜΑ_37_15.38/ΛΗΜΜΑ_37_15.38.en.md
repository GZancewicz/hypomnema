# Matthew 15 (ΙΕʹ) — Lemma 37 · Matt. 15:38 (cont.; citing 1 Cor. 2:10)

> **For the Spirit searches all things, even the depths of God.**[^1] (1 Cor. 2:10)

*This lemma carries no exposition of its own:* it is a Pauline proof-text which Theophylact adduces to close the thought of the preceding lemma — that the things which abounded, "which seven baskets contain," are the ones "which only the Holy Spirit knows." The "for" (*γάρ*) connects directly back to that phrase.[^2]

[^1]: The whole lemma is the citation of 1 Cor. 2:10, quoted as «Τὸ γὰρ Πνεῦμα πάντα ἐρευνᾷ, καὶ τὰ βάθη τοῦ Θεοῦ.» (the OCR inserts a spurious full stop after «Τὸ γὰρ»). *τὰ βάθη* is literally "the depths"; rendered "the depths of God" to stay transparent to the Greek rather than adopting the KJV's "the deep things of God." The verb appears here in the Byzantine spelling *ἐρευνᾷ* (Attic/critical *ἐραυνᾷ*), "searches out, examines." The connective *καί* is ascensive, hence "even" rather than "and."
[^2]: On the sentence this completes, see the preceding lemma (ΛΗΜΜΑ_36): the "seven baskets" are read as the spiritual surplus "which only the Holy Spirit knows" — to which this quotation supplies the scriptural warrant.
