# Matthew 15 (ΙΕʹ) — Lemma 19 · Matt. 15:22–23

> **A Canaanite *woman*,[^1] having come out from those borders, was crying out to him, [saying: Have mercy on me, Lord, son of David; my daughter][^2] is grievously vexed by a demon. But he did not answer her a word.[^3]**

For what reason, having forbidden the disciples to go away into *the* way of *the* Gentiles,[^4] does he himself come to Tyre and Sidon,[^5] the Gentile cities? Learn therefore that he did not come there as *one who was* to preach, since, as Mark says,[^6]

[^1]: This lemma continues directly from the preceding one, which breaks off at «καὶ ἰδοὺ γυνὴ» ("and behold, a woman"); the noun *γυνή* ("woman") therefore stands in the previous lemma, and «Χαναναία» ("Canaanite") here agrees with it. The supplied "*woman*" is italicized because the noun itself is not repeated in this lemma's Greek.
[^2]: The OCR jumps from «ἐκραύγαζεν αὐτῷ,» straight to «κακῶς δαιμονίζεται», dropping the clause «λέγουσα· Ἐλέησόν με, Κύριε, υἱὲ Δαυΐδ· ἡ θυγάτηρ μου» ("saying: Have mercy on me, Lord, son of David; my daughter"), which is securely recoverable from Matt. 15:22 and is presupposed by the exposition of the next lemmata. Supplied in brackets; not italicized, since these are the Gospel's own words, merely lost in transmission.
[^3]: «Ὁ δὲ οὐκ ἀπεκρίθη αὐτῇ λόγον» (Matt. 15:23a). OCR «λόων» = *λόγον*.
[^4]: An allusion to the sending-out charge, «εἰς ὁδὸν ἐθνῶν μὴ ἀπέλθητε» (Matt. 10:5). OCR «Τίνας ἔνεκεν» = *Τίνος ἕνεκεν*.
[^5]: OCR «Χιδῶνα» = *Σιδῶνα* ("Sidon"); «κηρύζεων» = *κηρύξων* (future participle, "in order to preach").
[^6]: The sentence breaks off at «ἐπεὶ ὡς ὁ Μᾶρκος φησί» ("since, as Mark says") and is completed in the next lemma, which supplies the Marcan words «...καὶ ἔκρυψεν ἑαυτόν» ("...and he hid himself"), a paraphrase of Mark 7:24.
