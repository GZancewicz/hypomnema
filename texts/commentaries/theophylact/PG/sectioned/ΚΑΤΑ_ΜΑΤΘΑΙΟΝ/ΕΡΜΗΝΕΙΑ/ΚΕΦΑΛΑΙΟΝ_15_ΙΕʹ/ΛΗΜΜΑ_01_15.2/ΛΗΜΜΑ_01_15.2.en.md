# Matthew 15 (ΙΕʹ) — Lemma 1 · Matt. 15:1-2

> **Then come to Jesus the Scribes and Pharisees from Jerusalem, saying: "Why *do* the…**[^1] (Matt. 15:1-2)

This lemma carries only the scripture words and breaks off mid-sentence; Theophylact's exposition does not yet begin here. The quotation is completed, and the comment taken up, in Lemma 2.[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Τότε προσέρχονται τῷ Ἰησοῦ οἱ ἀπὸ Ἰεροσολύμων Γραμματεῖς καὶ Φαρισαῖοι, λέγοντες· Διατί οἱ». The verb *προσέρχονται* is a historic present ("they come / approach"), here rendered "come" to keep the Greek tense. The Greek order is preserved: "come to Jesus the Scribes and Pharisees from Jerusalem." The clause breaks off at *Διατί οἱ* ("Why *do* the…"), before its verb, which arrives at the head of the next lemma (*παραβαίνουσι*, "transgress"); the supplied "*do*" is italicized. The OCR reads "Ἰφσοῦ" for *Ἰησοῦ* (Jesus), a low-stakes corruption noted for completeness.
[^2]: The verse-ref of this lemma spans Matt. 15:1 (the coming of the Scribes and Pharisees) into the opening of 15:2 (their question); the folder metadata's "15.2" is refined here to 15:1-2.
