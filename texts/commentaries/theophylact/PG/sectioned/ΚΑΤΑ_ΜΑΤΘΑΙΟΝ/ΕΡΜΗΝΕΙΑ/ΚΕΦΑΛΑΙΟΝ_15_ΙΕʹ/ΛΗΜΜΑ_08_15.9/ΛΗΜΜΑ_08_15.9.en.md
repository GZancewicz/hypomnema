# Matthew 15 (ΙΕʹ) — Lemma 8 · Matt. 15:9

> **But in vain do they worship me, teaching *as* teachings *the* commandments of men.**[^1]

He shows, through the voice of Isaiah, that they are, concerning his Father also, such as they are found *to be* concerning him *as well*; for being wicked, and through *their* wicked works removing themselves far from God, they were speaking the oracles of God with *the* mouth only. For assuredly in vain do they worship and think to honour God — those who by *their* works dishonour him.[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Μάτην δὲ σέβονταί με, διδάσκοντες διδασκαλίας ἐντάλματα ἀνθρώπων» (OCR *διὸασκαλίας* = *διδασκαλίας*). The clause *διδάσκοντες διδασκαλίας ἐντάλματα ἀνθρώπων* is grammatically compressed and admits more than one construal: *διδασκαλίας* (acc. pl.) and *ἐντάλματα* (acc. pl.) stand in apposition — "teaching *as* teachings the commandments of men," i.e. propounding mere human injunctions in the guise of *God's* doctrines. An alternative reads *διδασκαλίας* as an internal/adverbial accusative ("teaching *for* teachings…"). Rendered here in the appositional sense.
[^2]: The comment plays on the words of the lemmata just quoted: *μακρύνοντες ἑαυτοὺς τοῦ Θεοῦ* ("removing themselves far from God") echoes *πόῤῥω ἀπέχει* ("is far distant") of lemma 7, and *στόματι μόνον ἐλάλουν τὰ… λόγια* ("spoke… with the mouth only") echoes *τῷ στόματι… τοῖς χείλεσι*. OCR *διὰ σῶν ἔργων* is securely *διὰ τῶν ἔργων* ("by *their* works"). The .txt closes with a stray «ι Καὶ», which is the opening "And" (*Καὶ*) of the following lemma (15:10).
