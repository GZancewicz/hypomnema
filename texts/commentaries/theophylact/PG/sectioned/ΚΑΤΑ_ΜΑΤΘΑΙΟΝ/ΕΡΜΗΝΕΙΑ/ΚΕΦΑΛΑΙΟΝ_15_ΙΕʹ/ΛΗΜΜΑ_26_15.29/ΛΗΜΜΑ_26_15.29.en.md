# Matthew 15 (ΙΕʹ) — Lemma 26 · Matt. 15:29

> **And having departed from there, Jesus came beside the sea of Galilee, and having gone up into the mountain, he was sitting there; and**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture text and no exposition; it breaks off mid-sentence at* «καὶ» ("and"), *the sentence being continued and expounded in the following lemmata (Matt. 15:30–31).*[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Καὶ μεταβὰς ἐκεῖθεν ὁ Ἰησοῦς, ἦλθε παρὰ τὴν θάλασσαν τῆς Γαλιλαίας, καὶ ἀναβὰς εἰς τὸ ὄρος, ἐκάθητο ἐκεῖ· καὶ» (Matt. 15:29). The participles *μεταβάς* ("having departed/removed") and *ἀναβάς* ("having gone up") are kept as participles.
[^2]: The Gospel quotation continues in Lemma 27 (Matt. 15:30) and concludes in Lemma 28 (Matt. 15:31), where Theophylact's exposition begins.
