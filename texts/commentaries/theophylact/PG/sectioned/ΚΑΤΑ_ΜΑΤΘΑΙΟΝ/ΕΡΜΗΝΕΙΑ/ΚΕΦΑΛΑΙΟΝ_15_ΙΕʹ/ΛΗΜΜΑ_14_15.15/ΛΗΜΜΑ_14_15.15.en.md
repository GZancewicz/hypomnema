# Matthew 15 (ΙΕʹ) — Lemma 14 · Matt. 15:15

> **But Peter answering said to him: Explain[^1] to us this parable. (Matt. 15:15)**

Peter, since he knew that the law forbade *them* to eat all things, fearing to say to Jesus, "I am scandalized at the things you said, *which* appear to be unlawful," feigns ignorance and asks *the question*.[^2]

[^1]: OCR *θράσον*, securely *φράσον* (aorist imperative of *φράζω*, "declare / point out / explain"): "explain to us this parable."
[^2]: The sentence closes with *ἐπερωτᾷ* ("and questions / asks"), which trails off in the source (the manuscript's dash); "*the question*" is supplied for the bare verb. The point is that Peter's request in the lemma is a feigned ignorance — he is really scandalized, and afraid to say so, because Jesus' words about foods seemed to contravene the law.
