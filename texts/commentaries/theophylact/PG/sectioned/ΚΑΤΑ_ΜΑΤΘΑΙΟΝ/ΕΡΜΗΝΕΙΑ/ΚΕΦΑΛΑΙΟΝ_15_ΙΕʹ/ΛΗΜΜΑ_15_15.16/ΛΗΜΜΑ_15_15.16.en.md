# Matthew 15 (ΙΕʹ) — Lemma 15 · Matt. 15:16

> **But Jesus said, Are you also still[^1] without understanding? Not yet …**

*Continuation lemma:* this section carries only the scripture text, with no exposition of Theophylact's own. It breaks off mid-sentence at "Not yet …" (*Οὔπω*) and is completed in the following lemma with "…do you perceive, that everything entering into the mouth …" (*νοεῖτε, ὅτι πᾶν τὸ εἰσπορευόμενον εἰς τὸ στόμα …*).

[^1]: Greek *ἀκμήν* — an adverbial accusative, "even now, still, yet"; the sense is "Are you too still, even at this point, without understanding?" The sentence is left hanging at *Οὔπω* ("Not yet"), whose verb *νοεῖτε* ("do you perceive") opens the next lemma.
