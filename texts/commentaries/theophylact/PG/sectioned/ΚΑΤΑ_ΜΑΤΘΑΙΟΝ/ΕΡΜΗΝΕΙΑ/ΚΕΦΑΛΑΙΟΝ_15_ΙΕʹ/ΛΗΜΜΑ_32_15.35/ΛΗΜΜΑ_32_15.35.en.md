# Matthew 15 (ΙΕʹ) — Lemma 32 · Matt. 15:35

> **…he commanded the crowds to recline upon the ground; and…**[^1]

*A continuation of the running Gospel lemma, with no opening quotation mark in the source and no exposition here. The comment on the passage is given at Lemma 35.*

[^1]: This section has no «…» at its head: it continues the sentence begun in Lemma 31 (which broke off at *Καὶ*) and itself breaks off again at *καὶ* ("and…"), running on into Lemma 33 («λαβὼν τοὺς ἑπτὰ ἄρτους…»). Greek *ἀναπεσεῖν* — "to recline / lie back," the posture of guests at a meal.
