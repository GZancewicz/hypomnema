# Matthew 15 (ΙΕʹ) — Lemma 24 · Matt. 15:28

> **«Then Jesus, answering, said to her: O woman, great *is* your faith; be it done to you as you wish. And her daughter was healed from that hour.»[^1]**

Now Jesus made manifest the cause for which he was deferring the healing from the beginning; for in order that the faith and the understanding of the woman might shine forth, on this account Christ did not straightway from the beginning assent, but rather even sends *her* away. But now, since her faith has been uncovered, she is praised, hearing:[^2]

[^1]: Matt. 15:28. OCR «Πγύναι» = *Ὦ γύναι* ("O woman"); «γενηθήτω σοι ὡς θέλεις» = "be it done to you as you wish." The lemma agrees with the common text.
[^2]: The sentence breaks off at «ἀκούουσα·» ("hearing:") and is completed in the next lemma, which supplies the praise «Μεγάλη σου ἡ πίστις» ("Great *is* your faith").
