# Matthew 15 (ΙΕʹ) — Lemma 33 · Matt. 15:36

> **…having taken the seven loaves and the fishes, having given thanks he broke *them*, and gave *them* to his disciples; and the disciples to the crowd. And…**[^1]

*A continuation of the running Gospel lemma, with no opening quotation mark in the source and no exposition here. The comment on the passage is given at Lemma 35.*

[^1]: A continuation of the sentence begun at Lemma 32; it breaks off again at *Καὶ* ("And…"), running on into Lemma 34 («ἔφαγον πάντες…»). The participles *λαβὼν* ("having taken") and *εὐχαριστήσας* ("having given thanks") are kept as participles. The objects of *ἔκλασε* ("he broke") and *ἔδωκε* ("he gave") are unexpressed in the Greek and supplied in italics; likewise the clause "and the disciples *gave* to the crowd" carries no verb in the Greek. The source spells *ἰχθύας* as «ἱχθύας» (rough breathing), a trivial OCR slip.
