# Matthew 15 (ΙΕʹ) — Lemma 27 · Matt. 15:30

> **there came to him great crowds, having with themselves *the* lame, blind, mute, maimed, and many others, and they cast them down beside the feet of Jesus, and he healed them; so that the**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture text and no exposition; it is a continuation of the sentence begun in Lemma 26 (Matt. 15:29) and breaks off at* «τοὺς» ("the"), *the sentence being concluded and expounded in the following lemma (Matt. 15:31).*[^2]

[^1]: Greek «προσῆλθον αὐτῷ ὄχλοι πολλοὶ, ἔχοντες μεθ᾽ ἑαυτῶν χωλοὺς, τυφλοὺς, κωφοὺς, κυλλοὺς, καὶ ἑτέρους πολλοὺς, καὶ ἔῤῥιψαν αὐτοὺς παρὰ τοὺς πόδας τοῦ Ἰησοῦ, καὶ ἐθεράπευσεν αὐτούς· ὥστε τοὺς» (Matt. 15:30). *ἔῤῥιψαν* ("they cast/flung down") is a strong verb — the crowds set the sick down at his feet without ceremony; rendered "cast down." The disease-nouns are anarthrous in the Greek; the supplied "*the*" is italicized.
[^2]: The clause runs on into Lemma 28 (Matt. 15:31): «…ὥστε τοὺς ὄχλους θαυμάσαι…» ("so that the crowds marveled…"), where the exposition begins.
