# Matthew 15 (ΙΕʹ) — Lemma 13 · Matt. 15:14

> **Let them alone; they are blind guides of *the* blind, and if a blind *man* lead a blind *man*, both shall fall into a pit. (Matt. 15:14)**[^1]

He says that the traditions of the elders and the Jewish commandments shall be rooted up — not the things of the law, as the Manichaeans suppose; for the law is a planting of God; accordingly this[^2] was not rooted up. For its root remains, that is, the hidden[^3] spirit. But the leaves, namely the apparent letter, have fallen away; for we no longer understand the law according to the letter,[^4] but according to the spirit. And the Pharisees being given up as hopeless[^5] and incurable, he said the *word*, "Let them alone." From here, therefore, we learn that the being-scandalized of those who are scandalized willingly and *are* incurable is not harmful to us. And he calls them, *being* teachers, blind *guides* of the blind; and this he says so that he might draw the crowds away from them.

[^1]: The lemma continues directly from the preceding lemma's citation of Matt. 15:13, «Πᾶσα φυτεία ἣν οὐκ ἐφύτευσεν ὁ Πατήρ μου ὁ οὐράνιος, ἐκριζωθήσεται» ("Every plant which my heavenly Father did not plant shall be rooted up"); the whole exposition here turns on that plant-and-rooting image. *βόθυνον* is a "pit / ditch." Greek *ὁδηγοί … τυφλοί* is anarthrous — "*the* blind" supplies the article; "*man*" is supplied for the substantival adjectives *τυφλός … τυφλόν*.
[^2]: *οὗτος* (masculine) refers back to *ὁ νόμος*, "the law": the law itself was not the thing rooted up.
[^3]: OCR *κουπτόμενον*, securely *κρυπτόμενον* ("hidden / concealed"): the inner spirit of the law as against its outward "letter."
[^4]: OCR *καπὰ τὸ χράμμα*, securely *κατὰ τὸ γράμμα* ("according to the letter"), balancing *κατὰ τὸ πνεῦμα* ("according to the spirit") in the same clause.
[^5]: The participle is badly OCR-garbled as *Ἀσαννιωαιμένων ἂὲ ὄντων*; from the sense and the following *καὶ ἀνιάτων* ("and incurable") it is a perfect middle/passive genitive plural governing "the Pharisees," best reconstructed as *ἀπεγνωσμένων δὲ ὄντων* ("but being given up as hopeless / despaired of"). Rendered accordingly, with the corruption flagged; the exact word cannot be certain from the OCR.
