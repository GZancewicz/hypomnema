# Matthew 15 (ΙΕʹ) — Lemma 30 · Matt. 15:33

> **And his disciples say to him: Whence *are there* for us in *the* wilderness so many loaves, so as to satisfy so great a crowd?**[^1]

And yet they ought to have understood that even before, more *people* were fed in *the* wilderness;[^2] but they are without perception[^3] — in order that, when you see these *same men* afterward full of such great wisdom, you may marvel at the grace of Christ.

[^1]: Greek «Καὶ λέγουσιν αὐτῷ οἱ μαθηταὶ αὐτοῦ· Πόθεν ἡμῖν ἐν ἐρημίᾳ ἄρτοι τοσοῦτοι, ὥστε χορτάσαι ὄχλον τοσοῦτον;» (Matt. 15:33). The clause has no verb ("Whence for us…so many loaves"); the supplied "*are there*" is italicized. *χορτάσαι*, "to satisfy / fill / feed to the full."
[^2]: The source reads OCR «κλείους», securely = *πλείους* ("more / a greater number"), referring to the feeding of the five thousand (Matt. 14). Rendered "more *people*." (The OCR further garbles *σοφίας* as «αοφίας» and *Χριστοῦ* as «ριστοῦ», both secure and low-stakes.)
[^3]: Greek *ἀναισθήτως ἔχουσιν*, "they are in a state of insensibility / without perception / dull of understanding."
