# Matthew 15 (ΙΕʹ) — Lemma 9 · Matt. 15:10

> **And having called the crowd to *him*, he said to them: Hear and understand. Not that which**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture words, with no comment; the exposition is given below at lemma 10, after the sentence is completed.*[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Καὶ προσκαλεσάμενος τὸν ὄχλον, εἶπεν αὐτοῖς· Ἀκούετε καὶ συνίετε. Οὐ τὸ…». *προσκαλεσάμενος* is a middle participle, "having summoned / called to himself," hence "having called the crowd to *him*."
[^2]: The lemma breaks off mid-sentence at *Οὐ τὸ* ("Not that which…"); the sentence is completed in lemma 10 (…"enters into the mouth defiles the man").
