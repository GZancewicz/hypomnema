# Matthew 15 (ΙΕʹ) — Lemma 3 · Matt. 15:3

> **But he answering said to them: "Why do ye also transgress the commandment of God on account of your tradition? For God…**[^1] (Matt. 15:3)

This lemma carries only the scripture words and breaks off mid-sentence; the quotation is completed in Lemma 4, and no exposition stands in this lemma.[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Ὁ δὲ ἀποκριθεὶς εἶπεν αὐτοῖς· Διατί καὶ ὑμεῖς παραβαίνετε τὴν ἐντολὴν τοῦ Θεοῦ διὰ τὴν παράδοσιν ὑμῶν; Ὁ γὰρ Θεὸς». *ἀποκριθεὶς* kept as a participle ("answering"). *διὰ τὴν παράδοσιν ὑμῶν* — "on account of / because of your tradition."
[^2]: The clause breaks off at *Ὁ γὰρ Θεὸς* ("For God…"), before its verb *ἐνετείλατο* ("commanded"), which opens the next lemma.
