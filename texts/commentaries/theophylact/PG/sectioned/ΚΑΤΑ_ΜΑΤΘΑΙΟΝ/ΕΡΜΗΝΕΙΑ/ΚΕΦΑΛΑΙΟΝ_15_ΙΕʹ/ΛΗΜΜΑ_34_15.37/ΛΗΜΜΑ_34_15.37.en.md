# Matthew 15 (ΙΕʹ) — Lemma 34 · Matt. 15:37

> **…they all ate and were filled, and they took up what was left over of the fragments, seven baskets full. And the…**[^1]

*A continuation of the running Gospel lemma, with no opening quotation mark in the source and no exposition here. The comment on the passage is given at Lemma 35.*

[^1]: A continuation of the sentence begun at Lemma 33; it breaks off at *Οἱ δὲ* ("And the…"), running on into Lemma 35, which completes the quotation ("…*those* eating were four thousand men…") and then supplies Theophylact's exposition. Greek *σπυρίδας* ("baskets"): the large hampers of the four-thousand miracle, distinguished below (Lemma 35) from the *κόφινοι* of the five-thousand miracle.
