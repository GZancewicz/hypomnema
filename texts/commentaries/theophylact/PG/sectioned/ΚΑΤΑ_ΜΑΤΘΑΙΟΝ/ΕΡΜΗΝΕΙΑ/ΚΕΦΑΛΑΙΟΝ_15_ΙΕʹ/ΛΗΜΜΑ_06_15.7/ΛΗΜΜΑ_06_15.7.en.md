# Matthew 15 (ΙΕʹ) — Lemma 6 · Matt. 15:7

> **"Hypocrites! well did Isaiah prophesy concerning you, saying:**[^1] (Matt. 15:7)

This lemma carries only the scripture words and breaks off at "saying:"; the prophetic quotation itself (Isa. 29:13) is supplied in the next lemma, and no exposition stands here.[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Ὑποκριταὶ, καλῶς προεφήτευσε περὶ ὑμῶν Ἡσαΐας, λέγων·». *καλῶς* rendered "well / rightly"; *προεφήτευσε* is the aorist "prophesied," here "did prophesy."
[^2]: The sentence breaks off at *λέγων·* ("saying:"); it is completed in the following lemma with the words of Isaiah, "This people draweth near to me with their mouth…" (Matt. 15:8 / Isa. 29:13).
