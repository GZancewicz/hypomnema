# Matthew 15 (ΙΕʹ) — Lemma 12 · Matt. 15:13

> **Every plant which my heavenly Father planted not shall be rooted up;**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture words; Theophylact's exposition follows below at lemma 13 (with the continuation of the citation at 15:14, "let them alone; they are blind guides of *the* blind…").*[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Πᾶσα φυτεία ἣν οὐκ ἐφύτευσεν ὁ Πατήρ μου ὁ οὐράνιος, ἐκριζωθήσεται» (Matt. 15:13). The folder-name verse (15.12) is metadata only; the Greek here is the saying of 15:13, so the header and reference are corrected accordingly. *φυτεία* = "planting / plant"; *ἐκριζωθήσεται* = "shall be rooted up / uprooted."
[^2]: The lemma breaks off at the semicolon after *ἐκριζωθήσεται*; the comment (that it is the traditions of the elders and the Jewish injunctions, not the Law, that shall be uprooted — against the Manichaeans) is placed at lemma 13.
