# Matthew 15 (ΙΕʹ) — Lemma 4 · Matt. 15:4

> **…commanded, saying: 'Honor thy father and thy mother'; and, 'He that speaks evil of father or mother, let him die by death.'**[^1] (Matt. 15:4)

This lemma carries only the scripture words; no exposition stands here. The comment on the whole exchange (verses 3-6) is given in Lemma 5, after the next citation is completed.[^2]

[^1]: Greek «ἐνετείλατο, λέγων· Τίμα τὸν πατέρα σου καὶ τὴν μητέρα· καὶ ὁ κακολογῶν πατέρα ἢ μητέρα, θανάτῳ τελευτάτω.» This completes "For God…" begun in Lemma 3. *ὁ κακολογῶν* kept as a participle ("he that speaks evil of / reviles"). *θανάτῳ τελευτάτω* is a Hebraizing cognate construction, literally "let him end by death / let him die the death"; rendered "let him die by death" to keep the figure visible rather than smoothing to "let him surely die."
[^2]: The Gospel citation continues in the next lemma (Matt. 15:5), where Christ contrasts "But ye say…"; Theophylact's exposition begins there.
