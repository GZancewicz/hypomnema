# Matthew 15 (ΙΕʹ) — Lemma 22 · Matt. 15:24

> **...answering said: I was not sent except to the lost sheep of *the* house of Israel.»[^1]**

The disciples, being weighed down by the cry of the woman, were asking that he might send her away—that is, they were entreating *him* to dismiss her. And this they were doing, not as *being* without compassion, but rather wishing to persuade the Lord to have mercy on her. But he: "I was not sent," he says, "except to the Jews," who are lost sheep, *lost* from the wickedness of those entrusted with them.[^2] And through these things he publishes abroad the faith of the woman all the more.

[^1]: This lemma completes the sentence broken off in Lemma 21: «Ὁ δὲ ἀποκριθεὶς εἶπεν· Οὐκ ἀπεστάλην εἰ μὴ εἰς τὰ πρόβατα τὰ ἀπολωλότα οἴκου Ἰσραήλ» (Matt. 15:24). The opening words are printed as continuation; the supplied "*the*" before "house" is italicized (anarthrous «οἴκου»).
[^2]: Greek «ἀπὸ κακίας τῶν ἐμπιστευθέντων αὐτά» — "from the wickedness of those to whom they had been entrusted," i.e. the sheep (Israel) went astray through the fault of the leaders set over them. Theophylact glosses Christ's «τὰ πρόβατα τὰ ἀπολωλότα» ("the lost sheep") as the Jews, lost not by their own nature but by the failure of their shepherds.
