# Matthew 17 (ΙΖʹ) — Lemma 14 · Matt. 17:11–12

> **But Jesus answering said to them: Elias indeed comes first, and shall restore all things. But I say to you that Elias has already come, and they did not recognize him, but did to him whatsoever they wished. So also the Son of man is about to suffer by them. Then—**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the Lord's words; Theophylact supplies no separate exposition of them here.*[^2]

[^1]: μὲν … δὲ span the saying: «Ἠλίας **μὲν** ἔρχεται πρῶτον … Λέγω **δὲ** ὑμῖν» ("Elias, on the one hand, comes first … but I say to you"); rendered "indeed … but" to keep the lemma readable while preserving both particles. *ἀποκαταστήσει* = "shall restore / re-establish"; the same verb is expounded in the next lemma. The OCR corruptions «αὐτοῖ» (for αὐτοῖς), «Οτω» (for Οὕτω), and «μλλει» (for μέλλει) are secure and low-stakes.
[^2]: The verse quotation runs on into the trailing *Τότε* ("Then—"), which begins the quotation of 17:13 completed at the head of the following lemma; Theophylact's comment on these verses (11–12) is given there, under Lemma 15.
