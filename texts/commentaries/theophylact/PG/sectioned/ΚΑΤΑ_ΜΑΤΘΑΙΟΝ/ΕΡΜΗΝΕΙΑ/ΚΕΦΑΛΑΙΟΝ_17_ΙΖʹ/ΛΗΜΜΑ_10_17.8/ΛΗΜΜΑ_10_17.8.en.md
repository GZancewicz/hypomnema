# Matthew 17 (ΙΖʹ) — Lemma 10 · Matt. 17:8

> **…And having lifted up their eyes, they saw no one, except Jesus alone. (Matt. 17:8)**[^1]

The disciples, not bearing the radiance of the cloud, nor the voice, fell to the ground; and their eyes also were weighed down with sleep — and by "sleep" he means the great stupor[^2] *coming* from the sight. And so that the fear, remaining for long, might not cast out the memory of the things seen, he raises them up and encourages *them*; and he alone is found, so that you might not suppose that the voice was about Moses or Elias, but about him; for he himself is the Son.

[^1]: This lemma closes the Gospel quotation begun in Lemma 8 (17:6) and continued in Lemma 9 (17:7); the closing «-mark stands here. Greek «Ἐπάραντες δὲ τοὺς ὀφθαλμοὺς αὐτῶν, οὐδένα εἶδον, εἰ μὴ τὸν Ἰησοῦν μόνον.» The comment that follows expounds all three verses together.
[^2]: Greek *κάρωσις* — a heaviness/torpor/drowsiness, here "stupor." Theophylact reads the "sleep" of Luke's parallel (cf. Luke 9:32) not as literal slumber but as the dazed heaviness produced by the overwhelming *ὄψις* ("sight, vision").
