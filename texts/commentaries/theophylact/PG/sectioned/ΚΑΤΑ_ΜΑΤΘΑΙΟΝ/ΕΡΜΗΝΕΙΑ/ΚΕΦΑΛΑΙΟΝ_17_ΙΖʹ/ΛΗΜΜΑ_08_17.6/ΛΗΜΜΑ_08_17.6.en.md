# Matthew 17 (ΙΖʹ) — Lemma 8 · Matt. 17:6

> **And having heard *it*, the disciples fell upon their face, and were exceedingly afraid, and[^1]**

*This lemma carries the Gospel text only, with no exposition of its own.*[^2] The Gospel quotation runs on without a break through Lemma 9 («…Jesus, having come to *them*, touched them, and said: Rise up and do not be afraid.») and Lemma 10 («And having lifted up their eyes, they saw no one, except Jesus alone.»), where Theophylact's single comment on the whole passage (17:6–8) is given.

[^1]: Greek «Καὶ ἀκούσαντες οἱ μαθηταὶ, ἔπεσον ἐπὶ πρόσωπον αὐτῶν, καὶ ἐφοβήθησαν σφόδρα, καὶ» (OCR «ἱπεσον» for **ἔπεσον**). The sentence breaks off mid-clause at *καὶ* ("and"), completed in Lemma 9. The supplied "*it*" renders the bare aorist participle *ἀκούσαντες* ("having heard"), whose object (the voice of v. 5) is carried by context.
[^2]: Per the layout convention: where a lemma folder's text is a bare continuation of the scripture quotation with no comment, the exposition is deferred — here to Lemma 10.
