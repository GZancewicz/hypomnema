# Matthew 17 (ΙΖʹ) — Lemma 9 · Matt. 17:7

> **…Jesus, having come *to them*, touched them, and said: Rise up and do not be afraid.[^1]**

*This lemma carries the Gospel text only, with no exposition of its own.*[^2] It continues the sentence begun in Lemma 8 and runs on into Lemma 10 («And having lifted up their eyes, they saw no one, except Jesus alone.»), where Theophylact's comment on the whole passage (17:6–8) is given.

[^1]: Greek «προσελθὼν ὁ Ἰησοῦς, ἤψατο αὐτῶν, καὶ εἶπεν· Ἐγέρθητε καὶ μὴ φοβεῖσθε» (OCR «ἃ Ἰησοῦς» for **ὁ Ἰησοῦς**, «φοβεῖσυε» for **φοβεῖσθε**). The fragment opens with no «-quote mark, being the middle of the sentence begun in Lemma 8. *προσελθὼν* is a participle, "having come near / having approached"; "*to them*" supplies the dative that the Greek carries by construction.
[^2]: The exposition is deferred to Lemma 10, which comments on the whole of 17:6–8.
