# Matthew 17 (ΙΖʹ) — Lemma 15 · Matt. 17:13

> **Then the disciples understood that he spoke to them concerning John the Baptist. (Matt. 17:13)**[^1]

In saying, on the one hand, that "Elijah comes," he shows that he has not yet come, but shall come *as* forerunner of the second coming, and shall restore to the faith of Christ all the Hebrews who shall be found compliant, restoring them, as *it were*, to *their* ancestral inheritance *from which they had* fallen away; but in saying, on the other hand, that "Elijah has already come," he means John the Forerunner. And they did to him whatsoever they also wished, having murdered him;[^2] for by those *very deeds* through which they permitted Herod to murder *him*, being able to prevent *it*, they *themselves* murdered *him*. Then, accordingly, the disciples, having become sharper, understood that he called John "Elijah," because this *man* became forerunner of the first *coming*, as that *man* shall be *forerunner* of the second coming.

[^1]: The lemma opens with the tail of the sentence begun as «Τότε» ("Then") at the close of the previous lemma; the full clause is «Τότε συνῆκαν οἱ μαθηταὶ ὅτι περὶ Ἰωάννου τοῦ Βαπτιστοῦ εἶπεν αὐτοῖς» (Matt. 17:13). OCR «οὕποω» = οὔπω ("not yet"), «Ἑδραίους» = Ἑβραίους ("Hebrews"), «ἤᾶη» = ἤδη ("already"), «Πράρομον/Πρόαρομον» = Πρόδρομον ("Forerunner") — all secure.
[^2]: *Ἐποίησαν δὲ ἐν αὐτῷ ὅσα καὶ ἠθέλησαν φονεύσαντες αὐτόν*: "and they did to him whatsoever they also wished, having murdered him." Theophylact's point is that the people bear the guilt of John's death: by consenting to and not preventing Herod's act, they in effect committed the murder themselves.
