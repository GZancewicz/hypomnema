# Matthew 17 (ΙΖʹ) — Lemma 3 · Matt. 17:1

> **"And he brings them up into a high mountain privately, and[^1]**

[^1]: This lemma carries only the scripture quotation, with no exposition of its own; it breaks off mid-sentence at «καὶ» ("and"). The verse continues into the next lemma with «μετεμορφώθη ἔμπροσθεν αὐτῶν» ("he was transfigured before them," Matt. 17:2), where Theophylact's comment resumes — including his exposition of these very words ("into a high mountain," "privately"). Greek *ἀναφέρει* (OCR «ἀκαφέρει»), present tense, "brings up / leads up."
