# Matthew 17 (ΙΖʹ) — Lemma 12 · Matt. 17:9 (continuing; quoting Matt. 17:4)

> **"It is good for us to be here";**[^1]

for one must always advance and not remain in one degree of virtue and contemplation, but move on also to another place.[^2]

[^1]: This lemma completes the sentence broken off at the end of Lemma 11 ("But do not say, according to Peter…"). The quoted words «Καλόν ἐστιν ἡμᾶς ὧδε εἶναι» are Peter's, drawn back from Matt. 17:4 and here turned to a moral point; the folder's draws-from label (17.9) reflects the exposition's place in the sequence, not the source of the quotation.
[^2]: Greek «ἀεὶ γὰρ προκόπτειν δεῖ» (OCR «ἡεῖ» for **δεῖ**). The sense: the soul must keep advancing (*προκόπτειν*), not settle in a single *βαθμός* ("step, degree, rung") of virtue and contemplation, but pass on *ἀλλαχοῦ*, "elsewhere / to another place." The point rebukes Peter's wish to stay fixed on the mountain.
