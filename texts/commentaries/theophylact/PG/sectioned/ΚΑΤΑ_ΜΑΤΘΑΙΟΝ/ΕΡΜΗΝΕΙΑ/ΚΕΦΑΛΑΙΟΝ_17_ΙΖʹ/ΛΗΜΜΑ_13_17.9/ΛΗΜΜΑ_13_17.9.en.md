# Matthew 17 (ΙΖʹ) — Lemma 13 · Matt. 17:10

> **And his disciples asked him, saying: Why then do the scribes say that Elijah must come first? (Matt. 17:10)**[^1]

Deceiving the people, the scribes said that this *man* was not the Christ; for if he were, Elijah would have come first — being ignorant that *there are* two comings of Christ, of which the forerunner of the first, on the one hand, *is* John, but of the second, on the other hand, *is* Elijah. Which very thing, therefore, Christ also explains to the disciples. Hear:[^2]

[^1]: The folder metadata assigns this lemma to 17:9, but the Greek quoted — «Τί οὖν οἱ Γραμματεῖς λέγουσιν ὅτι Ἠλίαν δεῖ ἐλθεῖν πρῶτον;» — is the disciples' question of Matt. 17:10; header and reference corrected accordingly. The OCR reads «ἐπηρώτησαν αὐτων» for «ἐπηρώτησαν αὐτὸν» ("asked him"), a low-stakes error.
[^2]: The exposition breaks off at the imperative *Ἄκουε* ("Hear"), which introduces the Lord's answer quoted at the head of the following lemma (17:11–12).
