# Matthew 17 (ΙΖʹ) — Lemma 23 · Matt. 17:22

> **But while they were dwelling[^1] in Galilee, Jesus said to them: "The Son of man is about to be delivered into *the* hands of men, and[^2]**

Only the Gospel words are carried here; the exposition follows in the next lemma, once the sentence has been completed.

[^1]: Greek **ἀναστρεφομένων** ("while they were dwelling / going about / conducting themselves") — the participle denotes their staying and moving about in Galilee. OCR «εἶκαν» for **εἶπεν** ("said") and «παραδίθοσθαι» for **παραδίδοσθαι** ("to be delivered / betrayed") are secure corrections.
[^2]: The lemma breaks off at «καὶ» ("and—"); the sentence is completed at the head of the next lemma («ἀποκτενοῦσιν αὐτόν…», "they shall kill him…").
