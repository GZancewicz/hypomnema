# Matthew 17 (ΙΖʹ) — Lemma 17 · Matt. 17:16–17

> **And I brought him to your disciples, and they were not able to heal him. But Jesus answering said: O unbelieving and perverse generation, how long shall I be with you? how long shall I bear with you? Bring him to me here; and—**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture words; Theophylact supplies no separate exposition here.*[^2]

[^1]: The verse quotation breaks off at *καὶ* ("and—"), completed at the head of the following lemma by «ἐπετίμησεν αὐτῷ ὁ Ἰησοῦς …» (17:18). OCR «ησοῦς» = Ἰησοῦς, «ἕσομαι» = ἔσομαι — secure. *διεστραμμένη* = "perverse / twisted."
[^2]: Theophylact's comment on the father's complaint, on "O unbelieving generation," and on "how long shall I be with you?" is given under the following lemma (17:18), where the healing is narrated.
