# Matthew 17 (ΙΖʹ) — Lemma 21 · Matt. 17:21

> **but this kind does not go out except by[^1] prayer and fasting."**

The apostles were afraid lest they had perhaps lost the grace given to them against the demons; for this reason they ask privately, being in anguish. But the Lord, reproaching them as *being* rather imperfect,[^2] says that *it is* "because of your unbelief"; for if you had a warm and fervent faith, even though it seemed small, you would have accomplished great things. But where the apostles moved mountains, nowhere indeed has it been written — yet it was likely that they did move *them* and *that it was* not written; for not all things were written. And besides, *the* occasion did not require *it*, and for this reason they did not move *them*, but did other, greater things. See, then, what the Lord also said:[^3]

[^1]: Greek «οὐκ ἐκπορεύεται εἰ μὴ ἐν προσευχῇ καὶ νηστείᾳ» — literally "does not go out if not in prayer and fasting"; «εἰ μὴ» ("if not") is rendered "except." The OCR splinters «εἰ μὴ» as «εἰ, Ψὴ» across the line break; the reading is secure.
[^2]: Greek **ἀτελεστέροις**, comparative of *ἀτελής* ("imperfect, incomplete, immature," also "uninitiated"): "as *being* rather imperfect / somewhat immature." The word suggests spiritual immaturity rather than mere moral fault.
[^3]: This leads directly into the next lemma, which re-quotes «Ἐρεῖτε τῷ ὄρει τούτῳ, Μετάβηθι» ("You shall say to this mountain, Move").
