# Matthew 17 (ΙΖʹ) — Lemma 24 · Matt. 17:23

> **they shall kill him, and on the third day he shall be raised; and they were exceedingly grieved."**

Continuously he foretells concerning the passion, so that he might not be thought to suffer unwillingly, and at the same time so that he might train them, and *that* they might not be troubled by the unexpected when it should come to pass. And he joins to the grievous things also the joyful *one* — the *saying*, "He will rise again."[^1]

[^1]: The lemma reads «ἐγερθήσεται» ("he shall be raised"); in the comment Theophylact restates it with the synonym **«Ἀναστήσεται»** ("He will rise / rise again"). Both name the resurrection; the change of verb is his own paraphrase, not a variant in the quoted text.
