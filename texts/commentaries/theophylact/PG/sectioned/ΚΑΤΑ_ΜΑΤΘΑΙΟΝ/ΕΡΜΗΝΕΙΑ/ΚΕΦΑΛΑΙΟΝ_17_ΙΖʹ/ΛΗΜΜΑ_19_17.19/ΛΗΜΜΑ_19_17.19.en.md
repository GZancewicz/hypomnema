# Matthew 17 (ΙΖʹ) — Lemma 19 · Matt. 17:19

> **Then the disciples, having come to Jesus privately,[^1] said: "For what reason were we not able to cast it out?" But[^2]**

This lemma carries only the Gospel words; Theophylact's exposition of them does not begin here but is deferred — the quotation runs on through the following lemma, and the comment upon it is taken up at Lemma 21.

[^1]: OCR «κατ´ Μείαν»; securely **«κατ' ἰδίαν»** ("privately, apart, by themselves"), the standard Gospel idiom — and confirmed by the exposition at Lemma 21, which repeats κατ' ἰδίαν ("they ask privately").
[^2]: The lemma breaks off mid-sentence at «Ὁ δὲ» ("But he / And the—"); the subject and verb (**«Ἰησοῦς εἶπεν αὐτοῖς»**, "Jesus said to them") are supplied at the head of the next lemma.
