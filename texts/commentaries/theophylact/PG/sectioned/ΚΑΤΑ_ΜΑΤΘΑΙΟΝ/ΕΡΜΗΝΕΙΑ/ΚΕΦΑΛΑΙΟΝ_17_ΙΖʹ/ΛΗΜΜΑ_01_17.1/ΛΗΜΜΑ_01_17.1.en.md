# Matthew 17 (ΙΖʹ) — Lemma 1 · Matt. 17:1

> **... six days,[^1] Jesus takes along Peter, and James, and John his brother. (Matt. 17:1)**

This is not contrary to the thing said by Luke, that,[^2]

[^1]: The lemma opens mid-phrase in the source; the txt begins «ἡμέρας δξ» — where «δξ» is an OCR corruption of **ἕξ** ("six"). The preceding words «Καὶ μεθ᾽» ("And after") stand at the head of the chapter section. The full lemma is thus «Καὶ μεθ᾽ ἡμέρας ἓξ παραλαμβάνει ὁ Ἰησοῦς...» ("And after six days Jesus takes along..."). *παραλαμβάνει* is present tense, "takes along / takes with *him*."
[^2]: The comment breaks off here; the sentence is completed in the next lemma, which supplies Luke's words «Μετὰ δὲ τοὺς λόγους τούτους ἐγένοντο ὡσεὶ ἡμέραι ὀκτώ» (Luke 9:28).
