# Matthew 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 25 · Matt. 2:11

> **And having come into the house, they saw the child with Mary his mother. (Matt. 2:11)**

As soon as he was born, the Virgin laid the child down in the manger, for they did not then find a house; but after these things it is likely they found a house, where also the Magi found them. For they went up into Bethlehem, in order that they might be registered there, just as Luke also says.[^1] But a great multitude running together on account of the registration, they did not have for the moment a house, but he was born in the cave;[^2] then a house was found, where the Lord was seen by the Magi.

[^1]: Cf. Luke 2:1–5. Greek *ἵνα ἀπογραφῶσιν ἐκεῖ* — *ἀπογράφω* is the technical term for enrolment in a census/tax-register; rendered "be registered."
[^2]: Greek *ἐν τῷ σπηλαίῳ ἐτέχθη* ("he was born in the cave"). Theophylact reflects the older tradition (as in the *Protevangelium of James* and Justin Martyr) locating the Nativity in a cave (*σπήλαιον*), harmonizing it with Luke's manger (*φάτνη*), which he mentioned just above.
