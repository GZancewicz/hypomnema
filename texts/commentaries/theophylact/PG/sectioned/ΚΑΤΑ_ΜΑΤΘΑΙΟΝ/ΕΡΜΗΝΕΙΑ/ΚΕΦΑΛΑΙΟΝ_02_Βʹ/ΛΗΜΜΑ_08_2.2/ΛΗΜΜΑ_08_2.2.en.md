# Matthew 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 8 · Matt. 2:2

> **And we have come to worship him. (Matt. 2:2)**

These Magi are shown[^1] to be of great virtue. For if in a foreign land they were willing to worship, how would they not have spoken boldly and proclaimed *it* in Persia?

[^1]: Greek OCR reads *Χαίνονται*, securely = *Φαίνονται* ("they appear / are shown *to be*"); the correction is certain and low-stakes, noted for consistency with the other flagged OCR corruptions.
