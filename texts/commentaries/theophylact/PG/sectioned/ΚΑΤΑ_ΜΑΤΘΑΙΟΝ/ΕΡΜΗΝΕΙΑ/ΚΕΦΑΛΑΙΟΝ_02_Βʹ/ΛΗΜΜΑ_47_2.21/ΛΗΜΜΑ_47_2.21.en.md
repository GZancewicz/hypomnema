# Matthew 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 47 · Matt. 2:22

> **But having been warned in a dream, he withdrew into the parts of Galilee.** (Matt. 2:22)[^1]

Galilee was not *the* land of Israel, but was of the gentiles; wherefore also the Jews looked upon these *people* as an abomination.[^2]

[^1]: Greek *Χρηματισθεὶς δὲ κατ᾽ ὄναρ, ἀνεχώρησεν εἰς τὰ μέρη τῆς Γαλιλαίας*. The participle *χρηματισθεὶς* means "having been divinely warned / instructed by an oracle"; rendered "having been warned." *τὰ μέρη* = "the parts / regions."
[^2]: *τούτους* ("these") refers to the inhabitants of Galilee, reckoned among the gentiles; the supplied "*people*" makes the reference explicit and is italicized. *βδέλυγμα* = "abomination / an object of loathing." Theophylact's point is that the Jews despised the Galileans as gentile territory (cf. "Galilee of the gentiles," Isa. 9:1; Matt. 4:15).
