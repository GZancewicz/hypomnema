# Matthew 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 17 · Matt. 2:7

> **Then Herod, having secretly called the Magi. (Matt. 2:7)**[^1]

Secretly he called them, on account of the Jews. For he suspected that the Jews would perhaps make much of the child, and would take care to save it, as one about to free them. For this reason, therefore, he secretly plots.[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Τότε ὁ Ἡρώδης λάθρα καλέσας τοὺς Μάγους». *λάθρα* ("secretly, in secret") is the pivot of the whole comment and is rendered identically each time it recurs.
[^2]: Greek *ἐπιβουλεύει*, "plots against, schemes against, lays a plot" — a hostile designing; the present tense is kept ("he plots").
