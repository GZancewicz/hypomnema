# Matthew 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 24 · Matt. 2:10

> **And having seen the star, they rejoiced *with* exceedingly great joy. (Matt. 2:10)**[^1]

As those who had not been led astray, but had found the thing sought, they rejoiced.[^2]

[^1]: The folder-name verse is 2.9, but the Greek quoted is Matt. 2:10. OCR «Ἰόντες δὲ τὸν κστέρα» is securely **«Ἰδόντες δὲ τὸν ἀστέρα»** ("And having seen the star"). *χαρὰν μεγάλην σφόδρα* is a cognate accusative; the supplied "*with*" renders it into idiomatic English ("rejoiced *with* exceedingly great joy").
[^2]: Greek *Ὡς οὐ πλανηθέντες, ἀλλ᾽ εὑρόντες τὸ ζητούμενον, ἐχάρησαν* — "As those who had not been led astray, but had found the thing sought, they rejoiced." *πλανηθέντες* ("led astray / made to wander") plays on the wandering journey of the Magi; the aorist participles are kept as such, with "*those who had*" supplied to carry them into English.
