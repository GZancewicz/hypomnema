# Matthew 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 37 · Matt. 2:16

> **And in all its borders, from two years *old* and under, according to the time which *he* had ascertained exactly from the Magi. (Matt. 2:16)**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the continuation of the scripture verse, with no exposition of its own; Theophylact's comment on the slaughter of the infants is given under the preceding lemma.*[^2]

[^1]: The lemma continues Matt. 2:16 from the previous lemma («he sent forth and slew all the children in Bethlehem…»). Greek «ἀπὸ διετοῦς καὶ κατωτέρω» ("from two years old and downward"); the "*old*" is supplied. The verb *ἠκρίβωσε* ("inquired exactly, ascertained precisely") is the same root used of Herod's questioning of the Magi at Matt. 2:7; rendered "ascertained exactly." The OCR reads «παρὰ τὺν Μάγων», securely = **«παρὰ τῶν Μάγων»** ("from the Magi").
[^2]: The .txt file contains the «…» scripture quotation alone, terminating in the OCR's stray «.,» with no following prose. The exposition proper belongs to Lemma 36.
