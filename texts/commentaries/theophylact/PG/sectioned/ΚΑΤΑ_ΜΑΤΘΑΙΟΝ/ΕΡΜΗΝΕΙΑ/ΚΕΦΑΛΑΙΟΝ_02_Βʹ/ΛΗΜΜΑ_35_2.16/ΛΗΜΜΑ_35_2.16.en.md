# Matthew 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 35 · Matt. 2:16

> **Then Herod, having seen that he was mocked by the magi. (Matt. 2:16)**[^1]

Just as God mocked Pharaoh through Moses, so also *he mocked* Herod through the Magi; since both—both Herod and Pharaoh—*were* child-killers. Pharaoh, on the one hand, slaying the male *children* of the Hebrews in Egypt; but Herod, on the other hand, *slaying* the *male children* of those in Bethlehem.

[^1]: Minor OCR corruptions securely corrected: *ενεπαίχθη* → **ἐνεπαίχθη** ("was mocked, was made sport of"); *λάγων* → **μάγων** ("magi"). The verb *ἐμπαίζω* ("mock, make sport of") ties the lemma to the exposition, where the same verb describes God's mockery of Pharaoh.
