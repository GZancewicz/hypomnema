# Matthew 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 32 · Matt. 2:13

> **For Herod is about to seek the child, to destroy it. (Matt. 2:13)**

See *the* folly of *a* man: he is eager to overcome the counsel of God. For if, on the one hand, it is not from God, why are you afraid? But if, on the other hand, it is from God, how will you destroy the child?
