# Matthew 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 43 · Matt. 2:19

> **Behold, an angel of the Lord appears in a dream to Joseph in Egypt,** (Matt. 2:19)[^1]

[^1]: In the OCR the first word «Ἰβοὺ» is securely **«Ἰδοὺ»** ("Behold"); the reconstruction is certain. The verb *φαίνεται* is present tense, "appears." The sentence breaks off at the comma and is completed in the next lemma: «λέγων· Ἐγερθεὶς, παράλαβε τὸ παιδίον…» ("saying: Having arisen, take the child…").
