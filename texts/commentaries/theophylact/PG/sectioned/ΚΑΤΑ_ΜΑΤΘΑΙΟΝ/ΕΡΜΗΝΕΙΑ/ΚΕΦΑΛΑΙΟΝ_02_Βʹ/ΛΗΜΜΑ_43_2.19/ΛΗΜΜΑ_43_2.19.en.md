# Matthew 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 43 · Matt. 2:19

> **Behold, an angel of *the* Lord appears in a dream to Joseph in Egypt,** (Matt. 2:19)[^1]

[^1]: This lemma carries only the opening words of the sentence, which breaks off at the comma and is completed in the next lemma with «λέγων· Ἐγερθεὶς, παράλαβε τὸ παιδίον…» ("saying: Having arisen, take the child…"). Theophylact supplies no exposition here. In the OCR the first word is «Ἰβοὺ», securely = **«Ἰδοὺ»** ("Behold"); the reconstruction is certain and low-stakes. The verb *φαίνεται* is present tense ("appears"), retained as such. *Κυρίου* is anarthrous (no article), so the supplied "*the*" is italicized.
