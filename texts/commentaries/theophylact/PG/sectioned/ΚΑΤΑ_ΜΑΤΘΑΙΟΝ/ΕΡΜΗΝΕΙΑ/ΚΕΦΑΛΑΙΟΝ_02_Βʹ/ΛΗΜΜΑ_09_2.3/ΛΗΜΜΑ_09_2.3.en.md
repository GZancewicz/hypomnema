# Matthew 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 9 · Matt. 2:3

> **But Herod the king, having heard *it*, was troubled, and all Jerusalem with him. (Matt. 2:3)**

Herod was troubled, as a foreigner[^1] fearing for the kingdom; for he knew that he was unworthy of it. But the Jews — for what reason are they troubled? For it was necessary for them rather to rejoice, because a king would be theirs, worshipped by the Persian kings; but truly a senseless thing is wickedness.

[^1]: Greek *ἀλλόφυλος* ("of another tribe / foreigner / alien-born") — the point is that Herod was an Idumaean, not of the royal Judaean line, and so had reason to fear a legitimate-born king; hence "unworthy of it *[the kingdom]*."
