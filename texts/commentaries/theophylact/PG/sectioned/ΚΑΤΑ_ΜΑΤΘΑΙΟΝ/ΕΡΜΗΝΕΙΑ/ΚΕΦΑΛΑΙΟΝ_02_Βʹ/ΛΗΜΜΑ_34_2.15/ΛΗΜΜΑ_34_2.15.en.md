# Matthew 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 34 · Matt. 2:15

> **—death of Herod; that it might be fulfilled that which was spoken by the Lord through the prophet, saying: Out of Egypt I called my Son. (Matt. 2:15)**[^1]

The Jews say that this was spoken concerning the people—the one led back up out of Egypt through Moses. We say, therefore, that this, on the one hand, was said of the people typically,[^2] but, on the other hand, it came to pass upon Christ truly.[^3] Then, who is the Son of God?[^4] The people worshipping the idol Beelphegor[^5] and the graven images, or he who is truly the Son of God?

[^1]: The lemma opens by completing the sentence begun at the end of Lemma 33 («…and was there until the—»), here supplying *τελευτῆς Ἡρώδου* ("the death of Herod"). The prophetic citation is Hos. 11:1; Theophylact quotes it as *ὑπὸ τοῦ Κυρίου διὰ τοῦ προφήτου* ("by the Lord through the prophet"), following Matthew's own wording.
[^2]: The Greek here is OCR-corrupted: *Λέγομει οὖν, ὅτι καὶ τι καινὸν ἐλέχθη ἐπὶ τῷ λαῷ τυπικῶς*. It is securely recoverable as *Λέγομεν οὖν, ὅτι τοῦτο μὲν ἐλέχθη ἐπὶ τῷ λαῷ τυπικῶς* ("We say, therefore, that this, on the one hand, was said of the people typically"), which the following *ἀπέβη δὲ* ("but it came to pass") answers with the *μὲν … δὲ* antithesis. Translated on that reconstruction.
[^3]: Greek OCR *ἀξέβη*, securely = *ἀπέβη* ("turned out, came to pass, issued in"); the antithesis is *τυπικῶς* ("typically, as a type") versus *ἀληθινῶς* ("truly, in reality") — the Exodus deliverance was the type, Christ the true fulfilment.
[^4]: Greek OCR *ὁ Κὸς τοῦ Θεοῦ*, securely = *ὁ Υἱὸς τοῦ Θεοῦ* ("the Son of God"): the corrupted *Κὸς* is confirmed as *Υἱὸς* by the parallel *ὁ ὄντως Υἱὸς τοῦ Θεοῦ* ("he who is truly the Son of God") at the close of the same sentence.
[^5]: Greek *Βεελφεγώρ* — Baal-Peor (cf. Num. 25:3), retained here in Theophylact's form. His argument: the "son" who worshipped idols cannot be the true "Son" of Hos. 11:1; that title belongs properly to Christ.
