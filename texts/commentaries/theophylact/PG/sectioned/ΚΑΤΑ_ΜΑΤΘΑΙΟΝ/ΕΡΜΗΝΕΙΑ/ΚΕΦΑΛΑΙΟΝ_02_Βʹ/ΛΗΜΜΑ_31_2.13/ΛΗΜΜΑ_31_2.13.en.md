# Matthew 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 31 · Matt. 2:13

> **And be there, until I tell you. (Matt. 2:13)**

"Be" (*ἴσθι*),[^1] instead of *ἔσο*: be there, until you should be commanded by God. For we must do nothing outside the divine counsel.

[^1]: Greek *ἴσθι* is ambiguous, being the imperative of both *οἶδα* ("know" — "know!") and *εἰμί* ("be" — "be!"). Theophylact removes the ambiguity by glossing it with the unambiguous imperative *ἔσο* ("be"): *ἴσθι, ἀντὶ τοῦ ἔσο ἐκεῖ* — that is, the word means "be there," not "know." Rendered "be" to preserve his point.
