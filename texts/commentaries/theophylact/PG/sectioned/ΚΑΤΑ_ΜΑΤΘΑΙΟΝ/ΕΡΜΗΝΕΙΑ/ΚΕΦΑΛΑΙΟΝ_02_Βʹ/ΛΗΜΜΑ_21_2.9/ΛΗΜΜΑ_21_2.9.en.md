# Matthew 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 21 · Matt. 2:9

> **…having heard the king, went their way. (Matt. 2:9)**[^1]

For being themselves guileless, they supposed that he too was speaking without guile.[^2]

[^1]: This lemma completes the sentence begun in Lemma 20 with «Οἱ δὲ» ("But they,"): «Οἱ δὲ ἀκούσαντες τοῦ βασιλέως ἐπορεύθησαν» — "But they, having heard the king, went their way." The participle *ἀκούσαντες* ("having heard") is kept as such.
[^2]: Greek OCR «Ἄὰολοι γὰρ ὄντες αὐτοί, ἐνόμιζον κἀκεῖνον ἐὸόλως λέγειν» is securely **«Ἁπλοῖ γὰρ ὄντες αὐτοί, ἐνόμιζον κἀκεῖνον ἀδόλως λέγειν»**. *Ἁπλοῖ* ("simple, single-minded, guileless") and *ἀδόλως* ("without guile/deceit") form a deliberate pairing: because the Magi were themselves without guile, they assumed Herod likewise spoke without guile. Rendered "guileless … without guile" to keep the play visible.
