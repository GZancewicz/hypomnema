# Matthew 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 11 · Matt. 2:5

> **But they said to him: In Bethlehem of Judea; for thus it is written through the prophet. (Matt. 2:5)**[^1]

Of which prophet? Of Micah. For that one says,[^2]

[^1]: The folder-name verse is 2.4, but the lemma quotes Matt. 2:5 («Οἱ δὲ εἶπον αὐτῷ· Ἐν Βηθλεὲμ τῆς Ἰουδαίας...»); the header and reference are corrected accordingly.
[^2]: The comment breaks off in mid-sentence at *φησιν* ("says,"); the quotation from Micah is supplied in the following lemma, «Καὶ σὺ, Βηθλεέμ, γῆ Ἰούδα...» (Matt. 2:6, citing Mic. 5:2).
