# Matthew 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 33 · Matt. 2:14

> **But he, having risen, took the child and its mother *by* night, and departed into Egypt, and was there until the—[^1]**

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation and breaks off mid-sentence; it has no exposition of its own. The sentence is completed at the head of the next lemma, which supplies* «death of Herod» *and Theophylact's comment.*

[^1]: The quotation is cut off at *ἕως τῆς* ("until the—") and is completed at the opening of Lemma 34: *τελευτῆς Ἡρώδου* ("the death of Herod"). Minor OCR corruptions in the Greek are securely corrected: *ἀνεχώρῃσεν* → **ἀνεχώρησεν** ("departed"); *Αἴνυπτον* → **Αἴγυπτον** ("Egypt").
