# Matthew 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 16 · Matt. 2:6

> **I am the good shepherd.**[^1]

And by "people Israel" he means both those from the Hebrews who believed and those from the Gentiles. For "Israel" is interpreted "seeing God,"[^2] so that all those seeing God are Israelites, even if they be from the Gentiles.

[^1]: The words of Christ (John 10:11) which Theophylact supplies to complete the sentence begun in the preceding lemma («as he himself also says»). Greek «Ἐγώ εἰμι ὁ ποιμὴν ὁ καλός». This lemma continues the exposition of Matt. 2:6, "shepherd my people Israel."
[^2]: Theophylact's etymology: *Ἰσραήλ* = «ὁρῶν Θεόν» ("seeing God"). On this reading the name denotes a spiritual condition rather than physical descent, so that believing Gentiles too are counted "Israelites."
