# Matthew 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 5 · Matt. 2:1

> **From *the* East. (Matt. 2:1)**

And this too *is* for the condemnation of the Hebrews. For the ones, on the one hand, came from so great a distance; but the Hebrews, on the other hand, having Christ right there,[^1] were persecuting *him*.

[^1]: Greek *αὐτοῦ ἔχοντες τὸν Χριστόν*. *αὐτοῦ* is taken as the adverb "there, on the very spot" (the Hebrews had Christ present among them), which yields the sharpest contrast with the Magi who came "from so great a distance." It could grammatically be the pronoun ("having him, *namely* Christ"), but the adverbial sense fits the antithesis and is adopted here.
