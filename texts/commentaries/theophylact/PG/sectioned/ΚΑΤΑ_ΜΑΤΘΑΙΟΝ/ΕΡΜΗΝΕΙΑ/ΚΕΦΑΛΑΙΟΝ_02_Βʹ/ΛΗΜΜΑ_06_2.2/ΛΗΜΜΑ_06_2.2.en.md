# Matthew 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 6 · Matt. 2:2

> **Saying, Where is he who has been born king of the Jews? (Matt. 2:2)**[^1]

They say that these Magi were descendants of Balaam the seer; and *that*, having found that man's oracle — the "*There* shall rise a star out of Jacob"[^2] — they understood the mystery concerning Christ, and for this reason came, wishing to see the *one* born.[^3]

[^1]: Greek *ὁ τεχθεὶς βασιλεὺς τῶν Ἰουδαίων* — literally "the having-been-born king of the Jews," an aorist passive participle; rendered "he who has been born king of the Jews" to keep it verbal (cf. the closing *τὸ τεχθέν*, "the *thing/one* born").
[^2]: Balaam's oracle, Num. 24:17: *ἀνατελεῖ ἄστρον ἐξ Ἰακώβ* ("a star shall rise out of Jacob"). The verb *ἀνατελεῖ* ("shall rise") plays on *Ἀνατολή* ("the East / the rising"), the region the Magi came from.
[^3]: Greek OCR *έλοντας* is securely *θέλοντας* ("wishing"); the reconstruction is certain, noted only for consistency with the other flagged corruptions.
