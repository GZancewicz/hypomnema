# Matthew 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 12 · Matt. 2:6

> **And you, Bethlehem, land of Judah, are by no means least among the rulers[^1] of Judah, (Matt. 2:6)**

For since this *town* was small, they used to despise it; but now, on account of the Christ who came forth from it, it is renowned. For all come from the ends of the earth to worship this holy Bethlehem.

[^1]: Greek *ἐν τοῖς ἡγεμόσιν Ἰούδα* ("among the rulers / leaders / governors of Judah") — Matthew's citation reads *ἡγεμόσιν* where the Micah source (Mic. 5:2 LXX) reads *χιλιάσιν* ("among the thousands / clans of Judah"). Theophylact quotes Matthew's wording; rendered "rulers" to keep his text, not the prophet's, without conforming to a familiar Bible.
