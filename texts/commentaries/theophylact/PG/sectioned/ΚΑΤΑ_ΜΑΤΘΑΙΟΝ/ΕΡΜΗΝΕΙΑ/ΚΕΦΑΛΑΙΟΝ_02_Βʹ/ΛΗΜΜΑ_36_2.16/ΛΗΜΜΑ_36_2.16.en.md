# Matthew 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 36 · Matt. 2:16

> **He was exceedingly enraged, and having sent forth he killed all the children who were in Bethlehem. (Matt. 2:16)**

He displays his wrath against the Magi upon those who had done no wrong at all. But for what reason were the children permitted to be slaughtered? So that the wickedness of Herod might be shown. But perhaps you will say to me: "And what of this? On account of this—that his wickedness might be shown—were the children wronged?" Hear, then, that they were not wronged, but were deemed worthy of crowns.[^1] For everyone who suffers something evil here either suffers it for the dissolution of his sins, or for the addition of crowns; just as also these children shall be crowned the more greatly.

[^1]: Several minor OCR corruptions are securely corrected: *ἦνακεν* → **ἕνεκεν** ("for the sake of," in *τίνος … ἕνεκεν*, "for what reason"); *ἄειχθῇ* → **δειχθῇ** ("might be shown"); *ἠξιώβησαν* → **ἠξιώθησαν** ("were deemed worthy"); *ἀμαρτηματων* → **ἁμαρτημάτων** ("of sins"). None affects the sense.
