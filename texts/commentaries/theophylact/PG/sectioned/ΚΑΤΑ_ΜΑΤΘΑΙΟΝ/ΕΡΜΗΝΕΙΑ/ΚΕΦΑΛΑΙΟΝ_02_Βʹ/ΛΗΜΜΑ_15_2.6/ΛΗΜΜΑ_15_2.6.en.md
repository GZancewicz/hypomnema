# Matthew 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 15 · Matt. 2:6

> **Who shall shepherd my people Israel.**[^1]

"He shall shepherd," he said — but not "he shall tyrannize" or "he shall devour." For the other kings, on the one hand, *are* not shepherds, but wolves; but Christ, on the other hand, *is* a shepherd, as he himself also says:[^2]

[^1]: The concluding clause of the Micah 5:2 citation quoted at Matt. 2:6. Greek «Ὅστις ποιμανεῖ τὸν λαόν μου τὸν Ἰσραήλ».
[^2]: The sentence breaks off at «λέγει·» ("says:"); the quoted words of Christ are supplied at the head of the next lemma, «Ἐγώ εἰμι ὁ ποιμὴν ὁ καλός» (John 10:11).
