# Matthew 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 38 · Matt. 2:17

> **Then was fulfilled that which was spoken by Jeremiah the prophet, saying: (Matt. 2:17)**

In order that no one might suppose that the destruction of the infants came about with God unwilling,[^1] he shows that *God* both foreknew it and foretold it.[^2]

[^1]: Greek *ἄκοντος τοῦ Θεοῦ* — a genitive absolute, "*with* God being unwilling / against God's will." The point is that the massacre was not something God suffered helplessly.
[^2]: Greek *προεγίνωσκε καὶ προεῖπεν* ("foreknew and foretold"); the object "it" is supplied in each case. The OCR reads «δείνυσιν», securely = **«δείκνυσιν»** ("he shows"). The clause runs on toward the citation of the prophet, which the following lemmata supply.
