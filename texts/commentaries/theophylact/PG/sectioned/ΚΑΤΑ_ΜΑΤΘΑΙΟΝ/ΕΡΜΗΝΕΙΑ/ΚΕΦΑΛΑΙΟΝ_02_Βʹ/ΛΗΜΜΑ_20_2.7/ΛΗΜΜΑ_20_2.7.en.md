# Matthew 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 20 · Matt. 2:8

> **"But when you find *him*, report to me, so that I too, having come, may worship him." But they,[^1] (Matt. 2:8)**[^2]

[^1]: The lemma continues Herod's speech from the previous lemma and then adds the narrator's opening words «Οἱ δὲ» ("But they,"), which run on into Lemma 21 («…having heard the king, went their way»). The supplied "*him*" is the unexpressed object of *εὕρητε* ("you find").
[^2]: The folder-name verse is 2.7; the Greek is Matt. 2:8 (running into 2:9). Lightly OCR-garbled: «Ἐπάν ἀὲ εθρητε, ἀπαγγείλατ μοι» is securely **«Ἐπὰν δὲ εὕρητε, ἀπαγγείλατέ μοι»** ("But when you find *him*, report to me").
