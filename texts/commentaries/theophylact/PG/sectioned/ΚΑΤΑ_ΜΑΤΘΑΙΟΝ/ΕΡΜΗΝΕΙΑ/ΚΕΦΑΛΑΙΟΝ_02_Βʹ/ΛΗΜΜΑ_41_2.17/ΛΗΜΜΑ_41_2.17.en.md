# Matthew 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 41 · Matt. 2:18

> **and she would not be comforted, because they are not. (Matt. 2:18)**[^1]

In this life they are not, since the souls are immortal.[^2]

[^1]: The folder metadata assigns this to 2:17, but the quoted words conclude Matt. 2:18; corrected in the header and blockquote. The OCR reads «οὐκ εἰαίν», securely = **«οὐκ εἰσίν»** ("they are not").
[^2]: Greek «Ἐν τῷ βίῳ τούτῳ οὐκ εἰσίν, ἐπεὶ αἱ ψυχαὶ ἀθάνατοι» ("In this life they are not, since the souls immortal," the copula being unexpressed). Theophylact glosses the prophet's "they are not" as meaning that the infants are absent only from *this* life, not annihilated, their souls being deathless.
