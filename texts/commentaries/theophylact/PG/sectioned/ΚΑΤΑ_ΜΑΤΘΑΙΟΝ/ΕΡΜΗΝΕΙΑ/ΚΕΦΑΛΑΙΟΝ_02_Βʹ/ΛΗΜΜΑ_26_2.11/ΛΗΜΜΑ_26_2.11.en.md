# Matthew 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 26 · Matt. 2:11

> **And having fallen down they worshipped him. (Matt. 2:11)**

O, *the* illumination of a soul![^1] They were looking upon a poor *child*, and they were worshipping *him*. For they were fully assured that he is God, wherefore also they bring to him gifts, as to God and as to man. For hear:[^2]

[^1]: The OCR reads «ἱς ψυχῆς ἔλλαμψιν», which is corrupt at the first word; it is securely restored as **«Ὢ ψυχῆς ἔλλαμψιν!»** — an exclamation, "O, *the* illumination of *the* soul!" (i.e. of the Magi's souls, which perceived divinity in a pauper). *ἔλλαμψις* = "illumination, shining-in," a term of spiritual enlightenment.
[^2]: The exposition breaks off at *Ἄκουε γάρ·* ("For hear:"), introducing the gifts enumerated in the next lemma (Matt. 2:11b), where Theophylact explains gold, frankincense, and myrrh.
