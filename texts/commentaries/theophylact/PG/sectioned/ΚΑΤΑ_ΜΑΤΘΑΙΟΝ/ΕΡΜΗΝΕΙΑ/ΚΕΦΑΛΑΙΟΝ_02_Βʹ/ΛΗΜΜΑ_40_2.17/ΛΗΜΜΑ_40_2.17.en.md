# Matthew 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 40 · Matt. 2:18

> **Lamentation and weeping, and much mourning; Rachel weeping *for* her children, (Matt. 2:18)**[^1]

that is, Bethlehem,[^2]

[^1]: The folder metadata assigns this to 2:17, but the quoted words belong to Matt. 2:18; corrected in the header and blockquote. Theophylact's text reads «Θρῆνος καὶ κλαυθμὸς καὶ ὀδυρμὸς πολύς» ("Lamentation and weeping, and much mourning"), the order of the first two nouns differing from the common Gospel text's «κλαυθμὸς καὶ ὀδυρμός»; his wording is followed as it stands. In «Ῥαχὴλ κλαίουσα τὰ τέκνα αὐτῆς» the accusative *τὰ τέκνα* is the direct object of "weeping"; the "*for*" is supplied for English idiom.
[^2]: The gloss identifies "Rachel" with Bethlehem (as established in the previous lemma), and breaks off; the comment resumes in the following lemma with «and she would not be comforted…».
