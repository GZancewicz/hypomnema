# Matthew 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 4 · Matt. 2:1

> **Behold, Magi from *the* East arrived in Jerusalem. (Matt. 2:1)**[^1]

For what reason do the Magi come? For *the* condemnation of the Jews. For if the Magi, men *who were* idolaters, believed, what defense then *is left* to the Jews? And at the same time, also so that the glory of Christ might shine forth the more, the Magi bearing witness — they *who were* most of all subject to the demons, and enemies of God.

[^1]: Greek *Μάγοι* — the *magoi*, Persian priest-astrologers (whence "Magi"); retained rather than paraphrased as "wise men." *Ἀνατολῶν* is plural, "the risings *of the sun*," i.e. "the East"; the supplied "*the*" is italicized.
