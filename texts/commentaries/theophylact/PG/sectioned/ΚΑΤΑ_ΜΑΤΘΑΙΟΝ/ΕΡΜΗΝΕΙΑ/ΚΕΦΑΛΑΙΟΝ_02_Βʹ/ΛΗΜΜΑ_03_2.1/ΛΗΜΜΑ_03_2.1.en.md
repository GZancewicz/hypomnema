# Matthew 2 (Βʹ) — Lemma 3 · Matt. 2:1

> **The king. (Matt. 2:1)**

For there was also another Herod, the tetrarch;[^1] for this reason he sets down the title of "king."[^2]

[^1]: The other Herod is Herod Antipas, tetrarch of Galilee (the Herod of the Passion and of John the Baptist's death); the Herod of the Nativity is his father, Herod the Great. Theophylact's point: Matthew specifies "the king" to distinguish the reigning monarch from the later tetrarch.
[^2]: Greek *τίθησι τὸ ἀξίωμα τοῦ βασιλέως* — *ἀξίωμα* is "rank, dignity, title." The lemma breaks off here mid-thought (the source ends with a raised point), the exposition continuing in the following lemma.
