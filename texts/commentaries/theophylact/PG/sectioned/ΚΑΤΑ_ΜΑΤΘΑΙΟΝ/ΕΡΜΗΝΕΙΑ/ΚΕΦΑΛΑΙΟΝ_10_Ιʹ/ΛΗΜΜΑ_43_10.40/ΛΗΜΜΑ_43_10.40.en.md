# Matthew 10 (Ιʹ) — Lemma 43 · Matt. 10:40

> **He who receives you, receives me; and he who *receives* me—**[^1][^2]

[^1]: Greek «Ὁ δεχόμενος ὑμᾶς, ἐμὲ δέχεται· καὶ ὁ ἐμὲ …» (Matt. 10:40a). The clause is cut off at *ὁ ἐμὲ* ("and he who … me"); the governing participle *δεχόμενος* ("receiving") stands at the head of the next lemma, so the verb is supplied here in italics only to make the fragment readable in English.
[^2]: The OCR reads the final word as *ἐμὴ*; it is securely *ἐμὲ* ("me," accusative), the object of the *δεχόμενος* that opens Lemma 44.
