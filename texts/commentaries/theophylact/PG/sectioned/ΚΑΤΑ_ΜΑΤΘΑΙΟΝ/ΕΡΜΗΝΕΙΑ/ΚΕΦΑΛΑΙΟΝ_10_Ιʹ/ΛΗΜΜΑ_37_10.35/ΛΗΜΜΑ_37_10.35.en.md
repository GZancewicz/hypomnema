# Matthew 10 (Ιʹ) — Lemma 37 · Matt. 10:34–35

> **…have I come to cast peace, but a sword.[^1] For I came to divide a man against his father, and a daughter against her mother; and a daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law;[^2] and…** (Matt. 10:34–35)

[^1]: This lemma opens mid-clause: the negative *οὐκ* ("not") stands at the close of the preceding lemma (36), «...οὐκ», so that the full Gospel sentence reads *οὐκ ἦλθον βαλεῖν εἰρήνην, ἀλλὰ μάχαιραν* ("I came **not** to cast peace, but a sword," Matt. 10:34). *βαλεῖν* is rendered "to cast" (throw/put), keeping the concrete verb rather than a smoother "bring."
[^2]: Greek *νύμφην κατὰ τῆς πενθερᾶς αὐτῆς*. *νύμφη* means both "bride" and "daughter-in-law"; paired here with *πενθερά* ("mother-in-law") the sense is "daughter-in-law." Rendered so, with the ambiguity flagged.
