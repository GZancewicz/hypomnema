# Matthew 10 (Ιʹ) — Lemma 41 · Matt. 10:38

> **And follows after me,[^1] (Matt. 10:38)**

— that is, he lives according to my laws.[^2]

[^1]: The clause Theophylact singles out from Matt. 10:38 (the addition promised at the end of lemma 40), quoted so as to gloss it separately.
[^2]: Greek *τουτέστι, κατὰ τοὺς ἐμοὺς νόμους ζῇ* ("that is, he lives according to my laws"). After the gloss the source carries a dangling *Ὁ* — the article opening the next Gospel quotation, «Ὁ εὑρὼν τὴν ψυχὴν αὐτοῦ…» — which properly belongs to, and is taken up in, the following lemma (42).
