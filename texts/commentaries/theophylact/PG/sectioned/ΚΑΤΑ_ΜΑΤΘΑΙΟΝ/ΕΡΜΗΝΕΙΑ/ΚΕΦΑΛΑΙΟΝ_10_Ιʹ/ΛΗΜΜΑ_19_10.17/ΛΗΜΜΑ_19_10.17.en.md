# Matthew 10 (Ιʹ) — Lemma 19 · Matt. 10:17

> **But beware of men; for they will deliver you up to councils, and in their synagogues they will scourge you, and before**[^1] (Matt. 10:17)

[^1]: The Greek lemma ends abruptly at «καὶ ἐπὶ» ("and before / and unto"), with the object left dangling; the governing verb and its objects («ἡγεμόνας δὲ καὶ βασιλεῖς ἀχθήσεσθε» — "you shall be brought before governors and kings") arrive at the head of the next lemma (Matt. 10:18). The opening «Προσέχετε δὲ ἀπὸ τῶν ἀνθρώπων» is rendered "But beware of men" (δέ = "but"); *προσέχετε ἀπό* is the idiom "take heed of / beware of." The OCR reads «ἀπῆ» for «ἀπὸ», a certain, low-stakes correction.
