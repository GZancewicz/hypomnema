# Matthew 10 (Ιʹ) — Lemma 14 · Matt. 10:12–13

> **But entering into the house, greet it, saying: Peace to this house; and if on the one hand the house is worthy, let your peace come upon it, but if on the other hand it is not worthy, let your peace return to you. (Matt. 10:12–13)**[^1]

Understand *by* the greeting and the peace the blessing, which remains only with the worthy. Learn therefore from this that our own works most of all bless us.[^2]

[^1]: The folder metadata assigns this lemma to 10:12, but the quoted Greek runs through 10:13 (the clauses about the peace coming upon the house or returning); the header reflects the fuller span.
[^2]: Greek *μάθε οὖν ἐντεῦθεν ὅτι μάλιστα τὰ ἡμέτερα ἔργα εὐλογοῦσιν ἡμᾶς*. The point: the blessing "sticks" only where the recipient is worthy, so whether one is blessed depends on one's own works — one's works are what "bless" (bring the blessing upon) oneself.
