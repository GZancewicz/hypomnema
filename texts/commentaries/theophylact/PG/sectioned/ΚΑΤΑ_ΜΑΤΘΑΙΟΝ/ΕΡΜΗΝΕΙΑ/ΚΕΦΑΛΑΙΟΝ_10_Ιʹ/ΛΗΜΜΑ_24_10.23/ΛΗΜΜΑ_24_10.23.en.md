# Matthew 10 (Ιʹ) — Lemma 24 · Matt. 10:23

> **But when they persecute you in this city, flee into the other. Amen I say to you, you shall not have finished the cities of Israel until the Son of man comes.**[^1] (Matt. 10:23)

The things said above, on the one hand, *were* fearful — such as the "They shall deliver you up" and the "You shall be hated" — *and* were concerning the things about to happen after the Ascension; but the thing now said, on the other hand, *is* concerning the things before the cross. For "you shall not finish the cities of Israel" *while* being persecuted, and[^2] immediately I shall come to you. And he commands *them* to flee when persecuted; for to cast oneself openly into danger, and to become a cause of condemnation to the murderers, and to bring loss upon those about to be benefited through the preaching, is devilish.[^3] The "until the Son of man comes," then, do not understand concerning the second coming, but concerning the being-together and consolation before the cross.[^4] For having been sent and having preached, they returned again to Christ, and were together with him.

[^1]: Greek *οὐ μὴ τελέσητε τὰς πόλεις τοῦ Ἰσραήλ*: *τελέω* here = "finish, complete, get through *to the end of*"; the sense is "you will not have gone through / completed *the circuit of* the cities of Israel." Rendered "you shall not have finished the cities of Israel" to keep the plain verb; the exposition confirms the meaning ("you shall not finish the cities of Israel while being persecuted, and immediately I shall come to you").
[^2]: The OCR reads «ναὶ» ("yes / indeed"); in context this is almost certainly «καὶ» ("and"), the reading followed here. If «ναὶ» is retained it would read "yes, immediately I shall come to you," an emphatic affirmation — the sense is not materially altered.
[^3]: Greek *διαβολικόν ἐστι*: "is devilish / of the devil" (*διάβολος*). Two OCR corruptions are corrected in this sentence: «τοῖς οΟ νευταῖς» → «τοῖς φονευταῖς» ("to the murderers"), and «ζομιοῦν» → «ζημιοῦν» ("to cause loss / harm to"). *ζημιόω* = "to inflict loss/damage upon," here upon those who would have profited from the preaching.
[^4]: Greek *περὶ τῆς πρὸ τοῦ σταυροῦ συνουσίας καὶ παραμυθίας*: *συνουσία* = "being-together, company, presence, association"; *παραμυθία* = "consolation, encouragement." The point turns on reading "until the Son of man comes" not eschatologically (the Second Coming) but of Christ's rejoining the disciples during his earthly ministry. The OCR «μὴ παρὶ» is securely «μὴ περὶ» ("do not *understand* concerning").
