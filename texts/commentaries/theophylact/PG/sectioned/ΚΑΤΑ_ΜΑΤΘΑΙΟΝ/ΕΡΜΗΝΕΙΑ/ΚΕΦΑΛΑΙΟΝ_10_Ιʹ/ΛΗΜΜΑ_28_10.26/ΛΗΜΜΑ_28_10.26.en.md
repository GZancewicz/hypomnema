# Matthew 10 (Ιʹ) — Lemma 28 · Matt. 10:26

> **fear them; for there is nothing covered that shall not be uncovered, and hidden that shall not be known.**[^1]

Take comfort, he says, from the things *that concern* me. For if they called me the ruler of the demons, what *is so* terrible if they slander you, *who are* my own? For he called them household members — but not servants — showing his intimacy toward them.[^2] But take courage. For the truth shall not be hidden away, but time will show both your virtue and the wickedness of those who slander *you*; for there is nothing hidden that shall not be known — so that even if they slander *you*, yet afterward they will come to know you.

[^1]: Greek «φοβηθῆτε αὐτούς· οὐδὲν γάρ ἐστι κεκαλυμμένον ὃ οὐκ ἀποκαλυφθήσεται, καὶ κρυπτὸν ὃ οὐ γνωσθήσεται» — completing Matt. 10:25b–26, the imperative *φοβηθῆτε* joining the negative *μὴ οὖν* left hanging in the previous lemma ("*do* not, therefore, fear them"). *κεκαλυμμένον … ἀποκαλυφθήσεται* keeps a cover/uncover word-play ("covered … uncovered"); rendered so to preserve it, rather than "revealed."
[^2]: Greek *οἰκειότητα* — "closeness, kinship, belonging, familiarity"; here the intimacy of members of one's own house. Note the play on *οἰκιακούς* ("household members," 10:25) and *οἰκείους* / *οἰκειότητα* ("my own" / "intimacy") in the comment, all built on *οἶκος* ("house").
