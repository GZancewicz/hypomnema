# Matthew 10 (Ιʹ) — Lemma 34 · Matt. 10:32

> **Everyone therefore whoever shall confess in me[^1] before men, I also will confess in him before my Father who is in *the* heavens;[^2] …**[^3] (Matt. 10:32)

This fragment carries only the scripture lemma; Theophylact's exposition appears with the paired verse in the following fragment (Lemma 35, on 10:33), where he turns precisely on the phrase "confess **in** me" (rather than "confess me").

[^1]: Greek *ὁμολογήσει ἐν ἐμοὶ* — "shall confess **in** me," rendered woodenly rather than as the smoother "confess me," because Theophylact's comment below (Lemma 35) hinges on the preposition *ἐν* ("in"): to confess "in me" is to confess "in my power."
[^2]: Greek *ἐν οὐρανοῖς* is anarthrous (no article); the supplied "*the*" is italicized. "Heavens" is plural, as in the Greek.
[^3]: The lemma breaks off after the colon; it continues with the balancing clause about denial in the next fragment (Lemma 35, 10:33).
