# Matthew 10 (Ιʹ) — Lemma 10 · Matt. 10:9

> **Do not acquire gold, nor silver, nor copper for your belts, nor… (Matt. 10:9)**[^1]

[^1]: Greek «Μὴ κτήσησθε χρυσὸν, μηδὲ ἄργυρον, μηδὲ χαλκὸν εἰς τὰς ζώνας ὑμῶν, μὴ…» — *κτήσησθε* = "acquire, get, possess *for yourselves*." *χαλκὸν* = "copper/bronze," here coined money of that metal. *εἰς τὰς ζώνας ὑμῶν* = "for your belts/girdles" (the girdle serving as a purse). The quote breaks off at *μὴ* ("nor").
