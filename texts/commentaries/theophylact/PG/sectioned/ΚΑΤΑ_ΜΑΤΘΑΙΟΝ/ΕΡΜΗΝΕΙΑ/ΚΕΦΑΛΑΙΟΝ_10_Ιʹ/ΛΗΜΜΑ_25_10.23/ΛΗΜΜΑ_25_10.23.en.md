# Matthew 10 (Ιʹ) — Lemma 25 · Matt. 10:24

> **A disciple is not above the teacher, nor a servant above his lord;**[^1]

*This* lemma carries only the Gospel words; Theophylact's exposition of them opens in the following lemma, which continues the quotation with «It is enough for the disciple…».[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Οὐκ ἔστι μαθητὴς ὑπὲρ τὸν διδάσκαλον, οὐδὲ δοῦλος ὑπὲρ τὸν κύριον αὐτοῦ». The folder-name verse (10.23) is off by one: these are the words of Matt. 10:24. *διδάσκαλος* is rendered "teacher" and *κύριος* "lord" throughout, to keep the teacher/disciple and lord/servant pairs distinct; *δοῦλος* is "servant" (literally "slave/bondman").
[^2]: The sentence is grammatically complete but rhetorically open: Matthew 10:24–25 form one thought ("A disciple is not above the teacher … *it is* enough for the disciple that he become as his teacher"), and Theophylact treats them together. The comment is therefore given under the next lemma.
