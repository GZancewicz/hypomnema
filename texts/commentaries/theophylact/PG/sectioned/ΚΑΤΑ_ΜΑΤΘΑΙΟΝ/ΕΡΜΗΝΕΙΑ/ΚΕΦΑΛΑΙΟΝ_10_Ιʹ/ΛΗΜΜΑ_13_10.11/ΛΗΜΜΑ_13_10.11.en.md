# Matthew 10 (Ιʹ) — Lemma 13 · Matt. 10:11

> **But into whatever city or village you may enter, inquire who in it is worthy, and there remain until you may go out. (Matt. 10:11)**

He commands *them* not to go to all, so that, mingling with the unworthy, they might not be slandered; for if they come to the worthy alone, they will assuredly also be sustained.[^1] But he commands *them* to remain, and not to exchange house for house, so that they might not be blamed as gluttons, and might not seem to insult those who first received *them*.

[^1]: Greek *διατραφήσονται*, from *διατρέφω* ("to nourish thoroughly, maintain, keep fed") — i.e. the worthy will provide their upkeep, picking up the theme of «Ἄξιος ὁ ἐργάτης τῆς τροφῆς» ("the workman is worthy of his food") from the preceding lemma.
