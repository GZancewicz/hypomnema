# Matthew 10 (Ιʹ) — Lemma 2 · Matt. 10:1

*This lemma carries no scripture quotation of its own; it continues the exposition of Matt. 10:1 begun in the previous lemma.*[^1]

But he gives to them the *working of* miracles, so that, astounding *the hearers* through the miracles, they might have *their* hearers compliant toward the teaching.

[^1]: The file opens with an unrecoverable block of OCR garble — the Latin parallel column of Migne intruding upon the Greek — ending in a stray Greek fragment «…τες·» (the tail of a participle). Within this lost stretch stood the *μέν*-clause answering the "*δὲ*" of «δίδωσι δὲ αὐτοῖς» ("but he gives to them"); its content cannot be recovered and is not reconstructed here. Only the concluding sentence «δίδωσι δὲ αὐτοῖς τὸ θαυματουργεῖν, ὡς ἂν διὰ τῶν θαυμάτων καταπλήσσοντες πρὸς τὴν διδασκαλίαν πειθηνίους ἔχωσι τοὺς ἀκροατάς» is legible. *καταπλήσσοντες* = "striking with amazement, astounding" (object supplied: "the hearers"); *πειθηνίους* = "obedient, compliant, tractable."
