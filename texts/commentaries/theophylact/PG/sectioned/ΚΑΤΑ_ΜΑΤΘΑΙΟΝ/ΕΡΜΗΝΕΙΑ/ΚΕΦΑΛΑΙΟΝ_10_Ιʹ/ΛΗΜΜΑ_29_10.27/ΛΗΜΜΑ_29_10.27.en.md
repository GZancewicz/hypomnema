# Matthew 10 (Ιʹ) — Lemma 29 · Matt. 10:27

> **What I tell you in the darkness, speak in the light; and what you hear in the ear, proclaim upon the housetops.**[^1]

The things which, he says, I told to you alone, and in one place — for this is *the meaning of* "in the ear" and "in the darkness" — teach *these* with boldness and with loud voice, so that all may hear you. But since dangers also follow upon boldness, he adds:[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Ὃ λέγω ὑμῖν ἐν τῇ σκοτίᾳ, εἴπατε ἐν τῷ φωτί· καὶ ὃ εἰς τὸ οὖς ἀκούετε, κηρύξατε ἐπὶ τῶν δωμάτων» — Matt. 10:27. *τὰ δώματα* are flat house-roofs, the raised public place from which a thing is broadcast; "housetops" is kept. *παῤῥησία* (in the comment) is the "boldness / freedom / openness of speech" answering to speaking "in the light."
[^2]: The comment breaks off at *ἐπάγει* ("he adds / brings on *next*"); what he adds is the next lemma, «And do not fear *those* who kill the body…» (Matt. 10:28).
