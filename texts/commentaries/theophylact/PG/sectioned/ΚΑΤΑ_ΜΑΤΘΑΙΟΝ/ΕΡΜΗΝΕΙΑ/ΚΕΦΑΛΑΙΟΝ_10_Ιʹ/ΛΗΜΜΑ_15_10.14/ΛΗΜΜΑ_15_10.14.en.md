# Matthew 10 (Ιʹ) — Lemma 15 · Matt. 10:14

> **And whoever may not receive you, nor hear your words, going out of that house or of that city, shake off the dust of your feet. Verily I say to you—**[^1]

[^1]: The sentence breaks off mid-clause at «Ἀμὴν λέγω ὑμῖν» and is completed in the following lemma (Matt. 10:15). Several minor OCR corruptions in the source are securely recoverable: «δς» → *ὃς*, «Ἀκείνης» → *ἐκείνης*, «ἀκτινάξατε» → *ἐκτινάξατε* ("shake off").
