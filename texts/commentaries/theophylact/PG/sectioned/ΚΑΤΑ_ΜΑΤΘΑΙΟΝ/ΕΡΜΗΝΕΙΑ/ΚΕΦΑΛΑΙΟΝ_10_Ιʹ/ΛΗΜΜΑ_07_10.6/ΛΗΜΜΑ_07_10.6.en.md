# Matthew 10 (Ιʹ) — Lemma 7 · Matt. 10:6

> **but go rather to the lost sheep of *the* house of Israel.**[^1]

*This lemma carries only a continuation of the Lord's charge; it has no exposition of its own.* The words here complete the command begun in the previous lemma («Into a road of Gentiles do not go away, and into a city of Samaritans do not enter…»), and Theophylact's exposition of the whole sending is given under the next lemma (on «But as you go, preach…»), where he takes up all of Matt. 10:5–7 together.[^2]

[^1]: Greek «πορεύεσθε δὲ μᾶλλον πρὸς τὰ πρόβατα τὰ ἀπολωλότα οἴκου Ἰσραήλ» — the OCR reads *ἀπολωλάτα* for the perfect participle **ἀπολωλότα** ("lost, having perished") and *οκου* for **οἴκου** ("of *the* house"); both reconstructions are secure. The particle *δὲ* is rendered "but"; *μᾶλλον* "rather." The genitive *οἴκου* is anarthrous, so the supplied article "*the*" is italicized.
[^2]: The scripture citation «...» here breaks off from a sentence begun in the prior lemma and is itself completed and expounded in the following lemma; the commentary is placed there. See Lemma 8.
