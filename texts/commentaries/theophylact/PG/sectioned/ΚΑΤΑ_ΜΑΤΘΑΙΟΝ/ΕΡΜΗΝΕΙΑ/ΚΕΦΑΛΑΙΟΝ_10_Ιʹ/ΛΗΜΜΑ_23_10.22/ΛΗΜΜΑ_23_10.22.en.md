# Matthew 10 (Ιʹ) — Lemma 23 · Matt. 10:22

> **…*and* you shall be hated by all for my name's sake; but he who has endured to the end, this one shall be saved.**[^1] (Matt. 10:22)

The "by all" *stands* instead of "by many." For not all hated them, since there were also those who had received the faith.[^2] But he who has endured to the end, and not at the beginning only, that one shall partake of eternal life.

[^1]: This lemma continues the sentence begun at the close of the previous one (Matt. 10:21), which broke off at «Καὶ»; here it resumes «ἔσεσθε μισούμενοι ὑπὸ πάντων…». The initial "and" is thus present in the Greek (the «Καὶ» carried over) and is set in roman.
[^2]: Greek *οἱ παραδεξάμενοι τὴν πίστιν*: "those who had received / accepted the faith" — those from whom, being believers, no hatred came; hence "by all" cannot be strictly universal but means "by many."
