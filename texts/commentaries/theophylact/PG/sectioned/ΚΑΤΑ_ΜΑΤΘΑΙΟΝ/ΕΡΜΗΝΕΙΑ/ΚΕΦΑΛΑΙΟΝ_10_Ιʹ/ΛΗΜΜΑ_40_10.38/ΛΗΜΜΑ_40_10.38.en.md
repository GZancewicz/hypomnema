# Matthew 10 (Ιʹ) — Lemma 40 · Matt. 10:38

> **And whoever does not take his cross and follow after me is not worthy of me. (Matt. 10:38)**

Whoever, he says, will not renounce[^1] the present life, and give himself over to the reproachful death — for such was the cross among the men of old[^2] — is not worthy of me. But since many are crucified as robbers and thieves, he added the words:[^3]

[^1]: Greek *ἀποτάξεται* — "will renounce / bid farewell to / take leave of," a middle-future of *ἀποτάσσομαι*. The verb carries the sense of formally taking leave, as of a departure; rendered "renounce."
[^2]: Greek *τοῦτο γὰρ ὁ σταυρὸς παρὰ τοῖς παλαιοῖς* — literally "for this *was* the cross among the ancients," i.e. crucifixion was, among the men of antiquity, the death of shame (*θάνατον τὸν ἐπονείδιστον*, "the reproachful/disgraceful death"). The parenthesis interrupts the sentence "…is not worthy of me."
[^3]: The lemma breaks off at *προσέθηκε τὸ,* ("he added the…"); the clause added — «and follow after me» — is quoted and glossed in the following lemma (41).
