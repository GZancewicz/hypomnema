# Matthew 10 (Ιʹ) — Lemma 36 · Matt. 10:34

This fragment opens with the closing scripture phrase that completes the previous comment's sentence (Lemma 35, on 10:33) — "and those denying shall hear the *word*":

> **"I know you not."**[^1]

It then begins a new lemma:

> **Do not think that I came to cast peace upon the earth; not[^2] …**[^3] (Matt. 10:34)

[^1]: Greek «Οὐκ οἶδα ὑμᾶς» — "I know you not." This is the quotation toward which the previous fragment (Lemma 35) was building; it is the word the deniers "shall hear."
[^2]: Greek *οὐκ* ("not"), which negates the verb repeated in the next fragment.
[^3]: The lemma breaks off at *οὐκ* ("not …"); it is completed in the next fragment (Lemma 37, 10:35): "**not** I came to cast peace, but a sword."
