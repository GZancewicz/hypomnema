# Matthew 10 (Ιʹ) — Lemma 31 · Matt. 10:29-30

> **Are not two sparrows sold for an assarion?[^1] And one of them shall not fall upon the ground without your Father. And of you also[^2] the hairs of the head are all numbered; therefore do not …**[^3] (Matt. 10:29-30)

[^1]: Greek *ἀσσαρίου*, the genitive of *ἀσσάριον* — a small Roman copper coin (the *as*), the smallest unit of price. Retained as "assarion" rather than converted to any English-Bible equivalent ("farthing," "penny").
[^2]: The Greek *ὑμῶν δὲ* opens with the particle *δὲ* ("and/but"), here continuative; rendered "and" as the sentence runs on into the numbering of the hairs.
[^3]: The reading breaks off at *μὴ οὖν* ("therefore … not"): the negation *μὴ* governs the verb «φοβηθῆτε» ("fear"), which stands at the head of Lemma 33 (10:31), completing "therefore fear not." The clause «τρίχες τῆς κεφαλῆς … ἠριθμημέναι εἰσί» ("the hairs of the head are all numbered") belongs to 10:30.
