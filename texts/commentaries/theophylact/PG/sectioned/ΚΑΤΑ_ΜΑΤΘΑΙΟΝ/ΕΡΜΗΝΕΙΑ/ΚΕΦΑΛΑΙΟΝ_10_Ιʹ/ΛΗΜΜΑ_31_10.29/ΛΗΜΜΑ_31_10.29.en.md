# Matthew 10 (Ιʹ) — Lemma 31 · Matt. 10:29

> **Are not two sparrows sold for an assarion?[^1] And one of them shall not fall upon the ground without your Father. And of you[^2] also the …**[^3] (Matt. 10:29)

This fragment carries only the scripture lemma; Theophylact's exposition of these words appears in the following fragments (his comment falls with Lemma 33, on 10:31).

[^1]: Greek *ἀσσαρίου*, the genitive of *ἀσσάριον* — a small Roman copper coin (the *as*), the smallest unit of price. Retained as "assarion" rather than converted to any English-Bible equivalent ("farthing," "penny").
[^2]: The Greek *ὑμῶν δὲ* opens with the particle *δὲ* ("and/but"), here continuative; rendered "and" as the sentence runs on into the numbering of the hairs.
[^3]: The lemma breaks off mid-clause at *καὶ αἱ* ("and the …"); the sentence is completed in the next fragment (Lemma 32, 10:30), which supplies «τρίχες τῆς κεφαλῆς» ("hairs of the head").
