# Matthew 10 (Ιʹ) — Lemma 6 · Matt. 10:5

> **These twelve Jesus sent forth, having charged them, saying: Into *the* way of *the* Gentiles go not away, and into a city of *the* Samaritans enter not;[^1] (Matt. 10:5)**

[^1]: OCR reads «σἰσέλθητε» for «εἰσέλθητε»; securely recoverable. The quotation breaks off mid-verse. *εἰς ὁδὸν ἐθνῶν* — "into *the* way of *the* Gentiles," i.e. onto a road leading to the Gentiles; the articles "the" are supplied (the Greek is anarthrous).
