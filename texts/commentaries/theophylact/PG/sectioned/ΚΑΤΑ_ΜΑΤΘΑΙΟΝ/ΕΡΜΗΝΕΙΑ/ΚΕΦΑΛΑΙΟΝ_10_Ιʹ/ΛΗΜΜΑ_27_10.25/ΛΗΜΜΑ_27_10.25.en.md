# Matthew 10 (Ιʹ) — Lemma 27 · Matt. 10:25

> **If they called the master of the house Beelzeboul, how much more *will they call* those of his household? *Do* not, therefore,**[^1]

*This* lemma carries only the Gospel words, breaking off at "*Do* not, therefore"; Theophylact's exposition opens in the following lemma, which completes the quotation with «…fear them».[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Εἰ τὸν οἰκοδεσπότην Βεελζεβοὺλ ἀπεκάλεσαν, πόσῳ μᾶλλον τοὺς οἰκιακοὺς αὐτοῦ; Μὴ οὖν» — Matt. 10:25b. The second clause is verbless in the Greek (*πόσῳ μᾶλλον τοὺς οἰκιακοὺς αὐτοῦ*, literally "how much more his household-folk?"); "*will they call*" is supplied from the preceding verb *ἀπεκάλεσαν*. *οἰκιακούς* = "members of the household, domestics," here rendered "those of his household." On the spelling **Beelzeboul**: Theophylact writes *Βεελζεβούλ* (‑λ), not the "Beelzebub" (‑β) familiar from many English Bibles; his text is followed.
[^2]: The imperative *μὴ οὖν* ("*do* not therefore") governs a verb that appears only in the next lemma, «φοβηθῆτε αὐτούς» ("fear them"); the sentence and its comment are taken up there.
