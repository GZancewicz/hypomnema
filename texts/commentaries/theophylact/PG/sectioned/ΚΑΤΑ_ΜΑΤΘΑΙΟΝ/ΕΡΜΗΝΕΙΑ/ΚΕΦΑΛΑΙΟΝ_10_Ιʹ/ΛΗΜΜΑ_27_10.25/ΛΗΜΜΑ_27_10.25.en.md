# Matthew 10 (Ιʹ) — Lemma 27 · Matt. 10:25

> **If they called the master of the house Beelzeboul, how much more *will they call* those of his household? Do not, therefore,**[^1]

[^1]: Greek «Εἰ τὸν οἰκοδεσπότην Βεελζεβοὺλ ἀπεκάλεσαν, πόσῳ μᾶλλον τοὺς οἰκιακοὺς αὐτοῦ; Μὴ οὖν» — Matt. 10:25b. The second clause is verbless in the Greek (*πόσῳ μᾶλλον τοὺς οἰκιακοὺς αὐτοῦ*, literally "how much more his household-folk?"); "*will they call*" is supplied from the preceding verb *ἀπεκάλεσαν*. *οἰκιακούς* = "members of the household, domestics," here rendered "those of his household." The imperative «Μὴ οὖν» ("Do not, therefore") breaks off before its verb, which appears in the next lemma («φοβηθῆτε αὐτούς», "fear them"). On the spelling **Beelzeboul**: Theophylact writes *Βεελζεβούλ* (‑λ), not the "Beelzebub" (‑β) familiar from many English Bibles; his text is followed.
