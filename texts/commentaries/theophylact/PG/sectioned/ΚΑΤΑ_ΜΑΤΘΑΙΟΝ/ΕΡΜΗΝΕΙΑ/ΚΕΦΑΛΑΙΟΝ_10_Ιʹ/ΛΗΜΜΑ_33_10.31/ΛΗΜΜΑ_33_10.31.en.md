# Matthew 10 (Ιʹ) — Lemma 33 · Matt. 10:31

> **… fear;[^1] by much do you differ from[^2] sparrows, you.** (Matt. 10:31)

In order that they might not be troubled as *those* forsaken, he says: If not even a sparrow shall be seized[^3] while I am unaware,[^4] how shall I forsake you, whom I love? And displaying *his* precise[^5] knowledge and providence, he says that our[^6] hairs are numbered to him. But do not think that it is by God's working[^7] that the sparrows are ensnared, but that not even the catching of them[^8] is unknown to him.

[^1]: This fragment opens with the verb «φοβηθῆτε» ("fear"), whose governing negation *μὴ* ("not") stood at the end of the previous fragment (Lemma 32, 10:30). The whole clause is therefore "therefore fear **not**"; taken with this fragment alone the imperative is unnegated, so the negation is understood from the preceding words.
[^2]: Greek *πολλῷ στρουθίων διαφέρετε ὑμεῖς* — literally "by much you differ from sparrows, you," with an emphatic repeated subject *ὑμεῖς* ("you") rendered at the close. The verb *διαφέρω* + genitive means both "to differ from" and "to be superior to / of more value than"; "differ from … by much" preserves the former while carrying the sense of surpassing. The common English wording "ye are of more value than many sparrows" is not imported.
[^3]: Greek *κρατηθήσεται*, future passive of *κρατέω* ("seize, hold fast, catch"); here of a bird being caught.
[^4]: Greek *ἀγνοοῦντος ἐμοῦ*, a genitive absolute — literally "me being ignorant," i.e. "without my knowledge."
[^5]: OCR *ἀκρισῆ*; securely = **ἀκριβῆ** ("precise, exact"). The reconstruction is certain and low-stakes, noted for consistency with the other flagged corruptions.
[^6]: Theophylact writes *τὰς τρίχας ἡμῶν* ("**our** hairs"), where the Gospel text has *ὑμῶν* ("your"); translated as it stands on the page, per the rule of following his text.
[^7]: Greek *τοῦ Θεοῦ ἐνεργοῦντος*, genitive absolute — "God working / being at work," i.e. that God is the active cause of the sparrows' being trapped. The point is that God does not *cause* the snaring; he only is not *ignorant* of it.
[^8]: Greek *ἡ ἐκείνων ἄγρα* — *ἄγρα* is "the catching, the hunt, the taking" (also, concretely, "the prey"); here "their capture / the catching of them."
