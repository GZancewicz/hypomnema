# Matthew 10 (Ιʹ) — Lemma 35 · Matt. 10:33

> **… but whoever shall deny me before men, I also will deny him before my Father who is in *the* heavens.**[^1] (Matt. 10:33)

He urges *them* toward witness;[^2] for he is not content with faith according to the soul alone, but wills also that[^3] through the mouth. And he did not say, "Whoever shall confess *me*," but "in me" — that is, in my power; for *it is* by the help from above[^4] *that the one who confesses confesses*. But[^5] of the one denying he did not say "in me," but "me," showing that, not having the help from above, he denies. Everyone therefore who confesses that Christ is God will find Christ confessing concerning him to the Father, that he is a genuine servant. And those denying shall hear the *word*:[^6]

[^1]: This clause balances the confession-clause of the previous fragment (Lemma 34, 10:32); *ἐν οὐρανοῖς* ("in *the* heavens") is again anarthrous, the supplied "*the*" italicized.
[^2]: Greek *εἰς μαρτυρίαν προτρέπει* (OCR *μαρτύρισν*). *μαρτυρία* is "witness / testimony," and in this context "witness *unto martyrdom*" — the confession before men that may cost one's life. Rendered "witness" to keep the word's plain sense; the martyr-overtone is native to the term.
[^3]: Greek *ἀλλὰ θέλει καὶ τὴν διὰ στόματος* — "but wills also the *[faith/confession]* through the mouth." The feminine article *τὴν* points back to *πίστει* ("faith") / confession; the noun is left implicit in the Greek and supplied only by that article, so it is left unsupplied here to match.
[^4]: The Greek here is OCR-corrupt and elliptical: «τῇ γὰρ ἄνω … δὲ τοῦ ἀρνουμένου οὐκ εἶπεν, ἐν ἐμοί, ἀλλ᾽ ἐμέ». A phrase has dropped out. The sense, secure from the parallel structure and from Theophylact's usual wording, is: to confess "in me" is to confess *by the help / grace from above* (*ἡ ἄνωθεν βοήθεια*), whereas of the denier he says only "me," without "in," because the denier acts bereft of that help. The bracketed completion "*that the one who confesses confesses*" reconstructs the lost clause; translated conservatively, with the gap flagged rather than invented.
[^5]: Greek *δὲ* ("but/and"), a present particle, taken adversatively against the confessor just described; rendered "But."
[^6]: The lemma-comment breaks off at *τὸ* ("the …"), introducing a quotation; the quoted words «Οὐκ οἶδα ὑμᾶς» ("I know you not") open the next fragment (Lemma 36), completing the sentence: "shall hear the *word*: 'I know you not.'"
