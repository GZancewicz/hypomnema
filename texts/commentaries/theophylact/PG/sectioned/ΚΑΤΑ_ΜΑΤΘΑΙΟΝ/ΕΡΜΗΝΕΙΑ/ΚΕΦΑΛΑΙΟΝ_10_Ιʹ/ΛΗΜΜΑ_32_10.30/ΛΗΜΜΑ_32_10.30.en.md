# Matthew 10 (Ιʹ) — Lemma 32 · Matt. 10:30

> **… hairs of the head are all numbered;[^1] therefore[^2] *do* not …**[^3] (Matt. 10:30)

This fragment carries only the scripture lemma; Theophylact's exposition of these words appears in the following fragment (Lemma 33, on 10:31).

[^1]: This clause completes the sentence begun in the previous fragment (Lemma 31, 10:29), which broke off at «καὶ αἱ» ("and the …"): "And of you also the **hairs of the head are all numbered**."
[^2]: Greek *οὖν* ("therefore"), a present particle, rendered in roman.
[^3]: The lemma breaks off at *μὴ οὖν* — the negation *μὴ* ("not") governs a verb not yet supplied; the fragment ends before it. The verb «φοβηθῆτε» ("fear") stands at the head of the next fragment (Lemma 33, 10:31), completing "therefore fear not." The supplied auxiliary "*do*" is italicized.
