# Matthew 23 (ΚΓʹ) — Lemma 13 · Matt. 23:14

> **But woe to you, Scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, because you devour the houses of the widows, and for a pretext praying at length,[^1] therefore you shall receive greater judgment.** (Matt. 23:14)[^2]

He calls these *men* "hypocrites," as *those who* profess reverence and do nothing worthy of the profession, but under pretext of praying at length — that is, for a long time — devour the widows' possessions. For in reality they were mockers, deriding the simpler *folk* and draining them of their marrow.[^3] Wherefore also, "you shall receive greater judgment," because you devour all the possessions of the widows, to whom you ought rather to supply *aid* and to set right their poverty. And in another way too, the condemnation shall be greater, because through the outward show of a good work — of praying — they work out something wicked, the devouring of the widows' *goods*; for most worthy of condemnation is he who, through the show of *the* good, entices toward the evil.

[^1]: Greek *προφάσει μακρὰ προσευχόμενοι* — literally "for a pretext praying long/at length"; *μακρά* is here adverbial ("at length, for a long time"), as Theophylact himself explains below (*ἐπὶ πολὺν καιρόν*, "for a long time"). The supplied "*a*" before "pretext" is italicized.
[^2]: In the modern verse numbering this woe (devouring widows' houses) is Matt. 23:14, and the woe on shutting up the kingdom is 23:13; Theophylact's text (and this PG edition) presents them in the reverse order — the widows' woe first (this lemma), then the kingdom woe (next lemma). The folder metadata's "23.13" is corrected here to 23:14 by the actual content. Greek of the lemma: *«Οὐαὶ δὲ ὑμῖν, Γραμματεῖς καὶ Φαρισαῖοι ὑποκριταί, ὅτι κατεσθίετε τὰς οἰκίας τῶν χηρῶν…»*
[^3]: Greek *ἐκμυελίζοντες αὐτούς* — literally "marrow-sucking them," i.e. draining them dry, fleecing them of their substance; the vivid metaphor is kept.
[^4]: The scripture text of the next woe begins here (*Οὐαὶ ὑμῖν,* "Woe to you,") and is completed in the following lemma with «Γραμματεῖς καὶ Φαρισαῖοι ὑποκριταί, ὅτι κλείετε τὴν βασιλείαν…» (Matt. 23:13).
