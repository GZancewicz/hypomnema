# Matthew 23 (ΚΓʹ) — Lemma 31 · Matt. 23:35

> **…righteous,[^1] being shed upon the earth, from the blood of Abel the righteous, until the blood of Zacharias son of Barachias, whom ye slew between the temple and the altar. Amen I say to you, that there shall come[^2] (Matt. 23:35)**

This lemma carries only the continuation of the Gospel citation begun in the previous lemma («That upon you may come all the blood…»); Theophylact adds no exposition of his own here. His comment on these words follows in Lemma 32.

[^1]: The opening word «δίκαιον» ("righteous," neuter) agrees with «αἷμα» ("blood") from the previous lemma — «πᾶν αἷμα δίκαιον», "all righteous blood." The OCR reads «τοῦ Ἀικαίου» a few words later, securely = «τοῦ δικαίου» ("the righteous," of Abel).
[^2]: The citation breaks off at «ἥξει» ("there shall come"); it is completed in Lemma 32 with «…all these things upon this generation».
