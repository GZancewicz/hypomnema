# Matthew 23 (ΚΓʹ) — Lemma 28 · Matt. 23:33

> **Serpents, offspring of vipers, how shall you flee from the judgment of Gehenna? (Matt. 23:33)**[^1]

Not as commanding them, or as forcing them on toward murdering him, does he say the *words*, "Fill up the measure of your fathers," but he says something of this kind: Since you are serpents and offspring of such fathers, and have been flung headlong[^2] into so great a wickedness as to be incurable, in shorter *time* make haste to surpass your fathers; and this will be, if you murder me; for you will attain the very summit of wickedness, if you fill up the blood-guilt[^3] that is lacking to your fathers. How then, being such *men*, shall you flee the punishment?

[^1]: Greek «ὄφεις, γεννήματα ἐχιδνῶν, πῶς φύγητε ἀπὸ τῆς κρίσεως τῆς γεέννης;» *γεννήματα ἐχιδνῶν*, "offspring/brood of vipers." *γέεννα* (Gehenna) is left untranslated as the proper name of the place of judgment.
[^2]: Greek *ἐξετραχηλίσθητε* (from *ἐκτραχηλίζω*, literally "to break the neck, throw headlong"); the passive here means "you have been flung/plunged headlong."
[^3]: Greek *μιαιφονίαν* (from *μιαιφονία*, "murder-defilement, blood-guilt, foul slaughter"); rendered "blood-guilt." The verb *ἀναπληρώσητε* ("fill up, make complete") echoes *πληρώσατε* of the lemma above.
