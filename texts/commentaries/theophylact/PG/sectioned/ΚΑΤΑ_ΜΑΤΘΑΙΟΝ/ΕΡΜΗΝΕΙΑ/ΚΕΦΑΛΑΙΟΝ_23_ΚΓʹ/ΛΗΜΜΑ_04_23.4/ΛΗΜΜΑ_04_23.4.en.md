# Matthew 23 (ΚΓʹ) — Lemma 4 · Matt. 23:4

> **For they bind together burdens heavy and hard to bear, and lay *them* upon the shoulders of men, but with their finger they will not move them;**[^1] (Matt. 23:4)

This lemma carries only the Gospel words; Theophylact's exposition of these "heavy burdens" is given together with the next lemma, opening Lemma 5.

[^1]: Greek «Δεσμεύουσι γὰρ φορτία βαρέα καὶ δυσβάστακτα, καὶ ἐπιτιθέασιν ἐπὶ τοὺς ὥμους τῶν ἀνθρώπων, τῷ δὲ δακτύλῳ αὐτῶν οὐ θέλουσι κινῆσαι αὐτά». (OCR "τῷ Ἀὲ δακτύλῳ" = «τῷ δὲ δακτύλῳ».) *δεσμεύουσι* = "they bind together, tie up in bundles"; *δυσβάστακτα* = "hard to bear / hard to carry." The particle *γὰρ* ("for") ties this back to the preceding "they say and do not do." The object "*them*" (the burdens) is supplied with *ἐπιτιθέασιν*, which has none expressed.
