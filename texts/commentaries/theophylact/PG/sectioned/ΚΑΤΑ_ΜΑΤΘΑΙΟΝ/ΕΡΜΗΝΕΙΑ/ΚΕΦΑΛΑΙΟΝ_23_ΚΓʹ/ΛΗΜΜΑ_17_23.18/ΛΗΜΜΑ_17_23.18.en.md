# Matthew 23 (ΚΓʹ) — Lemma 17 · Matt. 23:18

> **…swears by the altar, it is nothing; but whoever swears by the gift *that is* upon it, he is bound.** (Matt. 23:18)[^1]

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text, without exposition. It continues the sentence begun in the previous lemma ("…And whoever…") and is itself continued in the next lemma; Theophylact's comment on the whole passage on oaths comes at lemma 19.*[^2]

[^1]: The fragment opens mid-sentence: *«…ὀμόσῃ ἐν τῷ θυσιαστηρίῳ, οὐδέν ἐστιν· ὃς δ᾽ ἂν ὀμόσῃ ἐν τῷ δώρῳ τῷ ἐπάνω αὐτοῦ ὀφείλει»*. On *ὀφείλει* ("he is bound") see the previous lemma. "The gift *that is* upon it" is the offering laid on the altar.
[^2]: See lemma 16, note 3: the exposition of the passage on swearing (vv. 16–22) is gathered at lemma 19 (Matt. 23:22).
