# Matthew 23 (ΚΓʹ) — Lemma 14 · Matt. 23:13

> **[Woe to you,][^1] Scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, because you shut the kingdom of the heavens before men; for you yourselves do not enter, neither do you allow those entering to enter.** (Matt. 23:13)

Not only, he says, are you unbelieving and have your life corrupted, but you also teach others not to believe in me, and through your life and through *your* example you corrupt *them*. For the people are wont to be made like their rulers, and the more so if it sees these *men* inclining toward the evil. Let every wicked teacher and ruler, therefore, see what he gains — *namely* the "woe" — since through his own life he does not permit some[^2] to advance toward the good.

[^1]: The scripture words *Οὐαὶ ὑμῖν,* ("Woe to you,") stand at the very end of the previous lemma; this lemma opens with the continuation *Γραμματεῖς καὶ Φαρισαῖοι ὑποκριταί…* The bracketed opening is supplied here to make the woe read whole.
[^2]: The final clause is OCR-corrupt in the source: *«…ὅτι τὸ οὐαὶ, ὡς διὰ τοῦ οἰκείου βίου μὴ ἐν τινας προκόπτειν εἰς τὸ ἀγαθόν»*, where *ἐν τινας* is securely to be read *ἐῶν τινας* ("permitting some/any"), giving *μὴ ἐῶν τινας προκόπτειν* ("not permitting any to advance"). The sense is that the wicked teacher's "gain" is the woe itself, incurred because his own manner of life keeps others from progressing toward the good. Translated on that reconstruction.
