# Matthew 23 (ΚΓʹ) — Lemma 32 · Matt. 23:36

> **…all these things upon this generation. (Matt. 23:36)**

Upon the Jews who then existed, he says, will come even blood unjustly shed;[^1] for they shall be punished worse than their fathers — they who were not brought to their senses even after so many examples; just as Lamech also was punished more than Cain, although he had not slain a brother, because he had not been brought to his senses by the example concerning Cain.[^2] "Therefore," he says, "there shall come upon you every blood, from Abel until Zacharias." And opportunely did he make mention of Abel; for as Abel was slain through envy, so also *was* Christ, being envied.

But of which Zacharias does he here make mention? Some, on the one hand, say *of the one* numbered among the twelve prophets; but others, on the other hand, *of* the father of the Forerunner.[^3] For there is an account handed down to us, that there was a certain place in the temple in which the virgins stood. This Zacharias, accordingly, being high priest, even after the Theotokos had given birth to Christ, set her in the place of the virgins; but the Jews, being indignant at this, killed him, as *one who* had ranked among the virgins a woman who had given birth. And *it is* nothing wonderful, if the father of the Forerunner also had a father Barachias by name, just as the one among the twelve prophets is also called son of Barachias; for *it is* likely that, just as the names of these *men* coincided, so also *did* those of their fathers.

[^1]: The OCR reads «κᾶν αἷμα», taken as «κἂν αἷμα» ("even blood") — that even innocent blood shed long ago will be requited on them. Given the parallel «πᾶν αἷμα» ("all/every blood") just below, «πᾶν» may be what stood here. Greek: «φησὶν ἥξειν κἂν αἷμα ἀδίκως ἐκχυθέν».
[^2]: Cf. Gen. 4:23–24 (Lamech's boast) and 4:8 (Cain and Abel). The point: Lamech, though he had Cain's punishment before him as a warning, was chastised the more for not heeding it.
[^3]: Theophylact reports two identifications of the "Zacharias son of Barachias" of Matt. 23:35: (a) Zechariah son of Berechiah, one of the twelve minor prophets (cf. Zech. 1:1); and (b) Zacharias father of John the Baptist, whose murder in the temple belongs to an apocryphal tradition (cf. Protevangelium of James 23–24). Greek: «Ζαχαρίου υἱοῦ Βαραχίου».
