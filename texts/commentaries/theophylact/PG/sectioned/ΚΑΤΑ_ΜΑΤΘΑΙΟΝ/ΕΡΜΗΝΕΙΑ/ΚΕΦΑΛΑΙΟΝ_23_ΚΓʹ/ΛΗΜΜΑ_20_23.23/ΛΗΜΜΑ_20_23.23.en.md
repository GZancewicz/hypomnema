# Matthew 23 (ΚΓʹ) — Lemma 20 · Matt. 23:23

> **…Scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, because you tithe the mint and the dill and the cummin, and you have left the weightier *matters* of the law: judgment and mercy and faith. These it was necessary to do, and not to leave those *undone*. (Matt. 23:23)**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture citation; Theophylact's exposition of it follows in the next lemma, where he takes up judgment, mercy, and faith together with the "blind guides."*[^2]

[^1]: The citation continues the "Woe to you" begun at the end of the previous lemma (the words «Οὐαὶ ὑμῖν» there); hence the supplied ellipsis. Greek: *τὸ ἡδύοσμον καὶ τὸ ἄνηθον καὶ τὸ κύμινον* — "mint, dill, and cummin." *ἄνηθον* is properly "dill" (KJV "anise"); rendered from Theophylact's Greek as "dill." *τὰ βαρύτερα τοῦ νόμου* = "the weightier *matters* of the law"; the noun is supplied.
[^2]: The lemma folder contains no comment of its own — only the Gospel text; the exposition is given under Lemma 21 (Matt. 23:24), which discusses *κρίσις, ἔλεος, πίστις* directly.
