# Matthew 23 (ΚΓʹ) — Lemma 6 · Matt. 23:6

> **And they love the first place at table at the feasts, and the first seats in the synagogues, and the**[^1] (Matt. 23:6)

This lemma carries only the Gospel words, and it breaks off mid-sentence at «καὶ τοὺς» ("and the"). The clause is completed in the next lemma with «ἀσπασμοὺς ἐν ταῖς ἀγοραῖς» ("the greetings in the marketplaces"), where Theophylact's exposition follows.[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Φιλοῦσι δὲ τὴν πρωτοκλισίαν ἐν τοῖς δείπνοις, καὶ τὰς πρωτοκαθεδρίας ἐν ταῖς συναγωγαῖς, καὶ τοὺς» (OCR "Ριλοῦσι" = «Φιλοῦσι»). *πρωτοκλισία* = "the first place at table / the chief reclining-couch" (literally "first reclining"); *πρωτοκαθεδρία* = "the first seat / chief seat." The particle *δὲ* is rendered "and." The folder-name verse (23.5) is corrected to 23:6, which these words expound.
[^2]: The following lemma continues: «ἀσπασμοὺς ἐν ταῖς ἀγοραῖς, καὶ καλεῖσθαι ὑπὸ τῶν ἀνθρώπων Ῥαββί, Ῥαββί» ("the greetings in the marketplaces, and to be called by men 'Rabbi, Rabbi'") — reaching into Matt. 23:7.
