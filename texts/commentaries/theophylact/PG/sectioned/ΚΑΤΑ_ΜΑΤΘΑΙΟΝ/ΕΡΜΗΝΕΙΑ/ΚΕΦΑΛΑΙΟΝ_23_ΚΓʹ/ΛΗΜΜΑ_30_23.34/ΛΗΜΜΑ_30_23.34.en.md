# Matthew 23 (ΚΓʹ) — Lemma 30 · Matt. 23:35

> **So that upon you may come all *the* blood—**[^1]

This lemma carries only the opening words of the scripture text; Theophylact supplies no exposition here. His comment on this verse is joined to the following lemmata (23:35–36), where he expounds "from *the* blood of Abel … to the blood of Zacharias" and which Zacharias is meant.

[^1]: Greek «Ὅπως ἔλθῃ ἐφ᾽ ὑμᾶς πᾶν αἷμα». The folder name gives the draws-from verse as 23:34, but these words are the opening of Matt. 23:35; the header and reference are corrected accordingly. *ὅπως* + subjunctive *ἔλθῃ*, "so that … may come"; the lemma breaks off before «δίκαιον, ἐκχυνόμενον…» ("righteous, being poured out…"), which resumes in the next lemma. The supplied "*the*" renders the anarthrous *πᾶν αἷμα* into idiomatic English.
