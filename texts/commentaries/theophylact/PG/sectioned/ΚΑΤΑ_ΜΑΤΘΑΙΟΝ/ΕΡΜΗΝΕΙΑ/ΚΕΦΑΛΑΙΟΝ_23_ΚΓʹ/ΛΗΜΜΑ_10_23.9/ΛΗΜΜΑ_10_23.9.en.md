# Matthew 23 (ΚΓʹ) — Lemma 10 · Matt. 23:9

> **…*call* not *anyone* your father upon the earth; for one is your Father, the *one* in the heavens. Neither…[^1] (Matt. 23:9)**

*This lemma carries only the scripture text; Theophylact's exposition is given below at Lemma 12.*[^2]

[^1]: A continuation of the preceding «Καὶ» ("And"). Greek order: *πατέρα μὴ καλέσητε ὑμῶν ἐπὶ τῆς γῆς* — "father do not call *of* you upon the earth"; the object "*anyone / any man*" is supplied, since the Greek has no explicit *τινα*. The OCR «εἶς» → *εἷς* ("one"). The lemma breaks off at «Μηδὲ» ("Neither"), continued in Lemma 11.
[^2]: See Lemma 12 for the combined exposition of Matt. 23:8–12.
