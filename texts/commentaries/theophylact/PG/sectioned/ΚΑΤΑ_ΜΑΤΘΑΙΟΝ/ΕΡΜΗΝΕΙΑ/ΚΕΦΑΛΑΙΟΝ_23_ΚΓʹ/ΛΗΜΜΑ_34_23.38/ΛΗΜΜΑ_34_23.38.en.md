# Matthew 23 (ΚΓʹ) — Lemma 34 · Matt. 23:38–39

> **Your house is left unto you desolate; for I say unto you, ye shall not[^1] (Matt. 23:38–39)**

This lemma carries only the Gospel citation; Theophylact's exposition of these words comes at Lemma 35.

[^1]: The citation breaks off at «οὐ μή με» ("ye shall… not… me"); it is completed in Lemma 35: «…see *me* from now on, until ye shall say, Blessed is he that cometh in the name of the Lord».
