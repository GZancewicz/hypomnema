# Matthew 23 (ΚΓʹ) — Lemma 09 · Matt. 23:8

> **But you, be not called Rabbi; for one is your teacher, the Christ; and you are all brethren. And…[^1] (Matt. 23:8)**

*This lemma carries only the scripture text; Theophylact's exposition of the whole sequence (Matt. 23:8–12) is given below at Lemma 12.*[^2]

[^1]: The OCR has minor slips, all secure: «ἐστικ» → *ἐστιν* ("is"), «ψμῶν» → *ὑμῶν* ("your"). The lemma breaks off at «Καὶ» ("And"), whose sentence is continued in the next lemma («…father do not call…»).
[^2]: Lemmata 09, 10, and 11 present the successive clauses of Matt. 23:8–11 as bare scripture quotations; Theophylact reserves his comment for Lemma 12, where he expounds the passage as a unit.
