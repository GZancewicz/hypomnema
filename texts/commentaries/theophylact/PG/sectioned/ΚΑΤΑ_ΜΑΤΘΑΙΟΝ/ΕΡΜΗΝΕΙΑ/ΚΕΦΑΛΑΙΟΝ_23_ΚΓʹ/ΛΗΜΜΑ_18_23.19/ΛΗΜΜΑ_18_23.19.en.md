# Matthew 23 (ΚΓʹ) — Lemma 18 · Matt. 23:19–21

> **Fools and blind! For which *is* greater, the gift, or the altar that sanctifies the gift? He therefore who swore by the altar swears by it and by all the things upon it; and he who swore by the temple swears by it and by the *one* dwelling in it; and he *who*…** (Matt. 23:19–21)[^1]

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text, without exposition. The sentence breaks off at "and he *who*…" and is completed in the next lemma ("…who swore by heaven swears by the throne of God…," Matt. 23:22), where Theophylact's exposition of the whole passage on oaths begins.*[^2]

[^1]: The lemma runs continuously through vv. 19–21: *«Μωροὶ καὶ τυφλοί, τί γὰρ μεῖζον, τὸ δῶρον ἢ τὸ θυσιαστήριον τὸ ἁγιάζον τὸ δῶρον; ὁ οὖν ὀμόσας ἐν τῷ θυσιαστηρίῳ ὀμνύει ἐν αὐτῷ καὶ ἐν πᾶσι τοῖς ἐπάνω αὐτοῦ, καὶ ὁ ὀμόσας ἐν τῷ ναῷ ὀμνύει ἐν αὐτῷ καὶ ἐν τῷ κατοικοῦντι αὐτόν, καὶ ὁ…»*. Theophylact varies the tense as the Gospel does: the aorist participle *ὀμόσας* ("he who swore") with the present *ὀμνύει* ("swears") — "the one who has sworn by the altar *thereby* swears by all upon it." Both are kept.
[^2]: See lemma 16, note 3, and lemma 19, which opens *«…ὀμόσας ἐν τῷ οὐρανῷ, ὀμνύει ἐν τῷ θρόνῳ τοῦ Θεοῦ…»* and then turns to the comment, *«Τυφλοὺς αὐτοὺς ὀνομάζει…»* ("He names them blind…").
