# Matthew 23 (ΚΓʹ) — Lemma 2 · Matt. 23:2

> **saying: Upon the seat of Moses sat the Scribes and the Pharisees;**[^1] (Matt. 23:2)

This lemma carries only the Gospel words, continuing the sentence begun in Lemma 1. Theophylact's exposition follows in Lemma 3.

[^1]: Greek «λέγων· Ἐπὶ τῆς Μωσέως καθέδρας ἐκάθισαν οἱ Γραμματεῖς καὶ οἱ Φαρισαῖοι». The verb *ἐκάθισαν* is aorist ("sat, took their seat"), rendered so here; the familiar English "sit" (KJV) flattens it to a present. *καθέδρα* = "seat, chair" (whence the image of the "seat/chair of Moses"). The name is genitive *Μωσέως*, "of Moses" — hence "the seat of Moses."
