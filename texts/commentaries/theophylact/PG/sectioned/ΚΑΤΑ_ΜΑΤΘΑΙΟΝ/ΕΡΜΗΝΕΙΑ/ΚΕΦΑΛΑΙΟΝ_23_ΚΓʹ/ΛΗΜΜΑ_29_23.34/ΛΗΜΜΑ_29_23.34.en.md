# Matthew 23 (ΚΓʹ) — Lemma 29 · Matt. 23:34

> **Therefore, behold, I send to you prophets, and wise men, and scribes; and *some* of them you will kill and crucify, and *some* of them you will scourge in your synagogues, and you will persecute *them* from city to city. (Matt. 23:34)**[^1]

He refutes them, having spoken falsely that "If we were in the days of our fathers, we would not have murdered the prophets." For behold, he says, I send prophets, and wise men. And he says these things concerning the apostles; for the Holy Spirit, having adorned them also with teachings, made them scribes — that is, teachers of the people — and prophets, and filled with all wisdom. And "I send," he said, showing the authoritative *character* of *his* divinity.[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Διὰ τοῦτο ἰδοὺ ἐγὼ ἀποστέλλω πρὸς ὑμᾶς προφήτας, καὶ σοφοὺς, καὶ γραμματεῖς· καὶ ἐξ αὐτῶν ἀποκτενεῖτε καὶ σταυρώσετε, καὶ ἐξ αὐτῶν μαστιγώσετε ἐν ταῖς συναγωγαῖς ὑμῶν, καὶ διώξετε ἀπὸ πόλεως εἰς πόλιν». The partitive *ἐξ αὐτῶν* ("from among them") is rendered "*some* of them," the pronoun supplied.
[^2]: Greek «δεικνὺς τὸ τῆς θεότητος ἐξουσιαστικόν». The OCR reads «ἐξουσιαΣπιΧΟν», securely = *ἐξουσιαστικόν* ("that which pertains to authority, the authoritative *quality/power*"); the substantival neuter *τὸ … ἐξουσιαστικόν* = "the authoritative *character* of *his* divinity/Godhead" — i.e. by saying "I send" (not "am sent") he displays the sovereign authority proper to God.
