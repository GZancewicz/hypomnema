# Matthew 23 (ΚΓʹ) — Lemma 15 · Matt. 23:15

> **Woe to you, Scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, because you go about the sea and the dry *land*, to make one proselyte; and when he has become *one*, you make him a son of Gehenna twofold more than yourselves.** (Matt. 23:15)

Not only, he says, do you corrupt the Jews, but also those who from *among* idolaters have come over to the Jewish religion; for these they used to name "proselytes." For on the one hand you are eager to turn someone toward the Jewish manner of life and *toward* circumcision; but when he has been made a Jew, then he perishes, being corrupted by your wickedness. And "a son of Gehenna"[^1] *is* he who is worthy of it and *who* has a certain kinship toward it, so as to be burned by it,[^2]

[^1]: Greek *υἱὸς γεέννης*, a Hebraism ("son of…" = one belonging to, or destined for). Theophylact glosses it by "kinship" (*οἰκειότης*): the man so corrupted becomes a proper possession of Gehenna, fit fuel for its fire. *Gehenna* (the Valley of Hinnom) is retained rather than rendered "hell," since Theophylact's play on "being burned by it" turns on the image of a burning place.
[^2]: The sentence breaks off here; the exposition does not continue in this lemma, and the following lemma resumes with the next woe (against the blind guides, Matt. 23:16). The trailing "so as to be burned by it" is left as it stands in the source.
