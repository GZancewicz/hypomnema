# Matthew 23 (ΚΓʹ) — Lemma 25 · Matt. 23:30

> **you say: If we were in the days of our fathers, we would not have been partakers with them in the blood of the prophets;[^1] so that—**[^2]

This lemma carries only the scripture text; Theophylact supplies no exposition here. His comment on these words is joined to the exposition of the following lemma (23:31), where he explains that in saying this they "lie openly."

[^1]: Greek «Εἰ ἦμεν ἐν ταῖς ἡμέραις τῶν πατέρων ἡμῶν, οὐκ ἂν ἦμεν κοινωνοὶ αὐτῶν ἐν τῷ αἵματι τῶν προφητῶν». The verb *ἦμεν* is imperfect ("we were"), rendered as an unreal-condition "if we were." *κοινωνοὶ αὐτῶν* is literally "partakers of them"; rendered "partakers with them." The opening *λέγετε* ("you say") continues from the preceding lemma, whose scripture text ends with «καὶ» ("and").
[^2]: The lemma breaks off after «στε», the OCR-truncated tail of **«ὥστε»** ("so that / wherefore"), which opens Matt. 23:31 and is completed in the next lemma with «μαρτυρεῖτε ἑαυτοῖς…».
