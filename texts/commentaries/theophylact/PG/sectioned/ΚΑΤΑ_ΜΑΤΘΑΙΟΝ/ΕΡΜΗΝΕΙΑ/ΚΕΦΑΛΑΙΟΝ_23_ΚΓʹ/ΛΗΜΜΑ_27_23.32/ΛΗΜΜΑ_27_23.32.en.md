# Matthew 23 (ΚΓʹ) — Lemma 27 · Matt. 23:32

> **And you, fill up the measure of your fathers;[^1]**

This lemma carries only the scripture text; Theophylact supplies no exposition here. His comment on these words is joined to the following lemma (23:33), where he explains that in saying "fill up the measure of your fathers" *the Lord* is not commanding or urging them on toward murder.

[^1]: Greek «Καὶ ὑμεῖς πληρώσατε τὸ μέτρον τῶν πατέρων ὑμῶν». *πληρώσατε* is an aorist imperative, "fill up / fill full"; the lemma breaks off, its exposition beginning in the next lemma.
