# Matthew 23 (ΚΓʹ) — Lemma 1 · Matt. 23:1

> **Then Jesus spoke to the crowds and to his disciples,**[^1] (Matt. 23:1)

This lemma carries only the Gospel words; it opens the discourse of Matthew 23. The sentence runs on into the next lemma with «λέγων» ("saying"), and Theophylact's exposition of the passage begins at Lemma 3.[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Τότε ὁ Ἰησοῦς ἐλάλησε τοῖς ὄχλοις καὶ τοῖς μαθηταῖς αὐτοῦ». The verb *ἐλάλησε* is aorist ("spoke"); rendered accordingly rather than as the smoother "spake unto." *τοῖς ὄχλοις* = "the crowds / the multitudes."
[^2]: The clause ends on a comma and is grammatically incomplete; the participle «λέγων» ("saying") that governs the following quotation stands at the head of Lemma 2.
