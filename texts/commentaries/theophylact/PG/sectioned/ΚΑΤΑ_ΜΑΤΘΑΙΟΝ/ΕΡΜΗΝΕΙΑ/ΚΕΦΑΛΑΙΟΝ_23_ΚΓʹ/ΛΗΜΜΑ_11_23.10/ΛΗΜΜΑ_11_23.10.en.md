# Matthew 23 (ΚΓʹ) — Lemma 11 · Matt. 23:10–11

> **…be called guides;[^1] for one is your guide, the Christ. But the greater of you shall be your servant. (Matt. 23:10–11)**

*This lemma carries only the scripture text; Theophylact's exposition is given below at Lemma 12.*[^2]

[^1]: A continuation of the preceding «Μηδὲ» ("Neither"): "neither be called guides." Greek *καθηγητής* ("guide, leader, one who goes before") is distinct from *διδάσκαλος* ("teacher") of v. 8 and from *πατήρ* ("father") of v. 9; rendered "guide" to keep it distinct from "teacher." Familiar English Bibles render it "master(s)." The OCR «ἔσται» stands for *ἔσται* ("shall be"); the doubled «ὑμῶν… ὑμῶν» is retained ("of you… your servant").
[^2]: See Lemma 12 for the combined exposition of Matt. 23:8–12.
