# Matthew 23 (ΚΓʹ) — Lemma 12 · Matt. 23:12

> **But whoever will exalt himself shall be humbled; and whoever will humble himself shall be exalted. (Matt. 23:12)**[^1]

Christ does not forbid someone to be called teacher, but the passionately[^2] wishing to be called *so*, and the striving in every way for the being called *it*. For the teacherly dignity[^3] belongs properly to God alone. And in saying "Call not *anyone* father," he does not forbid the honor toward parents — since he wishes us to honor our parents, and especially the spiritual fathers — but he leads *us* toward recognizing[^4] the true Father, that is, God. For properly God *is* Father; but the bodily *fathers are* not causes of the begetting, but ministers and co-causes.[^5] And showing the gain of humble-mindedness, he says that "the greater of you ought to be your servant and last." For whoever will exalt himself, thinking himself to be something, shall be humbled, being forsaken by God…[^6]

[^1]: The OCR «ὑώωθήσεται» → *ὑψωθήσεται* ("shall be exalted"), and «ταπεινωθήκεται» → *ταπεινωθήσεται* ("shall be humbled") — both secure, being the direct antitheses of *ὑψώσει / ταπεινώσει*.
[^2]: Greek *ἐμπαθῶς* — "with passion, passionately," i.e. with disordered attachment (*πάθος*); the point is not the title but the craving for it.
[^3]: Greek *τὸ διδασκαλικὸν ἀξίωμα* — "the teacher's rank / dignity / office." Rendered "teacherly dignity" to keep the adjective visible.
[^4]: The OCR «ἐνάνει» → *ἐνάγει* ("he leads on, urges toward"). The construction *πρὸς τὸ… ἐπιγινώσκειν… ἐνάγει* = "he leads *us* on toward recognizing…"; the object "*us*" is supplied.
[^5]: Greek *οὐκ αἴτιοι τῆς γεννήσεως, ἀλλ' ὑπουργοὶ καὶ συναίτιοι* — bodily fathers are not the *cause* (*αἴτιος*) of a child's coming-to-be but "under-workers" (*ὑπουργοί*, ministers/assistants) and "co-causes" (*συναίτιοι*); the true cause is God.
[^6]: The exposition breaks off mid-sentence at *ἐγκαταλειφθεὶς ὑπὸ τοῦ Θεοῦ* ("being forsaken by God"); the balancing clause (the one who humbles himself being exalted) is continued in the next lemma.
