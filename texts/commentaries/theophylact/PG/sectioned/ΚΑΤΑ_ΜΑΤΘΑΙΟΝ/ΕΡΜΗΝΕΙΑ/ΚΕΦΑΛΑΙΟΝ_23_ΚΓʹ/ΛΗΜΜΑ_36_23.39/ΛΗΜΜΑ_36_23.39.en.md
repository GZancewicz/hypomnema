# Matthew 23 (ΚΓʹ) — Lemma 36 · Matt. 23:39

> **Blessed is he that cometh. (Matt. 23:39)**

And the "from now on," understand *it* as *meaning* "after the being crucified"[^1] — not, however, that hour in which he was saying these things. For they saw him, from *the time* when he spoke these things, often; but after the being crucified they saw him not, nor shall they see *him* until his coming shall be present,[^2]

[^1]: «μετὰ τὸ σταυρωθῆναι» — "after the crucifixion," lit. "after the being-crucified" (articular infinitive). Rendered woodenly to keep the infinitive visible; Theophylact repeats the same phrase in the next sentence.
[^2]: «ἄχρις ἂν ἐνστῇ ἡ παρουσία αὐτοῦ» — "until his coming shall have set in / be at hand." The sentence breaks off here at the close of the extant section (end of the chapter's lemmata); the thought carries on into what follows in the PG text.
