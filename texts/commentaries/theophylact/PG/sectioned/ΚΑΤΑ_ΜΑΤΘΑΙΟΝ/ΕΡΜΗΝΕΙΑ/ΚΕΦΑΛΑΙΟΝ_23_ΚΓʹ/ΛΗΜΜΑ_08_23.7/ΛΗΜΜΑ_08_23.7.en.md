# Matthew 23 (ΚΓʹ) — Lemma 08 · Matt. 23:6

> **And the chief seats in the synagogues. (Matt. 23:6)**[^1]

Where they ought rather to teach the others also to be humble-minded, there those very men themselves were being corrupted.[^2] For they did all things for the sake of glory; and *while* doing these things they were not ashamed, but rather wished to be called Rabbi, Rabbi — that is, teachers.[^3]

[^1]: Greek «Καὶ τὰς πρωτοκαθεδρίας ἐν ταῖς συναγωγαῖς» — the words belong to Matt. 23:6 (the folder-name verse 23.7 is one off); Theophylact returns to comment on the "chief seats in the synagogues" already quoted in Lemma 06.
[^2]: The OCR reads «Κνθα μᾶλλον ὤμειλον»; securely *Ἔνθα μᾶλλον ὤφειλον* ("Where they ought rather"). *διεφθείροντο* = "were being corrupted / ruined."
[^3]: *Ῥαββί* is glossed here by Theophylact himself with *διδάσκαλοι*, "teachers."
