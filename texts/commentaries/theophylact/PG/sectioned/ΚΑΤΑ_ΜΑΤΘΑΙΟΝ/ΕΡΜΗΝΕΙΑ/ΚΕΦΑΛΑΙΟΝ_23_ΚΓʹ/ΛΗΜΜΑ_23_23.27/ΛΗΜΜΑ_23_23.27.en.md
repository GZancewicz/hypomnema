# Matthew 23 (ΚΓʹ) — Lemma 23 · Matt. 23:27–28

> **Woe to you, Scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, because you resemble whitewashed tombs, which outwardly, on the one hand, appear beautiful, but within are full of dead *men's* bones and of all uncleanness; so also you, outwardly, on the one hand, appear righteous to men, but within you are full of hypocrisy and lawlessness. (Matt. 23:27–28)**

And this parable too is understood similarly to the preceding *one*; for they were eager, on the one hand, to appear dignified in *their* outward condition, like the whitewashed tombs — that is, *tombs* whitened with gypsum and with lime — but, on the other hand, their inside was full of all uncleanness and of dead and rotted works.[^1]

[^1]: The Greek closes with a comma («ἦσαν πλήρεις,»), the sentence trailing off; the thought is nonetheless complete. The next lemma (Matt. 23:29) opens a fresh "Woe."
