# Matthew 23 (ΚΓʹ) — Lemma 33 · Matt. 23:37

> **Jerusalem, Jerusalem, thou that killest the prophets and stonest them that are sent unto her,[^1] how often did I wish to gather together thy children, in the manner in which a bird[^2] gathereth together her nestlings under her wings, and ye would not? Behold,[^3] (Matt. 23:37)**

This lemma carries only the Gospel citation; Theophylact's exposition of these words comes at Lemma 35.

[^1]: Theophylact's text reads «πρὸς αὐτήν» ("unto **her**"), shifting to the third person in the midst of the second-person apostrophe to Jerusalem ("thou… thy"); this is the Byzantine reading, where other witnesses (and the KJV) have «πρὸς σέ» ("unto thee"). Rendered as it stands.
[^2]: Greek «ὄρνις» is the generic "bird" (KJV, interpreting, "hen"); rendered "bird" per the plain Greek. «τὰ νοσσία» = "the young ones / nestlings."
[^3]: The citation breaks off at «Ἰδοὺ» ("Behold"); it continues in Lemma 34 with «…your house is left unto you desolate».
