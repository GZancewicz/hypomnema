# Matthew 23 (ΚΓʹ) — Lemma 24 · Matt. 23:29

> **Woe to you, Scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, because you build the tombs of the prophets, and adorn the monuments of the righteous, and… (Matt. 23:29)**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture citation, which breaks off mid-verse; the sentence is completed in the following lemma («λέγετε· Εἰ ἦμεν ἐν ταῖς ἡμέραις τῶν πατέρων ἡμῶν…», Matt. 23:30), where Theophylact's exposition resumes.*

[^1]: The Greek ends at «καὶ» ("and"), breaking off before the verb *λέγετε* ("you say") that opens the next lemma. No comment accompanies this fragment.
