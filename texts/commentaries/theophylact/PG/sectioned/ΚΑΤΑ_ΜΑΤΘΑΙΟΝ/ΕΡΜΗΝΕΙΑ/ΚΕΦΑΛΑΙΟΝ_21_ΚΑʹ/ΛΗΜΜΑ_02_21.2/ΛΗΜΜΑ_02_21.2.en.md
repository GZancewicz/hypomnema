# Matthew 21 (ΚΑʹ) — Lemma 2 · Matt. 21:2

> **saying to them: Go into the village *that is* opposite you, and immediately you will find an ass tied, and a colt with her; having loosed *them*, bring *them* to me; and if anyone say anything to you,** (Matt. 21:2)[^1]

*This lemma carries the scripture text only; the exposition resumes at Lemma 4.*

[^1]: The Greek: «λέγων αὐτοῖς· Πορεύθητε εἰς τὴν κώμην τὴν ἀπέναντι ὑμῶν, καὶ εὐθέως εὑρήσετε ὄνον δεδεμένην, καὶ πῶλον μετ᾽ αὐτῆς· λύσαντες ἀγάγετέ μοι». The participle *λύσαντες* ("having loosed") governs no expressed object and *ἀγάγετέ* ("bring") none either; both supplied "*them*" are italicized. *τὴν ἀπέναντι* is an article + adverb functioning attributively ("the opposite [village]"); the connective "*that is*" is supplied and italicized.
