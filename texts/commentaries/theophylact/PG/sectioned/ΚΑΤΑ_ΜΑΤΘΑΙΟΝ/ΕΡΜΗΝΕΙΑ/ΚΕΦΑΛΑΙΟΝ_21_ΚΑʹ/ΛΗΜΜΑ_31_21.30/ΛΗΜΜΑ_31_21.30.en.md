# Matthew 21 (ΚΑʹ) — Lemma 31 · Matt. 21:31

This lemma is a continuation: it completes a sentence begun in the preceding lemma, which ended by describing the first company as "such as the Jews were, who said:"[^1]

«All that God said we will do and hear;»[^2] but the other *company was that* of those who disobeyed, such as harlots and tax-collectors, but indeed also *that* of the people from the nations, who, from the beginning not having obeyed the will of God, later repented and gave heed. See therefore the wisdom of Christ. He did not straightway from the beginning say to them that "The tax-collectors and the harlots are better than you," but he took hold of them beforehand, and they confessed that, of the two sons, that one *is* obedient who did the will of the father. Then, those men having thus confessed, he added that[^3]

[^1]: The preceding lemma (30) breaks off at «οἷοι ἦσαν οἱ Ἰουδαῖοι, εἰπόντες·» ("such as the Jews were, who said:"), so that the quoted words with which this lemma opens are the completion of the Jews' saying, not a fresh Gospel lemma. Hence no bold-blockquote lemma heads this file.
[^2]: Greek «Πάντα ὅσα εἶπεν ὁ Θεὸς ποιήσομεν καὶ ἀκουσόμεθα» — the people's response at Sinai (cf. Exod. 24:7 LXX, which reads «Πάντα ὅσα ἐλάλησεν Κύριος ποιήσομεν καὶ ἀκουσόμεθα»). Theophylact quotes it as the type of those "who from the beginning promised." Rendered from his wording, not conformed to any English Bible.
[^3]: The sentence breaks off at *ἐπήγαγεν ὅτι* ("he added that"); it is completed in the next lemma with the Gospel words «John came in *the* way of righteousness…» (Matt. 21:32).
