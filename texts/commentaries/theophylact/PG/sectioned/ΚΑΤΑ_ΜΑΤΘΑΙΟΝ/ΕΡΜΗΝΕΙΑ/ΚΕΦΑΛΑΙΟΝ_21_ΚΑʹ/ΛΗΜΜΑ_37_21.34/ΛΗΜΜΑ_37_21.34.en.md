# Matthew 21 (ΚΑʹ) — Lemma 37 · Matt. 21:40

> **When therefore the lord of the vineyard comes, what will he do to those farmers? (Matt. 21:40)**[^1]

[^1]: This lemma carries only the scripture words, with no exposition of its own: it is the vineyard-owner's question, whose answer the hearers supply in the next verse. Theophylact expounds both the question and its answer together in the following lemma (38). Greek *γεωργοῖς* is rendered "farmers" throughout (the generic ἐργάτης of the soil, the tenant-cultivators of the parable), not the archaic "husbandmen." Greek *ὁ Κύριος τοῦ ἀμπελῶνος* is here "the lord of the vineyard" (the parable's master); the same word Theophylact will presently apply to God the Father.
