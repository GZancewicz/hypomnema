# Matthew 21 (ΚΑʹ) — Lemma 40 · Matt. 21:43

> **…this reason I say to you that the kingdom of God will be taken away from you, and will be given to a nation producing the fruits of it. (Matt. 21:43)**[^1]

And the[^2]

[^1]: Continues the sentence begun at the close of the previous lemma («Διὰ…»), here completed: *Διὰ τοῦτο λέγω ὑμῖν…* ("For this reason I say to you…"). Greek *ἀρθήσεται* "will be taken away / lifted off"; *ἔθνει* "to a nation / a people" — the ordinary word for the gentile nations, here "a nation" that bears fruit, over against the Jews; *ποιοῦντι τοὺς καρποὺς αὐτῆς* "producing the fruits of it" (of the kingdom).
[^2]: The lemma breaks off at *Καὶ ὁ* ("And the *one*…"); the sentence is completed in the next lemma with «πεσὼν ἐπὶ τὸν λίθον τοῦτον…» ("who falls upon this stone…," Matt. 21:44).
