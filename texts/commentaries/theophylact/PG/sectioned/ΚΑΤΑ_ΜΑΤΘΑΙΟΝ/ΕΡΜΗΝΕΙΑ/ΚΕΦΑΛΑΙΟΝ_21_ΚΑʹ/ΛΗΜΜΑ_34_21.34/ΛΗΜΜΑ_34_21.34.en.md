# Matthew 21 (ΚΑʹ) — Lemma 34 · Matt. 21:34-37

The season of the fruits drew near, according to the times of the prophets. For the servants sent forth *were* the prophets, whom also the husbandmen — that is, the false-prophets and false-teachers *arising* from time to time — abused in various ways. For one they beat, as *they beat* Micaiah, Zedekiah having struck him upon the cheek;[^1] and another they killed, as *they killed* Zechariah, between the temple and the altar;[^2] and they stoned *another*, as Zechariah the son of Jehoiada the high priest.[^3] But afterward the Son of God was sent, having appeared in *the* flesh. And the *saying*[^4]

[^1]: Cf. 1 Kings 22:24 (3 Kingdoms LXX), where Zedekiah son of Chenaanah strikes Micaiah son of Imlah upon the cheek. Greek *πατάξαντος αὐτὸν ἐπὶ σιαγόνα Σεδεκίου*, a genitive absolute.
[^2]: The one "killed between the temple and the altar" alludes to the Zechariah of Matt. 23:35.
[^3]: Zechariah son of Jehoiada the high priest, stoned in the court of the house of the Lord (cf. 2 Chron. 24:20-22). Theophylact thus distributes the three verbs of the parable (beat / kill / stone) among three prophetic figures, naming two distinct Zechariahs; translated as it stands.
[^4]: The lemma breaks off at *Τὸ δὲ* ("And the *saying* / And *as for* the…"); it is completed in the next lemma, which supplies the Gospel words «They will respect my son» (Matt. 21:37). "*saying*" is supplied to carry the neuter article *τό* across the break.
