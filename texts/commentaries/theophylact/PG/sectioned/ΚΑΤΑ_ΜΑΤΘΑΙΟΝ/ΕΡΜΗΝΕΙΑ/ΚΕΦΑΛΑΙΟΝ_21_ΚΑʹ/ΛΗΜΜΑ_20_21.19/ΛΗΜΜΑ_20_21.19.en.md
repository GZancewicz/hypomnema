# Matthew 21 (ΚΑʹ) — Lemma 20 · Matt. 21:19

> **a single fig tree by the road, he came upon it, and found nothing on it except leaves only; and it is said to it: "No longer let fruit come to be from you unto the age"; and immediately the fig tree was withered; and**[^1]

*This lemma continues the scripture quotation begun in Lemma 19 and carries no exposition of its own; the comment follows in Lemma 21, where Theophylact treats the whole passage together.*

[^1]: Greek «… συκῆν μίαν ἐπὶ τῆς ὁδοῦ, ἦλθεν ἐπ᾿ αὐτὴν, καὶ οὐδὲν εὗρεν ἐν αὐτῇ εἰ μὴ φύλλα μόνον· καὶ λέγεται αὐτῇ· Μηκέτι ἐκ σοῦ καρπὸς γένηται εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα· καὶ ἐξηράνθη παραχρῆμα ἡ συκῆ· καὶ …» Note *λέγεται αὐτῇ* ("it **is said** to it," passive) where the common text has *λέγει αὐτῇ* ("he says to it," active); translated as it stands per the rule of following the text on the page. *εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα* is rendered literally "unto the age" (i.e. "forever"); *παραχρῆμα* = "immediately, on the spot." The quote again breaks off at *καὶ* ("and"), completed in Lemma 21.
