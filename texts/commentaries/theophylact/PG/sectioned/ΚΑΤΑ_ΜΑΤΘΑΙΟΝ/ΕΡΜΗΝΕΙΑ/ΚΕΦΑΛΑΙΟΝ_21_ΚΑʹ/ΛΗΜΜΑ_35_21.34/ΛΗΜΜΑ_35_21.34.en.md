# Matthew 21 (ΚΑʹ) — Lemma 35 · Matt. 21:37

> **They will respect my son. (Matt. 21:37)**[^1]

He said *this* not being ignorant that they were about to kill him, but signifying that which was owed to come to pass. For it was necessary, he says, *that*, even if they killed the servants, yet at least the dignity of the Son should be respected. But the husbandmen, having seen him, said:[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Ἐντραπήσονται τὸν Υἱόν μου». The verb *ἐντρέπομαι* here means "to feel respect / reverence toward," and is picked up in the exposition as *ἐντραπῆναι* ("to be respected"). Rendered "respect" throughout to keep the wordplay visible.
[^2]: The lemma breaks off at *εἶπον·* ("said:"); it is completed in the next lemma with the Gospel words «This is the heir; come, let us kill him» (Matt. 21:38).
