# Matthew 21 (ΚΑʹ) — Lemma 26 · Matt. 21:24

> **But Jesus, answering, said to them: I too will ask you one word,[^1] which if you tell me, I too will tell you by what authority I do these things. The—[^2]**

This lemma too carries only the scripture text; the exposition of the whole pericope is given at Lemma 28 below.

[^1]: Greek «λόγον ἕνα» — "one word," that is, one matter or question.
[^2]: The lemma breaks off at «Τὸ» ("The—"), the definite article beginning "The baptism of John…," completed in the next lemma.
