# Matthew 21 (ΚΑʹ) — Lemma 43 · Matt. 21:45

> **And having heard his parables, the chief priests and the Pharisees knew that he was speaking about them; and (Matt. 21:45)**

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation, which breaks off mid-sentence at «καὶ» ("and"). The sentence is completed, and the whole is expounded, in the following lemma (44), which supplies the words «seeking to seize him, they feared the crowds, since they held him as a prophet» (Matt. 21:46).*[^1]

[^1]: The folder metadata assigns this lemma to Matt. 21:34, but the Greek quotation is securely Matt. 21:45 («Καὶ ἀκούσαντες οἱ ἀρχιερεῖς καὶ οἱ Φαρισαῖοι τὰς παραβολὰς αὐτοῦ, ἔγνωσαν ὅτι περὶ αὐτῶν λέγει»). The verse ref has been corrected accordingly.
