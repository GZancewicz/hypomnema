# Matthew 21 (ΚΑʹ) — Lemma 19 · Matt. 21:18

> **And in the early morning, returning to the city, he hungered; and having seen**[^1]

*This lemma carries no exposition of its own: the scripture quotation is broken off mid-sentence at "and having seen" and is continued through the next two lemmata (20–21), where Theophylact comments on the whole passage together.*

[^1]: Greek «Πρωΐας δὲ ἐπανάγων εἰς τὴν πόλιν, ἐπείνασε· καὶ ἰδὼν …» The quote breaks off at *καὶ ἰδὼν* ("and having seen"), completed in Lemma 20. *ἐπανάγων* is the participle "returning / going back up"; *Πρωΐας* is "at early morning." The particle *δὲ* is rendered "and."
