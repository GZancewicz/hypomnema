# Matthew 21 (ΚΑʹ) — Lemma 16 · Matt. 21:15

> **But the chief priests and the scribes, having seen the wonderful things which he did, and the children crying out in the temple and saying: Hosanna to the son of David, were indignant, and**[^1] (Matt. 21:15)

[^1]: This lemma carries only the scripture quotation and breaks off mid-sentence at «καὶ» ("and"); the sentence is completed in the following lemma, whose opening words «εἶπον αὐτῷ· Ἀκούεις τί οὗτοι λέγουσιν;…» ("they said to him: Do you hear what these are saying?…") continue the Gospel text (Matt. 21:16), where Theophylact's exposition of the whole exchange is given. *τὰ θαυμάσια* rendered "the wonderful things" (the marvels/miracles he had just done); *ἠγανάκτησαν* = "were indignant."
