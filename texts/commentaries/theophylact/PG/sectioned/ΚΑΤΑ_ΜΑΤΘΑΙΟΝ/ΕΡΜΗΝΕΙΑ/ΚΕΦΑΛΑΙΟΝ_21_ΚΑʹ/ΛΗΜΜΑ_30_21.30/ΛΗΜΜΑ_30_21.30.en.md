# Matthew 21 (ΚΑʹ) — Lemma 30 · Matt. 21:30–32

> **—having come to the second, he said likewise; but he, answering, said: I *will*,[^1] sir; and he did not go. Which of the two did the will of the father? They say to him: The first. Jesus says to them: Amen I say to you that the tax-collectors and the harlots go before you into the kingdom of God. For John came to you in *the* way of righteousness, and you did not believe him; but the tax-collectors and the harlots believed him; but you, having seen *it*, did not afterward repent so as to believe him. (Matt. 21:30–32)**

He introduces two orders:[^2] one, on the one hand, of those who promised from the beginning — such as were the Jews, having said:—[^3]

[^1]: Greek «Ἐγὼ, κύριε» — literally "I, sir," with the verb left unexpressed; the sense is "I *will go*, sir." Rendered "*I will*, sir," the supplied verb in italics. Here «κύριε» is the son's address to his father, "sir."
[^2]: Greek «τάγματα» — ranks, orders, drawn-up companies (a term with a military coloring); here two ordered classes of people.
[^3]: The exposition breaks off; the words Theophylact puts in the mouth of the Jews are completed in the next lemma: «Πάντα ὅσα εἶπεν ὁ Θεὸς ποιήσομεν καὶ ἀκουσόμεθα» ("All that God has said we will do and we will hearken to," cf. Exod. 24:3, 7).
