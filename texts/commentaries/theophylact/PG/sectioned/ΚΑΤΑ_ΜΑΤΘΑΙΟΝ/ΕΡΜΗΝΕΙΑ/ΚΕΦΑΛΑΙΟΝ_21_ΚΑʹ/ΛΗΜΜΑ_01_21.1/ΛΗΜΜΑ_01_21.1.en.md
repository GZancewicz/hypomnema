# Matthew 21 (ΚΑʹ) — Lemma 1 · Matt. 21:1

> **And when they drew near to Jerusalem, and came to Bethphage, toward the Mount of Olives, then Jesus sent two disciples,** (Matt. 21:1)[^1]

*This lemma carries the scripture text only; Theophylact's exposition is deferred, resuming at Lemma 4, where he expounds verses 1–5 together.*

[^1]: The lemma opens in the OCR as «αὶ ὅτε ἤγγισαν», where «αὶ» is a corruption of «καὶ» ("And"); securely restored. The verse otherwise follows the received text: «Καὶ ὅτε ἤγγισαν εἰς Ἱεροσόλυμα, καὶ ἦλθον εἰς Βηθφαγὴ πρὸς τὸ ὄρος τῶν Ἐλαιῶν». The preposition *πρὸς* ("toward, to") is a present word, hence roman.
