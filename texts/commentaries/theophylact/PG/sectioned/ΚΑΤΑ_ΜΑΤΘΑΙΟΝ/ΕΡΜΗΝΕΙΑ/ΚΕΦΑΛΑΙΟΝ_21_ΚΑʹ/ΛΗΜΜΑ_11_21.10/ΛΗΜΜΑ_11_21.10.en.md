# Matthew 21 (ΚΑʹ) — Lemma 11 · Matt. 21:10

> **And when he had entered into Jerusalem, all the city was shaken, saying: Who is this? And the...[^1] (Matt. 21:10)**

[^1]: This lemma carries only the scripture quotation. The opening «Καὶ» ("And") is supplied from the tail of the previous lemma; the quotation breaks off at «Οἱ δὲ» ("And the..."), completed at the head of the next lemma («ὄχλοι ἔλεγον…», "crowds said…"), where the exposition begins. There is no exposition in this section. Greek *ἐσείσθη* is literally "was shaken / quaked" (as of an earthquake), here of the city stirred into commotion.
