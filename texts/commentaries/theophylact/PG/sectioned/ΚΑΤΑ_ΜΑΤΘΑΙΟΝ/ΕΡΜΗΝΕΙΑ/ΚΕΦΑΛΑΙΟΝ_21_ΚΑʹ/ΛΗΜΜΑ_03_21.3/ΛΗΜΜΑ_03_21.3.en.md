# Matthew 21 (ΚΑʹ) — Lemma 3 · Matt. 21:3

> **you shall say that the Lord has need of them; and immediately he sends them,** (Matt. 21:3)[^1]

*This lemma carries the scripture text only; the exposition resumes at Lemma 4.*

[^1]: The OCR reads «ἐρεῖτε Ἐτι ἐ Κύριος αὐτῶν χρείαν ἔχει, εὐθέως δὲ ἀποστέλλει αὐτούς», corrupt at «Ἐτι ἐ», securely restored to «ὅτι ὁ» ("that the"): «ἐρεῖτε ὅτι ὁ Κύριος αὐτῶν χρείαν ἔχει». The final verb here is present-tense *ἀποστέλλει* ("sends"), whereas the received Gospel text reads the future *ἀποστελεῖ* ("he will send"); translated as it stands. Its subject is ambiguous — most naturally the owner, who "sends"/releases the animals — and the whole is rendered following the text on the page.
