# Matthew 21 (ΚΑʹ) — Lemma 18 · Matt. 21:17

> **And leaving them he went out, outside the city, into Bethany, and lodged there.** (Matt. 21:17)

He withdraws from these as *being* unworthy, and comes into Bethany, which is interpreted "house of obedience";[^1] for from the insubordinate he passes over to those obeying him, and lodges among them. For he says: "I will dwell in them and walk about *among them*."[^2]

[^1]: Theophylact gives the etymology *οἶκος ὑπακοῆς*, "house of obedience," to fit his point (Christ moving from the "insubordinate," *ἀνυπότακτοι*, to the "obedient," *ὑπακούοντες*). Other traditional etymologies of Bethany differ (e.g. "house of affliction," "house of dates"); the rendering follows the sense he himself supplies.
[^2]: A conflated citation of Lev. 26:12 / 2 Cor. 6:16: *ἐνοικήσω ἐν αὐτοῖς καὶ ἐμπεριπατήσω* ("I will dwell in them and walk about *among them*"). Translated as Theophylact quotes it.
