# Matthew 21 (ΚΑʹ) — Lemma 29 · Matt. 21:29

> **But he, answering, said: I will not. But afterward, having repented,[^1] he went. And—[^2]**

Scripture text only; Theophylact's exposition of the parable of the two sons follows at Lemma 30 (and continues at Lemma 31).

[^1]: Greek «μεταμεληθεὶς» — having changed his mind, regretted, repented (μεταμέλομαι); the same verb returns at v. 32 in "you did not… repent."
[^2]: The lemma breaks off at «Καὶ» ("And—"); the parable continues in the next lemma.
