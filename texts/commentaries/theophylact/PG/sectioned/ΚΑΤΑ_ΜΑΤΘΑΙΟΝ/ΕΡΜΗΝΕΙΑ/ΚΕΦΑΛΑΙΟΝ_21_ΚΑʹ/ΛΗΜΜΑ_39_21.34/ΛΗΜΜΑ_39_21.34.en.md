# Matthew 21 (ΚΑʹ) — Lemma 39 · Matt. 21:42

> **Jesus says to them: Did you never read in the Scriptures: The stone which the builders rejected, this one became *the* head of *the* corner; from *the* Lord this came about, and it is marvelous in our eyes? (Matt. 21:42)**[^1]

For this[^2]

[^1]: The quoted words are Ps. 117(118):22–23 LXX. Greek *εἰς κεφαλὴν γωνίας* is rendered literally "into *the* head of *the* corner" (the cornerstone / capstone); *ἐγενήθη* "became / was made"; *ἀπεδοκίμασαν* "rejected / disapproved (after testing)." The feminine *αὕτη* ("this came about," *παρὰ Κυρίου ἐγένετο αὕτη*) follows the Greek Psalm, where it refers to the whole event (the corner-making), not to the masculine "stone."
[^2]: The lemma breaks off at *Διὰ* ("Because of / For this"); the sentence is completed in the next lemma, which supplies «τοῦτο λέγω ὑμῖν…» ("For this reason I say to you…," Matt. 21:43).
