# Matthew 21 (ΚΑʹ) — Lemma 27 · Matt. 21:25

> **—baptism of John, whence was it? from heaven or from men? But they reasoned among themselves, saying: If we say, From heaven, he will say to us: Why then[^1] did you not believe him? But if—[^2]**

Scripture text only; the exposition of the whole pericope is given at Lemma 28 below.

[^1]: OCR «Διατί αν οὐκ»; read «Διὰ τί οὖν οὐκ» ("Why then… not"), the received wording. The «αν» is most likely a corruption of the particle «οὖν» ("then, therefore"), which is rendered here.
[^2]: The lemma breaks off at «Ἐὰν δὲ» ("But if—"), completed in the next lemma.
