# Matthew 21 (ΚΑʹ) — Lemma 13 · Matt. 21:12

> **And Jesus went into the temple of God, and cast out all those selling and buying in the temple; and the tables of the money-changers he overturned, and the seats of those selling the doves. And**[^1] (Matt. 21:12)

[^1]: This lemma carries only the scripture quotation and breaks off mid-sentence at «Καὶ» ("And"); the sentence is completed in the following lemma, whose opening words «λέγει αὐτοῖς· Γέγραπται…» ("he says to them: It is written…") continue the Gospel text (Matt. 21:13), and Theophylact's exposition of the whole episode is given there. The OCR of this lemma is lightly corrupt — «ἀνοράζοντας» for **ἀγοράζοντας** ("buying"), «κατ.κστρεψε» for **κατέστρεψε** ("overturned") — but every word is securely recoverable and none affects the sense. Greek *κολλυβιστῶν* is rendered "money-changers"; Theophylact defines the term in the next lemma.
