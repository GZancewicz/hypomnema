# Matthew 21 (ΚΑʹ) — Lemma 41 · Matt. 21:44

> **…who falls upon this stone will be shattered; but on whomever it falls, it will winnow him. (Matt. 21:44)**[^1]

*The* stone he names himself; and *as* builders, the teachers of the Jews, who, on the one hand, rejected him as useless,[^2] saying:[^3]

[^1]: Completes the sentence begun at the close of the previous lemma («Καὶ ὁ…»): *Καὶ ὁ πεσὼν ἐπὶ τὸν λίθον τοῦτον…*. Greek *συνθλασθήσεται* "will be broken to pieces / shattered." The verb *λικμήσει* is difficult: *λικμάω* properly means "to winnow" (to fan grain so the chaff is carried off), hence "to scatter like chaff"; many render it here "will grind to powder." Theophylact himself glosses it below (lemma 42) as *διασπερεῖ* ("he will scatter *them*"), understanding the dispersion of the Jews — so "winnow" is kept in the body, with his own "scatter" gloss following.
[^2]: Greek *ἀχρεῖον* "useless / unprofitable / good-for-nothing" (the builders' verdict on the rejected stone).
[^3]: The lemma breaks off at *λέγοντες·* ("saying:"); the builders' words are supplied at the head of the next lemma with «Σαμαρείτης εἶ…» ("You are a Samaritan…").
