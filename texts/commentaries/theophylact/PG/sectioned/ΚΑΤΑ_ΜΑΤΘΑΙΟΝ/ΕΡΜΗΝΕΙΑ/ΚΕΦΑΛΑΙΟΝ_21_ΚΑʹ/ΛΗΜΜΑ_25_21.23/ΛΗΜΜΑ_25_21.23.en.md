# Matthew 21 (ΚΑʹ) — Lemma 25 · Matt. 21:23

> **And to him, having come into the temple, there came to him, as he was teaching, the chief priests and the elders of the people, saying: By what authority[^1] do you do these things? and who gave you this authority? (Matt. 21:23)**

This lemma carries only the scripture text; Theophylact reserves his exposition of the whole pericope on authority (21:23–27) for Lemma 28 below, where the entire quotation has been laid out.

[^1]: Greek «Ἐν ποίᾳ ἐξουσίᾳ» — literally "*in* what authority." The preposition ἐν is here instrumental ("by, through what authority"), rendered "by" for the sense; the same phrase recurs in the Lord's reply (21:24) and in his closing refusal (21:27).
