# Matthew 21 (ΚΑʹ) — Lemma 7 · Matt. 21:8

> **But the very great crowd[^1] spread their garments in the road; and others were cutting branches from the trees, and were strewing *them* in the road; and the...[^2] (Matt. 21:8)**

[^1]: Greek OCR «Ὁ δ πλεῖστος ὄνλος», securely = **«Ὁ δὲ πλεῖστος ὄχλος»** ("But the very great crowd"). *πλεῖστος* is the superlative of *πολύς*; the phrase can also be rendered "the greater part of the crowd" / "the greatest part of the multitude." The collective singular *ὄχλος* takes the plural verb *ἔστρωσαν* ("spread") by construction according to sense.
[^2]: This lemma carries only the scripture quotation, which breaks off at «οἱ δὲ» ("and the..."); the sentence is completed at the head of the next lemma («ὄχλοι οἱ προάγοντες…», "crowds, the ones going before…"), where Theophylact's exposition begins. There is no exposition in this section.
