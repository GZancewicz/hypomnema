# Matthew 21 (ΚΑʹ) — Lemma 5 · Matt. 21:6

> **And the disciples, having gone and having done just as Jesus commanded them,** (Matt. 21:6)[^1]

*This lemma carries the scripture text only; the exposition follows at Lemma 6.*

[^1]: The Greek: «Πορευθέντες δὲ οἱ μαθηταὶ, καὶ ποιήσαντες καθὼς προσέταξεν αὐτοῖς ὁ Ἰησοῦς». Both aorist participles (*πορευθέντες*, *ποιήσαντες*) are kept as "having gone," "having done"; the sentence completes in the next lemma with «ἤγαγον τὴν ὄνον καὶ τὸν πῶλον» ("they brought the ass and the colt").
