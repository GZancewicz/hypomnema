# Matthew 21 (ΚΑʹ) — Lemma 22 · Matt. 21:21

> **And answering, Jesus said to them: Truly I say to you, if you have faith and do not doubt, not only will you do the *thing* of the fig tree, but even if you say to this mountain, "Be lifted up and be cast into the sea," it will happen; and**[^1]

*This lemma carries no exposition of its own: the scripture quotation is broken off mid-sentence at "and" and is continued in Lemma 23, where Theophylact comments on the whole saying.*

[^1]: Greek «Ἀποκριθεὶς δὲ ὁ Ἰησοῦς εἶπεν αὐτοῖς· Ἀμὴν λέγω ὑμῖν, ἐὰν ἔχητε πίστιν καὶ μὴ διακριθῆτε, οὐ μόνον τὸ τῆς συκῆς ποιήσετε, ἀλλὰ κἂν τῷ ὄρει τούτῳ εἴπητε· Ἄρθητι καὶ βλήθητι εἰς τὴν θάλασσαν, γενήσεται, καὶ …» *διακριθῆτε* is rendered "doubt," but the verb *διακρίνομαι* also means "to be divided / at variance / separated"; Theophylact exploits both senses in Lemma 24, so the gloss is provisional here. *τὸ τῆς συκῆς* = literally "the *thing* of the fig tree," i.e. what was done to it. The quote breaks off at *καὶ* ("and"), completed in Lemma 23.
