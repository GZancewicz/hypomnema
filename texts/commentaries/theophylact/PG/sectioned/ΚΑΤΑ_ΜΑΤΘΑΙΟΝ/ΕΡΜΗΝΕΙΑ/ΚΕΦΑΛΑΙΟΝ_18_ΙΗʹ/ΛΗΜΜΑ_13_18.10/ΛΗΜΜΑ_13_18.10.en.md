# Matthew 18 (ΙΗʹ) — Lemma 13 · Matt. 18:10-11

> **See that you despise not one of these little ones; for I say to you, that their angels in *the* heavens continually behold the face of my Father who *is* in *the* heavens; for the Son of man came to save that which was lost. (Matt. 18:10-11)**

He commands not to set at naught those reckoned little, that is, the poor in spirit, the great ones with God.[^1] For so much, he says, are they loved by God, as to have angels *as* guardians so as not to be harmed by demons. For each of the faithful, but rather even all men, we have angels. But of the little and humble in Christ the angels are so intimate[^2] with God, that they continually behold his face, standing before him.

And it appears from this, that even if we all have angels, yet the angels of sinners, as if being ashamed on account of our lack of boldness,[^3] neither do they themselves have boldness to behold the face of God, and perhaps to entreat on our behalf; but those *angels* of the humble-minded, these behold the divine face, on account of having boldness.

And why do I say, he says, that such ones have angels? I for this reason came, that I might save that which was lost, and *that* I might take to myself as my own[^4] those reckoned by the many *to be* of no account.

[^1]: Greek *τοὺς νομιζομένους μικρούς, τουτέστι τοὺς πτωχοὺς τῷ πνεύματι, τοὺς μεγάλους παρὰ τῷ Θεῷ* — the "little ones" (*μικροί*) of the lemma are glossed by Theophylact as "the poor in spirit" (cf. Matt. 5:3), who are "great" in God's sight; the paradox of little/great is his point.
[^2]: Greek *οἰκεῖοι τῷ Θεῷ* — "belonging to God's household, intimate, closely related to." The word is domestic/familial: the angels of the humble are, so to speak, members of God's household.
[^3]: Theophylact plays on the *παρρησία* word-group. The noun *παρρησία* is "boldness, frankness, freedom of speech, confidence *of access*"; here the negated adjective *τὸ ἡμῶν ἀπαρρησίαστον* ("our un-boldness, our want of confident access") is set against the angels who do or do not "have *παρρησία*" to behold God's face. Rendered "lack of boldness" / "boldness" to keep the pair visible.
[^4]: Greek *οἰκειῶμαι* (OCR *οἰκε φωμαι*), securely = *οἰκειῶμαι*, "to make my own, appropriate, claim as kin" — cognate with *οἰκεῖοι* above; Christ appropriates as his own household those the world counts worthless (*οὐδαμινούς*).
