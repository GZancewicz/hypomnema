# Matthew 18 (ΙΗʹ) — Lemma 8 · Matt. 18:6

> **one of these little ones, (Matt. 18:6)**

that is, of those who make themselves small and humble, although being great,[^1]—[^2]

[^1]: Greek *τουτέστι τῶν ἑαυτοὺς σμικρυνόντων καὶ ταπεινούντων, καίτοιγε μεγάλων ὄντων*. The verb *σμικρύνω* (= *μικρύνω*, "to make small, to diminish") is here reflexive — "those who make themselves small"; paired with *ταπεινοῦντας* ("humbling"). The concessive *καίτοιγε* ("although, and that too") introduces the paradox: they are in fact great. Theophylact reads the "little ones" not literally as children but as the self-humbling.
[^2]: The sentence continues into Lemma 9, which supplies the apodosis ("it is profitable for him that a millstone…") and the exposition.
