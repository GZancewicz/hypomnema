# Matthew 18 (ΙΗʹ) — Lemma 7 · Matt. 18:6

> **But whoever shall cause *one* to stumble.[^1] (Matt. 18:6)**

that is, shall insult *him*[^2]—[^3]

[^1]: Greek «Ὃς δ᾽ ἂν σκανδαλίσῃ». The particle *δέ* is rendered "but." The verb *σκανδαλίζω* means "to cause to stumble, to give offense to, to entrap," from *σκάνδαλον* ("a snare, a stumbling-block"); it is glossed consistently here and in the following lemmata as "cause to stumble." The object ("*one*" of the little ones) is not yet expressed in this clause — it is supplied in the next lemma — hence the italics.
[^2]: Greek *τουτέστιν ὑβρίσῃ* — "that is, shall treat insolently / outrage / insult." Theophylact glosses the broad *σκανδαλίζω* by the narrower *ὑβρίζω* ("to insult, to affront"); the pairing recurs below in Lemma 9 (*ὑβρίζοντες καὶ σκανδαλίζοντες*, "those who insult and cause to stumble").
[^3]: This lemma is only the opening words of the citation with Theophylact's one-word gloss; the sentence runs on unbroken through Lemmata 8 and 9, which supply the remaining phrases of Matt. 18:6 and the exposition proper.
