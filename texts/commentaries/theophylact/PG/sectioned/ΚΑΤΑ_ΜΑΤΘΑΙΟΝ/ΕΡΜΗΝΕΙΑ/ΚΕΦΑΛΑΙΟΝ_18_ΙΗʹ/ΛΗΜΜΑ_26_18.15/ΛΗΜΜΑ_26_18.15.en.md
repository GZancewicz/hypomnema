# Matthew 18 (ΙΗʹ) — Lemma 26 · Matt. 18:32–33

> **Then his lord, having summoned him, says to him: Wicked servant, all that debt I forgave you, since you besought me; ought not you also (Matt. 18:32–33)**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture words, with no exposition of its own; the citation breaks off mid-sentence and is completed in the following lemma.*[^2]

[^1]: The lemma corresponds to Matt. 18:32 and the opening of 18:33. Greek *προσκαλεσάμενος* ("having called *him* to himself, summoned"); *ὀφειλή* ("debt, that which is owed"); *ἐπεὶ παρεκάλεσάς με* ("since you besought/entreated me"). The clause ends with *οὐκ ἔδει καὶ σὲ* — "ought not you also *to*…," the sense completed in Lemma 27.
[^2]: In the source the folder contains only the Gospel quotation, cut off at *σὲ* ("you"). Theophylact's comment on this saying is supplied under the next lemma.
