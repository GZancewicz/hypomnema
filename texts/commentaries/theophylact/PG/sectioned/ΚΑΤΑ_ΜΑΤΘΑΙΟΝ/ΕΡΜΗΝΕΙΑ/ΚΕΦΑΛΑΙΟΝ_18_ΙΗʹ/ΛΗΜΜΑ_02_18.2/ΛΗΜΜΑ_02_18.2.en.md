# Matthew 18 (ΙΗʹ) — Lemma 2 · Matt. 18:2

> **And Jesus, having called *to himself* *a* child, set it in *the* midst of them, and**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the Gospel quotation, with no exposition; the sentence breaks off at "and" and is continued in the next lemma (Matt. 18:3).*[^1]

[^1]: The Gospel quotation is split across files by the sectioning tool: the sentence runs on into Lemma 3, which resumes with «εἶπεν·» ("said:"). Greek *προσκαλεσάμενος* is middle voice, "having summoned/called to himself"; the supplied *to himself* renders that voice. *παιδίον* is anarthrous, hence the supplied *a*, and *ἐν μέσῳ* likewise supplies *the*.
