# Matthew 18 (ΙΗʹ) — Lemma 11 · Matt. 18:8

> **But if thy hand or thy foot cause thee to stumble, cut it off,[^1] and cast *it* from thee: it is good for thee to enter into life lame or maimed,[^2] than, having two hands or two feet, to be cast into the eternal fire; and if the …[^3] (Matt. 18:8)**

[^1]: The OCR reads *Κκκαίον*; it is securely recoverable as **«ἔκκοψον»** ("cut *it* off"), the reading of Matt. 18:8, which the exposition in Lemma 12 confirms (*ἐκκόπτειν*, "to cut off"). The pronoun *αὐτόν* is masculine, agreeing with *ὁ πούς* ("the foot").
[^2]: Greek *χωλὸν ἢ κυλλόν* — "lame or maimed": *χωλός* refers to a crippled/lame foot, *κυλλός* to a crooked or maimed hand, aptly answering to "hand or foot" just named.
[^3]: The citation breaks off mid-sentence at *καὶ εἰ ὁ* ("and if the …"); it is completed at the head of Lemma 12 with *ὀφθαλμός σου σκανδαλίζει σε* ("thine eye cause thee to stumble"). This lemma carries no exposition of its own; the comment on the whole passage follows in Lemma 12.
