# Matthew 18 (ΙΗʹ) — Lemma 27 · Matt. 18:33

> **…to have mercy on your fellow-servant, as I also had mercy on you? And (Matt. 18:33)**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the continuation of the scripture citation, with no exposition of its own; it completes the sentence begun in Lemma 26 and runs on into the next lemma.*[^2]

[^1]: Continuation of Matt. 18:33 (no «…» quotation marks in the source, since it resumes the citation of the preceding lemma). Greek *ἐλεῆσαι… ὡς καὶ ἐγώ σε ἠλέησα* — "to have mercy… as I also had mercy on you." The trailing *Καὶ* ("And") begins the next verse, taken up in Lemma 28.
[^2]: The folder contains only the Gospel words, with no comment by Theophylact.
