# Matthew 18 (ΙΗʹ) — Lemma 22 · Matt. 18:26

> **Therefore the servant, having fallen down, did obeisance to him, saying: Lord, be patient with me, and I will pay back to you all *things*.[^1] (Matt. 18:26)**

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation, with no exposition; the sentence breaks off and the comment resumes at the head of the next lemma, which continues with «σπλαγχνισθεὶς δὲ ὁ Κύριος…» ("And the Lord, being moved with compassion…").*

[^1]: Greek *μακροθύμησον ἐπ᾿ ἐμοί* — "be long-suffering / forbearing toward me." "Be patient with me" renders the plea; *μακροθυμέω* is literally "to be long-tempered," the opposite of quick anger. The plea recurs verbatim from the fellow-servant in Lemma 24, a repetition on which Theophylact's comment there turns.
