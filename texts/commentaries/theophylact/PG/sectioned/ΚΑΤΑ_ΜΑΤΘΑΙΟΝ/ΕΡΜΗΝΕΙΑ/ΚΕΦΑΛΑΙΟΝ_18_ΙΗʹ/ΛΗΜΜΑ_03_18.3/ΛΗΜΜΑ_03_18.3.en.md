# Matthew 18 (ΙΗʹ) — Lemma 3 · Matt. 18:3

> **said: Amen I say to you, unless you turn and become as the children, you shall by no means enter[^1] into the kingdom of the heavens;**[^2]

*This lemma carries only the Gospel quotation, continued from the previous lemma (Matt. 18:2, "…and") and having no exposition of its own.*[^2]

[^1]: Greek *οὐ μὴ εἰσέλθητε* — the double negative *οὐ μή* is emphatic, "shall by no means / shall certainly not enter."
[^2]: The sentence is the continuation of Lemma 2: the Gospel quotation, cut by the sectioning tool, resumes here with *εἶπεν* ("said") governing "Jesus" from the prior lemma.
