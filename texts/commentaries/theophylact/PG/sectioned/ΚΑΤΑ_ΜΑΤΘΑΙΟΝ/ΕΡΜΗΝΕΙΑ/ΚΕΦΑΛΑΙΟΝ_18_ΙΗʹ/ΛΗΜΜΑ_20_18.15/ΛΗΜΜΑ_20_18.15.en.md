# Matthew 18 (ΙΗʹ) — Lemma 20 · Matt. 18:24

> **And when he had begun to settle *accounts*, there was brought to him one debtor of ten thousand talents; and as he did not[^1] (Matt. 18:24)**

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation, with no exposition; the sentence breaks off mid-clause and is completed at the head of the next lemma, which continues «ἔχοντος δὲ αὐτοῦ ἀποδοῦναι» ("as he had not *wherewith* to pay").*[^1]

[^1]: The lemma ends on the negative *μὴ* ("not"), which governs the participle *ἔχοντος* opening Lemma 21: *μὴ ἔχοντος δὲ αὐτοῦ ἀποδοῦναι* = "but as he did not have *wherewith* to pay." Theophylact's editor has split a single scriptural sentence across the two lemma-units.
