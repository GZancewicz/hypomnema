# Matthew 18 (ΙΗʹ) — Lemma 6 · Matt. 18:6

> **should cause to stumble[^1] one of these little ones who believe in me, it is profitable for him that an ass-*driven* millstone[^2] be hung around his neck, and *that* he be sunk[^3] in the deep of the sea.**

Not only, he says, must you be humble-minded, but also, if you honor others *who are* humble-minded for my sake, you shall receive a reward; for you receive me whenever you receive the children — that is, the humble-minded. Then also, from the contrary,[^4] he says that[^5]

[^1]: Greek *σκανδαλίσῃ* — "cause to stumble, trip up, give offense to, make to fall *into sin*"; from *σκάνδαλον*, a snare or stumbling-block. "Cause to stumble" is kept as the literal image; "scandalize/offend" are the traditional renderings.
[^2]: Greek *μύλος ὀνικός* — literally "an ass's / donkey-*driven* millstone," the large upper millstone turned by a donkey rather than by hand; hence the traditional "great millstone." The supplied *driven* marks the sense of *ὀνικός* ("of/pertaining to an ass").
[^3]: Greek *καταποντισθῇ* — "be sunk / plunged / drowned in the sea"; *πέλαγος* is the open sea, the "deep." Supplied *that* and *he* carry the shared *ἵνα* construction into the second clause.
[^4]: Greek *ἀπὸ τοῦ ἐναντίου* — "from the opposite/contrary *side*"; having spoken of the reward for receiving the lowly, he now argues from the reverse case, the punishment for causing them to stumble.
[^5]: The exposition breaks off mid-sentence at *ὅτι* ("that"); it is completed in the next lemma.
