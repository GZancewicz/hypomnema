# Matthew 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 20 · Matt. 1:14

> ***Eliakim*** **begat Azor; and Azor begat Sadoc; and Sadoc begat Achim; and Achim begat**[^1]

*This lemma carries no exposition: it is a continuation of the genealogical list, the names alone, with no comment by Theophylact.*[^2]

[^1]: The lemma is a bare stretch of the genealogy. Its first verb — OCR *ἐγέννησε* — has no expressed subject here, because the subject stood at the end of the previous lemma («Ἐλιακεὶμ δὲ», "and Eliakim"); the supplied "*Eliakim*" is therefore italicized. The clause breaks off mid-verse at «Ἀχεὶμ δὲ ἐγέννησε τὸν» ("and Achim begat —"), the object (Eliud) being supplied at the head of the next lemma. Minor OCR slip: «ἐγόννησε» for *ἐγέννησε* in the Azor clause, securely corrected.
[^2]: The Greek file contains only the four genealogical clauses (Azor, Sadoc, Achim, and the start of the next name); Theophylact reserves his comment on the genealogy for the fuller lemmata (see Lemma 22 on Matt. 1:16 and Lemma 23 on Matt. 1:17).
