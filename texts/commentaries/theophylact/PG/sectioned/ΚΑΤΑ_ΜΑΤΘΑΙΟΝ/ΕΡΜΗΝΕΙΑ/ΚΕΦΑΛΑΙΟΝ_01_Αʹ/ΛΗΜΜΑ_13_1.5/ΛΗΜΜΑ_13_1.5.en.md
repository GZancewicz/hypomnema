# Matthew 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 13 · Matt. 1:5

> **And Booz begat Obed, of Ruth. (Matt. 1:5)**

This Ruth was of another race,[^1] but nevertheless she was joined to Booz; so also the Church from the nations,[^2] being of another race and outside the covenants, forgot her people[^3] and the idolatrous objects of worship and her father the devil. And she was taken in marriage to the Son of God.

And Obed begat …[^4]

[^1]: Greek *ἀλλόφυλος* ("of another tribe / foreign / of another race"), repeated below of the Church: an alien, a non-Israelite. Ruth the Moabitess (Ruth 1:4) is the type of the Gentile Church.
[^2]: Greek *ἡ ἐξ ἐθνῶν Ἐκκλησία* — "the Church from the nations (Gentiles)."
[^3]: Greek *ἐπελάθετο τοῦ λαοῦ αὐτῆς* — echoing Ps. 44:11 LXX (Ps. 45:10), "forget your people and your father's house." Theophylact extends the figure: the Gentile Church forgot her people, the "idolatrous objects of worship" (*τῶν εἰδωλικῶν σεβασμάτων*), and "her father the devil" (*τοῦ πατρὸς αὐτῆς τοῦ διαβόλου*).
[^4]: The final clause «Ὠβὴδ δὲ ἐγέννησε τὸν» ("And Obed begat …") breaks off mid-sentence and is completed in the next lemma with «Ἰεσσαί» ("Jesse," Matt. 1:5–6). Names in conventional English biblical forms (Booz, Obed, Ruth).
