# Matthew 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 24 · Matt. 1:18

> **Now of Jesus Christ the birth was thus. For, his mother Mary having been betrothed to Joseph—[^1] (Matt. 1:18)**

For what reason did God permit her to be betrothed, and altogether to give men the suspicion that Joseph had consorted with her?[^2] That she might have a guardian[^3] in her misfortunes; for he both, in the flight into Egypt, took care of her and preserved her. And at the same time also, that it might escape the notice of the devil. On this account she was betrothed; for the devil, having heard that the Virgin shall conceive,[^4] was keeping close watch upon the Virgin. Accordingly, that the deceiver might be deceived, Joseph betroths the Ever-Virgin;[^5] and there comes to be a form only of wedlock, but not the reality.

[^1]: The lemma breaks off mid-sentence at «τῷ Ἰωσήφ» ("to Joseph"); the sentence is completed in the next lemma («Πρὶν ἢ συνελθεῖν αὐτούς…»). The verse-opening OCR «Μίνος ἕνεκεν» at the start of the exposition is *Τίνος ἕνεκεν* ("For what reason"), securely restored.
[^2]: Greek *συνεγένετο αὐτῇ*, from *συγγίγνομαι* — a discreet idiom for "had (sexual) relations with, consorted with." Rendered "had consorted with"; the point is the suspicion of ordinary marital union.
[^3]: Greek *κηδεμών* — "guardian, protector, one who cares for." Cognate with *ἐπεμελήθη* ("took care of") just below.
[^4]: Greek «ἐν γαστρὶ ἕξει», literally "shall have in *the* womb," i.e. "shall be with child / shall conceive"; echoing Isa. 7:14 / Matt. 1:23. Rendered "shall conceive" for the plain sense.
[^5]: Greek *ἀειπάρθενος*, "Ever-Virgin," a fixed Marian title. Note the wordplay Theophylact builds: *ἵνα ἀπατηθῇ ὁ ἀπατεών* — "that the deceiver (*ἀπατεών*, the devil) might be deceived (*ἀπατηθῇ*)."
