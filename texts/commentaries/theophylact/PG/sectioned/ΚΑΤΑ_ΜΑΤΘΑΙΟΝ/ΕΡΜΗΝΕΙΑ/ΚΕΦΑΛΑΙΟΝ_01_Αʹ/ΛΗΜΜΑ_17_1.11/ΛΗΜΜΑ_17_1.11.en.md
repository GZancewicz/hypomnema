# Matthew 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 17 · Matt. 1:11

> **…Josiah;[^1] and Josiah begot Jechonias and his brethren, at the removal[^2] to Babylon. (Matt. 1:11)**

By "the removal to Babylon" he means the captivity which they later underwent, being led away to Babylon all together. For at another time also the Babylonians had made an expedition, but had afflicted them more moderately; but then they removed them completely from their homeland.

[^1]: The bare "Josiah" completes the closing "and Amon begot…" of the preceding lemma (Matt. 1:10); this lemma's comment concerns Josiah → Jechonias, Matt. 1:11.
[^2]: Greek *μετοικεσία* — literally a "change of dwelling, migration," hence "deportation, removal." The whole comment is an explanation of this word (Theophylact glosses it as *αἰχμαλωσία*, "captivity"); "removal" is kept as the plain, non-committal rendering, and Theophylact's own gloss "captivity" stands in the body. Older English Bibles render the phrase "the carrying away to Babylon."
