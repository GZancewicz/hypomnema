# Matthew 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 4 · Matt. 1:1

> **Of the son of David.** (Matt. 1:1)

Since he said "Jesus," in order that you might not suppose that he speaks concerning another Jesus, he added, "of the son of David." For there was also another Jesus, a renowned one, the one who was general after Moses;[^1] but that one was called *the son* of Nave [i.e. Nun],[^2] and not of David; for he was before David by many generations, and he was not even from the tribe of Judah — from which David was descended — but from another.

[^1]: That is, Joshua, whose name in Greek is likewise *Ἰησοῦς* ("Jesus"); he led Israel (Greek *στρατηγήσας*, "having served as general / commanded the army") after the death of Moses. The whole point depends on the two men sharing the one Greek name.
[^2]: Greek *τοῦ Ναυῆ* — the Septuagint form *Ἰησοῦς [υἱὸς] Ναυή* (Joshua "son of Nun"); "Nave" transliterates the Greek *Ναυή*.
