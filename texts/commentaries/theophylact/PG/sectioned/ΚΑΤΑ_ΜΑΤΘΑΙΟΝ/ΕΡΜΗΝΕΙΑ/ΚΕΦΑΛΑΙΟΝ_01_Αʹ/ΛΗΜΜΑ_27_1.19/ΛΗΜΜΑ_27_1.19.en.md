# Matthew 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 27 · Matt. 1:18b (within the comment on 1:19)

> **She was found having *a child* in *the* womb.[^1] (Matt. 1:18)**

By whom was she found? By Joseph — that is, it was ascertained that she had conceived from *the* Holy Spirit; whence he wished to release her secretly, as no longer daring to have as wife her who had been deemed worthy of so great a gift.[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Εὑρέθη ἐν γαστρὶ ἔχουσα» — the words belong to Matt. 1:18b, here re-quoted in the course of expounding 1:19 (the folder's draws-from verse). Theophylact returns to "was found" in order to ask *τίνι εὑρέθη;* ("found by whom?") and to anchor Joseph's resolve to release her in his recognition of the miraculous conception. *ἐν γαστρὶ ἔχουσα* is literally "having in *the* belly/womb"; the object ("*a child*") is supplied, as the Greek idiom carries it.
[^2]: Greek *χαρίσματος*, "gift, grace, favor" (a *charism*); the sense is the grace of bearing the Christ. Joseph refrains not from suspicion but from reverence.
