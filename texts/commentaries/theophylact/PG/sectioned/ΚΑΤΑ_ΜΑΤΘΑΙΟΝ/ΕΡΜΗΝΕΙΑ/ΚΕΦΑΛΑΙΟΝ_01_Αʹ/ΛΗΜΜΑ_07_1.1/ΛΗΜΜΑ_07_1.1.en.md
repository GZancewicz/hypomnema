# Matthew 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 7 · Matt. 1:2

> **And Isaac begat Jacob; and Jacob begat Judah and his brothers.**[^1] (Matt. 1:2)

*The exposition of this lemma is not contained in the lemma-file, which carries only the scriptural quotation; Theophylact's comment on it continues in the following lemma* (Lemma 8: "Do you see that for this reason he made mention of Judah and his brothers, because from them were the twelve tribes").[^2]

[^1]: The folder metadata assigns this lemma to Matt. 1:1, but the Greek quotes Matt. 1:2 («Ἰσαὰκ δὲ ἐγέννησε τὸν Ἰακώβ· Ἰακὼβ δὲ ἐγέννησε τὸν Ἰούδαν καὶ τοὺς ἀδελφοὺς αὐτοῦ»); the header verse is corrected accordingly. OCR corruptions in the source (*Ἰσαλν* for *Ἰσαὰκ δὲ*, *Ἰακὼδ* for *Ἰακὼβ*) are securely recoverable and low-stakes.
[^2]: Per convention 3: where a lemma-file carries only the Gospel quotation and the exposition runs on into the next lemma, the quotation is rendered here and the continuation is noted rather than duplicated.
