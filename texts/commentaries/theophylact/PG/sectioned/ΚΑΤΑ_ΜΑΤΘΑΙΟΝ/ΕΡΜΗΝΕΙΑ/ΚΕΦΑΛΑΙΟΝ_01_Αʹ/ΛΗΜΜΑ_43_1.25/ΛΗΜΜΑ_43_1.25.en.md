# Matthew 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 43 · Matt. 1:25

> **The raven returned not into the ark, until the earth was dried up; (Gen. 8:7)**[^1]

for neither did it return afterward either. And again:[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Οὐκ ἐπέστρεψεν ὁ κόραξ εἰς τὴν κιβωτόν, ἕως οὗ ἐξηράνθη ἡ γῆ» — Theophylact's proof-text for the idiomatic "until," alluding to the raven of Noah's ark (Gen. 8:7). The LXX there reads that the raven "went out and did not return" (*οὐκ ἀνέστρεψεν*) until the water dried up; Theophylact's wording (*οὐκ ἐπέστρεψεν* … *ἕως οὗ ἐξηράνθη ἡ γῆ*) is a loose citation shaped to his point. Translated as he quotes it, not conformed to the LXX or any English Bible.
[^2]: The lemma breaks off at «καὶ πάλιν» ("and again:"), introducing the next proof-text; it is supplied in the following lemma as «Μεθ᾽ ὑμῶν εἰμι ἕως τῆς συντελείας τοῦ αἰῶνος» ("I am with you until the consummation of the age" — Matt. 28:20).
