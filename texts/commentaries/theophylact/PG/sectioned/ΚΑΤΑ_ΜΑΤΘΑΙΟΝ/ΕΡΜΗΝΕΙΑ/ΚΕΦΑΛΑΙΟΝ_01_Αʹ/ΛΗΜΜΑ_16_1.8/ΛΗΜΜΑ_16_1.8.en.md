# Matthew 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 16 · Matt. 1:8–10

> **…*and Abia*[^1] begot Asa; and Asa begot Josaphat; and Josaphat begot Joram; and Joram begot Ozias;[^2] and Ozias begot Joatham; and Joatham begot Achaz; and Achaz begot Ezekias; and Ezekias[^3] begot Manasses; and Manasses begot Amon; and Amon begot… (Matt. 1:8–10)**

[^1]: The subject "*and Abia*" (*Ἀβιὰ δέ*) stands at the end of the preceding lemma; here the Greek opens bare with *ἐγέννησε τὸν Ἀσά* ("begot Asa"), so the subject is supplied in italics.
[^2]: Between Joram and Ozias (Uzziah) Matthew's genealogy — which Theophylact quotes as it stands — omits three kings (Ahaziah, Joash, and Amaziah). The omission is a genuine feature of the received Matthean text, not an OCR loss, and is left uncorrected here per the rule of following the text on the page. *Ὁζίας* is given in its Matthean form "Ozias" rather than the Old-Testament "Uzziah."
[^3]: The OCR runs two words together as *ἘζεκίανἘςεκίας*; this is securely *Ἐζεκίαν· Ἐζεκίας* ("Ezekias; and Ezekias"), the boundary between the object of one clause and the subject of the next. Likewise *Ὁςίας* = **Ὁζίας**. Names given in Matthean forms (Ezekias, Manasses) rather than Hebrew (Hezekiah, Manasseh).
