# Matthew 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 21 · Matt. 1:15

> **…*begat* Eliud; and Eliud begat Eleazar; and Eleazar begat Matthan; and Matthan begat**[^1]

*This lemma carries no exposition: it is a continuation of the genealogical list, the names alone, with no comment by Theophylact.*[^2]

[^1]: A bare stretch of the genealogy. The opening word «Ἐλιούδ» ("Eliud") is the object of the verb left dangling at the end of the previous lemma («Ἀχεὶμ δὲ ἐγέννησε τὸν», "and Achim begat —"); hence the supplied "*begat*." The clause breaks off again mid-verse at «Ματθὰν δὲ ἐγέννησε τὸν» ("and Matthan begat —"), the object (Jacob) being supplied at the head of the next lemma. Minor OCR slip: «ἐγέννῃσα» for *ἐγέννησε* in the Matthan clause, securely corrected.
[^2]: The Greek file contains only the genealogical clauses (Eliud, Eleazar, Matthan, and the start of the next name); Theophylact reserves his comment on the genealogy for the fuller lemmata (see Lemma 22 on Matt. 1:16 and Lemma 23 on Matt. 1:17).
