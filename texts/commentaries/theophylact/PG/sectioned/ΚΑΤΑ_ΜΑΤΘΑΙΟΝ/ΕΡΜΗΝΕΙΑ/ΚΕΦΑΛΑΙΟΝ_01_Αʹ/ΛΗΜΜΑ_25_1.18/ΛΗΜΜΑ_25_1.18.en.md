# Matthew 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 25 · Matt. 1:18

> **…before they came together, she was found having in *the* womb from *the* Holy Spirit. (Matt. 1:18)**[^1]

The "coming together" here signifies the mingling;[^2] for before being mingled, she conceived. Whence the evangelist also is astonished, and cries out, "She was found" — as speaking about something marvellous.[^3]

[^1]: This lemma completes the sentence begun in the previous one (Lemma 24: "Now of Jesus Christ the birth was thus. For, his mother Mary having been betrothed to Joseph—"). Greek «εὑρέθη ἐν γαστρὶ ἔχουσα ἐκ Πνεύματος ἁγίου», literally "she was found having in *the* womb from *the* Holy Spirit," i.e. "found to be with child by the Holy Spirit." The anarthrous «Πνεύματος ἁγίου» takes supplied "*the*."
[^2]: Greek *μίξις* (verb *μιγῆναι*) — "mingling," a common euphemism for sexual union / intercourse. Theophylact glosses the verse's decorous *συνελθεῖν* ("to come together") by the plainer *μίξις*; rendered "mingling" to preserve his own choice of word.
[^3]: Greek *παράδοξον* — "paradoxical, contrary to expectation, marvellous, strange." The evangelist's very verb «εὑρέθη» ("she was found") is taken as a note of astonishment, as one reports an unlooked-for wonder. Rendered "marvellous"; "paradoxical / extraordinary" would serve equally.
