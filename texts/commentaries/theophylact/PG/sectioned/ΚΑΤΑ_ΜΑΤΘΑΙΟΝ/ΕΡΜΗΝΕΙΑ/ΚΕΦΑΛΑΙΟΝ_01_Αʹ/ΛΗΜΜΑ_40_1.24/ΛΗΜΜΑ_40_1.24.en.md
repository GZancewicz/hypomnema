# Matthew 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 40 · Matt. 1:24

> **But Joseph, having been awakened from his sleep, did as the angel of *the* Lord had commanded him.**[^1]

See a soul[^2] awakened, how quickly it was persuaded.

[^1]: Greek «Διεγερθεὶς δὲ ὁ Ἰωσὴφ ἀπὸ τοῦ ὕπνου, ἐποίησεν ὡς προσέταξεν αὐτῷ ὁ ἄγγελος Κυρίου» (Matt. 1:24). *Κυρίου* is anarthrous; supplied "*the*" italicized.
[^2]: Greek OCR *ψυχίν*, securely = *ψυχήν* ("soul"). Theophylact plays on the participle: Joseph, literally "awakened" (*διεγερθείς*) from sleep, is an image of a soul "awakened" (*διεγηγερμένην*) and hence swift to obey.
