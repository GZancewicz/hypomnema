# Matthew 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 8 · Matt. 1:2

Do you see that for this reason he made mention of Judah and his brothers — because from them were the twelve tribes?[^1]

[^1]: This lemma carries no scripture quotation at its head; it is Theophylact's exposition, expounding the preceding genealogical clause «Ἰακὼβ δὲ ἐγέννησε τὸν Ἰούδαν καὶ τοὺς ἀδελφοὺς αὐτοῦ» ("and Jacob begat Judah and his brothers," Matt. 1:2). The source text encloses the sentence in guillemets — «Ὁρᾷς ὅτι διὰ τοῦτο ἐμνήσθη τοῦ Ἰούδα καὶ τῶν ἀδελφῶν αὐτοῦ διότι ἀπ´ αὐτῶν ἦσαν αἱ δώδεκα φυλαί» (the source OCR reads *δώθεκα* for *δώδεκα*, "twelve") — but it is commentary, not a Gospel lemma, and is rendered here as prose. The folder-name verse (1.1) is corrected to 1:2, which is what the sentence actually expounds. *Ὁρᾷς* ("do you see") is Theophylact's habitual rhetorical address to the reader; *ἐμνήσθη* = "he made mention of / remembered," of the Evangelist. The point: the twelve tribes descend from Judah and his brothers (the sons of Jacob), which is why they are named together.
