# Matthew 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 29 · Matt. 1:20

> **Joseph, son of David. (Matt. 1:20)**

He called him son of David, reminding him that from the seed of David the Christ was prophesied to be;[^1] and by saying such things *he means* this: "Do not disbelieve, *now that you have* brought to mind David, who received *the* promise concerning the Christ."[^2]

[^1]: Greek *προεφητεύετο ὁ Χριστὸς ἔσεσθαι* (OCR *ἱσεσθαι* = *ἔσεσθαι*), "the Christ was being prophesied to be *from David's seed*"; the imperfect keeps the sense of a standing, ongoing prophecy.
[^2]: The Greek is compressed: *καὶ τοιαῦτα λέγων, ὅτι, μὴ ἀπίστει, ἐνθυμηθεὶς τοῦ Δαυίδ, ὃς ἐπαγγελίαν ἐδέξατο περὶ τοῦ Χριστοῦ.* The *ὅτι* introduces the angel's implied speech to Joseph; *ἐνθυμηθεὶς* is an aorist participle ("having called to mind / when you have recalled"). Rendered as reported exhortation to keep the addressee (Joseph) clear.
