# Matthew 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 18 · Matt. 1:12

> **And after the removal[^1] to Babylon, Jechonias begot Salathiel; and Salathiel begot… (Matt. 1:12)**

[^1]: Greek *μετοικεσία*, "removal / deportation" — the same term glossed by Theophylact in the previous lemma as "captivity"; see the note there. Names given in their Matthean forms (Jechonias, Salathiel) rather than the Hebrew (Jeconiah, Shealtiel).
