# Matthew 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 37 · Matt. 1:22–23 (Isa. 7:14)

> **Therefore the Lord himself shall give you a sign; (Isa. 7:14a)**[^1]

and, *he* straightway adding:[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Διὰ τοῦτο δώσει Κύριος αὐτὸς ὑμῖν σημεῖον». These are the words of Isaiah (Isa. 7:14a), which Theophylact quotes in fuller form than Matthew's own citation; they belong to his exposition of Matt. 1:22–23.
[^2]: Greek *καὶ εὐθὺς ἐπάγοντος* — a genitive-absolute participle continuing "hear Isaiah… saying… and straightway adding" from the previous lemma. The sentence breaks off mid-clause and is completed in the next lemma, which supplies «Ἰδοὺ ἡ Παρθένος» ("Behold, the Virgin…").
