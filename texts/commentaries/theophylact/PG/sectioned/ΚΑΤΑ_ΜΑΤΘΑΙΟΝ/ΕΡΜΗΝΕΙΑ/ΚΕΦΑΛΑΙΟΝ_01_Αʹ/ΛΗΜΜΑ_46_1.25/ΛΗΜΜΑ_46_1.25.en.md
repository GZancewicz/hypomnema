# Matthew 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 46 · Matt. 1:25

> **And he called his name Jesus. (Matt. 1:25)**[^1]

He shows here too the ready obedience of Joseph, that whatsoever the angel said to him, he did.[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Καὶ ἐκάλεσε τὸ ὄνομα αὐτοῦ, Ἰησοῦν», the close of Matt. 1:25.
[^2]: Greek *εὐπείθεια* — "ready obedience, willing compliance, docility"; rendered "ready obedience" to keep the note of glad, prompt submission. *κἀνταῦθα* (= *καὶ ἐνταῦθα*) "here too / also here" points back to the earlier note on Joseph's obedience (Lemma 41, on his taking Mary as directed).
