# Matthew 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 45 · Matt. 1:25

> **Her son, the firstborn. (Matt. 1:25)**[^1]

Not as though some second *child* had been born does he call this one "firstborn," but simply, as *one* born first and alone.[^2] For Christ[^3] is both firstborn, as *being* born first; and only-begotten, as not having a second brother.

[^1]: Greek «Τὸν υἱὸν αὐτῆς τὸν πρωτότοκον», from Matt. 1:25. On the term *πρωτότοκος* ("firstborn") Theophylact anticipates the inference — a "firstborn" implies a later-born — and denies it.
[^2]: Greek *ὡς πρῶτον τεχθέντα καὶ μόνον* — "as *one* born first and only/alone." The participle is OCR-garbled in the source as «τενθέντα»; securely = *τεχθέντα* ("born," aor. pass. ptcp. of *τίκτω*), matching *δευτέρου τινὸς τεχθέντος* just above (there OCR'd correctly). *μόνον* ("alone / only") looks ahead to *μονογενής* in the next clause.
[^3]: The source reads «Ὃ γὰρ Χριστὸς»; the relative/neuter *Ὃ* is an OCR slip for the article *Ὁ* — "For Christ *is* both firstborn… and only-begotten." Also in the lemma, «ποωτότοκον» is an OCR corruption of *πρωτότοκον*.
