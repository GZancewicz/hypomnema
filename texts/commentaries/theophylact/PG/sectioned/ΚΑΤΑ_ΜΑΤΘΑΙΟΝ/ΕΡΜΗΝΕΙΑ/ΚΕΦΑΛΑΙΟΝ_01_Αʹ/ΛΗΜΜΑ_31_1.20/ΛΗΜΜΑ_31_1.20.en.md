# Matthew 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 31 · Matt. 1:20

> **Mary your wife.[^1] (Matt. 1:20)**

This he says: "You perhaps think that she is an adulteress; but I tell you that she is your wife." That is, she has not been corrupted by anyone, but is your betrothed.[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Μαριὰμ τὴν γυναῖκά σου». Greek *γυνή* means both "wife" and "woman"; the angel's phrase, and Theophylact's gloss, press the sense "wife" — as against Joseph's suspicion of "adulteress" — so "your wife" is kept. The proper name is given in its conventional English form, Mary (Greek *Μαριάμ*).
[^2]: Greek *σή ἐστι μνηστή*, "she is your betrothed / espoused *one*." Theophylact resolves the seeming tension — calling her both "wife" and "betrothed" — by explaining "wife" as: undefiled, and belonging to you as your espoused.
