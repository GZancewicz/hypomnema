# Matthew 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 32 · Matt. 1:20

> **For that which is begotten in her is of *the* Holy Spirit. (Matt. 1:20)**[^1]

For not only has she been released from unlawful union, but she also conceived in accordance with a certain more divine manner. And so, rejoice for this reason all the more.[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Τὸ γὰρ ἐν αὐτῇ γεννηθὲν, ἐκ Πνεύματός ἐστιν ἁγίου». The participle *γεννηθέν* is "that which has been begotten/born," not "conceived" (the more familiar English-Bible wording); rendered "begotten" to keep Theophylact's own word. *Πνεύματος ἁγίου* is anarthrous, so the article "*the*" is supplied and italicized.
[^2]: Greek *ὥστε καὶ χαῖρε διὰ τοῦτο μᾶλλον*. The verb *ἀπήλλακται* ("has been released/kept free") is a perfect passive with subject unexpressed (Mary); *she* is supplied. *χαῖρε* is a singular imperative addressed to Joseph; *μᾶλλον* ("more/rather") is here "all the more."
