# Matthew 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 33 · Matt. 1:21

> **And she shall bear a son. (Matt. 1:21)**[^1]

In order that no one might say, "And whence am I to believe thee, that it is of *the* Spirit?" — he speaks concerning the future, that she shall bear a son. For if he shall prove true concerning this,[^2] *it is* quite evident that also the "*it is* of *the* Holy Spirit" is true. But he did not say, "she shall bear *a son* to thee," but simply, "she shall bear"; for she was not bringing forth for him, but for the whole inhabited world;[^3] nor was the grace confined to him alone, but was poured out upon all.

[^1]: Greek «Τέξεται δὲ υἱόν» (OCR «Πέξεται»); *τέξεται* is "she shall bear/bring forth." The particle *δὲ* is rendered "and."
[^2]: Greek OCR *ἀληθεύσω*; sense requires 3rd-person *ἀληθεύσῃ* ("if he — the angel — proves truthful"), the subject being the one now foretelling the birth. The verb *ἀληθεύω* is "to speak the truth, prove true." Translated per the sense; a literal first-person "if I prove true" does not fit the narrative.
[^3]: Greek *οὐ γὰρ ἐκείνῳ ἔτικτεν* (OCR *Κτικτεν*), imperfect "she was not bearing for him." The following verb *περιῖστατο* (from *περιΐσταμαι*) is rendered "was confined to"; more literally "did the grace stand around / devolve upon him alone."
