# Matthew 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 11 · Matt. 1:3–4

> **… *Esrom* begat Aram; and Aram begat Aminadab; and Aminadab begat Naasson; and Naasson begat** …[^1]

[^1]: A continuation of the genealogy with no exposition. The clause begins mid-sentence — its subject, *Esrom*, is carried over from the preceding lemma (supplied here in italics) — and breaks off again at "and Naasson begat …," completed in the next lemma with «Σαλμών» ("Salmon," Matt. 1:4). Greek: «ἐγέννησε τὸν Ἀράμ· Ἀρὰμ δὲ ἐγέννησε τὸν Ἀμιναδάβ· Ἀμιναδὰβ δὲ ἐγέννησε τὸν Ναασών· Ναασὼν δὲ ἐγέννησε τὸν». Proper names in their conventional English biblical forms.
