# Matthew 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 34 · Matt. 1:21

> **And thou shalt call his name Jesus. (Matt. 1:21)**

Thou shalt call *him*, as though a father, and as guardian of the Virgin; for do not suppose, Joseph,[^1] that, since the conception is from *the* Spirit, thou wilt leave the Virgin unaided. But thou shalt render service in all things.

[^1]: The vocative is OCR-garbled as «Ἐσὴφ»; securely = *Ἰωσήφ* ("Joseph"). Greek *ὡς δῆθεν πατήρ* — "as though a father" (*δῆθεν* marking the ostensible or apparent role, since Joseph is not the natural father). *ὑπολάμβανε* is a singular imperative, "do not suppose."
