# Matthew 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 15 · Matt. 1:7

> **And Solomon begot Roboam; and Roboam begot Abia; and Abia[^1]… (Matt. 1:7)**

*This lemma carries only the scripture text and no exposition: it is a portion of the genealogical list, which the sectioning has cut off mid-clause. The comment resumes at the "removal to Babylon" (Lemma 17).*

[^1]: The Greek is legible but OCR-corrupted at several names, all securely recoverable from the genealogy: *δολομῶν* = **Σολομῶν** (Solomon), *Ἀγέννησε* = **ἐγέννησε** (begot), *Ἀθιὰ* = **Ἀβιά** (Abia). The clause breaks off at "and Abia," whose verb and object are supplied at the head of the next lemma ("*and Abia* begot Asa"). The names are given in their conventional New-Testament (Matthean) forms — Roboam, Abia — rather than the Old-Testament Hebrew forms (Rehoboam, Abijah), since this is Matthew's own genealogy.
