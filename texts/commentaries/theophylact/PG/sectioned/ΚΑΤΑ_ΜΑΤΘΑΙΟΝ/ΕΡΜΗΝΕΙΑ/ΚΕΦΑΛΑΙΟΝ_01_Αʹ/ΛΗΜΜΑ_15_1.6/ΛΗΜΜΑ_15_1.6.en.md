# Matthew 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 15 · Matt. 1:7-10

> **And Solomon begot Roboam; and Roboam begot Abia; and Abia begot Asa; and Asa begot Josaphat; and Josaphat begot Joram; and Joram begot Ozias; and Ozias begot Joatham; and Joatham begot Achaz; and Achaz begot Ezekias; and Ezekias begot Manasses; and Manasses begot Amon; and Amon begot…[^1] (Matt. 1:7-10)**

[^1]: This stretch of the genealogy carries no exposition and is read continuously with the following comment-less fragment (Lemma 16). The Greek of this fragment is OCR-corrupted at several names, all securely recoverable: *δολομῶν* = **Σολομῶν** (Solomon), *Ἀγέννησε* = **ἐγέννησε** (begot), *Ἀθιὰ* = **Ἀβιά** (Abia). The names are given in their conventional Matthean forms (Roboam, Abia, Ozias, Ezekias) rather than the Old-Testament Hebrew forms (Rehoboam, Abijah, Uzziah, Hezekiah), since this is Matthew's own genealogy. Between Joram and Ozias, Matthew's list omits three kings (Ahaziah, Joash, Amaziah), a genuine feature of the received text left uncorrected here per the rule of following the text on the page.
