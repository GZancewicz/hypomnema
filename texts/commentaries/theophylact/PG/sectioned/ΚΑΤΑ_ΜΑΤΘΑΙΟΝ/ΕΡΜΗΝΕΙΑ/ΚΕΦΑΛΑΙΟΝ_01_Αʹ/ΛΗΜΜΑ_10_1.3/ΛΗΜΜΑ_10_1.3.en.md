# Matthew 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 10 · Matt. 1:3

> **And Phares begat Esrom; and Esrom** …[^1]

[^1]: A bare stretch of the genealogy with no exposition. The sentence breaks off mid-clause and is completed in the next lemma, which continues «… ἐγέννησε τὸν Ἀράμ» ("[Esrom] begat Aram," Matt. 1:3). Greek: «Φαρὲς δὲ ἐγέννησε τὸν Ἐσρώμ· Ἐσρὼμ δὲ». Proper names given in their conventional English biblical forms (Phares, Esrom).
