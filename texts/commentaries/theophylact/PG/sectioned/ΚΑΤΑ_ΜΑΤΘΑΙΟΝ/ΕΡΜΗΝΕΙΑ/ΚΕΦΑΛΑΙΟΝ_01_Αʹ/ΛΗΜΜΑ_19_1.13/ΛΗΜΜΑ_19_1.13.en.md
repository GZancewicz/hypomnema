# Matthew 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 19 · Matt. 1:13-15

> **…Zorobabel; and Zorobabel begat Abiud; and Abiud begat Eliakim; and Eliakim begat Azor; and Azor begat Sadoc; and Sadoc begat Achim; and Achim begat Eliud; and Eliud begat Eleazar; and Eleazar begat Matthan; and Matthan begat… (Matt. 1:13-15)**[^1]

[^1]: The bare "Zorobabel" completes the "and Salathiel begat…" of the preceding lemma (Matt. 1:12). Names given in their Matthean forms (Zorobabel, Abiud, Eliakim) rather than the Hebrew (Zerubbabel). Minor OCR slips corrected: «ἐγόννησε» for *ἐγέννησε* (Azor clause), «ἐγέννῃσα» for *ἐγέννησε* (Matthan clause). The reading breaks off at "and Matthan begat —," the object (Jacob) standing at the head of Lemma 22 (Matt. 1:16).
