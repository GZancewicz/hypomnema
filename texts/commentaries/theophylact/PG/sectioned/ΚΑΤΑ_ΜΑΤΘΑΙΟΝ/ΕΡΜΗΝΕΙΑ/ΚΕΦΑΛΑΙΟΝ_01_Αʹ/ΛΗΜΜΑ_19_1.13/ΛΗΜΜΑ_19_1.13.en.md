# Matthew 1 (Αʹ) — Lemma 19 · Matt. 1:13

> **…Zorobabel;[^1] and Zorobabel begot Abiud; and Abiud begot Eliakim; and Eliakim… (Matt. 1:13)**

*This lemma carries only the scripture text and no exposition: it continues the genealogy, cut off at both ends by the sectioning. The trailing "and Eliakim…" is completed at the head of the next lemma ("…begot Azor").*

[^1]: The bare "Zorobabel" completes the "and Salathiel begot…" of the preceding lemma (Matt. 1:12). Names given in their Matthean forms (Zorobabel, Abiud, Eliakim) rather than the Hebrew (Zerubbabel). The clause breaks off at "and Eliakim," whose verb and object stand at the head of the next lemma.
