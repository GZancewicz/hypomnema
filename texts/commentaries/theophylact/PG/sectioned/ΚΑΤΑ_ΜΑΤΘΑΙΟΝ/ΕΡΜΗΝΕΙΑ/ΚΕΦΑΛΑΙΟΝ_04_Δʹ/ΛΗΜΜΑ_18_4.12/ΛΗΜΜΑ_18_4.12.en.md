# Matthew 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 18 · Matt. 4:12

> **But Jesus, having heard that John was delivered up, withdrew into Galilee; and[^1] (Matt. 4:12)**

[^1]: Greek *Ἀκούσας δὲ ὁ Ἰησοῦς ὅτι Ἰωάννης παρεδόθη, ἀνεχώρησεν εἰς τὴν Γαλιλαίαν* — "But Jesus, having heard that John was delivered up, withdrew into Galilee." *παρεδόθη* ("was delivered up, handed over, betrayed"), i.e. John's arrest/imprisonment; the participle *ἀκούσας* ("having heard") is kept. OCR *ἀνενώρησεν* = *ἀνεχώρησεν* ("withdrew, departed"). The lemma breaks off at *καὶ* ("and"); the sentence is completed and expounded in the next lemma (4:13).
