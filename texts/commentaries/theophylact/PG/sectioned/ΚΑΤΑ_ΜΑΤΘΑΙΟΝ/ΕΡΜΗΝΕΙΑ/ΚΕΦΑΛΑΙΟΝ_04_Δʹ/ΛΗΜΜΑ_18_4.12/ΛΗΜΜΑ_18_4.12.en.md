# Matthew 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 18 · Matt. 4:12

> **But Jesus, having heard that John was delivered up, withdrew into Galilee; and[^1] (Matt. 4:12)**

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation, with no exposition of its own.*[^2]

[^1]: Greek *Ἀκούσας δὲ ὁ Ἰησοῦς ὅτι Ἰωάννης παρεδόθη, ἀνεχώρησεν εἰς τὴν Γαλιλαίαν* — "But Jesus, having heard that John was delivered up, withdrew into Galilee." *παρεδόθη* ("was delivered up, handed over, betrayed"), i.e. John's arrest/imprisonment; the participle *ἀκούσας* ("having heard") is kept. OCR *ἀνενώρησεν* = *ἀνεχώρησεν* ("withdrew, departed").
[^2]: The lemma breaks off at *καὶ* ("and"); the exposition begins in the next lemma (4:13), which completes the sentence ("…having left Nazareth, coming he dwelt in Capernaum…") and expounds it. Theophylact's comment there turns on *ἀναχωρεῖ* ("he withdraws"): Jesus withdraws to teach us not to throw ourselves into dangers.
