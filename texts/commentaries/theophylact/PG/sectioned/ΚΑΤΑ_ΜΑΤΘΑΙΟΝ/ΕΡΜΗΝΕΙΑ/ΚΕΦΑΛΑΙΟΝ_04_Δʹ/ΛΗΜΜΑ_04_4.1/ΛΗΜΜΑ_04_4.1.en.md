# Matthew 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 4 · Matt. 4:2

> **Forty days and forty nights. (Matt. 4:2)**[^1]

He fasts as many *days* as Moses also and Elijah *did*;[^2] for if more, the incarnation would have seemed *to be* an appearance.[^3]

[^1]: The folder-name verse (4.1) is metadata; these words continue Matt. 4:2, so the reference is corrected to 4:2.
[^2]: The forty-day fasts of Moses (Exod. 34:28; Deut. 9:9) and Elijah (1 Kings 19:8). "As many *days* as": the noun is supplied from the lemma's *ἡμέρας τεσσαράκοντα*.
[^3]: Greek *ἔδοξεν ἂν φαντασία ἡ σάρκωσις* — "the incarnation (enfleshment) would have seemed *to be* an appearance / a phantom." *φαντασία* here carries the technical, anti-Docetic sense: had Christ fasted beyond human endurance, his taking-on of flesh (*σάρκωσις*) would have looked like mere semblance rather than real bodily existence. The same word-family (*φαντασία*, "appearance to the imagination") recurs across Theophylact for illusory, non-real seeming.
