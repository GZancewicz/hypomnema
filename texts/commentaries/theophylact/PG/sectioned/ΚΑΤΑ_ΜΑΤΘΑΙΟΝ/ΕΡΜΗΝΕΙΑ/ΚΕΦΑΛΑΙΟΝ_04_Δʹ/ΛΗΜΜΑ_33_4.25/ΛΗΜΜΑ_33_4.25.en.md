# Matthew 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 33 · Matt. 4:25

> **And there followed him great crowds from Galilee, and Decapolis, and Jerusalem, and Judea, and beyond the Jordan. (Matt. 4:25)**[^1]

[^1]: The lemma's opening «Καὶ» is already anticipated at the close of the previous lemma (32), where the OCR runs on into "Καὶ" before breaking; here the full verse is given. *Δεκάπολις* ("Decapolis," the "Ten Cities") and *πέραν τοῦ Ἰορδάνου* ("beyond the Jordan") are rendered in their conventional forms.
