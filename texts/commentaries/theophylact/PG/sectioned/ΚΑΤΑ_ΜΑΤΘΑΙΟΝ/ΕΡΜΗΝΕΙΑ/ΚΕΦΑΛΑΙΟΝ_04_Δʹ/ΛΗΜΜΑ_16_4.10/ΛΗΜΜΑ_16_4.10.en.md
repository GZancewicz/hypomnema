# Matthew 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 16 · Matt. 4:10

> **"All these things I will give thee," (Matt. 4:9)**

— as *though they were* his own.[^1] But learn how the Scriptures are of benefit; for the Lord, from them, stopped the mouth of the enemy.[^2]

[^1]: Greek *ὡς ἴδια* ("as his own things"). This clause completes the sentence broken off at the end of the previous lemma ("…and saying:"): the devil's boast, "All these things I will give thee," is uttered as if the world were his own possession. The quoted words belong to Matt. 4:9, here recalled within Theophylact's comment on 4:10; "*though they were*" is supplied.
[^2]: Greek *ἐπεστόμισε τὸν ἐχθρόν* ("muzzled / stopped the mouth of the enemy"), from *ἐπιστομίζω* ("to put something to the mouth, to gag, silence"); the point is that Christ silenced the devil by citing Scripture (Deut. 6:13). OCR *ἐπεστέμισε* = *ἐπεστόμισε*; *ὠφελοῦσιν* ("are of benefit / profit").
