# Matthew 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 25 · Matt. 4:19

> **"Come after me, and I will make you fishers of men."[^1] But they,[^2] (Matt. 4:19–20)**

[^1]: Greek *δεῦτε ὀπίσω μου, καὶ ποιήσω ὑμᾶς ἁλιεῖς ἀνθρώπων.* The adverbial imperative *δεῦτε* ("come!", plural) is rendered "Come"; *ὀπίσω μου*, "after me." *ἁλιεῖς ἀνθρώπων*, "fishers of men," picks up the sense of the same word applied to the brothers' trade in the preceding lemma.
[^2]: Greek *Οἱ δὲ* — "But they," the *δέ* rendered "but." The sentence is left unfinished at the end of this lemma and resumed at the head of Lemma 26.
