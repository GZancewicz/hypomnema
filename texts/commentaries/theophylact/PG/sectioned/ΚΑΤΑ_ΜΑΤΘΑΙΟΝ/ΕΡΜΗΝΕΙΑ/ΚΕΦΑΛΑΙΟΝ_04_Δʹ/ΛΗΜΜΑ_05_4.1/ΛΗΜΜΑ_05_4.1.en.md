# Matthew 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 5 · Matt. 4:2

> **Afterward he hungered. (Matt. 4:2)**[^1]

When he gave way to *his* nature, then also he hungered, so that he might give occasion to the devil to approach and to grapple with him through the hunger, and thus he might make him topple and cast *him* down, and might bestow the victory upon us —[^2]

[^1]: The folder-name verse (4.1) is metadata; *ὕστερον ἐπείνασεν* ("afterward he hungered") closes Matt. 4:2, so the reference is corrected to 4:2. *συνεχώρησε τῇ φύσει* — "he gave way to / conceded to / made allowance for *his* nature," i.e., permitted the body its natural need.
[^2]: The imagery is from wrestling: *συμπλακῆναι* is to "close with, lock together, grapple." The verb rendered "make him topple," Greek *ῥέψῃ* (from *ῥέπω*, "to incline, sink, tip the scale"), is transitive here in an unusual sense — to make the opponent lose balance so as to throw him (*καταβάλῃ*, "cast down"). It is possibly an OCR variant for *ῥίψῃ* ("hurl"); either way the sense is the wrestler's takedown. The object throughout ("him") is the devil, whom Christ grapples with and overthrows, bestowing the victory on us. The sentence trails off without a full stop in this lemma.
