# Matthew 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 10 · Matt. 4:6

> **Upon *their* hands they shall bear thee up.** (Matt. 4:6)

*This is* not *written* concerning Christ,[^1] but concerning the saints, who need angelic help. But Christ has no need *of it*, being God,[^2]

[^1]: This lemma continues the sentence broken off at the end of the previous one ("And it is not written, the …"). The negation *οὐ* ("not") and the verb "is written" belong to that prior clause; hence they are supplied here in italics to complete the sense: the words «Upon their hands they shall bear thee up» (Ps. 90[91]:12) are not written of Christ.
[^2]: The exposition itself breaks off at *Θεὸς ὤν,* ("being God,"), the thought — that being God he needs no angelic help — left grammatically incomplete at the lemma's end.
