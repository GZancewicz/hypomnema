# Matthew 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 27 · Matt. 4:21

> **And having gone on from there, he saw other two brethren, James the *son* of Zebedee, and John his brother, in the boat with Zebedee their father. (Matt. 4:21)**[^1]

It is a very great virtue, to nourish their father in his old age,[^2] and to be nourished from righteous labors.

[^1]: Greek *Καὶ προβὰς ἐκεῖθεν* — "And having advanced/gone on from there"; *προβάς* is an aorist participle. "James the *son* of Zebedee" renders *Ἰάκωβον τὸν τοῦ Ζεβεδαίου*, where the Greek has only the article *τόν* + genitive; "son" is supplied. The OCR reads *Ἱκωβον* for *Ἰάκωβον* and *Ζεβεδαίου* correctly.
[^2]: Greek *τὸ γηροτροφεῖν τὸν πατέρα αὐτῶν* — the single verb *γηροτροφεῖν* means "to feed / care for *someone* in old age"; the articular infinitive functions as the subject of the sentence. Theophylact reads the sons' presence "in the boat with their father" as an act of filial support.
