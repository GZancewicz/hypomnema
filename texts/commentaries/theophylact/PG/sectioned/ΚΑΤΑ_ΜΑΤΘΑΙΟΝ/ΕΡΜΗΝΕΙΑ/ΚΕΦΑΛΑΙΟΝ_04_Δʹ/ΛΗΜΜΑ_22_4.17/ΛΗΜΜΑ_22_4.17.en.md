# Matthew 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 22 · Matt. 4:17

> **…Jesus began to preach and to say. (Matt. 4:17)**[^1]

From *the time* when John was bound, Jesus began to preach. For he was waiting for John first to bear witness concerning him and to prepare for him a way, through which he was about to come — just as also servants make *things* ready beforehand for *their* masters. For the Lord, being equal to the Father, himself also had John as *his* prophet, just as his Father and God had the prophets before John; but rather those *prophets* also belong both to the Father and to the Son.[^2]

[^1]: The scripture text at the head of this folder is *ἤρξατο ὁ Ἰησοῦς κηρύσσειν καὶ λέγειν* ("Jesus began to preach and to say"); the governing opening words *Ἀπὸ τότε* ("From that time") close the previous lemma (21). The subject *ὁ Ἰησοῦς* ("Jesus") is explicit in the Greek and stands after the verb *ἤρξατο*; the English moves it before "began" for natural word order — hence it is roman, not a supplied (italic) word.
[^2]: Greek *μᾶλλον δὲ κἀκεῖνοι τοῦ τε Πατρὸς καὶ τοῦ Υἱοῦ εἰσιν* — "but rather those *former prophets* too are of both the Father and the Son." The correction: it is not merely that the Son "had" John as the Father "had" the earlier prophets; rather, being one God, Father and Son alike own all the prophets in common. The particles *μᾶλλον δὲ* ("but rather") mark the self-correction.
