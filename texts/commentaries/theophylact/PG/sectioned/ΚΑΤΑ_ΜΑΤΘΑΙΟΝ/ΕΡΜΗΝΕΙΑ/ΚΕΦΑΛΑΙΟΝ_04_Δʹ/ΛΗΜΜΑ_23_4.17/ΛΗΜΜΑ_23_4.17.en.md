# Matthew 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 23 · Matt. 4:17

> **Repent; for the kingdom of the heavens has drawn near. (Matt. 4:17)**[^1]

The kingdom of the heavens is also Christ,[^2] but it is also the virtuous life. For when someone conducts *his* life as an angel upon earth, is he not indeed heavenly? So that in each of us is the kingdom of the heavens, if we live angelically.

[^1]: Greek *ἤγγικε* ("has come near, is at hand," perfect of *ἐγγίζω*). Rendered "has drawn near" to keep the perfect force. *Βασιλεία τῶν οὐρανῶν* is Matthew's characteristic phrase; kept literal as "kingdom of the heavens" (plural), as Theophylact's exposition trades on both meanings he assigns it.
[^2]: Greek *Βασιλεία τῶν οὐρανῶν ἐστὶ καὶ ὁ Χριστός* — "the kingdom of the heavens is Christ as well." Theophylact gives a double referent: the kingdom is (a) Christ himself and (b) the life of virtue; the particle pair *…ἐστι… ἔστι δὲ καὶ…* ("it is… but it is also…") holds the two senses together.
