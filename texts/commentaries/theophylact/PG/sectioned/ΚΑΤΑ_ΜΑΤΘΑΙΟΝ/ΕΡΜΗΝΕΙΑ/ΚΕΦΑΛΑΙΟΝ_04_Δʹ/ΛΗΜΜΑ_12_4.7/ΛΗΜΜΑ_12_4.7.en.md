# Matthew 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 12 · Matt. 4:8

> **Again the devil takes him up into *an* exceedingly high mountain, and shows him all the kingdoms of the world, and their glory, and** (Matt. 4:8)

*This lemma carries only the scriptural text, with no exposition of its own.*[^1]

[^1]: The lemma consists solely of the Gospel quotation (Matt. 4:8), which breaks off mid-sentence at *καί* ("and"). Both the verse and Theophylact's comment are continued in the next lemma, which supplies «λέγει αὐτῷ· Ταῦτα πάντα σοι δώσω, ἐὰν πεσὼν προσκυνήσῃς μοι» ("he says to him: All these things will I give thee, if falling down thou wilt worship me") and then expounds it.
