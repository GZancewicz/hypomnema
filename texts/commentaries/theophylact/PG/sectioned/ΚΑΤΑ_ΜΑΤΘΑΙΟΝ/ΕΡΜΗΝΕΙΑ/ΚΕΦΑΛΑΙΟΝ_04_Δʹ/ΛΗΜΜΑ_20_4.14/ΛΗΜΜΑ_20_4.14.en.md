# Matthew 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 20 · Matt. 4:14

> **That there might be fulfilled the *thing* spoken through Isaiah the prophet, saying: Land… (Matt. 4:14)**[^1]

[^1]: Greek *Ἵνα πληρωθῇ τὸ ῥηθὲν διὰ Ἠσαΐου τοῦ προφήτου, λέγοντος· Γῇ…* The quotation is cut off mid-word at *Γῇ* ("Land / earth," dative), the first word of the Isaiah citation (Isa. 9:1) that the Gospel is fulfilling. Theophylact's own name-form is *Ἠσαΐου*; rendered "Isaiah" per the proper-noun convention.
