# Matthew 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 1 · Matt. 4:1

> **Then Jesus was led up into the wilderness by the Spirit. (Matt. 4:1)**

Teaching us that after the baptism one must rather expect temptations, he is led up by the Holy Spirit; for he did nothing without the Spirit. And he is led up into the wilderness, so that he might show us that the devil tempts us then, when he sees *us* isolated and not being helped by one another. Therefore we must not, without deliberation, be confident in ourselves —[^1]

[^1]: The exposition breaks off in mid-sentence at Δεῖ οὖν ἡμᾶς μὴ ἀβούλως ἑαυτοῖς θαῤῥεῖν ("therefore we must not, without deliberation, trust in ourselves"), with no completing clause in this lemma file. The adverb *ἀβούλως* means "without counsel / without deliberation / rashly, imprudently"; *θαῤῥεῖν ἑαυτοῖς* is "to be confident in / rely upon ourselves." Note also the shift from the lemma's aorist *ἀνήχθη* ("was led up") to Theophylact's present *ἀνάγεται* ("is led up") in the comment.
