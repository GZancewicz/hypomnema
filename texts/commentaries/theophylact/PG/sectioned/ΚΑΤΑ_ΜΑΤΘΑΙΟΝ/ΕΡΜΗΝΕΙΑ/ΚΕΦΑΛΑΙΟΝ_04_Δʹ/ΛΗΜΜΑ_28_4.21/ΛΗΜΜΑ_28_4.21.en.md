# Matthew 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 28 · Matt. 4:21

> **mending their nets. (Matt. 4:21)**[^1]

For they were poor, and for this reason, not being able to buy new ones, they were stitching together the old ones.[^2]

[^1]: Greek *Καταρτίζοντας τὰ δίκτυα αὐτῶν* — "mending / putting in order their nets"; the participle *καταρτίζοντας* continues the description of James and John from the preceding lemma.
[^2]: Greek *Πένητες γὰρ ἦσαν, καὶ διὰ τοῦτο μὴ δυνάμενοι νέα ὠνήσασθαι, τὰ παλαιὰ συνέῤῥαπτον.* The OCR corruptions *νάα ὡνήσασθαι* → *νέα ὠνήσασθαι* ("to buy new *ones*") and *Κτὰ παλαιὰ* → *τὰ παλαιὰ* ("the old *ones*") are secure. *συνέῤῥαπτον* is imperfect ("they were sewing together"), rendered "they were stitching together." "*ones*" is supplied after the neuter plural adjectives.
