# Matthew 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 15 · Matt. 4:10

> **Then saith Jesus to him: Get thee behind me, Satan;[^1] for it is written: Thou shalt worship the Lord thy God, and him only shalt thou serve.[^2] (Matt. 4:10)**

The Lord is angered at him, when he saw him appropriating to himself the things of God,[^3] and saying:[^4]

[^1]: Greek *Ὕπαγε ὀπίσω μου, Σατανᾶ* ("Get thee behind me, Satan"). The common text of Matt. 4:10 reads simply *Ὕπαγε, Σατανᾶ* ("Get thee hence / Away, Satan"); the words *ὀπίσω μου* ("behind me") are the wording of Matt. 16:23 (to Peter). Theophylact's lemma carries the conflated/variant reading; translated as it stands, per following the text on the page.
[^2]: Two distinct verbs of worship are kept distinct: *προσκυνήσεις* ("thou shalt worship / do obeisance to," bodily reverence) and *λατρεύσεις* ("thou shalt serve," rendering the cultic service, *λατρεία*, due to God alone). English "worship … serve" preserves the pair.
[^3]: Greek *σφετεριζόμενον* ("appropriating to himself, claiming as his own"); *τὰ τοῦ Θεοῦ* ("the things of God"), i.e. the honor of worship that belongs to God alone.
[^4]: The comment breaks off at *καὶ λέγοντα·* ("and saying:"); the devil's words are supplied in the next lemma, which re-quotes «Ταῦτα πάντα σοι δώσω» ("All these things I will give thee") "as *though they were* his own."
