# Matthew 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 29 · Matt. 4:22

> **But they, immediately having left the boat and their father, followed him. (Matt. 4:22)**[^1]

Zebedee appears not to have believed, and for this reason they left him.[^2] Do you see when one must leave one's father? — whenever he hinders one toward virtue and godliness.[^3] But these, having seen the former ones following, reasonably followed, having imitated those men.

[^1]: Greek *Οἱ δὲ εὐθέως ἀφέντες τὸ πλοῖον καὶ τὸν πατέρα αὐτῶν, ἠκολούθησαν αὐτῷ.* The *δέ* is rendered "But"; the participle *ἀφέντες* ("having left") kept as a participle.
[^2]: Greek *Φαίνεται ὁ Ζεβεδαῖος μὴ πιστεύσας, καὶ διὰ τοῦτο ἀφῆκαν αὐτόν* — "Zebedee appears not having believed…," i.e. it appears that Zebedee had not believed, and so they left *him* (their father), whereas they had not left their nets as an abandonment of a believer.
[^3]: Greek *Ὁρᾷς πότε δεῖ ἀφεῖναι τὸν πατέρα, ὅταν ἐμποδίζῃ πρὸς ἀρετὴν καὶ θεοσέβειαν.* *θεοσέβεια* is "reverence/worship of God, piety, godliness," rendered "godliness"; the OCR *ποὸς* is *πρός* ("toward"). The pronoun "*one*" is supplied for the impersonal construction.
