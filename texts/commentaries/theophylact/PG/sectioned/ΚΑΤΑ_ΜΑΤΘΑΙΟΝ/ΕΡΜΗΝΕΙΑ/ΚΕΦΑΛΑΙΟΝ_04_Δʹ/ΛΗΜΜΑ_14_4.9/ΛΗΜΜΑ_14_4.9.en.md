# Matthew 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 14 · Matt. 4:9

> **"All these things I will give thee," he saith. (Matt. 4:9)**

For he reckons the world as being his own, out of arrogance;[^1] but this he says even now to the covetous, so that those who worship him, those *are the ones who* shall have these things.[^2] K[^3]

[^1]: Greek *ὡς ἴδιον ὄντα τὸν κόσμον λογίζεται, ὑπὸ ὑπερηφανίας* — literally "for, as being his own, he reckons the world, out of pride." *ἴδιος* = "one's own, private"; *ὑπερηφανία* = "arrogance, haughtiness, pride." The participle *ὄντα* ("being") is kept.
[^2]: Greek *ὥστε οἱ προσκυνοῦντες αὐτῷ, ἐκεῖνοι ταῦτα ἕξουσιν* — "so that those who worship him, they shall have these things." The OCR reads *ἕρουσιν*; taken as *ἕξουσιν* (future of *ἔχω*, "shall have"), which suits the sense (those who worship the devil, i.e. the covetous, obtain these worldly goods). The alternative *οἴσουσιν / αἴρουσιν* ("carry off") is less likely. The doubled subject ("those who worship him, those…") is Theophylact's emphatic Greek, kept rather than smoothed; "*are the ones who*" is supplied for the sense.
[^3]: The lemma breaks off at a single stray letter *Κ* in the source (the start of the next word/lemma), untranslatable as it stands.
