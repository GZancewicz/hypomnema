# Matthew 4 (Δʹ) — Lemma 26 · Matt. 4:20

> **immediately, having left the nets, they followed him. (Matt. 4:20)**[^1]

See obedient men — immediately they followed him.[^2] Whence it is clear that this calling is a second one.[^3] For having been taught beforehand by Christ, then having left him, they readily again followed him, having seen[^4] him.

[^1]: The subject of the sentence, "But they" (Greek *Οἱ δέ*), stands at the end of the previous lemma; here the sentence is completed: *εὐθέως ἀφέντες τὰ δίκτυα, ἠκολούθησαν αὐτῷ.* The participle *ἀφέντες* ("having left / let go") is kept as a participle.
[^2]: Greek *Ὅρα ὑπηκόους ἀνθρώπους, εὐθέως ἠκολούθησαν αὐτῷ* — "See obedient men, immediately they followed him." Theophylact echoes the lemma's own words.
[^3]: Greek *δευτέρα ἐστὶν ἡ κλῆσις αὕτη* — "this is a second calling," i.e. not their first encounter with Christ (cf. the earlier meeting narrated in John 1:35–42); "second" (*δευτέρα*) rendered predicatively.
[^4]: Greek OCR *δόντες αὐτόν*; securely reconstructed as *ἰδόντες αὐτόν* ("having seen him"), the initial *ἰ-* dropped in the OCR. The alternative *δόντες* ("having given") yields no sense here.
