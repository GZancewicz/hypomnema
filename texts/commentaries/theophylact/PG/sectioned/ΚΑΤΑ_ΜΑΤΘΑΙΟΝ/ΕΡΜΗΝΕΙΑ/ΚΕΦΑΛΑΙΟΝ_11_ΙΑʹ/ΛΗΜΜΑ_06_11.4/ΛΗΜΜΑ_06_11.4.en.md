# Matthew 11 (ΙΑʹ) — Lemma 6 · Matt. 11:4

> **Having gone, report to John what ye hear and see:**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture citation; there is no exposition of its own in the source. The catalogue of signs continues in the next lemma (Matt. 11:5).*[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Πορευθέντες ἀπαγγείλατε Ἰωάννῃ ἃ ἀκούετε καὶ βλέπετε·» (Matt. 11:4). The OCR reads «ἀαγγείλατε» for *ἀπαγγείλατε* ("report, announce back"); secure. The participle πορευθέντες ("having gone") is kept as a participle. This continues the answer begun in Lemma 5.
[^2]: The lemma breaks off at «βλέπετε·»; the following lemma resumes with the list «τυφλοὶ ἀναβλέπουσι, καὶ χωλοὶ περιπατοῦσι…» ("*the* blind receive sight, and *the* lame walk…," Matt. 11:5).
