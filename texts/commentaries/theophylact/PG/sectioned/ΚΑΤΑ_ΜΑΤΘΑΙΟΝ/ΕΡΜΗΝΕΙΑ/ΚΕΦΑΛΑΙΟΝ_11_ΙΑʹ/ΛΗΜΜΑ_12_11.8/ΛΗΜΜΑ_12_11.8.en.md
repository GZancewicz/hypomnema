# Matthew 11 (ΙΑʹ) — Lemma 12 · Matt. 11:9

> **But what did you go out to see? A prophet? Yes, I say to you, and more than a prophet.[^1] (Matt. 11:9)**

More than a prophet *is* John, because the other prophets only foretold about Christ, but this one also became an eyewitness[^2] — which is truly a great thing; and because the others, on the one hand, prophesied after being born, but this one, on the other hand, even while being in *the* womb of *his* mother recognized Christ and leaped.[^3]

[^1]: The folder metadata assigns this lemma to Matt. 11:8, but the quoted words — *προφήτην; … καὶ περισσότερον προφήτου* ("a prophet? … and more than a prophet") — are Matt. 11:9; the header verse is corrected accordingly. *περισσότερον προφήτου* = "more (abundant) than a prophet."
[^2]: Greek *αὐτόπτης* — "one who sees with his own eyes, eyewitness"; John saw Christ present in the flesh, whereas the earlier prophets only foretold him.
[^3]: Greek *ἐσκίρτα*, imperfect of *σκιρτάω* ("to leap, skip, gambol") — the leaping of John in Elizabeth's womb (cf. Luke 1:41). Rendered "leaped." The *μὲν … δὲ* pair is carried by "on the one hand … on the other hand."
