# Matthew 11 (ΙΑʹ) — Lemma 15 · Matt. 11:11

> **But the lesser in the kingdom of the heavens is greater than he.[^1] (Matt. 11:11)**

Since he had said many praises concerning John, lest they should think that that man is greater even than he himself, he says here more openly: that I, the lesser both according to age and according to your estimation, am greater than he in the kingdom of the heavens — that is, in the spiritual and heavenly good things.[^2] For here I am inferior to him — both in that he was born before me and in that he seems to you to be great — but there I am greater.

[^1]: Greek *ὁ δὲ μικρότερος* — "the lesser/smaller one." The common reading takes this as "he that is least in the kingdom of heaven"; Theophylact instead reads *ὁ μικρότερος* as Christ speaking of himself — the one "lesser" in age and in the people's estimation — as the exposition makes explicit ("I, the lesser…"). The comparative/superlative *μικρότερος* is rendered "lesser" to keep his reading; a footnote flags the divergence from the usual "least."
[^2]: Greek *ἐν τοῖς πνευματικοῖς καὶ οὐρανίοις ἀγαθοῖς* ("in the spiritual and heavenly good things"), glossing "in the kingdom of the heavens."
