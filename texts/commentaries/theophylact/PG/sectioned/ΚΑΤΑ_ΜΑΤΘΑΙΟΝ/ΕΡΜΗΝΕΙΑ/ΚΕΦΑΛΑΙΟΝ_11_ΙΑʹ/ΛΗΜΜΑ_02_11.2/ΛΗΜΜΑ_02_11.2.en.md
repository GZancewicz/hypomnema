# Matthew 11 (ΙΑʹ) — Lemma 2 · Matt. 11:2

> **But John, having heard in the prison the works of Christ, having sent two of his disciples,**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture citation; there is no exposition of its own in the source, the comment being deferred to the following lemma, where the question John's disciples put is expounded.*[^2]

[^1]: Greek «[Ὁ δὲ] Ἰωάννης ἀκούσας ἐν τῷ δεσμωτηρίῳ τὰ ἔργα τοῦ Χριστοῦ, πέμψας δύο τῶν μαθητῶν αὐτοῦ,» (Matt. 11:2). The article-plus-particle «Ὁ δὲ» physically stands at the end of Lemma 1; it is here rendered "But John." The two participles ἀκούσας ("having heard") and πέμψας ("having sent") are kept as participles per the formal-equivalence rule; the sentence is completed by the finite verb «εἶπεν» ("said") at the head of Lemma 3.
[^2]: Theophylact's text quotes the verse in installments; the lemma breaks off mid-sentence (before «εἶπεν αὐτῷ»), which opens Lemma 3.
