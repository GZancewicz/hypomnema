# Matthew 11 (ΙΑʹ) — Lemma 28 · Matt. 11:23

> **And you, Capernaum, who were exalted unto heaven, unto Hades shall you be brought down; for if in Sodom had been done the mighty works which were done in you, it would have remained until this day. Nevertheless I say to you, that for *the* land** [^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation and breaks off mid-verse at* «ὅτι γῇ» *("that for the land"); the sentence is completed, and the exposition supplied, in the following lemma.*[^2]

[^1]: OCR *Σοθόμοις* = **Σοδόμοις** (Sodom). *ἕως ἅδου καταβιβασθήσῃ* — "unto Hades you shall be brought down"; *ἅδης* left as "Hades," the Greek term for the realm of the dead. *ἔμειναν ἂν μέχρι τῆς σήμερον*: "it would have remained until this day" (the singular subject being the city of Sodom). The lemma ends "for *the* land" (*γῇ*), the supplied "*the*" italicized.
[^2]: The Gospel text divides across the folder boundary: the next lemma opens with «Σοδόμων ἀνεκτότερον ἔσται…» ("...of Sodom it will be more tolerable...") and there begins Theophylact's comment.
