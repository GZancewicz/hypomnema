# Matthew 11 (ΙΑʹ) — Lemma 18 · Matt. 11:13

> **For all the prophets and the law prophesied until John. (Matt. 11:13)**

And this too belongs to the same train of thought.[^1] For he says: I am the one who comes,[^2] for all the prophets have been fulfilled; but they would not have been fulfilled, unless I had come; therefore expect nothing further. And if[^3]

[^1]: Greek *τῆς αὐτῆς ἀκολουθίας ἐστί* — "is of the same sequence/consequence," i.e. it continues the same connected argument (the same *ἀκολουθία*, "logical train") as the preceding saying.
[^2]: Greek *ὁ ἐρχόμενος* — "the coming one / he who is to come," the messianic title (cf. Matt. 11:3, «ὁ ἐρχόμενος»).
[^3]: The sentence breaks off mid-clause at *Καὶ εἰ* ("And if"); it is completed in the next lemma, which supplies «θέλετε δέξασθαι, αὐτός ἐστιν Ἡλίας ὁ μέλλων ἔρχεσθαι» ("… you are willing to receive *it*, he is Elijah who is to come," Matt. 11:14).
