# Matthew 11 (ΙΑʹ) — Lemma 5 · Matt. 11:4

> **But Jesus answering, said to them: Having gone, report to John what ye hear and see:**[^1]

[^1]: Greek «Ἀποκριθεὶς δὲ ὁ Ἰησοῦς, εἶπεν αὐτοῖς·» (Matt. 11:4). The participle ἀποκριθεὶς ("having answered / answering") is kept as a participle; δὲ ("but") is a present particle and so roman. The quoted answer «Πορευθέντες ἀπαγγείλατε Ἰωάννῃ ἃ ἀκούετε καὶ βλέπετε·» continues immediately in the same verse and is here read together with the introductory clause.
