# Matthew 11 (ΙΑʹ) — Lemma 5 · Matt. 11:4

> **But Jesus answering, said to them:**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture citation; there is no exposition of its own in the source. The Lord's answer itself follows in the next lemmata (Matt. 11:4–6).*

[^1]: Greek «Ἀποκριθεὶς δὲ ὁ Ἰησοῦς, εἶπεν αὐτοῖς·» (Matt. 11:4). The participle ἀποκριθεὶς ("having answered / answering") is kept as a participle; δὲ ("but") is a present particle and so roman.
