# Matthew 11 (ΙΑʹ) — Lemma 25 · Matt. 11:20

> **Then Jesus began to reproach the cities in which the most of his mighty works had been done, because they did not repent. (Matt. 11:20)**[^1]

After the showing that he did all things, as many as were necessary, and that they remained unrepentant, then finally he reproaches *them*.

[^1]: Greek *δυνάμεις* ("powers, mighty works, miracles"); *αἱ πλεῖσται δυνάμεις* is the superlative, "the most / the very greatest number of *his* mighty works." Rendered "mighty works" throughout to keep the sense of miraculous deeds rather than abstract "powers." The verb *ὀνειδίζειν* ("to reproach, upbraid, cast in *someone's* teeth") is rendered "reproach."
