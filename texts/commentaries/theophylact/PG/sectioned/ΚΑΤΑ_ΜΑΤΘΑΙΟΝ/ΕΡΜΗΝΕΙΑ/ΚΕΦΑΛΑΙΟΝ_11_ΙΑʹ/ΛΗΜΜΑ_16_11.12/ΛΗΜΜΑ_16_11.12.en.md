# Matthew 11 (ΙΑʹ) — Lemma 16 · Matt. 11:12

> **But from the days of John the Baptist until now the kingdom of the heavens is taken by force,[^1] and *the* violent seize it. (Matt. 11:12)**

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation; Theophylact's exposition of it follows in the next lemma.*[^2]

[^1]: Greek *βιάζεται* is ambiguous between passive ("is forced, is taken by force") and middle ("forces its way, presses forward"). Theophylact's exposition in the following lemma resolves it in the passive-object direction — many "seize" (*ἁρπάζουσι*) the kingdom, i.e. faith in Christ, and "there is need of much force" (*βίας χρεία πολλῆς*) — so "is taken by force" is chosen, paired with *οἱ βιασταὶ ἀρπάζουσιν αὐτήν* ("*the* violent seize it").
[^2]: The lemma file contains no comment (and the closing guillemet is missing in the source); the exposition begins in Lemma 17, whose opening words *Φαίνεται ἀνακόλουθον εἶναι τοῦτο, οὐκ ἔστι δέ* ("This seems to be incoherent, but it is not") take up this very verse.
