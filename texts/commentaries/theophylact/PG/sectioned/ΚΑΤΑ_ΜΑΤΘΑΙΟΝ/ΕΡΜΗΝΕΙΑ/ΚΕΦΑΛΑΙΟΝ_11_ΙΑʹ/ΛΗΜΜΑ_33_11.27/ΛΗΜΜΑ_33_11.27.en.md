# Matthew 11 (ΙΑʹ) — Lemma 33 · Matt. 11:27

> **All things were delivered to me, (Matt. 11:27)**[^1]

not by *a* master,[^2] but,[^3]

[^1]: A short re-quotation of the opening words of 11:27 («Πάντα μοι παρεδόθη»), which Theophylact now parses phrase by phrase to press his point about *who* did the delivering.
[^2]: Greek «οὐχ ὑπὸ τοῦ δεσπότου» — "not by the master/lord (*δεσπότης*)." The term *δεσπότης* denotes a master over slaves, set here in deliberate contrast to "Father" in the next clause: the "delivering" is not a lord's disposal of a servant but a father's to a son. The article is rendered indefinitely ("*a* master") for the generalizing sense.
[^3]: The sentence breaks off at «ἀλλ᾽» ("but,") and is completed in the following lemma, which supplies the scripture words «by my Father».
