# Matthew 11 (ΙΑʹ) — Lemma 09 · Matt. 11:6

> **Blessed is he, whoever shall not be offended in me.[^1] (Matt. 11:6)**

For they themselves had much doubt about him.[^2]

[^1]: This lemma carries no «…» quotation mark at its head in the source; the scripture words are the completion of the *φησὶν, ὅτι* ("he says that—") left open at the end of the previous lemma. They are the Gospel text of Matt. 11:6, here set as the lemma. *σκανδαλισθῇ* = "be made to stumble / be offended / take offense."
[^2]: Greek *αὐτοὶ γὰρ πολὺν εἶχον περὶ αὐτοῦ δισταγμόν* — "for they themselves had much doubt about him." *δισταγμός* = "doubt, hesitation, wavering"; *αὐτοί* ("they themselves") points back to John's disciples, whose own uncertainty the beatitude addresses.
