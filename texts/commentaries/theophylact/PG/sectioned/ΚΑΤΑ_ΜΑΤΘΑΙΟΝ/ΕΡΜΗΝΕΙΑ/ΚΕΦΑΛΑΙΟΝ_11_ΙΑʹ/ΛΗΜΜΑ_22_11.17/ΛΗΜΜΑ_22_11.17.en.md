# Matthew 11 (ΙΑʹ) — Lemma 22 · Matt. 11:17 (cont.)

…*it does not*[^1] please *them*; even if one pipes, not even thus *does it please*.[^2]

[^1]: Supplied negative; the stray opening «…» in the source most likely masks the negative *οὐκ* governing the first verb.
[^2]: Greek: «ἀρέσκει, κἂν αὐλῇ, οὐδ᾽ οὕτως.» Joined to Lemma 21's «οἷς κἂν κλαίῃ τις…», the full sentence is: "to whom, even if one weeps, *it does not* please, and even if one pipes, not even thus." The point: whether approached through John's mourning or through Christ's festivity, the Jews were pleased by neither.
