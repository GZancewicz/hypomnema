# Matthew 11 (ΙΑʹ) — Lemma 22 · Matt. 11:17 (cont.)

*This lemma continues the sentence broken off at the end of Lemma 21; it carries no scripture quotation of its own (the opening «…» in the source is a spurious OCR mark).*[^1]

…*it does not*[^2] please *them*; even if one pipes, not even thus *does it please*.

[^1]: Greek: «ἀρέσκει, κἂν αὐλῇ, οὐδ᾽ οὕτως.» Joined to Lemma 21's «οἷς κἂν κλαίῃ τις…», the full sentence is: "to whom, even if one weeps, *it does not* please, and even if one pipes, not even thus." The stray opening «…» in the source most likely masks the negative *οὐκ* governing the first verb. The point: whether *approached* through John's mourning or through Christ's festivity, the Jews were pleased by neither.
[^2]: Supplied negative — see note 1.
