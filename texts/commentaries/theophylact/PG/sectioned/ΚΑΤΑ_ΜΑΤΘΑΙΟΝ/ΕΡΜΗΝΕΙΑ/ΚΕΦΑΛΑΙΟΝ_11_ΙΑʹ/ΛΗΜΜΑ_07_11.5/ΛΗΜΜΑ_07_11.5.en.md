# Matthew 11 (ΙΑʹ) — Lemma 07 · Matt. 11:5–6

> **… the blind receive sight, and the lame walk; lepers are cleansed, and the deaf hear; the dead are raised, and the poor have good tidings preached *to them*.[^1] And blessed is he, whoever shall not be offended in me. (Matt. 11:5–6)**

He did not say, "Report to John, that I am the coming one,"[^2] but as knowing that John for this reason sent the disciples, that they might see the miracles, he says that—[^3]

[^1]: The verb *εὐαγγελίζονται* is passive, literally "are good-newsed"; rendered "have good tidings preached *to them*" for idiomatic English (the bare passive "are evangelized" misleadingly suggests "are converted"). The recipient "*to them*" is supplied for the passive. The *εὐαγγελ-* stem ("good tidings / gospel") is expounded directly in the next lemma, where Theophylact takes up the phrase *πτωχοὶ εὐαγγελιζόμενοι*.
[^2]: Greek *ὁ ἐρχόμενος* ("the coming one / he that cometh"), the expected Messiah of the Scriptures — the very title used in John's question above (Matt. 11:3). The OCR *Ουκ πεν* is securely *Οὐκ εἶπεν* ("He did not say").
[^3]: The sentence breaks off at *φησὶν, ὅτι* ("he says that—") and is completed in the next lemma, whose opening words «Ἃ βλέπετε ἀπαγγείλατε τῷ Ἰωάννῃ» ("What you see, report to John") supply the content of what "he says."
