# Matthew 11 (ΙΑʹ) — Lemma 07 · Matt. 11:5–6

> **… *the* blind receive sight, and *the* lame walk; lepers are cleansed, and *the* deaf hear; *the* dead are raised, and *the* poor are evangelized.[^1] And blessed is he, whoever shall not be offended in me. (Matt. 11:5–6)**

He did not say, "Report to John, that I am the coming one,"[^2] but as knowing that John for this reason sent the disciples, that they might see the miracles, he says that—[^3]

[^1]: The nouns are anarthrous throughout (*τυφλοί, χωλοί, λεπροί, κωφοί, νεκροί, πτωχοί*), hence the supplied "*the*." The verb *εὐαγγελίζονται* is passive: "are evangelized / have good news preached to them"; rendered "are evangelized" to keep the *εὐαγγελ-* stem visible, since Theophylact expounds the word *πτωχοὶ εὐαγγελιζόμενοι* directly in the next lemma.
[^2]: Greek *ὁ ἐρχόμενος* ("the coming one / he that cometh"), the expected Messiah of the Scriptures — the very title used in John's question above (Matt. 11:3). The OCR *Ουκ πεν* is securely *Οὐκ εἶπεν* ("He did not say").
[^3]: The sentence breaks off at *φησὶν, ὅτι* ("he says that—") and is completed in the next lemma, whose opening words «Ἃ βλέπετε ἀπαγγείλατε τῷ Ἰωάννῃ» ("What you see, report to John") supply the content of what "he says."
