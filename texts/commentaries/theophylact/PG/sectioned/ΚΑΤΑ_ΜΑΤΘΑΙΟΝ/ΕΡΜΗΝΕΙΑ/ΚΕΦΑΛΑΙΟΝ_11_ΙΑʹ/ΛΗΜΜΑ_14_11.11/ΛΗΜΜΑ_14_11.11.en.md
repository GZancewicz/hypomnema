# Matthew 11 (ΙΑʹ) — Lemma 14 · Matt. 11:11

> **Amen I say to you, there has not arisen among those born of women anyone greater than John the Baptist. (Matt. 11:11)**

With affirmation he declares this, that there is no one greater than John; but having said "of women," he excepted himself.[^1] For Christ himself was one born of a virgin, not of a woman — that is, *of one* having come into marriage.[^2]

[^1]: Greek *ὑπεξεῖλεν ἑαυτόν* — "he took himself out from under (the statement), excepted himself." The point: by qualifying "born of women," Christ silently removes himself from the comparison, since he was born of a virgin.
[^2]: Greek *ἐλθούσης εἰς γάμον* ("*of one* having come into marriage / into wedlock"), a genitive absolute participle glossing "woman" (*γυνή*, which also means "wife"). Theophylact distinguishes *παρθένος* ("virgin") from *γυνή* taken as a woman who has entered marriage.
