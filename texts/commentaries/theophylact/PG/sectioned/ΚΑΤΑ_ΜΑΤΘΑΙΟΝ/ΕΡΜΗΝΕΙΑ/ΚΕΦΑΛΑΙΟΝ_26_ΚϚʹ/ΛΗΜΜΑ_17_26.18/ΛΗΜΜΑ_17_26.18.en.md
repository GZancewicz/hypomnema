# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 17 · Matt. 26:18

> **[But he] said: Go into the city to a certain *man*, and say to him: The teacher says: My time is near; with you I keep the Passover with my disciples. And... (Matt. 26:18)**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the Gospel citation of Matt. 26:18, with no exposition of its own; Theophylact's comment on the passage is gathered at lemma 18.*

[^1]: Greek *πρὸς τὸν δεῖνα* — "to such-a-one, to a certain *man*" (deliberately unnamed); the evangelist's own indefinite. "*man*" is supplied. The citation opens at «εἶπεν» (continuing «Ὁ δὲ» from the prior lemma) and breaks off at «Καὶ», completed in the next lemma. *πρὸς σὲ ποιῶ τὸ Πάσχα* = "with you I keep the Passover," which Theophylact glosses below as "in your house."
