# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 1 · Matt. 26:1

> **It came to pass, when Jesus finished all these words, he said to his disciples:**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture text; Theophylact appends no exposition to it. The sentence breaks off at "he said to his disciples," and the quoted saying together with its comment is taken up in Lemma 2.*[^2]

[^1]: Greek «ἐγένετο ὅτε ἐτέλεσεν ὁ Ἰησοῦς πάντας τοὺς λόγους τούτους, εἶπε τοῖς μαθηταῖς αὐτοῦ» (OCR *Ἰῃσοῦς*, *εἰπε*). The received text of Matt. 26:1 opens «Καὶ ἐγένετο»; the initial καὶ ("And") is absent here, so it is not supplied. *λόγους* = "words / sayings / discourses" — a back-reference to the preceding chapters (24–25) of eschatological teaching.
[^2]: See Lemma 2, which continues «Οἴδατε, ὅτι μετὰ δύο ἡμέρας τὸ Πάσχα γίνεται…» and there gives the exposition.
