# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 9 · Matt. 26:9

> **For this ointment could have been sold for much, and given to *the* poor. (Matt. 26:9)**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture text (the continuation of the disciples' words); Theophylact adds no exposition here.*[^2]

[^1]: Greek «ἠδύνατο γὰρ τοῦτο τὸ μύρον πραθῆναι πολλοῦ, καὶ δοθῆναι πτωχοῖς.» The verse has no opening «…» quotation marks in the source; it continues the disciples' complaint from the previous lemma. *πολλοῦ* is a genitive of price = "for much / for a great *sum*."
[^2]: The comment resumes at Lemma 10 with Christ's reply.
