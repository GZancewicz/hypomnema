# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 18 · Matt. 26:19

> **[And] the disciples did as Jesus had appointed for them, and prepared the Passover. (Matt. 26:19)**[^1]

"First *day* of the unleavened bread" — he means the day before the unleavened bread; *by way of illustration* I say something of this kind: on the Preparation,[^2] in the evening, those *men* were about to eat the Passover, and this *day* was called *the day* of the unleavened bread; the Lord, then, sends the disciples on the fifth *day*,[^3] which the evangelist names "first *day* of the unleavened bread," as being before the Preparation — on which Preparation, in the evening, they used to eat the unleavened bread. The disciples accordingly come, and ask: Where do you wish that we should prepare for you to eat the Passover? For neither did those *men* have, nor he himself, a house of *their* own; but he sends them off to a man not known *to them* nor knowing *them*, just as he did also in the case of the she-ass,[^4] showing them that he is able, even from bare words, to persuade even those who do not at all know him to receive him. For he wished to complete[^5] the Passover, that he might not seem to be opposed to the law; and "his time" he names the slaughter,[^6] so that we might learn that not being ignorant, nor unwilling, is he slain. And to the *phrase* "With you I keep the Passover" — *this standing* in place of "in your house" — he adds the *phrase* "with my disciples," that the preparation might become sufficient, as *for* many *who were* about to eat.

[^1]: The lemma completes the citation begun at «Καὶ» of the prior lemma: «καὶ ἐποίησαν οἱ μαθηταὶ ὡς συνέταξεν αὐτοῖς ὁ Ἰησοῦς, καὶ ἡτοίμασαν τὸ Πάσχα». OCR «τὸν Πάσχα» for *τὸ Πάσχα*. *συνέταξεν* — "arranged, ordered, appointed for."
[^2]: Greek *ἡ Παρασκευή* — "the Preparation," i.e. Friday (the day of preparing for the Sabbath); kept as "the Preparation," the standard designation.
[^3]: Greek *τῇ πέμπτῃ* — "on the fifth *day*" of the week, i.e. Thursday; "*day*" is supplied.
[^4]: Greek *ἐπὶ τῆς ὄνου* — "in the case of the she-ass," the donkey of the triumphal entry (cf. Matt. 21:2-3), fetched from strangers on Jesus' word alone.
[^5]: Greek *τελέσαι* (OCR «τολέσαι»), from *τελέω* — "to complete, accomplish, celebrate (a rite)"; of a festival it carries "duly celebrate / fulfill."
[^6]: Greek *σφαγή* / *σφαγιάζεται* — "slaughter" / "is slain, is sacrificed," sacrificial vocabulary (the slaying of a victim), binding Christ's death to the Passover lamb; "his time" (*ὁ καιρός μου*) is thus his appointed sacrificial death, undergone knowingly and willingly.
