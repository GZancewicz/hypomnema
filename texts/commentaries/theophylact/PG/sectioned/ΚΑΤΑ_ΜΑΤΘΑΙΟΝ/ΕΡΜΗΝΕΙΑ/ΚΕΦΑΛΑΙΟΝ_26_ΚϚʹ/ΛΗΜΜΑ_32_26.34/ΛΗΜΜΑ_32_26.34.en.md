# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 32 · Matt. 26:34

> **Jesus: Amen I say to you that in this night, before *a* cock crows, thrice you shall deny me. (Matt. 26:34)**[^1]

Out of much self-will[^2] Peter alone promises not to be offended; wherefore Christ let him fall, in order that he might persuade *him* to trust not in himself, but in God, and to consider the words of Christ more trustworthy than his own conscience; since it is also full of arrogance to say: —[^3]

[^1]: The file opens with the single word «Ἰησοῦς·» ("Jesus"), which completes the sentence broken off at the end of Lemma 31 («Ἔφη αὐτῷ ὁ… — Jesus said to him»). The blockquote here therefore begins "Jesus:" and continues with his answer, Matt. 26:34. *ἀλέκτορα* is anarthrous, so the article in "*a* cock" is supplied and italicized. *τρίς* ("thrice, three times") is kept in its Greek word-order position before the verb.
[^2]: Greek *αὐθάδεια* ("self-will, willful presumption, stubborn self-assurance"): «Ὑπὸ πολλῆς αὐθαδείας». The word denotes not mere pride but a headstrong reliance on one's own judgment — precisely what the following clauses correct ("trust not in himself, but in God").
[^3]: The exposition breaks off mid-sentence at «τὸ εἰπεῖν·» ("to say: —"); the quoted words that Theophylact calls "full of arrogance" are supplied at the head of Lemma 33 («Εἰ πάντες σκανδαλισθήσονται» — "If all shall be offended"). OCR «ἐπαὶ» is *ἐπεὶ* ("since"); «ὑπερεψίας» is *ὑπερηφανίας* ("of arrogance / haughtiness").
