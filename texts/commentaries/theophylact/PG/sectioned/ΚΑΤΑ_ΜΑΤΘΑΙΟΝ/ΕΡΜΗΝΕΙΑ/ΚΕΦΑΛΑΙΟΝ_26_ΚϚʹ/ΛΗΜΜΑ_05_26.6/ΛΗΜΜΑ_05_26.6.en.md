# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 5 · Matt. 26:6

> **But when Jesus had come to be in Bethany, in *the* house of Simon the leper,**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture text; the sentence breaks off, and the exposition is given in Lemma 6, which continues «προσῆλθεν αὐτῷ γυνὴ ἀλάβαστρον μύρου ἔχουσα…».*

[^1]: Greek «Τοῦ δὲ Ἰησοῦ γενομένου ἐν Βηθανίᾳ, ἐν οἰκίᾳ Σίμωνος τοῦ λεπροῦ» — a genitive absolute, "Jesus having come to be in Bethany." The supplied "*the*" before "house" renders the anarthrous *οἰκίᾳ*.
