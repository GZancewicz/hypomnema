# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 19 · Matt. 26:20

> **But evening having come, he was reclining with the twelve, and**[^1]

[^1]: The lemma carries only scripture and breaks off at «καὶ» ("and"); the sentence is completed in the following lemmata (26:21–22). Greek «Ὀψίας δὲ γενομένης, ἀνέκειτο μετὰ τῶν δώδεκα, καὶ». *ἀνέκειτο* = "was reclining *at table*"; the reclining posture becomes the pivot of the comment at Lemma 21.
