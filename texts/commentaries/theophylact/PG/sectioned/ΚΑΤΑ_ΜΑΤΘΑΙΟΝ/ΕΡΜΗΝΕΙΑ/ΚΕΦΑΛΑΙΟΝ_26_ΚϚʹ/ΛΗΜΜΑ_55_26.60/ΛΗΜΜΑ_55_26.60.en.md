# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 55 · Matt. 26:60

> **But afterward two false witnesses, having come forward,**[^1] (Matt. 26:60)

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation, which breaks off mid-sentence; the exposition belongs to the following lemma.*[^1]

[^1]: Greek «Ὕστερον δὲ προσελθόντες δύο ψευδομάρτυρες». *Ὕστερον* = "afterward, later, at last"; *δὲ* = "but/and"; *προσελθόντες* is an aorist participle ("having come forward, having approached"), kept as a participle. The governing verb is lacking here — it is *εἶπον* ("said"), which opens the next lemma («εἶπον· Οὗτος ἔφη…» = "said: This *man* declared…"), where the exposition then follows.
