# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 51 · Matt. 26:57

> **…having seized Jesus, led *him* away to Caiaphas the high priest, where the scribes and the elders were gathered together; but *Peter*— (Matt. 26:57)**[^1]

*Theophylact gives no exposition at this point; the Gospel quotation runs on into Lemma 52, where his comment follows.*

[^1]: Continuation of the sentence begun at the end of Lemma 50 («Οἱ δὲ…», Matt. 26:57). The lemma breaks off at «ὁ δὲ» ("but *he*—"), completed in Lemma 52 by «Πέτρος» ("Peter"). OCR «Καῖάφαν» = «Καϊάφαν» (Caiaphas); «Κπρεσβύτεροι» = «πρεσβύτεροι» (elders).
