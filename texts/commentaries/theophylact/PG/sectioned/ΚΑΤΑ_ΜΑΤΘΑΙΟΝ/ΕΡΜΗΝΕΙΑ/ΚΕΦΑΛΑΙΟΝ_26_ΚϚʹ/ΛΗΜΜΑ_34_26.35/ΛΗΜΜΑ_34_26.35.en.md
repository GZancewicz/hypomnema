# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 34 · Matt. 26:35

> **Peter says to him: Even if *it* be necessary for me to die together with you, I will by no means deny you. And in like manner also all the disciples said. Then — (Matt. 26:35)**[^1]

*This lemma file carries only the scripture quotation, with no exposition; the comment resumes in Lemma 35.*[^2]

[^1]: The lemma is the whole of Matt. 26:35 with the opening word of 26:36 («Τότε» — "Then"). *δέῃ* is impersonal ("it may be necessary"), so the subject "*it*" is supplied and italicized. *Κἄν* = "even if / even though." «οὐ μή σε ἀπαρνήσωμαι» is an emphatic double negative — "I will by no means deny you." «Ὁμοίως… εἶπον» — "said likewise," the object ("the same") left implicit in the Greek as in this rendering.
[^2]: The file breaks off at «Τότε» ("Then —"), which is completed at the head of Lemma 35 («…ἔρχεται μετ᾽ αὐτῶν ὁ Ἰησοῦς εἰς χωρίον λεγόμενον Γεθσημανή» — "Then Jesus comes with them into a place called Gethsemane"). Theophylact supplies no comment on the verse here; the sectioning places his exposition in the next lemma.
