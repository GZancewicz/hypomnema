# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 39 · Matt. 26:42

> **Again, a second time,[^1] having gone away he prayed, saying: My Father, if this cup cannot pass from me unless I drink it, let thy will be done; and …[^2] (Matt. 26:42)**

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation, with no exposition of its own; Theophylact's comment begins in the following lemma.*

[^1]: Greek *πάλιν ἐκ δευτέρου* — "again, for the second time." Both words are present, so both are rendered ("again … a second time"), not conflated.
[^2]: The lemma breaks off after «γενηθήτω τὸ θέλημά σου· καὶ» ("let thy will be done; and"). The connective «καὶ» is completed at the head of Lemma 40, which resumes «ἐλθὼν εὑρίσκει αὐτοὺς πάλιν καθεύδοντας» ("having come he finds them sleeping again").
