# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 59 · Matt. 26:69

> **But Peter was sitting outside in the courtyard, and one maidservant came to him, saying: You also were with Jesus the Galilean? But he**[^1] (Matt. 26:69)

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation, which breaks off mid-sentence; the exposition belongs to a following lemma.*[^1]

[^1]: Greek «Ὁ δὲ Πέτρος ἔξω ἐκάθητο ἐν τῇ αὐλῇ, καὶ προσῆλθεν αὐτῷ μία παιδίσκη, λέγουσα· Καὶ σὺ ἦσθα μετὰ Ἰησοῦ τοῦ Γαλιλαίου; Ὁ δὲ» (OCR corrections: *προσῆλθεν*, *παιδίσκη*, *ἦσθα*, *μετὰ*). *αὐλή* = "courtyard, court" — the open court of the high priest's house. The sentence breaks off at *Ὁ δὲ* ("But he"), and is completed in the next lemma: «ἠρνήσατο ἔμπροσθεν πάντων…» ("he denied before all…").
