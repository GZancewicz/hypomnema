# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 33 · Matt. 26:33–34

> **If all shall be offended: —**[^1] *(Matt. 26:33)*

For it shows him *to be* a braggart,[^2] and not recognizing his own weakness. Therefore the Lord foretells to him both the hour — that *it will be* in this night, and before *a* cock crows — and the number of the denials, that he will deny for the third time.[^3]

[^1]: The blockquote is the fragment of Peter's boast (Matt. 26:33) that Theophylact called "full of arrogance to say" at the close of Lemma 32; the sentence thus runs continuously across the lemma boundary. Greek (OCR-garbled): «Εἰ πάντες σανλαλιεθήσονται», securely *«Εἰ πάντες σκανδαλισθήσονται»* ("If all shall be offended"). The clause is cited only as far as Theophylact repeats it; Peter's full words are given at Lemma 34.
[^2]: Greek *ἀλαζών* ("boaster, braggart, impostor" — one who claims more than he is); «δείκνυσι γὰρ αὐτὸν ἀλαζόνα». Paired with "not recognizing his own weakness (ἀσθένειαν)."
[^3]: The comment lists the three things the Lord foretells: the hour, the sign (before cockcrow), and the number. «ὅτι ἐκ τρίτου ἀρνήσεται» — "that he will deny ἐκ τρίτου," i.e. "for the third time / thrice over." OCR «νυκιὶ» is *νυκτὶ* ("night"). The sentence trails without final punctuation in the source but is complete in sense.
