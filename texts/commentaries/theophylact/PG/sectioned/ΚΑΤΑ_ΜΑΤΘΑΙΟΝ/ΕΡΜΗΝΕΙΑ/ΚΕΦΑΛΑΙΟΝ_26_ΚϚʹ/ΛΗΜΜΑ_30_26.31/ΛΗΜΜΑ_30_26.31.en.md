# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 30 · Matt. 26:31

> **Then Jesus says to them: All you will be made to stumble[^1] in me in this night. For it is written: I will strike the shepherd, and the sheep of the flock will be scattered.[^2] But after[^3] (Matt. 26:31)**

*This lemma-folder carries only the scripture quotation, with no exposition of its own; the sentence breaks off at "But after," and Theophylact's comment on the verse follows in the next lemma (Lemma 31), which completes it: «…my being raised, I will go before you into Galilee» and expounds how the Father is said to "strike" the Son by permission.*

[^1]: Greek *σκανδαλισθήσεσθε* — "you will be made to stumble / caused to fall away / offended." The verb belongs to the *σκάνδαλον* ("stumbling-block") family; here the disciples' faith will be tripped up on the night of the arrest. Rendered "will be made to stumble"; the traditional "will be offended" is an alternative.
[^2]: The lemma quotes Zech. 13:7 as at Matt. 26:31: «Πατάξω τὸν ποιμένα, καὶ διασκορπισθήσεται τὰ πρόβατα τῆς ποίμνης» ("I will strike the shepherd, and the sheep of the flock will be scattered"). The OCR *τὸ τοιμένα* = *τὸν ποιμένα* ("the shepherd"), and *διασκορπισθήσε.αι* = *διασκορπισθήσεται* ("will be scattered"). The added *τῆς ποίμνης* ("of the flock") follows the text Theophylact quotes.
[^3]: The quotation is cut off at «Μετὰ δὲ τὸ» ("But after the…"), continued in Lemma 31 with «…ἐγερθῆναί με, προάξω ὑμᾶς εἰς τὴν Γαλιλαίαν» ("…my being raised, I will go before you into Galilee," Matt. 26:32).
