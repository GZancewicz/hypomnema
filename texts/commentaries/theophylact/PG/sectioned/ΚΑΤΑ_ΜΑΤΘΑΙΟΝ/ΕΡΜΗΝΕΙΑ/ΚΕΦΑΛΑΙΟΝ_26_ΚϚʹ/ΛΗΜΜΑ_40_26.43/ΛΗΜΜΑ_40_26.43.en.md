# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 40 · Matt. 26:43–44

> **… having come he finds them sleeping again; for their eyes were[^1] weighed down. And having left them, having gone away he prayed again a third time, saying the same word. (Matt. 26:43–44)**

Learn, O man, to pray continually in *your* temptations, hearing that the Lord also prayed many times.[^2] But having found them sleeping again, he did not reprove *them*, in order that he might not grieve *them*, but let *them* be. And having gone away he prayed a third time, confirming that which is human;[^3] for the number three is indicative of truth and of confirmation.[^4]

[^1]: Greek OCR «Σααν γὰρ αὐτῶν οἱ ὀφθαλμοὶ βεβαρημένοι» — «Σααν» is a corruption, securely = **«ἦσαν»** ("were"), giving "for their eyes were weighed down" (*βεβαρημένοι*, perfect passive participle, "heavy, weighed down"). The reconstruction is certain from the periphrastic construction and the parallel Gospel text.
[^2]: Greek *πολλάκις ηὔχετο* — "prayed often / many times"; the imperfect underscores repeated praying.
[^3]: Greek *βεβαιῶν τὸ ἀνθρώπινον* — "confirming the human *element*," i.e. giving assurance of the reality of his human nature (which naturally shrinks from death); "that which is human" keeps the neuter substantive.
[^4]: Greek *ἀληθείας γὰρ ἐνδεικτικὸς ὁ τρία ἀριθμὸς καὶ βεβαιώσεως* — "for the number three is indicative of truth and of confirmation (assurance)." The threefold prayer is read as a token of the genuineness and firmness of the thing done.
