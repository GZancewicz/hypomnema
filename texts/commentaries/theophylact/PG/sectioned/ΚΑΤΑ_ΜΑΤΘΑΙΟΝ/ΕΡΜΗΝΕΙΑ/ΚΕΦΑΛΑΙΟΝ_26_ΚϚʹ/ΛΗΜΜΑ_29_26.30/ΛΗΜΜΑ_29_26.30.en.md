# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 29 · Matt. 26:30

> **[And] having sung a hymn, they went out to the mount of Olives. (Matt. 26:30)**[^1]

After[^2] they had dined, they sang a hymn, so that we might learn that we too must do thus. And he goes to the mount of Olives, and not to another place, so that he might not seem to flee; for he does not withdraw to an unknown place, but to *one* familiar to the Jews. And at the same time he also retires from the murderous[^3] city, having abandoned it, so that he might be pursued, and might reprove them later, because they pursued *him* even *as he was* withdrawing.

[^1]: Greek «[Καὶ] ὑμνήσαντες, ἐξῆλθον εἰς τὸ ὄρος τῶν Ἐλαιῶν». The opening *καὶ* is carried over from the end of the previous lemma. *ὑμνήσαντες* is an aorist participle, "having sung a hymn"; rendered as such to keep the participle visible.
[^2]: The OCR *ἍΙεθʹ ὃ* is securely *Μεθ' ὃ* ("after which / after"); low-stakes restoration, noted for consistency with the other OCR corruptions.
[^3]: Greek *μιαιφόνου* — "blood-stained, murderous, defiled with (the guilt of) murder." A strong compound (*μίασμα* + *φόνος*); rendered "murderous" while preserving the note of pollution.
