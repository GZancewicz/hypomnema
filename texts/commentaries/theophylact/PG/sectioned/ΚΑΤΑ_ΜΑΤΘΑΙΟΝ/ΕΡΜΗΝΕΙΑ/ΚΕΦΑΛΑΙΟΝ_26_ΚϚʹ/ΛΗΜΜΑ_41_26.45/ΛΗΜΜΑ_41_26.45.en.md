# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 41 · Matt. 26:45

> **Then he comes to his disciples, and says to them: Sleep on now[^1] and take *your* rest. Behold, the hour has drawn near, and the Son of man is delivered up[^2] into *the* hands of sinners. …[^3] (Matt. 26:45)**

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation, with no exposition of its own; Theophylact's comment begins in the following lemma.*

[^1]: Greek *καθεύδετε τὸ λοιπόν* — "sleep *for* the rest / henceforth / now." *τὸ λοιπόν* ("as for the rest") is adverbial; "now" reads it temporally, as the exposition in the next lemma requires.
[^2]: Greek *παραδίδοται* — "is delivered up / handed over / betrayed"; the same verb whose participle names Judas ("the *one* betraying me"). Rendered "delivered up" here for the passive, keeping the courtroom/handing-over sense.
[^3]: The lemma breaks off after «εἰς χεῖρας ἁμαρτωλῶν» ("into *the* hands of sinners"). It is completed at the head of Lemma 42, which resumes «Ἐγείρεσθε, ἄγωμεν …» ("Rise up, let us go …").
