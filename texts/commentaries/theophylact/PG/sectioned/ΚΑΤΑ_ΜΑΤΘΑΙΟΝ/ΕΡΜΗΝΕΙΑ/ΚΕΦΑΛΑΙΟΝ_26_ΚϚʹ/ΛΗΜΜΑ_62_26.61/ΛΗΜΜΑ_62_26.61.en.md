# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 62 · Matt. 26:73-74

> **— a little while, those standing by, having come near, said to Peter: Truly you also are *one* of them; for indeed your speech makes you evident.[^1] Then he began to invoke curses *on himself* and to swear, that: I do not know the man.[^2] And immediately a cock crowed; and —**[^3]

[^1]: Greek «καὶ γὰρ ἡ λαλιά σου δῆλόν σε ποιεῖ» — "for indeed your speech makes you evident/manifest," i.e., Peter's Galilean manner of speaking betrays him. The γάρ ("for indeed") is rendered rather than dropped.
[^2]: «Τότε ἤρξατο καταναθεματίζειν καὶ ὀμνύειν» — «καταναθεματίζειν» is to call down curses (the intensive κατα- form); here it is self-imprecation ("*on himself*" supplied), Peter swearing under a curse that he does not know the man, paired with «ὀμνύειν» ("to swear").
[^3]: Like Lemma 61, this block is pure Gospel citation with no exposition; the exposition of the whole passage follows at Lemma 63. It opens with «μικρὸν», completing the «μετὰ» ("after") that ended Lemma 61 — hence "after a little while" — and breaks off at «καὶ», completed in Lemma 63 by «ἐμνήσθη ὁ Πέτρος …» ("Peter remembered …"). Minor OCR: «ο ἑστῶτες»→«οἱ ἑστῶτες» ("those standing"), «αὸν Κνθρωπον»→«τὸν ἄνθρωπον» ("the man"); securely recovered.
