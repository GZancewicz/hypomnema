# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 37 · Matt. 26:40

> **And he comes to the disciples, and finds them sleeping, and says to Peter: So, were you not able[^1] *for* one hour …[^2] (Matt. 26:40)**

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation, with no exposition of its own; Theophylact's comment begins in the following lemma.*

[^1]: Greek *ἰσχύσατε* (from *ἰσχύω*, "to be strong, to be able"); with the infinitive *γρηγορῆσαι* ("to watch"), which the source supplies only at the head of the next lemma, it means "you were not strong *enough* / not able to watch." The sense-verb thus straddles the section break: "were you not able … to watch with me?"
[^2]: The lemma breaks off mid-clause after «μίαν ὥραν» ("*for* one hour" — accusative of duration; the supplied "*for*" is italicized). The sentence is completed at the head of Lemma 38, which resumes with «γρηγορῆσαι μετ᾽ ἐμοῦ;» ("to watch with me?").
