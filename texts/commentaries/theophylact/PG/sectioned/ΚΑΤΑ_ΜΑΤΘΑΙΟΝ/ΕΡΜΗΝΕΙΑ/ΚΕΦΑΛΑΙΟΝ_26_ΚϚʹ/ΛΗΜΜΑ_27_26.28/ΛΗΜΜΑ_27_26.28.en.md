# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 27 · Matt. 26:29

> **But I say to you, that I will by no means drink from now on of[^1] (Matt. 26:29)**

*This lemma-folder carries only the opening words of the verse, with no exposition; the sentence breaks off and is completed in the next lemma (Lemma 28), which supplies «this fruit of the vine, until that day…» together with Theophylact's comment. The folder metadata verse (26.28) is corrected to 26:29.*

[^1]: Greek «Λέγω δὲ ὑμῖν, ὅτι οὐ μὴ πίω ἀπ' ἄρτι ἐκ…» ("But I say to you, that I will by no means drink from now on of…"). The initial *Δέγω* in the OCR is *Λέγω* ("I say"). The emphatic double negative *οὐ μὴ* + subjunctive is rendered "by no means … drink." The clause is cut off at *ἐκ* ("of / out of") and continues in Lemma 28 with *τούτου τοῦ γεννήματος τῆς ἀμπέλου* ("this fruit of the vine").
