# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 43 · Matt. 26:47-48

> **And while he *was* still speaking, behold, Judas, one of the twelve, came, and with him a great crowd with swords and clubs, from the chief priests and elders of the people. But the one betraying him gave them a sign, saying: "Whomever I kiss, he is *the one*; seize him." And** (Matt. 26:47-48)[^1]

This lemma carries only the Gospel quotation, which breaks off at "And";[^2] the exposition begins in the following lemma, where the quotation is continued («immediately having come to Jesus…»).

[^1]: The lemma is transmitted with several minor OCR corruptions, all securely recoverable: «ἰβοὺ» → **ἰδοὺ** ("behold"), «ώδεκα» → **δώδεκα** ("twelve"), «πρεσυυτέρων» → **πρεσβυτέρων** ("elders"), «Κδοοκεν» → **ἔδωκεν** ("gave"). None affects the sense. Greek «αὐτός ἐστι» is literally "he is"; the supplied "*the one*" completes the elliptical predicate.
[^2]: The clause ends on «Καὶ» ("And"), which runs directly into the next lemma's «εὐθέως προσελθὼν τῷ Ἰησοῦ» ("immediately having come to Jesus"). Theophylact has divided a single continuous Gospel sentence across the two lemma files.
