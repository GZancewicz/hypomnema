# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 54 · Matt. 26:60

> **—find *it*. And *when* many false witnesses had come forward, they did not find *it*. (Matt. 26:60)**[^1]

To Caiaphas they lead Jesus away; for that man was high priest of that year.[^2] There also the rest passed the night, not having eaten the Passover then, but having waited, so that they might kill the Lord, and having transgressed the law. For the Lord, on the one hand, ate *it* at the appointed time; but those men, on the other hand, in order only that they might kill the Lord, despised even the law.[^3]

[^1]: Continuation from Lemma 53 (Matt. 26:59–60): «εὗρον» completes the «καὶ οὐχ» of Lemma 53 — "and did not find *it*." The genitive absolute *πολλῶν ψευδομαρτύρων προσελθόντων* ("many false witnesses having come forward") is rendered "when many false witnesses had come forward." OCR «ψευδομαρτόρων» = «ψευδομαρτύρων».
[^2]: Greek *ἀρχιερεὺς τοῦ ἐνιαυτοῦ ἐκείνου* — "high priest of that year"; cf. John 11:49–51; 18:13. OCR «Κις τὸν Καάφαν» = «Εἰς τὸν Καϊάφαν»; «Κφωγεν» = «ἔφαγεν» ("ate").
[^3]: Greek *κατὰ τὸν νενομισμένον καιρόν* — "at the appointed/customary time," i.e. the time fixed by the Law. Theophylact's point: the Lord kept the Passover at its lawful time, whereas the rulers, in their haste to kill him, postponed their own eating of it and so themselves transgressed the Law. The supplied "*it*" is the object of *ἔφαγεν* (the Passover).
