# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 49 · Matt. 26:56

> **But all this has come to pass, so that the Scriptures of the prophets might be fulfilled. (Matt. 26:56)**[^1]

He shows their irrational undertaking,[^2] and that the seizing of him did not come to be from their own strength. "For when I was," he says, "in the temple, you were wishing to lay hold of me, and because I was not permitting you, you were not able; but now, willingly, I deliver up myself.[^3] For I know that it is impossible for the Scriptures to be proved false, which foretold your wickedness."

[^1]: This is the tail of the Gospel quotation begun in Lemma 48 (Matt. 26:55–56). The OCR of the source reads «τοῦτο δὲ δλον γέγονεν ἵνα πληρωθῶσιν αἱΓραφαὶ τῶν προφητῶν»; securely = «τοῦτο δὲ ὅλον γέγονεν ἵνα πληρωθῶσιν αἱ Γραφαὶ τῶν προφητῶν» (δλον→ὅλον; the word-split «αἱ Γραφαὶ»). *γέγονεν* is perfect: "has come to pass."
[^2]: Greek *ἐπιχείρησιν* — "attempt, undertaking, enterprise"; here the mob's assault on Jesus.
[^3]: Greek *ἐκὼν προδίδωμι ἐμαυτόν*. *προδίδωμι* can mean "betray" as well as "give up / deliver over"; rendered "deliver up" for the plain sense (he surrenders himself of his own accord), though the verb also echoes the betrayal-vocabulary of the passion. *ἐκών* = "willing, of my own will."
