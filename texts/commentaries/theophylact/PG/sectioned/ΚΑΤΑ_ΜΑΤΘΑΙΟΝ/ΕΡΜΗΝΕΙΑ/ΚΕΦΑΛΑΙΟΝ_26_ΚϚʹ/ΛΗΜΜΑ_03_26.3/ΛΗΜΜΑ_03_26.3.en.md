# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 3 · Matt. 26:3

> **Then were gathered together the chief priests and the scribes[^1] and the elders of the people into the court of the high priest, the one called Caiaphas, and**[^2]

*This lemma carries only the scripture text; the sentence breaks off at "and," and the exposition is given in Lemma 4, which continues «συνεβουλεύσαντο ἵνα τὸν Ἰησοῦν δόλῳ κρατήσωσι…».*

[^1]: Greek «καὶ οἱ Γραμματεῖς» ("and the scribes"). Many witnesses of Matt. 26:3 read only "the chief priests and the elders of the people," without "the scribes"; Theophylact's text includes them. Translated as it stands, per the rule of following the text on the page.
[^2]: Greek «Τότε συνήχθησαν οἱ ἀρχιερεῖς καὶ οἱ Γραμματεῖς καὶ οἱ πρεσβύτεροι τοῦ λαοῦ εἰς τὴν αὐλὴν τοῦ ἀρχιερέως τοῦ λεγομένου Καῖάφα, καὶ». *αὐλή* rendered "court" (the courtyard/palace-enclosure of the high priest).
