# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 45 · Matt. 26:51

> **And behold, one of those with Jesus, having stretched out *his* hand, drew his sword, and, having struck the servant of the chief priest, took off his ear. Then** (Matt. 26:51)[^1]

This lemma carries only the Gospel quotation, which breaks off at "Then";[^2] the exposition begins two lemmata further on (Lemma 47), after the Lord's reply has been quoted in full.

[^1]: The OCR «τὸν λήντίον» is a certain corruption of **τὸ ὠτίον** ("the ear," diminutive of *οὖς*), the reading required by «ἀφεῖλεν αὐτοῦ» ("took off from him"). Greek «ἐκτείνας τὴν χεῖρα» is literally "having stretched out the hand"; "*his*" is supplied for the possessive that the Greek carries by context.
[^2]: The clause ends on «Τότε» ("Then"), which runs directly into the next lemma's «λέγει αὐτῷ ὁ Ἰησοῦς» ("Jesus says to him"). Theophylact has divided Christ's words to Peter across Lemmata 45-47.
