# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 46 · Matt. 26:52

> **Jesus says to him: "Turn back your sword into its place. For all who take *the* sword, by *the* sword shall die; or** (Matt. 26:52)[^1]

This lemma carries only the Gospel quotation, which breaks off at "or";[^2] the exposition begins in the following lemma, after the Lord's saying about the twelve legions of angels has been quoted.

[^1]: Greek «οἱ λαβόντες μάχαιραν, ἐν μαχαίρᾳ ἀποθανοῦνται» — "those who took *a* sword, by *a* sword shall die"; *μάχαιρα* is anarthrous in both places, so the supplied "*the*" is italicized. The aorist participle «λαβόντες» ("having taken / who took") is rendered "who take" for the gnomic sense.
[^2]: The clause ends on «ἣ» ("or"), which runs directly into the next lemma's «δοκεῖς ὅτι οὐ δύναμαι…» ("do you think that I am not able…"). The single sentence of Matt. 26:52-54 is split across Lemmata 46 and 47.
