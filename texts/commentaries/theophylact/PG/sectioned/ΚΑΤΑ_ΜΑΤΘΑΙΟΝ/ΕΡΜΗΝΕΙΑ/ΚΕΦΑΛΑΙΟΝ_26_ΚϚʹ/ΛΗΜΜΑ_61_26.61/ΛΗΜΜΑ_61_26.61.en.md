# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 61 · Matt. 26:71-72

> **But when he had gone out into the gateway, another *woman*[^1] saw him, and says to those there: This one also was with Jesus the Nazarene;[^2] and again he denied with an oath, that: I do not know the man; and after —**[^3]

[^1]: ἄλλη is feminine ("another *female*"); the noun is unexpressed in the Greek, so "*woman*" is supplied. It denotes a second maidservant, distinct from the παιδίσκη of Matt. 26:69 (cf. Lemma 63, where Theophylact allegorizes the maid).
[^2]: Greek «Καὶ οὗτος ἦν μετὰ Ἰησοῦ τοῦ Ναζωραίου» — "This one **also** was with Jesus the Nazarene." Theophylact's text carries the emphatic καί ("also"), which the common critical text of Matthew here lacks; rendered as it stands on the page.
[^3]: This lemma is a continuous stretch of the Gospel text with no exposition of its own; Theophylact quotes the whole scene of Peter's denial in successive blocks (Lemmata 60–63) and comments on it only at Lemma 63. The block breaks off at «μετὰ» ("after"), completed in Lemma 62 by «μικρὸν … » ("… a little while"). Minor OCR corruptions in this block are securely recoverable: «λένει»→«λέγει» ("says"), «κτάλιν»→«πάλιν» ("again"), «αῖδα»→«οἶδα» ("I know").
