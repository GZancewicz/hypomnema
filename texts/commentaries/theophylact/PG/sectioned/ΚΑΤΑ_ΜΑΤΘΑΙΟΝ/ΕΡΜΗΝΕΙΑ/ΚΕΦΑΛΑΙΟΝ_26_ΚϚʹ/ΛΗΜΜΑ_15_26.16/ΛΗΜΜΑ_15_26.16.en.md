# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 15 · Matt. 26:16

*This lemma carries no scripture quotation at its head; it continues Theophylact's exposition of Matt. 26:16 ("he sought a good opportunity"), then breaks off into the citation of the next verse.*[^1]

He sought,[^2] therefore, a good opportunity to deliver him up to them *when he was* alone;[^3] for they feared the crowd, and for this reason they hired Judas, that he might disclose to them when he is by himself.

> **But on the *first day of the unleavened bread*... (Matt. 26:17)**[^4]

[^1]: In the running text the words «Ἐζήτει οὖν εὐκαιρίαν» echo the Gospel lemma of 26:16 but are Theophylact's own paraphrase-comment, not the quoted verse; the next actual Gospel quotation begins with «Τῇ δὲ», upon which this file breaks off.
[^2]: Greek *ἐζήτει*, imperfect — "he kept seeking / was seeking"; rendered "he sought" for smoothness, the iterative force noted here.
[^3]: Greek *μόνον αὐτὸν παραδοῦναι αὐτοῖς*: the adverb *μόνον* ("alone / only") is taken with the following clause ("when he is by himself," *καταμόνας*), i.e. to hand Jesus over while he was apart from the crowd; it could alternatively be read "merely to hand him over to them."
[^4]: The file ends mid-citation at «Τῇ δὲ» ("But on the…"); the Gospel quotation of Matt. 26:17 is continued in the following lemma. The bracketed continuation is supplied from that lemma for orientation and is italicized.
