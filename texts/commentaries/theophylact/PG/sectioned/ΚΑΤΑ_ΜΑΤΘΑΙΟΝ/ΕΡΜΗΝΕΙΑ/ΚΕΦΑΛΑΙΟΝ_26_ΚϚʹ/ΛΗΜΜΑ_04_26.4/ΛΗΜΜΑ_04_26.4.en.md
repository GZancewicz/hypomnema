# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 4 · Matt. 26:4–5

> **they took counsel together that they might seize Jesus by guile and kill *him*; but they said: Not on the feast, lest a tumult come to be among the people.**[^1]

Although the law commanded that there be a high priest for life,[^2] they made many of them unlawfully, changing them year by year; wherefore also they come to the high priest of that year to take counsel about murder — from whom rather it was necessary that murderers[^3] be punished. And by "chief priests" he means those who had already fulfilled their yearly ministration.[^4] But being about to perpetrate the unholy murder, they fear not God but the crowd. For they were afraid lest somehow, if they killed him on the feast, the crowd be roused to vengeance, or also abstain from bringing the customary sacrifices because of their bloody murder,[^5] and they themselves lose the gains from the sacrifices; and perhaps they feared this also, lest, if he were killed on the feast, his death become notable and glorious — for they wished to blot out the memory of him.

They, on the one hand, therefore, having thus taken counsel before the feast to kill *him* perhaps after the feast; but he, on the other hand, showing that he suffers not when they willed, but when he himself willed, permitted them to seize *him* at the time of the Passover — so that when the typical Passover was taking place, then also the true Passover might come forth.[^6] And it is possible also to discern their bloodthirstiness. For those who did not wish to kill him on the feast, when they found the betrayer, then they kill *him*, having despised even the crowd, only that their will might come to pass.

[^1]: Greek «συνεβουλεύσαντο ἵνα τὸν Ἰησοῦν δόλῳ κρατήσωσι καὶ ἀποκτείνωσιν· ἔλεγον δέ· Μὴ ἐν τῇ ἑορτῇ, ἵνα μὴ θόρυβος γένηται ἐν τῷ λαῷ» (OCR *ἄλεγον*, *θάρυβος*). *δόλῳ* = "by guile / by cunning."
[^2]: Greek *Τοῦ νόμου κελεύοντος [ἵ]να εἶναι ἀρχιερέα διὰ βίου* — a concessive genitive absolute ("the law commanding…"), rendered "Although the law commanded…"; *διὰ βίου* = "for life, throughout life."
[^3]: The OCR here is corrupt: «ὅθεν ἔδει μᾶλλον τοὺ‹ς› ‹σο›ναυτὰς κολάζεσθαι». The word garbled as *σοναυτὰς* is best reconstructed as **φονευτὰς** ("murderers"): the sense is that from the high priest — whose office it was to punish bloodshed — murderers ought rather to have been punished, whereas here men come to him to *plot* a murder. Marked [?] because the reconstruction, though strongly favoured by the sense (φ→σ, ευ→ο, and καθαρίζειν/κολάζεσθαι context), is not certain from the letters alone. An alternative would take the corrupt word as "such men / these men."
[^4]: Greek «ἀρχιερεῖς δὲ λέγει τοὺς πεπληρωκότας ἤδη τὴν ἐνιαύσιον λειτουργίαν» — i.e. the plural "chief priests" denotes former annual high priests who had already completed their year of office. *λειτουργία* rendered "ministration" (liturgical service).
[^5]: Greek *μιαιφονία* ("blood-pollution, foul murder"); rendered "bloody murder" here and "bloodthirstiness" below (same root), the crime being defiling as well as violent.
[^6]: Greek «ἵνα ὅτε τὸ τυπικὸν Πάσχα ἐγίνετο, τότε καὶ τὸ ἀληθινὸν Πάσχα ἐκβαίη» — the antithesis *τυπικόν* ("typical / figurative," the Mosaic paschal lamb) vs. *ἀληθινόν* ("true," Christ himself as the true Passover). *ἐκβαίη* ("might come forth / turn out / be accomplished").
