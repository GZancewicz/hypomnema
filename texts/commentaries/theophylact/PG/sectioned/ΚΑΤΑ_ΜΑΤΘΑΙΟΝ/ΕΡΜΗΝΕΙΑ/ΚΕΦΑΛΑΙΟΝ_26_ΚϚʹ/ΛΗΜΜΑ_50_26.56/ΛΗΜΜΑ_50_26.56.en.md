# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 50 · Matt. 26:56

> **Then all the disciples, having left him, fled. (Matt. 26:56) Now those *who*—**[^1]

*Theophylact gives no exposition at this point; the lemma runs directly on into the next Gospel quotation (Matt. 26:57), which continues through Lemmata 51 and 52, and his comment on this whole run of verses appears in Lemma 52.*

[^1]: The Gospel quotation of Matt. 26:56b, «Τότε οἱ μαθηταὶ πάντες ἀφέντες αὐτὸν ἔφυγον» ("Then all the disciples, having left him, fled"). The final words «Οἱ δὴ,» (for «Οἱ δὲ», "Now those who / but those who") open the quotation of Matt. 26:57, whose sentence is completed in Lemma 51: «…κρατήσαντες τὸν Ἰησοῦν, ἀπήγαγον πρὸς Καϊάφαν…» ("…having seized Jesus, led *him* away to Caiaphas…").
