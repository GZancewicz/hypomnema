# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 12 · Matt. 26:12–13

> **For she, in having cast this ointment upon my body, did *it* for the *purpose of* preparing me for burial. Amen I say to you, wherever […] (Matt. 26:12–13)**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture text, breaking off mid-sentence; Theophylact's exposition begins with the next lemma, which completes the quotation ("…this gospel is preached in all the world, that also which she did shall be spoken, for a memorial of her") and comments upon it.*[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Βαλοῦσα γὰρ αὕτη τὸ μύρον τοῦτο ἐπὶ τοῦ σώματός μου, πρὸς τὸ ἐνταφιάσαι με ἐποίησε. Ἀμὴν λέγω ὑμῖν, ὅπου ἐὰν…» *Βαλοῦσα* = "having cast / having poured"; *πρὸς τὸ ἐνταφιάσαι με* = "toward the preparing-me-for-burial," a purpose expression (articular infinitive), rendered "for the *purpose of* preparing me for burial." *Ἀμὴν* is transliterated per convention.
[^2]: The sentence breaks off at «ὅπου ἐὰν» ("wherever, if…"). The next lemma resumes with «κηρυχθῇ τὸ Εὐαγγέλιον τοῦτο ἐν ὅλῳ τῷ κόσμῳ, λαληθήσεται καὶ ὃ ἐποίησεν αὕτη, εἰς μνημόσυνον αὐτῆς» (Matt. 26:13).
