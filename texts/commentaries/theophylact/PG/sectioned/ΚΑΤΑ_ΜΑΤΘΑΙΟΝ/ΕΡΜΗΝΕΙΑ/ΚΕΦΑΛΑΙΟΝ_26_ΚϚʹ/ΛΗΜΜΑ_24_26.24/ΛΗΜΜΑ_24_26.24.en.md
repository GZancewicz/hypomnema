# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 24 · Matt. 26:26

> **But as they were eating, Jesus, having taken the bread and having given thanks, broke *it* and was giving *it* to the disciples, and said: Take, eat; this is my body.**[^1]

For this reason he added the *word* "as they were eating," in order that he might set forth the inhumanity of Judas: that at *the* table and in the fellowship of the same foods — when, even if he had been a wild beast, he would have become gentler — then not even being reproved did he understand, but, even tasting his body, he does not repent. But some say that, Judas having gone out, he imparted the mysteries to the other disciples. Therefore we too ought to do thus, and to bar the wicked from the mysteries. And he gives thanks *when* about to break the bread: on the one hand, in order that he might also teach us to offer the bread with thanksgiving; and on the other hand, also in order that he might show that he thankfully receives the breaking of his own body — that is, the putting-to-death — and is not displeased as at something involuntary; and in order that we too might thus thankfully receive martyrdom. [^1]: Folder metadata labels this 26.24, but the Greek «Λάβετε, φάγετε· τοῦτό ἐστι τὸ σῶμά μου» is Matt. 26:26 (the institution of the Eucharist); the header and blockquote ref are corrected accordingly. *ἐδίδου* is imperfect ("was giving"); the objects "*it*" are supplied.
[^2]: The exposition breaks off at «Εἰπὼν δὲ,» ("And having said,"); the sentence is completed in the following lemma (26:28).
