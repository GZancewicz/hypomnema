# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 16 · Matt. 26:17

> **But on the first *day* of the unleavened bread, the disciples came to Jesus, saying to him: Where do you wish that we should prepare for you to eat the Passover? But he... (Matt. 26:17)**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the Gospel citation of Matt. 26:17, with no exposition of its own; Theophylact's comment on the passage is gathered at the close of the sequence (lemma 18).*

[^1]: The citation opens with «Τῇ δὲ» carried over from the previous lemma and breaks off at «Ὁ δὲ» ("But he…"), completed in the next lemma. "*day*" is supplied — the Greek τῇ πρώτῃ τῶν ἀζύμων ("on the first of the unleavened bread") has no noun for "day."
