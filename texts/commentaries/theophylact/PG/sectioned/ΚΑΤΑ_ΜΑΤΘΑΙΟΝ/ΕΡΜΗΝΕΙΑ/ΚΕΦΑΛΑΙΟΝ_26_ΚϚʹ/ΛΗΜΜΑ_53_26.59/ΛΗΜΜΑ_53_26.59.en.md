# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 53 · Matt. 26:59

> **The chief priests and the elders and the whole council were seeking false witness against Jesus, so that they might put him to death, and did not— (Matt. 26:59)**[^1]

*Theophylact gives no exposition at this point; the sentence is completed in Lemma 54, where his comment follows.*

[^1]: The lemma breaks off at «καὶ οὐχ» ("and did not—"), completed in Lemma 54 by «εὗρον» ("find *it*"). Greek *συνέδριον* = the council, i.e. the Sanhedrin; *ἐζήτουν* (OCR «ἐξήτουν») is imperfect, "were seeking"; *ψευδομαρτυρίαν* = "false testimony."
