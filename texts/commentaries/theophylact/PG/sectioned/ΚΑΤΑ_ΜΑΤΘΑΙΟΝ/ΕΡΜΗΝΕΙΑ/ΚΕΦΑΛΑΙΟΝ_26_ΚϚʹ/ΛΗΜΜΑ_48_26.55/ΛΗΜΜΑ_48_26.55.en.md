# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 48 · Matt. 26:55

> **In that hour Jesus said to the crowds: "As against a robber did you come out, with swords and clubs, to seize me? Daily I sat with you teaching in the temple, and you did not seize me;** (Matt. 26:55)[^1]

This lemma carries only the Gospel quotation, which breaks off at "and you did not seize me";[^2] the exposition begins in the following lemma, after the quotation is completed («but this whole *thing* has come to pass that the Scriptures of the prophets might be fulfilled»).

[^1]: Greek *λῃστής* means "robber, brigand, bandit" (one who plunders by violence), not merely "thief"; rendered "robber." «ἐκαθεζόμην διδάσκων» — "I was sitting *and* teaching," the seated posture of the teacher; rendered "I sat… teaching."
[^2]: The clause ends on «καὶ οὐκ ἐκρατήσατέ με» ("and you did not seize me"), continued in the next lemma by «τοῦτο δὲ ὅλον γέγονεν ἵνα πληρωθῶσιν αἱ Γραφαὶ τῶν προφητῶν» ("but this whole thing has happened that the Scriptures of the prophets might be fulfilled"). Matt. 26:55-56 is split across Lemmata 48 and 49.
