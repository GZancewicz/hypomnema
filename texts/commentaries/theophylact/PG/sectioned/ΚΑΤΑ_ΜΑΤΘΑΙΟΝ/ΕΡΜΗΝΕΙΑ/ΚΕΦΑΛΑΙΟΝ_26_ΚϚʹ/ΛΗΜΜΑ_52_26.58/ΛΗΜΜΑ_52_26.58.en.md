# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 52 · Matt. 26:58

> **…*Peter* followed him from afar, unto the court of the high priest; and having entered within, he sat with the officers, to see the end. (Matt. 26:58)**[^1]

The other disciples, on the one hand, fled; but Peter, on the other hand, being more warmly disposed toward the teacher, followed from afar. And even if John also was following, yet not as a disciple, but as an acquaintance of the high priest.[^2]

[^1]: Continuation of the sentence begun at Lemma 51 (Matt. 26:58), here completed by «Πέτρος» ("Peter"). Greek *ὑπηρετῶν* = "officers, attendants, servants" (the high priest's men). *τὸ τέλος* = "the end / the outcome." OCR «δεῖν τὸ τέλος» = «ἰδεῖν τὸ τέλος»; likewise «ἠκολοσθει», «οθχ», «ἁς» = «ἠκολούθει», «οὐχ», «ὡς».
[^2]: Greek *γνώριμος τοῦ ἀρχιερέως* — "an acquaintance / a familiar of the high priest"; cf. John 18:15–16, where the unnamed "other disciple," traditionally identified as John, was "known to the high priest" (*γνωστὸς τῷ ἀρχιερεῖ*) and so gained entry to the court.
