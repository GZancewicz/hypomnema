# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 22 · Matt. 26:23

> **But he answering said: He who dipped his hand with me in the dish, this one will betray me. On the one hand the Son of**[^1]

[^1]: Scripture-only lemma, breaking off at «Ὁ μὲν Υἱὸς τοῦ» ("On the one hand the Son of…"); the sentence continues in Lemma 23 (26:24). Greek «Ὁ δὲ ἀποκριθεὶς εἶπεν· Ὁ ἐμβάψας μετ' ἐμοῦ ἐν τῷ τρυβλίῳ τὴν χεῖρα, οὗτός με παραδώσει.» *ἐμβάψας* = "having dipped"; *τρύβλιον* = "dish, bowl". The *μὲν* here anticipates a *δὲ* ("but woe…") that arrives in the next lemma.
