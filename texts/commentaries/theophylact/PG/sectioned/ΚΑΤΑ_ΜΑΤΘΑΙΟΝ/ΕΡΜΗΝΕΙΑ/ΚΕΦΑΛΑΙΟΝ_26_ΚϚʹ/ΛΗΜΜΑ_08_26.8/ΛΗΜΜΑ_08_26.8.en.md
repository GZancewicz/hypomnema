# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 8 · Matt. 26:8

> **But his disciples, having seen *it*, were indignant, saying: To what end *is* this waste? (Matt. 26:8)**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture text; Theophylact adds no exposition here, his comment continuing after the next lemma's citation.*[^2]

[^1]: Greek «Ἰδόντες δὲ οἱ μαθηταὶ αὐτοῦ, ἠγανάκτησαν, λέγοντες· Εἰς τί ἡ ἀπώλεια αὕτη;» *Εἰς τί* = "to what *purpose* / to what end / why." *ἡ ἀπώλεια* = "the waste / loss / destruction" — the noun means "loss, ruin," here of the ointment poured out; "waste" is the sense the disciples intend.
[^2]: The exposition proper resumes at Lemma 10, where Theophylact treats the disciples' complaint and Christ's reply together.
