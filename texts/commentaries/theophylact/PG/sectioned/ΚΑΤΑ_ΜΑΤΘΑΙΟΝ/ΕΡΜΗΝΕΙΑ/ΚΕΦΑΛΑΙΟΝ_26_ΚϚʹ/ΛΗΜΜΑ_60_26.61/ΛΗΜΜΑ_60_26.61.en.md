# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 60 · Matt. 26:70

> **denied before all, saying: I do not know what you are saying.**[^1] (Matt. 26:70)

*This lemma continues the sentence begun in the previous lemma ("But Peter was sitting… and one maidservant… But he —"); it carries only the scripture quotation, and passes on into the following lemma. No exposition attaches here.*[^1]

[^1]: Greek «ἠρνήσατο ἔμπροσθεν πάντων, λέγων· Οὐκ οἶδα τί λέγεις» (OCR corrections: *ἔμπροσθεν*, *τί λέγεις*), completing Matt. 26:70. The subject "he" (Peter) is carried over from lemma 59 (*Ὁ δὲ … ἠρνήσατο* = "But he … denied"). The narrative continues in the next lemma: «Ἐξελθόντα δὲ αὐτὸν εἰς τὸν πυλῶνα…» ("But when he had gone out into the gateway…").
