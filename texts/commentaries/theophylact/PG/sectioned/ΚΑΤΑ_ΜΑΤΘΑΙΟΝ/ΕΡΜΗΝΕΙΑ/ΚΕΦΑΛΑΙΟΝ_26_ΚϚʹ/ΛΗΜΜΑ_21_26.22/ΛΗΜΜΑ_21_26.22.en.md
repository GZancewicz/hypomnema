# Matthew 26 (ΚϚʹ) — Lemma 21 · Matt. 26:22

> **grieving exceedingly, they began to say to him, each of them: Surely it is not I, Lord?**[^1]

From this some suppose that in that year the Lord did not eat the Passover; for they say that standing they used to eat the lamb, but Christ was reclining — therefore he did not eat the Passover. We say, therefore, that he first ate the Passover standing; then, having reclined, he delivered his own mystery; for having first completed the typical *Passover*, thus he accomplished the true *one*.[^2] And he foretells the things concerning Judas, in order that he might correct him — *Judas* being shamed into reverence, if by nothing else, at least by the common table, and having come to know that he is about to betray God, him who knows the hearts. But the disciples were in anguish;[^3] for even if they had a clean conscience, nevertheless they trusted Christ more than themselves, as *knowing* that he better knew their hearts.

[^1]: Greek «Μή τι ἐγώ εἰμι, Κύριε;» — a question with *μή* expecting the answer "no". Literally "It is not I, is it, Lord?"; rendered "Surely it is not I, Lord?" to keep the negative expectation visible.
[^2]: The antithesis is *τὸ τυπικόν* ("the typical / figurative *Passover*") vs. *τὸ ἀληθινόν* ("the true *one*"). Both are neuter adjectives with the noun supplied; the contrast is the figural Mosaic Passover against the Eucharistic reality.
[^3]: Greek *ἠγωνίων* — "were in agony / distressed / anxious".
