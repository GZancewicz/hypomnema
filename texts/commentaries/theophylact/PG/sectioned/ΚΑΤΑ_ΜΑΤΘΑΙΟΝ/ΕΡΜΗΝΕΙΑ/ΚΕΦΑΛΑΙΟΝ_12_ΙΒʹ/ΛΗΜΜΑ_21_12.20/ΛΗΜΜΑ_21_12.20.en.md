# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 21 · Matt. 12:20

> **Until he cast forth judgment unto victory; and in the…**[^1]

[^1]: Greek «Ἕως ἂν ἐκβάλῃ εἰς νῖκος τὴν κρίσιν· καὶ τῷ…» (Isa. 42:3–4, at Matt. 12:20–21). *ἐκβάλῃ εἰς νῖκος τὴν κρίσιν* — "until he cast/bring forth judgment unto victory"; *κρίσις* here in the sense established earlier in the chapter as "judgment / discernment / teaching." The sentence is left unfinished at *καὶ τῷ* ("and in the…"); the completing words «τῷ ὀνόματι αὐτοῦ ἔθνη ἐλπιοῦσιν» are quoted and expounded at the head of Lemma 22.
