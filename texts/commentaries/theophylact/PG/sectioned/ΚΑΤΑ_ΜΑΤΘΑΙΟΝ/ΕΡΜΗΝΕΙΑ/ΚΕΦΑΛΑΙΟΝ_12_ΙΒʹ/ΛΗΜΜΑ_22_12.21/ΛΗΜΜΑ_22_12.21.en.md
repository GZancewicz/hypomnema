# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 22 · Matt. 12:21

> **…in his name shall *the* nations hope. (Matt. 12:21)**[^1]

For in order that they might not have *anything* to make excuse *of*,[^2] he endured all things, so that later he might condemn them, and might conquer *them* as they contradicted nothing.[^3] For what indeed did he not do in order that he might gain those *men*? But they did not will *it*; whence the nations hope in him, since the Jews did not will *it*.

[^1]: Greek «τῷ ὀνόματι αὐτοῦ ἔθνη ἐλπιοῦσιν» (Isa. 42:4, at Matt. 12:21), completing the sentence begun in Lemma 21. *ἔθνη* is anarthrous; the supplied "*the*" is italicized. *ἔθνη* = "nations / Gentiles"; rendered "nations."
[^2]: Greek *ἵνα… μὴ ἔχωσι προφασίζεσθαι* ("that they might not have *ground* to offer a pretext / make excuse"). The object is unexpressed in the Greek and supplied.
[^3]: Greek *νικήσῃ μηδὲν ἀντιλέγοντας* — literally "conquer *them* contradicting nothing," i.e. so that he might defeat them while they had nothing left to say against *him*; the participle is concessive-circumstantial.
