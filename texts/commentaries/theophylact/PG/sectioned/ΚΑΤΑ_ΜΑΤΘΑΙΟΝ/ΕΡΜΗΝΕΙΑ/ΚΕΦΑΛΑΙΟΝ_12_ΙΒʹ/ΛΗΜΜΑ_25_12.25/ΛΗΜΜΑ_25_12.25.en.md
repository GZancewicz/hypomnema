# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 25 · Matt. 12:25

> **But Jesus, knowing their thoughts, said to them: Every kingdom divided against itself is laid waste; and every city or house divided against itself shall not stand. And if the**[^1]

*This lemma folder carries only the scripture quotation, which breaks off mid-sentence at «Καὶ εἰ ὁ» ("And if the…"). The sentence is completed, and the exposition supplied, in the next lemma.*[^1]

[^1]: The Gospel quotation runs off at «Καὶ εἰ ὁ» and is continued in Lemma 26 with «Σατανᾶς τὸν Σατανᾶν ἐκβάλλει…» ("Satan casts out Satan…"). This folder contains no exposition of Theophylact's own. On «ἐρημοῦται» I render "is laid waste / made desolate"; «οὐ σταθήσεται» "shall not stand."
