# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 25 · Matt. 12:25

> **But Jesus, knowing their thoughts, said to them: Every kingdom divided against itself is laid waste; and every city or house divided against itself shall not stand. And if the**[^1]

[^1]: The quotation runs off at «Καὶ εἰ ὁ» ("And if the…") and is continued in Lemma 26 with «Σατανᾶς τὸν Σατανᾶν ἐκβάλλει…» ("Satan casts out Satan…"). On «ἐρημοῦται» "is laid waste / made desolate"; «οὐ σταθήσεται» "shall not stand."
