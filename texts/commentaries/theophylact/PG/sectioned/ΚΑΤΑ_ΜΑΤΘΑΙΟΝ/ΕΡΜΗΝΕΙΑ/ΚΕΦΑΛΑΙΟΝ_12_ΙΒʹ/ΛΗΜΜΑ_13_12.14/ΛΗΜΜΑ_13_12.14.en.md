# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 13 · Matt. 12:14

> **But the Pharisees, having gone out, took counsel against him, how they might destroy him. But Jesus, having known *it*, withdrew from there. (Matt. 12:14)**[^1]

[^1]: The lemma quotes Matt. 12:14 with only minor divergence from the common text. Greek *Ὁ δὲ Ἰησοῦς γνοὺς, ἀνεχώρησεν ἐκεῖθεν* ("But Jesus, having known *it*, withdrew from there"); the common text reads *γνοὺς ἀνεχώρησεν ἐκεῖθεν* likewise. The participle *γνοὺς* ("having known / having perceived") is kept as a participle; its object is unexpressed in the Greek, so the supplied "*it*" is italicized.
