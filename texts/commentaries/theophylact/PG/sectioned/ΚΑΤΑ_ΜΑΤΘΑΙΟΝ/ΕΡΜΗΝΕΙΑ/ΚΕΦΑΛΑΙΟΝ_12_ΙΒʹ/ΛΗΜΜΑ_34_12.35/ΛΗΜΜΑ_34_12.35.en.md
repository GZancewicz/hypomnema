# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 34 · Matt. 12:35

> **…*the* good man, out of the good treasure brings forth good things; and the evil man, out of the evil treasure brings forth evil things. (Matt. 12:35)**[^1]

When you see[^2] someone foul-mouthed, know that it is not only as much as he says that he has in his heart, but many times more. For indeed out of the abundance it is poured out even to the outside, and though he has a hidden treasure, he reveals *only* some small part *of it*. Likewise also the one who speaks good things has more in his heart.

[^1]: The lemma continues the sentence begun in Lemma 33 (the «ὁ» there = «ὁ ἀγαθὸς ἄνθρωπος» here). Theophylact's text reads simply «ἐκ τοῦ ἀγαθοῦ θησαυροῦ» / «ἐκ τοῦ πονηροῦ θησαυροῦ» ("out of the good treasure" / "out of the evil treasure"), without «τῆς καρδίας» ("of the heart") that some texts add after "treasure"; translated as it stands on his page.
[^2]: OCR «Ἵςαν ἴδης»; securely **«Ὅταν ἴδῃς»** ("When you see"), which the following «γίνωσκε ὅτι …» ("know that …") requires. Rendered accordingly; the corruption is noted for the record.
