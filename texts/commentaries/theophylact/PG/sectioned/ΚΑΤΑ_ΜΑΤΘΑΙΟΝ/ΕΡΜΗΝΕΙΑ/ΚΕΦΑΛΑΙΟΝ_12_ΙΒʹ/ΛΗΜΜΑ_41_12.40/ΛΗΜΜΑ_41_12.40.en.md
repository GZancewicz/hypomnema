# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 41 · Matt. 12:40

> **"For as Jonah was in the belly of the sea-monster three days and three nights, so also will the Son of man be in the heart of the earth three days and three nights." (Matt. 12:40)**[^1]

An evil generation, on the one hand, he calls them, as spiteful and treacherous;[^2] but adulterous, on the other hand, as standing off from God and cleaving to demons. And *as* a sign he names his resurrection, as *being* marvelous;[^3] for in the heart of the earth — I mean the lowest place — into which, that is to say, having descended, on the third day he rose. But the three days and the three nights understand *as* in part and not whole.[^4] For he died on the Preparation *day* — this was one day; and on the Sabbath *he was* dead — behold, another day too; and the night of the Lord's *day*, moreover, found him still dead.[^5] Three day-nights, therefore, are counted in part. Since even thus we are often accustomed to count.

[^1]: Greek *κῆτος* ("sea-monster / great fish"), the word of the Septuagint of Jonah (2:1); rendered "sea-monster" rather than importing "whale." *ἐν τῇ καρδίᾳ τῆς γῆς* — "in the heart of the earth."
[^2]: Greek *ἐπηρεαστὰς καὶ δολερούς* — *ἐπηρεαστής* is "one who insults / abuses / acts spitefully," *δολερός* "deceitful, treacherous." The pair is rendered "spiteful and treacherous."
[^3]: Greek *παράδοξον* — "contrary to expectation, marvelous, paradoxical"; here of the resurrection as the astonishing sign. "*As being* marvelous" supplies the participle.
[^4]: Greek *ἀπὸ μέρους νόησον καὶ μὴ ὁλοκλήρους* — "understand *them* in part [i.e. as reckoned by a part *standing for the whole*] and not as complete *days and nights*." The idiom is that of counting a partial day as a whole; the supplied "*as*" clarifies.
[^5]: Greek *τῇ παρασκευῇ* is the "Preparation" (Friday); *τῇ Κυριακῇ*, "the Lord's *day*" (Sunday). The reckoning: Friday (death) = one day; Sabbath (Saturday) = a second; the night leading into the Lord's day = the third — three *ἡμερονύκτια* ("day-nights") counted by parts.
