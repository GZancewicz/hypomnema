# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 42 · Matt. 12:41

> **"Men of Nineveh will rise up in the judgment with this generation, and will condemn it, because they repented at the preaching of Jonah; and behold, a greater than Jonah *is* here." (Matt. 12:41)**[^1]

Jonah, on the one hand, he says, after his exit from the sea-monster, having preached, was believed; but I, on the other hand,[^2] not even after the resurrection will be believed by you. Whence also you will be condemned by the Ninevites, who believed my servant Jonah without signs — and that though they were barbarians; *whereas* you, who were reared up together with prophets, who saw signs, did not believe me the Master.[^3] For this is what *the saying* "And behold, a greater than Jonah *is* here" signifies.[^4]

[^1]: Greek *μετενόησαν εἰς τὸ κήρυγμα Ἰωνᾶ* — "repented at [lit. *unto*] the preaching of Jonah." *πλεῖον Ἰωνᾶ* is neuter, "a greater *thing* than Jonah," rendered "a greater than Jonah" in the customary way; the supplied "*is*" fills the verbless clause.
[^2]: Greek *ἐγὼ δὴ οὐδὲ μετὰ τὴν ἀνάστασιν ὑμῖν πιστευθήσομαι*. The particle *δή* is intensive ("indeed"); paired against the preceding *μὲν* it functions as the answering "on the other hand," and is so rendered to keep the antithesis (Jonah believed / I not believed) visible.
[^3]: Greek *ὑμεῖς οἱ προφήταις συντεθραμμένοι, οἱ σημεῖα ἰδόντες, μὴ πιστεύσαντες ἐμοὶ τῷ δεσπότῃ* — the clause is a nominative construction without a finite verb, resumptive of "you will be condemned"; the supplied "*whereas*… did not believe" makes the English sentence. *δεσπότης* ("master, sovereign lord"), rendered "Master," set over against Jonah the *δοῦλος* ("servant, slave").
[^4]: The closing words are badly OCR-corrupted: after *Τοῦτο γὰρ σημαίνει τὸ, Καὶ ἰδοὺ πλεῖον* the source reads «Β εἰηΛοαι, ἀοοε· Εἰ οσε ρία οκα Ἀοσ ἰοο. Ἰωνᾶ ὧδε», an unrecoverable garble that is evidently a mangled repetition of the lemma *πλεῖον Ἰωνᾶ ὧδε* ("a greater than Jonah *is* here"). Only the securely legible frame is translated; the corrupt stretch is not rendered.
