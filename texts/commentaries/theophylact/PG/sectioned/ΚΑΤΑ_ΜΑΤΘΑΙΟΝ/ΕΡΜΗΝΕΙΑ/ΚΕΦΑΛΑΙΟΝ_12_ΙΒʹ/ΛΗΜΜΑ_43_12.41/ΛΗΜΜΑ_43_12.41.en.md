# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 43 · Matt. 12:42

> **A queen of *the* South will be raised up in the judgment with this generation, and will condemn it, because she came from the ends of the earth to hear the wisdom of Solomon; and behold, a greater than Solomon *is* here.**[^1]

The queen, on the one hand, he says, came from afar, although being a weak woman, in order that she might hear about trees and timbers and certain natural *things*.[^2] But you, on the other hand, though I came to you and *was* speaking unspeakable *things*,[^3] did not receive *me*.

[^1]: The folder metadata assigns this lemma to 12:41, but the Greek he quotes — «Βασίλισσα Νότου ἐγερθήσεται…» — is Matt. 12:42; the header and reference are corrected accordingly. The final clause is *καὶ ἰδοὺ πλεῖον Σολομῶντος ὧδε*: *πλεῖον* is neuter ("something more / something greater"), not the masculine "a greater *man*"; rendered "a greater than Solomon" to keep the plain force while retaining the neuter's abstraction. *ἐγερθήσεται* ("will be raised up / will rise") echoes *ἀναστήσονται* ("will rise/stand up") in the preceding Ninevites lemma; both are resurrection-flavoured verbs of standing up at the judgment.
[^2]: Greek *περὶ δένδρων καὶ ξύλων καὶ φυσικῶν τινων* — the wisdom of Solomon concerning natural things (cf. 1 Kings 4:33, Solomon "spake of trees"). *ξύλων* is rendered "timbers" (wood as material) beside *δένδρων* ("trees") as living plants; the pair may simply be a doublet for the vegetable world.
[^3]: Greek *ἀπόῤῥητα λέγοντα* — *ἀπόρρητα* is "unspeakable / secret / ineffable *things*," matters too lofty or hidden for ordinary speech, set in contrast to the queen's merely "natural things" above.
