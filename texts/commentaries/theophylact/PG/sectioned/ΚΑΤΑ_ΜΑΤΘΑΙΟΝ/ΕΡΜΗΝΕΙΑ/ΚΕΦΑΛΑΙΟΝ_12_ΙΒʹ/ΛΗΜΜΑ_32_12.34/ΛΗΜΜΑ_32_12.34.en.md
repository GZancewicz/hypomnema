# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 32 · Matt. 12:34

> **Offspring of vipers, how can you speak good things, being evil? (Matt. 12:34)**

Behold, he says, you, being evil trees, bear evil fruit, speaking ill of me; so that I too, if I were evil, would have brought forth evil fruit, and not such miracles. And he calls these men "offspring of vipers," since they were vaunting themselves over Abraham.[^1] Therefore he shows them that they are not from Abraham, but from forebears worthy of their wickedness.

[^1]: Greek *ἐπεὶ ἐπὶ τῷ Ἀβραὰμ ἐκόμπαζον* — "since they were boasting/vaunting over Abraham," i.e. taking pride in Abraham as their forefather (cf. "we have Abraham to *our* father," Matt. 3:9). The verb *κομπάζω* carries the note of empty, swaggering boast.
