# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 49 · Matt. 12:50

> **For whosoever shall do the will of my Father who *is* in *the* heavens, he is my brother and sister and mother. (Matt. 12:50)**

Not insulting[^1] his mother does he say these things, but correcting her glory-loving and human way of thinking.[^2] For he did not say that "She is not my mother," but that "If she does not do the will of God, the having begotten me will profit her nothing." For he does not deny the kinship according to nature, but adds also the *kinship* according to virtue. For no unworthy person is profited from kinship. Having corrected, then, the disease of vainglory, nevertheless he again obeys his mother when she calls. For the evangelist says:[^3]

[^1]: Greek OCR *Οὐχ ὑορίζων*, securely = *Οὐχ ὑβρίζων* ("not insulting / not treating with contempt / not dishonoring"). The reconstruction is certain; noted only for consistency with the other flagged OCR corruptions (*ἀστίν*→*ἐστίν*, *ὕτι*→*ὅτι*, *ἐὐαγγελιστής*→*εὐαγγελιστής*).
[^2]: Greek *τὴν φιλόδοξον αὐτῆς καὶ ἀνθρωπίνην γνώμην*. *φιλόδοξος* is literally "loving glory/reputation," i.e. inclined to vainglory (the *κενοδοξία*, "vainglory," named just below); *γνώμη* here is "mind / disposition / way of thinking / opinion." Rendered "her glory-loving and human way of thinking."
[^3]: The lemma breaks off with *Φησὶ γὰρ ὁ εὐαγγελιστής·* ("For the evangelist says:"), introducing a quotation that is supplied in the next lemma.
