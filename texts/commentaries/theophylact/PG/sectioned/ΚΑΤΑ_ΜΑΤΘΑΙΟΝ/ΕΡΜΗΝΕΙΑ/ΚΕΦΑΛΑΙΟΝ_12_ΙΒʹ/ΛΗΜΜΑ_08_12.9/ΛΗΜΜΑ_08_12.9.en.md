# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 8 · Matt. 12:9

> **And having departed from there, he came into their synagogue, and behold[^1]**

*This lemma carries only the scripture head, which breaks off at "and behold"; Theophylact's exposition begins in the next lemma (09), where the sentence is completed («a man was there, having his hand withered…»).*[^2]

[^1]: Greek *Καὶ μεταβὰς ἐκεῖθεν, ἦλθεν εἰς τὴν συναγωγὴν αὐτῶν, καὶ ἰδοὺ* (Matt. 12:9). *μεταβάς* is an aorist participle, "having departed / having gone over from *that place*," kept as a participle.
[^2]: The Gospel sentence and Theophylact's comment continue directly in lemma 09; there is no exposition attached to this fragment in the source.
