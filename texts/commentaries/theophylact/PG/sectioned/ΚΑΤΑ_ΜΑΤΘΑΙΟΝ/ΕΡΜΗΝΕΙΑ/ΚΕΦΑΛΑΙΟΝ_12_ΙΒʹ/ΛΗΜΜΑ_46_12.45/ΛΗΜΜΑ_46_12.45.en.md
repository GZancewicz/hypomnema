# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 46 · Matt. 12:46

> **But while he was still speaking to the crowds, behold, *his* mother and his brothers had stood outside, seeking to speak to him.**[^1]

The mother wished to display something human, that she has authority over the child.[^2] For she did not yet conceive anything great concerning him; therefore, then, even *while* he *was* speaking, she wishes to draw *him* to herself, taking pride as *though* the son *were* being made subject to her. What, then, *does* Christ *do*? Since he knew her aim, hear what he says.

[^1]: The folder metadata labels this lemma 12:45, but the quoted Greek — «Ἔτι δὲ αὐτοῦ λαλοῦντος τοῖς ὄχλοις…» — is Matt. 12:46; corrected in the header and reference. *εἱστήκεισαν* is pluperfect ("had stood / were standing"); rendered "had stood" to keep the tense visible.
[^2]: Greek *ὅτι ἐξουσιάζει τοῦ παιδός* — "that she exercises authority over / has power over the child (or: the boy)." *ὁ παῖς* is here Jesus as her son; the whole point is her (mistaken) assertion of maternal authority.
