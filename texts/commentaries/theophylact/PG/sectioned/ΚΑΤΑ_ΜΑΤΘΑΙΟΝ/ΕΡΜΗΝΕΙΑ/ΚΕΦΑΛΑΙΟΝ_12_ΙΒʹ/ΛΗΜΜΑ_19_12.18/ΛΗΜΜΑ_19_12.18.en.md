# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 19 · Matt. 12:19

> **Nor shall anyone hear his voice in the streets. (Matt. 12:19)**[^1]

For *he taught* not in the midst of the marketplace like the vainglorious,[^2] but in the temple and the synagogues, and on the mountain[^3] and beside the shores he taught.

[^1]: The lemma continues the quotation of Isa. 42:1–4 that Matthew cites (Matt. 12:18–21); the OCR reads «ὐἂὲ ἀκούσει τις…», securely recoverable as **«οὐδὲ ἀκούσει τις ἐν ταῖς πλατείαις τὴν φωνὴν αὐτοῦ»** ("nor shall anyone hear his voice in the streets"). Greek *πλατεῖαι* are the broad, open streets or public squares; rendered "streets."
[^2]: Greek *φιλόδοξοι* ("lovers of glory / vainglorious"); the OCR has *φιλάδοξοι*, an evident error. The same word governs the whole antithesis: unlike glory-seekers who court crowds in the open market, Christ taught in more retired settings.
[^3]: The OCR reads «ἐν τῶ Κριι», corrupt; from the run of teaching-places (temple, synagogues, [mountain], seashores) it is securely **«ἐν τῷ ὄρει»** ("on the mountain"), matching the Gospel record of Christ teaching on the mountain (Matt. 5:1) and by the sea (Matt. 13:1). Flagged as an OCR reconstruction.
