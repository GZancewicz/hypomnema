# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 37 · Matt. 12:37

> **…*out* of your words you will be justified, and out of your words you will be condemned. (Matt. 12:37)**[^1]

He frightens[?] us here,[^2] that even for the idle word we shall render an account — that is, for the false *word*, the slanderous *one*, or even the disorderly and ridiculous *one*. Then he places a testimony from the Scripture, so that he might not seem to say certain things of his own:

[^1]: This lemma opens mid-sentence, continuing the Gospel quote begun in the previous lemma (Matt. 12:36–37), which broke off at *ἐκ γὰρ τῶν* ("for out of the…"). The words here are *ἐκ τῶν λόγων σου δικαιωθήσῃ, καὶ ἐκ τῶν λόγων σου καταδικασθήσῃ* ("out of your words you will be justified, and out of your words you will be condemned"). The supplied "*out*" renders the fragmentary opening.
[^2]: The OCR reads «Ἐκ, οκεῖ ἐνταῦθα ἡμᾶς», which is corrupt. The syntax (a verb governing *ἡμᾶς* + a *ὅτι*-clause) best recovers as **«Ἐκφοβεῖ ἐνταῦθα ἡμᾶς, ὅτι…»** ("He frightens us here, that…"); a stray "Ἐκ" appears dittographed from the preceding lemma. Reading uncertain, hence [?]. The verb of the *ὅτι*-clause, OCR *Ἐήσόμεν λόγον*, is securely *δώσομεν λόγον* ("we shall render/give an account"), matching Matt. 12:36 *ἀποδώσουσι… λόγον*.
