# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 2 · Matt. 12:1

> **In that time Jesus went on the Sabbath[^1] through the sown fields. And his disciples hungered, and began to pluck ears of grain and to eat. (Matt. 12:1)**

Loosing the legal observances, he leads the disciples through the sown fields;[^2]

[^1]: Greek *τοῖς Σάββασι* — dative plural, the standard idiom for "on the Sabbath" (the plural form regularly carries a singular sense). Rendered "on the Sabbath"; the plural is preserved in the note. Contrast the singular *ἐν τῷ Σαββάτῳ* elsewhere in the same passage.
[^2]: The sentence breaks off here; it is completed at the head of the next lemma («ἵνα φαγόντες, λύσωσιν τοῦ Σαββάτου» — "so that, having eaten, they might loose the Sabbath").
