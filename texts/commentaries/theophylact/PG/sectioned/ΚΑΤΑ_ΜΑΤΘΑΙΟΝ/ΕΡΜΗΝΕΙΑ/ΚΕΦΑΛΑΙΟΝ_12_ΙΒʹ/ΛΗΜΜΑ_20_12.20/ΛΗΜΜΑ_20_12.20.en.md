# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 20 · Matt. 12:20

> **A bruised reed he shall not break, and smoking flax he shall not quench. (Matt. 12:20)**[^1]

He was able, he says, to break the Jews to pieces like a bruised reed, and to quench their wrath like smoking — that is, burning[^2] — flax; but he did not will *it*, until he should fulfill the economy,[^3] and conquer them through all things. For this is what the *word* that follows signifies.

[^1]: Greek «Κάλαμον συντετριμμένον οὐ κατεάξει, καὶ λίνον τυφόμενον οὐ σβέσει» (Isa. 42:3, as cited at Matt. 12:20). *λίνον τυφόμενον* is "smoking / smouldering flax" (a wick); Theophylact glosses *τυφόμενον* himself in the next line.
[^2]: Theophylact supplies a synonym for the Gospel's *τυφόμενον* ("smoking / smouldering"): *ἤτοι καιόμενον* ("that is, burning"). Rendered to keep his gloss visible.
[^3]: Greek *οἰκονομία* — the divine "economy," i.e. the dispensation or ordered plan of salvation; retained as "economy" per standard patristic usage rather than flattened to "plan."
