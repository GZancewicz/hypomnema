# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 44 · Matt. 12:43-44

> **But whenever the unclean spirit goes out from the man, it passes through waterless places, seeking rest, and does not find *it*; then it says: I will return[^1] into my house from where I went out. And having come, it finds *it* unoccupied, swept and adorned. Then**[^2]

[^1]: The OCR breaks the word as «Ἐπιστρέ. εἰς τὸν οἶκόν μου»; it is securely *Ἐπιστρέψω* ("I will return / turn back"), the future the whole clause requires.
[^2]: This lemma folder carries only the scripture quotation, with no exposition of its own; Theophylact's comment on the whole passage begins after the sentence is completed in Lemma 45. The lemma breaks off mid-sentence at *Τότε* ("Then"), which Lemma 45 resumes with *πορεύεται* ("it goes…"). Of the three participles describing the house, *σχολάζοντα* means "at leisure / empty / unoccupied" (standing idle, tenantless), beside *σεσαρωμένον καὶ κεκοσμημένον* ("swept and adorned/set in order").
