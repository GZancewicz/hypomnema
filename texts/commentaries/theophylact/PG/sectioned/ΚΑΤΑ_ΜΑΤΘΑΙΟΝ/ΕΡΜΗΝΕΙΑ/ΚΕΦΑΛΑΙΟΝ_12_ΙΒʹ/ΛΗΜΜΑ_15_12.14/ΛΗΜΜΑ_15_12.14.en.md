# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 15 · Matt. 12:15

> **And there followed him many crowds, and he healed them all; and[^1]**

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation, which breaks off mid-sentence; the verb it awaits ("he charged them…") and Theophylact's exposition follow in the next lemma (16).*[^2]

[^1]: The folder-name verse (12.14) is corrected to **Matt. 12:15**, which the Greek actually quotes: *Καὶ ἠκολούθησαν αὐτῷ ὄχλοι πολλοί, καὶ ἐθεράπευσεν αὐτοὺς πάντας· καὶ*. The common text of Matt. 12:15 reads *ἠκολούθησαν αὐτῷ ὄχλοι πολλοί, καὶ ἐθεράπευσεν αὐτοὺς πάντας*; the trailing *καὶ* leads into v. 16.
[^2]: The lemma ends open at *καὶ* ("and"); the sentence is completed in Lemma 16 with *ἐπετίμησεν αὐτοῖς…* ("he charged them…").
