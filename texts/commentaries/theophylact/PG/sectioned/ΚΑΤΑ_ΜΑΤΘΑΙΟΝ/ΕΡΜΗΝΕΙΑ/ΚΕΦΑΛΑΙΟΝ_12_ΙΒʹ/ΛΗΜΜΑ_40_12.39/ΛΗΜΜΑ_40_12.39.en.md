# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 40 · Matt. 12:39

> **"But he, answering, said to them: An evil and adulterous generation seeks after a sign, and a sign will not be given to it, except the sign of Jonah the prophet." (Matt. 12:39)**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the Gospel quotation; the exposition follows in the next lemma, after the completion of the saying (Matt. 12:40).*[^2]

[^1]: Greek *σημεῖον ἐπιζητεῖ* — *ἐπιζητεῖ* is intensive, "seeks after / demands," rendered "seeks after" to mark the prefix. The Greek OCR *σομεῖον* for *σημεῖον* ("sign") is a plain misprint.
[^2]: The quotation breaks off at *τὸ σημεῖον Ἰωνᾶ τοῦ προφήτου* ("the sign of Jonah the prophet"); the sentence continues at the head of the next lemma with *Ὥσπερ γὰρ ἦν Ἰωνᾶς…* ("For as Jonah was…"), where Theophylact's comment begins.
