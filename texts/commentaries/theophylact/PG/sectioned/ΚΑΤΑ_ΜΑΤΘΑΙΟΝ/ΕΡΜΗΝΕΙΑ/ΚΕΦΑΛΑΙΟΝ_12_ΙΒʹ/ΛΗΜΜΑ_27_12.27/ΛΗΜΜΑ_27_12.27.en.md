# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 27 · Matt. 12:27

> **And if I by Beelzebul cast out the demons, your sons — by whom do they cast *them* out? Therefore they themselves shall be your judges.**[^1]

Let it be, he says, that I am such a one; but your sons — that is, my disciples[^2] — by whom do they cast *them* out? For surely they too *do* not *cast out* by Beelzebul? But if they *cast out* by divine power, much more *do* I. For they work miracles in my name; therefore, then, they shall be for your condemnation, because, seeing even them working miracles in my name, you still slander me.[^3]

[^1]: Greek «οἱ υἱοὶ ὑμῶν ἐν τίνι ἐκβάλλουσι;» — "your sons, by whom (lit. *in* whom) do they cast out?" The instrumental «ἐν» matches «ἐν τῷ Βεελζεβούλ» in the previous clause and is rendered "by." «κριταί» = "judges." The supplied "*them*" (object of "cast out") is not in the Greek.
[^2]: Theophylact glosses «οἱ υἱοὶ ὑμῶν» ("your sons") as «οἱ ἐμοὶ μαθηταί» ("my disciples") — an unusual identification, since the phrase is often taken of Jewish exorcists; the interpretation stands in his text and is followed here.
[^3]: The lemma ends at «ἔτι διαβάλλετέ με» ("you still slander me"); «διαβάλλω» is rendered "slander / accuse falsely" consistently through this passage.
