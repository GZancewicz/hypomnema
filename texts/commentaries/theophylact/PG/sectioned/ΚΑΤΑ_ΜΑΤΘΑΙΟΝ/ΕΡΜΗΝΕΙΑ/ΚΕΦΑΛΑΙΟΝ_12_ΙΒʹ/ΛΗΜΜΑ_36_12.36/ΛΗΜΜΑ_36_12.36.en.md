# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 36 · Matt. 12:36

> **…idle, which men may speak, they shall render account concerning it in *the* day of judgment. (Matt. 12:36)**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the completion of the scripture quotation; it has no exposition of its own.*[^2] After the citation of Matt. 12:36 the text passes directly to the opening words of the next verse, «ἐκ γὰρ τῶν …» ("For out of the *[your words]* …"), which the following lemma completes and expounds.

[^1]: The lemma continues the sentence begun in Lemma 35 (the «πᾶν ῥῆμα» there = «πᾶν ῥῆμα ἀργὸν» ("every idle word") here). Full sense across the two lemmata: "But I say to you, that every idle word which men may speak, they shall render account concerning it in *the* day of judgment."
[^2]: The trailing «ἐκ γὰρ τῶν» is not Theophylact's comment but the incipit of the Matt. 12:37 lemma — «Ἐκ γὰρ τῶν λόγων σου δικαιωθήσῃ, καὶ ἐκ τῶν λόγων σου καταδικασθήσῃ» ("For out of your words you shall be justified, and out of your words you shall be condemned") — carried over into Lemma 37, where the exposition begins.
