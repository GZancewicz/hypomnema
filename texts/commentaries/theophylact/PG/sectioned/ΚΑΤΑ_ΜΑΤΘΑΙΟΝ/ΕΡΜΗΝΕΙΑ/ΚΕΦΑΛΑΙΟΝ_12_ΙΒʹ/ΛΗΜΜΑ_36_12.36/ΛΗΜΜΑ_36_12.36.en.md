# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 36 · Matt. 12:36

> **…idle, which men may speak, they shall render account concerning it in *the* day of judgment. (Matt. 12:36)**[^1]

[^1]: The trailing «ἐκ γὰρ τῶν» is not Theophylact's comment but the incipit of the Matt. 12:37 lemma — «Ἐκ γὰρ τῶν λόγων σου δικαιωθήσῃ, καὶ ἐκ τῶν λόγων σου καταδικασθήσῃ» ("For out of your words you shall be justified, and out of your words you shall be condemned") — carried over into Lemma 37, where the exposition begins.
