# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 48 · Matt. 12:49

> **he said: Behold my mother and my brothers.**[^1]

[^1]: This lemma folder carries only the scripture fragment that completes the sentence broken off in Lemma 47 (*καὶ ἐκτείνας τὴν χεῖρα αὐτοῦ ἐπὶ τοὺς μαθητὰς αὐτοῦ, εἶπεν· Ἰδοὺ ἡ μήτηρ μου καὶ οἱ ἀδελφοί μου* — "and having stretched out his hand over his disciples, he said: Behold my mother and my brothers"). It carries no exposition; Theophylact's comment on the saying resumes at Lemma 49 with the following verse (Matt. 12:50). The folder metadata labels it 12:47, but the words are the close of Matt. 12:49.
