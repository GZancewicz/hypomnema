# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 17 · Matt. 12:17

> **That there might be fulfilled the thing spoken through Isaiah the prophet, saying: Behold my servant whom[^1]**

[^1]: Greek *Ὅπως πληρωθῇ τὸ ῥηθὲν διὰ Ἠσαΐου τοῦ προφήτου λέγοντος· Ἰδοὺ ὁ παῖς μου ὃν* — Matt. 12:17 leading into the citation of Isa. 42:1 (v. 18). *παῖς* is ambiguous between "servant" and "child/son"; the Isaian Servant-song context and the LXX of Isa. 42:1 (*ὁ παῖς μου*) favor "servant," but the sense "child" (God's own) is also present. Rendered "servant" as primary. The clause ends open at the relative *ὃν* ("whom"), completed in Lemma 18 with *ᾑρέτισα* ("I chose"). The OCR *προσήτου* is securely *προφήτου* ("prophet"), and *Ἠσαίου* is *Ἠσαΐου* ("Isaiah").
