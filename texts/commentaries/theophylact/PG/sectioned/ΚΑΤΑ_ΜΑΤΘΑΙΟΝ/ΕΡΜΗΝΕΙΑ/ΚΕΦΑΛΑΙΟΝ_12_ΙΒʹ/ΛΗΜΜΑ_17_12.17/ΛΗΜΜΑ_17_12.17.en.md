# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 17 · Matt. 12:17

> **That there might be fulfilled the thing spoken through Isaiah the prophet, saying: Behold my servant whom[^1]**

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation, which breaks off mid-clause; the words that complete it ("…I chose; my beloved…") and Theophylact's exposition follow in the next lemma (18).*[^2]

[^1]: Greek *Ὅπως πληρωθῇ τὸ ῥηθὲν διὰ Ἠσαΐου τοῦ προφήτου λέγοντος· Ἰδοὺ ὁ παῖς μου ὃν* — Matt. 12:17 leading into the citation of Isa. 42:1 (v. 18). *παῖς* is ambiguous between "servant" and "child/son"; the Isaian Servant-song context and the LXX of Isa. 42:1 (*ὁ παῖς μου*) favor "servant," but the sense "child" (God's own) is also present. Rendered "servant" as primary. The OCR *προσήτου* is securely *προφήτου* ("prophet"), and *Ἠσαίου* is *Ἠσαΐου* ("Isaiah").
[^2]: The lemma ends open at the relative *ὃν* ("whom"); it is completed in Lemma 18 with *ᾑρέτισα* ("I chose").
