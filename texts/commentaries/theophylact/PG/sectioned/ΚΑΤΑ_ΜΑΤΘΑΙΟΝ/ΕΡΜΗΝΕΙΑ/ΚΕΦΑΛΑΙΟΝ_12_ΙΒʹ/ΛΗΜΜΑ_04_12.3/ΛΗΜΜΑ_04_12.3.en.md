# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 4 · Matt. 12:3

> **said to them: Have you not read what David did, when he himself hungered, and those with him? how —[^1]**

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text, with no exposition of Theophylact's own.*[^2]

[^1]: The lemma completes the *Ὁ δὲ* ("But he") left hanging at the end of the previous lemma: "But he said to them …" (Matt. 12:3). It then breaks off at *πῶς* ("how"), which is picked up at the head of the next lemma («εἰσῆλθεν εἰς τὸν οἶκον τοῦ Θεοῦ …» — "how he entered into the house of God …").
[^2]: The commentary on this stretch is deferred: Theophylact expounds vv. 3-4 together in the following lemma.
