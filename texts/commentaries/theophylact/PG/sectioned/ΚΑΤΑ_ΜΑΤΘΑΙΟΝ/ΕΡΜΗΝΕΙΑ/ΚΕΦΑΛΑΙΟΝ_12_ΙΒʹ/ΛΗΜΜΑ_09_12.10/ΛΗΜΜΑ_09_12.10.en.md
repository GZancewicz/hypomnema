# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 9 · Matt. 12:10

> **…a man was there, having his hand withered, and they questioned him, saying: "Is it lawful to heal on the Sabbaths?" — so that they might accuse him. (Matt. 12:10)**[^1]

The other evangelists say that Jesus questioned the Pharisees.[^2] It is possible, therefore, to say that, being envious, they anticipated *him* and questioned him, as Matthew says. Then Christ in turn questioned them, as *if* mocking and laughing to scorn their hardness *of heart*[^3] — which the other evangelists state. And they questioned him so that they might get an occasion for slandering *him*.

[^1]: This lemma continues the sentence broken off in lemma 08; the words *ἄνθρωπος ἦν ἐκεῖ, τὴν χεῖρα ἔχων ξηράν…* ("a man was there, having his hand withered…") complete «and behold». *ἵνα κατηγορήσωσιν αὐτοῦ* = "so that they might accuse him."
[^2]: The source reads *Ζαρισαίους*, an OCR error for *Φαρισαίους* ("Pharisees").
[^3]: Greek *τὴν πώρωσιν αὐτῶν διαγελῶν* — *πώρωσις* is "hardening / callousness (of heart)"; *διαγελῶν* = "laughing to scorn, deriding." The clause *ἵνα σχῶσιν ἀφορμήν* just below appears in the source as *ἵνα σκῶσιν*, an OCR error for *σχῶσιν* ("that they might get / have").
