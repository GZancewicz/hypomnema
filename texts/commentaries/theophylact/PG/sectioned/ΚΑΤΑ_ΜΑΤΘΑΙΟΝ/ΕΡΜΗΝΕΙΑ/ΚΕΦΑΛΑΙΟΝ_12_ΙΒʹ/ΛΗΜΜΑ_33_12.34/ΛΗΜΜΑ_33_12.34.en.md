# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 33 · Matt. 12:34

> **For out of the abundance of the heart the mouth speaks; the …[^1] (Matt. 12:34)**

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation; it has no exposition of its own.* The sentence runs directly into the following lemma, where the words «ὁ ἀγαθὸς ἄνθρωπος …» ("the good man …") continue the citation and Theophylact's comment begins.[^1]

[^1]: The Greek breaks off at «ὁ» ("the"), which is picked up in the next lemma by «ἀγαθὸς ἄνθρωπος» ("good man"). The quotation «Ἐκ γὰρ τοῦ περισσεύματος τῆς καρδίας τὸ στόμα λαλεῖ» is the second half of Matt. 12:34; the exposition proper is given at Lemma 34.
