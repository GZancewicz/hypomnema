# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 35 · Matt. 12:36

> **But I say to you, that every idle word which men may speak, they shall render account concerning it in *the* day of judgment. (Matt. 12:36)**[^1]

[^1]: The folder metadata assigns this lemma to Matt. 12:35, but the Greek «Λέγω δὲ ὑμῖν, ὅτι πᾶν ῥῆμα …» is the opening of Matt. **12:36**; the header and reference are corrected accordingly. The verse is split across Lemmata 35–36 in the source; given here as one continuous reading.
