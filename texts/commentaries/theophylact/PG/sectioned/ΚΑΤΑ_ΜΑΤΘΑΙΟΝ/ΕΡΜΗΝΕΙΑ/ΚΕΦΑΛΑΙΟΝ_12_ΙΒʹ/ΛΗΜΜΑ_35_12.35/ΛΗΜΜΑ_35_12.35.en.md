# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 35 · Matt. 12:36

> **But I say to you, that every word …[^1] (Matt. 12:36)**

*This lemma carries only the opening of the scripture quotation; it has no exposition of its own.* The sentence runs directly into the following lemma, where the words «ἀργὸν ὃ ἐὰν λαλήσωσιν οἱ ἄνθρωποι …» ("idle, which men may speak …") complete the citation.[^1]

[^1]: The folder metadata assigns this lemma to Matt. 12:35, but the Greek «Λέγω δὲ ὑμῖν, ὅτι πᾶν ῥῆμα …» is the opening of Matt. **12:36**; the header and reference are corrected accordingly. The quotation breaks off at «πᾶν ῥῆμα» ("every word"), continued by «ἀργὸν» ("idle") in the next lemma.
