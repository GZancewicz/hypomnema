# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 24 · Matt. 12:24

> **But the Pharisees, having heard *it*, said: This man does not cast out the demons, except by Beelzebul[^1] *the* ruler of the demons. (Matt. 12:24)**

And yet the Lord had withdrawn on account of them;[^2] but those men, even hearing *of him* from afar, slander *him*, and — though people were being benefited — they were thus enemies of *human* nature,[^3] as the devil is.

[^1]: Greek *Βεελζεβούλ*, retained in its conventional English form; *ἄρχων τῶν δαιμονίων* = "*the* ruler of the demons," anarthrous *ἄρχοντι* here (the supplied "*the*" is italicized).
[^2]: Greek *ἀνεχώρησεν ὁ Κύριος δι᾽ αὐτούς* ("the Lord withdrew on account of them" / "because of them"); referring back to Christ's withdrawal earlier in the chapter (Matt. 12:15). *καίτοι γε* = "and yet, although," concessive.
[^3]: Greek *ἐχθροὶ τῆς φύσεως* ("enemies of nature"); i.e. enemies of *human* nature / of humankind — hostile to the very good being done to people — like the devil, the primordial hater of mankind. The supplied "*human*" makes the sense explicit.
