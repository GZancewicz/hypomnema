# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 3 · Matt. 12:2

so that, having eaten, they might loose the Sabbath.[^1]

> **And the Pharisees, having seen, said to him: Behold, your disciples are doing what it is not permitted to do on the Sabbath. (Matt. 12:2)**

But he —[^2]

[^1]: These opening words complete the sentence begun at the end of the previous lemma ("Loosing the legal observances, he leads the disciples through the sown fields …"). Although the source wraps them in quotation marks («ἵνα φαγόντες, λύσωσιν τοῦ Σαββάτου»), they are Theophylact's own exposition, not a Gospel citation — hence set as prose, not as a bold lemma. On the construction: *λύσωσιν τοῦ Σαββάτου* uses the genitive *τοῦ Σαββάτου* ("loose / dissolve [the observance] of the Sabbath"); the more usual idiom takes the accusative (*λύειν τὸ Σάββατον*, "to break the Sabbath," as at Matt. 12:5-6 below).
[^2]: Greek *Ὁ δὲ* ("But he") breaks off here; the sentence is completed at the head of the next lemma with *εἶπεν αὐτοῖς* — "But he said to them …" (Matt. 12:3).
