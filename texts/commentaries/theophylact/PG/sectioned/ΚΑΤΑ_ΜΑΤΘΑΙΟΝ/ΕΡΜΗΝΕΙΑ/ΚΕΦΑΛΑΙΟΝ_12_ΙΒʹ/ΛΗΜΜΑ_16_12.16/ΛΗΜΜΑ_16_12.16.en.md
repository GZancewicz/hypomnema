# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 16 · Matt. 12:16

> **…he charged them that they should not make him manifest. (Matt. 12:16)**[^1]

Soothing[^2] the envy of those men, he does not wish to be made manifest. For he was earnestly striving in every way to heal them.[^3]

[^1]: The lemma completes the sentence begun in Lemma 15. Greek *ἐπετίμησεν αὐτοῖς ἵνα μὴ φανερὸν αὐτὸν ποιήσωσιν* — "he charged/rebuked them that they should not make him manifest." *ἐπετίμησεν* is strictly "rebuked, sternly charged"; rendered "charged" for the sense of a strict injunction laid on the crowds. The OCR *ἐαμὴ* is securely *ἵνα μὴ* ("that … not").
[^2]: Greek *παραμυθούμενος* — "consoling / soothing / assuaging"; here appeasing the Pharisees' envy by not letting his fame spread.
[^3]: Greek *Ἐσπούδαζε γὰρ παντὶ τρόπῳ θεραπεύειν αὐτούς* — "for he was eager / made it his earnest business in every manner to heal them." The antecedent of *αὐτούς* is genuinely ambiguous: grammatically the nearest plural is *ἐκείνων* ("those men," the envious Pharisees just mentioned), so the sense may be that he sought in every way to heal *them* of their envy; alternatively *αὐτούς* looks back to the crowds of v. 15 whom he keeps healing while shunning fame. Both readings are defensible; we leave "them" open in the body. The OCR *Ἐσποῦδαζε* is securely *Ἐσπούδαζε*.
