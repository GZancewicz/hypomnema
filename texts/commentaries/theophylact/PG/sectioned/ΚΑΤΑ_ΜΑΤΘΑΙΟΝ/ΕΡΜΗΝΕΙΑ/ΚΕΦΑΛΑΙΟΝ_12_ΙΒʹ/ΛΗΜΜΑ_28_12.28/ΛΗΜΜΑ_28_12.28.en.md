# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 28 · Matt. 12:28

> **But if I by the Spirit of God cast out the demons, then the kingdom of God has come upon you.**[^1]

This he says: that if by divine power I cast out the demons, then I am the Son of God, and I have come for your sake, that you might be benefited. So that my presence has come upon you.[^2] For this is the kingdom of God. Why then do you slander my coming, which has taken place for your sake? Or how[^3]

[^1]: Greek «ἔφθασεν ἐφ᾽ ὑμᾶς ἡ βασιλεία τοῦ Θεοῦ» — «φθάνω» here = "has arrived / has come upon, reached," rather than the classical "anticipate, come beforehand."
[^2]: Greek «ἡ ἐμὴ παρουσία» — "my presence / coming / advent"; Theophylact equates it below with «ἡ βασιλεία τοῦ Θεοῦ» ("the kingdom of God"). The same word «παρουσία» is rendered "coming" in the next sentence for readability, the referent being identical.
[^3]: The exposition breaks off at «Ἢ πῶς» ("Or how"), which are the opening words of the next scripture lemma (Matt. 12:29, «Ἢ πῶς δύναταί τις εἰσελθεῖν…»), continued and expounded in Lemma 29.
