# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 29 · Matt. 12:29–30

> **Or how can anyone enter into the house of the strong *man*, and plunder his goods, unless first he binds the strong *man*, and then he shall plunder his house?**[^1]

So far, he says, am I from having the demons as friends,[^2] that I even set myself against them and bind them — them who were strong before my coming; for having entered into the house — that is, the world — Christ plundered the goods of the demons, I mean human beings.[^3]

> **[He that is not with me is against me; and he that gathereth not] with me scattereth.**[^4]

How, he says, was Beelzebul going to cooperate with me, who rather works against me? For I teach virtue, but he wickedness. How then is he with me? And I gather human beings unto salvation, but he scatters. And he hints also at the Pharisees: that, while he was teaching and benefiting many, they were scattering the people, so that they would not come to him. Therefore he shows them to be, in very deed, demoniacal.[^5]

[^1]: Greek «τὰ σκεύη αὐτοῦ διαρπάσαι» — «σκεύη» = "vessels, goods, gear, chattels"; rendered "goods" (Theophylact glosses them below as "human beings"). «ἰσχυρός» = "the strong *man*" (the noun "man" is supplied). OCR «ὅιαρπάσαι» is securely «διαρπάσαι» ("plunder, spoil thoroughly").
[^2]: OCR «φλους» is securely «φίλους» ("friends"). The construction «τοσοῦτον ἀπέχω τοῦ… ὥστε…» = "so far am I from… that…"
[^3]: Greek «τοὺς ἀνθρώπους λέγω» — "I mean human beings (men)": the "goods/vessels" that Christ carried off from the demons are people.
[^4]: This second lemma quotes Matt. 12:30. The OCR is badly corrupt here, preserving only «…ἐμὴ συνάγων μετ᾽ ἐμοῦ, σκορπίζει.» The verse is securely «Ὁ μὴ ὢν μετ᾽ ἐμοῦ, κατ᾽ ἐμοῦ ἐστι· καὶ ὁ μὴ συνάγων μετ᾽ ἐμοῦ, σκορπίζει» ("He that is not with me is against me; and he that gathereth not with me scattereth"); the recoverable tail is translated and the lost opening supplied in brackets. This is the reason the folder (12.29) contains commentary through v. 30.
[^5]: OCR corruptions resolved: «Α μεελζεβοὺλ»→«ὁ Βεελζεβοὺλ»; «ἀντιπομττει»→«ἀντιπράττει» ("works against"); «διᾶάσκω»→«διδάσκω»; «ἐσεδάννυον»→«ἐσκέδαννυον» ("were scattering/dispersing"); «κρόσέρχεσθαι»→«προσέρχεσθαι»; «Αείνυσιν»→«Δείκνυσιν». The closing «δαιμονιώδεις τῷ ὄντι» = "truly / in very deed demoniacal (demon-like)."
