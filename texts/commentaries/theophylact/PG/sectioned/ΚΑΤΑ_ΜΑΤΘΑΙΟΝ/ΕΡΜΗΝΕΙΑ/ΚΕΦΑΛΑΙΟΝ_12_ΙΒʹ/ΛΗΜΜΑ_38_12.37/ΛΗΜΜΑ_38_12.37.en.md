# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 38 · Matt. 12:37

> **"Out of your words you will be justified, and out of your words you will be condemned." (Matt. 12:37)**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scriptural testimony and no exposition of its own; it is the very "testimony from the Scripture" which, at the close of the preceding lemma, Theophylact said he "places" so as not to seem to speak on his own authority.*[^2]

[^1]: Greek *Ἐκ τῶν λόγων σου δικαιωθήσῃ, καὶ ἐκ τῶν λόγων σου καταδικασθήσῃ* — the words of Matt. 12:37, here set out in full as the confirming citation. Theophylact treats the saying as though quoting a fixed "testimony," which is why it is repeated on its own after being quoted within the previous lemma.
[^2]: The Greek file contains no commentary beyond the quotation itself.
