# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 47 · Matt. 12:47-49

> **But someone said to him: Behold, your mother and your brothers stand outside, seeking to speak to you. But he, answering, said to the one who spoke to him: Who is my mother, and who are my brothers? And having stretched out his hand over his disciples,**[^1]

[^1]: The folder metadata labels this lemma 12:45, but the quoted Greek — «Εἶπε δέ τις αὐτῷ…» — spans Matt. 12:47-49; corrected in the header and reference. This lemma folder carries only the scripture quotation, with no exposition of its own. The sentence breaks off mid-clause at the participle *ἐκτείνας … ἐπὶ τοὺς μαθητὰς αὐτοῦ* ("having stretched out his hand over his disciples"); it is completed in Lemma 48, which supplies the words *εἶπεν· Ἰδοὺ ἡ μήτηρ μου…* ("he said: Behold my mother…"). *ἑστήκασι* is perfect ("have stood / stand"), *εἱστήκεισαν* in Lemma 46 being pluperfect.
