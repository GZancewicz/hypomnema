# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 12 · Matt. 12:13

> **Then he says to the man: "Stretch out your hand." And he stretched *it* out, and it was restored whole as the other. (Matt. 12:13)**[^1]

Many even now have withered hands, that is, *hands* unmerciful and ungiving; but when the evangelical word resounds in them, they stretch these out unto giving[^2] — even if the Pharisees, the boastful demons who have been cut off from us,[^3] on account of *their* enmity toward us, do not want our hands to be stretched out unto almsgiving.

[^1]: Greek *…καὶ ἀποκατεστάθη ὑγιὴς ὡς ἡ ἄλλη* (Matt. 12:13); the source reads *ὡς ὁ ἄλλη*, an OCR slip for *ὡς ἡ ἄλλη* ("as the other," i.e. the other hand). *ἀποκατεστάθη* = "was restored / re-established."
[^2]: Greek *εἰς μετάδοσιν*, "unto imparting / sharing / giving *a share*"; the cognate adjective *ἀμεταδότους* above ("ungiving, unwilling to share") belongs to the same root.
[^3]: Greek *οἱ ἀλαζόνες δαίμονες, οἱ ἀποκεκομμένοι ἡμῶν* — "the boastful/arrogant demons, who have been cut off from us." *ἀλαζών* = "boastful, arrogant, impostor"; *ἀποκεκομμένοι* is the perfect passive participle of *ἀποκόπτω*, "cut off / severed." Theophylact allegorizes the Pharisees as demons severed from the community of the faithful.
