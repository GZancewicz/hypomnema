# Matthew 12 (ΙΒʹ) — Lemma 26 · Matt. 12:26

> **…Satan casts out Satan, he is divided against himself; how then shall his kingdom stand?**[^1]

By making public their thoughts, he shows that he is God. And he defends himself to them from common examples, and shows their folly. For how do the demons cast out themselves, seeing that rather they hold themselves together?[^2] And "Satan" means the adversary.[^3]

[^1]: This blockquote completes the sentence begun in Lemma 25 («Καὶ εἰ ὁ Σατανᾶς τὸν Σατανᾶν ἐκβάλλει…»), which corresponds to Matt. 12:26 (the folder metadata reads 12.25, spanning the sentence that opens at v. 25). The article before «Σατανᾶν» is supplied by Greek case; the name is treated as fixed.
[^2]: Greek «ὅπου γε μᾶλλον ἑαυτοὺς συγκροτοῦσι» — «συγκροτέω» here means to band/hold together, close ranks, act in concert; the point is that demons reinforce one another rather than expel one another.
[^3]: Greek «Σατὰν δὲ λέγεται ὁ ἀντικείμενος» — Theophylact glosses the name etymologically: *Satan* = "the one who opposes / stands against," "the adversary" (the participle «ὁ ἀντικείμενος»).
