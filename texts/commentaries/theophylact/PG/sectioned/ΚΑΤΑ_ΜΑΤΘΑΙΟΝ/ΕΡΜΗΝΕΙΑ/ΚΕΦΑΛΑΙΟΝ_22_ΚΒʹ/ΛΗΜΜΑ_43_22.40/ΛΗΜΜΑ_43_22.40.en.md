# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 43 · Matt. 22:38

> **This *is the* first and great commandment,[^1] (Matt. 22:38)**

*the one* training us toward piety;[^2]

[^1]: Greek *Αὕτη πρώτη καὶ μεγάλη ἐντολή* — the folder's draws-from verse is given as 22:40, but the words quoted are Matt. 22:38 ("This is the first and great commandment"). The predicate is anarthrous and verbless: *αὕτη* = "this," with the copula and the article on "commandment" supplied and italicized.
[^2]: Greek *εἰς εὐσέβειαν ἡμᾶς γυμνάζουσα* — Theophylact's own gloss on the commandment, not part of the Gospel text. The present participle *γυμνάζουσα* ("training, exercising") is feminine, agreeing with *ἐντολή* ("commandment"); the supplied "*the one*" carries that agreement into English while keeping the participle. *εὐσέβεια* = "piety, godliness." The sentence runs on into the next lemma, where the second commandment is glossed in parallel ("rousing us toward the just dealings toward men").
