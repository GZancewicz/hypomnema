# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 10 · Matt. 22:9

> **go therefore to the outlets of the ways, and as many as you may find, call to the wedding. And**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text; the exposition follows under the next lemma (see Lemma 11 on v. 10).*[^2]

[^1]: Greek: «πορεύεσθε οὖν ἐπὶ τὰς διεξόδους τῶν ὁδῶν, καὶ ὅσους ἐὰν εὕρητε, καλέσατε εἰς τοὺς γάμους.» The word *διέξοδοι* means "ways-out, outlets, thoroughfares" (the points where roads branch off); Theophylact's exposition turns on this sense, reading them as deviations *off from* the ὁδός ("way") — see Lemma 11. The lemma ends with «Καὶ» ("And"), carried into the next lemma.
[^2]: The verse is quoted and broken off; the sentence continues into Lemma 11, «ἐξελθόντες οἱ δοῦλοι ἐκεῖνοι…» ("those servants, having gone out…," v. 10).
