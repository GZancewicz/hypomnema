# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 17 · Matt. 22:16

> **Teacher, we know that you are true, and you teach the way of God in truth, and it is no care to you concerning anyone, for you do not look at *the* face of men. Tell us therefore, what to you (Matt. 22:16)**[^1][^2]

[^1]: This lemma carries only the Pharisees' address, which breaks off at «τί σοι» ("what to you…") and is completed in the next lemma («δοκεῖ; Ἔξεστιν δοῦναι κῆνσον…», "seems? Is it lawful to give tribute…"). Theophylact gives no exposition here.
[^2]: Two points of wording: (a) The OCR «οὐ μέλλει σοι» is for *οὐ μέλει σοι* — "there is no care to you concerning anyone," i.e. "you defer to no one." (b) Greek *οὐ βλέπεις εἰς πρόσωπον ἀνθρώπων* — literally "you do not look into *the* face of men," a Semitic idiom for showing no partiality or respect of persons; rendered literally to keep the flattery's form.
