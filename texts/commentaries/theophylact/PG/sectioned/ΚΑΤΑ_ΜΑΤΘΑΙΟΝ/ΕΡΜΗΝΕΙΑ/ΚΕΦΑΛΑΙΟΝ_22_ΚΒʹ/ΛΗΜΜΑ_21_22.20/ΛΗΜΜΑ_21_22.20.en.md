# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 21 · Matt. 22:20

> **he says to them: Whose *is* this image and the inscription?**[^1] (Matt. 22:20)

*This lemma continues the sentence begun in Lemma 20 (the opening "And") and carries only the Gospel words; Theophylact's exposition of the whole tribute-scene (22:15–22) is deferred and given together at Lemma 23.*

[^1]: *Τίνος ἡ εἰκὼν αὕτη καὶ ἡ ἐπιγραφή;* — a verbless question; the supplied "*is*" is italicized. *εἰκών* is "image / likeness / portrait," *ἐπιγραφή* the "inscription / superscription" (the legend around the coin).
