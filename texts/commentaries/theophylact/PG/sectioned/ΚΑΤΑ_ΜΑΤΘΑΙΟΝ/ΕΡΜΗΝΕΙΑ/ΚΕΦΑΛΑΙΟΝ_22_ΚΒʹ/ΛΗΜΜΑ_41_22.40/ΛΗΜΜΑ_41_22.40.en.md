# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 41 · Matt. 22:37

> **"and with all your soul" —**

this, the vegetative;[^1] for plants *are* ensouled.[^2]

[^1]: Greek *τοῦτο, τὸ φυτικόν* ("this, the vegetative"); no verb expressed. Theophylact assigns "soul" (*ψυχή*) to *τὸ φυτικόν*, the plant-like or growth-and-nutrition part of the human soul (cf. Lemma 39, "in respect of that whereby it grows and is nourished… it is likened to plants").
[^2]: Greek *ἔμψυχα γὰρ τὰ φυτά* — literally "for plants *are* en-souled / ensouled things." The remark turns on a word-play untranslatable in English: *ψυχή* ("soul") stands inside *ἔμψυχος* ("having soul in it, animate"), so that plants — being *ἔμψυχα* — are precisely the fitting figure for the "soul" (*ψυχή*) clause. The connective *γὰρ* ("for") is present and rendered roman; "*are*" is supplied.
