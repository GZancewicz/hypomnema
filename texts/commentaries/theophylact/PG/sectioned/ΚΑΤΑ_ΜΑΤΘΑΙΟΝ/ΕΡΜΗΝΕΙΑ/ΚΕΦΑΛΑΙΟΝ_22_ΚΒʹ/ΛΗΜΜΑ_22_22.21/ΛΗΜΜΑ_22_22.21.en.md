# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 22 · Matt. 22:21

> **They say to him: Caesar's. Then he says to them: Render therefore the things of Caesar to Caesar, and the things of God to God. And**[^1] (Matt. 22:21)

*This lemma carries only the Gospel words; Theophylact's exposition of the whole tribute-scene (22:15–22) is deferred and given together at Lemma 23.*

[^1]: The lemma breaks off at *Καὶ* ("And"); the sentence is completed at the head of Lemma 23 ("…hearing, they marveled"). In the Greek *τὰ Καίσαρος* ("the things of Caesar") is anarthrous while *τὰ τοῦ Θεοῦ* ("the things of God") carries the article; the slight asymmetry is preserved. *ἀπόδοτε* is "give back / render / pay what is due" (the compound *ἀπο-* stressing a debt returned), which grounds Theophylact's exposition below.
