# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 38 · Matt. 22:39

> **and *the* second *is* like it: "You shall love your neighbor as yourself." In[^1]**

This lemma too carries only the Gospel text, and breaks off at "In"; the sentence is completed in the next lemma — «On these two commandments the whole law and the prophets hang» (Lemma 39) — where Theophylact's exposition begins.[^2]

[^1]: The lemma ends mid-clause at *Ἐν* ("In / On"), the opening of «Ἐν ταύταις ταῖς δυσὶν ἐντολαῖς…» ("On these two commandments…"), which is picked up at the head of Lemma 39.
[^2]: Greek *δευτέρα δὲ, ὁμοία αὐτῇ* — anarthrous *δευτέρα* ("a second / *the* second"), with the connective *δὲ* rendered "and." No verb is expressed; the supplied "*is*" is italicized, as is the supplied article "*the*."
