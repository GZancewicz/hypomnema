# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 12 · Matt. 22:11–12

> **But the king, having entered to behold those reclining, saw there a man not clothed in a wedding garment, and says to him: Friend, how did you enter here, not having a wedding garment? But he was muzzled.[^1] Then**[^2]

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text; the exposition follows under the next lemma (see Lemma 13 on v. 13).*

[^1]: Greek *ἐφιμώθη*, literally "he was muzzled/gagged" — i.e. reduced to speechlessness, having no defense to make. Rendered literally to preserve the image; the sense is "he was struck dumb."
[^2]: Greek: «Εἰσελθὼν δὲ ὁ βασιλεὺς θεάσασθαι τοὺς ἀνακειμένους, εἶδεν ἐκεῖ ἄνθρωπον οὐκ ἐνδεδυμένον ἔνδυμα γάμου, καὶ λέγει αὐτῷ· Ἑταῖρε, πῶς εἰσῆλθες ὧδε, μὴ ἔχων ἔνδυμα γάμου; Ὁ δὲ ἐφιμώθη. Τότε…». The lemma ends with «Τότε» ("Then"), carried into the next lemma (13), «εἶπεν ὁ βασιλεὺς τοῖς διακόνοις…» (v. 13).
