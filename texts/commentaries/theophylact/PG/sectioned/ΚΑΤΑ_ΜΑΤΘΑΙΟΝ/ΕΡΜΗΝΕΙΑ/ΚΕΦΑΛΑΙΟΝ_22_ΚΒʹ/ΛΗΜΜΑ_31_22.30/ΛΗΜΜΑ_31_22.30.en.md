# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 31 · Matt. 22:30

> **…resurrection, they neither marry, nor are given in marriage,[^1] but are as angels of God in heaven. But concerning the…**[^2]

This lemma carries no exposition of its own: it is a continuation of the Gospel quotation broken off in the previous lemma («For in the…») and running on into the next. Theophylact quotes the whole passage (Matt. 22:29–33) in fragments and then expounds it all together in Lemma 34 (Matt. 22:33), where the comment is given.

[^1]: Theophylact distinguishes the two verbs by voice and gender: *γαμοῦσιν* ("they marry," active, properly of the men) and *ἐκγαμίζονται* ("are given in marriage," passive, properly of the women). The pair together denies marriage to both sexes in the resurrection.
[^2]: The lemma ends mid-sentence at *Περὶ δὲ τῆς* ("But concerning the…"), completed in the next lemma with «resurrection of the dead». The clause *ὡς ἄγγελοι Θεοῦ ἐν οὐρανῷ εἰσι* is here anarthrous (*ἐν οὐρανῷ*, "in heaven," without the article the common text carries at *ἐν τῷ οὐρανῷ*); rendered as it stands.
