# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 28 · Matt. 22:27

> **And last of all the woman also died; in the, therefore,…**[^1]

*[Gospel lemma only, quoted continuously; no exposition here. Theophylact comments on the whole Sadducee pericope at Lemma 29.]*

[^1]: Greek «Ὕστερον δὲ πάντων ἀπέθανε καὶ ἡ γυνή· ἐν τῇ οὖν…» (OCR «Ὑστερυν» → **Ὕστερον**). The lemma breaks off at «ἐν τῇ οὖν» ("in the, therefore"), with the postpositive οὖν sitting inside the phrase; it is completed in the next lemma by «ἀναστάσει» — i.e. "therefore, in the resurrection." Left broken here to mirror the sectioning.
