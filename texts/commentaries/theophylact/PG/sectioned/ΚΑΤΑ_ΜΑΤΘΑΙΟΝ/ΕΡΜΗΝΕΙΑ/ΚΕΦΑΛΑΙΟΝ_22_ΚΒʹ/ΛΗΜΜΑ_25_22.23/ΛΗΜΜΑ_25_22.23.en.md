# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 25 · Matt. 22:23-24

> **…saying *that there* is no resurrection, and they questioned him, saying: Teacher, Moses said: If someone should die not having children, his brother shall marry[^1] his wife, and shall raise up seed *for* his brother. But[^2] there were among us seven…**

*[This section carries only the Gospel lemma, quoted continuously; it opens mid-sentence, continuing from the preceding lemma ("In that day there came to him Sadducees, who…"), and breaks off mid-sentence at "seven," completed in the next lemma with «ἀδελφοί» ("brothers"). Theophylact's exposition of the whole Sadducee pericope is gathered at Lemma 29.]*

[^1]: Greek «ἐπιγαμβρεύσει» — the technical verb for levirate marriage (*ἐπιγαμβρεύω*, "to marry as brother-in-law / take the widow of a deceased kinsman"), reflecting the law of Deut. 25:5. Rendered plainly "shall marry," since English has no single verb for the institution; the kinship sense is what the whole question turns on.
[^2]: Greek «Ἦσαν δὲ παρ᾽ ἡμῖν ἑπτά». The OCR reads «Ἄσαν … ἐπτὰ»; securely = **Ἦσαν** ("there were") and **ἑπτά** ("seven"). δέ rendered "but"; the existential "*there*" is supplied.
