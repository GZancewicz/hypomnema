# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 40 · Matt. 22:37

> **"You shall love your God with all your heart"[^1] —**

this *is* the animal part of man;[^2]

[^1]: Theophylact here re-quotes the first clause of the great commandment (Matt. 22:37) to expound it. As quoted, the Greek reads «Ἀγαπήσεις τὸν Θεόν σου ἐν ὅλῃ τῇ καρδίᾳ σου» — "You shall love **your God**," omitting *Κύριον* ("*the* Lord") which stood in the fuller citation at Lemma 37. Translated as it stands on the page, per the rule of following his text.
[^2]: Greek *τοῦτο τὸ ζωῶδες μέρος τοῦ ἀνθρώπου* ("this the animal part of man"); no verb is expressed, so "*is*" is supplied. Theophylact assigns "heart" (*καρδία*) to *τὸ ζωῶδες*, the animal or living-creature part — the seat of anger and desire (cf. Lemma 39, "in respect of that whereby it is angered and desires, *it is likened* to the animals").
