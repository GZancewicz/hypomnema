# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 20 · Matt. 22:19

> **Show me the coin of the tribute.[^1] But they brought to him a denarius. And**[^2] (Matt. 22:19)

*This lemma carries only the Gospel words; Theophylact's exposition of the whole tribute-scene (22:15–22) is deferred and given together at Lemma 23.*

[^1]: *τὸ νόμισμα τοῦ κήνσου* — "the coin (current money) of the tribute." *κῆνσος* is a Latin loanword (*census*), the poll-tax; kept as "tribute" throughout the pericope for consistency. *νόμισμα* is any legal-tender coin.
[^2]: The lemma breaks off at *Καὶ* ("And"); the sentence is completed in Lemma 21 ("And he says to them, Whose is this image…").
