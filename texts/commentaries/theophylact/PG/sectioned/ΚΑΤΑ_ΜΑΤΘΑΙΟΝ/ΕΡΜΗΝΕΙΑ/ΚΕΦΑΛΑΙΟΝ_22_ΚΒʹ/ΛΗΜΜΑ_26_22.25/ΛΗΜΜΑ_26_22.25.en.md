# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 26 · Matt. 22:25

> **…brothers, and the first, having married, died; and not having seed, left his wife to his brother;**

*[Gospel lemma only, quoted continuously. It opens mid-sentence, continuing "there were among us seven" from the preceding lemma. No exposition here; Theophylact comments on the whole Sadducee pericope at Lemma 29.]*[^1]

[^1]: OCR corruptions in the source are securely recoverable: «ὀ πρῶτος» → **ὁ πρῶτος** ("the first"); «γνναῖκα» → **γυναῖκα** ("wife"). Noted for consistency; low-stakes.
