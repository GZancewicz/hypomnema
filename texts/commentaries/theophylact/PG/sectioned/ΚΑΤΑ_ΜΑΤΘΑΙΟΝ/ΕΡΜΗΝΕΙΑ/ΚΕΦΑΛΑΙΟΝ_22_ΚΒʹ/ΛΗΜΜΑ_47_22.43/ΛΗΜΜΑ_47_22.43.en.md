# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 47 · Matt. 22:43

> **He says to them: How then does David in *the* Spirit call him Lord, saying:[^1] (Matt. 22:43)**

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text; Theophylact adds no exposition here. His comment on the passage is given at lemma 49.*

[^1]: Greek *Λέγει αὐτοῖς· Πῶς οὖν Δαυὶδ ἐν πνεύματι Κύριον αὐτὸν καλεῖ, λέγων·* — Matt. 22:43. *οὖν* = "then/therefore"; *ἐν πνεύματι* = "in *the* Spirit" (the article is supplied; the phrase is anarthrous, and Theophylact glosses it below at lemma 49 as "through the grace of the Spirit, having *him* revealed to him"). The clause breaks off at "saying," completed by the citation in the next lemma.
