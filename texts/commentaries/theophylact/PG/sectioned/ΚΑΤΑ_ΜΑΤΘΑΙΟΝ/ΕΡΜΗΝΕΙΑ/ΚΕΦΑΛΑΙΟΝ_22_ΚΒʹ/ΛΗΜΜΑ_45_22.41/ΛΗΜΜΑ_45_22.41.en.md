# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 45 · Matt. 22:41

> **But *the* Pharisees having been gathered together, Jesus questioned them,[^1] (Matt. 22:41)**

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text; Theophylact adds no exposition here. The sentence continues into the following lemmata (46–48), and his comment on the whole passage is given at lemma 49.*[^2]

[^1]: Greek *Συνηγμένων δὲ τῶν Φαρισαίων, ἐπηρώτησεν αὐτοὺς ὁ Ἰησοῦς* — Matt. 22:41. *δέ* = "but/and"; *συνηγμένων* is a perfect passive participle in a genitive absolute ("*the* Pharisees having been gathered together"); *ἐπηρώτησεν* = "questioned, put a question to."
[^2]: The clause breaks off at the participle "saying," which the next lemma supplies with Jesus' actual question.
