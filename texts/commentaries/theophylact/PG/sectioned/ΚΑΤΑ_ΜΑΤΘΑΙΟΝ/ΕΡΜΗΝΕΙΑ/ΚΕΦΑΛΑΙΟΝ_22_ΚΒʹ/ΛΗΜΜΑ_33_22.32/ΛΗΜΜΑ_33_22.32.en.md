# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 33 · Matt. 22:32

> **…of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob; God is not a God of *the* dead, but of *the* living.[^1] And…**[^2]

This lemma carries no exposition of its own: it is a continuation of the Gospel quotation, running on from the previous lemma («I am the God…») into the next («having heard, the crowds…»). Theophylact quotes the whole passage (Matt. 22:29–33) in fragments and then expounds it all together in Lemma 34 (Matt. 22:33).

[^1]: Greek *οὐκ ἔστιν ὁ Θεὸς Θεὸς νεκρῶν, ἀλλὰ ζώντων* — "God is not a God of dead *ones*, but of living *ones*." Both *νεκρῶν* and *ζώντων* are anarthrous; the supplied "*the*" is italicized. (The OCR *ρὐκ* is securely *οὐκ*, "not.")
[^2]: The lemma ends mid-sentence at *Καὶ* ("And…"), completed in the next lemma with «having heard, the crowds were astonished».
