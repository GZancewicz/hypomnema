# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 48 · Matt. 22:44

> **The Lord said to my Lord: Sit thou at my right hand, until I put thine enemies *as* a footstool of thy feet?[^1] If therefore[^2] (Matt. 22:44)**

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text; Theophylact adds no exposition here. His comment on the passage is given at lemma 49.*

[^1]: Greek *Εἶπεν ὁ Κύριος τῷ Κυρίῳ μου· Κάθου ἐκ δεξιῶν μου, ἕως ἂν θῶ τοὺς ἐχθρούς σου ὑποπόδιον τῶν ποδῶν σου* — Matt. 22:44, citing Ps. 110:1 (LXX 109:1). *ἐκ δεξιῶν μου* = "at my right hand" (lit. "from my right *parts*"); *θῶ* = aorist subjunctive "I put/place"; *ὑποπόδιον τῶν ποδῶν σου* = "a footstool of thy feet," "*as*" supplied. The Greek question mark here belongs to the enclosing question begun in lemma 47 ("How then does David call him Lord, saying: …?"); the citation itself is a statement.
[^2]: Greek *Εἰ οὖν* — the clause breaks off; it is completed in the next lemma: "If therefore David calls him Lord, how is he his son?" (Matt. 22:45).
