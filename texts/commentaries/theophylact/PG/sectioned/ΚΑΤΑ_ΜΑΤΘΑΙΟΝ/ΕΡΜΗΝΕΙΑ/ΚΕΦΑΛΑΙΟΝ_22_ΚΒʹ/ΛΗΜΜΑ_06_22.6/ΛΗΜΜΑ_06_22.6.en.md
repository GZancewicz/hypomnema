# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 6 · Matt. 22:6

> **rest, having seized his servants, insulted[^1] and killed *them*. And (Matt. 22:6)**

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text: it completes the clause begun at the end of lemma 5 ("but they") with its subject "rest" (οἱ δὲ λοιποί, "but the rest"). Theophylact quotes the parable continuously across lemmata 1–7 and gives his exposition in lemma 7.*

[^1]: Greek *ὕβρισαν* — "insulted, treated with insolence/outrage" (*ὑβρίζω*). Theophylact applies it in lemma 7 to the prophets who were "outraged," instancing Jeremiah cast into the pit of mire. The lemma breaks off at *Καὶ* ("And"), continuing into lemma 7, which carries the remainder of the parable (v. 7) and the whole exposition.
