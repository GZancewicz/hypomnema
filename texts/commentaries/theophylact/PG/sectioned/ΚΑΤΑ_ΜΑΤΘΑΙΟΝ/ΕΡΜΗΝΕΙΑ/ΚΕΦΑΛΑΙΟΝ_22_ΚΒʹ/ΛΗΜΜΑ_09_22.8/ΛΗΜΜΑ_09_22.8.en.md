# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 9 · Matt. 22:8

> **Then he says to his servants: The wedding, on the one hand, is ready, but those who had been called were not worthy;**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text; Theophylact supplies no exposition here. His comment is deferred, the parable's interpretation being gathered under the following lemmata (see Lemma 11 on vv. 9–10).*[^2]

[^1]: Greek: «Τότε λέγει τοῖς δούλοις αὐτοῦ· Ὁ μὲν γάμος ἕτοιμός ἐστιν, οἱ δὲ κεκλημένοι οὐκ ἦσαν ἄξιοι·». The μὲν … δὲ ("on the one hand … but") is rendered explicitly, as both particles are present in the text.
[^2]: The verse is quoted and then broken off with "·"; the sentence continues into the next lemma (10), «πορεύεσθε οὖν…» ("go therefore…," v. 9).
