# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 3 · Matt. 22:3

> **he sent his servants to call those who had been called[^1] to the wedding-feast, and they were not willing to come. (Matt. 22:3)**

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text. Theophylact quotes the parable continuously across lemmata 1–7 and gives his exposition in lemma 7.*

[^1]: Greek *τοὺς κεκλημένους* — perfect passive participle, "those who had (already) been called/invited." The point Theophylact later presses (lemma 8) is that they were *already* summoned yet had to be called again; "those who had been called" preserves the perfect force. *καλέσαι* ("to call") and *κεκλημένους* ("those called") share the root, which is lost in English.
