# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 32 · Matt. 22:31

> **…resurrection of the dead, have you not read that which was spoken[^1] to you by God, *who* says:[^2] I am the God…**

This lemma carries no exposition of its own: it is a continuation of the Gospel quotation, running on from the previous lemma («But concerning the…») into the next («of Abraham…»). Theophylact quotes the whole passage (Matt. 22:29–33) in fragments and then expounds it all together in Lemma 34 (Matt. 22:33).

[^1]: The OCR reads *τὸ ὀηθὲν*, securely for *τὸ ῥηθὲν* ("that which was spoken / uttered"); the reconstruction is certain, noted only for consistency with the other flagged OCR corruptions.
[^2]: Greek *ὑπὸ τοῦ Θεοῦ λέγοντος* — literally "by God saying," a genitive participle attached to "God." Rendered "*who* says" for readable English; the supplied relative is italicized.
