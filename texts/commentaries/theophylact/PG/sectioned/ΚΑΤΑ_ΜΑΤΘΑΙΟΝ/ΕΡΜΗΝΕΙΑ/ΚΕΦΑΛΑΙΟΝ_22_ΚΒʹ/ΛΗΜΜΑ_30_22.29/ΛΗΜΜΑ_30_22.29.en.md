# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 30 · Matt. 22:29

> **But Jesus answering said to them: You are led astray,[^1] not knowing the Scriptures, nor the power of God. For in the…**

*[Gospel lemma only; no exposition here. It breaks off at "For in the," completed in the next lemma with «ἀναστάσει» ("resurrection") — "For in the resurrection they neither marry nor are given in marriage…"]*

[^1]: Greek «Πλανᾶσθε» (present passive of *πλανάω*, "to lead astray, cause to wander"). Rendered as a passive, "you are led astray," to keep the sense that error has been worked upon them; it may also be taken middle-reflexively, "you err / you go astray."
