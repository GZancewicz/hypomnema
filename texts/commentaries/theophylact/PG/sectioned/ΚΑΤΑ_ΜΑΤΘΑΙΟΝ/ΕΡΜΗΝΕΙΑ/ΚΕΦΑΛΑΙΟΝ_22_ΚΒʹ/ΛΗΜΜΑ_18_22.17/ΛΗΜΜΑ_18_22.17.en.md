# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 18 · Matt. 22:17

> **seems? Is it lawful to give tribute to Caesar, or not? (Matt. 22:17)**[^1][^2]

[^1]: The quotation continues from the previous lemma, which broke off at «τί σοι» ("what to you"); joined, the two read «τί σοι δοκεῖ;» — "what seems to you? / what do you think?" Theophylact gives no exposition here; his comment resumes with the next lemma, where the Lord answers («Γνοὺς δὲ ὁ Ἰησοῦς τὴν πονηρίαν αὐτῶν…»).
[^2]: Greek *κῆνσον*, a Latin loanword (*census*), the head- or poll-tax paid to the Roman emperor; kept as "tribute" for sense, but the term is technical for the imperial capitation levied on subject provinces.
