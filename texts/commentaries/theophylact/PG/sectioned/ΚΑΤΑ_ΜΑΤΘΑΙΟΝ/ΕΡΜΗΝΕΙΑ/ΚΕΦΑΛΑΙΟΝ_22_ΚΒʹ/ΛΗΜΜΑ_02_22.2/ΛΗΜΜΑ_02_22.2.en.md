# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 2 · Matt. 22:2

> **The kingdom of the heavens was likened to a man, a king, who made a wedding-feast[^1] for his son[^2], and (Matt. 22:2)**

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text. Theophylact quotes the parable continuously across lemmata 1–7 and gives his exposition in lemma 7.*

[^1]: Greek *γάμους* — the plural noun *γάμοι* ("marriage, wedding") is regularly used of the wedding-celebration or wedding-feast; rendered "wedding-feast" throughout. The plural is idiomatic and does not imply multiple weddings.
[^2]: The OCR reads *τῷ υἱῷ αλ-οῦ*; *αλ-οῦ* is securely *αὐτοῦ* ("his"). The lemma breaks off at *καὶ* ("and"), continuing into lemma 3.
