# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 5 · Matt. 22:5

> **having made light *of it*, went away, the one on the one hand to his own field, the other on the other hand to his trade[^1]; but they[^2] (Matt. 22:5)**

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text. Theophylact quotes the parable continuously across lemmata 1–7 and gives his exposition in lemma 7, where he reads the "field" as the flesh-loving, luxurious life and the "trade" as the profit-loving life.*

[^1]: Greek *ἐμπορίαν* — "trade, commerce, business" (the OCR *εἰ,* for *εἰς*, "to," is corrected). Theophylact glosses it in lemma 7 as *τὸν φιλοκερδῆ βίον*, "the profit-loving life," since merchants (*ἔμποροι*) are "a most gain-loving race." The correlative *ὁ μὲν … ὁ δὲ* is here rendered "the one … the other," with the particles carried by "on the one hand … on the other hand."
[^2]: The lemma breaks off at *οἱ δὲ* ("but they / but the rest"); the subject is completed by *λοιποί* at the head of lemma 6 ("but the rest").
