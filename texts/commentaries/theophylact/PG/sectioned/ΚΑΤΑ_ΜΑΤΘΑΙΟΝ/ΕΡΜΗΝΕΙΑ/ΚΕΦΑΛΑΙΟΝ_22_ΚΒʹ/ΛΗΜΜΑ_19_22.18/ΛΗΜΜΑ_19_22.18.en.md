# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 19 · Matt. 22:18

> **But Jesus, having known their wickedness, said: Why do you tempt me, hypocrites?**[^1] (Matt. 22:18)

*This lemma carries only the Gospel words; Theophylact's exposition of the whole tribute-scene (22:15–22) is deferred and given together at Lemma 23.*

[^1]: The OCR reads «Αί μὲ πειράζετε», securely = **«Τί με πειράζετε»** ("Why do you tempt me"): *Αί* for *Τί*, *μὲ* for *με*. The verb *πειράζω* ranges over "test / try / tempt"; rendered "tempt" for the plain sense of a hostile trap. *πονηρία* is "wickedness / malice / knavery"; kept as "wickedness."
