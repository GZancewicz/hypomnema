# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 37 · Matt. 22:36–38

> **"Teacher, which commandment *is* great in the law?" But Jesus said to him: "You shall love *the* Lord your God with all your heart, and with all your soul,[^1] and with all your mind; this is *the* first and great commandment.**

This lemma carries only the Gospel text; Theophylact supplies no exposition here. His comment on the question and on the great commandment follows below, under Lemma 39, where the citation is completed with «On these two commandments the whole law and the prophets hang».[^2]

[^1]: The OCR reads «ἐν ὅλῃ πῇ ψυχῇ σου», where *πῇ* is a certain error for the article *τῇ* — «ἐν ὅλῃ τῇ ψυχῇ σου» ("with all your soul"). Low-stakes correction, noted for consistency.
[^2]: The Gospel citation runs continuously across Lemmata 37–39 (question, the two commandments, and the summary «all the law and the prophets hang»); Theophylact does not begin expounding until the citation is complete, at Lemma 39. In the exposition there he re-parcels the clauses of the first commandment («with all your heart… soul… mind») one by one, under Lemmata 40–42.
