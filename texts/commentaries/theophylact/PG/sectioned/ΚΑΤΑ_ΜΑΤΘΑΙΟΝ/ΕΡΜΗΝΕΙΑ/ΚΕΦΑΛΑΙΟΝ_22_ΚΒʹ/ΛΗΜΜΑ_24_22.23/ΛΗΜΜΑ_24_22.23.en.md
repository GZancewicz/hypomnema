# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 24 · Matt. 22:23

> **In that day there came to him Sadducees, the *ones***[^1] (Matt. 22:23)

*This lemma carries only the opening Gospel words; the sentence and Theophylact's exposition continue at Lemma 25 ("…saying there is no resurrection, and they questioned him…").*

[^1]: The lemma breaks off at the article *οἱ* ("the / who"), which introduces the participial phrase completed in Lemma 25: *οἱ λέγοντες μὴ εἶναι ἀνάστασιν* ("those who say there is no resurrection"). *προσῆλθον αὐτῷ* — "came to him / approached him."
