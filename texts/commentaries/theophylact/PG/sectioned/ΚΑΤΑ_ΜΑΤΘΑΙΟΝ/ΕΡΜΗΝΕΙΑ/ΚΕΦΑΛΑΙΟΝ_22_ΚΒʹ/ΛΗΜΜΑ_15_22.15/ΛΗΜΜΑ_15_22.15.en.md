# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 15 · Matt. 22:15

> **Then the Pharisees, having gone, took counsel how they might ensnare him in speech; and (Matt. 22:15)**[^1]

[^1]: This lemma carries only the opening of the Gospel quotation, which breaks off at «καὶ» ("and") and is continued in the next lemma («ἀποστέλλουσιν αὐτῷ τοὺς μαθητὰς αὐτῶν…»). Theophylact gives no exposition here; his comment on the passage begins with the following lemma. Greek *παγιδεύσωσιν ἐν λόγῳ* — "might trap/ensnare him in a word (in speech)," from *παγίς* ("snare, trap").
