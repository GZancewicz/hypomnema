# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 42 · Matt. 22:37

> **"and with all your mind" —**

this *is* the rational.[^1] So that the *duty* of loving God with the whole soul[^2] is this: to attend to him through all the parts and powers of the soul.

[^1]: Greek *τοῦτο τὸ λογικόν* ("this the rational"); no verb expressed, "*is*" supplied. Theophylact assigns "mind" (*διάνοια*) to *τὸ λογικόν*, the rational part (cf. Lemma 39, "in respect of that whereby it thinks, it is called rational"). With this the tripartite scheme of Lemmata 40–42 is complete: heart → animal, soul → vegetative, mind → rational.
[^2]: Greek *ὁλοψύχως* ("with the whole soul / whole-souledly"), an adverb here given nominal force. The clause *τὸ δεῖν ἀγαπᾷν τὸν Θεὸν ὁλοψύχως* is an articular infinitive, literally "the *that-one-must* love God whole-souledly"; rendered "the *duty* of loving God with the whole soul," with "*duty*" supplied to carry the force of *δεῖν* ("must / it is necessary").
