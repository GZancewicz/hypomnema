# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 46 · Matt. 22:42

> **saying: What think ye concerning the Christ? whose son is he? They say to him: David's.[^1] (Matt. 22:42)**

*This lemma carries only the Gospel text; Theophylact adds no exposition here. His comment on the passage is given at lemma 49.*

[^1]: Greek *λέγων· Τί ὑμῖν δοκεῖ περὶ τοῦ Χριστοῦ; τίνος υἱός ἐστι; Λέγουσιν αὐτῷ· Τοῦ Δαυΐδ* — Matt. 22:42. *Τί ὑμῖν δοκεῖ* is literally "What seems to you?" = "What think ye?"; the answer *Τοῦ Δαυΐδ* is a bare genitive, "David's" (i.e. "*the son* of David").
