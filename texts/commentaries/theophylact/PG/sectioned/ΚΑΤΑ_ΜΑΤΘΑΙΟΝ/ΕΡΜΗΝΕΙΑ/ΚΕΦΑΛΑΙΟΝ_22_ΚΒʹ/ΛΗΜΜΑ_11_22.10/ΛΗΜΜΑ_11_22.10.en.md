# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 11 · Matt. 22:10

> **And those servants, having gone out into the ways, gathered all whom they found, both evil and good, and the wedding was filled with *those* reclining. (Matt. 22:10)**[^1]

Since the former servants, those about Moses and the prophets, did not persuade *them*, then he sends other servants, the apostles, who called those from the nations — *people* not walking in the true way, but divided some one way, some another, and split apart into many ways and doctrines. Or rather, *people* who were at the outlets of the ways,[^2] that is, in much wandering and unevenness. For indeed they too were in faction among themselves, and were not in the ways, but at the outlets; and those are the evil doctrines which they laid down as dogma. Not all were content in the same way, but some in this manner, others in that manner.

But might it perhaps be better to understand *it* also thus? The way is the life and civic conduct of each; but the outlets off from the way *are* the doctrines. The Greeks, at any rate, having evil ways — that is, blameworthy lives — went out from *their* evil lives to the godless doctrines, having set up shameful gods as advocates for *their* passions. The apostles, therefore, having gone out from Jerusalem to the nations, gathered all, both evil and good — that is, both those filled with all vice, and those in a more moderate condition, whom he also names "good," as in comparison with those.[^3]

[^1]: Greek: «καὶ ἐξελθόντες οἱ δοῦλοι ἐκεῖνοι εἰς τὰς ὁδοὺς, συνήγαγον πάντας ὅσους εὗρον, πονηρούς τε καὶ ἀγαθοὺς, καὶ ἐπλήσθη ὁ γάμος ἀνακειμένων.» The lemma continues the sentence begun in Lemma 10 («…καὶ»); the supplied "*those*" before "reclining" (*ἀνακειμένων*, "those reclining at table") is not separately expressed.
[^2]: On *διέξοδοι* ("outlets, ways-out, branchings-off") see the note at Lemma 10. Theophylact's whole exposition puns on the word: the "outlets of the ways" (v. 9) are read as departures *off from* the ὁδός ("way, road"), i.e. false doctrines diverging from the true path of life. The bracketed sense-word here (OCR «ντας» = *ὄντας*, "being/who were") is a secure minor restoration.
[^3]: That is, "good" not absolutely but only relatively — by comparison with the more thoroughly wicked.
