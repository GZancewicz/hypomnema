# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 35 · Matt. 22:34

> **But the Pharisees, having heard that he had silenced[^1] the Sadducees, were gathered together to the same *place*, and…**[^2]

This lemma carries no exposition of its own: it is the opening fragment of the next Gospel pericope (the lawyer's question), quoted in pieces and running on into the following lemmata. Theophylact gives his comment further on, where the quotation of the passage completes.

[^1]: Greek *ἐφίμωσε* — "he muzzled, put to silence" (from *φιμόω*, "to muzzle"); a vivid word, as of stopping an animal's mouth. Rendered "silenced."
[^2]: The lemma ends mid-sentence at *καὶ* ("and…"), completed in the next lemma with «one of them, a lawyer, asked». The phrase *ἐπὶ τὸ αὐτὸ* ("to the same *place* / together") is idiomatic for a common gathering; the supplied "*place*" is italicized.
