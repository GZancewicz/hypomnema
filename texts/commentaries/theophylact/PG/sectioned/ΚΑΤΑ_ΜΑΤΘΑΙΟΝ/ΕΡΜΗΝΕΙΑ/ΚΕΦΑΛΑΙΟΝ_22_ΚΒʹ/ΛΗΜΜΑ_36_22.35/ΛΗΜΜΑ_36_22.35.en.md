# Matthew 22 (ΚΒʹ) — Lemma 36 · Matt. 22:35

> **…one of them, a lawyer,[^1] asked, tempting[^2] him, and saying:…**

This lemma carries no exposition of its own: it is a continuation of the Gospel quotation, running on from the previous lemma («…and») into the next («Teacher, which commandment…»). Theophylact gives his comment further on, where the quotation of the passage completes.

[^1]: Greek *νομικός* — "a lawyer, one skilled in the Law," i.e. an expert in the Mosaic Law, not a legal advocate in the modern sense.
[^2]: Greek *πειράζων* — "testing, tempting, putting to the test" (present participle); the same verb used throughout the Gospels for those who try to trap Jesus. Rendered "tempting" to keep the hostile-testing sense.
