# Matthew 8 (Ηʹ) — Lemma 23 · Matt. 8:26

> **he says to them: Why are you fearful, O you of little faith?[^1] (Matt. 8:26a)**

He did not call *them* unbelieving, but of little faith; for, on the one hand, in so far as they said, "Lord, save us," they show forth faith, but, on the other hand, the "We are perishing" *is* not of faith. For, that One sailing along *with them*, they ought not [to have feared … to them?],[^2] he shows that fear brings on the dangers. Wherefore also, having first stilled the storm of their soul, he then looses also that of the sea:

> **Then having arisen, he rebuked the winds and the sea; and there came a great calm. But the—[^3] (Matt. 8:26b)**

[^1]: The sentence continues from lemma 22, which broke off at «Καὶ» ("And"); this file resumes at «λέγει αὐτοῖς». Greek *Τί δειλοί ἐστε, ὀλιγόπιστοι;* — *δειλοί* = "cowardly / timid / fearful"; *ὀλιγόπιστοι* = "of little faith." The whole comment turns on the contrast *δειλοί / ὀλιγόπιστοι* versus *ἄπιστοι* ("unbelieving").
[^2]: The Greek here is defective in the OCR: «Ἐκείνου γὰρ συμπλέοντος, οὐκ … λοῖς, δείκνυσιν ὡς ἡ δειλία φέρει τοὺς κινδύνους.» A clause has dropped out after *οὐκ* ("not"); the stray «λοῖς» is the tail of a lost word (probably *αὐτοῖς*, "to them"). The sense of the missing clause is evidently "they ought not to have feared, *and* [rebuking] them he shows…"; the legible frame is translated and the gap bracketed with [?]. *ἡ δειλία φέρει τοὺς κινδύνους* = "cowardice / fear brings on the dangers."
[^3]: The second scripture portion (8:26b) quoted at the close of the comment; OCR «ιΤότε» carries a stray iota for «Τότε» ("Then"). *ἐγερθείς* = "having arisen / been roused"; *ἐπετίμησε* = "rebuked"; *γαλήνη μεγάλη* = "a great calm." The trailing «Οἱ δὲ» ("But the—") is completed by lemma 24, which supplies «ἄνθρωποι ἐθαύμασαν…» ("the men marveled…").
