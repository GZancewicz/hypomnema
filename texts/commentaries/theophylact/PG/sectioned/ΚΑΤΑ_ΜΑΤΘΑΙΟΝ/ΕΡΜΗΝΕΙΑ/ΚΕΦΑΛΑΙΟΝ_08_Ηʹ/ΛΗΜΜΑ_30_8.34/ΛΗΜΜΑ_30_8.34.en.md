# Matthew 8 (Ηʹ) — Lemma 30 · Matt. 8:34

> **— city went out to meet Jesus, and having seen him, they besought *him*, that he might depart from their borders.[^1]**

Having been grieved, and thinking that they would suffer something worse after these things, they beg him off.[^2] But you, learn that where *there is* a swinish life, Christ, on the one hand, does not remain there, but demons, on the other hand, *do.*[^3]

[^1]: OCR corruption: «ἄπως» is securely **«ὅπως»** ("that / in order that"). The lemma resumes the sentence broken off in Lemma 29, the subject *πόλις* ("city") following the "the whole —" there; thus "*the whole* city went out." *τῶν ὁρίων αὐτῶν* = "their borders / territory."
[^2]: Greek *παραιτοῦνται αὐτόν* — "they decline / beg off / entreat him *to leave*," i.e. they ask him to go away (matching the lemma's «that he might depart»). The middle *παραιτοῦνται* carries the sense of politely refusing his presence.
[^3]: The sentence closes the chapter and breaks off at «δαίμονες δέ» ("but demons —"), the verb «μένουσιν» ("remain") supplied from the parallel clause: where life is swinish, Christ does not remain, "but demons *do*." In the source «δαίμονες δέ» runs straight into the next chapter heading («ΚΕΦΑΛ.»).
