# Matthew 8 (Ηʹ) — Lemma 6 · Matt. 8:8

> **And answering, the centurion said, Lord, I am not sufficient that you should enter under my roof, but only speak with a word, and my servant shall be healed; for I also ...**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation; it breaks off at "for I also ..." and is completed at the head of the next lemma, where Theophylact's exposition begins.*[^2]

[^1]: «οὐκ εἰμὶ ἱκανὸς» — literally "I am not sufficient / adequate," commonly rendered "I am not worthy"; "sufficient" is kept to stay close to ἱκανός. «εἰπὲ λόγῳ» — "speak with a word / say *it* in a word"; the instrumental λόγῳ ("by a word") is present in the Greek, hence roman. OCR «ἐκατόνταρχος» = ἑκατόνταρχος.
[^2]: The quotation continues into the next lemma («ἄνθρωπός εἰμι ὑπὸ ἐξουσίαν...», "I am a man under authority..."), after which Theophylact comments on the centurion's reasoning and Christ's marvel at his faith.
