# Matthew 8 (Ηʹ) — Lemma 3 · Matt. 8:5

> **And to him having entered into Capernaum, there came to him a centurion, beseeching him and saying.**[^1]

Neither did this *man* come to *him* on the mountain, so that the teaching might not be broken off. And this *is* the same *one* as *the one* also in Luke.[^2] But if Luke says that he sent others *as* envoys to Jesus, this is not a contradiction against Matthew, who says that that very man came *himself*. For it was likely that, on the one hand, first he sent others, and then, the danger pressing *upon him*, he himself came and said[^3]

[^1]: Folder metadata assigns 8:4, but the Greek quoted — «Εἰσελθόντι δὲ αὐτῷ εἰς Καπερναοὺμ, προσῆλθεν αὐτῷ ἑκατόνταρχος...» — is **Matt. 8:5**. OCR reads «ποοσῆλθεν» for προσῆλθεν and «ἐκατόνταρχος» for ἑκατόνταρχος. «Εἰσελθόντι δὲ αὐτῷ» is a dative construction, literally "and to him having entered"; smoother would be "and as he entered."
[^2]: Cf. Luke 7:2–10.
[^3]: The sentence breaks off at «καὶ λέγειν» ("and to say"); the centurion's words follow at the head of the next lemma («Κύριε, ὁ παῖς μου...»). OCR «λέχειν» = λέγειν, «ἐπικειιμένου» = ἐπικειμένου ("pressing / lying upon").
