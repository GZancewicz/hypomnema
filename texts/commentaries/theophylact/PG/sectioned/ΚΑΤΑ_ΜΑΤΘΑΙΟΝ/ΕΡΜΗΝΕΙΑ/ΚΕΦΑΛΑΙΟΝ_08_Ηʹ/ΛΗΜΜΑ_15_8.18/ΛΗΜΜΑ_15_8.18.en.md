# Matthew 8 (Ηʹ) — Lemma 15 · Matt. 8:18

> **But Jesus, having seen many crowds around him, commanded *them* to depart to the other side.**

For he was not fond of glory,[^1] and at the same time he *wished* also to avoid the envy of the Jews. And[^2]

[^1]: Greek *φιλόδοξος*, "fond of glory / vainglorious / eager for reputation." The point: he did not linger to be admired by the crowds. The infinitive *ἐκκλίνειν* ("to turn aside from, avoid") in «τῶν Ἰουδαίων τὸν φθόνον ἐκκλίνειν» hangs without a governing verb; a verb of intent ("he *wished*") is supplied for sense.
[^2]: The exposition breaks off at the dangling *Καὶ* ("And"); the next lemma opens with the following scripture quotation (Matt. 8:19, «προσελθὼν εἷς γραμματεὺς…»).
