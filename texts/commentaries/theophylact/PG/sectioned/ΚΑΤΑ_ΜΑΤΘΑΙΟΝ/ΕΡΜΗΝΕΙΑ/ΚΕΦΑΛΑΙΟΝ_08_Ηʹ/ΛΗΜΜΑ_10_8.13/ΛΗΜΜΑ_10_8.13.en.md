# Matthew 8 (Ηʹ) — Lemma 10 · Matt. 8:13

> **And Jesus said to the centurion: "Go, and as you believed, let it be done to you." And his servant was healed in that hour, and the centurion, having returned to his house, in that same hour, found the servant being in health. (Matt. 8:13)**

He showed, from the *things* by which he healed by a word, that also concerning the casting-away of the Jews he speaks tru—[^1]

[^1]: The exposition breaks off at «ἀλη-», the word cut by the sectioning; it is securely «ἀληθεύει», "he speaks truly / it is true" — i.e. that his foregoing prediction about the Jews being cast off is trustworthy. Rendered "he speaks tru—" to mark the cut. «ἐξ ὧν λόγῳ ἰάσατο» = "from *the facts* by which he healed with a word."
