# Matthew 8 (Ηʹ) — Lemma 22 · Matt. 8:25

> **his disciples, having come *to him*, awoke him, saying: Lord, save us, we are perishing. And—[^1] (Matt. 8:25)**

*This lemma folder carries only the scripture quotation. The sentence began at the «Καὶ» ("And") that closed lemma 21 and breaks off again here at «Καὶ» ("And"); Theophylact's comment follows on the next lemma.*[^1]

[^1]: Greek «προσελθόντες οἱ μαθηταὶ αὐτοῦ ἤγειραν αὐτόν, λέγοντες· Κύριε, σῶσον ἡμᾶς, ἀπολλύμεθα. Καὶ…» — *προσελθόντες* = "having come to / approached"; *ἤγειραν* = "awoke / raised"; *ἀπολλύμεθα* = "we are perishing." The trailing «Καὶ» is completed by lemma 23, which supplies «λέγει αὐτοῖς· Τί δειλοί ἐστε…» ("he says to them: Why are you fearful…"). There is no commentary in this file.
