# Matthew 8 (Ηʹ) — Lemma 5 · Matt. 8:7

> **... says to him Jesus: I, having come, will heal him.**[^1]

He did not bring the servant upon a bed, believing that *Jesus* is able to heal him even when absent; wherefore[^2]

[^1]: «Ἐγὼ ἐλθὼν θεραπεύσω αὐτόν» — "I, having come, will heal him"; the emphatic ἐγώ ("I") is retained. The participle ἐλθών is kept as a participle ("having come") rather than smoothed to "I will come and heal."
[^2]: The comment breaks off at «διὸ» ("wherefore / for which reason"); the centurion's reply that follows is quoted at the head of the next lemma. OCR «τδν παῖδα» = τὸν παῖδα ("the servant"), «δί ναται» = δύναται ("is able").
