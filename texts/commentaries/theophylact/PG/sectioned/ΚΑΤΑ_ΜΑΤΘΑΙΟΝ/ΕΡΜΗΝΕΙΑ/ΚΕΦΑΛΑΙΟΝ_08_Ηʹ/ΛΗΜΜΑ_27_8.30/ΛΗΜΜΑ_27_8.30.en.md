# Matthew 8 (Ηʹ) — Lemma 27 · Matt. 8:30

> **Now there was, far off from them, a herd of many swine feeding; and the[^1] —**

*This lemma carries only the scripture text, with no exposition; the sentence breaks off at "and the," and is completed in the following lemma («demons besought him…»).*[^2]

[^1]: OCR corruption at the head: «ν ἐὲ μακρὰν» is securely **«Ἦν δὲ μακρὰν»** ("Now there was, far off"). The clause ends «οἱ δὲ» ("and the…"), which runs directly into Lemma 28, where the subject *δαίμονες* ("demons") follows.
[^2]: Theophylact quotes Matt. 8:30–31 across the lemma boundary, breaking mid-sentence; the continuation and its comment fall under Lemmata 28–29.
