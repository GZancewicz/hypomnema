# Matthew 8 (Ηʹ) — Lemma 27 · Matt. 8:30–31

> **Now there was, far off from them, a herd of many swine feeding;[^1] and the demons besought him, saying: If you cast us out, permit us to go away into the herd of the swine.[^2] And — (Matt. 8:30–31)**

[^1]: OCR corruption at the head: «ν ἐὲ μακρὰν» is securely **«Ἦν δὲ μακρὰν»** ("Now there was, far off"). The clause continues «οἱ δὲ» ("and the…"), whose subject *δαίμονες* ("demons") follows.
[^2]: OCR corruption: «ἐπίτρεύον» is securely **«ἐπίτρεψον»** ("permit / grant"), and «τὴν ἀγάλην» = **«τὴν ἀγέλην»** ("the herd"). Theophylact's text reads *ἀπελθεῖν εἰς τὴν ἀγέλην τῶν χοίρων* ("to go away into the herd of the swine"), where the common text of Matt. 8:31 has *ἀπόστειλον ἡμᾶς εἰς τὴν ἀγέλην* ("send us into the herd"); the paraphrase is translated as it stands.
