# Matthew 8 (Ηʹ) — Lemma 16 · Matt. 8:19

> **a certain[^1] scribe, having approached, said to him: Teacher, I will follow you wherever you may go. And**[^2]

[^1]: Greek *εἷς γραμματεύς*, literally "one scribe"; *εἷς* is used here for the indefinite *τις*, "a certain." Rendered "a certain scribe."
[^2]: The quotation (Matt. 8:19) runs on without a closing «»; it ends on the dangling *Καὶ* ("And") and is continued and closed in Lemma 17 with «λέγει αὐτῷ ὁ Ἰησοῦς· Αἱ ἀλώπεκες φωλεοὺς ἔχουσι…» ("Jesus says to him: The foxes have holes…"). Theophylact's exposition proper begins there.
