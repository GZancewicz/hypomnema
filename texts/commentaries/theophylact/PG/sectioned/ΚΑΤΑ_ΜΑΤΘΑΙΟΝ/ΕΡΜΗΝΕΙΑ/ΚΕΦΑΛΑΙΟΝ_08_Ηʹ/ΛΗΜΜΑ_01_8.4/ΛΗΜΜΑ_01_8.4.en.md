# Matthew 8 (Ηʹ) — Lemma 1 · Matt. 8:3

> **And having stretched out *his* hand Jesus touched him, saying: I will, be cleansed. And immediately his leprosy was cleansed. And ...**[^1]

[^1]: The folder metadata assigns this lemma to 8:4, but the Greek it quotes — «Καὶ ἐκτείνας τὴν χεῖρα ἥψατο αὐτοῦ ὁ Ἰησοῦς, λέγων· Θέλω, καθαρίσθητι. Καὶ εὐθέως ἐκαθαρίσθη αὐτοῦ ἡ λέπρα» — is **Matt. 8:3**; the quotation then runs on into 8:4 ("And he says to him..."). The OCR reads «ἰκτείνας» and «ἤψατο»; securely = ἐκτείνας ("having stretched out") and ἥψατο ("touched," + genitive αὐτοῦ). The article in «τὴν χεῖρα» ("the hand") carries the possessive, so the supplied "*his*" is italicized. «Θέλω» is Christ's answer echoing the leper's «ἐὰν θέλῃς» ("if you will") from 8:2; rendered "I will," i.e. "I am willing."
