# Matthew 8 (Ηʹ) — Lemma 24 · Matt. 8:27

> **the men marveled, saying: What manner *of man* is this, that even the winds and the sea obey him?[^1] (Matt. 8:27)**

For that which is seen, on the one hand, *is* of a man; but the works, on the other hand, *are* of God.[^2]

[^1]: The sentence continues from lemma 23, which broke off at «Οἱ δὲ» ("But the—"); this file resumes at «ἄνθρωποι». Greek *Ποταπός ἐστιν οὗτος* — *ποταπός* = "of what sort / what manner of"; *ὑπακούουσιν αὐτῷ* = "obey / hearken to him."
[^2]: Greek *Τὸ μὲν γὰρ ὁρώμενον, ἀνθρώπου· τὰ δὲ ἔργα, Θεοῦ.* — a compressed *μὲν … δὲ* antithesis with the verb "*is/are*" left to be supplied and the predicate nouns in the genitive of possession: "the visible *thing is* of a man, but the works *are* of God." *τὸ ὁρώμενον* = "that which is seen / the visible *aspect*."
