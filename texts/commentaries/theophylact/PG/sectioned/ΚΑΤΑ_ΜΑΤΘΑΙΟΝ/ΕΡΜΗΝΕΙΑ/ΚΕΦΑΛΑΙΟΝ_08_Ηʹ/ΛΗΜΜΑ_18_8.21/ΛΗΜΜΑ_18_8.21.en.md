# Matthew 8 (Ηʹ) — Lemma 18 · Matt. 8:21

> **But another of his disciples said to him: Lord, permit me first to depart and to bury my father. But he**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation, which breaks off mid-sentence at "But he"; the exposition begins in the next lemma, where the quotation is also completed.*[^1]

[^1]: The quotation (Matt. 8:21) runs on without a closing «»; it stops at *Ὁ δὲ* ("But he") and is continued and closed in Lemma 19 with «…Ἰησοῦς εἶπεν αὐτῷ· Ἀκολούθει μοι, καὶ ἄφες τοὺς νεκροὺς θάψαι τοὺς ἑαυτῶν νεκρούς» ("…Jesus said to him: Follow me, and let the dead bury their own dead"). Theophylact's exposition proper begins there.
