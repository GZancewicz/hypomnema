# Matthew 8 (Ηʹ) — Lemma 8 · Matt. 8:11

> **But I say to you, that many from *the* East and West will come, and will recline with Abraham and Isaac, and Jacob in the kingdom of the heavens, but the sons of the— (Matt. 8:11)**[^1]

*This lemma carries only the scripture quotation, with no exposition of its own; the comment on it stands in the following lemma.*[^1]

[^1]: The lemma consists solely of the Gospel words and breaks off mid-sentence at «οἱ δὲ υἱοὶ τῆς» ("but the sons of the—"). The sentence is completed at the head of the next lemma, «βασιλείας ἐκβληθήσονται…» ("…kingdom shall be cast out…"). «ἀπὸ Ἀνατολῶν καὶ Δυσμῶν» is anarthrous; the supplied "*the*" is italicized. «ἀνακλιθήσονται» = "will recline / be made to recline" (at table).
