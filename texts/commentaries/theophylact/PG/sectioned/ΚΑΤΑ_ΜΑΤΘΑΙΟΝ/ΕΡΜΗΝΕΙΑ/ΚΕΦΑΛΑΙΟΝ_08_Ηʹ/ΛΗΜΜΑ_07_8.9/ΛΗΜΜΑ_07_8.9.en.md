# Matthew 8 (Ηʹ) — Lemma 7 · Matt. 8:9–10

> **A man am I under authority,[^1] having under myself soldiers, and I say to this *one*, "Go," and he goes; and to another, "Come," and he comes; and to my servant, "Do this," and he does *it*. But Jesus, having heard, marvelled and said to those following: "Amen, I say to you, not even in Israel have I found so great faith." (Matt. 8:9–10)**

If I, he says, being the servant of a king, give orders to the soldiers under me, you much more are able to give orders to death and to the diseases, so as to withdraw from this one, but to be set upon another; for the bodily diseases are soldiers and avengers of God.[^2] Christ therefore marvels, saying that, Not even among the Israelites did I find so great faith as in the Gentile.

[^1]: The centurion's sentence begins in the previous lemma with «καὶ γὰρ ἐγὼ» ("for I too"); this lemma resumes it at «ἄνθρωπός εἰμι ὑπὸ ἐξουσίαν» ("a man am I under authority"). Word order kept close to the Greek; «εἰμι» is a present verb and so stands in roman.
[^2]: The Greek is OCR-corrupt here — «στρατιῶται καὶ ἔκδικοίἰαι ποῦ Θεοῦ» — securely recoverable as «στρατιῶται καὶ ἔκδικοι [εἰσι] τοῦ Θεοῦ», "are soldiers and avengers of God." The copula is unexpressed and supplied for sense. «ἔκδικος» = "avenger, executor of vengeance/justice."
