# Matthew 8 (Ηʹ) — Lemma 11 · Matt. 8:14

> **And Jesus, having come into the house of Peter, saw his mother-in-law laid *low*[^1] and being in a fever. And— (Matt. 8:14)**[^2]

[^1]: Greek «βεβλημένην», perfect passive participle of «βάλλω», literally "having been thrown/cast *down*" — i.e. lying prostrate, bedridden. Rendered "laid *low*"; the supplied "*low*" is italicized.
[^2]: The lemma is scripture only and breaks off at «Καὶ» ("And—"), resumed at the head of the next lemma, «ἥψατο τῆς χειρὸς αὐτῆς…» ("he touched her hand…").
