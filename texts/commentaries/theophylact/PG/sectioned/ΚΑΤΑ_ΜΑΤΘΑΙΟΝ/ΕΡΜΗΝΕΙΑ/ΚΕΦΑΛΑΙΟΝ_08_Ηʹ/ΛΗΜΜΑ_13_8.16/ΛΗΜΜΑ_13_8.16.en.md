# Matthew 8 (Ηʹ) — Lemma 13 · Matt. 8:16

> **But when evening had come, they brought to him many who were possessed by demons, and he cast out the spirits by a word, and[^1] he healed all who were ill, so that**[^2]

[^1]: The word rendered "and" is OCR-garbled here as «ννὶ» (reading on into «πάνπας τοὺνς κακῶς ἕνοντας»); securely = **«καὶ πάντας τοὺς κακῶς ἔχοντας ἐθεράπευσεν»** ("and he healed all who were ill"). The idiom *κακῶς ἔχοντας*, literally "having badly," means "being ill / faring badly."
[^2]: The quotation (Matt. 8:16) runs on without a closing «»; it stops at *ὅπως* ("so that") and is continued and closed in Lemma 14 with «πληρωθῇ τὸ ῥηθὲν διὰ Ἡσαΐου…» ("that there might be fulfilled that which was spoken through Isaiah…"). Theophylact's exposition proper begins there.
