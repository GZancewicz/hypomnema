# Matthew 8 (Ηʹ) — Lemma 28 · Matt. 8:31

> **— demons besought him, saying: If you cast us out, permit us to go away into the herd of the swine.[^1] And —**

*This lemma carries only the scripture text, with no exposition. It resumes the sentence broken off in Lemma 27 (its subject "demons" following the "and the" there), and itself breaks off at "And —," which is completed in the following lemma («he said to them: Go…»).*

[^1]: OCR corruption: «ἐπίτρεύον» is securely **«ἐπίτρεψον»** ("permit / grant"), and «τὴν ἀγάλην» = **«τὴν ἀγέλην»** ("the herd"). Theophylact's text reads *ἀπελθεῖν εἰς τὴν ἀγέλην τῶν χοίρων* ("to go away into the herd of the swine"), where the common text of Matt. 8:31 has *ἀπόστειλον ἡμᾶς εἰς τὴν ἀγέλην* ("send us into the herd"); the paraphrase is translated as it stands.
