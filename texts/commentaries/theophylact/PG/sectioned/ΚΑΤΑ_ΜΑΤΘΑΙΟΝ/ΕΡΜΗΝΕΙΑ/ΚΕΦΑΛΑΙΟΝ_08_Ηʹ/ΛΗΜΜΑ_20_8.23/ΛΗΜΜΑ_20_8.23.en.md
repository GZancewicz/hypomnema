# Matthew 8 (Ηʹ) — Lemma 20 · Matt. 8:23

> **And when he had embarked into the boat, his disciples followed him, and behold,[^1] (Matt. 8:23)**

*This lemma folder carries only the scripture quotation, which breaks off mid-sentence at «καὶ ἰδοὺ» ("and behold"); Theophylact's exposition of the storm at sea begins with the next lemma.*[^1]

[^1]: Greek «Καὶ ἐμβάντι αὐτῷ εἰς τὸ πλοῖον, ἠκολούθησαν αὐτῷ οἱ μαθηταὶ αὐτοῦ, καὶ ἰδοὺ…» — *ἐμβάντι αὐτῷ* is a dative participle-construction ("to him having embarked"), rendered "when he had embarked." The sentence is completed by lemma 21, which supplies «σεισμὸς μέγας ἐγένετο…» ("a great tempest arose…"). There is no commentary in this file.
