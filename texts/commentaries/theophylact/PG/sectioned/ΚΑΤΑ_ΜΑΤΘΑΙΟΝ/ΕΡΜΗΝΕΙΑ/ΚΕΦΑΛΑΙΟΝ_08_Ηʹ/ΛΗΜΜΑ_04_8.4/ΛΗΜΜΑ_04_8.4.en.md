# Matthew 8 (Ηʹ) — Lemma 4 · Matt. 8:6

> **Lord, my servant is laid in the house paralytic, grievously tormented. And ...**[^1]

[^1]: Folder metadata assigns 8:4, but the Greek — «Κύριε, ὁ παῖς μου βέβληται ἐν τῇ οἰκίᾳ παραλυτικός, δεινῶς βασανιζόμενος» — is **Matt. 8:6**. «ὁ παῖς μου» can mean "my boy" or "my servant/slave"; in a centurion's household the servant-sense is primary (Luke has δοῦλος at 7:2), but παῖς keeps the affectionate overtone. Rendered "servant." «βέβληται» is perfect passive, literally "has been laid / is thrown down," i.e. bedridden.
