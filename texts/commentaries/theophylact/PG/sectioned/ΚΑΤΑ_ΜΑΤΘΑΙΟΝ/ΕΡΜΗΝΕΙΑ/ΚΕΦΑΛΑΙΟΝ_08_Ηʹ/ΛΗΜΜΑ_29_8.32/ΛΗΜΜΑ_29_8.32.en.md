# Matthew 8 (Ηʹ) — Lemma 29 · Matt. 8:32–33

> **— he said to them: Go. And they, having gone out, went away into the herd of the swine.**

The demons ask *this* so that, having drowned the swine, they might grieve their masters, so that *these* might not receive Christ. But Christ permits the demons, showing what sort of bitterness they have toward men, and that, if they had authority and were not hindered, they would have handled us worse than the swine.[^1] For that *One* guards those who are demon-possessed, so that they do not[^2] kill themselves.

> **And behold, the whole herd of the swine rushed down the steep bank into the sea, and they died in the waters.[^3] And those feeding *them* fled, and having gone away into the city, they reported all things, and the things concerning the demoniacs. And behold, the whole —**

[^1]: Greek *χεῖρον ἂν τῶν χοίρων διέθεντο ἡμᾶς* — "they would have disposed / dealt with us worse than the swine," i.e. worse than *they dealt with* the swine (which they drowned). The comparative *χεῖρον … τῶν χοίρων* is rendered "worse than the swine."
[^2]: A stretch of OCR garbage stands in the source between «ὥστε μὴ» and «φονεύειν ἑαυτούς» — namely «Β εἰοίι κουωοαοοε, Πε εεροοε οοοεηι.» — which is not recoverable Greek but the mangled remains of a running header / column bleed; the sentence itself reads continuously as **«ὥστε μὴ φονεύειν ἑαυτούς»** ("so that they do not kill themselves"). The garbled characters are disregarded as non-textual.
[^3]: OCR corruption: «ἐν τοῖς ΗΑααιν» is securely **«ἐν τοῖς ὕδασιν»** ("in the waters"), and «θόσκοντες» = **«βόσκοντες»** ("those feeding / the herdsmen"). The clause ends «Καὶ ἰδοὺ πᾶσα ἡ» ("And behold, the whole —"), completed in the next lemma by *πόλις* ("city").
