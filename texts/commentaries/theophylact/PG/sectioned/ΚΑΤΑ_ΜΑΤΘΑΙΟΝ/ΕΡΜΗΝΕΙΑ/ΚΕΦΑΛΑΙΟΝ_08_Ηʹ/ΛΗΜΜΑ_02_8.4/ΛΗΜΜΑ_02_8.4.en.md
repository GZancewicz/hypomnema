# Matthew 8 (Ηʹ) — Lemma 2 · Matt. 8:4

> **... says to him Jesus: See *that* you tell no one; but go, show yourself to the priest, and offer the gift which Moses commanded, for a testimony to them.**

He touches the leper, showing that he is not subject to the law — which commands not to touch the leper — but is *its* Master,[^1] and that to the pure nothing *is* unclean, and that his holy flesh imparted sanctification. But fleeing the glory, he commands *him* to tell no one, but to show himself to the priest. For if the priest did not say that the leper had been cleansed, he would remain outside the city. And he commands *him* also to offer the gift for a testimony to the Jews, that is: When they accuse me as *one* abolishing the law, you shall be a witness[^2] — you who now have been commanded by me to bring forward the things ordained by the law.

[^1]: Greek «Δεσπότης τούτου ἐστί» — "he is Master of this." The antecedent of τούτου is the law (τῷ νόμῳ): Christ stands not under it but over it. Rendered "*its* Master." OCR here reads «Ἐτι» for ὅτι ("that") and «ναὶ ὅτι» for καὶ ὅτι ("and that"); both corrections are secure.
[^2]: The clause is OCR-corrupted as «Κσῃ μάρτυς οὐ ὁ νῦν κελευσθεὶς παρ᾽ ἐμοῦ προσ.αγαγεῖν»; securely = «ἔσῃ μάρτυς σὺ ὁ νῦν κελευσθεὶς παρ᾽ ἐμοῦ προσαγαγεῖν» ("you shall be a witness — you who now have been commanded by me to bring forward"). Also «ἔμινε» = ἔμενε ("would remain"). The sense: the priest's own certification of the cure, made per Mosaic prescription, will later refute the charge that Christ dissolves the law.
